## Strongest lawful development-panel route

GitBug-Java is the strongest newly identified metadata donor: its primary paper describes 199 reproducible 2023 bugs from 55 repositories, while the pinned root is MIT. It still cannot be admitted wholesale: its repository, workflow-log, dependency, bug-fix and project bytes need per-project/content-class rights, and public fixes cannot become protected outcomes.

BEARS adds a reproducible fail/fix lineage and GitHub-project slug for clustering; Bugs.jar adds eight project roots; both remain content-rights limited. QuixBugs is not source-disjoint and its own legal note records an uncertain IP chain despite the root MIT file. ManyBugs and BugsInPy lack a root licence at the audited pins.
