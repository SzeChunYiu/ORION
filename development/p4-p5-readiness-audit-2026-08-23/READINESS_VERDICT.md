## P4: what can advance now

### Lawful public-data lane

1. Make the design identity-complete: enumerate the four domains, eight mechanism IDs/rubrics, eligibility/exclusion rules, pair-construction rules, four comparator identities, attrition handling, and primary/replication allocation before case access.
2. Fetch and hash the exact CC BY arXiv full texts into a quarantined screening layer; retain only separately cleared text spans and exclude third-party figures/data/supplements unless independently licensed.
3. Replace first-in-response quota filling with a preregistered temporal rule. Add separately rights-bound provider and modality strata; do not infer content rights from metadata visibility.
4. Predeclare a **natural pair-origin taxonomy**. A restricted view should be an information state that existed independently of the benchmark authors (for example a versioned abstract/full-text state or a genuinely unavailable source component), not an outcome-authored redaction designed to force `CannotCheck`.
5. Freeze comparator native-output schemas and run the terminal-adapter fibre test on outcome-free development cases.
6. Report only eligibility counts and exclusions by domain x mechanism until external custody exists.

### Requires external custody

- raw source and full-text case bytes;
- material-claim eligibility and mechanism labels;
- the gold `ResolvedTrue / ResolvedFalse / CannotCheck` terminal;
- disagreement adjudication;
- primary versus source/author/DOI-disjoint replication allocation;

### P4 claim gate

A positive pilot is not the paper-level result. The broad claim becomes eligible for testing only when all 32 cells have frozen primary and replication quotas, the natural-pair criterion is independently satisfied, four comparator identities have terminal-attainable maps, and external raw-case/evaluator custody is signed and hashed. If a cell is short, the result is a source-cell negative and the source universe must expand prospectively; cases may not be reassigned after outcomes.

## Claim ceilings

### Safe now

- **P4:** the tested exact contracts show that scientific promotion can remain a distinct non-compensatory relation after donor-owned provenance/verification/custody mechanisms are granted; an information-equivalent typed product ties.
- **P5:** the architecture and theory specify a fail-closed minimal-revision process; the 21/24 archive is descriptive and its three errors seed prospective discriminator studies.
- **Both:** public-source and comparator audits narrow specific upstream blockers but do not create protected scientific outcomes.

### Conditional future claims

- **P4:** naturalistic three-terminal decision performance on the exact frozen panel, against the exact frozen comparator family, with provider/modality/time and source-disjoint replication boundaries stated.
- **P5:** protected fresh-transfer and harm performance on the exact frozen eight-class panel, against the exact six-arm family, with external custody and all non-compensatory gates stated.

### Not authorized

- universal scientific judgement;
- superiority to original systems that were not executed under a fair, frozen adapter;
- universal or provider-agnostic transport;
- beneficial self-improvement or reduced harm from local tests or the 21/24 diagnostic;
- external independence based only on role labels inside the same repository;
- turning a negative, null, harmful, or `CANNOT_CHECK` predecessor into a positive result by changing the study silently.
