# P11-P15 confirmatory execution packet

## Development question

Which remaining P11-P15 claim gaps can be closed with evidence already available
inside this checkout, and which must fail closed because their required authority
is external or has not been acquired?

## Atomic questions

1. Does P11I reproduce byte-for-byte, and can every terminal gate be shown to
   reject at least one admissible hostile result?
2. Can P12 isolate the informational value of a second signal while holding the
   budget, action set, scoring rule and independent family units fixed?
3. Can P13 exercise certificate corruption against gold support defined outside
   the certificate, with a nonzero opportunity denominator in every registered
   corruption world?
4. Which P14 external-validation prerequisites can be frozen and validated
   locally without representing an internally authored packet as external?
5. Can P15 advance from an unscoped methods directory to a frozen acquisition
   contract while refusing empirical execution before a donor matrix and
   protected corpus exist?

## Incumbent mechanics and negative history

- P11D/H are immutable adverse attack history; P11I is the active bounded r=7
  successor.
- P12A's one-axis comparators had smaller action sets.  Capability matching
  removes the apparent 0.33 margin and fails P12A's registered effect gates.
- P13A's unsafe-reuse endpoint was self-scored by the certificate that controlled
  reuse, producing zero opportunities.  A certificate-independent control has
  live opportunities but is locally authored finite-world gold, not external
  validation.
- P14C is a positive specification-conformance result; external scientific
  validity remains unmeasured.
- P15 has no protected scientific result.

## Bounded saturation assessment

The local search universe comprises the complete P11-P15 paper directories,
their runners and receipts, `src/orion/study/p11` through `p15`, all corresponding
unit tests, the programme's attainability/outcome-contingency instruments, and
the two harness surfaces named by P15.  Within that universe:

- P12 already exposes equal-action one-signal arms, but no prospectively frozen
  exact-allocation successor exists;
- P13 already exposes certificate-independent finite-world gold, but no protected
  corruption-panel successor exists;
- no P14 blinded external packet, adjudicator-custody record or external gold is
  present;
- no P15 donor matrix, paper issue or protected fault-injection corpus is present.

This saturates local execution availability, not the external scientific search
universe.

## Challenge to the saturation basis

Filename searches can miss data embedded in generic receipts, and an internally
authored object can be mistaken for independent evidence merely because it lives
in another file.  Every proposed input must therefore be content-bound and
classified by authority, not filename.  In particular, P13's gold is independent
of the certificate but not independent of the repository authors, and P14C's
separate specification is not external adjudication.

## Why earlier searches could have missed relevant knowledge

1. `matched budget` was treated as matched capability although action sets
   differed.
2. zero observed violations was treated as evidence without an opportunity
   denominator.
3. separate code/specification files were treated as independent custody.
4. a paper directory was treated as a paper result before a hypothesis existed.
5. repeated cells were at risk of being counted as independent RNG replicates.

## Frozen implementation hypotheses

- P11: exact replay plus hostile gate mutations will preserve the bounded positive
  terminal and demonstrate terminal responsiveness.  `n=3` independent RNG
  replicates; three geometry strata per replicate are fixed repeated conditions.
- P12B: with all arms sharing the same four exact allocations, the two-signal arm
  will exceed the stronger one-signal arm by at least 0.15 in mean exact-allocation
  rate across 32 independent family RNG blocks, with a family-block 95% bootstrap
  lower bound at least 0.12 and every fixed noise stratum at least 0.12.
- P13B: an authenticated certificate policy will reject every registered mutated
  certificate, produce zero gold-scored unsafe reuse in each adverse world, and
  retain lower valid-panel cost than always reopening.  This authorizes only a
  controlled finite-world claim.
- P14/P15: validators will emit blocked acquisition states until externally owned
  artifacts exist; no local placeholder can satisfy the missing authority.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen the design before promotion if any protected runner differs on replay; an
arm receives a hidden signal or a different action; a P12 family rather than its
RNG block is counted as independent; any P13 corruption world has zero
opportunities; P13 gold is read from a certificate; a locally authored P14 object
is accepted as external; or P15 executes without a content-bound donor matrix and
protected corpus.
