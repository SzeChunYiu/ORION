# Scientific question and exclusion boundary

## Primary question

For the uniquely resolved `Quantomatic/pyzx` commit whose hexadecimal object name begins with `dade7d46`, can the production graph-rewrite surface used by the ORION execution environment be represented by a finite **source-complete extensional move registry**?

“Source-complete” means complete relative to a prospectively frozen source boundary, mutation-root inventory, call-graph closure, parameter domains, and side conditions. “Extensional” means that syntactic aliases are quotiented only after equality of normalized graph effects is established on a declared separating basis.

The successor has two admissible scientific outcomes:

1. a registry-completeness receipt proving, relative to the frozen obligations, that every production move belongs to exactly one canonical effect class; or
2. a concrete counterexample identifying a reachable production mutation that the proposed registry or source boundary misses.

Only after outcome 1 may a separately frozen exact state-space experiment ask whether the complete quotient registry changes a material compiler conclusion.

## Material consequence

A successful registry theorem would provide an auditable production-language boundary, remove duplicate syntactic aliases from exact search, and make later completeness claims relative to a named production language rather than to a hand-curated list. A counterexample would be equally material: it would invalidate the prior registry boundary and prevent a false move-completeness claim.

A larger enumeration is not itself a contribution. Search volume, state count, wall-clock time, or a higher cap cannot satisfy this question.

## Frozen exclusions

This identity does not ask whether:

- the capped Round-2/Round-3 search would finish under a larger limit;
- every mathematically conceivable ZX rewrite is implemented by PyZX;
- the scanner finds unreachable or dead source code;
- two moves are equivalent merely because their names, local patterns, or small random tests match;
- source completeness implies hardware advantage, circuit-depth advantage, or a peer-reviewed theorem;
- the old terminal `CANNOT_CHECK_MOVE_COMPLETENESS` can be upgraded retrospectively.

## Units of analysis

- **Source mutation root:** a reachable callable or method that can mutate the graph or return a mutation applied by a reachable caller.
- **Move instance:** a mutation root together with a fully specified admissible parameter and side-condition assignment.
- **Normalized effect:** the deterministic before/after graph delta represented in the frozen canonical graph encoding.
- **Canonical effect class:** an equivalence class of move instances whose normalized effects agree on the complete separating basis.
- **Registry completeness:** every reachable production move instance in the frozen boundary maps to one and only one canonical effect class.
