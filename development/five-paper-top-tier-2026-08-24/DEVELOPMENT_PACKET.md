## 1. Governing publication workflow

This lane applies the current `SzeChunYiu/academic-paper-skills` contracts recursively rather than treating manuscript polish as a substitute for science. The loaded workflow includes:

- `nature-academic-search` and `nature-literature-pipeline`: auditable multi-source discovery, source-ecology routing, query/date/filter provenance, deduplication, source-level tagging, and explicit search for contradictory/limiting prior work;
- `nature-paper-card`: claim/evidence/boundary inventories for nearest donors;
- `nature-writing`: evidence-first rhetorical architecture `question/tension -> answer -> evidence chain -> boundary -> meaning`, analogue-paper calibration, explanatory sufficiency, and minimum-sufficient main-text evidence;
- `nature-reviewer`: separate editorial triage, validity review, positioning/novelty review, reproducibility/boundary review, and post-review decision engineering;
- `nature-ref-verifier` and `nature-citation`: field-level metadata/DOI/reference validation;
- `nature-figure`: claim-driven figure/evidence planning;
- `nature-polishing`: only after claim, evidence, boundary, consistency, and target-fit gates pass.

No journal prestige is used as an evidence-quality filter. Exact target criteria are resolved per paper and article type; a single universal `top tier` score is forbidden.

### Paper C — low-order decision, high-order value

Therefore every primitive nonzero trade occupies all `2^q` cells and has at least `2^(q-1)|c|` positive and negative mass. The registered parity construction attains equality. This establishes exponential *difference support* as unavoidable while leaving common-padding minimality open. Generic Markov-basis/Möbius mathematics receives donor credit.

## 4. Publication gates applied separately to every paper

### Scientific gate

- exact headline theorem stated with all quantifiers and assumptions;
- strongest alternative interpretation either discriminated or used to narrow the claim;
- negative/counterexample evidence retained visibly;
- no frozen result changed to obtain a positive story.

### Novelty/donor gate

- auditable search log records sources, exact queries, date, stable IDs, dedup and source depth;
- nearest primary donors receive explicit subtraction;
- search snippets and metadata alone cannot establish novelty;
- a close parent demotes or removes the affected claim.

### Reviewer gate

- editorial-triage simulation separated from technical validity review;
- validity, positioning, and reproducibility/boundary lenses each receive stable concern IDs and resolution tests;
- blocking issues close by evidence, analysis, correction, clarification, claim narrowing/removal, or target transfer, not by stronger adjectives.

### Manuscript gate

- question, answer, evidence chain, boundary and meaning are recoverable;
- central equations/theorems pass explanatory-sufficiency checks;
- repository paths/test names/PR history stay out of reader-facing prose except availability/reproducibility surfaces;
- main figures each perform a claim-bearing or orientation function.

### Release gate

- immutable receipt/report/result identities bound;
- new verifiers deterministic and independently structured where claimed;
- focused tests and repository checks green;
- remote branch precondition rechecked before every ref update;
- integration through PR only; no force update and no direct main push.

## 5. Honest top-tier definition

`TOP_TIER_READY` is permitted only as an internal *submission-readiness posture* after the above gates and exact target fit pass. It does not mean novelty is certified, peer review is complete, or acceptance is likely. A scientifically strong paper that fails the selected venue's breadth/priority criterion must be transferred rather than rhetorically inflated.

The non-quantum paper is not allowed to claim `D_4(C_5^3)=30` or `31 in C_0(C_5^3)` until those statements are actually proved. If exact D4 remains open, the paper can still become a rigorous specialist candidate, but it cannot be labelled Combinatorica/JCTA-ready merely because its prose is polished.
