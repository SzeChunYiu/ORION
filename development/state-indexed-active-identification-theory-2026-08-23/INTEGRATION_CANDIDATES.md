# Integration candidates: controlled-state active identification

No manuscript or predecessor packet was edited. These statements are candidate
insertions only after scientific and literature review.

## Candidate A — legal state-indexed frontier

For finite observable laboratory state \(s\), legal action set \(E(s)\),
state/action cost \(c(s,e)\), and joint observed branch kernel
\(Q_{s,e}(o,s'\mid w)\), the recurrence \(\Gamma_{n,s}\) exactly enumerates
world-risk vectors of all legal deterministic trees with at most \(n\)
acquisitions. This repairs the stationary model's inability to enforce
unavailable, destructive, replenishing, and no-repeat tests.

**Required wording:** donor-derived finite controlled-state theorem; not a new
POMDP alpha-vector result.

## Candidate B — robust value and policy/prior attainment split

For arbitrary fixed ex-ante \(\Pi\), deterministic robust value is
\(\min_{v\in\Gamma_{n,s}}h_\Pi(v)\); licensed world-independent randomization
uses \(\operatorname{conv}(\Gamma_{n,s})\). Policy minima are attained. A
least-favourable licensed prior need not exist when \(\Pi\) is nonclosed.

**Required wording:** distinguish deterministic theoretical value, randomized
theoretical value, and operational authorization to randomize.

## Candidate C — controlled-state Bayes recursion

Under one prior, condition jointly on outcome and observed next state. Empty
legal action sets force stopping. Zero-probability branches contribute zero and
do not authorize a posterior. The horizon is **at most** \(n\), not exactly
\(n\).

## Candidate D — exact rectangularity boundary

A fixed ex-ante prior family induces a set of path laws. Posterior-local minimax
solves the historywise-pasting rectangular hull. For a fixed policy, the two
robust expectations agree for every bounded loss exactly when the closed convex
induced path-law set is already stable under pasting. For the specified loss,
they differ exactly when rectangular closure strictly raises its support
functional.

**Required wording:** fixed prior and rectangular nature are distinct licensed
games; neither is called the “correct” one without protocol authority.

## Candidate E — zero-cost reachability front

Before applying information-per-cost obligations, enumerate legal zero-cost
paths. If they reach a target-sufficient transcript, no positive acquisition-
cost lower bound is possible. Downstream reduction must retain the full free
history/state rather than erase its information.

## Candidate F — controlled transcript purity

The complete transcript includes observed state transitions. Finite exact
target identification is equivalent to cross-target transcript-law singularity.
An observed stochastic state can separate targets; an unavailable action cannot.
