## 1. Scientific diagnosis

Paper 2 could not reach a top publication tier because its strongest empirical
objects and its stated theory lived at different scales.

- The controlled-index campaign is exact and useful, but synthetic and
  underpowered for its own frozen superiority margin.
- The MetaSyn run is external, but it tests a pinned retrieval/screening path,
  not the full multi-route controller.
- The external Wide acquisition family did not establish a positive acquisition
  effect.  In the archived adverse capture, the governed routes emitted only
  **7 of 229** gold identifiers; **222** never entered the candidate pool.
- Equal call counts hid unequal scientific opportunity: the lexical arm used
  three scoring-admissible calls per task, whereas the governed arm used one
  admissible call and two calls to interfaces unable to emit scorer-valid
  identities in that capture.
- The 400-case P2-X battery strongly isolates unresolved-route authority, but it
  supplies route materiality and state exactly.  It is not naturalistic
  open-world retrieval evidence.  Its ideal information-equivalent product
  ties 400/400, so centralized expressivity is not the residual.
- The previously frozen 768-cluster successor is a design, not an executed
  result.  Its four source-disjoint worlds, comparator reproductions,
  capability-matched external comparator, live receipts and independent
  evaluator/gold custody are not yet bound.

The scientific mistake would be to narrow the paper to whichever local metric
passed.  The upward correction is to compare complete scientific discovery
controllers on a joint frontier and make every adverse terminal identify which
part of that frontier is currently unreachable.

## 2. New theory: the acquisition--authority envelope

The manuscript now models a controller as four coupled operations:

1. route generation/selection;
2. evidence acquisition;
3. question-conditioned processing and option-preserving state construction;
4. scientific task closure.

For a common task distribution, source-access contract and charged budget, the
**acquisition--authority envelope** is the set of attainable vectors over:

- decision-relevant evidence discovery;
- final scientific utility;
- future-search optionality under hidden query/route changes;
- false task closure;
- valid closure yield; and
- fully charged cost.

False closure and validity are non-compensatory.  A high-quality report cannot
purchase authority to omit a decisive unresolved route.  A candidate earns the
wide claim only by exceeding the guarded frontier of the frozen donor-complete
policy class, not by beating one weak baseline.

### 2.1 Donor envelopment

The comparison class explicitly absorbs, rather than rhetorically excludes:

- SAGE-style lexical and corpus-side augmentation;
- AgentIR-style reasoning-aware retrieval;
- SIEVE-style fielded inspection and selective fetching;
- bibliography/citation expansion;
- HALT-style claim-evidence coverage;
- DeepControl/value-of-information and decision-theoretic continuation;
- conformal and evidence-aware stopping;
- finite obligation certificates and evidence-linked obligation graphs; and
- question-conditioned evidence plans/memory.

Adding any valid donor mechanism monotonically enlarges the attainable policy
class.  If a new donor reaches the candidate point after all costs are charged,
the claimed superiority is falsified.  This is intentional: nearest work raises
the frontier the theory must surpass.

### 2.2 Analytic results

The manuscript adds four distinct theorem identities.

1. **Run-conditional acquisition ceiling.**  A downstream selector cannot
   submit relevant evidence absent from the union emitted by all admissible
   routes.  This makes `7/229` an exact causal diagnosis of that capture: route
   generation was binding and reranking could not repair 222 never-acquired
   identifiers.  A companion constructive proposition proves that equal call
   counts alone place no nontrivial bound on the difference between arms'
   acquisition ceilings.
2. **Closure factorization.**  A product can implement the contract-correct
   closure decision iff closure truth is constant on each fibre of the state it
   retains.  This is necessary and sufficient, not an architectural preference.
3. **Indistinguishable-world lower bound.**  For a closable and materially open
   world with transcript laws `P0` and `P1`, false closure plus valid-closure
   shortfall is at least `1 - TV(P0,P1)`.  If the transcripts are identical, one
   error must be at least one half.
4. **Maximally permissive authority completion.**  Under a declared obligation
   and adversarial-realizability contract, closing only after every registered
   material obligation is discharged is sound, and any rule that closes a
   strict superset of histories is unsound on a compatible world.

The fourth result is deliberately contract-relative.  It does not smuggle in a
claim that finite web search enumerates the universe.  Independent route
observability, a closed registry, a validated population model, or
`CANNOT_CHECK` is still required.

### 2.3 Prospective positive mechanism family

The manuscript turns the prior ODAE idea into an explicit policy class rather
than a slogan.  At each step it selects among route invention, retrieval,
processing, contradiction resolution, state preservation/recovery and closure
verification using a charged index with three numerator terms:

1. a lower-confidence estimate of decision-relevant unresolved-obligation
   reduction;
2. option value retained for prospectively hidden future questions; and
3. expected separation of mixed closable/open state fibres.

The denominator is total charged action cost.  Missing route support produces
`CANNOT_CHECK`, not an assumed zero.  Generic value-of-information and adaptive
search remain donor-owned; the residual hypothesis is their non-compensatory
coupling to scientific obligations and closure identifiability.  This is frozen
as `P2-H-ODAE-ENVELOPE`, a prospective untested mechanism family.

## 4. Every adverse result becomes a distinct research family

### N1 — Candidate-generation ceiling (`222/229` never acquired)

**Research topic:** support-deficit-directed route invention.  Estimate which
evidence identity/source classes remain outside the current action support,
generate a materially different route, and verify that route outcome-blind
before protected use.

**Positive discriminator:** on fresh source-disjoint clusters, candidate
acquisition ceiling and decision-relevant route-use recall improve over a
donor-complete controller under identical admissible exposure.

### N2 — Equal calls, unequal admissible exposure

**Research topic:** exposure-equivalent experimental design.  Replace call-count
matching with pre-outcome interface-class witnesses and charged opportunity
measures.  An empty response marks the query outcome, not proof that the backend
could never emit the target identity class.

**Positive discriminator:** both arms have the same prospectively witnessed
source/interface opportunity; no outcome-bearing gold is leaked to workers.

### N3 — Coverage-first merge unsupported or harmful

**Research topic:** obligation-reduction acquisition.  Optimize expected
reduction of decision-relevant unresolved obligation mass, including
contradiction sides and downstream processing availability, rather than overlap
with the original query vocabulary.

**Positive discriminator:** fresh tasks where lexical/core coverage and
obligation reduction disagree, with the latter improving route-use recall and
scientific utility without a false-closure increase.

### N4 — Lexical echo can empty a common-vocabulary query

**Research topic:** hierarchical discriminativeness without a hard document-
frequency cliff.  Candidate replacements include domain-conditioned likelihood
ratios, shrinkage across corpus/domain/task levels, and an outcome-blind fallback
that preserves at least one semantically material term.

**Positive discriminator:** a frozen surface-confusable family in which all
material vocabulary is common globally but discriminative conditionally, plus a
clean family guarding against query broadening.

### N5 — Retrieved but not decision-usable

**Research topic:** option-valued state preservation.  Give state items value
for both current decision contribution and their ability to unlock a hidden
future question or route; charge compilation, caching and recovery.

**Positive discriminator:** future questions/routes frozen before state
construction and hidden from both arms; better future decision utility per total
cost than a donor cache/compiler.

### N6 — Provider invalidity and route censoring

**Research topic:** temporal common-world observability.  Bind route identities,
source snapshots/interface classes and pre-execution accessibility witnesses,
then distinguish common provider drift from arm-specific scientific failure.

**Positive discriminator:** high valid paired-task fraction across every arena,
with provider-invalid tasks mapped to `CANNOT_CHECK`, never to comparator loss.

### N7 — False closure versus always-open overcaution

**Research topic:** authority-complete but utility-preserving closure.  Infer or
acquire the minimal state refinement that splits mixed closable/open fibres,
rather than blocking on every unavailable non-material source.

**Positive discriminator:** lower or non-inferior false closure, retained valid
closure yield on clean closable tasks, and correct handling of non-material
unavailable decoys.

## 5. The 768-cluster upward successor

The existing `protocol/P2_TASK_WORLD_SUCCESSOR_V2.json` freezes:

- four strata with 192 source-disjoint task-world clusters each;
- 768 independent units total;
- four arenas;
- three primary comparators;
- identical query/provider/budget/scoring namespace and matched admissible route
  exposure;
- intersection across all comparator contrasts;
- planned effect `0.15` versus superiority margin `0.05`;
- declared joint target power `0.90`.

It still requires, before any claim-bearing execution:

1. archived reproduction of every baseline task world;
2. four immutable source-disjoint arena snapshots;
3. a capability-matched external comparator and donor-complete matched-host
   controller;
4. independent evaluator and gold/hidden-route custody;
5. live response, latency and total-cost receipts;
6. pre-execution route/interface observability witnesses;
7. a frozen output schema that independently reconstructs false-closure
   opportunities and valid-closure yield.

The protected hypothesis is **`P2-H-ENVELOPE-768`**, not a renamed version of a
failed V2 candidate.  A positive result must clear all acquisition, utility,
safety, closure-yield, validity, power and cost gates.  A failure opens the
responsible N-family above and a materially changed successor; it does not permit
margin relaxation, baseline deletion, task exclusion, or endpoint switching.
