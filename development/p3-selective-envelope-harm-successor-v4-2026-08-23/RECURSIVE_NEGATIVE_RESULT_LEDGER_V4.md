## Current frontier

The next positive empirical target is not “run more matchers.”  It is to create
an evaluable estimand: at least three lawful source-disjoint families with
content-addressed inputs and references and identified positive and negative
truth.  Only then can AML, LogMap and BERTMap be frozen, run without gold, and
compared with the selective controller family by family.

Until then, opening outcomes would convert a documented feasibility failure
into protocol leakage, not scientific progress.
