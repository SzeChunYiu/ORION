# Manuscript-ready P3 V4 text — not applied

## Abstract/result insertion

We identify a logically prior limitation of point-valued ontology-matching
evaluation.  When a reference alignment records positive correspondences but
is not authoritatively certified exhaustive over a frozen candidate universe,
an omitted candidate is compatible both with a true but unrecorded equivalence
and with genuine non-equivalence.  Its binary truth is therefore only partially
identified, so reference absence cannot license an obstruction label or an
unconditional harm score.  In a prospective metadata-only audit of seven OAEI
track families, none supplied complete reference rights, a cryptographic
release identity, and authoritative absence-as-negative semantics; zero were
eligible against a predeclared minimum of three.  We consequently opened no
new-family outcomes.  Exact source identities for AML, LogMap and BERTMap were
bound, but runtime and family-specific evaluation remain unexecuted.

## Theorem insertion

**Open-world reference non-identification.**  Let `U` be a frozen candidate
universe and `R+` an observed positive reference.  Unless source authority
certifies `R+` exhaustive over `U`, the truth of any `x in U \ R+` is not
identified by `(U,R+)`: a world in which `x` is a true but omitted equivalence
and a world in which `x` is not an equivalence induce the same observations.
Thus the identified set is `{GLUE, OBSTRUCTION}`.  Assigning obstruction to
`x` adds an unobserved closed-world axiom.

## Conditional frontier insertion

Under the retained V3 binary construction and loss, any coverage-one controller
that emits only correct singletons or the full binary envelope has mean harm
`H=0.25q`, where `q` is the full-envelope rate.  Noninferiority to the strictest
V3 AML regime therefore requires at least 89.919772% correct singleton output.
The sole frozen zero-failure positive cell selects only 0.306155%, yielding
conditional projected harm 0.24923461.  A sparse high-precision positive rule
therefore cannot resolve the V3 coverage-risk frontier.

## Limitation insertion

The theorem establishes non-identification under absent exhaustivity authority;
it does not establish that every ontology benchmark is incomplete.  The
seven-family audit was metadata-only and made no performance observation.
Coverage, harm, noninferiority, superiority, protected transport, coordinate
value, and population generalization remain untested.  V3 numbers remain valid
only under their explicitly retained binary construction.

## Broader positioning insertion

The result extends beyond ontology matching to discovery-style benchmarks in
which annotations document found positives but do not certify the negative
complement.  In such settings, evaluation should expose an authority-aware
identification envelope rather than silently convert missing labels into point
truth.  More models or more unlabelled candidates cannot repair this semantic
defect; only explicit negative evidence or a defensible closure authority can.
