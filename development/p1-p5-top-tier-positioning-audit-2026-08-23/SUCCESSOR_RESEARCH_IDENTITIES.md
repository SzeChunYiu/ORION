harm, null interaction, lack of eligibility, broken binding, or unavailable

## Admission order

1. Complete the shared novelty/dependency audit and safe metadata writeback.
2. Bind comparator identities and lawful terminal/action interfaces.
3. Bind owner-separated custody and content-class rights.
4. Run outcome-blind eligibility/coordinate-opportunity censuses.
5. Execute the local causal discriminators that determine whether wide campaigns
   are scientifically eligible.
6. Execute protected primary and source-disjoint replication waves.

Skipping an earlier stage does not create a negative result at a later stage; it
creates the later stage's declared `CANNOT_CHECK` terminal.
