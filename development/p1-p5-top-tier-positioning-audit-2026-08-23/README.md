**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**Scope:** current ORION Papers P1--P5, their theory/claim ledgers, the
comparator and source-census gaps, and the active-identification development

**Authority:** internal scientific/editorial audit. This is not external or

## Executive verdict

The current manuscripts are substantially more defensible than architecture-led
versions: their displayed claims are assumption-scoped, information-equivalent
donors are required to tie, and adverse or `CANNOT_CHECK` results remain visible.
That is necessary but not sufficient for a top-tier claim.

The rejection-level gap is now concentrated in three places:

1. **Empirical authority.** The widest results are authored/mechanical exact
   contracts. P1--P4 lack protected naturalistic source-disjoint validation;
   P5 has not executed its primary matched fresh-transfer campaign at all.
2. **Comparator authority.** Comparator counts are not frozen identities.
   P2 has one runnable candidate but no lawful task-authority adapter; P4 has no
   verified native three-terminal external comparator; P5 has no official MDA
   solver and its six-arm family is not named.
3. **Individual-paper novelty.** Fibre factorization, Bayes impurity, total-
   variation testing bounds, partial identification, set cover, and finite
   Bellman policy trees are shared donor mathematics. Repeating them as headline
   contributions in five papers creates a salami-slicing objection unless each
   manuscript clearly owns one different scientific object and one non-inherited
   result.

The safe upward move is therefore not to widen empirical wording. It is to make
the claim architecture sharper now, keep the completed provider-diverse
metadata writeback consistent without promoting it, honour the completed active-
identification review verdict that its finite core is donor-owned synthesis, and
then execute genuinely external,
source-disjoint discriminators under separated custody.

## Review criteria

Each paper was read against seven reviewer questions:

1. Is there one dominant scientific question?
2. Is the novelty residual explicit after granting the strongest nearest work?
3. Do the theorems predict the empirical contrasts actually run?
4. Are falsifiers capable of making the headline false rather than merely
   diagnosing a missing input?
5. Does the evidence have the authority implied by the title and abstract?
6. Is the paper scientifically distinct from the other four?
7. Are wider claims supported by the declared information, populations, and
   custody rather than by mathematical quantification alone?

Severity in the blocker matrix is:

- **A:** rejection-level for a broad/top-tier empirical claim;
- **B:** major scientific-positioning or construct-validity revision;
- **C:** editorial repair that should be completed before submission.
