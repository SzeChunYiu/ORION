## Development question

How should ORION treat strong nearest work as reusable scientific structure,
rather than as a perimeter that merely shrinks a novelty sentence?

The required transition is:

```text
primary donor source
-> atomic donor structure
-> exact or bounded reconstruction
-> conservative embedding
-> explicit added coordinates
-> boundary/equivalence theorem or strict-separation witness
-> strongest ideal donor product
-> fresh falsification
-> candidate envelope theory
```

Absorbing a donor never erases its citation, priority, assumptions, or failure
regime. A historical negative remains historical evidence. It may motivate a
successor boundary theorem or enlarged method, but it cannot be relabelled as a
positive run.

## Atomic questions

1. **D1 — donor identity:** Is every load-bearing donor claim tied to a primary
   source or official implementation and its exact assumptions?
2. **D2 — structure extraction:** Are the donor's state coordinates,
   transitions, invariants, authority rules, and failure conditions represented
   without ORION renaming them into apparent inventions?
3. **D3 — reconstruction:** Does an independent executable or formal proof
   reproduce the donor-native result on the donor's declared domain?
4. **D4 — conservative embedding:** Is there an explicit map from donor objects
   into the proposed envelope that preserves every registered donor-native
   judgment?
5. **D5 — extension:** Which coordinates, obligations, or admissible transitions
   exist in the envelope but not in the reconstructed donor?
6. **D6 — separation:** Is there a human-auditable proof or prospectively frozen
   witness on which the added structure changes a registered outcome?
7. **D7 — ideal product:** Does an ideal donor product supplied with the same
   added information tie the envelope? If so, the result is a composition or
   interface theorem, not inherent superiority.
8. **D8 — falsification:** Do donor-assumption deletion, label permutation,
   seeded-answer, faulty-solver, and mutation controls reject invalid envelopes?
9. **D9 — authority:** Are reconstruction, embedding, separation, and external
   novelty authority kept distinct?

## Incumbent mechanics and negative history

ORION already contains useful but incomplete pieces:

- `knowledge.nearest_work` records comparisons, absorption dispositions,
  candidate deltas, and falsifiers;
- `knowledge.mechanism_assimilation` checks donor grounding, assumptions,
  rebranding, fake composition, and authority escalation;
- `knowledge.scientific_structure_assimilation` protects upstream scientific
  structure;
- the development protocol requires saturation before implementation;
- several paper programmes already preserve parent-subsumption and ideal-product
  ties as negative results.

The missing core object is an executable **envelope gate**. Today a
`CANDIDATE_DELTA` can be reported without proving donor reconstruction,
conservative embedding, a strict witness, or comparison with the strongest
information-matched donor product. That gap permits nearest-work search to act
as claim subtraction instead of theory construction.

## Knowledge and search-universe saturation

Bounded inputs used to freeze this packet:

- repository-native nearest-work, mechanism-assimilation, structural-
  assimilation, novelty-certificate, superiority, and donor-gate code/tests;
- Paper 1 donor-engulfment and protected-minimal-escalation artifacts;
- Paper 6 conservative donor-embedding and ideal-product equivalence artifacts;
- Q1 support-reduction, support-one sharpness, and weighted-objective boundary
  artifacts;
- SAGE, *One Reflection Is Not Enough: Self-Correcting Autonomous Research via
  Multi-Hypothesis Failure Attribution*, arXiv:2606.31478, as the immediate P1
  donor case.

This basis is sufficient to freeze the gate's information contract. It is not a
certificate that every relevant donor or theorem has been found.

## Challenge to the saturation basis

The search may look falsely flat if:

- a donor's executable semantics differ from its prose taxonomy;
- a composition is already standard under another field's vocabulary;
- the proposed extra coordinate is derivable from donor-visible state;
- the separation witness was authored after seeing the candidate;
- an ideal donor product closes the apparent gap;
- the embedding preserves output labels but not trajectories, costs, authority,
  or failure behavior;
- a nonlinear constraint invalidates a linear support/generalization argument;
- a harness manifest contains the answer it later appears to discover.

Every envelope case must therefore register search reopen routes, an ideal donor
product, and hostile mutants before it can report a strict candidate extension.

## Why earlier searches could miss the useful structure

1. The novelty workflow asks whether a difference survives, not whether the
   donor is reproduced as a special case.
2. Mechanism receipts are atomic; they do not require a global conservative
   embedding theorem.
3. Comparator studies can use deliberately weak donors rather than the strongest
   information-matched product.
4. Negative studies are stored by programme ownership, so their cross-paper
   boundary/equivalence content is not automatically promoted into general
   theory.
5. Code-level labels can conceal that two methods are extensionally identical.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

Add a provider-neutral `orion.knowledge.donor_envelope` module. Its immutable
case object will require:

- admitted mechanism/structure receipt identities;
- donor-claim and donor-assumption identities;
- reconstruction proof/test identities;
- an explicit embedding-map identity and preservation obligations;
- added-coordinate identities;
- separation proof/witness identities;
- strongest ideal-donor-product comparator identities;
- registered falsifiers and fresh-evaluation identities;
- unresolved route identities.

The assessor will fail closed in this order:

1. missing admitted absorption;
2. missing donor reconstruction;
3. missing conservative embedding;
4. no added coordinate — report exact/equivalent reconstruction, not failure;
5. missing strict-separation evidence — report conservative envelope only;
6. missing ideal donor product;
7. missing falsifier;
8. missing fresh evaluation;
9. otherwise report `CANDIDATE_STRICT_ENVELOPE`.

No verdict grants publication novelty, scientific truth, or permission to erase
negative history.

## Frozen RED tests

- no admitted donor receipt blocks;
- a claimed envelope without donor reconstruction blocks;
- output-only mapping without preservation obligations blocks;
- exact reconstruction with no added coordinate is recorded positively as an
  absorbed special case, never as ORION novelty;
- added coordinates without a strict witness remain a conservative envelope;
- strict witness without an ideal donor product blocks superiority language;
- ideal-product equality is recorded as an equivalence/composition boundary;
- missing falsifier or fresh evaluation blocks strict-envelope promotion;
- unresolved literature/formulation routes block strict-envelope promotion;
- all results expose `publication_novelty_authorized == False`;
- duplicate/blank identities and an overlap between donor and added coordinates
  are rejected;
- a historical-negative identity must be preserved when a successor is derived
  from it.

## First applications

### P1 / SAGE

Reconstruct SAGE's failure levels and deterministic
category/severity-to-intervention router as donor-native structure. Test whether
protected lower-level alternative exclusion, same-task counterfactual recovery,
sibling/invariant preservation, and dependency-impact reopening form a
conservative strict extension. A SAGE-faithful comparator and an ideal product
receiving the same information are mandatory.

### Q1

Treat support-one failure as a sharpness witness and the outside-weight-cone
support-three case as a phase-boundary witness. The broader `d`-Tag theorem is
not admitted: Restore rank/sign constraints require additional syndrome rank
`r`, an exact deletion penalty `L`, and cost-free reconstruction. Until those
axioms survive exhaustive counterexample search, the general statement remains
a conjecture.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen research if:

- a donor reconstruction fails;
- a preservation obligation is violated;
- an ideal donor product closes the strict witness;
- a new donor subsumes an added coordinate or separation regime;
- a hostile mutant passes;
- an embedding depends on hidden evaluator information;
- fresh evaluation is negative or `CANNOT_CHECK`;
- the harness cannot prove answer-free, revision-bound execution.

## Claim boundary

This packet freezes a governance and representation mechanic. It does not prove
that P1 envelops SAGE, that Q1 generalizes beyond its declared grammar, that the
research harness caused any discovery, or that any paper is top-tier ready.
