## Anti-tautology challenge

The experiment is invalid if:
- gold says “high-level mutation is necessary” because ORION's policy says so rather than because the hidden response matrix shows all admissible lower-level repairs fail;
- ORION alone sees the response matrix, cause id, family id or protected sibling labels;
- the primary parent gets fewer intervention calls or fewer repair candidates;
- protected sibling tests are counted only for ORION;
- the K/W/M hierarchy is encoded in opaque ids or case order;
- the oracle ceiling is runnable as a candidate;
- control tasks are under-sampled relative to the registered H2 precision requirement;
- a mutation that restores the motivating case but breaks a protected sibling is counted as root success;
- a post-outcome change alters candidate ordering, intervention budget, margins or exclusions.

## Why previous work missed this

V1 mainly asked whether a system reached a reframe at all. The current R3 authority-value design conditions on a supplied diagnosis, so it cannot repair the upstream 47/48 abstention bottleneck and, after donor absorption, risks measuring policy conformance. The new successor makes *counterfactual intervention selection and mutation necessity* the operative mechanism, then measures actual protected task recovery.

## Frozen mechanism hypothesis

A high-level FORMULATION/SEARCH_UNIVERSE mutation is admissible only when a bounded certificate establishes all four:

1. **lower-level exclusion** — every registered evidence/execution repair that could explain the observed symptom fails to restore the motivating target, or is mechanically unavailable;
2. **target effect** — the proposed high-level mutation restores the motivating target under a frozen intervention;
3. **protected preservation** — independent sibling/invariant checks remain valid after the mutation;
4. **impact binding** — the closures invalidated/reopened by the mutation match the frozen dependency response.

Evidence/execution controls should resolve at the lower level and never satisfy the high-level necessity certificate.
