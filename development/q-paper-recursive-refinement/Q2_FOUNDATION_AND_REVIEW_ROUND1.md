## 1. Foundation spine

### Question

What research-control discipline should an auditable AI-assisted scientific programme apply after a strong candidate is negative, donor-absorbed, saturated or temporarily uncheckable, so that failure remains informative rather than being erased by post-outcome claim mutation or endless retry?

### Answer

The ORION-Q case study operationalizes negative-result recovery as a sequence of typed terminals plus prospectively frozen successor questions. In the closed programme, this discipline preserves donor absorptions and refutations as first-class evidence and supports a recovery arc that ends in a sharper all-`n` theorem rather than retroactively relabeling earlier failures as successes.

### Decisive evidence chain

1. Frozen protocols preserve positive, negative, absorbed, mixed, saturated, lower-bound and cannot-check terminals.
2. Multiple apparently positive mechanisms are later donor-absorbed rather than retained as novelty claims.
3. The native R6 gate remains negative and its protected discriminator remains sealed.
4. Explanatory recovery proceeds through R6N refutation, R6O second refutation, R6P finite closure, R6R prospective prediction and R6S theorem.
5. The final programme closure packet and 47-result receipt index make that research DAG replayable/auditable.

### Boundary

This is one scientific programme. It does not establish causal superiority over ordinary researchers, generic agent loops, or donor-only stopping; it does not establish a cross-domain productivity gain; provenance itself is not the novelty.

### Meaning

Autonomous-science systems need an explicit theory of what to do with negative evidence. A workflow that preserves failure state and freezes successors can be evaluated for false novelty, calibrated stopping and productive recovery rather than simply counting positive outputs.

## 4. Editorial triage — Nature Computational Science

**Posture:** `TECHNICAL_CASE_NOT_REVIEW_READY` for a general-method Article.

### Why

Nature Computational Science's editorial framing asks whether a method solves an important problem, advances the state of the art, has practical/scientific value and is compellingly validated, with real data/relevant benchmarks emphasized for method claims.

Q2 has an important problem and unusually deep trace, but the present design has no prospective matched comparison showing that RECOVERY changes outcomes relative to NAIVE_ITERATION, DONOR_STOP or an ordinary agentic loop across independent programmes.

### Stretch-target blocker

`ADD_DECISIVE_EVIDENCE`: execute the frozen cross-domain recovery protocol. This cannot be closed by manuscript polish.
