# Paper 2 V12--V15 integration: hostile selective-journal preflight

Date: 2026-08-24

## Review setup

- **Input scope.** The current Paper 2 LaTeX manuscript, its V2 claim ledger,
  and the V12--V15 source-state packets were reviewed after manuscript
  integration.
- **Assessment boundary.** This is a pre-submission simulation. It is not peer
  review, an editorial decision, or a prediction of acceptance.
- **Exact target.** Unresolved. The fallback model is a generic selective
  top-tier research or framework journal.
- **Publication model assumed.** Technical validity is necessary but not
  sufficient. The manuscript must also establish a clear, substantial field
  advance, a decisive evidence chain, readable evaluation, and reproducibility.
  No journal-specific word, display, data, or article-type rule is asserted.
- **Article type.** Unresolved between theory/framework research and an
  empirical systems article. This unresolved identity materially affects the
  decision standard.
- **Shared claim summary.** The manuscript proposes an
  acquisition--authority envelope, gives several conditional theorems about
  acquisition ceilings, closure-state factorization, indistinguishable worlds,
  robust completion, and donor saturation, and locates heterogeneous empirical
  results within that theory. It explicitly does not claim deployed open-world
  superiority.
- **V12--V15 evidence.** V12 is a failed exact-identity gate followed by
  diagnosis. V13 is a positive two-record non-simulation witness with a 4/7
  support ceiling. V14 is a frozen commit--path identity mismatch. V15 is a
  coherent signed provider-snapshot witness exposing 61 dataset blobs, with no
  index parse, census, model, ranking, metric, or independent custody. V15B is a
  successor-only correction of an unexecuted route template.
- **Missing materials affecting confidence.** An exact target and article type,
  an independent full-text novelty assessment of the theorem package, an
  externally held matched outcome study, closed per-review rights, and a
  submission-ready author/disclosure package are absent.
- **Isolation caveat.** The three reports below were produced sequentially in
  one model context. Technical mutual blindness cannot be guaranteed, so any
  overlap must not be treated as independent reviewer consensus. Each report
  was frozen before the synthesis was written.

## Editorial triage simulation

**Triage posture:** `technical_case_not_review_ready` under the assumed generic
selective model.

The question is important and recoverable. The manuscript also demonstrates
unusually strong adverse-result and provenance discipline. The central paper
identity is nevertheless unresolved. As a theory/framework article, the draft
must show why the formal results are a nontrivial advance over their nearest
decision-theoretic, information-state, stopping, and retrieval analogues. As an
empirical systems article, it lacks a decisive external test of the envelope or
of a system-level advantage. The current manuscript tries to carry both cases,
producing a 21,000-word main-text source with a very long abstract and a large
chronology of V1--V15 development results.

The V12--V15 integration is correctly bounded and does not create a new
performance claim. It therefore improves integrity but cannot close the
selective-journal maturity gap. Before external review, the paper needs one
dominant article identity, a shorter claim-led evidence chain, and either a
stronger theorem-level novelty case or the independently held cross-arena study
already identified by the manuscript.

## Reviewer 1

**Emphasis:** validity, methods, data, and inference.

### Overall assessment

The manuscript is careful about what its empirical records do not establish.
Its strongest validity feature is the refusal to convert source availability,
diagnosis, or same-workspace execution into performance or custody. The central
weakness is not a hidden false positive. It is that the heterogeneous evidence
does not decisively test the paper's broad system-level object.

### Central claim and evidence readout

The formal claims appear conditional and auditable from the supplied source.
The empirical campaigns validate mechanisms, diagnose ceilings, or retain
adverse results. None tests acquisition-envelope superiority across naturalistic
external worlds under matched access, budget, comparator, and authority
contracts.

### Major strengths

- Adverse comparator results and failed gates remain visible.
- V12--V15 preserve chronology, source identity, rights, and custody boundaries.
- The manuscript separates route acquisition, within-pool screening, route
  stopping, and task closure instead of averaging them into one score.

### Major Concerns

#### R1-M1 [central_claim_evidence]

**Severity:** Major
**Blocking:** Yes
**Target criterion:** Decisive evidence for the central advance
**Claim pointer:** The acquisition--authority envelope is presented as the
appropriate comparison object for open-world scientific discovery systems.
**Evidence pointer:** Abstract; Introduction; Results; Conclusion; registered
768-cluster four-arena target.
**Concern:** The completed experiments do not compare complete controllers on
the proposed envelope across matched naturalistic worlds. The strongest
external records are adverse, provider-invalid, bounded, or within-pool. The
only direct cross-arena superiority test remains prospective.
**Alternative interpretation:** The paper is a conceptual and mathematical
framework illustrated by case studies, rather than an empirically validated
systems advance.
**Why it matters:** A selective empirical article cannot ground its main
system-level claim in mechanism demonstrations and unresolved future tests.
**Resolution test:** Choose one closure. Either reposition the manuscript as a
theory/framework article and remove any implication of demonstrated system
benefit, or execute a source-disjoint, capability-matched, independently held
cross-arena study under the manuscript's own non-compensatory rule.

#### R1-M2 [operational_identifiability]

**Severity:** Major
**Blocking:** Yes
**Target criterion:** Validity and falsifiability of the proposed framework
**Claim pointer:** The donor-complete class and compatible-world safe set make
comparison donor-monotone and closure maximally permissive.
**Evidence pointer:** Acquisition--authority envelope definitions;
donor-monotonicity; maximal-completion and donor-saturation theorems;
Limitations.
**Concern:** The most consequential sets are defined relative to declared donor
and compatible-world classes whose empirical completeness cannot be verified in
the present study. The manuscript acknowledges this, but it does not yet give a
practical identification or sensitivity analysis showing how conclusions change
under plausible donor or world misspecification.
**Alternative interpretation:** The formalism can classify authority after the
scientifically difficult completeness assumptions have already been supplied,
without resolving how those assumptions are justified.
**Why it matters:** The framework risks becoming formally sound but weakly
discriminating in the open-world setting it is intended to govern.
**Resolution test:** Provide a nontrivial robustness or partial-identification
result under donor/world misspecification, or narrow the contribution to a
contract-relative authority calculus and make empirical completeness explicitly
outside scope.

#### R1-M3 [v15_source_to_population]

**Severity:** Major
**Blocking:** No
**Target criterion:** Reproducible inference
**Claim pointer:** V15 exposes a coherent source frontier with 61 dataset blobs.
**Evidence pointer:** V15 result, implementation correction, custody request,
Results integration, and Availability.
**Concern:** Snapshot coherence does not establish that the index parses, that
the 61 blobs define eligible source-disjoint reviews, that per-review reuse is
lawful, or that any matched outcome can be measured. The 404 attestation, 0/3
custody state, and unexecuted route correction keep the population transition
open.
**Alternative interpretation:** V15 is a source-state provenance result only.
**Why it matters:** Readers may otherwise mistake a repaired repository binding
for a runnable empirical study.
**Resolution test:** Retain the present claim ceiling. For empirical use, obtain
the external custody signature, exact per-review rights, one immutable-V14-route
label-blind census, and a new prospective performance freeze before outcomes.

## Reviewer 2

**Emphasis:** contribution, prior work, significance, and selective-journal
positioning.

### Overall assessment

The paper has a potentially useful organizing idea and an unusually honest
evidence hierarchy. The current draft does not yet prove that its mathematical
package or empirical program constitutes a selective-journal field advance.
Listing strong neighbouring systems and absorbing them into a donor class is not
the same as establishing theorem-level novelty over their underlying ideas.

### Central claim and evidence readout

The claimed advance is the comparison object and its authority calculus, not a
new retriever. The paper's theorems formalize intuitive constraints on missing
acquisition support, state sufficiency, indistinguishable worlds, safe closure,
and donor saturation. The draft needs a sharper subtraction against prior
formal results and a clearer statement of what becomes possible only because of
this paper.

### Major strengths

- The paper positions strong neighbours as donors rather than weak baselines.
- It does not hide that an information-equivalent ideal product ties.
- The donor-saturation boundary turns repeated empirical failure into a useful
  research discriminator without claiming that failure proves universal donor
  optimality.

### Major Concerns

#### R2-M1 [theorem_level_novelty]

**Severity:** Major
**Blocking:** Yes
**Target criterion:** Originality and substantial advancement
**Claim pointer:** Four analytic results locate the remaining open-world
discovery problem.
**Evidence pointer:** Abstract; Introduction contribution list; Related work;
theorem statements and short proofs.
**Concern:** Several headline results are close in form to general set-inclusion,
factorization-on-fibres, binary-testing, and maximal-safe-set constructions.
The Related work section names application neighbours but does not conduct
theorem-by-theorem subtraction against the nearest formal literature.
**Alternative interpretation:** The novelty lies primarily in synthesis and
application-specific interpretation, not in the mathematical results
themselves.
**Why it matters:** A selective theory/framework paper needs a precise residual
advance after standard mathematical structure is removed.
**Resolution test:** Complete a verified full-text formal prior-art matrix for
each theorem. State the strongest donor theorem, the exact nontrivial residual,
and one result, counterexample, or operational consequence not obtained by a
direct relabeling of standard structure. If the residual is synthesis, claim it
as such and select an article type that values frameworks.

#### R2-M2 [breadth_without_decisive_consequence]

**Severity:** Major
**Blocking:** Yes
**Target criterion:** Selective-journal significance and readership
**Claim pointer:** The envelope is offered as a general theory of open-world
scientific discovery.
**Evidence pointer:** Title; Abstract; Introduction; Conclusion.
**Concern:** The manuscript ranges over retrieval, screening, memory, stopping,
and authority, but the broad consequence remains prospective. The current case
does not show that adopting the envelope changes a deployed scientific result,
prevents a real false closure, or yields a verified cross-domain advantage.
**Alternative interpretation:** The framework is important for a specialist
community interested in evaluation governance, but broad readership has not
been demonstrated.
**Why it matters:** Breadth of vocabulary does not itself establish broad
scientific importance.
**Resolution test:** Supply one decisive naturalistic case where the framework
changes an authority-bearing conclusion under matched alternatives, or target a
specialist theory/methods venue and calibrate the significance claim accordingly.

#### R2-M3 [article_identity]

**Severity:** Major
**Blocking:** No
**Target criterion:** Article-type fit and evaluability
**Claim pointer:** The paper combines formal theorems, synthetic mechanism
experiments, public screening campaigns, external retrieval probes, and a long
versioned failure chronology.
**Evidence pointer:** Full manuscript structure.
**Concern:** No single evidence class dominates. This makes it difficult for an
editor to decide whether the paper should be judged as theory, methods,
algorithmic systems, or research.
**Alternative interpretation:** The material may support two papers, one on the
contract-relative theory and one on the empirical screening/acquisition program.
**Why it matters:** Mixed article identity weakens both novelty evaluation and
reader navigation.
**Resolution test:** Write a one-sentence article contract, retain only evidence
needed for that contract in the main text, and relocate the remaining campaign
chronology to a linked supplement or evidence report.

## Editor synthesis (post-review; simulated)

### What external review changed relative to triage

The reports sharpen the triage concern into three separable risks. The first is
missing decisive evidence for a system-level claim. The second is missing
theorem-level novelty subtraction for a theory claim. The third is the absence
of a bounded submission object and independent reproduction of its decisive
evidence.

### Consensus strengths

Because reviewer isolation was not guaranteed, overlap is descriptive only.
All three reports nevertheless identify the same visible strengths: adverse
results are preserved, V12--V15 respect their authority boundaries, and source
integrity is not mistaken for performance.

### Publication-criteria blockers

- The exact journal and article type are unresolved.
- The current draft does not yet establish a selective-journal significance
  case under either a theory or empirical identity.
- The theorem package lacks theorem-by-theorem full-text novelty subtraction.

### Technical blockers

- No completed matched external study tests the full acquisition--authority
  claim.
- The practical consequences of donor/world misspecification are not resolved.
- The decisive empirical case is not independently reproducible under separated
  custody.

### Major repairable concerns

- Rebuild the paper around one article identity.
- Add one evidence-authority display and remove repeated campaign chronology.
- Complete target-specific formatting, metadata, clean build, and visual review.

### Claim-recalibration opportunities

- A defensible current paper is a contract-relative theory/framework with
  bounded empirical illustrations.
- V13 supports non-simulation of one metadata coordinate, not benefit.
- V15 supports coherent source-state availability, not a runnable population or
  screening performance.

### Clarity and reporting issues

- The abstract is too long and carries too much campaign detail.
- Metric terminology and internal terminal language should be normalized.
- Exact hashes should remain accessible but move out of the central narrative.

### Optional enrichment requests

- Additional same-pool residual families are optional and would not close the
  central decision risks.
- Cosmetic prose widening without new evidence is not useful.

### Where reviewer emphasis differs

Reviewer 1 places the largest weight on empirical identification and
misspecification. Reviewer 2 places it on formal novelty and significance.
Reviewer 3 treats the submission object and independent reproducibility as the
most immediate blockers.

### Simulated decision posture

`central_case_requires_new_decisive_evidence` for an empirical systems article.
For a theory/framework article, the posture is
`promising_major_revision_case` after novelty subtraction, operational
consequences, and major compression. These are simulated postures, not journal
decisions.

### Do before submission

1. Fix the exact journal, article type, audience, and article contract.
2. Choose the decisive evidence path for that contract.
3. Complete formal prior-art subtraction or the independent cross-arena study.
4. Compress the main text and isolate provenance chronology in an archival layer.
5. Run a clean build, page inspection, reference audit, and exact-target checklist.

### Do not waste effort on

- Running repository pytest or CI as a substitute for scientific evidence.
- Retuning another residual on opened development pools to force a positive
  result.
- Rebranding V13 metadata separation or V15 snapshot coherence as performance.
- Widening the language beyond the evidence or deleting adverse results.
- Adding optional experiments that do not discriminate the central theory or
  system claim.

### Stop or transfer conditions

- If independent external custody and matched outcomes cannot be obtained, stop
  presenting the work as an empirically validated system-level advance. Submit a
  contract-relative theory/framework paper after novelty subtraction and
  compression.
- If full-text subtraction shows that the headline theorems are standard under
  new names, transfer the contribution to a methods, perspective, or governance
  article and claim synthesis rather than mathematical novelty.
- A scientifically adverse cross-arena result should remain in the record. It
  can refine the theory or identify the next discriminator, but it cannot be
  edited into superiority.
