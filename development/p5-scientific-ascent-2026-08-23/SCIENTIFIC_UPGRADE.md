# P5 scientific ascent: minimal method revision under observational equivalence

## Upward move

P5 no longer stops at a governance architecture for a named controller. Its
central object is the **minimal authorized revision front** induced by a latent
failure situation, a partial order of scientific changes, and hard
causal/preservation/transfer/authority constraints. A reviser observes only an
evidence interface. This reframes failure-driven self-improvement as a general
decision problem under observational equivalence.

## Analytic results added

1. **Revision-factorization theorem.** Exact revision selection through an
   interface is possible iff the required revision front is constant on every
   interface fibre.
2. **Arbitrary-loss mixed-fibre bound.** The best possible revision policy has
   the conditional Bayes risk inside each fibre. Zero-one loss reduces to
   decision-fibre impurity.
3. **Protected-authority impossibility.** When two internally identical
   transcripts require opposite protected promotion decisions, no internal
   rule is both sound and complete.
4. **Discriminator-cover theorem.** On a finite ambiguous fibre, a fixed test
   panel permits exact selection iff it distinguishes every pair requiring a
   different revision. The least-cost fixed panel is the induced weighted
   set-cover optimum. Adaptive testing is exact iff every terminal leaf is
   revision-decision-pure.

The factorization and Bayes identities are standard decision-theoretic facts
specialized to typed revision fronts; they are not advertised as new general
statistics. The distinctive composition is the revision target, the
pair-cover intervention criterion, and its protected-authority consequence.

  decisions it supports.
- Reiter and de Kleer/Williams model-based diagnosis: competing hypotheses,
  conflicts and discriminators.
- Chernoff sequential experimental design and Golovin/Krause adaptive
  submodularity: information acquisition and adaptive test selection.

  Ratchet, Verifier-as-Gatekeeper, PACE/SEA, PAST-Bench and SEVA: proposer,

These mechanisms become donor components. They improve P5 only when their
outputs split a previously mixed **required-revision** fibre. None is displaced
by component renaming, and no component becomes its own promotion authority.

## Publication boundary

The theory is general across finite decision alphabets and arbitrary
well-founded revision partial orders. The cover theorem is finite and
deterministic; stochastic identification, finite-sample power, latent-family
misspecification and naturalistic transfer remain open. The existing local
hostile tests establish implementation semantics only. Transferable governed
method improvement remains `CANNOT_CHECK` until the protected campaign runs.
