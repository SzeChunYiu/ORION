## Results paragraph

In a new before-outcome same-session transport study, we selected the five largest review decisions in the CC0 SYNERGY V1 release using publisher-documented record counts without using labels or model outcomes. A label-blind adapter removed every normalized content identity present in the SWIFT development panel and every identity shared between selected successor reviews. The resulting five review-level units contained 101,044 canonical screening records (40,442 Walker; 38,009 Brouwer; 8,783 Hall; 6,621 Wassenaar; 7,189 Leenaars), with zero final SWIFT overlap and zero final pairwise successor overlap. The learner/balancer main effect at recall@10% was +0.008834 (strictly positive in three of five decisions and exactly zero in two), failing the frozen +0.010859 magnitude and four-of-five strictly-positive sign gates. Its unweighted mean effects were +0.031876 at recall@5%, +0.019245 at recall@20%, and +0.030373 at WSS@95; these were frozen secondary diagnostics and do not replace the failed recall@10% primary gates. The preserved candidate-minus-u4 full-arm means were -0.011842 at recall@10% and -0.041524 at WSS@95, so the original relative margin and work-saving gates again failed, while the harm and absolute-work-saving gates passed.

## Interpretation paragraph

The content-disjoint result does not confirm the SWIFT-derived learner/balancer attribution under its frozen primary rule. It does, however, localize the remaining scientific question: the learner/balancer contrast was never negative at recall@10%, and its earlier, later, and 95%-recall work-saving diagnostics were positive on average, suggesting that a future outcome-unopened study should preregister a rank-continuous recall-effort curve while retaining the adverse recall@10% result as a locked coprimary endpoint. No V5 arm replaces the candidate or supports controller, ASReview-software, ORION, or domain-general superiority.

## Limitations paragraph

The five review decisions, not their 101,044 records, are the review-level units. Although exact normalized-content overlap was removed, all five decisions share the SYNERGY V1 release, OpenAlex representation, and local adapter; content disjointness alone does not establish stochastic independence. SYNERGY labels encode final full-text inclusion, so screening-stage transport is not established. Four discarded within-review duplicate-content rows had conflicting source labels, but the preregistered label-blind rule retained the lexicographically smallest OpenAlex identifier and was not altered after outcomes. Source selection, population, protocol, and implementation were cryptographically frozen before V5 label values and model outcomes, but download and execution occurred in the same local lane without independent custody or external execution.

## Conclusion sentence

The strongest warranted conclusion is a preregistered adverse transport result with positive tail diagnostics: the V4 learner/balancer mechanism did not satisfy the content-disjoint recall@10% magnitude or sign gates, and the next discriminator requires a new source family, a prospectively frozen continuous recall-effort estimand, and preferably independent custody.
