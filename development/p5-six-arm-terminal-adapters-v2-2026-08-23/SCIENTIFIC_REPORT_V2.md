## Main result

For an adapter-visible map g and true minimal revision map r, an exact
deterministic adapter exists exactly when r is constant on every fibre of g.
Six prospectively frozen same-visible-symptom counterexamples show that the raw
interfaces fail this criterion:

- a successful SWE-agent patch can be execution or evaluator repair;
- improved MOSS replay can be within-class model or representation repair;
- a DGM archive-best child can be model expansion or execution repair;
- a compiling ADIAS issue patch can be within-class model or execution repair;
- Double Ratchet DRAWBACK can arise from evidence or evaluator failure; and
- a ScienceClaw open need can require evidence or measurement repair.

Thus zero raw native symptoms license a singleton P5 class. Unsupported, mixed,
ambiguous, empty, invalid, error, timeout and abstention fibres must remain
UNRESOLVED while retaining the exact native terminal.

## Synthetic conformance

The deterministic adapter passed 90/90 prospectively frozen synthetic cases:

- 70 correctly remained UNRESOLVED;
- 20 certificate/write-surface cases emitted their supported action type;
- six same-visible-symptom mixed fibres were proved; and
- every ERROR, TIMEOUT, ABSTAIN, EMPTY, PARTIAL and INVALID terminal was
  retained as UNRESOLVED.

This is bounded conformance, not science performance.

## Resource and execution boundary

Exact native metadata improves the preflight, but zero of six arms is
confirmatory-ready. No arm has a complete content-addressed matched bundle of
candidate-visible inputs, model/provider/service, environment/container,
token/tool/time/USD/compute/retry/network envelope, isolated writes, lawful
content and service rights, adapter, and independent protected scorer.

ADIAS retains restricted CC BY-NC-SA source rights. Double Ratchet's official
flow repeatedly observes eval_locked and needs a development surrogate plus a
once-only external protected score. ScienceClaw has no native eight-class
selector; adding one is a material successor method, not an adapter relabel.
