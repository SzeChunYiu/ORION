## Development question

Can ORION distinguish and jointly model:

1. a solution assembled from donor fragments (`a1 × b2 × c3`);
2. a solution transferred from a structurally analogous but semantically distant field;
3. a partial donor/transfer solution completed by a genuinely generated residual;

without confusing any of them with fixed-regime search, macro formation, donor absorption, hidden-answer access, or self-issued novelty?

## Atomic development questions

### A1 — provenance partition

Can every finite candidate semantic node and edge be classified relative to frozen local closure, donor maps, and proposal origin?

### A2 — donor product

Can the harness retain all inclusion-minimal donor compositions and exact interaction order rather than only the first successful product?

### A3 — bridge residual

Can it recognize a new edge/topology when all component nodes are donor-owned?

### A4 — transfer debt

Can it distinguish exact relational/axiomatic transport from surface analogy with missing premises or validation correspondence?

### A5 — residual completion

Can it represent several incomparable minimal residual decompositions rather than selecting one rhetorically favorable residual?

### A6 — structural navigation

Can it retain a Pareto frontier over reach, distortion, resources, and authority debt without an arbitrary scalar score?

### A7 — theorem-identifying experiments

Can it compute the exact minimum experiment panel required to distinguish a finite alternative family?

### A8 — historical morphology

Can the existing ten-coordinate artifact morphology be preserved while adding candidate source, trigger, edited layer, validation, and scope?

## Current saturation assessment

The nearest mechanism families are absorbed:

- schema instantiation, modular subassembly, and chaining;
- structure-mapping and axiomatic analogy;
- conceptual integration/blending;
- C-K concept/knowledge expansion;
- Boden/Wiggins transformational creativity;

The residual is not a new generic creativity operator. It is the typed coupling of:

```text
donor product closure
+ structural transfer debt
+ explicit semantic residual family

+ theorem-identifying experiments
+ scientific validity/novelty/adoption separation
```

## Basis challenge

Saturation could be false if:

1. a discovery candidate has a load-bearing provenance class outside local/donor/generated/unresolved;
2. an important cross-domain transfer cannot be represented by either relational mapping or theory interpretation;
3. a candidate’s novelty residual cannot be represented over nodes and edges of a semantic graph;
4. human-history episodes require a source mode beyond search/composition/transfer/completion/hybrid;
5. domain-specific validation cannot be represented without adding a new regime layer;
6. a new prior formalism already owns the exact integrated calculus.

Potentially missing parent domains:

```text
formal concept formation
mathematical analogy and theory interpretation
mechanism discovery
computational creativity
design theory
abduction and model-based reasoning
scientific instrumentation
knowledge graph/hypergraph synthesis
proof planning and rippling
categorical program synthesis
```

Prior-miss hypotheses:

- terminology may be distributed across philosophy of science, design, theorem proving, and systems assurance;
- mathematical analogy may be described axiomatically rather than as structure mapping;
- bridge novelty may appear in architecture/composition literature rather than creativity literature;
- residual completion may be hidden under theory repair, model revision, concept formation, or invention.

Reopen triggers:

- a counterexample to the provenance partition under frozen equivalence;
- a new morphology coordinate in a source-grounded episode;
- a donor that absorbs the integrated residual;
- a structural transfer requiring a new debt coordinate;
- an executor finding that the reference classification is tautological or non-identifying;
- a frontier campaign where the three modes do not predict materially different actions.
