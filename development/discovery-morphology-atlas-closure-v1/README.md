# Discovery morphology atlas closure V1 — development packet

Date: 2026-08-19

Issue: #506.  
Schema dependency: #514 (`DiscoveryEpisode.v1`, source roles, deterministic pre-cutoff projection).  
Recursive-study dependency: #513/#507.  
Authority boundary: this packet studies whether a **bounded descriptive morphology** is useful across four validation domains. It cannot establish private historical cognition, a universal basis of creativity, Jump competence, novelty of the morphology vocabulary, or protected benchmark success.

## 1. Atomic closure question

After source-grounded annotation of at least 50 diverse discovery/invention artifacts, does a small common set of structural change families remain useful for **describing what changed in the public artifact/contract** across mathematics/formal work, empirical science, engineering/design, and everyday practical invention — while retaining explicit fixed-regime/no-Jump controls and without pretending those tags explain the private cognitive process that produced the artifact?

Candidate terminal, only if the frozen atlas and finite validator survive:

`USEFUL_MORPHOLOGY_BASIS_FOUND`

The intended result is intentionally weaker than `NEW_EPISTEMIC_MECHANIC`. A useful basis may be assembled almost entirely from mature parent fields.

## 2. Atlas frozen before basis/checker

`DISCOVERY_MORPHOLOGY_ATLAS_V1.json` was committed before this closure hypothesis and before the basis/checker.

Frozen corpus size: **51 episodes**.

Domain composition:

- `MATHEMATICS_FORMAL`: 16;
- `SCIENCE`: 15;
- `ENGINEERING_DESIGN`: 12;
- `EVERYDAY_PRACTICAL`: 8.

Five explicit `NO_JUMP_CONTROL` episodes are retained:

- AlphaTensor;
- AlphaDev;
- FunSearch;
- self-supervised theorem discovery inside a fixed axiom system;
- 51 Pegasi b detection within an established observational/modeling regime.

These controls prevent the morphology from treating every impressive new result as a regime invention.

Every episode is constrained to:

- a primary publication, patent, archival proposal, or direct reproduction of a primary historical source;
- a modest `documented_artifact_claim` about what the source actually contains;
- `private_cognitive_sequence = CANNOT_INFER`;
- morphology tags explicitly marked `STRUCTURAL_INTERPRETATION`;
- at least two competing interpretations;
- `MECHANISM_EXTRACTION_ONLY` historical use;
- `protected_confirmatory_gold = false`.

The atlas therefore does **not** encode the claimed morphology as historical fact.

## 3. Candidate 10-dimensional morphology

The basis to freeze after this packet is:

1. `REPRESENTATION_CHANGE` — materially changes the coordinates, descriptive objects, or organization used to state/derive the contract;
2. `NEW_OBJECT_OR_STATE_VARIABLE` — introduces a new load-bearing object/state variable or latent entity in the public artifact;
3. `ABSTRACTION_EQUIVALENCE_STRUCTURE` — creates an abstraction, equivalence, quotient, diagonal/coding or other structural identification that changes reachable derivations;
4. `NEW_LAW_CONSTRAINT_OR_INVARIANT` — adds a load-bearing law, constraint, invariant, conservation/symmetry relation, or comparable formal regularity;
5. `BRIDGE_CORRESPONDENCE` — explicitly relates previously separate descriptions/domains/representations so consequences can transport or compose;
6. `NEW_OPERATOR_PROCEDURE` — introduces a reusable procedure, proof/search/construction operator, algorithm, experimental procedure or manufacturing action;
7. `SYSTEM_COMPOSITION_ARCHITECTURE` — reorganizes components/interfaces into a new architecture whose composition is load-bearing for function or inference;
8. `INTERFACE_MEASUREMENT_REDESIGN` — materially changes the observation, intervention, manipulation, measurement or human-machine interface through which evidence/function is obtained;
9. `FUNCTION_AFFORDANCE_INVENTION` — establishes a new useful function/affordance under engineering or practical constraints rather than merely a truth-valued claim;
10. `FIXED_REGIME_SEARCH_CONTROL` — records important discoveries where the result is obtained inside a registered language/operator/evaluator regime and therefore is a mandatory non-Jump control.

The ten names are **descriptive coordinates**, not claims of atom independence. Multiple tags per episode are allowed and expected.

## 4. Strong parent ownership — subtract before claiming

The basis deliberately absorbs mature parents:

- representation/abstraction learning, formal equivalence and mathematical representation theory own broad parts of representation/abstraction;
- BACON/GLAUBER/AM/EURISKO own historical machine generation of theoretical terms, concepts, objects/operators and heuristics;
- C-K theory/Gero/design-prototype work own concept/knowledge co-expansion, design function and prototype semantics;
- analogy/identity-abduction/design-by-analogy own cross-domain correspondence;
- experiment design, measurement theory and #459 interface adequacy own much of observation/intervention redesign;
- algorithms/program synthesis/library learning own procedure/operator discovery;
- affordance/tool-innovation literature owns major function/repurposing families;
- P3/P7 own ORION correspondence/transport semantics;
- P4/P8 own validity/authority;
- #500/#501 own the separate question of whether a representational transition earns a protected Jump terminal.

A positive #506 terminal therefore says only that these coordinates form a useful **cross-domain annotation/research basis** after donor subtraction.

## 5. Why the search could still be falsely saturated

### History-of-science/source-custody challenge

Famous retrospective narratives often compress decades of evidence and competing explanations into one “eureka.” If tags were inferred from biographies or later textbook names, the atlas would encode hindsight. The closure must use only the public artifact/source and preserve `CANNOT_INFER` for undocumented cognition.

### Formal-morphology challenge

A ten-label vocabulary can appear complete simply because labels are broad. The validator therefore checks coverage and cross-domain reuse, but breadth alone cannot establish atomicity. A future source that requires a genuinely different public structural relation must reopen the basis.

### Engineering/design challenge

Truth-valued scientific categories underdescribe invention. Function/affordance and system/interface coordinates are retained explicitly so engineering/everyday artifacts are not forced into “new hypothesis” language. C-K/design-prototype parents remain mandatory comparisons.

### Fixed-regime-control challenge

New outputs can be major discoveries without a representational Jump. AlphaTensor, AlphaDev, FunSearch, fixed-axiom theorem discovery and exoplanet detection are retained specifically to falsify the shortcut `important discovery => regime invention`.

## 6. Reopen triggers

Reopen the basis and reset the post-basis saturation rounds if any of the following occurs:

- a primary-source episode cannot be represented by the ten coordinates without inventing an additional reusable structural relation;
- a coordinate is used only as an implementation/textual label and has no cross-episode public structural meaning;
- two coordinates prove observationally equivalent across all registered validation regimes and no protected/useful distinction remains;
- the design/everyday subset requires a validation coordinate erased by the truth-centric basis;
- an explicit no-Jump control cannot be represented without assigning a transformative tag that changes the intended scientific interpretation;
- source review reveals that a documented artifact claim is materially overstated or based only on a retrospective secondary narrative;
- a post-freeze primary-source search identifies a missing transformation family that changes the #507 ownership map or #506 morphology.

## 7. Frozen adequacy gates for the candidate terminal

The closure validator must fail closed unless all of the following hold:

- exactly 51 frozen episodes;
- four domain buckets with exact frozen counts 16/15/12/8;
- at least five explicit no-Jump controls;
- exactly ten registered basis dimensions;
- every dimension appears in at least five episodes;
- every dimension appears in at least two domain buckets;
- at least one dimension spans all four domain buckets;
- every episode uses only registered dimensions;
- every episode has a non-empty source locator and documented artifact claim;
- every episode keeps private cognition at `CANNOT_INFER`;
- every morphology label is explicitly a `STRUCTURAL_INTERPRETATION`;
- every episode has at least two competing interpretations;
- every episode remains `MECHANISM_EXTRACTION_ONLY` and is forbidden from protected confirmatory-gold use;
- every `NO_JUMP_CONTROL` includes `FIXED_REGIME_SEARCH_CONTROL`;
- two post-basis primary-source saturation rounds produce no material new morphology dimension.

The validator must emit `CANNOT_CHECK` if any load-bearing gate is missing.

## 8. Falsifiers and nonclaims

A green closure must not be interpreted as:

- proof that ten dimensions are the universal atoms of discovery;
- proof that historical scientists used these internal cognitive operations;
- a frequency estimate over human innovation;
- evidence that the atlas sampling is population-representative;
- a protected benchmark of Jump competence;
- evidence that historical famous answers may be used as candidate-visible training/gold;
- novelty of any donor-owned coordinate;
- evidence that a tagged structural change was scientifically necessary rather than merely present in the final artifact;
- evidence that ORION can generate any of the transformations.

The useful result is narrower: a **source-bounded, cross-domain, non-authorizing morphology** is stable enough to organize future invented/prospective atom studies and to tell fixed-regime discovery from candidate regime-changing structure.
