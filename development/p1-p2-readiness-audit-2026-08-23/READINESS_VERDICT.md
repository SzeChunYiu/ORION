## Severity scale

- **A — submission-blocking for a top-tier claim:** the title-level scientific
  question is not answered at the required authority.
- **B — major scientific revision:** the current result can be reviewed, but
  novelty, construct validity, or claim architecture is not yet persuasive at a
  top-tier bar.
- **C — integrity/package correction:** does not create missing science, but
  must be repaired before circulation so that evidence authority is not
  overstated.

source-disjoint wave.
