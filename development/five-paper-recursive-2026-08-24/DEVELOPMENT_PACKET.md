## 2. Recursive workflow imported from academic-paper-skills

The lane follows the evidence-first recursion:

`best evidence -> scientific argument -> manuscript content selection -> close analogue-paper study -> figure/evidence planning -> natural scholarly prose -> exact journal/reporting adaptation -> editor/reviewer preflight -> revision closure`

The workflow is repeated until no unresolved blocking concern can be closed from existing evidence or a rigorously derived additive theorem increment. No cosmetic experiment or inflated claim is introduced to manufacture venue fit.

Relevant skills are used as contracts rather than copied prose:

- `nature-paper-card`: source boundary, evidence inventory, claim-evidence matrix, conclusion boundaries;

## 4. Atomic scientific upgrades under review

### A2 — arbitrary-block MultiTag deletion theorem
For `b>=2` Restore branches, define the natural `b`-way local Restore functional: value one when all letters are the same nonidentity letter, otherwise the number of nonidentity letters. One letter can increase this functional by at most `b-1`. Therefore the A1 rank-deletion proof extends to the explicit `b`-block grammar in the objective cone `mu >= (b-1)t_R`, still yielding `support(R)<=rank(V_R)<=s+1`.

### B2 — restricted Davenport certificate invariant
For a finite abelian signature group `G` and allowed production alphabet `A`, the exact terminal length of the zero-sum deletion certificate language is the largest `A`-sequence with no nonempty zero-sum subsequence, equivalently `D_A(G)-1`. For `G=C_2^d` and a spanning alphabet containing a basis this equals `d`. This is donor zero-sum mathematics; the residual compiler result is the exact R6I production instantiation and separation from intrinsic support.

### C4 — Boolean-lattice marginal-trade minimality
If a signed multiplicity difference `delta:2^[q]->Z` has every proper upper marginal zero, Möbius inversion forces `delta(S)=(-1)^(q-|S|)c`. Hence every nonzero exact trade preserving all proper labeled marginals uses every Boolean cell and has at least `2^(q-1)|c|` positive and negative differing-column mass. The existing parity construction attains equality. This closes the concern that its exponential *differing support* is merely an inefficient construction; common padding is a separate optimization question.

### D2 — cyclic authority calculus

Authorization is characterized by finite untainted proof trees; unsupported cycles cannot self-authorize; seeded cycles may derive; fair forward chaining is order-independent; refutation is monotone; alternative derivations survive exactly when one proof tree remains untainted. Fixed-point/provenance mathematics is donor material. The paper must instantiate the calculus on both the TARE forecast falsifier and the independent Paper-C decision/value/optimizer separation.

## 5. Per-paper recursive gates

Each paper must ship:

1. `EVIDENCE_AND_ARGUMENT_MAP.md` — source inventory, headline claims, evidence, strongest alternative interpretation, limitations;
2. `ANALOGUE_AND_DONOR_MAP.md` — 3–6 close analogue/donor papers when available, with explicit adopt/adapt/reject decisions;
3. canonical manuscript draft with abstract, introduction, theorem/methods/results, discussion, limitations, reproducibility and references;
4. claim ledger separating theorem-grade, finite exact, bounded evidence, interpretive and open claims;
5. figure contract that asks what each figure must prove rather than forcing a quantitative plot;
6. target-journal card and exact pre-submission checklist;
7. three frozen reviewer reports (validity; prior-work/significance; reproducibility/boundaries) plus editor synthesis;
8. machine-checkable verifier for every new theorem increment and parent-receipt binding;
9. final readiness record listing external-only gates honestly.

## 6. Merge rule

Integration happens through a PR to `main`. No direct push to `main`, force update, bypass of failed checks, or mutation of protected/frozen receipts. The PR may merge only when:

- all new theorem verifiers and focused tests pass;
- manuscript/claim-ledger cross-checks pass;
- current-main divergence has been reconciled without force;
- no reviewer `Blocking Yes` concern remains that can be resolved internally;
- exact venue and reference requirements are either verified or explicitly listed as external submission-time gates;
- no paper claims external novelty certification, independent replication, physical quantum advantage or peer acceptance.
