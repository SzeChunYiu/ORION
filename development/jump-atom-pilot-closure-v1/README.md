## 1. Atomic development questions

### J1 — opportunity/tension detection

Is `epistemic tension` one scientifically identifiable atom, or an envelope over distinct evidence families (unification debt, invariance/symmetry opportunity, counterfactual inconsistency, cross-domain correspondence, affordance/function opportunity, derivational inefficiency) whose materiality cannot be inferred from ordinary zero-error public metrics alone?

### J2 — thought-experiment/counterfactual experience synthesis

Is `thought experiment` one load-bearing operator, or a proposal envelope over separable operations such as ordinary intervention selection, assumption relaxation, frame transformation, domain/system coupling, and formal counterexample construction, each of which can change bounded reach independently?

### J3 — concept/primitive invention

Is `concept invention` one epistemic transformation, or an outcome label over separable transformations such as latent-object introduction, contextual splitting, quotient/identification, representation lift, and bridge/correspondence construction, with classic discovery/design/representation parents already owning major subfamilies?

## 2. Bounded saturation assessment

### Round A — inherited donor map

The #506/#507 donor sweep already narrows the pilot space:

- Farmer, arXiv:2608.02505: representation-grounded cross-domain identity abduction / correspondence generation;
- Shen, Druckmann & Zou, arXiv:2605.11258: analogical reasoning explicitly expands scientific solution generation through relationally matched cross-domain analogues;
- Duraisamy, arXiv:2506.21329: scientific thinking as exploration of imaginary/counterfactual spaces, including temporary law relaxation;
- Wang & Buehler, arXiv:2606.01444: representational-regime revision with typed artifacts/operations/verifiers and provenance-preserving transition pressure;
- Ota, Osa & Harada, arXiv:2606.28747: discovery can accumulate useful theorems inside a fixed formal action/axiom regime, a mandatory `SEARCH_DISCOVERY / NO_JUMP` control;
- C-K design theory: speculative concept space and established knowledge space co-expand; concept formation is not reducible to truth-valued hypothesis selection;
- BACON/GLAUBER: theoretical terms and qualitative scientific concepts were already generated in classic machine-discovery systems;
- AM/EURISKO: mathematical concepts, domain objects/operators and heuristics were already objects of heuristic discovery/search;
- DreamCoder/Stitch/LILO-class library learning: reusable abstractions/operators can be synthesized from solution corpora;
- design-by-analogy / affordance-tool innovation / question-generation parents already occupy additional #506 subfamilies.

These parents are not proof that the pilot terminals are negative. They define what ORION may not rename as novel.

### Round B — current adversarial refresh

A fresh primary-source pass reinforces the decomposition boundary:

- Farmer's Abduction Loop is explicitly multi-stage (representation generation, motif extraction, canonicalization, cross-domain retrieval, identity-hypothesis generation, adversarial verification) and defaults to abstention rather than treating `analogy` as one magic score.
- Shen et al. operationalize analogical generation as a particular cross-domain solution-space expansion mechanism, not a generic scientific-opportunity oracle.
- Bao et al., arXiv:2606.08251, show that contemporary scientific LLM idea generation has mode-collapse/divergence limits and weak agreement between automated evaluators and scientist judgment; broad `interestingness` is not a reliable protected-materiality substitute.
- Duraisamy proposes complementary imaginary-space generation and deterministic validation rather than a monolithic discovery action.
- Wang & Buehler explicitly distinguish regime revision from ordinary output generation/search.
- Ota et al. provide a strong fixed-regime discovery counterexample to claims that every new theorem/result needs a representational Jump.

## 3. Challenge to the saturation basis

### J1 challenge

Papers name opportunity signals by their scientific object: symmetry, bridge relation, compression, inconsistency, affordance, surprise, unexplained patch, etc. A search for `tension detector` alone would create fake novelty. Conversely, a generic interestingness/surprise evaluator can look strong by flagging everything unless false-opportunity controls and same-public-trace/different-protected-semantics cases are retained.

### J2 challenge

`Thought experiment` is a historical umbrella term. Experiment design, counterexample generation, causal intervention, assumption relaxation, frame transformation, toy-model construction and design prototyping have different parent literatures and different execution/validation semantics. Treating them as one atom merely because all produce a hypothetical setup would collapse information-hiding responsibilities.

### J3 challenge

`Concept invention` is even broader. Historical systems already introduced theoretical terms and mathematical concepts; design theory has concept-space operators; representation learning covers splits/lifts/latents; analogy covers bridges; library learning covers abstractions/operators. A broad output label can hide multiple independently load-bearing transformations.

## 4. Reopen triggers

Reopen the donor search or terminal freeze if any of the following appears:

- a formalism supplies one common J1 observable whose semantics soundly determine all frozen material-opportunity families from the same public information and resources;
- a single J2 operator has one explicit semantics under which each frozen special operation is merely parameter choice and no child has an independently removable bounded-reach effect;
- a single J3 transformation formalism establishes equivalence of the frozen introduce/split/quotient/lift/bridge families at the registered resolution, including preservation and validation semantics;
- a protected prospective experiment shows one broad atom has incremental value after all children/parents receive matched information/resources;
- a primary source materially changes the donor ownership map.

## 5. Frozen hypotheses and falsifiers

### H-J1 — the broad tension atom decomposes

Candidate terminal: `MULTIPLE_TENSION_ATOMS_REQUIRED`.

Freeze four zero-error matched families. In each, ordinary public success/residual/confidence can be identical between a material-opportunity world and a valid no-opportunity control; the separating evidence belongs to a distinct structural witness family. A broad `EpistemicTensionCandidate.v1` remains useful as a typed envelope, but it cannot manufacture those witnesses.

Falsify if one registered public-only signal separates every frozen pair without protected labels or subtype-specific evidence and without over-alarming the paired controls.

### H-J2 — the broad imagination atom decomposes

Candidate terminal: `MULTIPLE_IMAGINATION_ATOMS_REQUIRED`.

Freeze a finite capability matrix with distinct contracts that are uniquely unlocked by assumption relaxation, frame transformation, system coupling, and formal counterexample construction, plus an ordinary-intervention control. Removing one special operator must lose its own contract while preserving the others; therefore the children have independent bounded-reach semantics at this resolution.

Falsify if one child-free parent operation reproduces every special contract under the same information/resource bound, or if the apparent children are observationally equivalent in the frozen matrix.

### H-J3 — the broad concept-invention atom decomposes

Candidate terminal: `MULTIPLE_CONCEPT_ATOMS_REQUIRED`.

Freeze five transformation families with distinct old/new structural relations: introduce a latent object, split a context-dependent variable, quotient/identify redundant states, lift to a relational representation, and construct a bridge correspondence. Each has a separate bounded contract and a strong parent lineage. `ConceptCandidate.v1` remains a proposal envelope around the transformation trace; it is not one atomic generator.

Falsify if all five transformations reduce to one registered operation with no independent protected reach/preservation consequence, or if a stronger parent already proves the whole broad envelope equivalent at this resolution.

## 6. Frozen case families

### J1 — 4 matched opportunity pairs

1. exact local predictions, but a verified cross-context unification deficit vs legitimate domain specialization;
2. exact predictions, but a transformation exposes a stable invariant vs a symmetry-looking coincidence with no protected consequence;
3. exact local solutions, but a verified cross-domain structural correspondence unlocks transfer vs a surface analogy with no structural map;
4. current objective fully satisfied, but an artifact has a verified new affordance/function vs novelty with no executable functional consequence.

### J2 — 5 operation cases

1. ordinary intervention is sufficient — no special thought-experiment atom should fire;
2. assumption relaxation uniquely separates live structures;
3. reference-frame/observer transformation uniquely exposes the discriminator;
4. coupling two previously separate systems uniquely exposes disagreement;
5. formal counterexample construction uniquely separates conjectures.

### J3 — 5 transformation cases

1. `INTRODUCE_LATENT_OBJECT` unlocks an intervention-transfer contract;
2. `SPLIT_CONTEXTUAL_VARIABLE` resolves incompatible local laws while preserving local successes;
3. `QUOTIENT_IDENTIFY_STATES` exposes an invariant under the resource bound;
4. `LIFT_RELATIONAL_REPRESENTATION` makes a mechanism locally derivable under the bound;
5. `BRIDGE_CORRESPONDENCE` composes two exact local theories into a new protected transfer contract.

## 7. Non-compensatory gates

- protected gold regime/concept names are never candidate-visible;
- zero residual never implies no opportunity, and novelty never implies material opportunity;
- J1 evidence-family warnings cannot authorize revision or Jump;
- J2 hypothetical proposals cannot score their own discrimination or acquire theory authority;
- an ordinary experiment-design control must remain a valid no-special-operator case;
- J3 lexical renaming / definitional sugar without bounded reach does not count;
- J3 old-regime `UNRESOLVED` may not be relabeled impossible;
- all preservation/correspondence and protected consequence authority remains external;
- a decomposition terminal is not evidence that any child generator is competent.

## 8. Implementation scope

Implement only an additive finite study packet that:

- freezes the J1 matched evidence families and checks that each requires an external subtype-specific discriminator;
- freezes the J2 operator/contract incidence matrix and mechanically checks independent unique reach plus the ordinary-intervention control;
- freezes the J3 transformation/contract incidence matrix and parent lineage and mechanically checks independent transformation effects;
- matches exact pre-frozen counts and terminals;
- emits no runtime generator and grants no scientific/novelty/adoption/issue-closure authority.

No frozen manuscript, protected benchmark result, Jump controller or broad #500/#501 capability claim is authorized.
