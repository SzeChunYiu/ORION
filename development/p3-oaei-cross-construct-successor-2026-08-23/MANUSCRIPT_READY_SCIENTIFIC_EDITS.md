## Proposed theory paragraph

A distribution-free covering envelope $E$ exists exactly when $I(x)\subseteq E(x)$ for every attained $x$; hence $I(x)$ is the unique inclusion-minimal covering envelope. In particular, whenever observationally equivalent worlds realize both glue and obstruction, no singleton action is coverage-valid without an additional identification assumption. This is the standard identified-set argument specialized to our setting, not a new general theorem. It sharpens the role of conflict preservation: agreement between correlated algorithms cannot itself certify fibre constancy.
