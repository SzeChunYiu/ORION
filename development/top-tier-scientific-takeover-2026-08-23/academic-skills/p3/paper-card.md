[Paper: PDF pp. 1-3] When heterogeneous scientific sources do not identify one global account, which claims are point identified, which observation would shrink the compatible set and which action is justified under remaining ambiguity?

## 11 Correct Interpretation of the Conclusions

[Paper: PDF p. 1, Abstract] The formal results concern identification under declared world classes and losses. Empirical results test mapping-level and exact-contract slices. They do not establish naturalistic envelope validity, independent multi-domain gold or end-to-end provider superiority.
