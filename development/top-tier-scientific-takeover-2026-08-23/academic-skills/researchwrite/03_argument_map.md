# P1-P5 argument map

## Shared tension

Autonomous scientific systems can appear to improve while the evidence does not identify the scientific state, method revision or authority transition being claimed.

## Central programme question

What information and authority interfaces are sufficient for acquisition, integration, verification and method revision to support an exact scientific action?

## Paper-specific theses

1. **P1:** responsibility must be coupled to a legal scientific-transition relation; exact interface sufficiency characterizes when the coupling is recoverable.
2. **P2:** safe open-world closure is an acquisition-and-authority problem; downstream optimization cannot beat the acquisition ceiling.
3. **P3:** scientific integration should return a compatible portrait envelope and identified sets before binary actions.
4. **P4:** benchmark interpretation must separate construction, identifiability, terminal attainability and panel resolution; promotion is non-compensatory.
5. **P5:** minimal revision is an adaptive discrimination problem over observationally equivalent causes, while promotion custody remains external.

## Supporting chain

`observable interface -> compatibility fibre -> identified target/set -> attainable terminal -> non-compensatory authority -> exact or fail-closed action`

## Counterarguments and answers

- **These are synthetic mechanisms.** Correct for the current decisive experiments; the papers must claim exact-contract mechanism evidence, not natural prevalence.
- **A strong donor product can tie.** Correct; the contribution is the sufficient relation and its boundary, not inherent centralization superiority.
- **Negative empirical terminals weaken the programme.** They are retained as discriminators that locate missing state, comparator strength or construction defects. They cannot be called positive outcomes.
- **Five papers overlap.** Each owns a different target relation: revision licence, closure, portrait identity, promotion, or method change. Cross-paper repetition should be reduced and the shared interface stated once per paper.

## Final move

Present a general interface theory with five target-specific instantiations, while letting each manuscript stand on its own evidence and authority boundary.
