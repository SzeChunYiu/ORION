## 06 Core Idea

1. **Surface method.** Represent admissible scientific revisions as epistemic transition envelopes.
2. **Core insight.** Exact licensing is possible precisely when the exposed interface is sufficient for the responsibility-to-action relation.
3. **General lesson.** [Analysis] Method repair and scientific authority are different targets and must be evaluated with different gold relations.

## 07 Method Overview

[Paper: PDF pp. 7-19] Inputs are candidate-visible problem states and controlled transcripts. The system computes responsibility, preservation and reopen obligations, maps them to a minimal legal action, and emits a fail-closed terminal when the relation is not identified. Host custody retains hidden labels. Output is a typed revision decision, not an unrestricted self-modification.
