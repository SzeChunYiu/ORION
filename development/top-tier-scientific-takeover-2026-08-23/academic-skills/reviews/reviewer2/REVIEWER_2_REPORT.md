# Frozen Reviewer 2 Report

## Review scope

This is an isolated Reviewer 2 assessment of five manuscripts at commit `8d47c546591a3c96dc5cf202f7e227d13251221c`. The target is *Nature Machine Intelligence*, the requested content type is Article, and the stage is initial submission. I assessed scientific validity separately from originality, significance, breadth, and journal priority.

I reviewed only the manuscript packet reconstructed from that commit. I did not inspect another reviewer report, editorial triage, concern ledger, or synthesis. I did not use later working tree changes. I did not run repository tests or CI. The report is frozen to this source state.

The relevant NMI standard is a substantial original research study with a complex story, normally involving several techniques or approaches. The review standard also asks whether the data and analyses support the conclusions, whether the work advances understanding, whether prior work is represented adequately, and whether the result has significance for the field and related fields.

## Overall assessment

The five manuscripts show unusually careful claim limiting and preserve adverse outcomes rather than converting them into wins. That integrity is valuable. It does not, however, supply the missing scientific authority. Across the packet, the positive evidence is dominated by authored exact contracts, deterministic harnesses, local code path reproductions, interface conformance, or small constructed studies. The naturalistic studies that would test the broad scientific claims are prospective, unbound, or unexecuted.

The common mathematical core is also much less original than the framing suggests. Fibre constancy, Bayes risk under coarsened information, data processing, total variation testing bounds, set cover for discriminatory tests, finite horizon decision frontiers, and robust completion are established ideas. The papers often acknowledge this correctly, yet then distribute closely related specializations across five manuscripts. The residual scientific contribution must therefore be carried by a distinctive empirical finding. At this commit, that finding is not present at the breadth required for an NMI Article.

## Cross manuscript concern

### R2-X-M1. The five papers reuse one conceptual core without a convincing publication boundary

**Axis.** Originality, contribution partition, and publication ethics.

**Severity.** Major.

**Blocking.** Yes.

**Target criterion.** A substantial original research study whose advance is clear relative to prior and companion work.

**Claim pointers.** P1 `sections/02a-minimal-escalation-theory.tex` lines 90 to 232. P2 `sections/acquisition_authority-envelope.tex` lines 228 to 374. P3 `sections/36-partial-identification-theory.tex` lines 91 to 169. P4 `sections/02a-verification-axis-identifiability.tex` lines 11 to 126. P5 `sections/02a-minimal-method-revision-theory.tex` lines 49 to 137.

**Evidence pointers.** P1 `sections/06-related-work-boundary.tex` lines 130 to 143. P3 `sections/20-related-work.tex` lines 12 to 48. P5 `sections/06a-related-work.tex` lines 6 to 21. These passages acknowledge Blackwell comparison, statistical sufficiency, partial identification, robust decisions, and classical diagnostic test selection as direct donors.

**Concern.** Each paper restates a closely related proposition. A required decision factors through an observed interface exactly when it is constant on interface fibres. Mixed fibres create irreducible risk. Additional information can refine fibres. The same logic is renamed for revision authority, scientific closure, portrait identity, verification authority, and method revision. This makes the manuscripts mutually cannibalizing. It is not clear which theorem or empirical result belongs uniquely to which paper, or why five Articles are needed rather than one integrated theory plus domain studies.

**Alternative interpretation.** The five domains may be legitimate applications of a shared theory. If so, the shared theory should be cited once and the domain papers should stand on independent empirical discoveries.

**Why it matters.** NMI priority depends on a clear, nonduplicative advance. Repeated specializations of the same standard result cannot each carry Article level originality.

**Minimum sufficient resolution test.** Provide a public claim dependency matrix across P1 to P5. For every theorem and central empirical claim, name the first paper that owns it, the exact nonoverlap in later papers, and the unique falsifier. Remove duplicated theorems or cite the owning paper. Each surviving manuscript must then show an independent empirical result that remains important even if the shared fibre theorem is treated as prior work.
