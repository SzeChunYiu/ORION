## Why the programme is not yet top-tier as a whole

The central problem is not pytest or manuscript formatting. It is an authority
gap between the strongest sentences and the strongest evidence.

1. **Most effects are generator-local.** Many exact results are valid, but they
   concern authored finite worlds, deterministic contracts, or same-repository
   replays. They do not yet estimate effects under natural scientific evidence.
2. **Several apparent wins disappear against information-equivalent donors.**
   This is scientifically useful, but it rules out architecture ownership and
   requires a theory of donor sufficiency rather than another branded component.
3. **External independence is mostly absent.** Local independent
   implementations are not independent source selection, evaluator custody,
   semantic-host custody, or clean-room reproduction.
4. **Some original gates were inert, saturated, unfair, or unattainable.** A
   passing replacement cannot retroactively repair the old claim. The successor
   must ask a new question whose gate can genuinely distinguish the theories.
5. **The papers fragment one larger object.** State, acquisition, verification,
   responsibility, authority, reopening, transport, learning, and harness
   execution are presented as neighbouring mechanisms rather than as theorems
   about one scientific-transition system.

## Upward theory: epistemic transition envelopes

The programme should be organized around the theory introduced in P1:

- scientific changes form a well-founded partial order, not a fixed `K/W/M`
  chain;
- hard evidence, task, preservation, impact, and resource conditions define an
  admissible transition set;
- its minimal elements form the epistemic transition envelope;
- an arbitrary product of nearest-work mechanisms is exactly sufficient if and
  only if its exposed interface is constant on every required-decision fibre;
- a lossy interface has irreducible error equal to its conditional Bayes
  decision-fibre impurity;
- a stronger donor theory extends an old envelope conservatively exactly when
  new admissible transitions neither undercut an old minimum nor create a new
  incomparable minimum.

This moves upward without pretending that ORION invented belief revision,
diagnosis, value of information, typed memory, dependency reopening, or formal
verification. Those become donor mechanisms feeding a more general transition
theory.

## Non-negotiable result policy

- A historical negative remains negative for its frozen claim.
- A donor tie remains a donor tie; it becomes a sufficiency theorem or a new
  missing-information question, not an ORION win.
- A successor gets a new claim identity, outcome-blind gate, and protected
  evidence unit.
- Wider wording is earned by a wider theorem or wider evidence. It is never
  obtained by deleting a qualifier from a finite study.
- Each adverse result below remains an active research topic until it reaches a
  positive, negative, absorbed, impossibility, or genuinely unresolved terminal.

## Portfolio order by scientific leverage

1. **Q1 analytic generalization.** It already has an all-`n` theorem and exact
   counterexample; a broader grammar/resource theorem is the shortest path to a
   genuinely wide flagship result.
2. **P1 envelope theory plus R7A.** The general theory now exists, but the
   naturalistic decision-fibre result is the decisive empirical bridge.
3. **P11--P13 phase/signal/certificate theorems.** These have clean bounded
   positives and explicit hostile regimes from which broader structural laws
   can be derived.
4. **Q2/Q3/Q4 cross-domain prospective campaigns.** These provide external
   trajectory, calibration, and real-decision evidence for the umbrella theory.
5. **P2--P10/P14--P15 external panels.** Freeze once, share source/evaluator
   custody where scientifically appropriate, but keep hypotheses and analysis
   units distinct.

## Publication criterion

No paper is called top-tier because a checklist is green. A paper reaches the
target only when it has all three:

1. a sharp theorem, causal mechanism, or externally meaningful empirical
   regularity that remains after the strongest donor is granted;
2. evidence capable of falsifying that claim at the scale and domain named in
   the title/abstract;
3. a manuscript organized around that result rather than around the history of
   the ORION repository.
