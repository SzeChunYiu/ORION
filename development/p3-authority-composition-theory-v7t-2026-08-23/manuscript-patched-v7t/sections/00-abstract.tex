% Abstract (standalone fragment included by main.tex).
Cross-domain scientific synthesis usually underdetermines a unique global
account: sources expose different referents, constructs, operationalizations,
contexts, modalities and provenance, and extraction may hide the coordinate
that would decide whether two claims are identical. We introduce the
\emph{epistemic portrait envelope}, the set of global scientific portraits
compatible with the observed source-local projections and their licensed
mapping constraints. This changes integration from selecting one canonical
graph to three separable questions: which claims are point identified, which
additional observation would shrink the envelope, and which downstream action
is justified while identification remains partial. Eight formal results give
the framework its scope. A fibre criterion proves that a query is identified
exactly when it is constant across all worlds yielding the same observation;
otherwise no observation-only rule can be uniformly correct. An information-
refinement theorem proves that retaining more truthful source information can
only shrink query sets and weakly lower the minimax decision floor. A support
theorem proves that atlas-wide coordinate variation cannot identify
coordinate value when the coordinate never contrasts inside a registered
comparison; a joint-coordinate support report is therefore required. A credal
risk envelope gives exact robust merge, separation and deferral conditions for
an arbitrary licensed set of binary laws through its probability infimum and
supremum; sharp closed intervals are a special case, while outer intervals give
only conservative bounds. A joint-
completion result shows why pairwise-compatible mappings need not compose into
a coherent global portrait. A positive-reference non-identification theorem
shows that omission from a reference cannot by itself identify obstruction:
without explicit negative evidence or authoritative exhaustivity over a frozen
candidate universe, both glue and obstruction remain compatible with the
observation. Glue, typed obstruction, plural view and
unresolved state become special cases of this set-valued semantics rather than
unrelated output labels. A universal factorisation theorem further shows that
the identified-set map is the coarsest sufficient claim interface: any other
sufficient product must refine it, and information-equivalent implementations
must tie under the same loss. An authority-aware fibre criterion further
places claim-relevant source, rights, execution, reference and custody
failure outside the scientific truth codomain and shows why passed local
preflights do not identify an end-to-end claim without an explicit
composition relation.

Existing evidence tests slices of the theory without being promoted to
end-to-end authority. In a disjoint, prospectively execution-frozen 32-case
public-reference holdout, coordinate-governed mapping made zero false merges
against 0.1875 for flat predicate canonicalization; the paired difference was
$-0.1875$ (95\% bootstrap CI $[-0.34375,-0.0625]$), while its false-split
difference from an exact-coordinate conservative control was $0.000$ with
interval $[0.000,0.000]$. A separate 400-contract exact battery isolated the
scientific-identity decision after strong semantic alignment: the
claim-relative layer decided 400/400 exactly versus 250/400 for a strong
semantic product, while an information-equivalent typed product tied at
400/400. Most importantly, an archived partial-observation study found nine
two-case observationally indistinguishable orbits in a 36-case constructed
corpus, imposing a 27/36 ceiling on every rule reading only the released
projections. That adverse result motivates the envelope and its
floor-adjusted successor endpoint; it is not relabelled as a positive external
result. Naturalistic envelope validity, independent multi-domain gold,
raw-text robustness and downstream decision gains remain unexecuted. A
prospective metadata-only audit of seven OAEI track families found zero with
the complete rights, immutable identity and binary-truth authority required to
open outcomes, against a predeclared minimum of three; no new-family label,
matcher output or performance result was accessed. A distinct outcome-blind
direct-certificate calibration subsequently admitted three rights-audited
byte-identical ontology families and point-identified 4,838 canonical pairs
(4,789 glue, 49 explicit obstruction, zero conflicts) without using absence or
reasoner inference. That calibration executes the finite authority semantics;
it does not measure naturalistic cross-ontology transport or comparator
performance. A subsequent outcome-blind native preflight makes AML and LogMap
smoke-ready on authored 16-class fixtures, with a guarded upstream LogMap RDF
header defect, while BERTMap stops before training because the pinned DeepOnto
lock resolves an incompatible Transformers API. Thus two of three native
toolchains pass smoke, but scientific comparator readiness remains zero of
three: no gold, protected case, scoring or naturalistic transport was opened.
