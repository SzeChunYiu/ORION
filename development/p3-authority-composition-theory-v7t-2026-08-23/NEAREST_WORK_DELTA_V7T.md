Crossref reports a CC-BY-4.0 version-of-record licence and provides the

be a full-text adjudication.

## Disposition

`ABSORB_DIAGNOSIS__RETAIN_AUTHORITY_COMPOSITION_RESIDUAL`

The abstract reports empirical uncertainty, errors, and inaccuracies in
automatic ontology matching on real relational data, questions fully automated
reliability, and motivates semi-supervised uncertainty management. P3 must
therefore not claim as novel that automated ontology matching is uncertain,
that human oversight can matter, or that unqualified automation is unreliable.

The surviving V7T residual is different:

1. matcher uncertainty concerns uncertainty in a produced correspondence;
2. authority failure concerns whether the named scientific comparison has an
   admissible value at all under its source, rights, execution, reference, and
   custody gates; and
3. local matcher smoke does not compose into global comparison authority
   without an explicit cross-stage relation.

The new theory represents the second state as
`bottom_authority`/`CANNOT_CHECK`, outside the scientific truth codomain. This
prevents missing execution from being laundered into an observed loss, tie, or
negative mapping.

## Remaining literature gap

The Crossref searches were not exhaustive and the closest 2024 paper was not
read in full because the publisher PDF route was blocked. Before submission,
an independent literature review must search Semantic Web, ISWC/ESWC, OAEI,
JMLR/ML evaluation, selective prediction, and reproducible benchmarking for a
prior formalism that places an execution/rights/custody failure terminal
outside the scientific truth codomain and proves a composition criterion.
Until then, novelty is `CANNOT_CHECK`, while the theorem's correctness can be
reviewed separately.
