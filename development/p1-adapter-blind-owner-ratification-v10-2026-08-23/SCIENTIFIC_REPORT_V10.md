## Newly isolated construct-validity gap

V9 records who must sign and what fields must exist.  It does not record the
information set from which the algebra or its semantic review was produced.
That matters because the 720 survivor maps, compatibility cells, and source
terminal profiles are already present in the shared/public research record.

If an algebra is authored or approved after inspecting those artifacts, a
passing audit cannot distinguish a pre-existing target meaning from a target
meaning selected because it helps a desired adapter.  A signature can prove
approval of bytes; it cannot retrospectively prove that adapter candidates did
not influence the bytes.  This is semantic post-selection, analogous to
outcome-aware endpoint construction, even when no case row or performance
outcome is opened.

This is not a claim that any current participant actually post-selected
semantics.  Exposure history is unbound, so the proper disposition is

independent authoring and adapter-aware post-selection remain observationally
equivalent.  With it, the unchanged audit becomes a genuine out-of-construction
test.
