# Adapter-aware semantic post-selection nonidentification theorem (P1 V10)

## Setting

Let \(S\) denote the frozen P1 source-terminal profiles and unchanged gates,
\(\mathcal F\) the 720 not-disproved source-to-R7 maps, and \(T\) a completed
seven-target algebra.  Let \(A(S,T)\) be the unchanged finite adjudication.
The content-validity question is whether \(T\) records target semantics that
exist independently of the candidate adapter rather than semantics selected
because of their effect on \(A\).

Define \(E=1\) when the algebra author or independent semantic reviewer had
access, before content-addressed commitment, to any frozen source-terminal
profile, compatibility matrix, map registry, audit result, or case/outcome
artifact.  Define \(I=1\) when target-semantic independence is evidenced by
either:

1. a signed target algebra whose immutable timestamp precedes the P1
   source-terminal/adjudication programme; or
2. a prospective custody receipt proving that only the frozen target-side
   allowlist was delivered until the algebra and semantic review were signed.

## Theorem

A schema-valid, rights-valid and correctly signed algebra with a `CONFORMANT`
review does not, by itself, identify target-semantic independence when \(I\) is
unbound.  In particular, if precommitment exposure \(E\) is unobserved, the
same completed bundle is observationally compatible with both:

- target-side semantic construction independent of \(A(S,T)\); and
- adapter-aware semantic selection influenced by the desired audit result.

Therefore construct independence remains `CANNOT_CHECK` until a historical-
precedence or adapter-blind custody witness binds \(I=1\).

## Proof

The completed bundle records algebra content, roles, rights, signatures, time,
and review disposition.  None of those fields records the information set used
to choose that content.  When the precommitment information set is unobserved,
two data-generating histories are compatible with the same bytes: one in which
the target meaning was determined from target-side evidence alone, and one in
which the author or reviewer inspected adapter-side candidates before choosing
or approving the meaning.  Because the observed bundle is identical under
both histories, the history—and hence independence from the audit—is not
identified.  Temporal precedence or a content-addressed allowlist/custody
receipt distinguishes the histories.  \(\square\)

## Minimality corollary

V9's four authority acts remain non-substitutable:

1. vocabulary-owner/delegate target semantics;
2. host recommendation/execution authority;
3. target-corpus rights;
4. author-independent semantic review.

No fifth principal is required.  The smallest repair is one ordering predicate
attached to acts 1 and 4: target-side commitment must precede adapter-side
exposure.  Removing that predicate leaves the two histories above
observationally equivalent.  Removing any V9 authority act leaves at least one
of G01/G08/G10/G12 without its unique custodian class.

## Scope boundary

This theorem does not say the current target semantics are wrong, that a valid
algebra cannot be written, or that any of the 720 maps is impossible.  It does
not convert absent access evidence into a negative.  It establishes only that
post hoc signatures cannot retrospectively prove semantic independence.
