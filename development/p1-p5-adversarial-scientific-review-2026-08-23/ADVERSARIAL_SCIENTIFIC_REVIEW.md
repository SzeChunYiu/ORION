## 2. Severity and recursive-research rule

- **Fatal:** a theorem, result, rights assertion, or archive identity is false or
  unusable inside its current stated scope. It must be repaired or removed before
  circulation.
- **Major:** the current bounded claim can survive, but the gap blocks the
  advertised top-tier/general claim.
- **Minor:** a precision, notation, integration, or reproducibility defect that
  still must be fixed before submission.

Every adverse result below is immutable under its original study identity. A
materially changed source universe, population, comparator, intervention,
endpoint, theorem assumption, or custody arrangement receives a successor
identity. The successor may end **positive, negative, harmful, null/interaction,
or `CANNOT_CHECK`**. Recursive research is not a promise that every result will
become positive. Wider claims are earned by wider valid evidence; they are not
earned by deleting boundaries.
