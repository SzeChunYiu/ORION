# P1 scientific-content takeover

## Objective

Move Paper I upward from one minimal-escalation contract to a general theory of
epistemic transition envelopes: when arbitrary products of diagnosis, repair,
belief revision, model discovery, planning, and dependency-maintenance
mechanisms are sufficient for authorized scientific change, when their exposed
interfaces are necessarily lossy, and when stronger donor theories can be
absorbed conservatively.

## Scientific defects addressed

1. The title and opening currently describe a broad recursive-research
   architecture, while the nearest-work audit leaves a narrower composition
   claim.
2. The paper reports an ideal-product tie but does not formalize why that tie is
   required once the donor product receives the same semantics.
3. The paper reports a donor-complete greedy-product separation but does not
   isolate the missing information that makes such a separation possible.
4. The underpowered 66-case precursor appears before the powered successor and
   obscures the active claim.

## Scientific changes completed in this takeover

- Generalize from a finite revision set to well-founded partial orders over
  questions, representations, measurements, model classes, experiments,
  search policies, and methods.
- Define the epistemic transition envelope as the minimal admissible front of
  an arbitrary conjunction of hard scientific gates.
- Prove exact donor substitution if and only if the donor interface is
  constant on required-decision fibres.
- Prove the optimal zero-one error of a lossy interface equals its conditional
  Bayes decision-fibre impurity.
- Recover the licence-coupling impossibility as a corollary rather than the
  entire theory.
- Prove a necessary and sufficient conservative-extension criterion for
  absorbing stronger donor theories without silently changing an old front.
- Prove universal representation of antichain-valued transition policies.
- Rewrite the title, abstract, introduction, related-work position,
  limitations, and conclusion around the envelope theory.
- Present the powered successor and exact-contract battery before the retained
  historical precursor without deleting or relabeling the precursor.
- Add and live-verify the closest classical information-order parent:
  Blackwell, *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments*, Annals of Mathematical
  Statistics 24(2):265--272, DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177729032.

## Immediate empirical successor

The wide R7A design remains the next authority-bearing study: eight causal
families by four protected scientific domains, 384 independent source clusters
plus 64 unresolved clusters per wave, two source-disjoint waves, nine frozen
comparator families, and joint max-T power approximately 0.920 at the planning
effect. It is not executable as a scientific result until source snapshots, a
strongest runnable external comparator, changed semantic hosts, independent
scorers, and protected execution custody are actually bound.

## Authority boundary

These are analytic results and manuscript-structure changes. They do not create
new empirical observations, external validation, independent custody, or
model-general scientific-agent evidence. Historical adverse results remain
unchanged.
