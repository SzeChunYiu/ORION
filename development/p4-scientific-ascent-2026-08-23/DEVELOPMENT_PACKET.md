## Scientific ascent delivered

### 1. Verification-axis theory

New identity: `P4.AXIS.T1.EXACT_ATTAINABILITY.V1`.

For target terminal `Y`, system representation `D`, and available output alphabet `A`, the manuscript now derives the exact finite-space optimum

`R*(D,A) = 1 - E_D max_{t in A} P(Y=t | D)`.

Zero error is attainable iff every positive-mass `D`-fibre carries one gold terminal and that terminal is in `A`. This separates:

- an unidentifiable task (mixed semantic fibres),
- a representation/interface-limited system (missing state or terminal),
- a nuisance-decodable construction,
- a saturated panel,
- and an actual decision-rule failure.

### 2. Donor-product factorization theorem

New identity: `P4.AXIS.T2.DONOR_FACTORIZATION.V1`.

A product of provenance, verification, custody, freshness and authorization donors is extensionally equivalent to the target scientific-promotion relation iff the relation factors through the state exposed by that product. Mixed donor fibres impose an irreducible Bayes-error lower bound. This both widens and disciplines P4:

- it explains why a generic donor relation can fail even when its local components succeed;
- it predicts the ideal P4-X B3 tie when all target-sufficient coordinates and the same predicate are supplied;
- it rules out any inherent centralization advantage.

### 3. Claim-level identifiability bound

New identity: `P4.AXIS.T3.CLAIM_IDENTIFIABILITY.V1`.

If two scientific worlds with opposite competence claims induce observable
records at total-variation distance `d`, every equal-prior adjudicator has error
at least `(1-d)/2`.  Identical records therefore force `CANNOT_CHECK` for the
wider competence interpretation even when a narrower output score separates.
This turns the difference between terminal expressiveness and epistemic
judgement into a formal identifiability question.

### 4. Verification-axis adequacy envelope

New identity: `P4.AXIS.D1.ADEQUACY_ENVELOPE.V1`.

A broad scientific interpretation now requires four conditions jointly:

1. **semantic attainability** from intended scientific information;
2. **nuisance resistance** under a declared audit class;
3. **matched terminal attainability** for the comparison arms;
4. **nonzero panel resolution** on the claimed axis.

This yields a research action for each adverse outcome rather than a post-hoc relabeling: repair the construction, enlarge the interface, expose target-sufficient state, or improve the decision relation.

### 5. Strongest donors absorbed rather than competed away

The manuscript now treats the following as donor-owned:

- Partial Evidence Bench-style incomplete-view and gap reporting;
- AuthGraph-style provenance/authorization alignment;
- FAVA-style evidence-backed permission graphs and deterministic authorization;
- CVA-style principal/request/context/policy binding;
- existing source-aware verification, attribution, claim auditability, evidence escalation, abstention, contamination auditing, evaluator auditing, and protected execution.

The residual is target scientific-promotion sufficiency and its verification-axis conditions, not provenance or authorization in the abstract.
