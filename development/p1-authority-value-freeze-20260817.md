## Challenge to saturation

A result can be artificially positive if:
- the gold authorization rule is copied from the ORION licensing implementation;
- the strong parent is denied diagnosis/repair information that ORION receives;
- blanket refusal scores as safe success;
- dependency correctness is defined only by ORION-specific names;
- pair/family ids leak the answer;
- candidate-specific budgets differ;
- repeated repair candidates are treated as independent task units;
- a successful local patch is declared scientifically valid despite stale dependent closures;
- the holdout is generated after inspecting candidate outputs.

The frozen experiment therefore separates the host task contract from candidate policy, gives every arm identical diagnosis/intervention evidence, includes legitimate-change controls and conservative/no-change baselines, defines task-level aggregation before execution, and binds a fresh corpus before arms run.

## Why earlier evidence did not answer this

V1 mostly measured whether diagnosis/reframe was reached at all. The current V2.0 scaffold tests attribution and licensing together, so a diagnosis improvement could obscure whether authority semantics add anything after attribution. The R3 successor explicitly conditions on the same diagnosed cause and intervention evidence across all arms to identify the authority/reopening layer.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

On fresh cause-confusable scientific-state tasks, strong attribution/replay can identify the responsible stage and a local patch can often restore the immediate task. However, without explicit scientific mutation authority and dependency accounting, a repair policy will either (a) over-mutate protected/unrelated state, (b) leave dependent closures stale, or (c) avoid those errors through excessive refusal. `typed_permission + dependency_scoped_reopen` is predicted to increase **scientifically valid repair** while preserving root recovery and near-zero unauthorized mutation.

## Reopen triggers

- a donor is found that already implements the residual composition;
- candidate view or identifiers leak gold/pair/family identity;
- the independent scorer disagrees on authorization/dependency gold;
- a strong class-conditioned/minimal-repair parent matches full ORION within the frozen equivalence margin;
- fresh holdout reverses the development direction;
- root recovery falls below the frozen non-inferiority margin;
- any protected/forbidden mutation is accepted by the full arm;
- a post-freeze design change would alter the primary endpoint or decision rule (requires v2.2, never rewrite v2.1).
