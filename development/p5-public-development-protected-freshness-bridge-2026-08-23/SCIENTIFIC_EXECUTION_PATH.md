`P5_BRIDGE_PUBLIC_DEVELOPMENT_BOUND__PROTECTED_PROVIDER_AND_SIX_ARM_EXECUTION_CANNOT_CHECK`

The bridge makes five scientific advances over the count-only wide protocol:

1. it names eight scientific domains and gives each an eligibility rubric;
2. it gives the six-comparator multiplicity family a stable set of slot IDs and
   states exactly why zero of six is confirmatory-ready;
3. it separates lawful public development from protected confirmation;
4. it requires two source/provider/time-disjoint 768-cluster waves plus
   no-revision harm sentinels; and
5. it turns external custody into a concrete provider acceptance contract with
   rights, task, evaluator, access-log, result-export and verifier fields.

The route upward is therefore wider than a single public software benchmark,
but not less rigorous. Public implementation data cannot be relabelled as
evidence-, measurement-, model-, representation- or evaluator-revision gold.

## The wider two-wave design

Each wave contains 768 powered endpoint clusters:

- eight domains;
- eight revision classes;
- twelve source-disjoint clusters per domain x class;
- 96 same-visible-symptom blocks per wave, each containing one globally
  source-disjoint cluster from every revision class; and
- an additional 96 no-revision safety sentinels per wave, excluded from the
  powered main endpoint but required as a non-compensatory harm gate.

The two waves therefore contain 1,536 powered endpoint clusters and 192 safety
sentinels. The replication wave must be source-, provider-, case-author-,
adjudicator-, epoch- and fresh-target-disjoint from the primary wave. If full
provider separation is infeasible, that limitation is preregistered and the
broad transport claim is not authorized.

The predecessor power calculation is not final authority for this material
change. Power must be recomputed after all six exact arms and the actual
same-symptom dependence structure are frozen.

## Eight domains

1. biomedical and clinical;
2. experimental life science;
3. earth and environmental science;
4. physical science;
5. engineering and robotics;
6. computational scientific software;
7. social, behavioral and economic science; and
8. mathematical, statistical and formal systems.

These are target strata, not observed coverage. The first outcome-blind task is
a rights-and-eligibility census. Any zero or rights-ineligible cell is an
immutable source-universe negative. Cases are never moved between cells after
outcomes.

## Claim gate

A broad result is eligible for review only if both waves pass every one of the
six paired contrasts, correct minimal revision, no excess broad revision,
preservation, fresh transfer, no-revision sentinels, harm ceilings, and
worst-domain/worst-class floors. A positive primary-only pilot cannot authorize
cross-provider or cross-time transport.

Possible terminals include supported, primary-only/nonreplicated, no
incremental value, harmful refutation, interaction/heterogeneity, joint null,
source-universe or rights negative, comparator-interface limited, and
`CANNOT_CHECK`.
