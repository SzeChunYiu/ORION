# ORION-Q MAX-R6D six-term TARE partition / representation co-optimization protocol

Date: 2026-08-21
Parent programme: #679
Authority: open-subject method-language development only; not R6 and no novelty authority.
Predecessors:
- exact TARE-3 frame-only collapse negative;
- verified MAX-R6B TARE transformation-reuse donor negative;
- verified MAX-R6C dual-lane residual diagnosis authorizing a new method-language protocol.

## Research residual

The strongest fixed-block TARE-3 experiment collapsed to the exact frame-only donor, and donor-owned common `U_tag` / correction reuse was substantially worse than independent blocks on the frozen H4 and equilibrium-N2 batches. Canonical ORION and ORION-Q then independently identified

`RESP:METHOD_LANGUAGE_INADEQUATE_AFTER_DONOR_CLOSURE`.

The new operation tested here is **not** another isolated auxiliary-frame search. It couples two choices that were previously frozen separately:

1. which three of six fixed Pauli terms belong to each corrected TARE-3 block;
2. the exact auxiliary representation of each resulting block.

The candidate therefore searches the exact finite set of 3+3 partitions and exact TARE-3 representations jointly.

## Donor subtraction

Zero novelty credit is assigned to:

- TARE, Tag/Restore, free auxiliary anticommuting frames, labels, native matches or Uanti;
- splitting a larger Pauli sum and composing the blocks by LCU;
- anticommuting/unitary partitioning, clique grouping or set partitioning;
- global Clifford/Pauli-network optimization;
- common-Clifford extraction or TARE transformation reuse;
- coefficient factorization;
- dynamic programming or exhaustive finite search as generic techniques.

The bounded residual is only the **TARE-specific coupling of an exact 3+3 term partition with exact auxiliary-representation selection under the same normalization/resource envelope**. No novelty authority is granted by this protocol or by an open-subject positive.

## Frozen open subjects and six-term batches

Use only the already-open H4 and equilibrium N2 subjects from MAX-R6B. The stretched-N2 prospective discriminator remains unread.

For each subject, reconstruct exactly the same six-term batch frozen by MAX-R6B:

1. run the unchanged candidate-blind P10 scan in non-overlapping 12-term windows;
2. keep the best improving non-direct triple in each window under ordering
   `(-rank2_fraction, -rank2_delta, term_indices)`;
3. sort window champions by that same ordering;
4. take exactly the first two champions and union their six source term indices.

No source term may be substituted after the R6D outcome is known.

## Exact 3+3 partition universe

Let the frozen six source terms be positions `0..5` in ascending source-term-index order.

Enumerate each unordered 3+3 partition exactly once by requiring position `0` to be in block A. This gives exactly `C(5,2)=10` candidate partitions.

R6D is a fixed corrected-TARE-3 comparison. A partition is eligible only when **both** triples are not already pairwise anticommuting. Direct triples belong to the direct-unitary donor and are excluded rather than charged as TARE. The original R6B partition is guaranteed to provide at least one eligible partition.

For every eligible partition serialize both triples, their source indices and coefficient vectors. Every coefficient appears exactly once.

## Matched normalization/resource coordinates

For a triple with coefficients `alpha`, use the frozen TARE normalization

`Lambda_3 = sqrt(3) ||alpha||_2`.

For a 3+3 partition,

`Lambda_batch = Lambda_3(A) + Lambda_3(B)`.

Every eligible candidate/incumbent point holds fixed:

- the same six source Pauli terms and six coefficients;
- two corrected TARE-3 blocks;
- two five-exponential Uanti realizations (10 total rotations);
- two-bit internal TARE label width;
- two outer LCU blocks and one outer address bit;
- the same source parsing/mapping semantics.

The development vector is therefore

`(Lambda_batch, C_support)`

with no scalar exchange rate between the coordinates.

## Candidate point

For each eligible partition, independently run the already-hostile-verified exact joint TARE-3 solver on block A and block B and define

`C_candidate = C_exact_joint(A) + C_exact_joint(B)`.

The new method-language operation is the global selection of a partition together with those exact representations; the single-block solver itself receives no new novelty credit.

Every chosen exact-joint block must retain its proof-carrying symplectic/label/Restore/cost witness.

## Strongest incumbent point

For the same partition compute:

1. `C_frame = B_FRAME_ONLY_STRONG(A) + B_FRAME_ONLY_STRONG(B)` using the complete exact Uanti-minimum frame donor;
2. `C_reuse`, if any, from the frozen MAX-R6B signed-phase common-Tag/correction reuse search on these two blocks.

Define

`C_incumbent = min(C_frame, C_reuse if valid)`.

Thus the incumbent receives exact partition enumeration, the strongest independent frame-only block implementation, and donor-owned transformation reuse. The candidate cannot obtain a positive by withholding a known donor capability.

## Non-compensatory envelope comparison

The incumbent envelope at normalization budget `L` is

`E_inc(L) = min C_incumbent(p)` over every eligible partition `p` with `Lambda_batch(p) <= L + 1e-12`.

A candidate point `q` has a strict budget-matched improvement only when

`C_candidate(q) < E_inc(Lambda_batch(q))`.

Because the incumbent is allowed every partition with equal or *lower* normalization, this cannot claim a support win by spending extra normalization.

For each subject report:

- all 10 unordered partitions and eligibility reason;
- every eligible candidate/incumbent point;
- incumbent Pareto envelope;
- candidate Pareto envelope;
- the deterministic best strict improvement ordered by
  `(C_candidate - E_inc, Lambda_batch, partition)`;
- all exact witnesses whether positive or negative.

## Hostile verification

Before chemistry support may be claimed:

1. a six-position enumerator must produce exactly 10 unique complementary 3+3 partitions, each once;
2. every eligible row must conserve all six source terms and coefficients exactly once;
3. candidate cost must equal the sum recomputed from its two serialized exact-joint witnesses;
4. frame-only cost must equal the sum recomputed from its two serialized donor witnesses;
5. any reuse cost must pass the signed-phase R6B witness checks unchanged;
6. the normalization envelope comparison must be cross-checked by a second direct scan implementation on deterministic synthetic six-term panels;
7. no protected stretched-N2 content may be read.

Any integrity failure is distinct from a scientific negative and cannot be coerced into support.

## Open-development gate

`MAX_R6D_SIXTERM_PARTITION_REPRESENTATION_COOPT_SUPPORTED` requires:

1. hostile verification passes;
2. both H4 and equilibrium N2 contain at least one eligible 3+3 partition;
3. every exact candidate and incumbent witness used by the envelopes passes;
4. the candidate has at least one strict budget-matched improvement over the strongest incumbent envelope on **both** H4 and equilibrium N2;
5. the protected stretched-N2 discriminator remains unread.

A positive remains below R6. It must receive an independent replay, another hostile donor/novelty pass and a separately frozen prospective protocol before protected access can be reconsidered.

A negative is preserved and returns the residual to ORION/ORION-Q without weakening this gate.
