## Benchmark impossibility lemma — load-bearing

Let `S` be the strongest visible typed research state and `A` the registered research-operator set. Suppose:

1. gold next action is a deterministic function `f : S -> A`;
2. every held-out state lies in training support: `Supp(S_test) subseteq Supp(S_train)`;
3. a generic donor learner may memorize the exact observed mapping on training support.

Then a consistent table learner can achieve perfect held-out next-action accuracy. No ORION controller restricted to the same state/action mapping can exceed that solve-rate ceiling.

**Consequence:** MAX-R3 is invalid unless it requires structural extrapolation beyond a known state->known-action map.

Qualifying extrapolation must include at least one of:
- an operator/schema absent from the registered donor skill library;
- a state structure outside training support;
- a new representation/interface that changes reachable operations;
- a new higher-order composition rule not in the incumbent grammar;
- a new proof/invariant object required to make an existing composition admissible.

## Maximal question

> Can P10 diagnose that the donor-composed quantum research language is structurally insufficient and construct a **new typed research-operator schema** that is outside the frozen incumbent closure, expands verified held-out reach across quantum domains, and survives the strongest program-synthesis/library-learning/self-evolving-agent baselines under matched information, verifier and compute budgets?

This is the first MAX stage eligible for `SELF_EXPANDING_METHOD_LANGUAGE` language.

## Closed-world successor families

At least three materially different families must be executed before a negative can close MAX-R3.

### R3-A — higher-order failure-conditioned library growth

Existing successful-trace library learning is available to the incumbent.

Protected tasks are selected so:
- successful source solutions alone do not expose the needed abstraction;
- repeated **failed** partial traces share a typed obstruction pattern;
- a new higher-order operator compresses/redirects the search only after that obstruction is recognized;
- origin tasks are excluded from transfer evaluation.

Strong baselines:
- DreamCoder/library learning from successes;
- library learning supplied both success and failed traces;
- CEGIS/counterexample-guided grammar induction;
- generic evolutionary program search;
- P10 obstruction-conditioned schema generation.

Positive requires a residual beyond the strongest failure-aware library baseline, not merely beyond success-only DreamCoder.

### R3-B — cross-representation obligation transport

A donor method solves a subproblem after a representation change, but the incumbent has no registered operator that transports the original task's proof/error/access obligations into the new representation and back.

Protected candidate must synthesize a correspondence/transport schema such that a commuting obligation diagram verifies.

Controls:
- notation-only changes;
- stronger-oracle laundering;
- dropped obligations;
- partial transport whose admissible completions disagree;
- known canonical linear-algebra transforms.

Strong baselines:
- generic schema/lens/refinement synthesis;
- theorem/proof transport;
- program transformation;
- P7/P6 ORION closure-transport mechanics as incumbent donor structure.

### R3-C — active method-language expansion under scarce verifier budget

Candidate method schemas are not enumerable for free. Their applicability is partially unknown and expensive to verify.

P10 may generate/query/test schemas using scoped negative history and value-of-information.

Strong baselines:
- Vista-style gated verification;
- Bayesian metareasoning/value-of-information;
- active program synthesis/CEGIS;
- bandit/evolutionary search;
- generic self-evolving scientific skill induction.

Positive requires better **verified held-out reach** at matched verifier budget, not only fewer verifier calls.

### R3-D — parametric symbolic method-schema induction

The missing object is a reusable parameterized research/algorithm schema, not one finite action.

Candidate targets:
- recurring phase/reflection/control schema;
- recurrence/invariant for a parametric quantum-program family;
- cross-size operator construction;
- structured access/interface transformation.

Strong baselines:
- symbolic regression;
- CEGIS(T);
- QSynth;
- evolutionary DSL synthesis;
- generic theorem/proof induction.

A P10 claim survives only if those parents do not obtain the same held-out family under matched budget.

## Exact closure test

Before any `new schema` credit, canonicalize the candidate and ask:

`candidate in Closure_B(incumbent grammar)?`

Where `Closure_B` includes every registered ordinary composition, macro/library abstraction, parameter substitution and representation transport available to the donor supersystem under bound `B`.

Possible closure results:
- `IN_CLOSURE`;
- `OUTSIDE_FROZEN_CLOSURE`;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

Only `OUTSIDE_FROZEN_CLOSURE` may support MAX-R3 expansion language. This is benchmark-relative, not global novelty.

- semantic correctness;

- error/resource contract;
- no stronger oracle;

## Cross-domain requirement

The strongest MAX-R3 positive requires one operator schema originating from evidence in at least two quantum domains and improving protected tasks in a third domain after identity remint, without target-domain retuning.

A single-domain method invention is a lower rung and does not close the MAX programme.

## Hostile controls

- candidate is a renamed donor skill;
- candidate is ordinary composition under a different syntax;
- hidden answer leaks through operator/type names;
- generic program synthesis denied a constructor available to P10;
- P10 receives stronger verifier feedback;
- target domain appears in training metadata;
- representation edit silently changes the problem/access model;
- failure-only learner gets more traces than baseline;
- candidate wins only by more compute;
- candidate solves origin tasks but not held-out families;
- post-outcome grammar tuning;
- novelty self-certified from benchmark closure.
