# Dual-harness agreement benchmark V0 — frozen protocol

Date: 2026-08-21
Branch: `claude/orion-harness-verification-b17qdj`
Status: FROZEN BEFORE EITHER LANE'S OUTCOME EXISTS.
Authority: benchmark/diagnostic only. Neither lane's output grants scientific, novelty,
promotion, or R6 authority; divergence between lanes is data, not failure.

## Question posed identically to both lanes

> Given the committed R6 receipts through R6O (family saturation; support dominance
> verified with a refutation at the Tag-repair gap; enlarged-donor closure refuted by the
> converse frame-for-Tag regime; chemistry donor-exact on all recorded optima), which
> epistemic layer is responsible for the remaining gap, and what is the correct next
> research move for the ORION-Q MAX programme?

## Lane A — generic ORION harness (host-driven)

`orion-harness` workspace; recursive solve (default mode) with `--max-iterations 3`;
this session acts as external host, servicing capabilities ONLY with evidence drawn from
committed repository receipts (file paths cited in every WEB_SEARCH item; no external
web). The reconstruct summary and terminal constitute Lane A's answer. All receipts
retained in the workspace and archived.

## Lane B — ORION-Q campaign harness (native typed controller)

A new campaign manifest `orion-q:post-r6o-diagnosis` built on the shared campaign layer:
initial observations transcribed verbatim from the committed R6N/R6O/R6M/R6L receipts;
frozen responsibility hypotheses with discriminating expected-observation sets, each
bound to a typed revision/computation; decisions made by the production
`orion.transfer.v2` / `orion.self_orion` modules through `campaign_control` (no LLM in
the loop; no free-text reasoning). One cycle: the native decision is Lane B's answer.
The manifest must not encode the answer: every hypothesis must be observation-
discriminable, and at least three materially different responsible layers must be
representable (donor-family incompleteness; representation-regime uncharacterized;
method-language inadequacy; search incompleteness).

## Comparison coordinates (fixed now)

1. Diagnosed responsible layer (Lane A prose-normalized vs Lane B `hypothesis_id`).
2. Selected next move (Lane A recommendation vs Lane B selected revision/computation).
3. Evidence basis (receipts cited by A vs observations consumed by B).
4. Agreement with the two independently launched frontier lanes (R6P donor-extension,
   R6Q regime-characterization) — scored only after A and B are recorded.
5. Cost profile: A = host capability receipts consumed; B = cycles and receipts.

## Outcome space (all valid)

AGREE / PARTIAL (same layer, different move, or vice versa) / DISAGREE. Any outcome is
recorded verbatim in `DUAL_HARNESS_AGREEMENT_BENCHMARK_V0_RESULTS.json` with both lanes'
raw receipts archived. The benchmark is reusable: future waves re-pose new frontier
questions to both lanes under this same protocol and accumulate an agreement series.

## Claim boundary

This measures inter-instrument agreement on one question instance. It is not a
statistical claim about either harness's general reliability.
