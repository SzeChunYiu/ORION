## Mandatory donor subtraction

### TARE / Schillo--Sturm--Quay

Reference: arXiv:2601.05740v4, `TARE: Block Encoding Linear Combinations of Pauli Strings Without Ancilla State Preparation`, last revised 2026-05-13.

Absorbed with zero novelty credit:

- TARE construction itself;
- arbitrary target Pauli strings subject to the donor theorem limits;
- freely chosen mutually anticommuting auxiliary families;
- Tag/Restore construction and stabilizer formalism;
- logarithmic ancilla scaling and width/depth tradeoffs;
- the `2m-1` Uanti Pauli-exponential construction;
- split/composed handling for larger operators;

The candidate may only claim the residual exact joint compiler objective; it may not claim the freedom to choose `R_k` or native matches.

### Direct anticommuting unitary partitioning

Izmaylov/Yen/Verteletskyi and related unitary-partitioning/grouping work owns anticommuting grouping, clique construction and the normalized direct anticommuting-unitary identity. These are donor primitives.

### Clifford / symplectic synthesis

Generic Clifford and binary-symplectic synthesis owns the ability to satisfy/optimize symplectic constraints and realize Pauli-frame transformations. The candidate receives no novelty credit for solving symplectic equations or using binary symplectic state.

Rengaswamy--Calderbank--Kadhe--Pfister, arXiv:1803.06987, is included as an explicit older donor for enumerating/optimizing symplectic realizations of constrained Clifford operators.

### Symphony global BSF compiler

Yang et al., arXiv:2608.11579, submitted 2026-08-12, owns global binary-symplectic simplification and rescheduling for Pauli-exponential Hamiltonian-simulation circuits, including generalized controlled-Pauli Clifford transformations and strong two-qubit-count/depth reductions.

Symphony gets first right of refusal as a downstream/global Pauli compiler where semantics apply. Its published object is not the same fixed-block TARE Tag/Restore joint auxiliary-frame optimization objective.

### Low-ancilla block encodings via Hamiltonian simulation

Zhang--Shao, arXiv:2607.01843, submitted 2026-07-02, provides approximate low-ancilla block encodings derived from Hamiltonian evolution / generalized signal processing, including one-ancilla constructions and alternative depth/ancilla tradeoffs.

This is a stronger/different block-encoding interface donor. It prevents broad claims that the candidate is globally minimal in ancilla or block-encoding cost; it does not own the exact three-term TARE joint compiler object.

### FOQCS-LCU

Fast one-qubit-controlled SELECT and available structure-specific PREP constructions are absorbed as outer-control donors. Generic chemistry coefficient PREP may not be silently costed as zero. The earlier optimistic interface envelope remains controlling evidence.

### Low-gate second-quantized block encoding

### Non-Clifford Fusion

### Controlled time-evolution sign-flip grouping
