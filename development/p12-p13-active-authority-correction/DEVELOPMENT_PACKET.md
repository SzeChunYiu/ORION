# P12/P13 active-authority correction

## Development question

How must the active paper authority change when a later hostile audit proves
that a positive historical terminal was decided by a confounded or entailed
metric, while preserving the historical protocol, runner, result and replay
adjudication byte for byte?

## Roles and independent challenge

- The statistical auditor checks the independent unit, comparator capability,
  estimand and threshold reachability.
- The adversarial methods auditor checks whether the treatment and outcome can
  vary independently and whether the registered hostile worlds press the gate.
- The referee/lifecycle auditor checks that no publication surface continues to
  promote a terminal after its load-bearing evidence is withheld.

The correction is accepted only where all three readings agree on the exact
claim/evidence path and on what narrower claim, if any, remains measured.

## Atomic questions

1. Does P12A compare the number of signals while holding the permitted action
   set fixed?
2. Could either shipped one-axis baseline have reached the winner's achieved
   score under a perfect value of the named mechanism?
3. Does a capability-matched one-signal baseline satisfy P12A's registered
   positive gates?
4. Is P13A's zero `unsafe_reuse` rate contingent on any registered world, or is
   it entailed by `RCS` reusing exactly when `supported` is true?
5. Which P13A quantities remain measurements after the entailed zero-harm claim
   is withheld?
6. Which README, manuscript, readiness and ledger surfaces currently treat the
   affected historical terminals as active authority?
7. Can a machine-readable active-authority record bind the hostile audit and
   stop those surfaces from drifting back to the historical terminal?

## Recovered evidence and defects

### P12A

- The historical runner and V2 replay adjudication are reproducible and remain
  historical evidence.
- `JOINT_FROZEN` may emit four allocations; each named one-axis comparator may
  emit only two. Identical total budget therefore does not imply matched
  capability.
- `ADAPTIVE_STATE_ONLY` achieves `0.463134765625` and has a measured perfect-
  signal ceiling of `0.4754638671875`, below `JOINT_FROZEN`'s achieved
  `0.858154296875`.
- `0.3826904296875` of the `0.39501953125` pairwise margin (96.9%) is beyond the
  shipped baseline's reachable support.
- With the same four-action set on the one- and two-signal arms, the mean gain is
  `0.040771484375`, the worst-family gain is `0.001953125`, and the historical
  gate battery returns `P12A_JOINT_ALLOCATION_SUPERIORITY_GATE_NOT_MET`.

### P13A

- The historical runner and V2 replay adjudication are reproducible and remain
  historical evidence.
- The RCS policy reuses exactly when `supported` is true. The published harm is
  `REUSE and not supported`. That conjunction is false by the policy definition
  for every sampled world.
- Registered certificate perturbations move the RCS action but do not move the
  zero `unsafe_reuse` outcome. The zero is therefore an entailed property of the
  scorer/policy pair, not hostile evidence that RCS avoided a reachable harm.
- A narrower wrong-answer measurement has a real denominator: zero wrong
  answers on the 768 episodes where reuse could have returned an answer.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

1. Add one machine-readable active-authority adjudication per paper. Each record
   binds the historical receipt and later hostile instrument, names the withheld
   claim, preserves the historical terminal, and states the successor required
   for promotion.
2. P12's active authority becomes `P12A_SUPERIORITY_AUTHORITY_WITHHELD` for the
   signal-count mechanism claim. The positive historical terminal remains in
   the unchanged V1/V2 artifacts.
3. P13's active authority becomes
   `P13A_ZERO_UNSAFE_REUSE_AUTHORITY_WITHHELD` for the zero-harm and synthesized
   superiority claims. Narrower descriptive/correctness/cost measurements stay
   visible but do not restore the withdrawn causal/safety synthesis.
4. Update every current README, manuscript, result section, claim ledger and
   readiness decision that promotes the affected claim.
5. Add tests that recompute the hostile facts and require all current
   publication surfaces to point to the new active adjudication rather than the
   historical positive terminal.

## Explicit non-solutions

- Do not edit historical protocols, runners, result receipts or replay
  adjudications.
- Do not rename `CANNOT_CHECK`/withheld authority to `PASS`.
- Do not lower a historical gate after seeing its outcome.
- Do not treat 8,192 or 12,288 sampled episodes as independent study-level
  replications.
- Do not let a narrower descriptive measurement compensate for a failed
  load-bearing gate.

## Prospective successors

- P12B must give every one- and two-signal arm the identical four-action set,
  freeze the policy family and use family/domain blocks as the generalization
  units.
- P13B must independently corrupt certificate/witness validity so unsafe reuse
  is reachable under the RCS policy, and must compare cost on a matched frontier
  rather than combine RCS-only instrument metrics into superiority evidence.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen this packet if any affected publication surface remains positive, if a
historical artifact changes, if a successor reuses outcome-informed thresholds,
or if an independent audit finds that the proposed narrower claim also lacks a
reachable denominator or matched comparator.
