### 3.2 Paper C

Paper C is independent enough for a standalone theorem paper. Its exponential parity construction receives a new exact minimality theorem:

> Let `delta: 2^[q] -> Z`. If every proper upper marginal `sum_{S superset T} delta(S)` vanishes, then `delta(S)=(-1)^(q-|S|) c`, where `c=delta([q])`. Therefore any nonzero trade preserving all proper labeled marginals occupies all `2^q` Boolean cells; after cancellation, each side contains at least `2^(q-1)|c|` columns. The parity construction attains equality.

Thus the exponential trade support is information-theoretically unavoidable, not an avoidable weakness of the construction. The theorem will be accompanied by an additive executable verifier and a standalone manuscript package.

This supplies an independent second compiler family while preserving the explicit boundary that no Qualtran/Qet physical-resource integration has been executed.
