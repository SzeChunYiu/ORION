## Paper-level repair decisions

### P1

Reframe as responsibility-conditioned epistemic reconstruction. Keep the
hidden-formulation failure, limited discriminating subset and lexical-cue audit in
the main narrative. The 2,882 worlds and 400-case successor are exact-contract
tests, not naturalistic diagnostic evidence. Restore broader claims only after
independently authored cases, blinded responsibility labels, native independent
comparators, protected shortcut tests and receipt-bound execution identities.

### P2

Reframe as fail-closed route control. The controlled margin is underpowered, the
external full-system campaigns failed, and the zero-width interval at the failure
floor is a measurement artifact. Exact route-contract success cannot establish
retrieval realism or discovery superiority. A future external claim requires
matched native systems, frozen retrieval snapshots, blinded relevance/closure
judgment, task-level inference and separate infrastructure-failure terminals.

### P3

Reframe as coordinate-preserving canonicalization of already structured records.
Only 13 of 32 cases discriminate the central choice; several ablations are null,
the ideal typed product ties when given the same structure, and raw-text
extraction was not tested. Any broader claim requires raw evidence, separate
extraction and canonicalization scores, blinded multi-annotator gold, a protected
corpus and independently implemented comparators.

### P4

Reframe as non-escalating promotion semantics. Preserve the H3 empty-list failure
as a benchmark-design negative. The 420 mechanical cases and gate-specific
ablations measure specification coverage and terminal expressiveness, not
scientific judgment. Broader claims require independently sampled claims, blinded
expert adjudication, native systems, independently generated attacks and separate
false-promotion, false-blocking, abstention and review-cost estimands.

### P5

Reframe as a protected self-improvement evaluation protocol. Report all three
errors in the 21/24 internal diagnostic. Remove unexecuted result-table shells from
the evidential narrative. No improvement, safety, recurrence or cost claim exists
until a frozen subject, matched unchanged baseline, protected fresh tasks,
independent adjudication and at least one complete propose/change/replay/fresh
cycle have executed.

### P6

Make vertical non-laundering the contribution: a donor-native certificate does
not retain scientific standing after a relevant change unless every declared
continuity obligation has a current witness. Disjoint-frame commutation,
reachability reopening and affected-set monotonicity are supporting lemmas. Fix
the false necessity wording: overlapping write frames permit non-commutation but
do not make it necessary for every transformer. The proof package must include
assumption audits, countermodels, selective invalidation tests, genuinely
different certificate schemas and independent proof review.

### P7

Make the soundness/completeness boundary central. Exact contract matching or a
registered bridge is a decidable sufficient condition for closure transport, not
a semantically complete condition. Separate definitions, assumptions and derived
theorems; prove strict incompleteness, exhibit an unsound relaxation, characterize
registry completeness, and use a genuinely heterogeneous partial bridge matrix.

### P8

Withdraw the claim that 169 successful chains establish heterogeneous
composition: the audited loop repeats one composition and ignores donor variables.
Retain the negative disclosure. A positive theory paper requires explicit
donor-specific embeddings, a donor-sensitive terminal, conservative-embedding
proofs, non-laundering counterexamples, formally independent alternative support
families and mutant-killing cases. Until then the claim is donor-parametric only.

### P9

Own the diagnostic protocol, not a new architecture or universal representation
theorem. Distinguish finite-sample collisions from population non-identifiability;
test alphabet, feature, order, multiplicity, candidate and row permutations where
the representation can express them. Do not infer that neural escalation is
unnecessary from a selected classical grid. Use independently authored/generated
tests and independently reproduce the ceiling, predictions and explicit inference.

### P10

Remove the superiority title and terminal. Define a registered method language,
closure, access/resource model, obstruction certificate and `CANNOT_CHECK`
boundary. Prove certificate soundness and bounded completeness or explicit
incompleteness. Add macro-rejection cases, a verifier-backed genuine-extension
witness, access-model invariance, minimality and an independent checker. Autonomous
invention, transfer and cross-domain superiority remain future questions.

### P11

Center the preregistered P11D falsification and P11H pooled negative. P11E is a
replication of a surviving residual; P11G is only an arm-specific local positive.
The correct conclusion is that task-conditioned state construction can reduce

dependence. Separate data seeds, estimator seeds and technical replays; disclose

### P12

Present an analytical existence result: heterogeneous requirement worlds exist in

### P13

Present a responsibility-carrying-state contract and controlled conformance

## Negative-result conversion rule

“Turn negative into positive” means extracting a valid bounded contribution, not
changing the sign of an observation. Permitted outcomes are:

- a falsified generalization;
- a sharp obstruction or impossibility boundary;
- a mechanism diagnosis;
- a sound conformance limitation;
- a prospectively frozen successor experiment.

The original negative remains in the manuscript and ledger. A repair run is not
an independent scientific replication. If no honest bounded contribution or
protected successor exists, the negative remains negative.

### Wave E — protected evidence
