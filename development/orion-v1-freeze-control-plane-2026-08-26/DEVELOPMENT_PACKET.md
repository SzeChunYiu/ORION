## Development question

How can ORION freeze its first versioned architecture and local formalism while
making it mechanically impossible to confuse internal closure with external
scientific, novelty, publication, physical-quantum, or quantum-advantage
authority?

## Atomic fibers

1. Enumerate each load-bearing component and its typed dependencies.
2. Separate theorem schema, mechanized proof, finite enumeration, empirical
   result, execution receipt, governance rule, and external authority.
3. Give every open issue exactly one final disposition without deleting
   history or laundering `CANNOT_CHECK`.
4. Separate locally repairable gaps from compute, access, custody, rights, and
   independent-review gaps.
5. Freeze machine jobs before execution and forbid manuscript edits by the
   computation owner.
6. Gate P16–P18 so no paper is drafted merely because an issue exists.
7. Make the final terminal a derived checker result, never an author-written
   assertion.

## Expert panel and delegated vetoes

- Formal-methods lead owns theorem identity, assumption closure, countermodels,
  and the final local-formalism condition.
- Systems/reproducibility lead owns component identity, content digests,
  denominator completeness, checker hostility, and CI.
- Quantum-transfer lead owns the Q/QG/QN namespace census, source-to-target
  correspondence, donor matching, and full resource accounting.
- Publication-authority lead owns claim ceilings, external-authority separation,
  and manuscript admission.
- Execution/empirical lead owns prospective freezes, raw manifests, negative
  controls, resource ledgers, and immutable result packets.

No one role can issue the final terminal alone.

## Incumbent mechanics and negative history recovered

The control plane reuses rather than replaces:

- OSTC status non-fungibility and execution/science noninterference;
- theorem-identifying harnesses and registered negative twins;
- dynamic epistemic-state separation of terminal labels from next actions;
- content-addressed receipts and claim-authority ledgers;
- donor-first frontier comparison and no hidden scalarization;
- the P1–P15 twenty-five-packet mixed-result discipline;
- the repository's branch-lane and immutable-history protocol.

The main negative controls are historical: vacuous biconditionals, circular
gold, hidden-answer leakage, denominator drift, stale hash bindings, metadata
promoted as execution, same-owner replay promoted as independence, and
bounded internal results promoted as external authority.

## Saturation assessment

### Knowledge

The internal repository already contains the required status algebra, receipt
semantics, theorem ledgers, issue coordinators, and quantum structural donor
families. The bootstrap does not claim novelty over configuration management,
formal verification, scientific workflow provenance, or release manifests.
A later novelty review is external.

### Search universe

The first tranche covers the merged foundations, discovery V1–V3,
dynamic-epistemic state, execution integrity, P1–P15, ORION-Q, quantum/QG/QN,
and P16–P18 candidates. It is deliberately incomplete until the GitHub issue
and tree censuses are content-bound.

### Formulation

The strongest formulation is not “all science is solved.” It is:

> architecture/specification/local-proof and implementation closure for the
> declared V1 classes, with every external or heavy successor explicitly
> ledgered and no authority laundering.

## Challenge to the saturation basis

The apparent repository-wide saturation may be false because:

- issue titles can remain open after results merge;
- multiple namespaces can encode the same quantum object;
- result files can exist without current-main binding;
- a green finite reference can coexist with scientifically blocked jobs;
- full-suite greens can omit a shard or environment identity;
- publication surfaces can disagree with active authority;
- an unmodeled representation may sit outside the current component graph.

Therefore the bootstrap records partial coverage and refuses the freeze
terminal.

## Why prior searches could miss material knowledge

1. issue/PR/result identity is many-to-many;
2. quantum labels (`Q`, `QG`, `QN`, `ORION-Q`, `RG`) are not a semantic
   namespace;
3. merged work can remain administratively open;
4. branch forests can contain superseded but apparently stronger statements;
5. large generated artifacts can evade text-centric census;
6. external rights/custody blockers cannot be inferred from code presence.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen the architecture or a component whenever:

- a current-main path implements a load-bearing judgment not represented in
  the component graph;
- two components claim the same authority with incompatible semantics;
- a theorem loses an assumption, falsifier, or content binding;
- a supposedly complete issue census misses a page or PR/issue distinction;
- a checker mutation can promote `OPEN_BOOTSTRAP` to `FROZEN`;
- a quantum bridge hides oracle/compiler/verification resources;
- a paper surface exceeds the authority ledger;
- a later exact-source CI run contradicts a retained host receipt.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

A small additive package of canonical JSON ledgers plus one standard-library
checker can bootstrap the control plane without touching protected execution
or manuscript paths. The checker must reject incomplete coverage, unknown
issue dispositions, dependency cycles, missing job packets, paper promotion,
invalid hashes, and any unearned final terminal.

## Falsifiers

The hypothesis fails if the checker can be made green after:

- deleting an issue row or component dependency;
- inserting an unknown disposition;
- creating a dependency cycle;
- setting the final terminal while one coverage flag is false;
- changing a bound package byte;
- marking P16–P18 draftable before prerequisites;
- removing a required execution packet;
- promoting quantum structure to physical or advantage authority.

## Implementation boundary

This tranche is additive. It edits no historical result, P1 finalizer/Slurm/
LUNARC/custody/OID path, existing manuscript, active claim authority, or
computation packet. `paper_authority_delta = NONE`.
