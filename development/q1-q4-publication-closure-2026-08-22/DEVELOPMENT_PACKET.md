This packet grants no scientific, novelty, theorem, causal, or superiority
authority. It defines questions and release tests for a publication audit.

## Non-negotiable integrity rule

A negative, null, contaminated, donor-absorbed, invalid, or `CANNOT_CHECK`
result remains immutable in its original scope. A new method or harness repair
creates a new version and requires a prospectively frozen successor test. It
cannot turn an earlier result into a positive result.

## Atomic publication questions

### Q1

1. Can the all-n support-two statement be reconstructed from definitions and a
   complete proof contained in the submission, without relying on an authority
   string or an internal JSON receipt?
2. Does an independently executed checker reproduce every finite lemma and
   witness used by the proof?
3. Is the contribution significant under Quantum's current criteria after the
   TARE donor freedoms receive zero novelty credit?
4. Does the paper distinguish a theorem about a frozen structural objective
   from physical circuit-cost or chemistry-performance claims?

### Q2

1. Does the declared transition graph validate only artifact identity, or does
   it test the semantics required to admit a successor edge?
2. Is the 51-receipt publication universe outcome-independent, or is it a
   retrospective narrative denominator?
3. Can the current evidence support only feasibility/auditability, or can a
   protected comparison support effectiveness?
4. Is Artificial Intelligence the correct target after exact scope and
   field-advance criteria are applied?

### Q3

1. Are responsibility labels falsifiable and mutually exclusive from the
   deferred event used to score them?
2. Is Lane B a genuinely separately frozen measurement instrument, or does a
   question-specific manifest/controller encode the expected answer?
3. Does the custody chain establish pre-outcome separation beyond source-file
   import separation?
4. Are the historical D2/D3 defects consistently reported against the repaired
   current harness without modifying the original series?
5. Is TMLR in scope for this protocol paper?

### Q4

1. Do matched comparisons hold serialized information fixed while changing
   only the intended representation or policy factor?
2. Are positive worlds constructive stress tests conditioned on a trap, or do
   they define an external population on which comparative performance can be
   estimated?
3. Can information, representation, and consumer-policy effects be separated
   in a factorial successor design?
4. Is there a nontrivial theorem or protected external task distribution that
   can support a general result beyond authored examples?
5. Is TMLR in scope for this framework?

## Portfolio-wide publication questions

1. Does every manuscript have a chaptered TeX source, bibliography, build
   command, clean-room dependency bundle, compiled PDF, and visual inspection
   receipt?
2. Are repository paths, branch/commit history, script names, receipt filenames,
   and internal project labels moved to Methods, availability, an artifact
   appendix, or repository documentation unless scientifically load-bearing?
3. Does every headline claim have a claim/evidence/boundary/alternative/
   resolution record?
4. Are all references verified at field level and are all nearest-work claims
   based on read primary sources rather than metadata?
5. Are author, affiliation, contribution, funding, conflict, licence, archive,
   AI-use disclosure, and data/code availability fields either complete or
   explicitly marked as author actions?

   the manuscript rather than absent from the project.
2. Q2 may be a formal safety-invariant paper rather than an effectiveness paper.
3. Q3's main negative may partly be a scoring-construct defect rather than an
   observed failure of the instruments.
4. Q4's strongest publishable object may be a theorem or adversarial-test
   catalogue rather than six empirical superiority claims.
5. TMLR may be a scope mismatch for Q3/Q4 even if its rigor-first acceptance
   philosophy is attractive.
6. Current harness code may already repair defects that the frozen Q3 manuscript
   still lists as open.

## Reopen triggers

- A complete proof or counterexample changes Q1 theorem authority.
- A blind semantic audit changes Q2 edge-validity evidence.
- A newly frozen Q3 cohort uses exclusive forecasts, a fixed controller, and
  protected outcomes.
- A Q4 factorial or external-task study changes the representation/policy
  attribution.
- A newly located primary source absorbs or materially narrows any claimed
  residual.
- A clean build or replay disagrees with a claimed result.
- The active PR #829 changes the manuscript or package under audit.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

The publication repair should be additive until the active PR #829 lane is
resolved. This lane may add audit reports, clean-room verification tools,
chaptered TeX package infrastructure, and prospectively frozen successor
protocols. It must not silently edit PR #829-owned scientific masters or result
artifacts.

The smallest sufficient repair sequence is:

1. freeze and inspect the exact candidate;
2. produce three independent reports and a post-review synthesis;
3. verify references, statistics, claims, replay, and packaging;
4. distinguish manuscript defects from missing science;
5. implement non-overlapping infrastructure and self-contained proof/material
   that do not change scientific outcomes;
6. file exact active-lane findings for conflicting manuscript edits;
7. freeze any new scientific successor before execution;
8. release only claims that pass the corresponding prospective or proof gate.

## Stop conditions

- Do not claim submission readiness while a central P0 issue is open.
- Do not claim superiority without a fair prospective comparator and protected
  outcome.
- Do not replace a donor tie or null with a weaker comparator merely to obtain a
  win.
- Do not call public code open source without an explicit licence.
- Do not call an artifact independently verified when only an author-authored
  receipt was inspected.
