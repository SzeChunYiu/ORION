# Editor synthesis: Q1-Q4 publication closure

Date: 2026-08-22

Candidate reviewed: PR #829 at
`158fcb08b612ffc82f5a5d2bed4917409084ded8`

This synthesis follows three independent reviews and a cross-review round. The
reviewers had deliberately different remits:

1. **Formal-validity reviewer:** theorem completeness, construct validity,
   estimands, causal language, and claim authority.
2. **Novelty and venue reviewer:** nearest-work subtraction, residual
   contribution, significance, and target-journal fit.
3. **Reproducibility reviewer:** claim-to-artifact recovery, protocol chronology,
   executor attribution, TeX/PDF portability, and deterministic replay.

The reports were frozen before cross-sharing. In the reconciliation round all
three reviewers agreed on every P0 finding below. Precision corrections are
incorporated here; the synthesis does not overwrite the frozen reports.

## Editorial decision

**Decision: major revision; current claims do not establish submission
readiness.**

The four papers contain credible and in several places unusually well-bounded
scientific objects. The fixed-ref audit reproduced the principal deterministic
computations. That does not make the manuscripts top-tier ready. Each paper has
at least one central gap between the evidence recovered and the claim or venue
case presented to a reader.

No current paper supports superiority in the field. A strong exact theorem can
meet a top journal's bar without an empirical superiority claim; a methods or
systems superiority claim requires prospectively frozen, matched comparisons
against the strongest donors. Negative, null, contaminated, and donor-absorbed
results must remain part of the record.

## Q1 editorial disposition

### What survives review

For the exact frozen grammar and raw support-count objective, the evidence
supports a serious candidate theorem: an optimum exists with frame support at
most two. Exact `8 < 9` and `5 < 6` witnesses show that natural support-one
subfamilies are insufficient. These negative results improve the theory paper
by locating a sharp structural boundary.

The all-n executable regenerated 43,688 Lemma-B tuples, zero support-3-through-8
failures, the exact four support-two boundary failures, 18,432 Lemma-E cases
with zero violations, 210 verified descents, and 70 equality stress instances.
Its regenerated JSON differed from the committed receipt only in declared
runtime.

### What blocks release

The article contains a proof sketch, not a reviewer-checkable proof. The
complete definitions, zero-sum lemma, local inequality, exchange accounting,
well-founded descent, and identification with `D++` live in a development
protocol. A journal reader must not need repository archaeology to verify the
headline theorem.

The external novelty case is also incomplete. The donor matrix must subtract
exact/optimal Clifford synthesis, symbolic-Pauli optimization, Pauli-collection
transformations, commuting and anticommuting Pauli grouping, unitary
partitioning, and block-encoding access-model trade-offs. The paper must show
why none of those results already implies the support-two closure theorem under
this coupled grammar and objective.

### Release gate

1. Define the feasible set, objective, Tag and Restore conditions, exchange,
   support, and `D++` without internal project identifiers.
2. State and prove every lemma, including the exact exchange map and cost
   change; remove any retain/remove-subset ambiguity.
3. Prove termination and exact equivalence between the support-at-most-two
   family and `D++`.
4. Incorporate the finite exhaustive certificate and checker as an archival
   supplement with a locked environment and independent proof-only review.
5. Complete primary-source donor subtraction and explain significance as a
   structural theorem that provides evidence of a very significant advance
   beyond the state of the art, not a physical-resource result.
6. Place the submitted version in arXiv's quant-ph section, as required by
   *Quantum*.
7. Build chaptered TeX and a clean *Quantum*-ready PDF.

No new experiment is needed for the core theorem. Claims about circuit depth,
T count, width, fault-tolerant cost, chemistry advantage, or compiler
superiority require a new protected comparison.

## Q2 editorial disposition

### What survives review

The checker reproducibly binds an author-declared archive: 51 receipts, 23
included nodes, 28 exclusions with reasons, 13 asserted successor edges, 10
negative or partial nodes, and seven standalone nodes without invented
successors. This is a useful machine-readable record of one research programme.

### What blocks release

The 51-receipt set was fixed after completed work and was selected to represent
the desired lineage. Immutability after declaration does not make it a
prospectively complete or outcome-independent denominator. The validator checks
paths, counts, relation strings, and selected required edges. It explicitly
does not validate scientific causality. Thus it preserves assertions; it does
not demonstrate that a predecessor scientifically licensed its successor.

One such graph does not establish improved research quality, productivity,
novelty, time-to-discovery, or an advance to the overall field of artificial
intelligence. The nearest-work section must subtract CEGAR/CEGIS, truth
maintenance and belief revision, Registered Reports and preregistration,
adaptive experimental design, autonomous laboratories, workflow provenance,
event sourcing, model lifecycle governance, and model-based diagnosis.

### Release gate

For a bounded case-study paper:

1. Call the object an **asserted transition graph**, not a causal graph.
2. Disclose that the archive denominator is retrospective and author-declared.
3. Bind each showcased edge to predecessor protocol/outcome, diagnosis,
   donor-refusal record, successor freeze/outcome, chronology predicates, the
   exact licensing clause, and independent adjudication status.
4. Formalize and verify only genuine safety invariants: append-only negative
   persistence, no retroactive promotion, and freeze chronology.
5. Remove effectiveness, productivity, non-cherry-picking, and superiority
   implications.

For an AIJ regular article, add either a nontrivial general formal result or the
prospective comparison frozen in `Q2_PROSPECTIVE_COMPARATIVE_PROTOCOL.md`, with
its exact primary estimand, strongest comparator, and programme-cluster
power/precision gates satisfied.

## Q3 editorial disposition

### What survives review

The chronology and scientific analyzers are recoverable. The QG19 53-row panel
found no support-three gap. The QG20 complete registered `n=1/n=2` census of
39,489 instances found zero label mismatches. Both analyzer outputs reproduced
byte-identically. Invalid and contaminated slots were retained rather than
silently replaced.

The defensible scientific negative is:

> On the complete registered low-order census, the anticipated
> objective-rescale mismatch did not occur; all-n invariance remains open.

### What blocks release

The registered Q3-R2 labels are not mutually exclusive. The statement that a
change *can* matter and should be retested is not falsified by zero low-order
mismatches. Q3-R1 similarly records an epistemic status rather than a
falsifiable forecast. The move score is nondiscriminating because performing a
registered test can count as aligned under either scientific terminal. The
headline “both diagnoses were wrong” is therefore not established by the
current scoring construct.

The Lane-B controller contains question-specific branches and consumes
manifests that already encode the decisive typed observation. This is not an
evaluation of one stable, general instrument. Replacement Lane-A receipts name
a provider and model but lack provider response IDs, raw request/result chains,
signed metadata, and an exact consumed-receipt manifest. They establish a
committed host judgment, not auditable model execution.

The manuscript also describes D2/D3 as historical defects while the current
source contains a later repair at
`a195690c602b2493a7205c2b380b1774f376eea7`. The historical limitation remains
valid; the repaired code is a new, unevaluated instrument version and must be
reported separately.

### Release gate

For the present historical case audit:

1. Remove the “wrong diagnosis” and reliability/corroboration headlines.
2. Separate epistemic status, mutually exclusive forecast, probability,
   action, and deferred decision value.
3. Describe Lane B as two frozen question-specific rules, not a validated
   general typed controller.
4. Weaken Lane-A wording to host-attested judgment unless raw authenticated
   provider receipts can be bound.
5. Bind every unit to its exact historical instrument commit; separately state
   the later D2/D3 repair and its targeted tests.
6. Preserve Q3-R2, contamination, and historical D2/D3 defects unchanged.

Any claim about diagnostic accuracy, agreement value, reliability, repaired
harness performance, or superiority requires the new cohort frozen in
`Q3_P1_PROSPECTIVE_PROTOCOL.md`.

## Q4 editorial disposition

### What survives review

All eight runners execute successfully and preserve their qualitative gates and
manuscript-level values. Four result files reproduce byte-for-byte. Four show
only last-bit floating-point drift under Python 3.12.13/NumPy 2.3.5, so the
scientific conclusions recover but universal byte-identity does not.

The constructed worlds can serve as deterministic seeded synthetic adversarial
unit tests or constructive examples. The ideal-VOI tie, model-selection donor
absorption, and no-advantage remint regime are valuable boundary results and
must remain central.

### What blocks release

Git history supports a pre-result protocol freeze for N4-A and N4-B only. For
N4-C, N4-D, N4-E, and N4-F3, protocol, runner, and result first appear in the
same commit. This does not prove manipulation, but it cannot substantiate the
claim that all six studies were prospective.

Positive worlds were authored to punish selected shortcuts and could be
invalidated if the hostile control did not bite. Such conditioning establishes
constructed counterexamples, not population-level comparative performance.
The “matched information” contract also permits either removing a type/scope
field or changing the consumer rule. Those are different interventions.

The general thesis may be an instance of Blackwell informativeness/garbling,
value of information, sufficient statistics, active learning, dependency
tracking, provenance, truth maintenance, or typestate. Each world needs a
strongest-donor comparison before a broader theoretical novelty claim survives.

### Release gate

1. Label N4-A/B as prospectively frozen and C/D/E/F3 as co-developed
   retrospective deterministic constructions unless independent pre-result
   custody evidence exists.
2. Call all six adversarial unit tests or constructive examples, not empirical
   proof of general superiority.
3. Separate field availability, field inference, and policy consumption in a
   factorial design.
4. Distinguish semantic/numerical replay from byte identity; pin Python, NumPy,
   BLAS, platform, and canonical numeric rounding.
5. Add a machine-readable study manifest binding protocol, runner, result,
   environment, seed, and source digests.
6. Complete classical and current-system donor subtraction and retain all ties,
   nulls, and no-advantage regimes.

External, prospective, or superiority claims require the fresh study frozen in
`Q4_FACTORIAL_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.md`.

## Packaging and repository-leakage decision

No Q1-Q4 candidate is submission-complete. At the reviewed ref, Q1 has no TeX
package. Q2-Q4 contain Pandoc wrapper templates but not chapter-level source.
The target CI is not green:

- Q1 Quantum preflight: failed during PDF build on an undefined control
  sequence.
- Q2 AIJ package: failed during PDF build on an undefined control sequence.
- Q3 and Q4 TMLR packages: failed because `lmodern.sty` was absent.
- generic render preflight: failed because `lmodern.sty` was absent.
- V2 figure workflow: failed before producing the verified bundle.

Passing publication-manifest checks do not override failed PDF/package jobs.
Each paper needs one canonical `main.tex`, one TeX file per chapter, a verified
bibliography with claim-local citations, a clean dependency installation, a
compiled venue-style PDF, page rendering and visual inspection, and an
anonymity audit where required.

The review PDF must read as an independent paper. Internal project banners,
branch names, cuts, receipt filenames, script paths, P/Q ownership language,
and repository workflow details belong in an artifact appendix or repository
documentation unless scientifically load-bearing.

## Portfolio release rule

No readiness label, checker PASS, terminal string, receipt count, or successful
replay grants theorem, novelty, causal, model-attribution, generalization, or
superiority authority. Release requires the claim-specific evidence named in
`PUBLICATION_GATE_MATRIX.json` and a clean package build.
