# Primary-source donor screen and venue criteria

Screen date: 2026-08-22

This is a hostile search seed, not a completed novelty determination. Every
paper below was verified on its primary arXiv abstract page. A final manuscript
must read the full text, trace references and citations, and record exact claim
subtraction in a donor matrix.

## Venue criteria applied

### Q1: Quantum

Quantum asks whether the work is technically correct, clear, and reproducible,
but it is also highly selective: correct incremental work is below threshold.
Original research must make a very significant technical or conceptual
contribution and provide evidence that it goes significantly beyond the state
of the art. The journal does not require an empirical field-superiority result;
a sufficiently significant exact theorem can suffice. The submitted version
must already have appeared in the quant-ph section of arXiv.

- Author instructions: <https://quantum-journal.org/instructions/authors/>
- Editorial policies: <https://quantum-journal.org/editorial-policies/>
- Referee instructions: <https://quantum-journal.org/instructions/referees/>

### Q2: Artificial Intelligence

The proposed regular-article route requires a contribution that matters to the
overall field of artificial intelligence, not only an internally auditable
representation of one completed programme. Final formatting, declarations, and
article-type rules must be resolved from the official guide.

- Guide for authors:
  <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence/publish/guide-for-authors>
- Journal page:
  <https://www.journals.elsevier.com/artificial-intelligence>

### Q3 and Q4: TMLR

TMLR's principal gates are that claims are supported by accurate, convincing,
and clear evidence, and that the work is of interest to some part of its
audience. SOTA performance and a conventional novelty bar are not required, but
originality/non-overlap, learning-centered scope, anonymous review, and the
mandatory style remain requirements.

- Journal: <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/>
- Author guide: <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html>
- Acceptance criteria: <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html>
- Editorial policies: <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html>
- Submission instructions: <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/submissions.html>

## Q4 donor families and direct overlaps

The headline proposition that erasing decision-relevant state can reduce
utility may be an instance of classical decision theory rather than a new
general theory. Mandatory classical subtraction includes Blackwell comparison
of experiments and garbling, value of information, sufficient statistics,
decision-theoretic active learning, truth maintenance, incremental computation,
dependency tracking, provenance, typestate, refinement types, capability
security, and proof-carrying data.

## Donor-matrix release test

For each headline claim, the final matrix must contain:

```text
claim_id
candidate_object
donor_object
candidate_assumptions
donor_assumptions
candidate_objective
donor_objective
exact_or_empirical
overlap
subtracted_content
residual_contribution
full_text_pages
primary_source_url
source_version
fulltext_digest
access_date
reviewer
verification_date
```

A residual remains `OPEN` until at least two independent readers agree that the
nearest donor does not subsume it.
