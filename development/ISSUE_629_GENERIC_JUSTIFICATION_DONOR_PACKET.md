# Development packet — issue #629 generic justification donor subtraction

## Development question

Does a generic proof/justification-relevant standing representation—of the kind suggested by TMS, dynamic assurance and proof-carrying authorization—already solve the DPAIR-1..4 future-standing families without bespoke projection-schema refinement?

If yes, the ORION-specific state-coordinate refinement claim must be struck and the generic justification representation becomes the mandatory donor baseline.

## Atomic fibres

1. represent current scientific standing as one or more typed justification conditions rather than a scalar label;
2. define a small generic condition language over future transition events, not over benchmark family names;
3. compile DPAIR-1..4 lineage into that condition language;
4. evaluate conditions under the future transition to decide whether current standing remains valid or must reopen;
5. freeze the condition interpreter before evaluating the unseen obligation-provenance family;
6. verify zero-shot correctness on DPAIR-4 without adding a new condition operator;
7. compare against the bespoke F1 three-coordinate projection and F2 four-coordinate projection;
8. if the donor representation closes all families, record `GENERIC_JUSTIFICATION_DONOR_SUFFICIENT` for this state-representation atom;
9. do not claim novelty for the condition language or justification graph;
10. preserve full lineage as the fidelity/reconstruction ceiling.

## Incumbent mechanics and negative history

- TMS/JTMS records reasons for beliefs and retracts dependents when justifications fail.
- Dynamic assurance cases maintain claims, evidence and arguments under system/environment/evidence change.
- Proof-carrying agent actions/authorization bind admissibility to explicit justification/evidence objects.
- NEURA retains scientific claims only when supported by checkable evidence/domain axioms.
- The current ORION F1->F2 projection experiment is a CEGAR application using bespoke coordinate families; it is not entitled to beat a generic justification parent by construction.

## Parent-domain recovery

Mandatory parents include Doyle TMS/JTMS, ATMS, proof-carrying authorization, dynamic assurance/change-impact, scientific proof-carrying workflows, policy-version-bound authority and artifact/evidence dependency systems.

The donor baseline should be granted a generic condition vocabulary powerful enough to express the *registered* future validity contracts without benchmark-family labels.

## Saturation assessment

The DPAIR-1..4 families all share the same abstract structure:

```text
current standing is justified by a reason
future transition changes context
reason either remains valid or becomes invalid
standing preserves or reopens accordingly
```

Therefore a generic justification donor is the correct strongest-parent challenge before interpreting CEGAR coordinate refinement as incremental value.

## Challenge to saturation basis

The donor could be unfairly weakened if:

- condition operators are bespoke to each pair family;
- DPAIR-4 requires adding a new interpreter operator after outcome access;
- ORION sees full lineage while the donor sees only current summary;
- donor justifications omit authority/obligation scope that is explicitly available in lineage;
- donor is denied the same future transition event;
- the condition language is non-executable or secretly calls protected gold.

## Miss hypotheses

A mature logic/assurance representation may be more expressive than the small V0 condition language. A V0 donor positive therefore weakens ORION; a donor negative would not establish ORION superiority until a stronger parent language is also tested.

## Reopen triggers

Reopen if:

- the generic donor needs a benchmark-family label to predict standing;
- DPAIR-4 requires a post-outcome language extension;
- the donor cannot reconstruct its condition from the same lineage available to ORION;
- a richer proof/justification donor trivially subsumes the V0 condition language;
- future families require semantic changes to the condition language itself.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

A stdlib-only generic condition interpreter with a fixed vocabulary such as

```text
ALWAYS_VALID
INVALIDATE_ON_CHANGE(domain, prior_scope)
INVALIDATE_WHEN_CAPABILITY_ADDED(capability)
VALID_FOR_TARGET(domain, target)
```

can compile DPAIR-1..4 lineage before evaluation and achieve 100% held-out future-standing accuracy, including zero-shot DPAIR-4, without adding a new condition operator.

If supported, terminal for this exact state-representation atom:

`GENERIC_JUSTIFICATION_DONOR_SUFFICIENT`.

The no-man's-land then moves to recursive changes that alter the justification semantics/condition language itself, or to resource-bounded scientific performance where generic/full proof replay is too costly.
