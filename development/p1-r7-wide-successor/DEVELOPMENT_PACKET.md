## Development question

Can a role-free, source-grounded ORION responsibility-discrimination policy outperform every frozen donor-complete comparator by a scientifically meaningful margin on a wide, independently acquired naturalistic frame, under identical visible evidence, protected evaluation, adequate cluster-level power, and an equally sized source-disjoint changed-host replication?

The target is a new successor claim. It is not a rerun of P1.H1 until a favourable sample appears, and it cannot supersede the immutable P1.H1 `NOT_SUPPORTED` terminal.

## Atomic development questions

1. Can acquisition enumerate an outcome-blind frame with enough independent sources in every responsibility-class × domain cell?
2. Can each admitted source supply a same-source adverse/control pair whose two members share the scientific objective and artifact family?
3. Can every candidate and comparator receive byte-equivalent visible scientific evidence, with gold, pair role, source class, query identity, evaluator disposition, and protected outcomes absent from the candidate boundary?
4. Can a changed open-weight semantic host be bound to an exact revision and run without a deterministic keyword table co-designed with the corpus?
5. Can the scorer reconstruct every endpoint without importing P1 R6 scoring or terminal functions and remain outside candidate-author custody?
6. Is the independent unit the admitted source pair, rather than correlated episode rows or repeated model samples?
7. Does the candidate beat every frozen primary comparator by more than the registered superiority margin while satisfying every non-compensatory harm and integrity gate?
8. Does the complete result reproduce on an equally sized, source-disjoint acquisition frame and changed host?

## Negative history and incumbent evidence

- P1 V1 H1 is immutable: ORION and the registered baseline each achieved 1/48 root-task successes. That historical claim is `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- R2–R4 failed before a valid naturalistic outcome because acquisition could not populate the frozen routes or matched controls. These are acquisition `CANNOT_CHECK` records, not scientific zeros.
- R6's repaired 2020 deterministic-host run passes its internal gates after role-free identifiers, but remains conditional on source-disjoint replication, semantic-host execution, and external evaluator custody.
- R6 is not a clean fairness confirmation: ORION receives `pair_evidence.source_claim` as `evidence_note`, while B3 receives only dossier and guarded probes. Its `same_visible_evidence` declaration is therefore not mechanically established.
- R6's deterministic provider is a corpus-coupled keyword host. It cannot establish performance of a semantic reasoner.
- R6's comparator uses a private probe-cost stopping penalty and returns `UNRESOLVED` without probing on many episodes, while ORION deterministically executes up to two probes and is not penalized on the endpoint for doing so. A shared probe ceiling is not a matched compute or incentive envelope.
- The historical live V1 provider adapter discards the provider's response text and always emits `reframed=False`; without a typed response parser it is a transport/cost probe, not a live scientific answer adapter.
- The frozen R6 2019 protocol has 24 independent pairs and four unresolved sources. It remains useful for its bounded predecessor claim, but it is underpowered for the R7 wide claim and is not reused as R7's primary evidence.
- Post-outcome repairs do not retroactively create protected attribution. Every predecessor artifact and digest remains append-only.

## Bounded saturation assessment

### Knowledge and mechanism coverage

The incumbent set covers static P1-X, search/evidence repair, representation/interface repair, implementation/environment repair, measurement/evaluator repair, objective/model-class revision, problem-boundary revision, abstention/unresolved handling, greedy restoration, uniform and cheapest-first probing, uncertainty/VOI policies, and donor-complete composition.

This is sufficient to freeze a wide comparative successor. It is not a novelty certificate and does not support a claim about science in general.

### Search-universe coverage

The acquisition frame must cover six registered responsibility strata and at least four declared scientific domains. Source eligibility, query families, rank limits, duplicate identities, exclusion roots, pair matching, and all skip reasons are frozen before any candidate outcome is accessed.

### Formulation coverage

The primary claim concerns source-grounded public scientific failure/control pairs admitted by the frozen frame. It does not cover private laboratory work, undisclosed failures, all scientific disciplines, or arbitrary autonomous agents.

## Challenge to the saturation basis

The frame could appear wide while remaining falsely flat if:

- four nominal domains share one benchmark or measurement pipeline;
- dossiers encode responsibility through vocabulary, source identity, ordering, action availability, or opaque-handle implementation;
- one comparator lacks evidence or actions available to ORION;
- repeated episode/model rows are treated as independent sources;
- acquisition replaces harmful sources or stops after favourable interim outcomes;
- a keyword host memorizes corpus language rather than using the visible evidence;
- per-stratum non-inferiority is imposed on cells too small to admit even one loss;
- zero-width intervals arise from identical outputs or an evaluator at its measurement floor;
- a self-authored evaluator is described as protected independent custody.

## Hypotheses for missed knowledge

H-MISS-1: a current self-revising or deep-research agent may subsume the proposed policy when given the same actions and evidence.

H-MISS-2: scientific diagnosis may require a responsibility coordinate outside the six registered strata; such a finding reopens the ontology rather than being forced into the nearest class.

H-MISS-3: apparently domain-general gains may be driven by one domain or acquisition template.

H-MISS-4: dossier construction may itself perform the high-level diagnosis, leaving little work for the candidate.

H-MISS-5: source-level paired success may conceal systematic harm on controls, unresolved cases, or one protected stratum.

## Frozen R7 sampling design

### Primary frame

- 192 independent matched source pairs: eight manuscript-mandated causal families × four scientific domains × six distinct sources.
- At least 64 additional independent genuinely unresolved sources: four domains × sixteen distinct sources. With zero false resolutions, 64 supports a one-sided exact 95% upper bound below 5%; a zero-event bounded gate at a smaller `n` would not support that population-rate claim.
- One admitted study/artifact family contributes at most one independent primary cluster. DOI aliases, companion papers, shared datasets, and repository mirrors are deduplicated prospectively.
- The same source must contain the adverse and matched control condition.
- All 256 primary sources must be admitted or dispositioned under the frozen acquisition rules before candidate scoring begins.
- No outcome-dependent replacement, deletion, query change, cell reweighting, or early efficacy stop is permitted.

### Replication frame

- An equally sized 192-pair + 64-unresolved source-disjoint frame.
- No normalized URL, DOI, stable artifact identity, title/first-author/year identity, or official repository identity may overlap the primary frame or P1-X/R1–R6 exclusions.
- The replication uses a changed semantic host and separately bound evaluator implementation/custody.

### Independent unit and weighting

The independent unit is the admitted source cluster. The 32 domain × causal-family cells receive equal weight in the primary estimand. The eight families map prospectively to the six revision decision classes but remain separate sampling and harm strata. Repeated model samples, pair members, probes, or bootstrap draws are not independent units.

The proposed design is a large-effect design. For a paired contrast near 0.20 against a 0.05 margin with discordance near 0.40, approximately 135 pairs provide 80% power and 179 provide 90%; 192 preserves the balanced 8 × 4 × 6 grid. If the true contrast is 0.15 under the same assumptions, the requirement is roughly 410 pairs. Before the execution freeze, an outcome-blind simulation under the final max-T procedure must bind the power/sensitivity table; invalid or protected R6 outcomes may not calibrate it. This is a planning calculation, not an assurance of a positive result.

## Frozen primary estimand and hypotheses

For source cluster `s` and arm `a`, define:

`S_s(a) = 1`

only when arm `a` achieves evaluator-confirmed protected final scientific success on both the adverse and matched-control members without violating any integrity or harm gate; otherwise `S_s(a) = 0`.

For each frozen primary comparator `b`:

`Delta_b = E_frame[S_s(ORION_R7) - S_s(b)]`.

Primary nulls are `H0,b: Delta_b <= 0.05`. The wide superiority claim requires every max-T simultaneous one-sided 95% lower bound from the source-cluster, family/domain-stratified paired randomization/bootstrap procedure to be strictly greater than `0.05`. The comparator set is fixed before outcomes and no observed "strongest" baseline may be selected post hoc. The global intersection-union audit also rejects only if every comparator-specific level-0.05 null rejects. Failure of one contrast cannot be compensated by another.

The same complete rule must pass in the source-disjoint replication. A positive primary without a positive replication is `PASS_PENDING_REPLICATION`, never the wide terminal.

## Frozen comparator requirements

- P1-X/static policy with hidden responsibility unavailable;
- greedy immediate-restoration policy;
- uniform probing;
- cheapest-first probing;
- uncertainty/VOI proxy;
- donor-complete policy with the complete registered action set;
- strongest runnable external or self-revising research agent available at freeze time, bound to the same evidence, action, model, retrieval, and budget envelope.
- forced-budget and matched-schedule donor-complete variants, so a private stopping penalty cannot create an artificially weak comparator.

If the strongest external comparator cannot be executed fairly, R7 may support only a claim explicitly scoped to the runnable registered comparators. It may not silently omit that comparator and retain the wide wording.

## Non-compensatory gates

- zero gold, source-class, query, pair-role, evaluator-disposition, protected-outcome, path, ordering, or action-availability leakage;
- byte-equivalent candidate-visible scientific evidence for every arm;
- identical admissible action and probe budgets, with every access charged;
- identical model-call, token, retrieval, verifier/evaluator-call, tool-call, and wall-clock envelopes, or a prospectively frozen cost-adjusted frontier reported for every arm;
- complete denominators, including errors, abstentions, unresolved cases, and harmful outcomes;
- no false resolution of genuinely unresolved sources;
- no harmful lower-level skip or unnecessary high-level escalation beyond the frozen familywise bounds;
- simultaneous per-domain and per-responsibility harm intervals using adequately sized strata, not pointwise n=1–2 zero-loss rules;
- operator coverage and native lineage checks must execute and pass;
- protocol, corpus-frame, host, candidate, comparator, scorer, and environment digests must match the pre-outcome binding receipt;
- independent evaluator custody is required for claim authority.

Secondary endpoints and mechanism hypotheses are gated after the primary family and use a prospectively frozen Holm or closed-testing procedure. They cannot rescue a failed primary claim.

## Frozen implementation hypothesis

If responsibility discrimination has general source-grounded value beyond donor-complete composition, then on the balanced 192-pair naturalistic frame ORION R7 will exceed every frozen primary comparator by more than 0.05 in cluster-level protected paired success, without violating any non-compensatory gate, and the result will repeat on the equally sized source-disjoint changed-host replication.

No protocol guarantees this hypothesis will pass. A guarantee would require biased acquisition, weakened baselines, relaxed margins, or post-outcome selection and is incompatible with the paper's scientific claim.

## Frozen hostile tests

- remint and permute episode/source/query/pair identifiers without changing any arm result;
- permute pair order and action order;
- inject gold-correlated dossier cues and require the isolation audit to fail;
- invert probe observations and verify decisions change only through accessed probes;
- give one arm one extra evidence byte and require fairness refusal;
- build one immutable candidate-visible episode byte payload, pass the identical payload digest to every arm, retain all provider transcripts, and reject any per-arm evidence augmentation;
- remove, duplicate, or silently drop a cluster and require denominator failure;
- exceed a probe/action budget and require failure before scoring;
- substitute a protocol, corpus, host, arm, or scorer digest and require binding refusal;
- provide identical candidate outputs across distinct arm digests and require zero-resolution refusal;
- run label permutation and negative controls;
- mutate each non-compensatory gate and show a hostile fixture is admitted only by the mutation;
- prove the independent scorer imports neither R6 scoring nor R6 terminal functions;
- rerun the same inputs and require byte-identical receipts.

## Reopen and stopping rules

Reopen before outcome access if acquisition cannot populate the balanced frame, a domain is only nominally distinct, visible evidence cannot be equalized, a stronger comparator cannot be bound fairly, the semantic host cannot be pinned, evaluator custody cannot be separated, or any hostile test fails.

After outcome access, R7 is immutable. A valid adverse result is retained verbatim. Any successor must have a new protocol identifier and change a scientifically material causal ingredient; no margin relaxation, hard-cell deletion, endpoint switch, baseline weakening, or harmful-source replacement is allowed.

The programme may stop claiming wide P1 superiority if adequately powered, source-disjoint successor families saturate without benefit or establish a rigorous lower bound. Historical labels remain queryable even after a later positive successor.
