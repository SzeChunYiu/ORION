# Results: P3 coordinate-necessity atlas extension v1

Executed 2026-08-21 against the protocol frozen in `FREEZE_2026-08-21.md`, which
was written before any case was built or any arm re-run. Reported as it came
out, including the two arms that stayed negative and the one place the build did
not conform to its own freeze.

## 1. The two arms now measure something

`python -m orion.study.p3.public_reference_audit`, same instrument both sides.
**Before** is the frozen `public-reference-v1.1-confirmatory` atlas (n=32,
untouched). **After** is `coordinate-necessity-v1` (n=56: those same 32 cases
plus 24 added ones).

| arm | before treated | before changed | before | after treated | after changed | after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `remove_referent` | 32 / 32 | 0 | FAIL | 56 / 56 | 0 | **FAIL** |
| `remove_construct` | 19 / 32 | 0 | FAIL | 43 / 56 | 0 | **FAIL** |
| `remove_measurement` | **0 / 32** | 0 | CANNOT_CHECK | **24 / 56** | **4** | **PASS** |
| `remove_temporal_context` | **0 / 32** | 0 | CANNOT_CHECK | **24 / 56** | **4** | **PASS** |
| `remove_modality_polarity_attribution_discourse` | 19 / 32 | 6 | PASS | 43 / 56 | 6 | PASS |
| `force_compatibility_without_obstruction` | 32 / 32 | 6 | PASS | 56 / 56 | 14 | PASS |
| **overall** | | | FAIL (exit 3) | | | FAIL (exit 3) |

Every prediction in `FREEZE` §6 came out exactly as recorded, including the
overall `FAIL`.

**What the two repaired arms show.** `remove_measurement` and
`remove_temporal_context` are `PASS`/`COORDINATE_LOAD_BEARING`: each altered the
compared inputs on 24 of 56 cases and moved 4 decisions, an effect rate of
0.167 per treated case at a resolution of 1/24 = 0.042. On all four differ cases
per coordinate the full system answers the gold relation and the ablated system
answers `COMPATIBLE` — the removal makes the system wrong, which is what
"necessary" was supposed to mean. Both arms were `CANNOT_CHECK` before, with a
treated set of zero.

The evidence is thin and the artifact says how thin: 4 decisions per arm, at a
resolution of 1/24. A coordinate shown load-bearing on 4 constructed cases is
established as load-bearing *in the rule*, not as frequent in scientific text.

**What the two unrepaired arms show, unchanged.** `remove_referent` stripped a
coordinate populated on 56 of 56 cases and moved no decision;
`remove_construct` stripped one populated on 43 of 56 and moved no decision.
Both stay `FAIL`/`COORDINATE_NOT_LOAD_BEARING` — real negatives, now at finer
resolution (1/56 and 1/43, from 1/32 and 1/19). Per `FREEZE` §1 no case was
added on which those two coordinates differ, precisely so that this repair could
not convert a published negative into a pass.

On the extended atlas ORION scores accuracy 1.0 (56 of 56) against the flat
comparator's 0.75. **That number is not evidence and must not be quoted as
any.** The added cases' gold is derived by a rule that agrees with
`compare_meaning`'s own decision procedure on these two coordinates, so ORION
answering them all is a property of the construction. See §5.5.

The false-split guard cannot be repaired by any corpus:
`exact_coordinate_baseline` returns only `COMPATIBLE` or `UNRESOLVED` and has no
branch that separates, so its false-split denominator is zero on every atlas.
That is a comparator-design defect, out of scope here per `FREEZE` §1. The
abstention guard is likewise still empty: no gold-`UNRESOLVED` case was added,
also by declaration.

## 3. Identifiability: did the repair ship a construction cue?

`orion.study.p3.atlas_identifiability`, nine declared probes, `max_recovery =
0.0`, every gold relation audited one-vs-rest, three corpora, two splits — all
fixed in `FREEZE` §5 before any of it ran.

| corpus | primary split (in-sample) | secondary split (hash parity) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **extended atlas (the shipped artifact, n=56)** | **PASS on all 4 labels, best informedness 0.0 on all 9 probes** | FAIL on `DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT`: `case_family` at 0.786 |
| added cases alone (n=24) | FAIL: `ordinal_quartile` at 0.375 (`COMPATIBLE`), 0.300 (`DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT`) | FAIL: `ordinal_quartile` at 0.583, `case_family` at 0.500 (`DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT`) |
| parent atlas alone (n=32, frozen, inherited) | FAIL: `ordinal_quartile` at 0.513 on both labels | PASS, best informedness 0.0 |

Read against `FREEZE` §5.3 and §5.5:

- **On the artifact that ships, under the split declared primary, nothing
  recovers anything.** Every probe returns informedness 0.0 for every label,
  including `coordinate_arity` and `coordinate_missingness` — the P4-shaped cues
  a repair that populates a coordinate is most likely to introduce. No probe was
  flagged high-cardinality on the extended atlas.
- **Every failure above sits on one to two positive eval cases.** The extended
  atlas's parity failure is one `DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT` case on the eval half:
  the parity fit-half of the `same_construct_different_measurement` family
  happened to carry a `DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT` plurality, so the lookup predicts
  it for the whole family (1 true positive, 6 false positives). This is the
  split-sampling thinness `FREEZE` §5.2 named as the reason in-sample governs.
  It is also a real limitation and is recorded as one in §5 below.
- **The added-cases-only failure is a tie-break at n=24.** Restricted to the 24
  added cases, an ordinal quartile holds 6 cases; a 2/2/2 tie is broken toward
  the alphabetically greatest label, which is `DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT`. It does
  not survive into the 56-case atlas, where quartiles hold 14. By `FREEZE`
  §5.3's rule this is nonetheless **a cue present in the new cases**, and is
  reported as one rather than argued away.
- **The parent atlas leaks under the in-sample split, and that is a new finding
  about a frozen artifact.** `ordinal_quartile` recovers `CONTRADICTORY` at
  informedness 0.513 on `public-reference-v1.1-confirmatory` (4 signatures over
  32 cases — coarse, not memorisation): the emission order of the frozen atlas
  carries the label, because its `CONTRADICTORY` cases cluster in the file. It
  recovers nothing out of sample. Adding 24 cases dilutes it to 0.0 in the
  extended atlas. This was not introduced by this repair; it was found by
  running the check on the parent as a baseline, which `FREEZE` §5.3 required
  for exactly this purpose.

## 4. One non-conformance, found by the check and fixed against the freeze

`FREEZE` §3.4 required all 24 added cases to share one `case_id` length,
separator count **and alphabetic prefix**. The first build used a 16-character
hex digest, whose leading non-digit run is not constant: it emitted **seven**
distinct prefixes (`coordinate-synth-`, `coordinate-synth-bd`,
`coordinate-synth-dfbd`, …), and the in-sample audit read them as a shortcut
cue recovering `COMPATIBLE` at 0.375 and `DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT` at 0.450 on the
added cases, 0.214 and 0.481 on the extended atlas.

That is a build defect against the frozen specification, not a protocol
revision, so it was corrected: the digest is now rendered as 16 zero-padded
decimal digits, making the prefix constant by construction. Nothing else moved
— no gold, no coordinate, no count, no family, no discipline — and the arm table
in §1 is byte-identical before and after the correction, since the comparison
rule never reads an identifier. The superseded build is kept beside the current
one under `*_v0-nonconforming-case-ids.*` so the sequence stands on the record,
and `shape_invariants` is now a build-time blocker so the same class of
non-conformance cannot ship silently again.

## 5. Limitations, stated with the result

1. **The 24 added cases are synthetic.** Their gold follows from
   `SYNTHETIC_COORDINATE_STANDARD.json` by one deterministic rule, not from an
   upstream expert corpus; MUSE, SciFact and SciSchema are not reachable from
   this environment. The atlas establishes that the two coordinates are
   load-bearing in `compare_meaning`; it establishes nothing about how often
   such pairs occur in public scientific text, and it may not be substituted for
   the public-reference atlas in an external-validity claim. `BUILD_REPORT.json`
   carries this sentence in `external_validity`.
2. **Four differ cases per coordinate is thin.** Resolution 1/24 per arm; two
   `DISTINCT_MEASUREMENT` cases land on the parity eval half. A label that
   occurs in exactly one `case_family` is recoverable from the family token
   under any split whose fit half happens to favour it, which is what the
   secondary-split failure in §3 is. Widening the added stratum, or spreading
   each gold relation across more than one family, would close it.
3. **The false-split comparator and the abstention denominator are still
   empty**, by declaration, and are not repaired here.
4. **The frozen parent atlas's ordinal-order leak (§3) is unaddressed.** It
   belongs to `public-reference-v1.1-confirmatory`, which this work does not
   modify.
5. **The added cases are an ablation denominator, not an accuracy benchmark.**
   Their gold is derived from the standard table by a rule that coincides with
   what `compare_meaning` does with those coordinates, so the full system is
   correct on all 24 by construction. That is exactly what an ablation arm
   needs — the arm's question is whether *removing* the coordinate moves the
   decision, which is a property of the system and not of the gold — and it is
   exactly what an accuracy claim must not be built on. No accuracy, false-merge
   or superiority number computed over this atlas may be reported as evidence
   about ORION's competence on the added cases.
