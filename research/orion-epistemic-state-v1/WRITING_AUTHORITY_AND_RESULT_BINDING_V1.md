# Writing Authority and Result Binding V1

The manuscript-writing lane owns all P1–P15 prose, claims, figures, tables, novelty interpretation, and journal adaptation. Computation sessions own frozen protocols, raw outputs, resource ledgers, and machine-readable result packets only.

A result may fill a manuscript placeholder only when its binding packet names the exact job, endpoint, comparators, resources, case denominator, leakage/censoring/precondition tests, raw digests, transfer result, terminal, and authority ceiling.

Positive, null, adverse, donor-equivalent, lower-bound, and `CANNOT_CHECK` outcomes are equally valid inputs. The writing lane computes the maximal supported claim antichain and preserves counterexamples. A new boundary-escape successor receives a new identity and freeze.

No computation session may update an active paper authority. No manuscript may call an internal second implementation external independence.
