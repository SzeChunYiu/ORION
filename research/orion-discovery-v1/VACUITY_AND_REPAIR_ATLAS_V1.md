## Five recurring defect families

### 1. Premise imposed by construction

**Example:** P5/P14 T19.

The structural runner set two transcripts equal. It could therefore only report indistinguishability. The result restated a premise rather than testing whether an honest improvement and evaluator-gaming candidate can become observationally indistinguishable under a self-controlled evaluation game.

**Repair:** `DISC-T19-01` must generate the observations from candidate/evaluator interactions. The equal-transcript collision must emerge, and a protected-root condition must produce a contrasting positive regime.

### 2. Hard stratum absent

**Examples:** P9 and P10-01.

- P9's 4×4 intervention matrices made greedy set cover optimal on all 1,820 cases. Exact minimum identification was tested, but the greedy-vs-exact comparator claim was not.
- P10's first two formula families used clause/variable ratio 0.8 and produced no UNSAT formulas. Checking remained easy, but the hard synthesis/decision side was absent.

**Repair:** make hard-stratum membership a precondition checked before scoring. P10-02 did this and obtained 797 UNSAT cases. P9 receives a separately frozen 5×6+ successor.

### 3. Reference defined from candidate

**Example:** first CM/T13 draft.

The declared composite was `g∘f`, then the harness tested whether it equalled `g∘f`.

**Repair:** independently enumerate the declared composite plus perturbations. The repaired atlas produced 6,377,292 noncommuting cases, proving the reference relation could disagree.

### 4. Hidden cause used as detection output

**Example:** first P15 key-compromise arm.

The harness incremented failure directly from a compromise flag. It measured fixture membership rather than whether compromise was detectable from observable signals.

**Repair:** derive detection only from candidate-visible observables. The repaired arm produced 3 detected and 28 undetected capability sets.

### 5. Correct theorem overgeneralized across loss geometry

**Example:** P12/T17.

The half-gap proof shape from 0/1 terminal error was carried into real-valued action loss. A third action could hedge both worlds and beat half the cross-action gap.

**Repair:** replace the universal bound with the exact fibrewise Bayes/minimax geometry in `EPISTEMIC_DECISION_GEOMETRY_V1.md`. T12 remains the no-hedge 0/1 special case.

## Programme implications

1. **Every future theorem needs an alternative family.** Without it, “agreement” has no exact scientific meaning.
2. **Preconditions are part of the theorem interface.** A hard stratum discovered after scoring invalidates the old job; it motivates a successor.
3. **Vacuity detection is a positive result.** It reveals a missing scientific coordinate and should be published in the failure history.
4. **Independent implementations do not cure a shared non-identifying design.** Two checkers can agree perfectly on a tautology.
5. **External blockers are not compute jobs.** Proof review, novelty review, custody, and blinded adjudication require different principals.
6. **Counterexamples can strengthen the programme.** The T17 failure produced a more general exact theory rather than a cosmetic patch.
