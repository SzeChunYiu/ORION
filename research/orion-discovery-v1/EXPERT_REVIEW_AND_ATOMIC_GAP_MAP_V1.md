## Atomic missing capabilities

### A. Autonomous tension generation

Current code assesses caller-supplied tensions. Missing:

- raw residual/anomaly/opportunity mining;
- distinction between noise, saturation, contradiction, and unification opportunity;
- question-value estimation under scientific cost;
- generation of discriminating observations.

### B. Mutable scientific question space

Current goal evolution does not yet establish that ORION can generate a materially better scientific question rather than repair a supplied one.

Required:

- question origin;
- incumbent-question dominance or limitation;
- new observable consequences;
- no novelty from wording alone.

### C. Generative representation and primitive invention

Current concept records validate supplied declarations. Missing:

- generator over new typed objects and correspondences;
- semantic type checking;
- old-closure reducibility referee;
- hidden consequence and transfer.

### D. Instrument/intervention language

Missing general objects:

```text
InstrumentCandidate
InterventionCandidate
ConstructionFeasibilityReceipt
MeasurementErrorContract
SimulatorToPhysicalCorrespondence
```

### E. Theory-identifying experiment synthesis

Current thought experiments score registered predictions. Missing:

- generation of the intervention itself from an alternative family;
- minimum-cost partition refinement;
- guarantee that the experiment separates load-bearing hypotheses;
- safety/rights constraints.

### F. Model chronology and contamination

Source chronology exists. Model chronology, embeddings, caches, package databases, and training exposure need explicit contracts.

### G. Prospective external custody

The completed execution wave showed that some remaining blockers are not compute blockers. They require a party outside the programme. The framework must represent that boundary rather than repeatedly attempting same-owner substitutes.
