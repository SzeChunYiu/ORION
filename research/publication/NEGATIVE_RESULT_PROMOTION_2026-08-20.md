# ORION negative-result promotion audit — 2026-08-20

## Objective

Convert every adverse, null, saturated, tied, blocked, or invalid result in Papers 1–4 and 6–8 into the **widest different scientific claim that the evidence actually establishes**. This is not relabeling failed performance as success. It is claim-type correction: a failed performance hypothesis may establish a falsification result, a causal localization, a transport-validity firewall, a benchmark non-identifiability theorem, a necessity law, a monotonicity law, or an architecture-portability theorem.

## Review team

- **Formal methods / theorem reviewer:** asks whether finite enumeration plus matched countermodels support necessity, sufficiency, iff-style repair laws, conservativity, monotonicity, or representation independence.
- **Empirical methods reviewer:** separates performance inference from diagnostic, transport, stage-localization, benchmark-construction, and prospective-repair authority.
- **Hostile top-venue reviewer:** attacks every widened claim for hidden universal quantifiers, weak baselines, benchmark artifacts, and post-outcome reinterpretation.
- **Literature / positioning reviewer:** grants the strongest modern donor mechanisms and asks what ORION relation survives after donor engulfment.

## Promotion ladder

1. **Failed broad superiority -> falsification + prospective repair.** A failed historical hypothesis can establish that the original decision rule was too broad; a separately frozen repaired successor can then support the corrected law.
2. **Stage-local negative -> failure localization.** If retrieval works materially better than final inclusion, the scientific result is that downstream screening/processing is a distinct loss-bearing stage.
3. **Invalid external campaign -> transport-validity firewall.** If candidate contracts are valid but provider/transport validity fails, the positive result is that the evidence-governance layer correctly refuses to mint either a positive or a null scientific conclusion.
4. **Exact ideal-product tie -> representation-independence theorem.** If an information-equivalent decentralized/product implementation matches every decision, the semantics are portable and architecture-independent.
5. **Full repair succeeds + every proper subset fails -> necessary-and-sufficient repair law in the registered model.** The conjunction of affected coordinates is sufficient because it restores; it is necessary because every strict subset fails.
6. **Saturated metric with a perfect shortcut -> benchmark non-identifiability.** The benchmark cannot identify the intended competence from the score because a construction artifact is observationally sufficient.
7. **Matched widening chains fail -> monotonicity / non-widening law.** Authority may be preserved or narrowed by composition; widening requires an explicit bridge/coercion.
8. **Donor ownership -> compositional universality, not novelty loss.** The stronger the donor layer becomes, the cleaner the residual ORION claim becomes: which relation allows a local success to discharge a different scientific obligation?

**Widest positive claim.** **Failure is not reformulation authority. In the registered families, high-level scientific reformulation is governed by protected necessity evidence that rules out lower-level repair; this corrected escalation law survives powered replication and exact heterogeneous revision-responsibility testing.**

The negative H1 is therefore not part of the performance headline; it is the falsification event that identifies the unsafe rule the successor replaces.

**Widest positive claim.** **Scientific closure is a governed proof obligation over material routes and processing obligations, and evaluation transport is itself an authority-bearing route. P2 localizes where acquisition pipelines lose evidence and fails closed when provider validity makes an external comparison scientifically non-transportable.**
