# AB R20 — extensional production-registry transfer protocol

Status: `PROTOCOL_FROZEN__PRODUCTION_REGISTRY_REQUIRED`

## Source subject

Select one real optimizer/compiler version and bind its complete source tree, build flags, dependencies, target semantics, feasible representation, and registered objective. The production move registry must be executable or mechanically extractable from the bound source. A benchmark generator or optimizer that enumerates final candidates without exposing semantics-preserving reducing moves is not a registry.

## Completeness obligation

For the smallest complete production domains, enumerate every feasible state and every semantics-preserving strict-support reduction admitted by the production representation. Reconcile the source-extracted move set against that extensional relation.

A PASS requires:

1. every declared production move preserves semantics;
2. every move is objective-nonincreasing and strictly decreases the termination measure;
3. every weak proof-language move has an exact production lift;
4. every extensional reducing move is generated by the registry;
5. deletion of any registered move or addition of any omitted admissible move is detected by a hostile control;
6. all critical local peaks required by the normal-form claim are joined;
7. the maximum weak terminal has a production preimage;
8. that witness is irreducible under the complete production registry.

## Search-value experiment

After completeness is established, compare native production search with certificate-aware search under matched semantics, target instances, resource caps, compiler flags, and stopping rules. Report wall time, peak memory, explored states, generated moves, objective, timeout, and first disagreement. Certificate preprocessing and checker time must be charged.

## Interaction registry

If reducing cross-component moves have support two, materialize the exact graph whose vertices are component certificate units and whose edges are all reducing pairs. Verify the surviving budget `alpha(H)` and interaction tax `tau(H)`. For higher-arity moves, use the exact hypergraph and transversal receipt. A sampled or hand-written interaction list cannot support a production lower bound.
