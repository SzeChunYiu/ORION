## Theorem NQ-R20.1 — replay-order noncircularity

Let `T` be a numerical terminal to be established by an independent replay, and let `S(T)` be a structural specialization whose hypotheses include `T`. Then `S(T)` may be used after the replay as a cross-language consistency check, but it cannot be used to prune the replay subject, accept a candidate, define the denominator, or verify `T`.

### Proof

Any such use makes acceptance depend on a statement whose authority is exactly the replay's conclusion. A false candidate satisfying the circular assumption could then pass without independent evidence. Post-terminal comparison does not alter the replay subject or acceptance rule and is noncircular.
