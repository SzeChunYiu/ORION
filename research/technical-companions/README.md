# ORION technical companions

Technical companions preserve implementation-level derivations, historical working manuscripts, claim ledgers, and evidence packets that support the five flagship papers but are not independent publication identities.

A technical companion may be cited by a flagship paper for provenance or implementation detail. It may not be counted as an additional ORION paper, inherit a flagship number, or acquire publication authority independently of the paper-specific evidence gates.

Current companions:

- `mechanics-of-mechanics-v1/` — historical Shadow mechanics programme, dissolved into the scientific ownership of ORION-P1 and ORION-P5 while preserving its original manuscript/evidence archive.
