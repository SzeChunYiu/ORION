\section{Problem}
A recursively improving research system cannot be developed safely as an unstructured sequence of feature additions.  Each step in the research workflow is itself a research object: it has inputs, outputs, state, observations, actions, transition laws, invariants, objectives, metrics, uncertainty, resources, failure modes, storage, provenance, dependencies, verification obligations, parent disciplines, search-coverage obligations and a bounded stopping rule.

ORION therefore applies its own research principle to its mechanics.  For a mechanic $i$, development is modeled as
\[
\text{atomize}\to\text{question}\to\text{search}\to\text{absorb}\to\text{reconstruct}\to\text{challenge}\to\text{specify}\to\text{benchmark}\to\text{learn/reopen}.
\]
The process is deliberately open to discovering that its current decomposition is incomplete.  A flat literature route does not by itself establish saturation; newly discovered parent disciplines, representations, failure variations or verification obligations reopen the affected coordinate.
