# Literature-based discovery

## Why ORION studies this field

Literature-based discovery (LBD) studies how useful relationships can remain latent across disconnected bodies of literature. It is a direct parent discipline for ORION's bridge/JUMP behavior and for the question `how can relevant public knowledge remain invisible to a search process?`

## Initial transfer candidates

- disconnected-literature / A-B-C bridge search;
- explicit distinction between retrieval and discovery;
- bridge explanation rather than raw co-occurrence;
- open vs closed discovery formulations;
- search-space reduction without pretending the reduced space is complete.

All are research inputs. None becomes an ORION mechanic without a scoped contract and benchmark.
