# Measurement Science / Psychometrics

Status: `OPEN_RESEARCH_PROGRAM`.

Why relevant: ORION evaluates models, instruments, benchmarks and its own method. It therefore needs mature concepts for construct validity, method effects, generalizability, item/condition variation, invariance and latent measurement rather than treating observed benchmark scores as transparent capability measurements.

Initial objective: map psychometric/measurement concepts onto ORION's existing measurement, evaluator, benchmark and authority coordinates; determine which are equivalent, complementary or genuinely missing before changing the core.
