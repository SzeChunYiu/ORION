## External parent-domain findings

### Information retrieval / evidence synthesis

Empirical evidence-synthesis work shows that search system/database choice changes recall and that errors or missing terms can materially reduce retrieval. Relative-recall benchmarking provides one way to test search-string sensitivity against known relevant publications. These findings motivate explicit route/source coverage and benchmark-based search audits rather than treating one retriever as the knowledge universe.

### Open-world / diversified retrieval

Recent work on diverse-perspective retrieval and coverage-conditioned RAG treats coverage across perspectives/subquestions as a distinct objective. Query expansion can help in some regimes and hurt in others, so ORION should maintain heterogeneous route families rather than assuming more generated query text is always better.

### Autonomous scientific research evaluation

Recent autonomous-research benchmarks separate wide literature discovery from deep target finding and show large remaining failure rates. Hidden-target/rediscovery designs are useful for ORION because they test whether the system can recover scientific core and evidence rather than merely produce research-like prose.

### Software architecture

Parnas's information-hiding account of modular decomposition and ports/adapters architecture support decomposing ORION by volatile responsibility and keeping runtime vendors outside the research kernel. This motivates provider protocols for LLM, retrieval, verification, and later tools.

### Literature-based discovery

Swanson's `Undiscovered Public Knowledge` gives an important parent formulation: relevant public knowledge can remain undiscovered when logically connected fragments live in disconnected literatures, and retrieval cannot be positively verified as globally complete. ORION therefore requires a `LITERATURE_BRIDGE` search family alongside parent-discipline and adversarial-omission routes.

### Information-search stopping

Information-search research distinguishes query abandonment/patch switching from stopping the entire task. ORION's bootstrap saturation gate currently governs task-level finalization more strongly than within-route stopping. This becomes an explicit search-control research fiber rather than being silently treated as solved.

### Scientific ignorance / known unknowns

Work on machine-readable scientific ignorance shows value in representing explicit challenges, limitations and future directions as objects that can guide interdisciplinary exploration. ORION's generic residual algebra may need a richer ignorance representation; this remains proposal-only until benchmarked.

## Scoped growth decision

The broad autonomous-kernel knowledge program remains **NON-FLAT** because the later search rounds opened route-level stopping and ignorance-representation fibers.

The narrower engineering question `is the minimum kernel ready for a provider-neutral LLM integration and known-world governed solve?` is answered **yes by interface/test evidence**, not by a claim of global research saturation. The new fibers do not change the integration port or authority boundary, but they explicitly prevent upgrading this verdict into general autonomous-research maturity.
