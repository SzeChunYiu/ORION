## Proposed step-specific contract

**Transition model — two loops with a hard ordering.**

```text
fast:  action -> observation -> TaskEpisode (frozen BEFORE root-cause interpretation)
slow:  episodes -> contrastive analysis -> lesson proposal
       -> replay/transfer validation -> scoped method update
```

The episode freeze precedes interpretation so a later explanation can never rewrite the evidence it explains. A failed fresh transfer bounds or contradicts a lesson — it is never averaged away. Promotion creates a new lesson version; source episodes are immutable.

**Storage.** Raw episodes are the canonical memory root; consolidation is a *defeasible derived view* (incumbent cites the agentic-memory failure mode: rewriting consolidated memory degrades performance even when experiences were useful). Same append-only-events + derived-view discipline as CROSS.AUTHORITY and ANSWER.STORAGE.

**State — lesson authority ladder (bounded, non-automatic).**

```text
CANDIDATE < VERIFIED_LOCAL < CONDITIONALLY_REUSABLE < PROOF_BACKED
SUPERSEDED = lineage, not a higher state
```

A candidate cannot become reusable because a generator says it sounds general; reusability requires independently registered verification plus a fresh successful transfer under the relevant contract (or a proof-backed route).

**Mathematics — routing, never truth.** Experience statistics may set an operator routing score (incumbent gives an illustrative cost/verification-debt/success-rate form with policy coefficients), under the boxed structural rule:

```text
experience-conditioned priority  ≠  epistemic authority
```

A high success rate cannot weaken a proof obligation; a historically poor operator remains usable when a structural witness restores a previously broken assumption (no self-reinforcing blacklists).

**Observability — memory performance as process variables.** The retrieval fibre must answer, per use: was a relevant prior failure present in the bound universe? retrieved? rejected on which structural witness? did the chosen operator already carry a failure boundary here? was a lane omitted by a stale coverage index? did context compilation drop a mandatory atom under budget? These six are the cell's observable vector — not one opaque retrieval score.

**Invariants.** Contradictory episodes remain attached to any strategy motif they contradict; motif applicability is context-indexed; frequency alone never promotes a motif.
