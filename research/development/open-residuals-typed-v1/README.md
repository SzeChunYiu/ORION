# Open residuals, typed by responsibility

The licensing matrix built for #137 says responsibility determines authority:
only a formulation or search-universe responsibility licenses rewriting the
question. Applied here to this session's own open problems, because the same
discipline should govern what I do about them. Typing first is not ceremony —
it is what stops evidence problems being "solved" by redefining the question,
which is the failure mode the matrix exists to refuse.

## The residuals

| 8 | `EVIDENCE_BOUND` support/influence unestablished | **EVIDENCE** | acquire evidence | axes explicit, gated, resolver named |

## What the typing changes

**Exactly one residual is FORMULATION, and it is the only one that licenses a
reframe.** That is #2. The suite cannot resolve H1's margin, so the question as
posed cannot be answered by this instrument — and the honest move is to change
what is claimed, not to keep running the instrument until a number appears.

Every other residual would have been made *worse* by reframing. #1 is missing
evidence: redefining the study to avoid needing a provider would be scoping the
claim to fit the obstacle. #3 and #6 are execution faults in my own tooling —
the question is fine, the tools are sloppy. #4, #5 and #7 are measurement and
representation limits, where the correct response is to state the bound and name
the experiment that would move it.

Six of eight are therefore **not mine to solve by thinking harder about them**.
Three need an external input, three need mechanical repair, and only one needs
the question rewritten. That distribution is itself the useful output: the
temptation under pressure is to treat every blocker as a formulation problem,
because reframing is the move that always feels available.

## Bounded saturation

Two residuals are terminal rather than open, and saying so is the stopping
criterion rather than an excuse:

- **#7** — with 48 cases and an unbounded space of text statistics, some
  statistic always separates. Two exploitable rules were found and removed; the
  floor is published. A third round relocates the residual rather than removing
  it, which is why it was declined.
- **#5** — the boundary is not in the public text. No rule can recover a
  distinction the case does not contain, and tuning a strength to match gold on
  three cases is fitting, not detecting.

The remaining six have named resolvers, which is the test a residual has to pass
to stay open rather than be closed as unknowable.
