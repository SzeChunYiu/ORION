## Why this pass exists

A prize-scale theory cannot be obtained by renaming ORION mechanisms. The strongest test is to find adjacent work that already owns the attractive abstractions and subtract them. This pass therefore searches specifically for theories of sufficiency, warrant transmission, proof-obligation discharge, claim authorization, evidence adequacy, authority narrowing, and target-bound reuse.

The result is deliberately constraining: several tempting ORION headlines are **not novel enough** at programme scale. The residual becomes narrower, but more scientifically meaningful.

## Nearest prior-art collisions

### 1. Epistemic transmission already studies when warrant moves across inference

The philosophy of epistemic transmission distinguishes closure of justification from genuine transmission of justification/warrant and develops conditions under which warrant does or does not transmit across an inferential link. ORION therefore cannot claim the generic discovery that local justification sometimes fails to transmit to a conclusion.

**Residual:** machine-checkable scientific workflow transitions are broader than belief-to-belief entailment. P1/P2/P3/P4/P6/P7/P8 include reformulation permission, route/task closure, identity authorization, promotion, post-change standing, closure transport, and heterogeneous authority composition, many of which are not merely deductive entailments between propositions.

### 2. Formal verification already discharges proof obligations

Proof-obligation generation/discharge is foundational formal-methods terminology and machinery. Recent verification work continues to reason explicitly about when an obligation may be discharged and when it remains open.

**Residual:** ORION must not claim the term or generic concept of obligation discharge. Its target is the semantics connecting heterogeneous *scientific* objects and status transitions to evidence, identity, scope, epoch, support, blockers, and authority across an autonomous research lifecycle.

### 3. Clinical AI already separates evidence sufficiency and claim authorization

The 2026 `Evidence-Graded Decision Authorization` preprint for clinical AI explicitly separates evidence extraction, sufficiency grading, and claim-level authorization, with claim-type-specific admissibility thresholds. This is a close conceptual parent for P4-like reasoning.

**Residual:** ORION cannot claim the generic principle that evidence sufficiency should gate a claim. The programme-level target must show that the same typed relation governs *different scientific object classes and transitions*—not only output claims in one clinical decision setting—and must recover paper-local laws without custom decision logic.

### 4. Legal-agent work already distinguishes valid records from adequate findings

Recent agentic-AI oversight work states that valid runtime records do not automatically establish legally operative findings and requires type/relation information connecting records to the target determination.

**Residual:** ORION cannot claim the general insight that a log/record needs target-relevant semantics. Its candidate contribution is a cross-scientific calculus spanning inferential, evidential, identity, closure, revision, and authority transitions, with explicit uncertainty/revocation/revalidation semantics.

### 5. Statistical decision theory already owns generic sufficiency

Blackwell sufficiency and related decision theory characterize when one experiment/information structure is at least as informative as another for decision problems.

**Residual:** P1 Objective-Basis Sufficiency and the transfer Abstract-Signature Decision Sufficiency result are bounded instantiations, not ownership of sufficiency theory. Their role is to motivate a separation between information sufficient for a decision and authority sufficient for a scientific status transition.

### 6. Security already owns no-authority-amplification / narrowing principles

Capability security and current agent-authorization work formalize scope attenuation, blast-radius monotonicity, and composition soundness.

**Residual:** P8 must not claim generic authorization monotonicity. Its result concerns target-bound *scientific discharge* across heterogeneous authorization and scientific-verification objects, including scientific type, evidence support, blockers, epoch, protected coercion, and non-inversion between permission and proposition support.

### 7. Causal inference already owns transportability

Causal transportability formally asks when causal knowledge can move across populations/settings.

**Residual:** P2's transport result concerns whether the *evaluation/acquisition process itself* has authority to support a comparison, plus material route and processing obligations. It is not a new general theory of causal transportability.

## Surviving foundational target

The most defensible cross-paper breakthrough target after donor subtraction is not “authority conservation” and not “evidence sufficiency.” It is a **cross-lifecycle scientific-status factorization**.

### Scientific Status Transition Factorization Conjecture (SSTF)

For a collection of locally valid artifacts/judgments `A` and a target scientific obligation `o`, an admissible status change should factor into four non-substitutable conditions:

1. **Native validity** `V(A)` — each relied-upon local artifact is valid for the object it actually certifies.
2. **Target information sufficiency** `S(A,o)` — the available information is sufficient to decide the target relation under the registered scientific model.
3. **Target-bound discharge entitlement** `E(A,o)` — an explicit scientific inference/identity/closure/promotion/revalidation/transport/authority bridge is entitled to connect those valid inputs to the exact target object, scope, content identity, and epoch.
4. **No unresolved load-bearing blocker** `B(A,o)` — every blocker required by the target relation is refuted/discharged; unresolved blockers remain explicit rather than being averaged away.

The prospective theorem target is

`AdmissibleStatusChange(A,o) <=> V(A) AND S(A,o) AND E(A,o) AND B(A,o)`

for a precisely defined class of scientific workflows.

This is currently a **conjecture/theorem target**, not an established universal iff statement.

### Why this is stronger than no-amplification alone

No-amplification says what may *not* happen. SSTF also says what is positively sufficient. It predicts that a scientific transition succeeds exactly when all four factors hold and fails when a load-bearing factor is removed. That permits paired necessity/sufficiency theorems, representation-independence results, and experimentally falsifiable ablations.

### How existing ORION results instantiate the factors

- **P1:** strong local diagnosis/performance is not enough; responsibility/necessity plus protected admission supplies target-bound reformulation entitlement. Objective-Basis Sufficiency shows that richer generation is unnecessary once target decision information is sufficient.
- **P2:** route-local stop may be valid and decision-sufficient for a route while material-route coverage/processing entitlement for task-global closure is absent. Invalid provider transport blocks comparison authority.
- **P3:** semantic/representational compatibility may be strong while target scientific identity entitlement is absent.
- **P4:** provenance/verification/custody/generic authorization can all be locally valid while target promotion entitlement is absent; H3 shows that a score may also fail target-information sufficiency because the evaluator is non-identifying.
- **P6:** donor certificates remain natively valid; complete affected scientific revalidation supplies the missing target bridge, with full repair sufficient and every strict subset insufficient in the registered model.
- **P7:** donor navigation validity is preserved; complete affected closure repair and exact intermediate bridges govern target closure restoration/composition.
- **P8:** donor-local authority is conserved; compatible/narrowed scientific type or protected coercion plus complete support and blocker clearance determines target discharge across the complete registered 13×13 donor-pair product.

## What would be genuinely surprising

The programme becomes much more than a taxonomy if the factorization produces **new predictions before experiments are run**. High-value prospective predictions include:

1. a strong new scientific-agent system will tie ORION whenever it exposes an SSTF-sufficient interface, even if its architecture is radically different;
2. a system with excellent local verification but one deliberately omitted discharge-entitlement coordinate will fail only the matched target transitions and retain all native-valid controls;
3. a benchmark with high apparent accuracy but deficient target-information sufficiency will be diagnosed as non-identifying before model comparison;
4. a reduced interface will remain decision-equivalent until a hidden load-bearing coordinate is introduced, at which point the exact divergence family will be predicted in advance;
5. a scientific status can be restored after change by revalidating exactly the affected target bridge while preserving unrelated native-valid assurance.

These predictions are much more important than another retrospective reinterpretation because they can make the theory risky and independently testable.

## What must not be claimed yet

Do not currently claim:

- a universal law of epistemic transmission;
- a universal no-authority-amplification theorem;
- invention of proof-obligation discharge;
- invention of evidence-sufficiency-gated claim authorization;
- generic statistical sufficiency;
- generic causal transportability;
- universal minimality of the ORION coordinates;
- Nobel-level or field-defining status.

A field-level claim becomes defensible only after the generic calculus proves SSTF for a nontrivial workflow class, survives prospective hidden falsifiers, makes novel confirmed predictions, and transfers to real scientific tasks across independent domains and implementations.
