## 1. Purpose

The current ORION papers provide large protected empirical separations and exact bounded formal laws, but they do not yet establish a universal theory of autonomous science. This Grand Challenge defines the independent cross-domain programme required to test whether those paper-local results are instances of one deeper factorization of scientific status change.

The aspiration is deliberately field-level: a genuine breakthrough would have to compress several previously separate failure modes into one risky law, make new predictions before outcome access, survive independent implementations and naturalistic scientific artifacts, and improve consequential scientific decisions. Prestige cannot be produced by wording. The programme earns wider authority only through prospective evidence.

## 2. Canonical conjecture

### SSTF.G1 — cross-domain scientific-status factorization hypothesis

For a defined class of scientific workflows, let `A` be the locally relied-upon artifacts or judgments and `o` the target scientific obligation. Define:

- `V(A)`: every relied-upon local artifact is natively valid for the object it actually certifies;
- `S(A,o)`: the available information is sufficient to decide the exact target relation under the registered scientific model;
- `E(A,o)`: a registered target-bound inference, identity, closure, promotion, revalidation, transport, or authority bridge is entitled to connect those inputs to the exact target object, content, scope, and epoch;
- `B(A,o)`: no load-bearing target blocker remains unresolved.

The surviving foundational conjecture is:

> **An admissible scientific-status change occurs exactly when native validity, target information sufficiency, target-bound discharge entitlement, and blocker clearance all hold for the target transition.**

Prospective theorem target:

`AdmissibleStatusChange(A,o) <=> V(A) AND S(A,o) AND E(A,o) AND B(A,o)`

### Embedded SDSP law

The **Scientific Discharge Sufficiency Principle (SDSP)** is a component of this conjecture, primarily constraining `E`:

> Processing, aggregating, translating, repeating, optimizing, or composing locally valid artifacts does not by itself enlarge the different scientific obligations they are entitled to discharge. A broader target requires a registered sound bridge or inference rule, new authorized evidence, or protected revalidation supplying the missing target-bound premise.

The programme permits legitimate novel inference. A new conclusion may be established when a sound rule and its premises satisfy `V`, `S`, `E`, and `B`. It rejects only unlicensed status change produced by local success or computation alone.

## 3. Current authority

`SSTF.G1` is a `PROGRAMME_CONJECTURE`, not an established universal iff theorem.

Existing bounded support includes:

- P1 protected reformulation and revision-responsibility separations, plus Objective-Basis Sufficiency on a frozen decision-complete family;
- P2 material-route closure, processing-obligation separation, and evaluation-transport failure;
- P3 claim-relative identity and integration separations;
- P4 scientific-promotion and evaluator-identifiability results;
- P6 necessary-and-sufficient restoration in a finite certificate-lifting model;
- P7 necessary-and-sufficient closure restoration and exact bridge composition;
- P8 complete finite target-bound composition across the registered thirteen-family donor product;
- repeated information-equivalent product ties supporting representation independence;
- a protected transfer result where an abstract compatibility signature is decision-sufficient while richer receipts retain audit value.

Those outcomes motivate SSTF. They cannot confirm it on their own because the synthesis was formulated after the paper-local outcomes were known.

## 4. Breakthrough criteria

A field-level claim requires all of the following.

1. **Conceptual compression.** One typed calculus encodes reformulation, closure, identity, promotion, post-change standing, navigation closure, and cross-domain authority without paper-specific decision code.
2. **Factor necessity and sufficiency.** Interventions on `V`, `S`, `E`, and `B` produce the predicted selective status-transition changes, and the conjunction is sufficient on the registered workflow class.
3. **Prospective prediction.** The factorization predicts new failures, ties, and repairs before hidden outcomes are accessed.
4. **Causal intervention.** Adding the factorized status-transition layer changes target decisions while holding donor-native mechanisms and visible information fixed.
5. **Clean-utility preservation.** The safety gain is not produced by blanket refusal or systematic under-claiming.
6. **Cross-domain invariance.** The law survives different disciplines, institutions, implementations, artifact types, and scientific epochs.
7. **Independent reconstruction.** Separate teams reproduce the formal judgments, case bindings, and headline effects without importing the primary implementation.
8. **Consequential benefit.** The intervention reduces invalid scientific conclusions, avoidable expert correction, unsafe experiment or release decisions, or wasted scientific work in settings where errors matter.

Failure on any criterion narrows the programme claim.

## 5. Participating scientific domains

The first execution should include at least five independently owned domains with native scientific artifacts rather than one reskinned synthetic benchmark.

1. **Biomedical evidence synthesis and claim release.** Systematic-review inclusion, evidence grading, clinical-claim authorization, and updates after support revocation.
2. **Experimental physics or autonomous instrumentation.** Calibration validity, measurement interpretation, anomaly escalation, model revision, and result release.
3. **Materials or chemistry closed-loop experimentation.** Objective revision, experiment-selection authority, provenance-bound measurements, and transport across instruments or protocols.
4. **Computational discovery, theorem proving, or machine-learning experimentation.** Proof or result certificates, benchmark promotion, evaluator/model changes, and reuse after code or data drift.
5. **Environmental, public-health, or policy evidence synthesis.** Heterogeneous source transport, population and epoch change, conflicting support families, and decision release.

A sixth domain should be held out for transfer and selected after the calculus and interfaces are frozen but before outcome access.

## 6. Unit of analysis

Each case contains:

- one or more donor-native artifacts or judgments with independently verifiable local validity;
- a different target scientific object whose status may or may not change;
- explicit values or evidence for `V`, `S`, `E`, and `B` under a registered domain model;
- the exact target obligation, scope, content identity, epoch, support requirements, and blocker state;
- candidate-visible information that excludes protected target labels and shortcut-revealing family names;
- a gold target transition produced by domain experts under a predeclared adjudication protocol;
- case-level content and provenance bindings.

The decisive cases hold local donor success constant while changing exactly one target factor. Matched clean cases satisfy all four factors. Legitimate-inference cases ensure the theory cannot pass by simply prohibiting all authority growth.

## 7. Experimental arms

All primary arms receive the same donor-native information and resource envelope.

### A0 — strongest domain-native system

The best available native workflow or mechanism for the domain, evaluated on its own declared object and then mapped to the target status decision through the domain's ordinary practice.

### A1 — donor-complete product without explicit factorization

A deliberately strong integrated product containing mature local mechanisms such as retrieval, verification, provenance, certificates, authorization, custody, uncertainty, transport metadata, or replanning. It lacks only the complete explicit target factorization or equivalent relation under test.

### A2 — SSTF factorized status-transition layer

The same donor-complete product plus the generic typed object, obligation, bridge, support, blocker, revalidation, revocation, and factor-evaluation rules declared in the foundational protocol. Domain customization is limited to type and rule declarations. Paper-specific or case-family-specific decision code is prohibited.

### A3 — information-equivalent independent product

A separately implemented product carrying the same scientific objects, factors, and sound discharge rules as A2. It tests representation independence and guards against an ORION-centralization claim.

### Controls

- **compensatory scalar product:** allows strong local signals to compensate for a failed load-bearing factor;
- **blanket fail-closed control:** refuses every nontrivial transition, testing whether safety is produced by indiscriminate abstention;
- **target-agnostic aggregation control:** combines all locally valid artifacts without target-bound factorization;
- **registered factor ablations:** remove or corrupt `V`, `S`, `E`, or `B` one at a time;
- **registered bridge ablations:** remove one admissible target bridge while preserving the local artifacts;
- **information-reduction controls:** expose reduced interfaces predicted to tie on one family and diverge when a hidden load-bearing coordinate is introduced.

## 8. Primary outcomes

### Primary safety outcome

**False scientific-status transition rate** over cases whose gold target obligation does not permit the proposed transition.

Examples include false reformulation, false task closure, false identity merge, false claim promotion, stale standing preservation, false closure inheritance, and unauthorized cross-domain discharge.

### Co-primary clean outcome

**Correct clean scientific-status transition rate** over cases in which `V`, `S`, `E`, and `B` all support the target transition.

The programme succeeds only if the safety improvement is accompanied by clean performance above a predeclared non-inferiority margin determined through outcome-blind power analysis.

### Exact-decision outcome

**Exact target terminal accuracy** over the full registered terminal set, preserving distinctions such as `DISCHARGE`, `CONTINUE/REOPEN`, `CANNOT_CHECK`, and `BLOCK`.

### Factor attribution outcome

**Correct limiting-factor identification**: whether the system identifies the missing or failed factor and cites the exact target object and obligation responsible for the terminal.

## 9. Secondary outcomes

- target, subject, content, scope, and epoch attribution accuracy;
- selective reopening or revalidation cost;
- amount of unaffected native-valid assurance retained;
- false-null and false-superiority rate under invalid evaluation transport;
- independent replay and audit time;
- robustness to evidence correlation, provider failure, evaluator corruption, representation change, revocation, partial observability, and epoch drift;
- downstream expert correction burden;
- scientific decision latency and resource use;
- domain-expert assessment of whether the final status transition is actionable and correctly justified.

No secondary outcome may replace a failed primary endpoint after outcome access.

## 10. Prospectively frozen predictions

Before hidden-case generation, freeze at least these predictions.

### PRED-1 — four-factor sufficiency

On cases within the registered workflow class, A2 will select the correct target status whenever all four factors hold and will not require extra ORION-specific conditions.

### PRED-2 — factor-selective necessity

Removing one load-bearing factor will change only the matched target transitions that require it while preserving donor-native local verdicts and unrelated controls.

### PRED-3 — local-success separation

A1 will match A2 on donor-native judgments but make more false target-status transitions where the missing information is specifically `S`, `E`, or `B` rather than native validity.

### PRED-4 — clean preservation

A2 will retain high clean transition accuracy and materially outperform the blanket fail-closed control.

### PRED-5 — representation independence

A3 will be decision-identical or statistically equivalent to A2 within a predeclared exact or practical criterion.

### PRED-6 — legitimate novel inference

A2 will permit new conclusions when a registered sound inference rule and its premises satisfy all four factors. It will outperform a trivial rule that never permits scientific status to grow.

### PRED-7 — evaluator-identifiability diagnosis

The calculus will identify at least one previously unexecuted benchmark whose score is predicted to fail target-information sufficiency because a target-irrelevant construction cue identifies the label. A frozen audit will confirm or refute the prediction.

### PRED-8 — target-entitlement failure

A strong local-verification product with deliberately omitted target entitlement will fail exactly the matched status transitions while retaining all local and clean controls.

### PRED-9 — blocker preservation

An unresolved load-bearing blocker will remain `CANNOT_CHECK` despite unrelated local success; a refuted blocker will clear the terminal when the other factors hold.

### PRED-10 — sufficient-interface tie and hostile divergence

The calculus will prospectively identify a reduced interface predicted to tie a richer representation on one target family and the exact hidden coordinate whose introduction causes divergence.

### PRED-11 — selective restoration

After a scientific object changes, revalidating the complete affected target bridge will restore standing while preserving unrelated assurance; strict-subset revalidation will fail on the matched factorized family.

### PRED-12 — invalid transport firewall

A2 will refuse to mint either a positive or valid null when the provider or evaluation transport contract fails, while retaining power on valid transports.

## 11. Case-generation and custody

- The object, obligation, factor, bridge, arm, scoring, exclusion, and analysis schemas are frozen before hidden outcomes.
- Development cases are public and excluded from confirmatory inference.
- Protected cases are generated or selected by independent host lanes using committed secret seeds or externally held sampling procedures.
- Candidate-visible packets exclude gold labels, family names, custody classes, and target-revealing shortcuts.
- Scored execution is separated from protected evaluation.
- File, network, environment, and protected-label access are recorded where technically possible.
- The evaluator becomes eligible to access protected gold only after all primary arms finish.
- Independent reconstruction uses separately written decision code and recomputes canonical case and result digests.
- Domain experts adjudicating gold do not see system outputs before the gold freeze.

## 12. Statistical and formal analysis

The final plan is frozen after outcome-blind power analysis and before protected execution.

At minimum:

- paired effects for A2 versus A1 on false transitions and exact target decisions;
- domain-stratified uncertainty intervals;
- clean non-inferiority analysis;
- factor-by-intervention interaction analysis testing selective failures;
- per-domain and per-family direction checks;
- exact accounting of all failures, abstentions, timeouts, and candidate-caused errors;
- multiplicity control or a declared hierarchy for multiple co-primary claims;
- separate confirmatory and exploratory tables;
- equivalence or exact-match analysis for A2 versus A3;
- model checking of the registered formal fragment;
- independent reproduction of every headline count and digest.

Margins and exclusions cannot be selected from observed protected effects.

## 13. Primary falsifiers

`SSTF.G1` is rejected, narrowed, or absorbed if any of the following occurs.

1. A target status is correctly changed while one declared load-bearing factor is false or unresolved and no equivalent representation of that factor exists.
2. All four factors hold, yet the target status repeatedly fails to change for reasons not expressible in the registered scientific object and bridge language.
3. A proposed factor is inert under intervention.
4. A1 matches A2 on decisive target cases despite lacking an explicit or equivalent factorization.
5. A2 reduces false transitions only by suppressing clean valid transitions.
6. A2 blocks legitimate registered novel inference because it mistakes all authority growth for laundering.
7. The generic calculus requires paper-specific algorithmic branches or ad hoc exceptions.
8. An information-equivalent A3 implementation disagrees materially with A2.
9. The effect disappears on naturalistic artifacts, independent institutions, or held-out domains.
10. A prior formalism already entails an equivalent cross-lifecycle factorization at equal or broader scope.
11. Independent teams cannot reproduce the case bindings, factor judgments, or effects.
12. The intervention produces no meaningful improvement in consequential scientific or human decision outcomes.

A falsifier is a scientific result, not a reason to conceal the campaign.

## 14. Human-benefit evaluation

A broad-impact claim requires direct outcomes rather than analogy. Domain teams should predeclare one consequential measure, such as:

- invalid clinical or scientific claim releases prevented;
- unsafe or scientifically unauthorized experiments blocked without loss of valid experiments;
- expert-hours saved in audit, revalidation, or correction;
- stale conclusions correctly reopened after evidence, model, instrument, or evaluator change;
- false scientific identity merges prevented;
- resource waste avoided by preserving unaffected valid certificates or closure;
- downstream decisions improved under blinded expert adjudication.

ORION should not claim benefit to humankind merely because the formalism is elegant or an agent benchmark improves.

## 15. Governance and independence

- Each domain has a native scientific owner and an independent claim-authority auditor.
- At least one external institution owns a protected domain and its gold adjudication.
- The main authors do not tune candidate logic after protected outcomes.
- The information-equivalent implementation is written by a separate team.
- Negative, null, tie, contamination, invalid-transport, and incomplete-execution records remain in the archive.
- All changes after freeze are classified as execution repair, protocol amendment, or new study; none silently alters the confirmatory subject.

## 16. Success ladder

### Level 1 — generic formal compatibility

One calculus recovers the existing registered paper decisions without paper-specific decision branches.

### Level 2 — prospective factor prediction

The calculus correctly predicts new factor-specific failures, ties, or repairs before outcome access.

### Level 3 — cross-domain causal effect

A2 reduces false target transitions versus A1 while preserving clean valid transitions across independently owned domains.

### Level 4 — independent and naturalistic transfer

External institutions and information-equivalent implementations reproduce the factorization on native artifacts and held-out domains.

### Level 5 — consequential scientific benefit

The intervention measurably improves real scientific decisions, safety, audit burden, or resource use.

Only after Levels 1–5 should ORION present SSTF as a candidate foundational principle of autonomous science. Even then, priority and long-term significance are judged by the scientific community and subsequent history, not by the project itself.
