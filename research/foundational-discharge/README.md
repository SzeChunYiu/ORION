# ORION foundational scientific-status programme

This directory is the canonical home for the third-pass foundational research programme. It separates established bounded paper results, the scientific-discharge calculus, the nearest-prior-art subtraction, the surviving foundational conjecture, and the prospective external validation required for a field-level claim.

## Canonical concepts

The programme uses two related but non-identical concepts.

### Scientific Discharge Sufficiency Principle (SDSP)

SDSP is the no-unlicensed-amplification and target-bridge component of the calculus:

> Processing a locally valid scientific artifact cannot by itself enlarge the set of different scientific obligations it is entitled to discharge. A broader target requires a registered sound inference or transport bridge, new authorized evidence, or protected revalidation supplying the missing target-bound premise.

SDSP is represented by the scientific-discharge protocol's conservativity, no-amplification, bridge-necessity, representation-independence, unresolved-obligation, and revocation targets.

### Scientific Status Transition Factorization Conjecture (SSTF)

Hostile prior-art subtraction shows that generic sufficiency, proof-obligation discharge, warrant transmission, transportability, and authority attenuation are already established parent domains. The surviving programme-level residual is therefore SSTF:

> For a defined class of scientific workflows, an admissible target status change factors into four non-substitutable conditions: native validity `V`, target information sufficiency `S`, target-bound discharge entitlement `E`, and no unresolved load-bearing blocker `B`.

Prospective target:

`AdmissibleStatusChange(A,o) <=> V(A) AND S(A,o) AND E(A,o) AND B(A,o)`

SSTF is not a universal theorem. Existing ORION papers are bounded instantiations motivating the conjecture.

Do not use SDSP and SSTF as competing names for one object. SDSP is a theorem family inside the proposed calculus; SSTF is the broader positive factorization conjecture surviving the prior-art collision.

## Integrity sequence

The publication sequence is:

`original hypothesis and terminal -> positive successor hypothesis and authority label -> cross-paper conjecture -> prospective independent prediction and confirmation`

Retrospective compatibility with known paper outcomes is useful for theory construction but is not new confirmation. A universal or foundational claim requires the hidden falsifier suite and cross-domain Grand Challenge to be frozen and executed without outcome-conditioned changes.

## Breakthrough rule

The programme does not claim prize status, universal validity, or broad benefit by rhetoric. The strongest route is:

1. one generic calculus encodes all registered paper decisions without paper-specific algorithms;
2. SSTF or one of its factors makes novel predictions before outcome access;
3. interventions on `V`, `S`, `E`, or `B` produce the predicted selective status-transition changes;
4. a factorized discharge layer reduces unauthorized target transitions without clean-utility loss;
5. independent institutions and information-equivalent implementations reproduce the result on naturalistic artifacts and held-out domains;
6. the intervention measurably improves consequential scientific or human decisions.

Only evidence can move the programme up this ladder.
