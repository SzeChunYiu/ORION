# All-eight scientific status-transition theorem target — V2

Date: 2026-08-20  
Scope: ORION Papers 1–8  
Authority: programme synthesis over already frozen paper-local results; not a universal theorem.

## 1. Research question

The eight papers study different scientific operations, but their strongest surviving results can be written as one question:

> When may a locally valid scientific object change the status of a different scientific obligation?

The eight transition coordinates are

`Omega = (R, K, I, V, A, C, T, U)`

where:

- `R` — reformulation / revision authority (P1);
- `K` — task-global closure authority (P2);
- `I` — scientific identity / integration authority (P3);
- `V` — verification-to-promotion authority (P4);
- `A` — self-modification proposal-to-adoption authority (P5);
- `C` — post-change certificate standing / restoration (P6);
- `T` — closure transport through transformations (P7);
- `U` — cross-domain discharge / authority composition (P8).

The paper-local systems are not claimed to be identical. The programme claim is that each supplies an independently nontrivial instance of the same no-laundering question.

## 2. Scientific Status Transition Factorization

For target obligation `o`, let an artifact `x` expose four factors:

- `N(x,o)` — native validity for the artifact's own registered object;
- `S(x,o)` — target-relevant information sufficiency;
- `E(x,o)` — target-bound discharge entitlement / authority;
- `B(x,o)` — absence of an unresolved load-bearing blocker.

The current programme conjecture is

`Admissible(x -> o) <=> N(x,o) AND S(x,o) AND E(x,o) AND B(x,o)`

for a defined class of scientific workflows.

This is a conjectured normal form, not a definition of success and not yet a universal theorem. A valid proof must derive these factors from primitive workflow events and show that the factorization is both sound and non-vacuously complete for the chosen workflow class.

## 3. No-laundering law

Let `D(x)` be the set of obligations that `x` is entitled to discharge. Let `F` be any transformation, repetition, composition, recommendation, optimization, reformatting, or proposal operation that adds neither new authorized evidence nor a registered sound bridge.

The all-eight Scientific Discharge Sufficiency target is

`D(F(x)) subseteq Closure_R(D(x))`

where `R` contains only registered sound inference, transport, revalidation, or authority bridges.

Interpretation:

> Local validity may be preserved, transformed, or composed, but it does not silently mint authority over a different scientific obligation.

## 4. Eight bounded instantiations

### P1 — reformulation authority

A local performance signal or failure does not by itself authorize high-level reformulation. The positive successor is protected escalation sufficiency: responsibility/necessity evidence plus protected admission governs when revision is warranted. On decision-complete objective portfolios, protected admission can be sufficient even when dynamic objective generation adds no decision value.

### P2 — closure authority

A route-local success or stop condition does not authorize task-global closure when a material route remains unresolved. P2-X supplies an exact positive successor: all material route obligations must be discharged or explicitly represented as unresolved. Transport validity is separately required before an external comparison can acquire scientific standing.

### P3 — identity authority

Representation-level compatibility does not by itself authorize scientific identity or safe integration. P3-X succeeds 400/400 while the strongest ordinary semantic product reaches 250/400 and canonical matching 50/400; an information-equivalent typed implementation carrying the same identity relation also reaches 400/400 with zero mismatches. The positive result is therefore identity-authority sufficiency plus implementation portability under information equivalence.

### P4 — promotion authority

Verification and generic permission do not by themselves authorize scientific claim promotion. The protected authority arm gives zero false promotions on the registered stress family while the strongest proxy promotes 180/360 incorrectly, with both clean on 60/60 clean controls. A separate saturated benchmark result becomes an evaluator-identifiability theorem target: a metric with no resolving power cannot support the intended competence claim.

### P5 — adoption authority

An internally generated diagnosis or revision proposal does not authorize its own adoption. The registered Self-ORION controller grants diagnosis/proposal/T7 recommendation no revision, adoption, promotion, merge, or global-stop authority. The 21/24 hidden-cause diagnostic archive retains three real errors, making the separation non-vacuous: fallible diagnosis can guide investigation without becoming a self-issued adoption certificate. Fresh-task improvement remains a separate performance extension rather than the authority theorem's premise.

### P6 — restoration of scientific standing

Native-valid certificates do not automatically preserve scientific standing after a material change. In the registered finite lifting model, complete affected-bridge revalidation is necessary and sufficient for restoration, with 155 exact restoration witnesses and 1,055 strict-subset necessity countermodels while unaffected donor-valid certificates remain reusable.

### P7 — closure transport

A donor-valid navigation transform does not automatically carry task-global closure. Complete repair of the affected closure contract restores closure in the registered model; heterogeneous transforms compose through exact intermediate contracts or certified bridges, with matched missing-bridge variants demonstrating necessity.

### P8 — cross-domain discharge composition

Local authority does not widen simply because multiple locally valid donors are combined. Across 39,936 registered states and all 169 ordered donor pairs, compatible/narrowed or protectedly bridged compositions succeed; matched unbridged widening variants fail while donor-local verdicts remain valid. This is a scientific discharge-composition result, not a claim to generic computer-security authorization theory.

## 5. Recursive conversion rule for adverse outcomes

Every adverse or null paper-local outcome is handled by the same research rule:

1. preserve the original frozen terminal;
2. identify the scientific variable the adverse result actually resolves;
3. formulate the strongest positive successor that would have predicted both the successful and adverse cases;
4. label the successor separately as bounded, bounded-formal, theorem-schema, or prospective;
5. require an evidence chain before the successor becomes the manuscript headline;
6. prohibit any wording that implies the original failed superiority hypothesis passed.

Examples:

- exact tie -> sufficiency / portability theorem candidate;
- strict-subset failure -> necessity witness;
- saturated benchmark -> measurement-resolution / identifiability result;
- unavailable external campaign -> prospective transfer extension;
- fallible self-diagnosis -> error-containment condition for non-self-promotion;
- donor-local validity without target success -> non-laundering or bridge-necessity witness.

## 6. Strongest current all-eight theorem schema

The current supported programme-level statement is:

> Across eight independently constructed ORION paper families, a locally valid scientific success signal does not automatically acquire authority over a different status transition. Correct transitions require target-relevant information and target-bound authority, with unresolved load-bearing blockers represented explicitly; where a protected bridge or complete revalidation supplies the missing premise, the target transition is restored without invalidating unaffected donor-local facts.

Authority: `THEOREM_SCHEMA_SUPPORTED_BY_INSTANTIATIONS`.

This statement is stronger than a list of mechanisms and narrower than a universal law. It is supported by the eight paper-local result families but has not yet been proved complete for arbitrary scientific workflows.

## 7. Breakthrough theorem target

A genuine field-level theorem would require a primitive workflow semantics `W`, independent of the desired factorization, and proofs of:

1. **Soundness:** every admitted target transition has a valid native-evidence/bridge chain.
2. **No silent amplification:** authority-neutral processing cannot widen target discharge authority.
3. **Normal-form completeness:** every semantically admissible transition in `W` has a certificate `(N,S,E,B)`.
4. **Factor independence:** each factor is necessary in at least one family; none is a definitional synonym for admissibility.
5. **Representation invariance:** information-equivalent realizations carrying the same scientific relation induce identical transition decisions.
6. **Composition:** exact intermediate contracts or protected bridges compose without hidden widening.
7. **Revocation:** invalidated load-bearing support removes dependent authority without erasing unaffected native facts.
8. **Prospective prediction:** the same calculus predicts new failures before outcome access on held-out naturalistic scientific tasks.

## 8. Maximum defensible positive headline

> **Scientific status is not fungible. Across eight ORION paper families, reformulation, closure, identity, promotion, adoption, post-change standing, closure transport, and cross-domain authority behave as distinct scientific transitions whose local evidence cannot silently substitute for one another. The strongest bounded results identify explicit sufficiency, necessity, portability, restoration, error-containment, and composition laws for those transitions.**
