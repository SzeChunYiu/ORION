# Nearest-Work Absorption and ORION Residual V1

## Status

This is a hostile donor-subtraction map dated **2026-08-25**.  It identifies
mechanisms ORION should absorb rather than relabel as original.  The residual is
a research target, not an external novelty certificate.

## 1. Classical scientific-discovery and problem-solving donors

| Parent | Donor-owned structure absorbed by ORION | What it does not by itself supply |
|---|---|---|
| Klahr–Dunbar Scientific Discovery as Dual Search | Coordinated search in hypothesis and experiment spaces; evidence updates hypothesis generation | Representation/ontology search, proof/authority state, donor residual and claim synchronization |
| Schunn–Klahr four-space model | Adds data-representation and experimental-paradigm spaces to hypothesis and experiment search | Scientific admission, resource Pareto accounting, method-language obstruction and open morphology |
| Gentner structure mapping | Cross-domain transfer should preserve relational structure rather than superficial attributes | Target-native validity, broken-correspondence debt, authority and donor-first-refusal accounting |
| Machamer–Darden–Craver mechanism theory | Mechanisms as organized entities and activities productive of regular change | A general executable discovery controller, theorem-identifying comparison, proposal origin and frontier dominance |
| Kaplan–Simon insight model | Effective representations, generators, constraints and invariant noticing govern insight | Donor-union subtraction, typed scientific support families and prospective novelty authority |
| Knoblich–Ohlsson representational change | Constraint relaxation and chunk decomposition break impasses | General scientific layer diagnosis, method/instrument invention and admission/custody |
| Hatchuel–Weil C–K theory | Controlled expansion between concepts undecidable in current knowledge and the knowledge space | Exact scientific validity, donor closure, resource contracts and claim-authority separation |

ORION V3 therefore must not claim originality for dual-space search, four-space
search, structural analogy, mechanism construction, representation change,
constraint relaxation, concept–knowledge co-expansion or universal search.

## 3. Candidate programme-level residual

After absorbing those structures, the candidate ORION residual is the coupled
object:

```text
TYPED SCIENTIFIC KNOWLEDGE WEB
+ ALTERNATIVE SUPPORT HYPEREDGES
+ RESPONSIBILITY-LAYER DIAGNOSIS
+ IDEAL DONOR-UNION FIRST REFUSAL
+ SET-VALUED MINIMAL SEMANTIC RESIDUAL
+ THEOREM-IDENTIFYING HARNESS
+ VECTOR PROOF/EXPERIMENT ECONOMY
+ HISTORICAL / COUNTERFACTUAL / PROSPECTIVE TRIANGULATION
+ SCIENTIFIC VALIDITY / NOVELTY / ADOPTION AUTHORITY SEPARATION
+ CHANGE-IMPACT PROPAGATION INTO PAPER CLAIM ATOMS
```

The strongest candidate claim is not that no prior system contains any of
these pieces.  It is that no located parent currently supplies their exact
**non-compensatory integration** and the corresponding frontier-dominance
criterion:

> preserve the ideal donor product on its closure, strictly expand verified
> reach outside that closure, identify the minimal load-bearing residual, and
> do so without information, resource, false-promotion or authority advantage.

That residual must be attacked through the external novelty lane.  A missed
source may absorb it.

## 4. Required superiority comparisons

A top-tier comparison must include, where technically applicable:

1. a retrieval/reasoning research agent;
2. Co-Scientist-style multi-agent generation/reflection/evolution;
3. AlphaEvolve-style evaluator-guided program evolution;
4. a domain-native autonomous discovery system;
5. an ideal donor product receiving all legal combined interfaces;
6. Discovery V1 without typed knowledge-web/change-impact state;
7. Discovery V2/V3.

The primary endpoint is not average idea quality.  It is the conjunction of:

- correct responsible-layer diagnosis;
- donor-closure conservativity;
- strict outside-closure success;
- false-promotion non-amplification;
- Pareto resource noninferiority;
- hidden and held-out scientific consequences;
- independent validity;
- externally reviewed residual novelty.
