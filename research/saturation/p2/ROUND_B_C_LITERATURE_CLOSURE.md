## Discriminator

P2 does not compete on whether retrieval agents can plan, reformulate queries, estimate coverage, judge sufficiency, or stop efficiently. A material parent would need to collapse the residual distinction:

> local acquisition evidence/utility/coverage/sufficiency may stop or redirect a route, but unresolved task-global scientific-search obligations—including unavailable/censored routes—remain open unless independently discharged.

### Round-B result

RAAC is the closest new search-control work surfaced after Round A. It directly removes any remaining safe wording around `coverage-aware termination` as novel. The systematic-review confidence paper similarly removes `information sufficiency` as a novel stopping principle. Neither converts a local stopping decision into authority to certify a scientific task complete when separately registered routes/obligations remain unresolved.

**Round B: `NO_MATERIAL_CLAIM_CHANGE`; donor wording must stay narrow.**
