## 1. One-sentence bounded argument

In open-world scientific-literature discovery, P2 separates **acquisition control** from **scientific-closure authority**: retrieval quality, utility, sufficiency, estimated coverage or route-local stopping may guide what the system does next, but task-level closure is withheld while independently registered search obligations remain unresolved, unavailable or censored; the frozen controlled index and bounded external probes test this fail-closed mechanism without claiming matched open-web superiority.

## 2. Supported claim

- route labels do not establish evidential independence; backend/query-derivation/capture identity must support it;
- route-local STOP and task-global scientific closure are distinct;
- unavailable/censored route obligations remain open rather than becoming negative evidence;
- content identity and question-conditioned processing state are distinct;
- acquisition mechanisms are donor-composable and do not inherit global scientific authority;
- frozen controlled-world behavior is reproducible and directionally supports the designed mechanisms;
- external MetaSyn/Wide/Deep probes are bounded stage evidence, not matched ORION superiority.

## 3. Explicit nonclaims

- generic lexical/dense/generative/agentic retrieval novelty;
- generic query expansion/reformulation novelty;
- generic learned STOP/CONTINUE novelty;
- generic sufficiency/coverage/utility stopping novelty;
- generic systematic-review automation novelty;
- proof of open-world completeness;
- matched external Wide/Deep/SAGE superiority;
- provider/runtime/cost superiority;
- literature saturation.

## 4. Terminology ledger

| Canonical term | Meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `acquisition layer` | mechanisms that find/read/screen evidence | calling it the authority layer |
| `scientific-closure authority` | permission to declare the task sufficiently covered/closed | `confidence` as a synonym |
| `earned route independence` | independence supported by backend/query/capture process | provider-name diversity |
| `route stop` | local decision to stop/switch/reformulate a route | `task complete` |
| `task stop / closure` | global authority terminal | inferring from one route |
| `content identity` | source/content-version identity | question-conditioned read state |
| `question-conditioned processing state` | whether content was processed for this question/schema/version | treating as new evidence identity |
| `open obligation` | unresolved/unavailable/censored required route/check | negative evidence |
| `CANNOT_CHECK` | authority terminal under missing required evidence | zero/failure |

## 6. Argument map

1. Search systems can become better at acquiring evidence without becoming justified in saying the scientific search is complete.
2. Modern IR/deep-research/systematic-review methods already own retrieval, screening, search planning, coverage estimation and stopping mechanisms.
3. The residual systems problem is authority: which local signals may alter acquisition, and which evidence can close the global task?
4. P2 formalizes route identity/independence, question-conditioned processing state, route stopping and global obligations.
5. The complete-gold controlled suite tests the mechanism under a knowable denominator; bounded external probes expose real transport/retrieval/screening failure modes.
6. The result supports mechanism behavior, not matched open-web superiority.
7. The scientific lesson is fail-closed composition: better acquisition modules can be swapped in without gaining permission to erase unresolved obligations.

## 7. Section contracts

### Abstract
Lead with acquisition-vs-closure problem, then mechanism, controlled evidence, bounded external evidence, explicit CANNOT_CHECK boundary, and architectural implication. Avoid presenting +0.31282 as inferential superiority.

### Introduction/problem
Connect P2 to information retrieval and information-science concepts, not only AI agents. Explain why provider/tool diversity is not evidence diversity and why open-world stopping is an information-authority problem.

### Nearest work
Organize by function: retrieval/query planning; systematic review/screening; stopping/sufficiency/coverage; benchmark/evaluation; memory/read state. The residual should emerge after donor subtraction.

### Results
Keep controlled mechanism evidence and external stress evidence stage-separated. Never mix complete-gold denominator results with open-web provider results into one headline.

### Limitations
Provider mutability, benchmark contamination, open-world denominator uncertainty, synthetic/easy controlled tasks, and unavailable authorities are central boundaries, not boilerplate.
