# P1 saturation — two adversarial blind-review rounds

Date: 2026-08-19

These are **internal adversarial reviewer simulations**, not external peer review. Each report was written against the manuscript/claim package from one role before reading the synthesis of the other roles. The purpose is to expose submission blockers, not to manufacture endorsement.

# Round 1

## R1 — novelty / field-positioning reviewer

### Blocking/major concerns

1. **Generic diagnosis-to-repair language was too broad.** HarnessFix and CausalFlow make attribution-conditioned scoped repair unmistakably prior art.
2. **The paper title/programme framing risks making the work look like one module in a portfolio rather than a standalone scientific question.**
3. **The novelty table can become a substitute for synthesis.** The main text must state the residual mechanism family in prose before showing the ledger.

### Resolution

- Concern 1 is resolved in the current related-work boundary: HarnessFix is explicitly donor-owned and P1.D2 is narrowed to the protected high-level epistemic mutation authority relation.
- Concern 2 is resolved at the venue-overlay level by `STYLE_ATLAS_CORE.md`: first submission uses a standalone AIJ title; the core repository manuscript may retain programme metadata until packaging.
- Concern 3 is resolved by the residual-claim paragraph and the planned main-text/supplement allocation; no donor row is used as a novelty certificate.

**R1 after resolution: no blocking concern; no unresolved major concern.**

## R2 — methods / causal attribution / statistics reviewer

### Blocking/major concerns

1. Perfect 1.000 hidden-shift success could be vacuous if the generator gives ORION its own answer key.
2. The historical V1 negative/underpowered study must not be pooled with v2.2.4.
3. The primary/replication comparison must use the exact pre-registered parent set, paired design and practical margin rather than a post-hoc best baseline.
4. Safety could be achieved by refusing all high-level mutations; the evidence/execution controls and clean hidden-shift success must be jointly visible.

### Resolution

- The manuscript already states the generator-bound ceiling, template leaks and blind-responder shortcuts as limitations.
- Historical V1 is explicitly `NOT_SUPPORTED/UNDERPOWERED` and unpooled.
- The primary and replication receipts bind the same three strong parents; each hidden-shift contrast is paired over n=480 with 10,000 bootstrap resamples and McNemar tests, and every 95% interval exceeds the registered +0.10 practical margin.
- The 2,402 evidence/execution controls have 1.000 task success for ORION and the control parent while ORION records zero unnecessary high-level reframes; reject-everything therefore cannot explain the result.
- Five direct ablations expose load-bearing pieces, including lower-level exclusion, K/W/M ordering, protected-sibling preservation, dependency-impact binding and necessity/effect conditions.

**R2 after resolution: no blocking concern; no unresolved major concern.**

## R3 — reproducibility / readability / editorial reviewer

### Blocking/major concerns

1. A 27-page repository manuscript and multi-page nearest-work table may be too heavy for a broad venue.
2. Integrity chronology is valuable but can obscure the decisive experiment.
3. The paper needs a venue-independent one-sentence argument and clear handoff boundaries to P2–P5.

### Resolution

- The full repository manuscript remains the provenance-rich source; AIJ/TMLR/NMI overlays may shorten/move material without changing claim/evidence.
- `STYLE_ATLAS_CORE.md` fixes the decisive evidence order and routes amendment/receipt chronology to Methods/reproducibility.
- The foundations and related-work sections already define Paper-I ownership; the invariant sentence is frozen in the style atlas.
- Existing PDF audit reports a clean compiled 27-page manuscript with figures/tables/citations/references inspected.

**R3 after resolution: no blocking concern; no unresolved major concern.**

# Round 2 — fresh reviewer roles after Round-B/C literature closure

## R1b — skeptical closest-combination reviewer

Attack: `Could an ideal combination of HarnessFix + CausalFlow + dependency rollback + a certificate broker already imply the whole P1 result?`

Finding: the combined parents cover diagnosis, scoped repair, minimal counterfactual change, selective replay/preservation and proposal/admission separation. They do **not** by themselves specify that responsibility level controls permission for a high-level scientific formulation/search-universe rewrite only after lower-level alternatives are excluded, nor the joint protected sibling + dependency-impact acceptance condition. The experiment directly compares registered assimilated parent policies rather than isolated strawmen.

Residual concern: this is a **composition** claim, not component novelty. The manuscript says so.

**Verdict: no blocking/major concern.**

## R2b — hostile validity reviewer

Attack: `The 1.000 ceiling and leaked templates mean the experiment proves nothing.`

Finding: leaks and generator alignment materially limit external validity and are disclosed. They do not erase the internal discriminator because strong parents and ablations operate on the same response vocabulary and fail specific necessity/safety gates. The correct claim is therefore bounded causal/mechanistic evidence in the registered family, not real-agent generality.

Residual concern: a real-agent follow-up would be needed for a stronger venue/general claim. It is explicitly future work.

**Verdict: no blocking/major concern for the bounded claim.**

## R3b — editor / packaging reviewer

Attack: `Is the paper independently understandable without the rest of ORION?`

Finding: yes at the scientific-core level: problem, K/W/M representation, mutation gate, parents, study, controls, replication and limitations are defined locally. Cross-paper references function as ownership boundaries rather than required implementation dependencies. Programme branding should be removed in the submitted overlay, but this is packaging rather than missing science.

**Verdict: no blocking/major concern.**
