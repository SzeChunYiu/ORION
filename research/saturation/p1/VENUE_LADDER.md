# P1 venue ladder and portability contract

Date: 2026-08-19

The scientific core is invariant. Venue overlays may change emphasis, length, section order, title/abstract compression, cover materials and display allocation. They may not strengthen the claim without new evidence.

## 1. Aspirational primary — Nature Machine Intelligence

### Current official Article constraints

- main text up to 3,500 words excluding abstract, Methods, references and figure legends;
- abstract up to 150 words and unreferenced;
- up to 6 figures/tables combined;
- Introduction (without heading) -> Results -> Discussion -> Methods;
- broad intelligibility beyond the immediate subfield is expected.

### P1 fit test

Potential fit exists because P1 concerns scientific-agent reasoning/governance rather than a narrow implementation trick. However, NMI needs a compelling field-level question and broader relevance. The current evidence remains a controlled mechanical-world result.

**Overlay-only changes:** standalone title, broad-reader opening, 150-word abstract, Results compression, six-display allocation, non-specialist explanation of K/W/M and mutation authority.

**Would require new science:** any claim that the mechanism improves real/open-ended scientific agents, transfers across base models, or establishes practical scientific benefit beyond the frozen mechanical family.

NMI remains aspirational until a hostile editorial-style review says the bounded result is interesting enough without claim inflation.

## 2. Strong-fit fallback — Artificial Intelligence (AIJ)

AIJ welcomes broad AI work including automated reasoning, knowledge representation, planning/action, multi-agent systems and reasoning under uncertainty. Its current guidance emphasizes mature complete work, relevant proofs and/or experimental data, thorough connection to literature, and convincing motivation/implications.

### P1 fit

Strong conceptual fit if written as an AI problem of **when an agent is justified in changing the representation/formulation it is reasoning within**, connected carefully to belief revision, diagnosis, truth maintenance, planning reformulation, metareasoning and agent repair.

**Overlay-only changes:** deepen formal/AI motivation, reduce programme branding, move exact ORION engineering receipts to supplement, emphasize relation to established AI formalisms.

**Possible new-science request from reviewers:** broader finite/formal characterization of admissible mutation or additional non-LLM agent examples. Not assumed necessary before submission.

## 3. Correctness-focused fallback — TMLR

TMLR's most important acceptance question is whether claims are supported by accurate, convincing and clear evidence; the venue explicitly permits modest/non-SOTA contributions and accepts claim reduction as a valid response to evidence gaps.

### P1 fit

The bounded mechanical primary+replication+independent verification aligns well with this criterion if the paper makes clear what the audience learns beyond reimplementation.

**Overlay-only changes:** compact ML-style framing, emphasize falsifiable claim/evidence alignment, put detailed formal historical material in related work/supplement, anonymize and follow TMLR package rules.

**Potential blocker:** if reviewers see no transferable insight beyond the frozen toy/mechanical benchmark. The manuscript must articulate the general *decision problem* without implying untested empirical generality.

## 4. Secondary fallback — JAIR

Keep as a candidate after the corpus pass. Use only after verifying current scope and studying comparable formal/agent papers. Do not copy AIJ/TMLR assumptions into JAIR without a fresh official check.

## 5. Portability matrix

| Element | Scientific core | NMI overlay | AIJ overlay | TMLR overlay |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| central claim | invariant bounded necessity result | invariant | invariant | invariant |
| title | standalone scientific problem | broad-reader, low jargon | formal AI problem emphasis | precise claim/mechanism emphasis |
| abstract | evidence-bound core | <=150 words, broad context | fuller formal motivation allowed | compact claim/evidence/boundary |
| related work | donor-complete ownership | highly synthesized | deepest formal lineage | concise but sufficient correctness/novelty boundary |

| figures/tables | all evidence archived | <=6 main displays | as justified | as justified |
| programme/P1–P5 boundary | scientific ownership only | minimize portfolio framing | concise cross-paper boundary | concise cross-paper boundary |
| claims about real agents | NOT CLAIMED | NOT CLAIMED | NOT CLAIMED | NOT CLAIMED |
