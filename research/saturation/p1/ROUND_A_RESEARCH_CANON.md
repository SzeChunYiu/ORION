## 1. One-sentence bounded argument

In research workflows where an observed failure may belong to different epistemic levels, P1 tests whether a **high-level formulation/search-universe mutation** should require a protected necessity certificate that combines lower-level exclusion, same-task counterfactual recovery, invariant/sibling preservation, and dependency-impact binding; in the frozen 2,882-world mechanical family this conjunction beats every assimilated strong parent in both the primary and disjoint replication while withholding high-level mutation on evidence/execution controls, without claiming model-general or open-ended scientific superiority.

Any manuscript sentence that cannot be connected to this sentence must be justified as necessary background, reproducibility, limitation, or moved out of the main argument.

## 7. Argument map

1. **Problem:** more search/retry is not an adequate response when the formulation itself is wrong, but high-level reformulation is dangerous when the failure is merely evidence or execution.
2. **Prior art:** modern agents already provide persistent state, failure attribution, scoped repair, recovery, dependency rollback, recursive audit and metareasoning.
3. **Residual question:** what evidence should license a high-level scientific K/W/M mutation after those ordinary mechanisms are granted?
4. **Mechanism:** protected necessity certificate = lower-level exclusion + counterfactual same-task recovery + invariant/sibling preservation + dependency-impact match.
5. **Test:** frozen hidden shifts plus large evidence/execution controls under matched intervention budgets and strong donor-derived parents.
6. **Result:** supported in primary and disjoint replication; independent verifier agrees exactly.
7. **Mechanism identification:** registered ablations break the exact safety/success coordinates the certificate is designed to protect.
8. **Boundary:** this is a bounded mechanical necessity result, not evidence of general autonomous scientific competence.

## 8. Section contracts

Must contain: problem, exact residual, experiment scale, decisive paired result, clean-control/safety signal, reproducibility, boundary.

### Introduction/foundations

### Related work
Must be mechanism-organized, not paper-list-organized.  
Every close donor gets a disposition and an exact effect on the residual.  
Any phrase of the form `none of the prior work...` must survive the newest failure-repair literature.

### Results
Lead with the registered primary comparison and replication, then clean controls, then ablations, then cost/whole-grid details.  
Do not lead with protocol amendment chronology.

### Discussion/limitations
Explain what the result teaches about **admission of high-level epistemic change**, not that ORION is generally a better scientist.  
State why mechanical worlds were necessary for protected causal control and what they fail to establish.
