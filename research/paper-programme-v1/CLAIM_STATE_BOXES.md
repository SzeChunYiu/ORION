## How to read a box

| Lane | What it licenses |
|---|---|
| **Active claim** | carries authority now, within the scope stated on the item |
| **Historical result, retained** | found, kept, and never relabelled; it is evidence, not an open task |
| **Not claimed - frozen, not yet executed** | a registered protocol with no result; absent work, not a negative one |
| **Not claimed - externally blocked** | cannot be decided in this repository at any effort; needs external custody |
| **Repair in flight** | a defect with a named PR, not a scientific state |

The two *Not claimed* lanes are the ones most often conflated. A frozen protocol with no
result is not a negative result, and an externally blocked question is not a failed one.
Neither is evidence against the paper; neither is evidence for it.
