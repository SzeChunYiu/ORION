## Assimilation rule

A nearest-work mechanism is not merely cited. It must receive one disposition:

- **ADOPT** — same assumptions and role survive in ORION;
- **ADAPT** — mechanism survives but representation/authority boundary changes;
- **COMPOSE** — useful only as part of a larger ORION mechanic;
- **DEFER** — promising but the prerequisites/evidence are absent;
- **REJECT** — incompatible with ORION invariants or falsified for the scoped use.

Publication novelty is never inferred from the count of absorbed mechanisms. The more ORION successfully absorbs, the smaller and sharper the surviving claim should become.
