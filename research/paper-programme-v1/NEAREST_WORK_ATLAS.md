## Assimilation rule

A nearest-work mechanism is not merely cited. It receives one disposition:

- **ADOPT** — same assumptions and role survive in ORION;
- **ADAPT** — mechanism survives but representation/authority boundary changes;
- **COMPOSE** — useful as one component of a larger ORION mechanic;
- **DEFER** — promising but prerequisites/evidence are absent;
- **REJECT** — incompatible with ORION invariants or falsified for the scoped use.

A disposition describes what ORION should learn from the work; it does not import the work's empirical claims or authority. Every adopted/adapted/composed mechanism retains its external evidence identity and its assumptions/failure modes.

Publication novelty is never inferred from the count of absorbed mechanisms. The more ORION successfully absorbs, the smaller and sharper the surviving claim should become.
