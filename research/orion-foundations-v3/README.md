# ORION Foundations V3 — theory-complete, execution-separated

**Programme parent:** ORION issue #1220  
**Authority:** `THEORY_FORMALISM_COMPLETE_FOR_DECLARED_WORKFLOW_CLASS__EMPIRICAL_AND_EXTERNAL_VERIFICATION_PENDING`  
**Paper authority delta:** `NONE`

This package replaces a finite-fixture-first presentation with a complete mathematical theory for the declared ORION workflow class. It supplies:

- a primitive operational semantics independent of the desired theorem;
- the six-witness Scientific Advance normal form and the four-factor SSTF quotient;
- complete theorem statements and derivations OSTC-T0 through OSTC-T23;
- eight programme-level breakthrough laws;
- 77 paper-level theorems with explicit proofs for P1–P15;
- an explicit P1 theory supersession and ownership boundary;
- machine-readable programme and paper theorem ledgers plus an assumption ledger;
- a pinned Lean 4 mechanized core and theorem-by-theorem formalization boundary;
- a CI-bound completion receipt;
- a frozen execution-only backlog and an execution-agent prompt.

The remaining work is deliberately limited to computation, independent proof reconstruction/review, external adjudication, protected execution, and novelty review. Later execution agents may instantiate, test, refute, or narrow the theory; they may not silently redesign it after outcomes.
