# P1–P15 complete upward theory map V1

This document fixes one irreducible theoretical object per paper. Later computation may confirm, refute, or narrow an instantiation; it may not redesign the theorem after outcomes.

## P1 — Minimal responsibility-conditioned epistemic mutation

### Formal object
Let formulations form a poset `(F,<=)` ordered by exposed distinctions. For responsibility `r`, let `Pi_r` be the required target partition and `Pi_f` the partition exposed by formulation `f`. Define adequacy `A_r(f)` iff `Pi_f` refines `Pi_r`.

### P1-T1 Minimal repair theorem
The valid repairs above `f` are

\[
Rep_r(f)=\{g\ge f:A_r(g)\}.
\]

Minimal repairs are exactly the minimal elements of this upset. If adequacy is generated by a closure operator `cl_r`, then `cl_r(f)` is the unique least repair. Any strict subrepair fails to expose at least one required distinction.

### P1-T2 Failure-attribution impossibility
A failure transcript identifies a responsible layer only if the intervention-response signatures of candidate causes are distinct. Otherwise the correct disposition is a set-valued diagnosis or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

### P1-T3 Recursive reconstruction termination
A reconstruction controller terminates when every accepted repair strictly decreases a well-founded unresolved-obligation rank and retains negative-history constraints.

### P1-T4 Protected adoption
A diagnosis or proposed reframe never authorizes its own adoption; P5/T19 supplies the general custody theorem.

### Computational instantiation only
RR1/LUNARC may test one protected tuple/family. It is not the source of these theorems and cannot narrow the theoretical object by implementation convenience.

## P2 — Assumption-indexed open-world closure

Model a world as a possibly extensible set of material routes and an observation process. T12 proves closure impossible for any extension-closed class containing observationally identical complete/incomplete worlds. Positive closure is licensed only under an explicit bounded-universe, coverage, capture, or censoring model. Route stop, source-family stop, task closure, and population completeness are distinct predicates.

## P3 — Scientific gluing and obstruction

Let source-local portraits form a finite diagram `D:C->S` with restriction/identity maps on overlaps. A global portrait is a cone over `D`. It exists iff the compatibility equations admit a cone; it is unique up to isomorphism when the overlap maps are jointly monic. Empty cone sets are gluing obstructions; several non-isomorphic cones represent irreducible plural portraits. Forced canonicalization without a cone is a scientific identity error.

## P4 — Authorized scientific full abstraction

P4 owns T2/T3 for scientific promotion. A benchmark/evaluator supports a competence or promotion claim only when its observable interface is sufficient for the target terminal and nuisance variables do not determine the label independently of competence. Promotion additionally requires entitlement and blockers, so evaluator sufficiency is necessary but not itself authority.

## P5 — Protected recursive improvement

P5 owns T19/T21 as applied to self-change. Proposal and adoption are distinct modalities. A valid adoption theorem requires protected evaluator identity, fresh-transfer evidence, negative-history retention, external adoption authority, and a well-founded change rank. Same-owner PASS signals are internal conformance, not protected improvement authority.

## P6 — Conservative certificate lifting and selective revalidation

Represent certificate dependencies as a support hypergraph over scientific coordinates. A regime change invalidates exactly support families intersecting the changed semantic slice. With one support family, the unique required revalidation set is the backward dependency slice. With alternatives, preserving a target is equivalent to restoring at least one complete family; minimum-cost repair becomes a hitting/set-selection problem. Donor-native certificates remain valid on their native objects.

## P7 — Closure-carrying regime transport

A regime transform carries task closure only through a morphism preserving obligation meaning, evidence meaning, coverage, objective, and epoch, or through explicit bridges for changed coordinates. Composition obeys T10/T13. Noncommuting regime squares produce path dependence and require reopen or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## P8 — Least non-amplifying scientific-authority algebra

Authority types form an attenuation preorder. Ordinary composition can preserve or narrow authority; widening requires a protected coercion carrying evidence. Scientific discharge is the least closure under valid inference, typed coercion, support, blocker, epoch, and revocation rules. Shared and decentralized implementations are extensionally equal when they expose the same semantics.

## P9 — Interventional failure-location identifiability

Failure causes are latent hypotheses with intervention-response signatures over information, accessibility, computation, model capacity, and method coverage. Exact diagnosis is possible iff the selected intervention set separates every pair of causes. Minimum diagnostic design is the minimum separating set. Overlapping signatures require set-valued diagnosis.

## P10 — Obstruction-certified method-language expansion

Method languages are closure systems. A genuine expansion requires an exact old-closure obstruction, an outside-closure semantic edit, and new held-out reach. Names, macros, parameter changes, or more search inside the old closure are not expansion. Minimal extensions are minimal generators of the newly reached target family under the frozen closure semantics.

## P11 — Responsibility-relative state/computation/optionality law

P11 owns offline placement. State construction trades one-time compiler work and memory against repeated downstream computation, future responsibility coverage, drift recovery, and reconstruction cost. The scalar break-even law is T16; the general object is a Pareto phase surface over vector resources and responsibility families. A compiled state is valid only for responsibilities whose partitions it refines.

## P12 — Online vector scientific metareasoning

P12 owns sequential allocation among acquisition, state construction, reasoning, verification, and recovery. Exact certificates yield zero hindsight regret; coarsened certificates produce the T17 indistinguishability lower bound. A robust allocator must either observe sufficient price/charge state or pay information-acquisition cost before acting.

## P13 — Responsibility lattice and safe scientific reuse

Responsibilities induce partitions/quotients, ordered by refinement. The coarsest state serving a responsibility family is their join. Reuse is responsibility-relative and regime-relative, not provenance-relative. Drift transports certificates exactly when the new responsibility partition remains refined and the semantic/authority bridge is valid.

## P14 — Causal governance and evaluation identifiability

Governance is a causal intervention on promotion, reopening, and stopping decisions. A governance experiment is informative only when its gates are attainable, policies do not read gold, multiple terminals are reachable, useful-discovery retention prevents blanket abstention, and adjudication is independent. The causal estimand is reduction in false scientific transitions at bounded loss of valid transitions and explicit governance cost.

## P15 — Layered execution integrity and scientific noninterference

P15 owns T20. Attribution, replay, agreement, attestation, scientific validity, and claim authority are separate coordinates. Production receipts may establish the first coordinates without implying the latter. Threshold/key-custody assumptions must be explicit. A publication event is linearizable only when the claimed occurrence, environment, content, validation, and authority records refer to one bound execution and final scientific object.

## Programme synthesis

P1–P8 primarily govern scientific-status transitions. P9–P13 govern capability origin, placement, and responsibility. P14 governs the transition policy. P15 governs occurrence-level integrity. T23 couples the layers without collapsing them.
