# P8 additive bridge — authority for learned, transferred and invented methods

**Status:** additive successor text for #423/#424. The current P8 manuscript, formal core, benchmark and peer-review package remain historical authority and are not rewritten.

## Provenance classes remain distinct

P8 records method provenance explicitly: donor realization, transferred, composed, generalized, specialized, learned prediction, invented candidate, verified candidate and adopted method. A provenance label describes where an object came from; it is not a correctness or novelty verdict.

## Typed method authority

Method authority is a product of independent coordinates: `VALIDITY`, `APPLICABILITY`, `TRANSFER`, `NOVELTY`, `UTILITY`, `ADOPTION` and `SEARCH_STOP`. Capability outputs have a declared coercion matrix. P6 fibre membership and P7 navigation grant no method-authority coordinate. P9 prediction and P10 generation are likewise read-only capability outputs: model confidence, learned applicability, structural similarity or generation novelty cannot be coerced into scientific authority.

P4 verification may support validity/applicability/transfer/utility only. A current novelty-review lane may support novelty only. P5/host governance owns adoption. Global task/search closure requires its own external task-closure authority. Illicit cross-coordinate coercions are `BLOCKED`; a legal authority lane without bound evidence remains `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## Clean promotion path

A machine-generated candidate therefore begins with all scientific coordinates unresolved. It may earn P4 validity/applicability/transfer/utility, then bounded novelty authority, and only afterward host adoption. No single lane can manufacture the whole vector. A candidate can be valid and useful but non-novel, novel-looking but invalid, or verified but not adopted.

## Forward revocation and reopen lineage

Authority is epoch/version bound rather than permanently monotone. A fresh counterexample can revoke validity/applicability/utility; prior art revokes novelty; protected harm revokes utility/adoption; representation changes reopen applicability/transfer/search-stop; evaluator changes reopen validity/utility; subject-identity changes reopen the entire authority vector. New records point to the previous digest, preserving historical truth about what was authorized at the earlier epoch instead of rewriting history.

## P9/P10 anti-laundering discriminator

`P8.P9P10AntiLaundering.v1` freezes fifteen synthetic coercion/revocation cases. It attacks P9 confidence→validity/adoption, P10 generation→novelty, P7 local exhaustion→global closure, P6 fibre→applicability, P4 validity→novelty and novelty→correctness, while retaining clean legal P4, novelty-review, host-adoption and task-closure controls. It also tests prior-art/counterexample revocation and representation/evaluator reopening. All fifteen frozen outcomes pass, yielding `P8_P9_P10_ANTI_LAUNDERING_CLEAR` for this bounded contract suite.

That terminal is derived, not declared. Each of the four published rates — contract accuracy, illicit-coercion block rate, clean legal coverage, revocation accuracy — is a guard exercise with a stated denominator, and the terminal is the non-compensatory roll-up of the four: `P8_P9_P10_ANTI_LAUNDERING_CLEAR` only when every one of them is exercised and unviolated, `…_VIOLATED` when any case fails, `…_CANNOT_CHECK` when any of the four slices is empty, because a rate over no cases is an absent measurement rather than a perfect one. Each is emitted in `terminal_basis` alongside its denominator. Until 2026-08-21 the word was a string literal beside the rates and would have been printed unchanged had none of the fifteen passed (`research/failures/2026-08-unconditional-terminal-self-issued-authority/`); the published verdict is unchanged by the repair because the shipped rates really are 1.0.

That `CLEAR` is deliberately narrow: it establishes that the implemented capability/authority type boundary rejects these frozen laundering attacks without becoming broken shut. It does not establish that a generated method is actually correct, novel, useful or worth adopting; those scientific facts still require the owning authority lanes. The suite's stated ceiling is emitted as `declared_claim_ceiling_from_input`, under that name because it is supplied by the input panel: the run reproduces it and cannot earn, widen or dispute it. The four rates also grade a panel whose fifteen `expected` labels are the shipped authority tables transcribed, so they measure that the implementation matches its own declared contract — not that the contract is right.

## Programme boundary

This bridge is the constitutional interface P9 and P10 must obey. P9 can rank, predict and learn; P10 can generate, combine and challenge. Neither can self-certify. The stronger their capabilities become, the more important it is that the authority vector remains externally earned and revocable.
