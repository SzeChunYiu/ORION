- Paganelli, Grossi, Giachero, O'Brien & Kiss, *When is randomization advantageous in quantum simulation?*, arXiv:2604.07448 (2026-04-08).

The paper develops a sparse-QSVT composite stochastic decomposition in which dominant Hamiltonian terms are handled deterministically and smaller contributions are sampled stochastically, with explicit error propagation and regime/crossover analysis.
