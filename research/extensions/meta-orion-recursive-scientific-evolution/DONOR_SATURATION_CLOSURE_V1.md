## 9. Benchmark implication

The initial RSSI pilot has already produced the correct subtraction pattern:

1. individual parent rules fail outside their owned family;
2. a simple donor union solves the first pilot families;
3. therefore those families are controls, not an ORION residual;
4. the first interaction case keeps task success identical while making naive donor-union scientific-standing migration wrong;

This is **benchmark non-vacuity only**. It does not yet beat the strongest donor meta-product, because that product may implement the coordination rule too.

The next research target is therefore exactly correct:

> implement the strongest donor coordination, then construct interaction families whose effect cannot be reduced to a trivial union or an already-owned transport/assurance rule.
