## Purpose

Every scientifically meaningful frontier atom receives its own ORION research programme. Recursion is aggressive in exploration and fail-closed in promotion.

The programme is not a claim that all attractive nouns are real atoms. Its job is the opposite: recursively decompose, compare, falsify, subsume, retain interactions, and stop when no protected scientific residual survives.

## Recursive research cell

Every atom study is run through the same independent roles:

1. **Frontier architect** — states the highest scientifically meaningful claim ceiling and candidate decomposition.
2. **Ownership / donor lead** — finds strongest prior formalism, nearest empirical work, and existing ORION owner before any novelty language.
3. **Theory lead** — seeks exact theorem, lower/upper bound, counterexample, equivalence, impossibility, or reduction.
4. **Experiment lead** — designs the smallest protected discriminator and strongest same-information/resource-matched parents.
5. **Statistical lead** — freezes endpoints, seeds, uncertainty, multiplicity, and replication semantics before outcomes.
6. **Systems lead** — exposes hidden resource movement: preprocessing, memory, model size, inference, tools, search, verifier calls, wall/energy proxies.
7. **Hostile reviewer** — attempts answer laundering, leakage, donor subsumption, alternate explanations, hidden compute, distribution artifacts, and interaction-only reductions.
8. **Replication lead** — requires fresh process/seed/domain replay and immutable receipts where feasible.
9. **Cross-domain translator** — tests whether the residual transfers to at least one materially different domain before any domain-general wording.
10. **Negative-history curator** — exports failed definitions, nulls, harmful overuse, non-identifiability, donor ownership, and reopened conditions.
11. **External authority owner** — remains separate from the evaluator and alone may promote scientific/novelty/adoption state.

## Recursive lifecycle

For atom `A`, execute:

```text
DEFINE(A)
  -> OWNERSHIP_SUBTRACTION(A)
  -> DECOMPOSE(A -> children)
  -> FORMAL_ATTACK(A)
  -> PROTECTED_DISCRIMINATOR(A)
  -> FREEZE_PROTOCOL(A)
  -> PREOUTCOME_HOSTILE_REVIEW(A)
  -> EXECUTE(A)
  -> FACTORIAL_INTERACTION_REVIEW(A, siblings)
  -> REPLICATE(A)
  -> CROSS_DOMAIN_PRESSURE(A)
  -> NOVELTY_SUBTRACTION(A)
  -> DISPOSITION(A)
  -> RECURSE(children that survive)
```

A positive parent does not make children positive. A negative parent does not forbid a child if a protected discriminator identifies a narrower residual.

## Mandatory atom packet

Each node must bind:

- atom id and parent path;
- target contract(s);
- highest claim ceiling;
- strongest donor/ORION parents;
- resource bound and information boundary;
- positive and no-atom controls;
- ablation/replacement parents;
- decomposition and interaction hypotheses;
- protected fields inaccessible to proposal/training;
- evaluator id and distinct authority owner;
- pre-outcome protocol/freeze identity;
- success, null, harmful, invalid, and cannot-check terminals;
- recursion stop rule;
- negative-history export destination.

## Recursion spawn rules

Spawn a child study when at least one holds:

1. removing the child changes a protected reachable contract under the same resource bound;
2. two matched public cases require different child semantics to separate protected outcomes;
3. a theorem/counterexample shows the parent envelope conflates distinct mechanisms;
4. the interaction analysis shows a stable child-specific effect beyond siblings;
5. donor subtraction leaves a narrower unowned residual;
6. the child changes a resource frontier even when final accuracy is matched;
7. the child changes future optionality/recoverability while current-task performance is matched;
8. the child changes a higher sufficiency rung while a lower rung is matched.

Do **not** spawn merely because a new vocabulary term can be invented.

## Frozen recursion dispositions

Use the merged common vocabulary exactly:

- `SUBSUMED`
- `ATOM_INCREMENTAL_VALUE`
- `INTERACTION_ONLY`
- `REDUNDANT_EQUIVALENT`
- `DOMAIN_SPECIFIC`
- `OVERREACH_HARMFUL`
- `NON_IDENTIFIABLE`
- `NO_INCREMENTAL_VALUE`
- `CANNOT_CHECK`
- `IRREDUCIBLE_AT_CURRENT_RESOLUTION`

Child-specific scientific terminals may be more informative, but must map to one common disposition.

## Stop rules

The merged seven bounded recursion stops remain authoritative:

- donor/existing-formalism subsumption;
- no protected discriminator;
- implementation-only split with no bounded epistemic effect;
- non-unique observationally equivalent decomposition;
- interaction term is the only meaningful object;
- split requires inaccessible/private cognition;
- anecdote-specific over-decomposition.

Additionally for Frontier V2, a branch may pause as `CANNOT_CHECK` when the only decisive test requires unavailable model, Lean, hardware, credential, or external scientific authority. That is a runtime gate, not a scientific null.

## Meta-science boundary

Automated research agents, AI Scientist systems, AM/EURISKO-style heuristic discovery, operator/library learning, and recursive meta-discovery are direct parents. ORION therefore does not claim novelty for `LLM recursively researches subproblems`.

The empirical meta-question is narrower and testable: **does bounded recursive atom governance improve discovery quality, reduce false novelty/monolithic abstractions, or improve negative-knowledge reuse relative to flat research search under matched research budgets?** This becomes its own atom study, not a premise.

## Programme terminal policy

There is no terminal called `EVERYTHING_IS_AN_ATOM`.

A successful recursive programme may end with fewer atoms than it started with. The intended output is a map of supported, subsumed, interaction-only, domain-specific, harmful, unresolved and irreducible scientific responsibilities, each with provenance and resource boundaries.
