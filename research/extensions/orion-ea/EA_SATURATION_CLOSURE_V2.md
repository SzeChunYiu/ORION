## 2. Round E — belief-bottleneck / state-estimation vocabulary

- Bayesian filtering;
- posterior belief state;
- belief bottleneck;

- belief-aware RL;
- partial observability;
- progress-aware state update.

### Material contractions

ABBEL directly trains:

```text
b_t + a_t + o_t -> b_{t+1}
```

and trains actions to condition on the posterior belief rather than full history.

Agent-BRACE learns a separate structured belief model composed of atomic natural-language claims with explicit certainty/unknown levels and jointly optimizes the policy.

ReBel uses belief-consistency supervision as a dense process-level learning signal. PABU learns selective belief retention. BeliefMem preserves multiple uncertain alternatives. Dynamic Belief Graphs learns interdependent belief dynamics from language evidence.

### Consequence

The phrase **“Epistemic Autoregression” cannot itself denote the novelty of learning an explicit recurrent belief state.** That object is occupied.

Round E: `MATERIAL_CLAIM_CONTRACTION`.

## 3. Round F — POMDP / control / typed-hybrid-state vocabulary

### Findings

### Consequence

Even the broader premise **“belief alone is insufficient; expose additional typed ledgers and gate action”** is not a clean EA novelty surface.

The ORION-specific failure/obligation/representation combination remains useful as a test object, but currently only as a composition/application residual.

## 9. Programme pivot — EAJ

The only remaining candidate commensurate with the “revolutionary” objective is:
