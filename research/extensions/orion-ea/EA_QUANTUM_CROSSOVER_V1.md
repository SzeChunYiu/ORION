# ORION-EA quantum crossover V1

**Status:** prospectively frozen transfer design; no quantum-performance claim.  
**Parents:** #957, ORION-Q, #694 obligation transport, #695 protected skill admission.  
**Purpose:** use quantum research state as an exact stress test for epistemic revision before any “self-expanding language” claim.

Quantum is useful here not because EA owns quantum reasoning, but because ORION-Q already exposes exact failure modes that ordinary conversational memory benchmarks often hide: access/oracle assumptions, representation identity, error semantics, resource costs, proof obligations and scoped failed routes.

## 1. Mapping into `EpistemicState.v0`

For each exact quantum item, model-visible EA state binds an opaque structural projection of:

```text
QuantumProblemContract
  -> semantic target claim(s)
  -> available access/interface evidence
  -> candidate route/method claims
  -> resource/error obligations
  -> representation identity
  -> failed-route records
  -> dependencies among those objects
```

The quantum evaluator retains full domain semantics and computes the protected post-intervention truth. EA sees no hidden route label or gold outcome.

## 2. Q-EA1 — free block-encoding assumption retracted

Pre-state:

- original problem supplies classical term-list access;
- route claim `C_route` depends on evidence `E_block`: “required block encoding is available under the declared construction cost”;
- downstream resource/advantage claim `C_resource` requires `C_route`;
- unrelated route `C_alt` is independently supported.

Intervention:

- retract/invalidate `E_block` because the construction receipt is absent or found invalid.

Gold EA behavior:

- retract `E_block`;
- reopen/retract `C_route` as dictated by exact item semantics;
- reopen/retract dependent `C_resource`;
- preserve `C_alt` and unrelated quantum facts;
- do **not** silently replace the original problem's access model with a stronger oracle.

Primary hostile:

- fluent answer still recommends the route but delta state remains stale → failure.

## 3. Q-EA2 — state-preparation cost revealed

Pre-state:

- two admissible algorithms are represented;
- one route's resource claim omits a previously unknown state-preparation construction cost;
- final Pareto/preference claim depends on the incomplete resource object.

Intervention:

- update the preparation-cost evidence from `UNKNOWN` to an exact/certified value.

Gold:

- update that evidence;
- invalidate/recompute only affected resource/preference claims;
- preserve semantic correctness claims that do not depend on cost;
- permit final preference to reverse or become Pareto-incomparable according to evaluator gold.

Purpose:

- separate **algorithm correctness** from **resource entitlement**.

## 4. Q-EA3 — access contract changes

Pre-state:

- route requires `CONTROLLED_HAMILTONIAN_EVOLUTION`;
- old contract supplied only ordinary Hamiltonian evolution or term-sampling access;
- route therefore has an active access blocker.

Two protected variants:

### Q-EA3a material change

A new externally supplied contract genuinely adds controlled evolution with declared cost/proof.

Gold:

- set new access/representation state;
- stale/reopen the old access-specific failure record;
- permit route reconsideration;
- preserve unrelated failures.

### Q-EA3b identity/remint only

Interface id/name changes but the constructible access capabilities are semantically unchanged.

Gold:

- no access failure reopening;
- route remains blocked.

This is the direct quantum analogue of EA F4/F5.

## 5. Q-EA4 — error norm/tolerance obligation changes

Pre-state:

- a resource/accuracy certificate is bound to a particular norm and tolerance;
- a downstream scientific claim depends on that certificate.

Intervention:

- target error semantics change under an explicit new problem contract.

Gold:

- old certificate becomes `UNKNOWN`/requires revalidation unless an exact transport law is registered;
- dependent claim reopens;
- the underlying algorithm representation need not be refuted;
- no result may remain “verified” merely because numerical accuracy looked good under the old norm.

Hostile controls:

- looser tolerance makes candidate appear cheaper;
- baseline keeps old stricter tolerance while EA silently switches.

## 6. Q-EA5 — representation remint vs genuine representation edit

Construct paired items:

### Remint

The Pauli/interface/route object gets new opaque identities but is semantically equivalent with the same registered obligations.

Expected delta: identity/version only; no failure or certificate reopening.

### Genuine edit

A semantics-preserving but materially different representation changes access/resource derivation or proof obligations.

Expected delta:

- representation transition;
- transport preserved obligations where a registered map exists;
- old nontransportable obligations become unresolved;
- scoped failures reopen only where their `required_same` conditions changed.

This consumes #694-style obligation transport as the exact parent. EA does not claim the transport law; it must predict which state coordinates require transport/reopen.

## 7. Q-EA6 — stale route failure after representation change

Pre-state:

- route/method `M1` failed because of a representation-specific implementation obstruction;
- failure record explicitly requires old representation semantics;
- raw “failed before” memory would permanently prune it.

Intervention:

- a genuine representation/interface edit removes that specific condition.

Gold:

- failure record becomes stale/reopenable;
- `M1` returns to candidate status, not verified-success status;
- a fresh execution/verification obligation is emitted/activated;
- unrelated method failures remain active.

Negative-transfer control:

- representation name changes only; failure must remain active.

## 8. Q-EA7 — independent support / theorem preservation

Pre-state:

- a theorem/resource bound has two independently valid derivations or evidence paths;
- one supporting computational receipt is invalidated.

Gold:

- invalid receipt retracts;
- theorem/claim remains active through independent support;
- any child relying specifically on the failed derivation's stronger assumptions reopens.

Purpose:

- prevent “one faulty receipt invalidates all quantum knowledge”.

## 9. Baselines

Quantum crossover uses the **same** EA baseline ladder where possible:

- typed text serialization;
- recurrent/latent state;
- State-Commitment-style committed-state baseline;
- external state/versioned memory;
- exact dependency/truth-maintenance engine;
- DGRR-style rollback;
- ORION-Q's native exact access/obligation machinery as a diagnostic ceiling;
- EA typed-delta predictor.

Domain knowledge/tool access must be matched. ORION-Q evaluator gold is never model-visible.

## 10. Primary quantum transfer metric

Do not select “best quantum route” as the EA metric. Use the same state-revision metrics as EA-1:

- delta operation F1;
- exact post-state reconstruction;
- unrelated-state preservation;
- false reopen / missed reopen;
- access-assumption laundering;
- error/resource-obligation laundering;
- stale-failure negative transfer;
- final verified route/action correctness as secondary downstream evidence.

## 11. Transfer split

At minimum:

- develop/freeze on synthetic generic EA worlds plus one subset of ORION-Q exact access/representation families;
- protect at least one materially different quantum family for final transfer;
- remint all route/interface/operator names;
- do not let family identity select the delta template;
- retain no-edit and `CANNOT_CHECK/UNKNOWN` controls.

A quantum transfer positive requires the *same* delta vocabulary and kernel semantics frozen before seeing the protected quantum outcomes.

## 12. Scientific boundary

A positive Q-EA result would support only:

> the EA state-update mechanism transfers to a structure-native quantum research-state setting with exact access/resource/failure obligations.

It would **not** by itself establish:

- a better quantum algorithm;
- a new quantum method;
- quantum advantage;
- a general scientific-reasoning architecture;
- self-expanding reasoning language.

Any new quantum scientific artifact routes back through ORION-Q's independent correctness/resource/novelty process.

## 13. Escalation to Q-EA method-language expansion

Only after EA-1 and Q-EA1–Q-EA7 demonstrate a real learned-state residual may the programme use ORION-Q's bounded method-language worlds for EA-5.

The exact requirement then remains:

```text
protected target not reachable under registered old language/budget
+ candidate new representation/operator/interface
+ semantic/access/resource proof
+ held-out transfer
+ donor-complete program/library/evolutionary search comparison
```

Until those gates exist, “self-expanding quantum reasoning language” remains an unearned future hypothesis.
