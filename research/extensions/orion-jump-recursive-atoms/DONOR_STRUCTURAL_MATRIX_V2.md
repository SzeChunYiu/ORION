# ORION recursive discovery atoms — donor structural matrix V2

**Status:** additive successor to V1. Primary-source/field-structure research through 2026-08-19. Still not literature saturation or novelty authority.

V2 adds historical machine discovery, design theory, belief revision/truth maintenance, mathematical proofs/refutations, and 2026 failure-process work. The main result of this pass is **claim subtraction**: several candidate ORION atoms have much deeper prior art than an LLM-era survey alone suggests.

## 1. Classic machine scientific discovery: concept/law/operator invention is not new

### BACON family

Patrick Langley's `Data-Driven Discovery of Physical Laws` (Cognitive Science, 1981) describes BACON.3 as using general heuristics over several levels of description to formulate hypotheses, define theoretical terms, relate experiments, collapse equivalent conditions and discover irrelevant variables. Bradshaw, Langley & Simon (Science, 1983) report computer rediscovery of quantitative laws including Black-style specific heat structure. Later reviews of BACON/GLAUBER and related systems describe discovery of empirical laws, theoretical terms and qualitative chemical concepts.

**Absorb:** data-driven theoretical-term formation, empirical law search, multilevel descriptive search, and recursive use of general discovery heuristics are historical AI-discovery prior art.

### AM / EURISKO

Lenat's `AM: An Artificial Intelligence Approach to Discovery in Mathematics as Heuristic Search` (1976) explicitly treats mathematics as development of new concepts under heuristic search; AM agenda tasks include defining/exploring concepts and inspecting empirical regularities.

Lenat's EURISKO papers (`Theory formation by heuristic search`, 1983; `EURISKO: A program that learns new heuristics and domain concepts`, 1983) make the meta-level even clearer: the system is designed to discover new heuristics, domain concepts, objects and operators because a fixed heuristic corpus limits transfer.

**Absorb:** concept invention, operator invention, heuristic invention and recursive/meta discovery are not ORION novelty primitives.

### Meta-DENDRAL / rule induction

Meta-DENDRAL and related early systems search for production rules/models under domain constraints.

**Consequence for #500/#512/#507:** a Jump cannot be defined merely as `creates a theoretical term`, `defines a new concept`, `learns an operator`, or `improves its discovery heuristics`. The residual is the bounded epistemic **necessity/grounding/preservation/novel-consequence/authority** composition under strong historical and current parents.

## 2. Computational creativity: transformational spaces have a mature formal field

- Wiggins (2006), `A preliminary framework for description, analysis and comparison of creative systems`, formalizes Boden-style exploratory and transformational creativity in search-space terms.
- Espírito Santo, Wiggins & Cardoso (arXiv:2405.02148) develops formal definitions of novelty/transformativeness using Formal Learning Theory.
- Schapiro, Black & Varshney (arXiv:2504.18687) propose a graphical theory of transformational scientific creativity via changes to axioms/rules.

**Absorb:** search-space transformation, conceptual-space transformation, and formal transformativeness are not new ORION framings.

**Residual:** scientific responsibility/necessity, protected novel consequence, old-regime correspondence, no-Jump controls, and independent authority under a resource-bounded regime.

## 3. Design/invention theory: concept and knowledge spaces already co-evolve

### C-K theory

Hatchuel & Weil's C-K design theory (ICED 2003 and subsequent advanced formulations) explicitly separates:

- `C`: concepts/propositions undecidable in current knowledge;
- `K`: currently established knowledge;

with `C->C`, `C->K`, `K->C`, and `K->K` operations supporting joint concept/knowledge expansion.

### Design prototypes

Gero's `Design Prototypes: A Knowledge Representation Schema for Design` (AI Magazine 1990) provides a knowledge representation framework for design and distinguishes routine, innovative, and creative design.

### Geneplore / preinventive structures

Creative-cognition work separates generation of preinventive structures from later exploration/interpretation and studies emergent/unanticipated properties.

**Absorb:** speculative concept space distinct from current knowledge, co-expansion of concept/knowledge, and generation/exploration phases are mature design/creativity structures.

**Consequence:** engineering/everyday invention needs validation by function/affordance/construction, not only scientific truth. Prototype/construction may often be an **epistemic interface between concept and knowledge spaces**, rather than a universal standalone atom.

## 4. Tool/affordance innovation is an existing formal problem

Collis, Kinghorn & Buckley, `Understanding Tool Discovery and Tool Innovation Using Active Inference` (arXiv:2311.03893), explicitly distinguishes tool discovery from tool innovation and models affordances as hidden-state factors that can support offline induction of a required tool property.

Robotics affordance literature separately studies object-action-effect relations and transfer.

**Absorb:** tool innovation/affordance discovery are direct parent fields.

**Possible ORION residual:** source/prototype lineage + cross-domain analogy + bounded functional reach + negative controls + scientific/engineering validation composition—not tool innovation itself.

## 5. Analogy / cross-domain correspondence is a strong generation parent

- Farmer, `Abduction Without a Body?` (arXiv:2608.02505): representation generation, motif extraction, canonicalization, cross-domain retrieval, identity-hypothesis generation, adversarial verification, abstention.
- Shen, Druckmann & Zou, `Unlocking LLM Creativity in Science through Analogical Reasoning` (arXiv:2605.11258): cross-domain relational analogies expand scientific solution generation and improve biomedical tasks in the reported study.
- Design-by-analogy literature treats analogy as a process-level design mechanism across representation forms.

**Absorb:** generative cross-domain analogy is not an ORION atom by default.

**Residual test:** find useful *distant* structural correspondences that unlock protected contracts while rejecting false analogies, using P3-style mapping/obstruction validation and matched retrieval/analogy parents.

## 6. Question/conjecture generation is also parent-occupied

- AutoDiscovery (arXiv:2507.00310): open-ended scientific question/hypothesis exploration via Bayesian surprise/MCTS.
- FirstResearch (arXiv:2607.05682): auditable Research Question Certificate with primitives, assumptions, mechanism, tension, falsifier, decisive test and failure update.
- Mathematical conjecture generation systems formalize conjecture spaces and use structural/invariance search; current LLM/formal pipelines add proof/checking loops.

**Absorb:** open-ended question generation, tension-based question certificates, and mathematical conjecture generation are existing fields.

**Residual:** question generation that materially expands protected downstream scientific reach under independent significance/validity authority, beyond surprise/certificate/conjecture parents.

## 7. Reusable operator/library learning is mature

DreamCoder, Stitch (arXiv:2211.16605), and LILO (arXiv:2310.19791) learn reusable abstractions/libraries from solution corpora. Library growth can change bounded search/derivation cost without a scientific regime Jump.

**Absorb:** compression-driven reusable operator discovery.

**Residual:** prove/establish that the current operator closure is inadequate for a protected contract, then generate a genuinely absent reusable operator that transfers—closer to P10 #427/#430 than a core ORION atom.

## 8. Scientific concept formation has direct formalization

Aguilar & Aguirre, `How are Scientific Concepts Birthed?` (arXiv:2509.10740), formalizes theoretical-physics concept formation with cognitive typing rules (distinction, property preservation, concept change) composed with algebraic/functional operations and demonstrates typed program-synthesis reconstruction of a historical path.

**Absorb:** typed concept-transformation rules and concept-formation-as-program-synthesis are donor-owned structures.

**#512 residual:** bounded protected concept generation under contamination resistance, correspondence, fresh consequences, transfer, and no-concept controls versus these/classic/modern parents.

## 9. Thought experiments / imaginary spaces

Duraisamy, `Active Inference AI Systems for Scientific Discovery` (arXiv:2506.21329), explicitly advocates exploratory reasoning in imaginary/counterfactual spaces, including temporary relaxation of physical constraints, followed by deterministic/empirical validation. Bayesian experimental design, MDA, and counterexample-guided methods own strong fixed-regime discrimination baselines.

**Absorb:** imaginary-space exploration and assumption relaxation as scientific-discovery proposal mechanisms.

**#511 residual:** registered frame/assumption manipulations must outperform ordinary experimental design on frozen cases where the latter cannot discriminate under the same bound.

## 10. Useful discovery without Jump

Ota, Osa & Harada, `Self-Supervised Theorem Discovery in a Formal Axiomatic System` (arXiv:2606.28747), demonstrates theorem/lemma discovery inside a fixed formal axiomatic system.

Classic AM/BACON systems also show impressive discovery under largely human-specified representational/operator frameworks.

**Mandatory control:** `SEARCH_DISCOVERY / LIBRARY_GROWTH / NO_JUMP` must remain first-class. Discovery and creativity are not synonymous with regime change.

## 11. Mathematical failure can generate concepts: Lakatos parent

Lakatos, `Proofs and Refutations`, treats mathematical growth as an interaction among conjectures, proofs, counterexamples, proof analysis and concept/definition changes. Counterexamples can lead to lemma incorporation, domain restriction, monster/exception handling, or proof-generated concepts.

**Absorb:** failure-to-concept/theorem revision in mathematics has a deep methodology.

**Failure benchmark consequence:** distinguish legitimate local theorem repair, legitimate concept revision, and ad hoc scope-changing that merely protects a claim from counterexamples.

## 12. Belief revision / truth maintenance / nogoods are deep prior art for failure semantics

### Doyle TMS

Doyle, `A truth maintenance system` (Artificial Intelligence 1979), records reasons for beliefs, revises beliefs when discoveries contradict assumptions, and uses dependency-directed backtracking/explanations.

### de Kleer ATMS

`An assumption-based TMS` (Artificial Intelligence 1986) reasons over sets of assumptions, maintains inconsistent/nogood environments, supports multiple solutions and context switching.

### AGM / iterated belief revision

Alchourrón, Gärdenfors & Makinson (1985) formalize contraction/revision of theories. Darwiche & Pearl (Artificial Intelligence 1997) show that rational iterated revision needs epistemic-state information beyond the current belief set alone.

### Constraint/nogood learning / CEGIS

Conflict/nogood learning and counterexample-guided synthesis already use scoped negative information to avoid repeated dead ends.

**Absorb:** context-dependent negative constraints, reason maintenance, contraction/revision, reopen/context switching and failure-guided search are not ORION novelty.

**#508 residual:** scientific responsibility across theory/measurement/execution/representation/objective/evaluator, transport/staleness across regime changes, preserved certified successes/live alternatives, publication/visibility, independent refutation authority, and fresh transfer value over these formal parents.

## 13. Failure is a process; silent failure matters

Recent direct agent studies strengthen the chronology:

- Fei et al., `How Do Agents Fail on AutoResearch` (arXiv:2608.14905): 100 frontier research tasks, 8 harness-model combinations, 800 trajectories, 45 process-level failure patterns; argues for a metacognitive check/revise/question loop.
- Rawat & Flek, `Plausible but Wrong` (arXiv:2604.25345): scientific workflows can return physically inconsistent but plausible outputs without visible error/self-diagnosis.
- Zhao et al., `Failure as a Process` (arXiv:2607.09510): studies failure onset/evolution/recovery across complete coding-agent trajectories.
- Trehan & Chopra (arXiv:2601.03315): autonomous-research failures include implementation drift, context degradation, premature success and weak experimental taste.
- Sun et al. (arXiv:2606.31270): failed trajectories can improve later computer-use behavior.

**Absorb:** failure-driven improvement and process-level failure analysis.

**Framework consequence:** failure chronology is at least:

```text
OCCURS
-> BECOMES OBSERVABLE
-> DETECTED
-> RECOGNIZED MATERIAL
-> RESPONSIBILITY INVESTIGATED
-> NEGATIVE KNOWLEDGE FROZEN
```

A memory/revision system can be perfect downstream yet useless if silent failure is never detected.

## 14. Negative-result visibility is a separate epistemic coordinate

Franco, Malhotra & Simonovits (Science 2014) provide direct evidence of publication bias in a cohort of known social-science studies: strong results were more likely to be published while null results were more likely not to be written up. Broader systematic-review/meta-science literature supports the file-drawer/publication-bias problem. Chauhan (arXiv:2606.04220) specifically pressures AI-scientist pipelines trained on literature missing negative results; treat its quantitative amplification model as a hypothesis, not settled fact.

**Framework consequence:** `failure truth/quality` and `failure visibility/accessibility` must remain separate coordinates.

## 15. What remains meaningfully ORION-shaped after V2

The research surface has narrowed considerably. The strongest remaining candidates are not generic creativity mechanisms but **compositions/discipline**:

1. **bounded reachability witness** across heterogeneous validation domains;
2. **responsibility-gated expansion**: evidence for why search/model/operator/representation/question expansion is warranted;
3. **no-Jump controls** and false-expansion/overjump penalties;
4. **source/cutoff/hindsight discipline** for extracting morphology from historical episodes;
5. **scoped scientific failure knowledge** with representation/measurement/objective/evaluator compatibility and reopen rules;
6. **correspondence/preservation/reopening** across regime change;
7. **protected novel consequence** not available to proposer;
8. **proposal/evaluator/authority separation**;
9. **recursive atom subtraction**: donor subsumption, non-identifiability and interaction-only effects are valid terminals.

This is a smaller and more defensible programme than `ORION invents concepts/operators/questions/affordances`.
