Date: 2026-08-19  
Issue: #506

## Bounded candidate terminal

`USEFUL_MORPHOLOGY_BASIS_FOUND`

The word **useful** is load-bearing. This packet does not claim a universal ontology of discovery, a cognitive process model, or a novel set of epistemic atoms. It finds that a ten-coordinate **public-artifact morphology** is stable enough across a frozen 51-episode corpus and two fresh source-challenge rounds to organize future bounded/prospective ORION studies.

Issue closure remains an external repository action after the stacked schema/CI gates survive.

## Expert-panel result

The closure was pressure-tested through four roles.

1. **History of science / source custody** — only source-grounded public artifacts and explicitly documented results are treated as facts; private cognition is `CANNOT_INFER`.
2. **Formal morphology** — tags describe structural relations in the published/patented artifact and may overlap; they are not asserted atomic or mutually exclusive.
3. **Engineering/design** — function, affordance, architecture and interface changes remain distinct from truth-valued scientific claims so engineering/everyday invention is not forced into hypothesis-only language.
4. **Hostile leakage/evaluation** — famous cases remain mechanism-extraction data only; explicit fixed-regime controls block hindsight from turning every major result into a Jump.

The panel converges on a smaller result than a new creativity theory: **the common research coordinates are useful, while most of their semantics are donor-owned.**

# 1. Frozen corpus

`DISCOVERY_MORPHOLOGY_ATLAS_V1.json` contains **51 source-grounded episodes**.

| domain bucket | episodes |
| --- | ---: |
| mathematics / formal | 16 |
| empirical science | 15 |
| engineering / design | 12 |
| everyday / practical | 8 |
| **total** | **51** |

The corpus spans nineteenth-century mathematics/science and patents through modern algorithmic/scientific discovery systems. It is not intended to estimate the population frequency of discovery mechanisms; diversity is used as a structural stress test.

## Source/cognition boundary

Every row requires:

- a primary publication, primary patent, archival proposal, or direct reproduction of a primary historical source;
- a modest documented-artifact claim;
- `private_cognitive_sequence = CANNOT_INFER`;
- morphology role `STRUCTURAL_INTERPRETATION`;
- at least two competing interpretations;
- `MECHANISM_EXTRACTION_ONLY` historical use;
- `protected_confirmatory_gold = false`.

This prevents a later textbook narrative such as “X invented Y by imagining Z” from silently becoming machine-readable cognitive supervision.

# 2. Explicit no-Jump controls

Five cases are intentionally labeled `NO_JUMP_CONTROL` and carry `FIXED_REGIME_SEARCH_CONTROL`:

1. AlphaTensor — new matrix-multiplication algorithms inside a registered tensor-decomposition game/evaluator;
2. AlphaDev — new sorting routines inside an instruction-level search game;
3. FunSearch — program search/evolution under a fixed evaluator;
4. self-supervised theorem discovery inside a fixed axiom/inference system;
5. 51 Pegasi b — an important empirical discovery inferred within an established radial-velocity measurement/modeling regime.

These controls encode a central #500/#501 falsifier:

```text
important new result != evidence of representational regime invention
```

A discovery may be scientifically major while remaining `SEARCH_DISCOVERY / NO_JUMP` at the registered resolution.

# 3. Ten-coordinate public-artifact morphology

## `REPRESENTATION_CHANGE`

Material change to coordinates, descriptive objects, or organization used to state/derive the contract.

Frozen support: **12 episodes / 3 domain buckets**.

Strong parents include scientific representation theory, representation/abstraction learning and P3/P7. Lexical renaming does not qualify.

## `NEW_OBJECT_OR_STATE_VARIABLE`

A new load-bearing public object/state variable/latent entity or explicitly modeled component.

Frozen support: **10 / 3 domains**.

BACON/GLAUBER, AM/EURISKO and latent-variable/representation-learning work are mandatory parent fields. The label does not imply ORION novelty for theoretical-term invention.

## `ABSTRACTION_EQUIVALENCE_STRUCTURE`

Abstraction, equivalence, quotient, coding/diagonalization or comparable structural identification that changes the public derivational space.

Frozen support: **5 / 2 domains**.

This remains a broad descriptive family, not a claim that all abstractions are one cognitive atom.

## `NEW_LAW_CONSTRAINT_OR_INVARIANT`

A load-bearing law, formal constraint, invariant, conservation/symmetry relation, or comparable public regularity.

Frozen support: **10 / 2 domains**.

Symbolic law discovery, theorem discovery and symmetry/invariance methods are direct parents.

## `BRIDGE_CORRESPONDENCE`

An explicit structural relation between previously separate descriptions/domains/representations enabling transport or composition.

Frozen support: **6 / 3 domains**.

P3/P7, identity-abduction and design-by-analogy/relational-transfer work are direct owners. Surface similarity is insufficient.

## `NEW_OPERATOR_PROCEDURE`

A reusable algorithm, proof/search/construction operator, experimental/manufacturing procedure or action schema.

Frozen support: **18 / all 4 domains**.

This is the only V1 dimension spanning every frozen domain bucket. AM/EURISKO, program synthesis, library learning and procedure/experiment design are direct parents.

## `SYSTEM_COMPOSITION_ARCHITECTURE`

Components/interfaces/subprocedures reorganized into a public architecture whose composition is load-bearing for function or inference.

Frozen support: **13 / 3 domains**.

Systems architecture and C-K/design-prototype work are strong parents.

## `INTERFACE_MEASUREMENT_REDESIGN`

Material change to observation, intervention, manipulation, measurement, experimental or human-machine interface.

Frozen support: **8 / 3 domains**.

Experiment/measurement design and #459 interface adequacy own core semantics. The tag itself grants no scientific authority.

## `FUNCTION_AFFORDANCE_INVENTION`

A new useful public function/affordance under engineering or practical constraints.

Frozen support: **16 / 3 domains**.

This coordinate is necessary to avoid reducing design to a truth-valued claim. C-K/design theory and affordance/tool-innovation parents are direct comparisons.

## `FIXED_REGIME_SEARCH_CONTROL`

An important result produced inside a registered language/operator/evaluator or observational regime and retained as a non-Jump control.

Frozen support: **5 / 2 domains**.

This is a control coordinate rather than a creative transformation.

# 4. Why this basis is not a new atom claim

The donor subtraction is substantial:

- classic machine-discovery systems already introduced terms/concepts/laws;
- AM/EURISKO already searched concepts, objects, operators and heuristics;
- C-K theory already formalizes concept/knowledge co-expansion for design;
- analogy/identity-abduction already owns structural correspondence generation;
- representation learning and mathematical abstraction own splits/lifts/equivalences/latents;
- program synthesis/library learning owns reusable operator discovery;
- experiment design and measurement theory own large parts of interface change;
- affordance/tool-innovation theory owns major function/repurposing mechanisms.

Therefore the defensible ORION residual in #506 is a **typed common research morphology plus strict source/hindsight boundaries**, not novelty for the coordinate names.

# 6. Executable hostile closure

`build_discovery_morphology_closure_report()` recomputes the corpus properties rather than trusting prose.

It checks:

- exact 51-episode corpus and exact 16/15/12/8 domain counts;
- exactly ten registered dimensions;
- frozen per-dimension support counts;
- ≥5 episode support and ≥2 domain support for every dimension;
- at least one dimension spanning all four domains;
- unique episode/dimension identities;
- source locator and documented artifact claim presence;
- primary-source-kind requirement;
- `CANNOT_INFER` private cognition;
- `STRUCTURAL_INTERPRETATION` morphology role;
- ≥2 distinct competing interpretations;
- no protected historical gold;
- explicit fixed-regime tags on every no-Jump control;
- basis support metadata matching the actual atlas;
- two post-basis rounds / eight primary sources / zero new dimensions.

Hostile tests force `CANNOT_CHECK` for private-cognition leakage, historical-gold promotion, malformed controls, unknown morphology, basis metadata drift and material post-freeze novelty.

The report grants no historical-cognition, atom-independence, Jump, novelty, protected-benchmark or issue-closure authority.

# 8. Limitations / nonclaims

This corpus is deliberately broad, but it remains a curated research atlas rather than a statistically representative sample. Episode tags are reviewer structural interpretations; overlapping tags are expected. The finite stability rounds cannot prove completeness over all future science, mathematics, engineering or invention.

The closure therefore supports only the bounded statement:

> Across the frozen 51 source-grounded episodes and two post-freeze challenge rounds, a ten-coordinate public-artifact morphology is sufficient to organize the observed structural changes while retaining explicit no-Jump controls and strict hindsight/cognition boundaries.

That is enough for `USEFUL_MORPHOLOGY_BASIS_FOUND`; it is not enough for a universal discovery ontology or a positive ORION Jump claim.
