Date: 2026-08-19

## Proposed bounded terminal

`RECURSIVE_ATOM_STUDY_CALCULUS_FROZEN`

This is a **method/governance result**, not a discovery-capability result. It says ORION now has a stable bounded way to ask whether a proposed epistemic mechanic deserves to exist, decompose it when warranted, stop when the residual is donor-owned/non-identifiable/interaction-only, and retain the negative evidence.

The terminal is earned only after the dependency PRs merge through their exact-head gates and this closure PR itself passes exact-head repository verification.

## Why one common calculus survives the pilots

The first six pilot questions do not share a substantive scientific mechanic; they share a **study contract**.

The Jump-side pilots pressure:

- J1 / #510: matched public success with different protected opportunity semantics;
- J2 / #511: independently removable hypothetical-operation reach;
- J3 / #512: independently removable structural-transformation reach.

The failure-side pilots pressure:

- F1 / #516: matched public trajectory with different protected material-failure semantics;
- F2 / #517: parent-formalism subsumption of a proposed new extraction primitive;
- F3 / #518: semantic reduction of staleness to an existing transport owner.

A common packet is still useful because each study must bind the same governance coordinates: regime, contracts, information/resource bound, positive and no-atom cases, strongest parents, ablations/replacements, decomposition/interaction hypotheses, stop rule, protected fields, evaluator and external authority.

The scientific outcomes are intentionally allowed to differ.

## Factorial/interaction semantics

The calculus adopts #513's set-level interaction report as bookkeeping, not authority.

For child candidates `a` and `b`, the study records deltas for `a`, `b`, and `a+b` under the same registered regime/information/resource bound. This exposes:

- child-specific reachable contracts;
- shared individual contracts;
- synergy contracts reachable only in combination;
- contracts lost in combination.

The closure packet then demonstrates four required interpretations:

1. **standalone effect** -> child may deserve a separate bounded study (`ATOM_INCREMENTAL_VALUE` at this resolution);
2. **synergy only** -> `INTERACTION_ONLY`, not two fictional standalone atoms;
3. **same individual reach and no pair gain** -> `REDUNDANT_EQUIVALENT` for the registered case;
4. **children not observationally separable** -> `NON_IDENTIFIABLE`, regardless of attractive labels.

The interaction report itself continues to grant no atom-independence claim.

## Bounded recursion stops

The calculus freezes all seven #507 stops:

- donor/existing-formalism subsumption;
- no protected discriminator;
- implementation-only split with no bounded epistemic effect;
- non-unique observationally equivalent decompositions;
- interaction term is the only independently meaningful object;
- further split requires inaccessible/private cognition;
- further split would become anecdote-specific rather than reusable.

`IRREDUCIBLE_AT_CURRENT_RESOLUTION` is therefore operational: it means the registered study cannot justify another scientifically meaningful split. It is not a metaphysical assertion about the final atoms of reasoning.

## Negative-history feed

Every study must preserve at least eight negative categories:

- failed definition;
- failed decomposition;
- null ablation;
- harmful overuse;
- donor subsumption;
- false positive;
- inactive/no-atom condition;
- unresolved/non-identifiable case.

#507 does not create a second failure-memory architecture. The durable handoff is deliberately composed from the existing programme:

- #514 `FailureEpisode.v1` / episode provenance for source-faithful chronology;
- #513 `FailureKnowledge.v1` for a non-authorizing scoped negative lesson;
- #518/P7 for semantic applicability/reopening across regime change;
- #508 for the broader empirical failure programme.

## Nonclaims

This closure does not establish:

- a universal atomic basis of scientific reasoning;
- that any J1/J2/J3/F1 child implementation is scientifically effective;
- a universal opportunity detector, imagination engine or concept generator;
- a universal failure ontology;
- Jump capability;
- novelty of recursive meta-discovery (AM/EURISKO and later operator/library-learning parents already pressure that claim);
- publication readiness.

The result is smaller and useful: **ORION can now subject proposed epistemic mechanics to a common bounded recursive study without forcing proliferation, positive terminals, or self-authority.**
