## Expert-panel synthesis

Four independent lenses were used:

1. **Formal epistemics / identifiability** — can the broad label be determined from the registered public state, and do candidate children have independently removable bounded-reach effects?
2. **Scientific-agent / experiment design** — distinguish ordinary active experiment selection from operations that change assumptions, frames, coupled systems or formal worlds.
3. **Representation / conceptual-change theory** — compare latent-object, split, quotient, lift and bridge transformations against P7, C-K, classic machine discovery, representation learning and analogy parents.
4. **Hostile evaluation** — preserve zero-error no-opportunity controls, ordinary-experiment controls, lexical-renaming controls, protected-answer separation and explicit authority boundaries.

The common conclusion is architectural subtraction: retain #513's generic proposal records, but do not promote the broad nouns `tension`, `thought experiment`, or `concept invention` into single permanent runtime mechanics merely because they are useful study labels.

# J1 — epistemic opportunity/tension

## Why zero prediction error is not the discriminator

A zero-error state can coexist with very different structural situations:

- two local theories may be exact yet lack a verified bridge/unification;
- a transformation pattern may be a genuine invariant or a coincidence;
- a cross-domain resemblance may be a structural correspondence or only surface analogy;
- an unusual artifact feature may expose a usable affordance or merely be novel.

The frozen pairs make ordinary public success/error signals identical inside each pair while protected opportunity materiality differs. Consequently, a deterministic signal using only that matched public tuple cannot classify both members correctly. The separating information must be supplied by the relevant structural witness family or the state remains unresolved.

This is not a proof that opportunity recognition is impossible. It is a proof that the broad label does not own the semantics of its evidence sources.

## Parent-field pressure

The previous #506/#507 donor sweep already found close parents for major subfamilies:

- Bayesian surprise / uncertainty / anomaly / MDL parents for statistical or compression signals;
- Farmer's representation-grounded identity abduction and Shen et al.'s cross-domain analogical reasoning for correspondence opportunities;
- design/affordance theory for functional opportunity;
- symmetry/invariance and constraint reasoning for transformation-stable structure;
- question-selection and open-ended scientific-agent work for problem/opportunity ranking.

The current research refresh also makes broad `interestingness` an unsafe substitute: scientist-in-the-loop work reports substantial divergence/mode-collapse and evaluator-alignment limitations in open-ended scientific ideation.

## Frozen matched pairs

1. unification deficit vs legitimate domain specialization;
2. stable invariant vs symmetry-looking coincidence;
3. verified structural analogy vs surface analogy;
4. executable new affordance vs novelty without function.

Each pair has a distinct external witness family and keeps ordinary public success/error state matched.

## J1 disposition

Candidate terminal: `MULTIPLE_TENSION_ATOMS_REQUIRED`.

`EpistemicTensionCandidate.v1` should remain a generic non-authorizing record for registering a hypothesized tension and its required/defeating witnesses. The result does not justify a universal tension detector and does not establish any child detector's efficacy.

# J2 — thought-experiment / counterfactual-experience synthesis

## The umbrella problem

`Thought experiment` groups operations with different semantics:

- choose an ordinary intervention inside the incumbent model;
- relax or negate an assumption;
- change observer/reference frame;
- couple previously separate systems/domains;
- construct a formal counterexample/toy world.

Calling each output a hypothetical setup does not make the transformations equivalent.

## Finite independent-reach check

The packet freezes five contracts, each requiring one operation. With all operations enabled, all five contracts are reachable. Remove exactly one operation: its corresponding contract becomes unreachable while the other four remain reachable.

That gives each operation an independently removable bounded-reach effect at the registered resolution. In particular, the ordinary physical intervention case is deliberately marked `special_thought_operation = false`; it remains an active/Bayesian experiment-design control rather than being rebranded as ORION imagination.

The four special cases are:

1. assumption relaxation uniquely identifies which incumbent postulate causes a conflict;
2. frame transformation uniquely exposes frame-dependent vs invariant structure;
3. system coupling uniquely separates local theories through coupled behavior;
4. formal counterexample construction uniquely separates conjectures.

## Donor pressure

Duraisamy explicitly treats imaginary/counterfactual-space exploration and deterministic validation as complementary modes rather than one monolithic recipe. Counterexample-guided methods already own formal counterexample construction; active/Bayesian design owns ordinary interventions; representation and causal literatures own frame/intervention semantics; composition/coupling is its own structural operation family.

## J2 disposition

Candidate terminal: `MULTIPLE_IMAGINATION_ATOMS_REQUIRED`.

`ThoughtExperiment.v1` remains useful as a proposal envelope: it records `operation_kind`, assumptions held/relaxed, a setup, expected competing outcomes, execution mode, cost and safety constraints. The broad record cannot be used as evidence that one universal imagination operator exists.

# J3 — concept / primitive invention

## Classic discovery already crosses the naive novelty boundary

The inherited donor sweep is decisive about what ORION may not claim:

- BACON-family systems introduced theoretical terms while discovering empirical laws;
- GLAUBER reconstructed qualitative chemical concepts;
- AM developed new mathematical concepts through heuristic search;
- EURISKO extended this toward domain concepts/objects/operators and heuristics;
- C-K design theory explicitly separates speculative concept space from established knowledge and studies their joint expansion;
- library/abstraction learning synthesizes reusable abstractions/operators;
- representation learning and causal refactorization cover additional structural changes;
- analogy/identity-abduction work covers bridge/correspondence generation.

Therefore `system proposes a new named primitive` is not an ORION-specific discriminator.

## Finite independent-transformation check

The packet freezes five transformations, each with a distinct contract, parent family and preservation obligation:

1. `INTRODUCE_LATENT_OBJECT` — hidden mechanism enables intervention transfer;
2. `SPLIT_CONTEXTUAL_VARIABLE` — incompatible local laws become context-resolved while local successes survive;
3. `QUOTIENT_IDENTIFY_STATES` — identification exposes an invariant under the bound;
4. `LIFT_RELATIONAL_REPRESENTATION` — a nonlocal mechanism becomes locally derivable under the resource bound;
5. `BRIDGE_CORRESPONDENCE` — two exact local theories compose into a transfer contract.

With all transformations present, all five contracts are reachable. Removing one transformation removes its own contract while preserving the others. At this resolution the transformations therefore have independent bounded-reach semantics rather than being one atomic operation.

## J3 disposition

Candidate terminal: `MULTIPLE_CONCEPT_ATOMS_REQUIRED`.

`ConceptCandidate.v1` should remain a strict proposal envelope around an explicit transformation trace, structural declarations, semantic judgments, correspondence, claimed unlocked contracts and protected consequence requests. Its existing `PROPOSAL_ONLY` and no-validity/no-novelty/no-adoption boundaries are exactly the right interpretation.

This terminal does not prove that all five children deserve new ORION implementations. Each child must first compare against its strongest parent and may terminate as donor-subsumed.

# Consequences for #507 / #500 / #501

## #507 recursive atom calculus

Together with the failure-side pilot packet, these studies exercise several legitimate recursion outcomes:

- broad-label decomposition by observational non-identifiability (J1/F1);
- finite independent-reach decomposition (J2/J3);
- parent-formalism subsumption (F2);
- cross-owner semantic reduction (F3).

That supports keeping one generic `BoundedEpistemicAtomStudy.v1` rather than one class per attractive noun. It still does **not** close #507: the full #506 ownership map, factorial interaction protocol across surviving children, negative-history feed, recursion-depth/non-identifiability bookkeeping, and two no-material-change rounds remain parent deliverables.

## #500 / #501 Jump programme

Nothing here establishes a successful Jump. The result only says a future `JUMP()` controller should not assume the causal chain is one tension atom -> one imagination atom -> one concept atom. Evidence and transformation families may be plural, donor-owned, domain-specific or interaction-only.

A positive protected Jump still needs:

- honest old-regime bounded insufficiency or `UNRESOLVED`;
- a proposal frozen before protected consequences;
- preserved/reopened old knowledge under explicit transport;
- protected novel consequence / proof / function;
- independent verification;
- external adoption/authority.

## #513

No destructive rewrite is justified. Keep the proposal records generic:

- `EpistemicTensionCandidate.v1` records witness-relative tension hypotheses;
- `ThoughtExperiment.v1` records the exact operation kind rather than defining a universal taxonomy;
- `ConceptCandidate.v1` records an ordered transformation trace and remains `PROPOSAL_ONLY`.

That is compatible with the decomposition result.
