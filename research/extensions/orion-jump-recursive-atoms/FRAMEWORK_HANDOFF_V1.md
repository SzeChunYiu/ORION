## 2. Research ownership after donor absorption

### Concept formation

Aguilar & Aguirre (arXiv:2509.10740) already formalize scientific concept formation using cognitive typing rules and typed program synthesis. ORION therefore treats **typed concept transformation, distinction, property preservation, and concept change as direct prior art/donor structure**. The residual is whether a bounded ORION system can select/generate useful transformation traces on protected invented worlds, with correspondence and novel consequences, beyond these and other strong parents.

### Cross-domain abduction / analogy

Farmer (arXiv:2608.02505) supplies a representation-grounded Abduction Loop for identity abduction; Shen, Druckmann & Zou (arXiv:2605.11258) provide strong analogical-reasoning pressure. Cross-domain analogy/correspondence is therefore a mandatory baseline and likely child atom, not an ORION novelty phrase.

### Thought experiments / imagination

Duraisamy (arXiv:2506.21329) explicitly proposes exploration of imaginary counterfactual spaces, including temporary violation of physical laws, coupled to later validation. ORION's #511 residual must be narrower: can a registered assumption/frame manipulation produce a more decision-relevant discriminator than ordinary experimental design under a frozen family and budget?

### Regime transitions

Wang & Buehler (arXiv:2606.01444) already formalize verified representational-regime transitions and provenance-preserving transport. ORION's higher-order mechanics, P3, and P7 should consume that as direct structure rather than claim first regime-change formalization.

### Discovery without Jump

Ota, Osa & Harada (arXiv:2606.28747) show self-supervised theorem discovery inside a fixed formal axiomatic system. This is a mandatory negative control for #500/#506: useful discovery can be pure search/library growth without a regime Jump.

### Failure-driven improvement

Sun et al. (arXiv:2606.31270) and existing Reflexion/selective-replay/debugging parents establish that failed trajectories can improve later behavior. #508 must therefore earn only the narrower claim that **scoped negative knowledge with compatibility/reopen semantics** prevents repeat dead ends without harmful negative transfer.

## 3. Cross-paper routing

### P1

Consumes bounded reachability/failure witnesses only as successor research. Current P1 evidence remains the frozen credential-free mutation-necessity claim. Future P1 may test whether an atom/revision is necessary under a bounded old regime, but no current result becomes Jump evidence.

### P3

Provides correspondence/obstruction/plural-view machinery for `ConceptCandidate.correspondence_map` and regime transition witnesses. P3's scoped public-reference result does not validate generated concepts.

### P4 / P8

Own external validation/authority. None of the new objects may write P4/P8 state. A `ConceptCandidate`, `ThoughtExperiment`, or `FailureKnowledge` record can request/evidence a check, never pass itself.

### P5

Owns self-change adoption and future prospective experiments. Existing Self-ORION V3 T8 remains failure/revision-level instrumentation, not a Jump benchmark. #500/#501 results must enter P5 through a new protocol version if they ever earn an architecture change.

### P6 successor / #463

Owns formal composition with `HigherOrderEpistemicMechanic.v1`. The new `BoundedEpistemicAtomStudy.v1` asks whether a proposed mechanic/atom materially expands bounded reach; `FailureKnowledge.v1` adds the missing staleness/reopen semantics for negative history.

### P7

Owns evidence/closure/obligation transport under representation/objective change. A new concept/regime does not get to claim preservation just because it supplies a correspondence map.

### P9

May learn from `FailureKnowledge`, tension, thought-experiment, and concept proposal episodes only after their schemas stabilize and protected evaluation material is excluded from training. Learned scores cannot become reachability/validity authority.

### P10

Can become the empirical operator/concept-generation consumer after P9 freezes. #429 and #506 supply historical morphology; #501 supplies contamination-safe prospective Jump tests. Historical reconstruction remains mechanism-discovery evidence, not proof of invention.

## 4. Recursive study sequence

Pilot sequence:

```text
#510 TENSION / OPPORTUNITY DETECTION
  -> #511 THOUGHT-EXPERIMENT SYNTHESIS
    -> #512 CONCEPT / PRIMITIVE INVENTION
```

Each atom is tested independently before any full Jump composition claim. If these pilots show that the common `BoundedEpistemicAtomStudy.v1` packet is missing fields, version it rather than mutating old study records.

Later candidate children from #506 include analogy generation, affordance/function discovery, prototype/construction, question/conjecture generation, serendipity capture, operator invention, conservative representation change, and meta-Jump/operator-language revision. Open each only after ownership/donor checks show a real unowned atom.

## 5. Failure feed

Every atom study should eventually emit failure records for at least:

- false positive / overuse;
- missed positive / underuse;
- wrong responsibility attribution;
- failed discriminator;
- failed interaction/decomposition hypothesis;
- negative transfer on fresh task;
- context change that reopens an old failure;
- donor subsumption / no incremental value.

Those records feed #508/#509 and later #477 inquiry. A null atom study is therefore programme knowledge, not discarded engineering history.
