# P9 donor saturation ledger V3

Status: **OPEN — round 3**.  This ledger records primary-source mechanism dispositions for the refounded P9 programme.  A donor is not a competitor to route around.  P9 adopts useful mechanisms, strengthens the mandatory baseline, strikes inherited novelty language, and tests only the residual.

No entry grants P9 scientific or novelty authority.

## Disposition vocabulary

- **ADOPT** — use the donor principle/mechanism directly when task mapping is valid.
- **ADAPT** — preserve the donor mechanism but bind it to ORION's explicit objects/evidence boundaries.
- **BASELINE** — mandatory pressure test.
- **DEFER** — relevant but not justified for the surviving P9 claim.
- **STRIKE** — language P9 is no longer allowed to claim as novel.

## Representation / graph donors

### Graphormer — Ying et al., 2021, arXiv:2106.05234

**Essence.** A standard Transformer becomes a strong graph learner when graph structure is encoded explicitly; Graphormer introduces structural encodings and shows that several GNN behaviours can be represented within the architecture.

**Strength frontier.** Powerful global attention with structural bias; strong evidence that `Transformer + correct structural encoding` is a serious graph baseline rather than a strawman sequence model.

**Weakness for P9.** Does not itself supply ORION's epistemic object semantics, local representation transport, failure lineage, or authority boundary; those are task information, not architecture novelty.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT structural-encoding principle + BASELINE`.  Any P9 graph experiment must compare against a strong structure-aware Transformer, not a vanilla Transformer.

**STRIKE.** `Transformers cannot reason over graphs`; `explicit graph structure itself is novel`.

**Residual discriminator.** Same topology with different typed relation/effect; local-map/cycle information not reducible to ordinary structural encodings; failure/history coordinates.

### Heterogeneous Graph Transformer — Hu et al., 2020, arXiv:2003.01332

**Essence.** Node- and edge-type-dependent attention/parameters for heterogeneous graphs, plus temporal encoding and scalable sampling.

**Strength frontier.** Direct pressure on any P9 claim that merely assigns types to claims/evidence/method nodes and relation types to edges.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT heterogeneity semantics + BASELINE`.  HGT-class typed attention is the floor for A1 typed-graph experiments.

**STRIKE.** `typed nodes/edges` as architecture novelty.

**Residual discriminator.** Typed epistemic coordinates that require non-edge objects, local chart transports, explicit non-identifiability/failure history, or candidate-mechanic semantics beyond standard heterogeneous attention.

### GraphGPS — Rampášek et al., 2022, arXiv:2205.12454

**Essence.** Modular graph Transformer recipe combining positional/structural encodings, local message passing, and global attention.

**Strength frontier.** Shows that local/global composition and careful encodings often matter more than a branded monolithic architecture.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT modular graph-recipe doctrine + BASELINE`.

**STRIKE.** `local + global reasoning combination` as P9 novelty.

**Residual discriminator.** P9 must beat or supplement a tuned GPS-class model on the exact atom that requires additional information/computation.

### GraphBFF — Bechler-Speicher et al., 2026, arXiv:2602.04768

**Essence.** Billion-parameter heterogeneous graph foundation-model recipe, scaling laws, pretraining and transfer to unseen graphs.

**Strength frontier.** Prevents a small bespoke P9 model from being compared only with small GNNs and then claiming a universal structural advantage.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT scaling-pressure doctrine + BASELINE/RESOURCE THREAT`; full billion-scale reproduction is outside bounded P9 unless a smaller discriminator survives first.

**STRIKE.** `graph models do not scale`; `small-model result proves asymptotic architecture superiority`.

**Residual discriminator.** Sample/compute efficiency, OOD/composition behaviour, or exact information distinctions that scale alone cannot recover when the input omits the decisive coordinate.

## Local-map / sheaf / higher-order donors

### Knowledge Sheaves — Gebhart, Hansen & Schrater, 2021, arXiv:2110.03789

**Essence.** Knowledge-graph embeddings as approximate global sections of cellular sheaves with relation-induced consistency constraints; supports composite relation reasoning.

**Strength frontier.** Very close to P3's local-view / map / consistency intuition.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT local-space + transport-consistency language where operationally useful + BASELINE`.

**STRIKE.** `local vector spaces connected by maps`, `global consistency from local maps`, or sheaf vocabulary as ORION novelty.

**Residual discriminator.** ORION-specific typed epistemic roles, recoverable obstruction/UNKNOWN, evidence/failure lineage, or a task where learned sheaf transport plus ORION coordinates outperforms the donor-complete simpler model.

### Neural Sheaf Diffusion — Bodnar et al., 2022, arXiv:2202.04579

**Essence.** Learnable sheaf geometry and matrix-valued restriction maps extend graph diffusion, especially under heterophily/oversmoothing pressure.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT learnable restriction-map primitive + BASELINE` for A1 only when local-map data is actually present.

**STRIKE.** `matrix-valued relation transport` as P9 novelty.

**Residual discriminator.** Epistemic transport plus explicit cycle/obstruction semantics or failure state beyond diffusion/classification.

### Deep Neural Sheaf Diffusion — Bourgerie, Girdzijauskas & Fodor, 2026, arXiv:2605.19021

**Essence.** Replaces sheaf Laplacian diffusion with sheaf adjacency and architectural stabilizers to retain useful signal at depth; contrasts matrix-valued edge functions with scalar attention.

**P9 disposition.** `BASELINE` if a deep/local-map branch survives A1.  Do not assume ordinary NSD is the strongest sheaf comparator.

**STRIKE.** `deep sheaf aggregation itself is new`.

### Benchmarking Sheaf Neural Networks for Inductive Tasks — Fiorini, Coppola & Liò, 2026, arXiv:2608.02558

**Essence.** Controlled 1,890-run inductive benchmark across sheaf design choices and modern GNN components; restriction maps matter, but surrounding architectural recipe explains more variance and the strongest baselines remain ahead on the benchmark suite.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT negative-design lesson`.  Sheaf machinery enters only after a transport-specific frozen discriminator.

**STRIKE.** `sheaves generally outperform strong graph baselines`.

**Residual discriminator.** A local-transport task where HGT/GPS + explicit cycle features remains insufficient under matched information/resources.

## Reusable module / library donors

### DreamCoder — Ellis et al., 2020, arXiv:2006.08381

**Essence.** Wake-sleep Bayesian program learning that grows reusable symbolic libraries plus neural search guidance; abstractions are compositional, interpretable and transferable.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT library-growth/reuse philosophy + BASELINE` where tasks map to executable programs.

**STRIKE.** `learning reusable interpretable problem-solving abstractions` as P9 novelty.

### Stitch — Bowers et al., 2022, arXiv:2211.16605

**Essence.** Corpus-guided top-down synthesis for library learning; orders-of-magnitude efficiency gains over prior deductive abstraction search with comparable/better compression.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT efficient abstraction-extraction pressure + BASELINE` for mechanic discovery.

**STRIKE.** `extracting reusable abstractions from solution corpora` as P9 novelty.

### LILO — Grand et al., 2023, arXiv:2310.19791

**Essence.** LLM-guided synthesis + Stitch compression + language documentation; learned libraries improve complex inductive synthesis.

**P9 disposition.** `BASELINE/DONOR` for any language-grounded library-learning variant; mostly P10-facing after P9 freezes non-language mechanics.

### From Reasoning Traces to Reusable Modules — Kong et al., 2026, arXiv:2606.18089

**Essence.** Hierarchical latent-selection account of reusable reasoning skills/routing; experiments support complementary SFT/RL roles, RL extraction/recombination of atomic modules, and advantages of compound traces over isolated atomic traces.

**P9 disposition.** `ADOPT module-extraction + compound-trace curriculum insights + BASELINE` under A2/A8.

**STRIKE.** `reasoning traces contain reusable modules`; `RL can discover/recombine atomic reasoning modules`.

**Residual discriminator.** Explicit typed applicability/effect/failure/binding/provenance and non-identifiability, with incremental OOD value beyond module/routing learning.
