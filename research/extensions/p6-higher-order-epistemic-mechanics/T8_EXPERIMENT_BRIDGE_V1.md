## 1. Why T8 is necessary

T1–T7 establish bounded executable semantics on finite hostile panels: responsibility may remain ambiguous, interface defects can block broader revision, narrower admissible mechanics can dominate broader ones, hard verification can outrank scalar computation value, uncertainty can be contained without model rewriting, social reports can remain correlated, and those receipts can be composed into a read-only Self-ORION next-step recommendation. None of that demonstrates that the architecture improves scientific self-revision.

T8 therefore changes the object of study from **formal contract behavior** to **prospective falsification of the control policy**.

## 2. Benchmark-local responsibility labels, not a universal ontology

The open #452 foundations issue has not frozen the global taxonomy of scientific revision responsibility. T8 therefore freezes labels only inside a particular benchmark packet. The development panel currently instantiates evidence, measurement, within-class model, model-class, representation-regime, execution and evaluator repair plus `UNRESOLVED`. Objective and method-basis revision remain protected follow-up classes.

This is a deliberate narrowing: a benchmark can be executable before the programme claims a universal ontology.

## 3. Protected cause-confusable cases

Each case separates:

```text
candidate-visible symptom and hypotheses
from
protected discriminator outcomes and protected gold responsibility.
```

A family is useful only when similar visible symptoms support different protected gold revisions or an explicit unresolved terminal. This makes the central empirical question identifiable: does a policy use discriminating evidence to select the responsible revision layer rather than reflexively applying a broad repair?

The public candidate packet is deterministically derived from the protected suite and strips protected outcomes/gold labels. A salted content commitment binds the protected suite without revealing those fields.

## 5. Adaptivity intervention controls

The same full T7 policy is evaluated under normal, permuted, no-feedback, contradictory and deterministic-random diagnostic observations. This directly tests the non-vacuity condition:

```text
evidence-responsive revision control
=>
changing the evidence should sometimes change the decision.
```

If the policy is invariant to those interventions, the implementation cannot claim that the revision decision was caused by the observed discriminator evidence.

## 6. Independent scoring

The candidate policy returns a content-digested proposal only. The protected scorer separately computes:

- revision responsibility accuracy;
- false broad-revision rate;
- correct unresolved count/rate;
- diagnostic actions and cost.

Later protected execution records, bound to the exact decision digest, separately record fresh-transfer success, harmful regression, preservation violation and evaluator/authority violation. No scalar weighted score replaces these endpoints.
