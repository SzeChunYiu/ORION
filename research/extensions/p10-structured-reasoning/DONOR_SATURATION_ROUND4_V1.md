## Historical parent correction

A particularly close classical parent is Breese & Heckerman, *Decision-Theoretic Troubleshooting: A Framework for Repair and Experiment* (UAI lineage; arXiv:1302.3563). It extends diagnostic belief updating to decisions over three classes of action:

1. acquire an observation;
2. repair a component and observe consequences;
3. change configuration and observe consequences.

Cost-bounded active classification/POMDP work likewise makes evidence acquisition versus decision under finite resources an explicit decision problem. Earlier sequential-diagnosis work compares heuristic and decision-theoretic test selection under diagnostic cost.

These parents mean P10 cannot claim the abstract distinction among `seek evidence / repair / reconfigure / decide` as new, even outside modern agent literature.
