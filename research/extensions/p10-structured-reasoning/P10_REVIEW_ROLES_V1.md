# P10 claim-expansion reviewer roles V1

Any one role may block escalation beyond the completed source-transfer claim.

1. **Lean/Mathlib reviewer** — native state must come from the exact frozen Lean/Mathlib environment rather than source-text surrogates; dependency features must be pre-tactic and leakage-safe.
2. **Methods reviewer** — checks held-out-module purity, chronology, uncertainty, identical eligible transitions across comparators, and deterministic regeneration.
3. **Hostile reviewer** — attacks theorem/module identity leakage, future-step leakage, source/runtime substitution, near-duplicate contamination, and post-outcome exclusions.
4. **Nearest-work reviewer** — requires faithful LeanDojo/ReProver- and TacMiner-class comparisons where those are load-bearing to a novelty claim.
5. **Claims reviewer** — selects only the highest rung whose full frozen gate passed and retains null/unavailable arms.
