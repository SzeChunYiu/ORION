# P10 no-outcome-inflation rule V1

The review branch must preserve these distinctions:

- derived statistics from frozen V2.1 data may refine uncertainty/breadth descriptions but cannot become a new preregistered endpoint;
- protocol documents do not count as positive experimental evidence;
- native acceptance of eight exact files does not imply Mathlib-wide semantic correctness;
- source-level next-action transfer does not imply proof-state transfer or prover utility;
- an unavailable strong comparator blocks the corresponding novelty rung rather than being treated as evidence for it.

The branch should maximize positive claims only by executing stronger tests and clearing stronger comparators.
