This ledger narrows the refounded P10 claim before any live LLM experiment. Sources are treated as donors, not threats.

## Round 1 — symbolic/graph/evidence-controller vocabulary — MATERIAL

Material parents:

- SymPlanner (arXiv:2505.01479): LM proposals separated from symbolic execution/verification and iterative correction;
- TAPE (OpenReview 2026): plan graph + external solver + constrained execution/replanning;
- Grounded Continuation (arXiv:2605.14175): claim/evidence dependency graph + symbolic support/retraction;
- EoG / Think Locally, Explain Globally (arXiv:2601.17915): bounded semantic extraction + deterministic graph controller and belief propagation;
- graph-learning planning / GTA: graph representation/inductive structure can materially affect language-agent planning;
- SCOPE (arXiv:2606.22488): incomplete symbolic world revision under feedback.

**Material consequence:** strike generic novelty for `LLM + structured workspace`, graph planning, explicit evidence graph, deterministic graph traversal, symbolic verification, replanning, and symbolic-world revision. Freeze fixed-proposal A0 so proposal intelligence cannot explain a controller result.

## Round 2 — diagnosis/recovery vocabulary — MATERIAL DONOR EXPANSION, NO NEW ATOM

**Consequence:** the A0 responsibility categories are increasingly a composition of donor-owned functions, not P10 novelty. ## Round 3 — triage/minimal-intervention/underspecification vocabulary — NO MATERIAL CONTROL-ATOM CHANGE
