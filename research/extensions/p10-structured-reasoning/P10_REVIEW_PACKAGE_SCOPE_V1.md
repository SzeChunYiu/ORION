# P10 review-package scope V1

The review package should be judged in two layers:

- **Completed bounded paper:** frozen source-transfer result, invalidation history, nulls, native identity/acceptance audit, mutation controls, and derived module robustness.
- **Claim-expansion research:** frozen native-state/dependency experiment and subsequent invariant-state/search extensions. These are not manuscript results until executed.

A reviewer should be able to accept the completed bounded paper even if every claim-expansion experiment is later null, while a stronger standalone novelty claim requires those extensions to clear their own gates.
