# Complete support-7 obstruction elimination for `C_7^3` — V1

Status: **bounded machine-checked structural theorem**. Global `D_3(C_7^3)` value and novelty/priority remain **CANNOT_CHECK**.

## Statement

Let `B` be a zero-sum sequence over `C_7^3` with

- `|B|=37`, and
- zero-sum packing number `z(B)<=3`.

Then

`|supp(B)| >= 8`.

Equivalently, **no support-7 length-37 total-zero packing obstruction exists**.

## Reduction to a finite weighted projective problem

By `SUPPORT7_PROJECTIVE_ARC_REDUCTION_V1.md`, a hypothetical support-7 obstruction has:

1. seven distinct projective directions forming a seven-point `(7,3)`-arc in `PG(2,7)`;
2. multiplicities `1<=m_i<=6` summing to 37.

Put `w_i=7-m_i`. Then `1<=w_i<=6` and `sum_i w_i=12`.

For projective representatives `q_i`, write the actual support vectors as `v_i=lambda_i q_i`, `lambda_i in F_7^*`. The total-zero condition is

`sum_i w_i lambda_i q_i = 0`.

Set `c_i=w_i lambda_i`. Then `c=(c_i)` is a full-support kernel vector of the `3 x 7` matrix with columns `q_i`. Conversely every positive weight vector `w` of total 12 and every full-support projective kernel vector `c` determines, up to one global nonzero scalar, the unique lift `lambda_i=c_i/w_i`.

Thus the enumeration is complete and duplicate-controlled at the projective-support level.

## Exhaustive cover

The independent projective generator gives 54 projective support classes. Across them:

- positive deficit profiles `w`: **462**;
- full-support projective kernel vectors `c`: **7,400** across the 54 classes;
- total weighted scalar lifts: **3,418,800**.

The primary exact search checks every lift.

### Necessary short-zero filter

Any zero-sum subsequence of length at most seven would leave a total-zero complement of length at least 30. That complement must have packing number at most two (otherwise the removed zero-sum plus three complement blocks gives four in `B`), contradicting `D_2(C_7^3)=29`.

Hence a genuine obstruction must be 7-short-zero-free.

Exactly **14,860** of the 3,418,800 weighted lifts pass this necessary condition.

### Exact four-pack test

For a 7-short-zero-free total-zero sequence of length 37, every nonempty zero-sum block has length at least eight. Existence of four disjoint zero-sums is equivalent to a four-block zero-sum partition of the whole sequence: if four blocks leave a nonempty remainder, that remainder is itself zero-sum and can be merged into one of the four blocks.

In any four-block partition of length 37 with every block length at least eight, a smallest block has length 8 or 9. After removing it, a smallest block in the remaining three-block partition again has length 8 or 9. The last two blocks have total length 19--21.

The primary search therefore enumerates all zero-sum submultisets of lengths 8--13 by a meet-in-the-middle sum table, chooses the first two blocks only from lengths 8--9, and checks whether the remaining total-zero sequence splits into two nonempty zero-sums. This is exhaustive for a four-pack.

Result: **all 14,860 short-free candidates admit a four-way zero-sum partition.** No support-7 obstruction survives.

## Independent verification

`verify_support7_all_independent_v1.cpp` is deliberately structured differently:

- short-zero-freeness is recomputed by scanning a separately generated flat list of all count vectors of total weight 1--7, rather than the primary recursive prefix search;
- four-pack existence is checked by forming the set of all sums of two disjoint zero-sum submultisets and testing complement membership, rather than the primary nested 8/9-block factorization search;
- the verifier is sharded by projective class and checks the frozen per-class short-free counts.

The executable projective cover is separately regenerated by `generate_support7_projective_arcs_v1.py`; the C++ search does not decide which 54 projective classes exist.
