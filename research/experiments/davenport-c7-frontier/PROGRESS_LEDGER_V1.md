## Physical/combinatorial picture under test

Working interpretation only: `D_k` is a neutral-cluster packing threshold in `F_p^3`. A zero-sum subsequence is a neutral cluster and a multiwise obstruction is a jammed charge configuration.

Two candidate mechanisms are being separated:

- **capacity jamming:** clusters compete for shared coordinate/projective resources;
- **spectral jamming:** the set of allowed zero-sum lengths develops gaps that prevent enough disjoint clusters.

This language is heuristic until converted into exact invariants/lemmas.
