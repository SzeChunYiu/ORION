## 1. Multiwise Davenport framework

Michael Freeze and Wolfgang A. Schmid, “Remarks on a generalization of the Davenport constant,” *Discrete Mathematics* 310 (2010), 3373–3389, DOI `10.1016/j.disc.2010.07.028`, defines the multiwise constants `D_k(G)`, develops general bounds, and proves eventual arithmetic progression with difference `exp(G)`.

**Subtraction:** definitions, eventual linearity, general factorization interpretation, and broad `D_k` motivation are donor-owned.

The weighted multiwise/coding-theory direction is developed by L. E. Marchan, O. Ordaz, I. Santos and W. A. Schmid, “Multi-wise and constrained fully weighted Davenport constants and interactions with coding theory,” arXiv `1407.1966`.

**Subtraction:** links between multiwise zero-sum constants, constrained constants, codes, and projective caps are adjacent prior art. The present paper should not market the use of projective support geometry by itself as new.

## 2. Cyclic length-four index donor

Y. Li, C. Plyley, P. Yuan and X. Zeng, “Minimal zero sum sequences of length four over finite cyclic groups,” *Journal of Number Theory* 130 (2010), 2033–2048, DOI `10.1016/j.jnt.2009.12.005`, proves the prime-power, order-coprime-to-six case used in the `a=3` boundary reduction.

Later work extends or reorganizes the length-four index problem, including Li–Peng, arXiv `1303.1682`; Shen–Xia–Li, arXiv `1402.0219`; and Zeng–Qi, *Colloquium Mathematicum* 146 (2017), 157–163, DOI `10.4064/cm6702-1-2016`.

A new preprint by H. Li, P. Yuan, S. Yuan and W. Zhang, “A Multiplicative Fourier Proof of the Length-Four Index Conjecture,” arXiv `2608.12310` (submitted 2026-08-12), claims the full conjecture for every cyclic order coprime to six. This strengthens the surrounding donor landscape but is not needed for the present prime-order application, which was already covered by the 2010 theorem.

**Subtraction:** every invocation of a length-four index-one theorem is donor-owned. The ORION contribution is the capacity-aware embedding, exact radial surcharge, and explicit multiplier constructions around that donor theorem. The manuscript should cite the 2026 preprint as the current endpoint of the general index-conjecture line while using the narrowest established result needed for its proof.

## 3. Short-zero-sum inverse structure

X. Zeng and P. Yuan, “On Sequences Without Short Zero-Sum Subsequences,” *Electronic Journal of Combinatorics* 30(4) (2023), P4.21, DOI `10.37236/11963`, studies inverse structure for sequences without short zero-sum subsequences under sumset-size hypotheses.

**Subtraction:** inverse short-zero-sum structure is an active established topic. The manuscript must distinguish its constrained maximal-pair setting, exact occurrence-depth method, and multiwise packing objective from general inverse zero-sum sequence results.

## 4. Restricted-sumset and maximal-sequence neighborhood

Restricted sumset methods, maximal zero-sumfree structure, Property B phenomena, and unsplittable minimal zero-sum sequences form a substantial adjacent literature. They are relevant to the still-open high-multiplicity classification on the type-`a=2` top-overlap face.

**Subtraction:** any eventual classification proof using orbit hitting, translated triangles, or restricted sumsets must be positioned as an application or refinement of that literature, not as a wholly new sumset principle without a dedicated subtraction.
