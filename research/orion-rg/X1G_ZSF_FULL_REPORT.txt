MAXIMAL ZERO-SUM-FREE SEQUENCES AND THE INVERSE DAVENPORT PROBLEM FOR C_5^3
============================================================================

HEADLINE NUMBERS (D(C_5^3) = 13 by Olson, so d(C_5^3) = 12)
  zero-sum-free sequences of length 12, up to GL(3,5) : 26,369 classes
                                        total         : 39,147,296,000 sequences
  minimal zero-sum sequences of length 13, up to GL   :  3,325 classes
                                        total         :  4,897,256,000 sequences
  (the second is the inverse Davenport problem proper for C_5^3)

PRIOR ART CHECK (done first)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rank 2 is classical: Property B (Gao-Geroldinger; proved for prime n by Reiher)
plus the Bhowmik-Schlage-Puchta / Gao-Geroldinger description of maximal
zero-sum-free sequences over C_n^2.  Rank 3 is NOT known: the searches
surfaced complete inverse results only for special families (C_2^2 + C_2n,
Schmid EJC 18 (2011) #P33; C_3 + C_3 + C_3d, Bhowmik-Schlage-Puchta 2007),
and the literature explicitly motivates rank-2 structure by the fact that the
rank-3 Davenport constant is unresolved in general.  No source gave a
structural classification for C_p^3, and none gave a rank-3 analogue of
Property B or a counterexample to one.  So this is new territory; nothing
here is a rediscovery of a result I found in the literature.

CORRECTION TO THE STATED ANCHOR.  The task gave the rank-2 form as
  e^{p-1} * prod_{i=1}^{p-1}(a_i e + f)                            [shape A]
That is INCOMPLETE.  The full statement needs a second shape
  e^{p-2} * prod_{i=1}^{p}(a_i e + f)  with  sum a_i = 1           [shape B]
My rank-2 computation confirms it: 4 of the 18 classes over C_5^2 have max
multiplicity 3, so shape A (which requires multiplicity 4) cannot hold for
them.  Shape B is exactly the case where the element of multiplicity p-1 in
the associated minimal zero-sum sequence S*(-sigma(S)) is the completion
-sigma(S) itself, so Property B is untouched.

VALIDATION ANCHOR: C_5^2, length 8 = d(C_5^2)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enumerated ALL 8400 zero-sum-free sequences of length 8 over C_5^2 with NO
normalization, so the orbit-stabilizer identity is checked against ground
truth rather than a slice:
    18 GL(2,5)-classes;  sum of |GL(2,5)|/|Stab| = 8400 = enumerated total.
    17 classes have |Stab|=1, one (e^4 f^4) has |Stab|=2.
    ALL 18 fit shape A or shape B (14 fit A, 4 fit only B), tested by brute
    force over all 480 ordered bases.  0 fit neither.
The pipeline therefore reproduces the known rank-2 structure theorem before
being pointed at the unknown rank-3 case.

RANK 3 -- VALIDATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Bit primitive: the u128 translation `shft` is OR-linear, so checking it on
    all 125 singleton masks for all 125 translations is a COMPLETE proof, not
    a sample.  Verified 125x125 = 15625/15625.
  * Two independently structured enumerators (position-recursive vs
    code-recursive, different pruning) both give 3,275,411 normalized
    zero-sum-free sequences of length 12.
  * COMPLETENESS on a slice: an independent Python brute force over the
    |supp| <= 5 slice produced 7302 sequences; the C enumerator produced the
    IDENTICAL SET (0 only-in-python, 0 only-in-C).
  * Orbit-stabilizer: for EVERY one of the 26,369 classes, N(C)/|Stab(C)|
    equals the observed multiplicity in the normalized enumeration exactly.
    0 mismatches; |Stab| divides N in all 26,369 cases; total 3,275,411.
  * Anti-collapse: inside one multiplicity profile, 129 sequences with
    distinct subsequence-sum signatures (an independently computed
    GL-invariant) gave 129 DISTINCT canonical forms.
  * Canonicalizer regression: the optimized canon2 reproduces the already
    validated canon byte-for-byte on all 98622 D_2 witnesses.
  * Anchor: e1^4 e2^4 e3^4 appears as a class.
  * CROSS-CLASSIFICATION IDENTITY.  Every minimal zero-sum sequence U of
    length 13 equals S*(-sigma(S)) for S = U minus any one element, and the
    S's producing a given U are exactly U\{u} for u in supp(U).  Hence
        #(zero-sum-free length 12) = sum over U of |supp(U)|.
    Computed: 39,147,296,000 = 39,147,296,000.  EXACT.
    PROVENANCE.  The two headline counts rest on DIFFERENT evidence.  26,369 is
    validated the strong way: predicted count N(C)/|Stab(C)| equals observed
    count for every single class, against a normalized enumeration whose
    completeness was separately checked on a slice.  3,325 is NOT validated that
    way -- there is no independent normalized enumeration of length-13 minimal
    zero-sum sequences here.  It rests on (a) the surjection proof above, (b)
    the sum-over-supp identity, which does break loudly if two classes collapse
    to one canonical form, and (c) an anti-collapse control at L=13: 338
    provably-inequivalent representatives gave 338 distinct canonical forms.
  * 120 random minimal-zero-sum reps brute-forced over all 2^13 subsets:
    every one has exactly one zero-sum sub-multiset (itself).  0 failures.

STRUCTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROVED.
  P1  sigma(S) != 0 for every zero-sum-free S, so U = S*(-sigma(S)) is defined,
      has length 13 = D(C_5^3), and is a MINIMAL zero-sum sequence.
  P2  INDUCTIVE REDUCTION.  If g in supp(S) has multiplicity 4, then
      S = g^4 * T and the image of T in G/<g> = C_5^2 is zero-sum-free of
      length 8, i.e. MAXIMAL, hence of shape A or B by the rank-2 theorem.
      Proof: if a sub-multiset T' of T sums to 0 in G/<g>, then sigma(T') = jg
      for some j in 0..4; j=0 makes T' a zero-sum in S, and j>0 makes
      T' * g^{5-j} a zero-sum in S since m(g) = 4 >= 5-j.  Both contradict ZSF.
      Verified on 300 random such classes: 0 failures, and the quotients hit
      all 18 rank-2 classes.
      COVERAGE: 17,153 of 26,369 classes (65.0%), 25,472,576,000 sequences.
      The same argument fails at multiplicity 3: j = 1,2 are then unblocked.

VERIFIED BY EXHAUSTION over all 26,369 classes (census, not proof):
  T1  PROJECTIVE SQUAREFREENESS.  supp(S) injects into PG(2,5): no two support
      elements are scalar multiples of each other.  This is NOT forced
      elementwise -- u^2 (2u)^1 is itself zero-sum-free, and the only local
      constraint is m(u) <= 2, m(2u) <= 1 -- yet it never occurs.  It also held
      for all 1405 extremal D_2 witness classes.  Two independent complete
      censuses, ~41 billion sequences, no exception.
      >>> OPEN QUESTION.  Is every maximal zero-sum-free sequence over C_p^3
      >>> projectively squarefree?  This is the one regularity found here that
      >>> looks like a theorem waiting for a proof.
  T2  max multiplicity is 2, 3 or 4 (410 / 8,806 / 17,153 classes).
  T3  the projective support never contains a full line (meets a line in <= 5).
  T4  |supp(S)| in {3,...,10}.

IS THERE A RANK-2-STYLE PARAMETRIC DESCRIPTION?  NO, and here is the obstruction.
  The rank-2 form works because SOME element has multiplicity p-1 or p-2 and all
  remaining elements lie in a single coset of the cyclic group it generates.
  In rank 3 that fails structurally:
    * 9,216 of 26,369 classes (35.0%, 13,674,720,000 sequences) contain NO
      element of multiplicity 4, so P2 gives no reduction at all.
    * 410 classes have max multiplicity 2 -- as spread out as a length-12
      sequence over C_5^3 can be.  Example, brute-force re-verified:
        (001)^2 (010)^2 (011)^2 (014)^1 (100)^2 (101)^2 (104)^1
    * Only ONE class of the 26,369 contains a basis with all three
      multiplicities equal to 4, namely e1^4 e2^4 e3^4 itself.  The naive
      "Property C" guess -- every maximal zero-sum-free sequence is
      g1^4 g2^4 g3^4 -- is false by a factor of 26,369 to 1.

THE RANK-3 ANALOGUE OF PROPERTY B IS FALSE FOR C_5^3.
  Property B (rank 2, proved for prime n by Reiher) says every minimal zero-sum
  sequence of maximal length over C_n^2 has an element of multiplicity n-1.
  The naive rank-3 analogue would force multiplicity 4 in every minimal
  zero-sum sequence of length D(C_5^3) = 13.  It fails:
      max multiplicity 4 : 2,489 classes   (3,671,144,000 sequences)
      max multiplicity 3 :   832 classes   (1,222,020,000 sequences)
      max multiplicity 2 :     4 classes   (    4,092,000 sequences)
  All FOUR max-multiplicity-2 classes, each brute-force verified over all 2^13
  subsets to have exactly one zero-sum sub-multiset (itself):
      (001)^2 (010)^2 (011)^2 (100)^2 (101)^1 (132)^2 (142)^1 (414)^1   |Stab|=2
      (001)^2 (010)^2 (011)^2 (100)^2 (104)^1 (113)^2 (144)^1 (402)^1   |Stab|=1
      (001)^2 (010)^2 (011)^2 (100)^2 (111)^2 (124)^1 (134)^1 (441)^1   |Stab|=1
      (001)^2 (010)^2 (011)^2 (100)^2 (111)^1 (314)^2 (341)^2           |Stab|=4
  The most symmetric maximal-length minimal zero-sum sequence is
      e3^4 e2^4 e1^4 (e1+e2+e3),   |Stab| = 6, orbit 248,000.
  NOTE ON SCOPE: "Property B" is a rank-2 statement; no rank-3 version is
  standard in the literature.  This is not a counterexample to a published
  conjecture -- it is the statement that the obvious generalization is false,
  with explicit witnesses.

CLOSING THE OPEN ITEM FROM THE D_2 CLASSIFICATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 1,238 zero-sum-free length-12 classes that arise as complements of a
length-7 minimal zero-sum inside an extremal D_2 witness are
      1,238 of 26,369 classes            =  4.69%
      1,815,236,000 of 39,147,296,000    =  4.64% by sequence weight
All 1,238 were confirmed to be genuine members of the independently produced
26,369-class list -- a cross-check between the two classifications.

Is there a clean separating criterion?  Not found.  Exact NECESSARY conditions
(each missing 0 of the 1,238, but each admitting many non-complements):
      |supp(S)| <= 8          (excludes all 3,585 classes with |supp| 9 or 10)
      mb >= 6                 (excludes all 346 classes with mb = 5)
      sigma-completion max multiplicity >= 3  (excludes all 24 with max 2)
Complements are strongly biased toward small support and large mb: 100% of
k=4 classes with mb=11 are complements (14/14), versus 37/9,392 at k=8.  The three
conditions together still admit 21,522 non-complements, so they are far from
sufficient, and no exact criterion emerged from the invariants computed.
