## Negative-to-positive successor — Causal Repair Transfer and Meta-Improvement (CRTMI)

CRTMI takes the three immutable errors from the 21/24 result as initial seeds:

1. retrieval miss vs representation gap;
2. environment/tool failure vs implementation bug;
3. representation gap vs method-basis gap.

For each, freeze an intervention-backed discriminator, then test whether the corrected responsibility changes repair selection, whether the repair beats matched donor controls on fresh tasks, whether protected assurance admits it, and whether retaining that episode improves later diagnosis/repair selection.

The same doctrine applies to every later harmful transfer or null generation. A failure first receives a responsibility hypothesis, then a materially different discriminator/mechanic search. New method-basis edits are only admissible after lower-level causes are ruled out.
