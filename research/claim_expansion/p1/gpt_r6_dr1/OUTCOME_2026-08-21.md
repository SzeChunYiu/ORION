## What this does and does not establish

It establishes that the R6 comparison is now **made**: the ablation arm executed the
mechanism it ablates, on every episode, verified rather than assumed, and the guard
that checks it raises on the failed encoding rather than scoring it.

It does **not** close #649, and this campaign grants no adoption, promotion or merge
authority. Three limits are load-bearing:

1. **The provider is the frozen deterministic host, not a semantic model.** Per
   `DEVELOPMENT_PACKET.md`, a positive primary still requires the changed-host 2019
   replication. Nothing here substitutes for it.
2. **A large `ARD − BASE` margin is partly structural.** The frozen protocol resolves
   BASE to `UNRESOLVED` whenever the two native responsibilities map to distinct P1
   families, which is most episodes. The repair's contribution is that BASE's
   `UNRESOLVED` is now demonstrably an *ambiguity* outcome — `DIAGNOSE` ran and
   returned a non-empty native responsibility set on 48/48, and BASE reached
   `IDENTIFIED` on 5 — rather than the inert artifact it was.
3. **The repair deliberately did not touch the decision function.** Provider,
   keyword table, mapping, probe priority, budget, gates and thresholds are used
   byte-identically from the frozen core. Only the root world's reachability changed.

## Reproducibility

Two independent executions of `run_dr1_campaign.py` produce **identical scored
fields and identical per-episode choices for all three arms**. The payload
`digest` differs between them, because `trace_id` and `receipt_id` are uuid-minted
per run and are carried in the rows. A differing receipt digest across runs is
therefore expected and is not a differing result; the scored fields are the
comparable surface.

The receipt carries all **96** root-run coverage reports (48 episodes x 2 native
arms). Every one has `never_executed == ["REOPEN", "SATURATE_BOUNDED"]` --- that
is, `DIAGNOSE` executed on all 96.
