## Successor lesson

R4 proved that within-source pairing solves the R3 control scarcity problem: every admitted substantive source carries its own no-high-level control. The remaining failure is the brittle requirement that every retrieval query must admit. The next successor should use **all qualifying frozen-query sources**, preserve source-disjointness, and class-balance the analysis rather than requiring a perfect 4/4 retrieval yield in every class. This successor may reuse the 26 R4 sources because no policy outcome has been observed on them.
