## What is no longer a novelty claim

P1-U does **not** claim invention of:

- objective/goal evolution;
- generic problem or representation reformulation;
- belief revision/minimal change;
- model-class expansion under M-open checks;
- experiment design/value of information;
- failure localization;
- provenance-preserving regime transitions;
- dependency reopening/rollback;
- generic state compilation;
- generic mutation authorization.

## Surviving upward research object

The broad candidate contribution is the **responsibility-acquisition and regime-selection problem**:

> Given multiple plausible failure levels and a finite budget of discriminating actions, can ORION acquire the evidence needed to choose the scientifically correct revision level, route among donor mechanisms more reliably than a donor-complete controller, and discover new discriminators/operators from residual failures that transfer across domains?

This residual is stronger than static P1-X decision semantics because the protected responsibility coordinate is not supplied to runnable systems.

## Strongest donor-complete comparator contract

The comparator is allowed:

- fixed-goal search/tool use;
- reflection/self-critique;
- objective evolution (SAGA-style);
- M-open predictive checks and hypothesis expansion (MDA-style);
- active/VoI experiment selection;
- model/harness/environment fault localization;
- representation/task reformulation;
- provenance-preserving regime revision;
- minimal-change/dependency-aware revision;
- current ORION-like generic protected authorization machinery;
- the same ARD discriminator action set and exact costs.

It is **not** given protected gold responsibility labels. The full-information ideal product remains a separate analysis ceiling.

## Contrary/limiting evidence retained

1. P1 historical broad H1: failure does not imply high-level reformulation.
2. P1 objective-basis sufficiency: dynamic objective invention is unnecessary when the basis is already decision-complete.
3. P1-X ideal typed product tie: complete responsibility semantics erase any static representational advantage.
4. Planning reformulation literature shows many gains can arise from representation/search transformations without objective change.
5. MDA shows some apparent model inadequacy can be resolved by designed experiments and model-space expansion without changing the broader scientific problem.

These are not defects to hide. They define the exact regimes ARD must distinguish.
