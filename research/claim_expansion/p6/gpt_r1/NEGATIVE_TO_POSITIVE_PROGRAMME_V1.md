`Minimal Revalidation Closure (MRC)` retains every theorem countermodel and classifies it as hidden support, spurious support, alternative support, action-equivalence ambiguity, cycle/fixed-point, representation-only invalidation, responsibility change, evaluator-semantic change or non-realizability.

Each negative follows: freeze countermodel -> identify minimal missing primitive/assumption -> re-prove soundness/minimality/conservativity/revocation, or prove a sharp impossibility/lower bound -> recover the finite predecessor as a corollary -> test real-system revalidation savings.

Exact minimality is never claimed when support is unobservable/incomplete. The positive result in that regime is the strongest safe over-approximation plus the minimal extra information needed to recover exactness.

No hostile countermodel deletion, theorem-by-exception, hidden-assumption insertion after proof failure or empirical timing result may substitute for machine-checked theorem authority.
