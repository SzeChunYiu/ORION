1. donor-native validity is preserved by a forgetful map;
2. if that map erases a non-inert scientific obligation coordinate, scientific admissibility is not reflected;
3. when all scientific obligations are discharged, the enrichment conservatively reduces to donor-native validity;
4. after change, any changed scientific coordinate must be preserved or revalidated for certificate continuity.
