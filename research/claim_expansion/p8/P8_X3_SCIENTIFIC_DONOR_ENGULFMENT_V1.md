# P8-X3 scientific donor engulfment V1

Date: 2026-08-19
Parent: #535

This addendum extends `P8_X2_DONOR_ENGULFMENT_V1.md` after a post-X2 hostile literature pass found material scientific-agent authority donors. X2 is retained as an honest six-donor preliminary formal layer; X3 is the final ten-donor science target.

## CogEEGAgent
**Unique part absorbed:** an EEG-specific scientific harness separates semantic from scientific authority; the LLM proposes registered analyses while deterministic components validate typed contracts, isolate confirmation access, and authorize evidence-bound release.

**P8 improvement:** adopt domain scientific release gating as a first-class donor authority type, then make it composable with delegation/action certificates and transportable across `(domain,kind,scope,content,epoch)`. P8's target is cross-domain lifting/propagation, not invention of scientific-release separation.

## ScientistOne / Chain-of-Evidence
**Unique part absorbed:** claim taxonomy, evidence-chain requirements, deterministic grounding, per-claim verification, dropping/softening unsupported claims, method-code and score/reference integrity audits.

**P8 improvement:** treat a verified claim-evidence chain as a lower-level scientific authority certificate. P8 adds cross-capability composition, type narrowing, blocker semantics, support-family revocation and protected coercion so a verified local claim cannot silently become a broader scientific conclusion through downstream delegation/composition.

## Xcientist research harness
**Unique part absorbed:** persistent contract-governed artifacts linking evidence, ideas, implementation, ablations, validation and bounded revision; explicit claim-drift control.

**P8 improvement:** preserve Xcientist artifact/accountability lineage as scientific support and add formal authority propagation over reuse: downstream agents may consume the validated artifact but must preserve/narrow its scientific type and current support/epoch conditions before discharging a new obligation.

## StatefulDiscovery
**Unique part absorbed:** explicit investigation state coordinating frontier selection, evidence acquisition and evidence-calibrated claim adjudication; structured hypotheses/local adjudication contribute to supported/high-value claims.

**P8 improvement:** adopt evidence-calibrated claim status as a donor adjudication verdict and integrate it with non-compensatory authority propagation. A locally adjudicated claim becomes a typed support object rather than an unrestricted authority token.

## Stronger combined donor product
The final P8 donor product now contains both sides of the modern landscape:

**Action/delegation governance:** FAVA + PCAA + HDP + SentinelAgent + APC/Bounded Agents + ECA.

**Scientific claim/release governance:** CogEEG scientific harness + ScientistOne CoE + Xcientist + StatefulDiscovery.

P8-X3 therefore cannot claim novelty for evidence-bound scientific release, chain-of-evidence verification, contract-governed research artifacts, evidence-calibrated claim adjudication, typed verifier certificates, delegation provenance, or authority narrowing.

## Remaining improvement target
The final question is a composition problem:

> Can one typed authority calculus preserve these heterogeneous donor verdicts while governing how authority to establish a scientific conclusion flows across action, delegation, evidence, claim-verification and scientific-release boundaries?

P8-X3 adds:
- a shared complete scientific-discharge type `(domain,kind,scope,content,epoch)`;
- monotone narrowing across heterogeneous authority hops;
- subject/epoch-bound protected coercions;
- `REFUTED / UNDETERMINED / ESTABLISHED` blocker semantics;
- exact alternative-support-family revocation;
- explicit non-inversion between action/release permission and proposition support;
- decentralized ideal-product equivalence.

## Wider candidate claim
> P8 generalizes modern agent authorization and domain scientific verification into a cross-domain scientific-authority lifting calculus: action/delegation certificates, typed evidence, claim-evidence chains and scientific release/adjudication verdicts remain donor-owned local authorities, while P8 specifies how their authority can be safely narrowed, composed, coerced, blocked, revoked and transported to discharge a different scientific obligation.
