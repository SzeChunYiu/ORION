## Donor subtraction
Authorization logics already formalize principals, `says`, delegation and `speaks-for`; capability/IAM systems already constrain delegated power. Macaroons support decentralized delegation with monotonic attenuation through caveats, including third-party caveats. Modern FAVA/AuthGraph/CVA-style agent authorization further binds context, evidence, provenance and policy. P8 cannot claim generic non-amplifying delegation, attenuation or authorization certificates as novel.

The residual is scientific authority: whether evidence/state/analysis authority for one responsibility can compose into authority for a downstream scientific responsibility, what explicit bridge discharges widening, and how revocation propagates after evidence, representation, objective or evaluator change while preserving unaffected scientific standing.

## Negative-to-positive successor — Authority Bridge and Revocation Closure (ABRC)
