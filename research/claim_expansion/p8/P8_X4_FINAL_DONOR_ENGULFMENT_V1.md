# P8-X4 final donor engulfment V1

Date: 2026-08-19
Parent: #535

This is the final donor envelope for the current P8 science pass. It preserves X2/X3 as dated intermediate fixed points and incorporates the material donors discovered after those enumerations.

## Donor 11 — Authorization Evidence Chains (EP-AEC)
**Unique part absorbed:** transport-agnostic composition of heterogeneous authorization receipts; native verifier dispatch; canonical action binding; fail-closed requirement expressions; composition without inventing a universal receipt format.

**P8 improvement:** adopt heterogeneous receipt composition directly. Replace action-digest sufficiency with a second, scientific target: the composed receipts may authorize/verify a canonical action while the resulting judgment can discharge only an explicitly typed scientific obligation. P8 therefore adds scientific type, support, blocker and coercion semantics *above* EP-AEC's receipt composition, not instead of it.

Important donor lesson absorbed: equality of a join/binding key is a composition key, not proof of scientific sufficiency.

## Donor 12 — Evidence-Ledger Adjudication
**Unique part absorbed:** claim-evidence packets, explicit support relations, and routing of unsupported/contradicted/mixed-evidence claims back to the author rather than forced acceptance.

**P8 improvement:** treat the evidence-ledger verdict as a local scientific support authority. P8 adds cross-domain type/narrowing, alternative complete support-family revocation, delegation-chain composition and protected coercion. An evidence-ledger-supported claim may therefore be safely reused without becoming an unrestricted authority token.

## Donor 13 — Authority Resolution Framework (ARF)
**Unique part absorbed:** a cross-domain Authority Relation binding actor, action, object, bounded context, justification chain and a calibration/divergence measure.

**P8 improvement:** adopt the cross-domain authority-relation framing and specialize the target relation to **scientific obligation discharge**. P8 adds a complete scientific type `(domain,kind,scope,content,epoch)`, non-compensatory blockers/support, alternative derivation/revocation, and protected coercion so cross-domain authority can be composed without treating generic contextual authorization as scientific sufficiency.

## What P8 can no longer claim as component novelty
P8 does not own:
- action/delegation authorization;
- authority narrowing or cascade containment;
- cross-domain generic authority relations;
- heterogeneous authorization-receipt composition;
- cryptographic delegation provenance;
- typed evidence certificates;
- evidence-bound domain scientific release;
- chain-of-evidence claim verification;
- research-artifact accountability;
- evidence-calibrated claim adjudication;
- evidence-ledger traceability/routing;
- generic abstention, provenance or revocation.

## Final improvement target
P8's final object is the **scientific-discharge composition layer** over all of those local authorities:

1. retain every donor-native verdict and certificate;
2. bind a target scientific obligation by `(domain,kind,scope,content,epoch)`;
3. require scientific authority to preserve/narrow across every heterogeneous hop;
4. allow only explicit subject/epoch-bound protected coercions to change scientific type;
5. represent blockers as `REFUTED/UNDETERMINED/ESTABLISHED`;
6. preserve authority under revocation exactly while an independent complete support family survives;
7. separate action/release permission from proposition support in both directions;
8. prove an equally typed decentralized donor product is extensionally equivalent.

## Wider final candidate claim
> P8 composes modern action authorization, delegation provenance, heterogeneous authorization receipts, cross-domain authority relations, typed verifier certificates, scientific release gates, chain-of-evidence verification, research harnesses and evidence-ledger adjudication into a common scientific-discharge calculus. Local donor authority remains intact, while authority to establish a scientific conclusion propagates only through typed non-widening chains or protected coercions, fail-closed blockers and surviving complete support families.
