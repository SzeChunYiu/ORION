## Added P8 composition semantics
A donor-derived judgment may discharge a target scientific obligation only through:
1. a native-valid donor authority/verification/adjudication object;
2. compatible complete scientific type `(domain,kind,scope,content,epoch)` or an explicit complete protected coercion;
3. non-widening scientific authority across heterogeneous chain hops;
4. blocker state `REFUTED`; `UNDETERMINED -> CANNOT_CHECK`, `ESTABLISHED -> BLOCK`;
5. at least one surviving complete support family.

The calculus also preserves relation non-inversion: action/release denial is not scientific refutation when independent evidence establishes a proposition, and action/release authorization is not unrestricted scientific discharge.
