# P8-X2 donor engulfment and improvement map V1

Date: 2026-08-19
Parent: #535

## Doctrine
P8 treats modern authorization/delegation/evidence systems as the authority substrate to absorb, not as concepts to subtract away. Their strongest mechanisms remain intact. The scientific question is how authority to act or delegate can be lifted into authority to discharge a scientific obligation without laundering scope, evidence, freshness, blockers or support lineage.

## Donor 1 — FAVA evidence-backed permission graphs
**Unique part absorbed:** structured permission IR, evidence-backed permission/data-flow graph, deterministic SMT authorization and external runtime enforcement before effectful execution.

**P8 improvement:** retain FAVA action permission as a lower-level authority certificate. Scientific discharge additionally binds the judgment to a target scientific type and support state. A fully authorized action is therefore reusable evidence about execution authority but does not self-authorize a scientific conclusion.

## Donor 2 — Proof-Carrying Agent Actions (PCAA)
**Unique part absorbed:** runtime-neutral action identity, pre-action admissibility, assumption capture, approval semantics, outcome closure, portable runtime/approval/outcome receipts and replay-ready proof.

**P8 improvement:** attach a scientific-discharge type to the portable action certificate and make type compatibility/narrowing explicit across reuse. The action certificate remains portable even when its scientific use is too broad, stale or of the wrong evidential kind.

## Donor 3 — Human Delegation Provenance (HDP)
**Unique part absorbed:** cryptographically verifiable human authorization context propagated through an append-only signed delegation chain.

**P8 improvement:** preserve the HDP chain as authority provenance while requiring scientific-discharge scope to be non-increasing along the chain. Proven delegation answers who authorized the action; P8 adds what scientific obligation the resulting judgment is entitled to discharge.

## Donor 4 — SentinelAgent delegation-chain calculus
**Unique part absorbed:** deterministic authority narrowing, policy preservation, forensic reconstructibility, cascade containment, scope-action conformance and output-schema conformance, with intent treated separately.

**P8 improvement:** extend the same narrowing discipline to scientific-discharge types. A delegation hop may retain or narrow the scientific scope/kind/content/epoch it can establish, but cannot silently widen it. Intent preservation is not used as a substitute for scientific support.

## Donor 5 — Bounded Agents / Agentic Principal Chain (APC)
**Unique part absorbed:** accumulated session-state authorization, delegated scope/budget restriction, composition closure, enforcement outside the model, Blast Radius Monotonicity and Composition Soundness under stated conditions.

**P8 improvement:** carry scientific-discharge authority through the principal chain as a typed non-increasing capability. Composition closure over actions is retained; P8 adds a parallel scientific composition rule so a sequence of individually allowed actions cannot jointly manufacture a conclusion outside the inherited scientific type/support contract.

## Donor 6 — Evidence-Carrying Agents (ECA)
**Unique part absorbed:** action-critical predicates backed by typed verifier certificates, with deterministic gates granting only privileges supported by those certificates and explicit residual verifier error.

**P8 improvement:** reuse typed verifier certificates as lower-level support objects, then distinguish the privilege they authorize from the scientific obligation they may establish. Certificate typing helps P8 rather than competing with it; P8 adds target scientific type, blocker and support-family semantics.

## Additional scientific pressure — mechanism fidelity
Current AI-scientist evidence shows that a right-looking result can coexist with a mechanism claim contradicted by the agent's own data. P8 treats this as support for keeping result/action correctness distinct from authority to establish a broader scientific mechanism claim; it does not claim that failure mode as P8's invention.

## Absorbed authority product
The strongest P8 donor product contains, where applicable:

`evidence-backed permission graph + portable action certificate + cryptographic delegation provenance + deterministic authority narrowing/cascade containment + accumulated principal-chain composition + typed verifier certificates + continuing revocation/provenance`.

P8-X2 grants this product its native authority verdicts.

## P8-X2 improvement properties
1. **scientific authority lift** — a native-valid donor authority certificate can discharge a scientific obligation only through compatible scientific type/support/blocker state;
2. **typed delegation narrowing** — scientific discharge type may be preserved or narrowed across delegation hops, never widened without a protected coercion;
3. **protected coercion** — an explicit complete bridge can translate a scientific type when direct compatibility fails; generic composability/intent is insufficient;
4. **three-state blockers** — `REFUTED` permits the blocker condition to clear, `ESTABLISHED` blocks, `UNDETERMINED` yields `CANNOT_CHECK` rather than being treated as absent;
5. **support-family revocation** — revoking one premise/support route preserves discharge when an independent complete support family survives; all complete support families broken removes the scientific authority;
6. **relation non-inversion** — action denial does not imply the proposition is scientifically false/unsupported, because independent evidence can establish it; action authorization does not imply scientific discharge;
7. **ideal decentralized equivalence** — a decentralized donor product supplied with the same scientific type, blocker, support and coercion rules ties exactly.

## Candidate wider claim
> P8 provides a scientific-authority lifting calculus over modern delegation and proof-carrying authorization systems: native action/delegation authority and provenance remain intact, while authority to establish a scientific conclusion flows only through monotone type-preserving delegation or protected coercion, fail-closed blocker semantics and surviving complete support families.

This is a positive composition architecture: P8 strengthens the strongest donor authority systems at the scientific conclusion boundary instead of claiming their mechanisms as new.
