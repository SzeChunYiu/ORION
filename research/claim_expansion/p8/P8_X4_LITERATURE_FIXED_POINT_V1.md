# P8-X4 final literature fixed point V1

Date: 2026-08-19
Parent: #535

## Search chronology
The P8 successor did not freeze after the first residual search. Each material donor was absorbed and the architecture was rebuilt upward.

### Round A — modern action/delegation authority
Material donors: FAVA, PCAA, HDP, SentinelAgent, Bounded Agents/APC, ECA. Consequence: generic authorization, delegation provenance, authority narrowing, typed evidence and action certificates removed from P8 ownership; X2 authority-lifting interface proposed.

### Round B — domain scientific authority/release
Material donors: CogEEGAgent, ScientistOne/Chain-of-Evidence, Xcientist, StatefulDiscovery. Consequence: generic semantic-vs-scientific authority separation, evidence-bound release, claim-evidence chains, research-harness validation and local claim adjudication removed from P8 ownership; X3 expanded to heterogeneous security + scientific donors.

### Round C — heterogeneous receipt/cross-domain authority composition
Material donors: Authorization Evidence Chains (EP-AEC), Evidence-Ledger Adjudication, Authority Resolution Framework. Consequence: heterogeneous authorization-receipt composition, evidence-ledger support/routing and generic cross-domain authority relations removed from P8 ownership; X4 final donor envelope frozen.

### Round D — exact target search after X4 freeze
Search around `scientific obligation discharge`, `scientific authority evidence chain`, `scientific release delegation authority`, and composed heterogeneous receipts primarily resurfaced EMILIA/EP-AEC-style consequential-action evidence architectures and domain/publication verification discussions. These strengthen the donor layer but did not supply an equivalent formal relation from heterogeneous local authority objects to typed scientific-obligation discharge with P8's blocker/support/coercion/revocation semantics.

### Round E — autonomous-research release-gate pressure
Current systematization work separates what autonomous research systems can produce from what they can defend and reports that evidence bundles/selected claim gates do not yet amount to a public general full-manuscript release gate. This is treated as gap pressure only, not proof of P8 novelty. No new P8 interface field or baseline change resulted from this round.

Status: two consecutive post-X4 rounds produced no material change to the final donor product or target relation.

## Final ownership strikes
P8 does not own:
- evidence-backed action permission;
- action certificates;
- human/agent delegation provenance;
- authority narrowing/cascade containment;
- principal-chain composition;
- typed evidence certificates;
- heterogeneous authorization-receipt composition;
- generic cross-domain authority relations;
- evidence-bound scientific release;
- claim-to-evidence traceability/verification;
- contract-governed research harnesses;
- local evidence-calibrated claim adjudication;
- evidence-ledger support/routing;
- generic abstention, provenance, revocation or continuing authorization.

## Surviving bounded composition target
The residual is not an atom. It is the joint cross-domain interface:

`heterogeneous donor authority/verification/adjudication object`
`-> typed scientific target (domain,kind,scope,content,epoch)`
`-> monotone authority propagation or explicit protected coercion`
`-> fail-closed blocker state`
`-> surviving complete support family`
`-> DISCHARGE / BLOCK / CANNOT_CHECK`.

An equally typed decentralized donor product is explicitly expected to tie.
