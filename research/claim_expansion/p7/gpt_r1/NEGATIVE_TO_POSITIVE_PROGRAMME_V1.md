# P7-U GPT-R1 negative-to-positive calculus programme

Parent: #655
Target: `GENERAL_CLOSURE_PRESERVING_SCIENTIFIC_TRANSFORMATION_CALCULUS`

`Obligation Transport Synthesis (OTS)` retains every locally-valid/global-invalid workflow and classifies it as missing transform semantics, missing bridge, responsibility mismatch, loss/optionality, stochasticity, cycle/fixed point, representation transport, hidden dependency or genuine non-composability.

For each negative: freeze the workflow -> synthesize the minimal bridge/contract obligation -> prove the corresponding conditional composition law -> test on fresh transform families -> require false-closure reduction without recompute-everything degeneration.

If no bridge yields general composition, the programme produces the sharp necessary/sufficient non-composability boundary. Counterexamples, missing-bridge cases and finite predecessor outcomes remain immutable.
