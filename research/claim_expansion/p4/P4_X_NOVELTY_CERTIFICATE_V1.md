> for **scientific claim promotion**, exact claim/artifact version, claim scope, evidence independence, freshness/epoch, provenance, checker admissibility, evaluator custody and unresolved scientific obligations form a non-compensatory promotion relation; generic authorization and high confidence cannot silently discharge a missing scientific-promotion coordinate.
