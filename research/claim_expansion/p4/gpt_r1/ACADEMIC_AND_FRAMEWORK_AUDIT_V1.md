## Academic-paper-skills scope

This pass applies current literature search, reference verification, three separately frozen reviewer lenses, statistics/independent-unit review, and a final claim/evidence/boundary consistency pass. No journal-specific prestige criterion is assumed.

## Current donor frontier

The 2026 frontier materially strengthens the P4 comparator:

- **Partial Evidence Bench** (Tallam, arXiv:2605.05379) directly studies authorization-limited evidence, unsafe completeness, completeness awareness and structured gap reporting.
- **AuthGraph** (Wang, Li & Tian, arXiv:2605.26497) aligns execution provenance with a clean authorization graph and detects parameter-source deviations under indirect prompt injection.
- **FAVA** (Zhang et al., arXiv:2607.27267) lowers natural-language constraints into evidence-backed permission graphs and uses deterministic SMT authorization before effectful execution.
- **CVA** (Llambí-Morillas & Fernández-Fernández, arXiv:2607.21325) formalizes cryptographically verifiable authorization binding principal, request, context and policy satisfaction.
- provenance tracing, proof-carrying action, abstention/calibration, chain-of-evidence systems, contamination auditing and responsibility-aware state are direct donor structure.

Generic authorization, provenance, evidence-backed permission graphs and partial-evidence awareness are therefore not P4 novelty claims. The residual is scientific conclusion promotion responsibility under heterogeneous evidence.

## Reviewer 1 — validity/methods, frozen

**P4-R1-01 — Benchmark identifiability is a prerequisite, not a secondary analysis. Blocking: Yes.**
The predecessor H3 failure is correctly retained. Any new benchmark must defeat source-count, missingness, template, length, metadata and construction-label shortcuts before a score can be interpreted.

**P4-R1-02 — Correct answer is not promotion authority. Blocking: Yes.**
Hold answer correctness equal when possible and vary provenance, dependence, statistics, scope, time, custody or reproducibility. Otherwise P4 can collapse into factual verification.

**P4-R1-03 — Clean positives must be sufficiently represented. Blocking: Yes.**
A safety-only system can win false-promotion metrics by abstaining. Clean valid promotion, false rejection and resource cost are non-compensatory guards.

## Reviewer 2 — prior work/contribution, frozen

**P4-R2-01 — Generic authorization/provenance is donor-owned. Blocking: Yes for novelty wording.**
FAVA, AuthGraph and CVA make fine-grained, context/evidence-bound agent authorization an explicit research object. P4 must not claim first authorization graph/certificate semantics.

**P4-R2-02 — Partial evidence awareness is donor-owned. Blocking: Yes.**
Partial Evidence Bench already tests overclaiming under authorization-limited views. P4 must move upward to whether scientific evidence licenses a conclusion, including statistics, source dependence, scope, temporal validity and evaluator authority.

**P4-R2-03 — Scientific-promotion obligations must be independently grounded. Blocking: Yes.**
A new obligation discovered from failures cannot be defined by the same candidate that benefits from it.

## Reviewer 3 — reproducibility/generalization, frozen

**P4-R3-01 — Case/construction is the independent unit. Blocking: Yes.**
Repeated generations, paraphrases or remints are technical repeats. Multiple independently generated constructions are required for H3.

**P4-R3-02 — Evaluator and producer lineage must differ. Blocking: Yes.**
Self-verification invalidates external promotion authority.

**P4-R3-03 — Fresh split and exact revision binding are mandatory. Blocking: Yes.**
Evidence generated against another subject revision or a post-hoc evaluator is `CANNOT_CHECK`, not transferable authority.

## Editor synthesis

The broad P4-U claim survives donor subtraction. The central residual is a **scientific promotion relation** that integrates evidence sufficiency, provenance/dependence, analysis validity, scope, temporal validity, reproducibility, contamination/custody and evaluator authority, while retaining clean promotion utility. Benchmark identifiability is part of the scientific method, not only benchmark hygiene.

## Framework consistency

The current framework contains a strong executable external-authority spine in `ExternalEvidenceManifest.v1`:

- each record binds exact subject revision, evidence artifact, evaluator artifact, producer/verifier process lineage, evaluation epoch and split;
- producer and verifier must be independent;
- evaluator and subject bindings must match the manifest;
- records must be frozen before candidate and fresh;
- missing/duplicate/mismatched/self-verified/post-hoc/non-fresh evidence yields `CANNOT_CHECK`;
- a verified `FAIL` remains `FAIL` rather than being softened.

P4 has explicit required external criteria for source attribution, contamination audit, evaluator locking, held-out access logging, matched verifier baseline and false-promotion improvement.

This is fully consistent with P4-U's authority semantics. It is evidence infrastructure, not proof of general authorization superiority.

## Negative-to-positive successor — Scientific Promotion Obligation Discovery (SPOD)

The predecessor H3 `NotSupported` result remains immutable. SPOD converts such null/failure cases into causal search over candidate missing obligations:

`evidence sufficiency / provenance / source dependence / statistical validity / scope / temporal validity / reproducibility / state responsibility / contamination-custody / evaluator validity / benchmark identifiability`.

For each failure, freeze one discriminator that changes a suspected obligation while preserving answer content and other coordinates. A new obligation is admitted only if strong verifier/provenance/authorization donors fail prospectively, an independently grounded evaluator identifies the obligation, and the new rule transfers to a fresh construction/domain while preserving clean promotion.

Generic policy learning or authorization-graph construction is donor-owned. The residual is scientific promotion responsibility and its benchmark-identifiability consequence.

## Broad positive terminal

`GENERAL_SCIENTIFIC_AUTHORIZATION_SUPERIORITY` requires:

1. lower false scientific promotion than the strongest runnable donor-complete comparator;
2. clean-promotion/false-rejection non-inferiority or superiority;
3. success after shortcut removal and across independently generated benchmark constructions;
4. responsibility-rung superiority across answer/verify/promote/repair;
5. at least one failure-derived promotion obligation or benchmark mechanic with fresh transfer;
6. external evidence satisfying the canonical manifest and independent verification rules.
