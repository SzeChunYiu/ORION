## Gold scientific-promotion terminal

Terminals are `PROMOTE`, `BLOCK`, `CANNOT_CHECK`.

- `BLOCK` for definitive invalidity of support, provenance, checker, evaluator custody, artifact/version binding, or claim scope.
- `CANNOT_CHECK` for stale epoch, unresolved evidence-independence, or unresolved scientific authority obligation.
- `PROMOTE` only when all hard scientific-promotion obligations are discharged.

## Arms

### P4-X — non-compensatory scientific promotion

Uses the complete hard-coordinate relation above. Soft confidence and non-load-bearing latency never substitute for a hard obligation.

### B1 — strong donor-complete generic authorization product

Receives the same information and grants current donor strengths: support verification, provenance, checker validity, evaluator custody, generic authorization, artifact/version binding and epoch handling. It **does not create an additional scientific-promotion authority relation** for claim scope, evidence independence, or unresolved scientific obligation state. Thus it is intentionally strong and differs only at the disputed promotion interface.

### B2 — compensatory all-signal product

Uses every candidate-visible hard signal plus confidence, but aggregates them into a weighted/mean score; high aggregate support may compensate one failed hard coordinate. This isolates the non-compensatory algebra from feature availability.

### B3 — ideal fully typed donor product

Receives exactly the P4-X scientific-promotion type/rules and must be extensionally identical. A tie is required as a boundary theorem: P4-X claims no inherent expressivity or centralization advantage over an information-equivalent product.
