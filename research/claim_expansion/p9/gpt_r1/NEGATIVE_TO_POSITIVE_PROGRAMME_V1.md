# P9-U GPT-R1 negative-to-positive accessibility programme

Parents: #662, #586
Target: `GENERAL_REPRESENTATION_ACCESSIBILITY_SCALING_LAW`

`Adaptive Access Geometry Discovery (AAGD)` treats every failed/tied frontier cell as a causal research object. The frozen discriminator checks information mismatch/leakage, prompt-length effects, preprocessing answer computation, architecture substitution, extra-compute closure, exact donor solution, absent local operator, wrong serialization/access geometry and hypothesis-class inadequacy.

Only after lower-level explanations fail may a new access coordinate, query interface, local operator or representation be proposed. It counts as positive evidence only if it prospectively moves a frozen model/data/inference frontier, transfers to fresh tasks or another model family, and remains advantageous after representation-construction cost is charged.

Graph prompting, relational architectures, context compression and generic structured CoT remain donor-owned. Negative/no-crossing cells remain visible; no post-hoc scale-grid edits, family deletion, free preprocessing or information leakage is allowed.
