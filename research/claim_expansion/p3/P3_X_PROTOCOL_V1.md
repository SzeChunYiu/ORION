# P3-X prospective science-only protocol V1

Date: 2026-08-19
Parent: #531
Authority: PRE_OUTCOME

## Research question
Can a claim-relative scientific compatibility layer add a discriminating decision contract above strong structured semantic integration, without reclaiming scientific-variable decomposition, ontology/schema matching, provenance, pluralism, or inconsistency handling as atomic novelty?

## Donor subtraction
Explicitly donor-owned before this study: I-ADOPT-style structured scientific-variable descriptions; ontology/schema/entity alignment; provenance representation; provenance-as-epistemic-stance approaches; semantic/referential/schema/logical interoperability; uncertainty/inconsistency-aware integration; ordinary global consistency checking. P3-X claims none of those atoms as first-of-kind.

## Exact tested residual
For already-structured candidate mappings, P3-X computes one of `GLUE`, `OBSTRUCTION`, `PLURAL_VIEW`, `UNRESOLVED` from claim-relative load-bearing coordinates. It separates technical representational compatibility from authority to assert scientific identity.

## Exact domains
1. ENVIRONMENTAL_OBSERVABLE
2. CLINICAL_MEASUREMENT
3. MATERIALS_PROPERTY
4. SOCIAL_BEHAVIORAL_CONSTRUCT
5. PHYSICS_ENGINEERING_OBSERVABLE

These are exact contract families, not claims of deployed ontology-engine generality.

## Archetypes
1. CLEAN_IDENTITY -> GLUE
2. CONSTRUCT_MISMATCH -> OBSTRUCTION
3. MEASUREMENT_MISMATCH -> OBSTRUCTION
4. CONTEXT_SCOPE_MISMATCH -> OBSTRUCTION
5. PROVENANCE_STANCE_CONFLICT -> PLURAL_VIEW
6. ONE_TO_MANY_PLURAL -> PLURAL_VIEW
7. LOCAL_MATCH_GLOBAL_CYCLE_CONFLICT -> OBSTRUCTION
8. MISSING_LOAD_BEARING_COORDINATE -> UNRESOLVED

## Arms
- `P3_X`: full claim-relative compatibility/obstruction contract.
- `B1_STRONG_SEMANTIC_PRODUCT`: receives the same structured construct/measurement/context/provenance/missingness information and correctly rejects direct coordinate mismatches, but treats provenance as trace rather than a scientific stance constraint, chooses one canonical target for plural mappings, and accepts pairwise-compatible mappings without the P3-X global cycle authority check.
- `B2_CANONICAL_MATCH`: canonical merge when local semantic match score is high; no plural/unresolved authority state.
- `B3_IDEAL_TYPED_PRODUCT`: receives the exact P3-X scientific coordinates/rules and must be extensionally identical to P3-X. Any inherent-expressivity/centralization claim is forbidden.

## Design
- Development: 5 domains x 8 archetypes x 5 variants = 200 `NON_AUTHORIZING_DEV` cases.
- Protected: 5 domains x 8 archetypes x 10 disjoint variants = 400 cases.
- Surface labels/identifiers are deterministic nuisance data and are not used by P3-X.
- Missing load-bearing coordinates are never imputed as equality.

## Primary
`ESID` (Exact Scientific Integration Decision): exact match to the four-way gold terminal.

Primary comparison: P3-X minus B1 paired ESID.
Prospective practical margin: +0.10.

## Non-regression gates
- clean GLUE accuracy P3-X = 1.0;
- false GLUE rate on non-GLUE cases = 0;
- `UNRESOLVED` remains distinct from `OBSTRUCTION`;
- B3 exact decision mismatch count = 0;
- positive P3-X minus B1 direction in every domain.

## Analysis
- exact paired counts;
- domain-stratified bootstrap 20,000 replicates for paired ESID difference;
- McNemar discordance for P3-X vs B1;
- per-domain effects;
- no inferential population-generalization claim from the exact contract families.

## Claim ladder
A1: bounded integrated scientific-compatibility decision layer over strong structured semantics.
A2: bounded principle that representational compatibility can be weaker than scientific identity authority.
A3: ontology-engine/deployed/open-ended cross-domain generality.

A1/A2/A3 remain `CANNOT_CHECK` until protected execution, independent reproduction, and novelty authority. A3 requires real substrate/engine/domain evidence and is not earned by this exact-contract battery.

## Immutability
After the protected outcome-access receipt is committed, case rules, arms, scoring, margin, analysis, and terminal mapping cannot change. Null/harmful outcomes remain the result.
