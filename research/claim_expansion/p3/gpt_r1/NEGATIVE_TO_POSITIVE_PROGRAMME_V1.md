# P3-U GPT-R1 negative-to-positive programme

Parent: #651
Target: `GENERAL_SCIENTIFIC_IDENTITY_INTEGRATION_SUPERIORITY`

## Mission

Convert retained typed-product ties, false-merge/false-split cases, plurality loss, state-insufficiency and transport failures into positive fresh-task evidence by discovering the missing scientific identity responsibility, not by relabeling old outcomes.

## First successor family — CICD

`Causal Identity Coordinate Discovery` localizes whether an identity failure is caused by extraction, construct semantics, measurement semantics, context/temporal validity, provenance/stance, state/interface insufficiency, representation transport, or genuine non-identifiability. It freezes a one-coordinate discriminator before proposing a new coordinate.

Generic representation learning, feature discovery, entity matching and ontology induction are donor-owned. The residual is the scientific rule that determines when the current coordinate basis is insufficient, which new coordinate is warranted, and which prior integrations must reopen.

## Positive conversion ladder

1. Re-test every failure under coordinate-equalized extraction.
2. If the error survives, isolate one missing/incorrect identity coordinate with a surface-confusable pair.
3. Freeze the candidate coordinate/mechanic and its authority/reopen consequence.
4. Test on fresh pairs from at least one disjoint domain.
5. Require downstream scientific utility gain, not only pairwise matching gain.
6. Preserve false-merge as non-compensatory and maintain source round-trip.

## Stop rule

Continue materially different coordinate/mechanic searches after a negative. Stop only at the broad positive terminal, a rigorous lower bound/impossibility for the registered family, or demonstrated saturation after independent successor families.

## Prohibited recovery

No post-outcome gold relabeling, pair deletion, margin relaxation, weaker baseline, F1 aggregation that hides false glue, or paper-only assertion of a coordinate as implemented runtime capability.
