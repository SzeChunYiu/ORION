# P2-U GPT-R1 negative-to-positive programme

Parent: #650
Target: `GENERAL_OPEN_WORLD_DISCOVERY_SUPERIORITY`

## Mission

Convert retained P2 false-closure, missed-route, retrieved-but-unprocessed, optionality-loss and provider-invalid regimes into prospectively positive fresh-task outcomes without rewriting the historical records.

## First successor family — ODAE

`Obligation-Directed Adaptive Exploration` maintains explicit candidate scientific obligations and chooses among route generation, retrieval, processing, contradiction resolution, state compile/cache/recover, and closure verification under one charged budget.

Generic coverage, VoI, adaptive stopping and retrieval novelty are donor-owned. The residual is the cross-stage responsibility relation linking them to scientific obligations and task-global closure.

## Negative seeds

- N1 missed material route despite local saturation;
- N2 decisive evidence retrieved but absent from decision-usable state;
- N3 duplicate evidence mistaken for coverage;
- N4 contradiction discovered after apparent closure;
- N5 state compression destroys a future-route cue;
- N6 provider invalidity makes the route non-authorizable;
- N7 clean closed task where never-stop/always-unresolved loses utility.

## Positive conversion tests

For each seed, freeze a surface-confusable pair and a direct causal discriminator. The proposed mechanism must improve fresh cases sharing the causal relation rather than only the authored instance. Every positive result must retain clean-case utility and charge all retrieval/state/evaluator costs.

## External programme

The exact authored arena is non-authorizing mechanism development. The decisive campaign requires multiple naturalistic scientific domains plus one systematic-review-like evidence universe with independently curated decision relevance, hidden material routes, provider custody, and held-out future query changes.

## Failure doctrine

A new loss is retained and routed to the responsible stage. It triggers a new materially different mechanism search. Prohibited recovery moves are endpoint switching after outcomes, removing hard families, weakening the donor-complete baseline, relaxing margins, or scoring `CannotCheck` as a competitor failure.

## Authority

This programme may propose search/state/closure mechanics but cannot self-authorize scientific superiority or runtime registration. External evidence remains governed by the repository evidence contract.
