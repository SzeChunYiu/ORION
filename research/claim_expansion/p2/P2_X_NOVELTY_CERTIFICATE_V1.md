## Atomic residual

> A scientific-acquisition closure rule in which route-local stopping and task-global closure are separate authority relations: material unavailable/censored/provider-invalid routes are unresolved closure authority rather than negative evidence, and acquired content may remain globally unresolved until question-conditioned processing obligations are discharged.

## Strongest absorbed threats

- HALT owns verification-aware per-claim evidence-coverage stopping and abstention when coverage cannot be verified.
- decision-sufficiency/confidence and utility/EVPI work own purpose-aware stopping.
- Don't Stop Early owns a coverage-driven deep-research architecture with evidence-based completion criteria.
- KnowPlan owns finite atomic source obligations and an explicit closure certificate over declared source-inventory dimensions.
- iCORE owns generic work/obligation graphs linked to evidence.
- Science of Intent owns generic open-world closure-gap / misclosure terminology.
- systematic-review hybrid-search work owns the observation that one search route/database may be insufficient.

P2-X therefore claims none of those components or terms.

## Hostile product attack

The primary B1 is deliberately donor-complete: it receives route identities/status, HALT-like evidence coverage, decision sufficiency, utility stop, finite certificates, deduplication, and question-conditioned processing state. Its only systematic gap is that global closure is computed over available material routes, excluding unavailable/censored/provider-invalid ones.

B3 receives the exact P2-X unresolved-route semantics and ties extensionally (400/400, zero decision mismatches). Therefore P2-X has no inherent expressivity or centralization advantage over an information-equivalent ideal product.
