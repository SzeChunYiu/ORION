# ORION-01–05 science-first publication policy

The programme aims first at the strongest defensible top-tier venue. A strong
specialist journal is the fallback only after at most three prospectively
frozen, scientifically distinct breakthrough rounds fail to establish enough
significance for the top-tier route.

Fallback may narrow claims, positioning, and venue. It may not weaken proofs,
hostile controls, adverse/null-result preservation, independent replay,
custody, prior-art subtraction, rights, or `CANNOT_CHECK` boundaries.

## Ordering

1. establish one current-main evidence and status layer;
2. close the paper's declared science rounds with an exact terminal;
3. choose top-tier or specialist claim scope from the same evidence;
4. rebuild and bind manuscript, PDF, figures, reviewer package, and rights;
5. obtain current specialist novelty/fit review and external scientific review;
6. bind exact upload bytes and submission receipt.

No manuscript/package terminal can close an open science gate. No local test,
hash, finite witness, or same-owner cross-language implementation can create
novelty, external independence, production value, journal authority, or
submission authorization.

## Round accounting

- post-outcome threshold or parameter retuning is not a new round;
- removing a feature or comparator after exposure is not a new round;
- widening the same grammar after failure is not automatically a new mechanism;
- every null, adverse, resource-bound, counterexample, and `CANNOT_CHECK`
  outcome remains part of the final paper evidence;
- a round is consumed only by a prospectively frozen scientific execution or
  an adjudicated theorem attempt under its declared rules.

## Permitted final science dispositions

```text
TOP_TIER_SCIENCE_GATE_PASS__PROCEED_TO_TOP_TIER_PREPARATION__SUBMISSION_NOT_YET_AUTHORIZED
TOP_TIER_SIGNIFICANCE_GATE_NOT_MET__SCIENTIFIC_CORE_CLEAR__PROCEED_TO_SPECIALIST_PREPARATION__SUBMISSION_NOT_YET_AUTHORIZED
SCIENTIFIC_CORE_NOT_CLEAR__DO_NOT_PREPARE_SUBMISSION
```

None of these authorizes submission until the downstream manuscript, rights,
external-review, venue, and exact-byte gates also pass.
