# ORION-01–05 provisional venue ladder

This is a **targeting ladder**, not a readiness, fit, acceptance, novelty, or
submission decision. Science closes first under `SCIENCE_STATUS_V1.json` and
`PUBLICATION_GATE_V1.json`. The author then chooses the exact journal and
article type from the evidence-supported claim scope.

**Recheck official venue criteria, scope, article types, policies, and current
submission requirements immediately before every submission.** A venue name
below never overrides an open scientific, external-review, rights, or
exact-upload-byte gate.

## Fallback rule

Fallback narrows significance claims and positioning; it never lowers proof,
replay, hostile-control, adverse-result, custody, rights, or novelty standards.
If the scientific core is still unclear after the bounded rounds, the outcome
is **do not prepare a submission**, not automatic specialist submission.
