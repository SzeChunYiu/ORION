# 04 — Self-ORION

Self-ORION means **ORION is the research object and the primary governed problem-solving process**, not uncontrolled self-editing.

## Development trajectory

1. **LLM-led bootstrap** — external LLM-led development constructs the minimum viable recursive machine.
2. **Shadow Self-ORION** — ORION observes its failures, researches causes and proposes repairs, but external development remains the primary authority.
3. **Governed Self-ORION** — after fresh demonstrations of failure localization, open-world research, candidate construction and transfer, ORION becomes the primary process for ordinary development.
4. **Self-sustaining program** — ORION continuously updates object knowledge `K`, search-universe knowledge `W`, and method knowledge `M` under distinct authority gates.

## Self-development readiness gate

ORION is not ready to drive its own development merely because many modules exist. It must demonstrate on fresh tasks that it can:

1. observe a consequential failure/residual;
2. localize the responsible layer;
3. search outside its current vocabulary/conceptual neighborhood;
4. discover relevant external knowledge;
5. absorb it without authority leakage;
6. construct competing repair hypotheses;
7. freeze a discriminator/benchmark before outcome access;
8. produce an implementation candidate;
9. compare it to the incumbent under matched resources;
10. survive fresh assurance;
11. preserve null/harmful outcomes;
12. recommend only the scoped change supported by evidence.

A failure is valuable if it becomes reusable method knowledge. A consequential failure that is neither explained nor converted into future protection remains an open Self-ORION residual.
