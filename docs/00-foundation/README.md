# 00 — Foundation

This subtree contains the principles that define ORION independently of any currently fashionable model, discipline, retrieval method or implementation.

## Invariant kernel

ORION recursively:

1. represents a scoped research object/question;
2. searches heterogeneous routes for relevant knowledge, evidence, methods, failures and tools;
3. absorbs contributions as contextual projections with provenance;
4. maps equivalences, complements, conditional differences and genuine contradictions;
5. reconstructs a richer global portrait rather than voting on sources;
6. detects residuals in both the object model and the research process;
7. diagnoses the responsible layer before revision;
8. reframes only the implicated question/representation/decomposition/search policy/method/evaluator;
9. reopens dependent closure and searches again;
10. preserves negative history and never lets proposals mint authority by themselves;
11. applies the same process to ORION through stricter Self-ORION governance.

The framework is intentionally **knowledge-absorbent**. A newly discovered discipline may change ORION's world model, search universe or internal representation, but it does not automatically replace the invariant core.

## Fundamental state

The bootstrap uses three coupled state classes:

\[
(K_t, W_t, M_t)
\]

- `K_t` — current knowledge/global portrait of the research object;
- `W_t` — current model of the relevant knowledge/search universe;
- `M_t` — current research method/ORION machinery.

A research episode may update one or more coordinates, but the evidentiary bar is different for each. Scientific claims require evidence; search-universe changes require evidence of omitted relevance/coverage; method changes require governed Self-ORION evaluation and fresh assurance.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Search/generation is proposal, not scientific authority.
- Different words do not imply different concepts; different concepts do not imply contradiction.
- Context must be aligned before contradiction/equivalence claims.
- Negative/null/failed history remains addressable.
- Reframing stales dependent closure rather than silently inheriting it.
- Saturation is bounded flatness under a declared search/formulation universe, never universal completeness.
- A self-improver cannot certify itself by weakening its evaluator.
