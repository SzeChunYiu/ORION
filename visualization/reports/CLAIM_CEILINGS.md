# ORION evidence-atlas claim ceilings

## Purpose

The evidence atlas is a presentation and audit layer over committed ORION
receipts. It does not run new scientific experiments, adjudicate publication
priority, or grant authority to a result. Every plot must be read together with
its source path, scope, status, and authority fields in
`data/derived/atlas.json`.

## Global non-escalation rules

1. **Bytes are not truth.** A source path, byte count, or SHA-256 digest binds
   the visualized value to repository bytes. It does not prove scientific
   correctness, novelty, independence, or reproducibility elsewhere.
2. **Execution is not scientific validity.** A completed run, passing test,
   replay, or finite certificate supports only its registered execution or
   finite scope.
3. **One favorable coordinate is not a passing vector gate.** Multi-endpoint
   terminals remain governed by the complete frozen rule.
4. **Missing authority stays missing.** `UNKNOWN`, `CANNOT_CHECK`,
   `NOT_AUTHORITY`, `GATE_NOT_MET`, and not-executed outcomes may not be plotted
   or narrated as success.
5. **Adverse history is cumulative evidence.** A later bounded positive does
   not delete an earlier failure, discrepancy, invalid run, or null result.
6. **Internal is not external.** Repository authorship, same-context checking,
   and locally replayed receipts do not satisfy independent external validation.
7. **No portfolio score.** P1-P15 use heterogeneous evidence units, estimands,
   scopes, and gates. The atlas does not average them into readiness, quality,
   or acceptance probabilities.

## Paper-specific ceilings

| Paper | What the atlas may show | Ceiling that must remain visible |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Receipt-bound recursive-reconstruction mechanics and registered local outcomes. | No broad reconstruction/generalization or external-authority claim follows from local evidence. |
| P2 | Exact retrieval coordinates, including nDCG, beside the frozen terminal. | Overall `FAIL` remains `FAIL`; nDCG cannot be selected as a substitute terminal. |
| P3 | Receipt-bound portrait/projection results and recorded uncertainty where supplied. | No global-domain coverage or external validation is inferred. |
| P4 | Bounded evidence-admission/authorization outcomes. | Content binding and local checks do not establish independent scientific authority. |
| P5 | Registered Self-ORION mechanics and model/execution identity fields. | Requested `glm-5.2` versus served `glm-5.3` is identity drift; the run cannot establish the requested-model condition. |
| P6 | Explicit finite certificates and their verifier outcomes at the registered domain. | A complete finite witness is not a universal theorem or independent validation. |
| P7 | Planned and observed denominators and their exact residual. | 738 planned versus 736 observed makes the run invalid; “nearly complete” is not an admissible promotion. |
| P8 | Only the exact structured authority result and status bound in its cited receipt. | No broader autonomy or external-authority claim may be inferred from the display. |
| P9 | Exact diagnostic/learning rows, including the digits status. | Digits remains `CANNOT_CHECK`; the recorded replay discrepancy cannot be silently resolved in the favorable direction. |
| P10 | Protocol and gate state for the prospective study, plus separately scoped earlier artifacts. | The prospective study was not executed; code, protocol text, or historical artifacts are not prospective results. |

## Permitted language

- “The cited receipt records … at the frozen/bounded scope.”
- “The normalized atlas preserves the source value/status …”
- “The local verifier/replay passed for the registered input.”
- “This endpoint failed / remains `CANNOT_CHECK` / was not executed.”

## Prohibited language without new authority

- “The framework is externally validated,” “proven generally,” or “universally
  correct.”
- “ORION is superior,” when matched, independent, preregistered baselines and
  promotion gates have not supplied that authority.
- “Top-tier ready,” “journal ready,” or an acceptance probability derived from
  repository tests, plots, hashes, or local receipts.
- Any wording that hides or averages away `FAIL`, adverse, null, `UNKNOWN`,
  `CANNOT_CHECK`, `GATE_NOT_MET`, `NOT_AUTHORITY`, or not-executed outcomes.

## Reader check

Before quoting a figure, identify: (1) its source receipt, (2) the evidence unit
and denominator, (3) the exact terminal, (4) the authority level, and (5) the
claim ceiling. If any item is absent, report `CANNOT_CHECK` rather than filling
the gap from visual appearance.
