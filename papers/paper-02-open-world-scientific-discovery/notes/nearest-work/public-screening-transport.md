# Detail — public-screening transport sources

This note closes the two source citations introduced with the public-screening
transport section. They establish the identities and documented scope of the
public research objects used by the experiments; neither is evidence for an
ORION outcome.

## Physiotherapy screening corpus — `dhrangadhariya2023citationdataset`

DataCite resolves DOI
[`10.5281/zenodo.10423427`](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.5281/zenodo.10423427)
to *Dataset for Machine Learning Assisted Citation Screening for Systematic
Reviews*, with Anjani Dhrangadhariya, Roger Hilfiker and Henning Müller as
creators, publication year 2023, publisher Zenodo and resource type Dataset.
The [primary Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/api/records/10423427) independently
exposes the same title, creators and DOI, a CC BY 4.0 licence, and the
53,881,052-byte file named `citation_screening_deduplicated_corpus.csv`.

**Relevance boundary.** This citation supports the identity, licence and source
size of the fixed public corpus named in the manuscript. It does not establish
that one physiotherapy pool represents review populations, that a
source-specific adapter transports, or that any experimental arm is superior.
Those questions remain governed by the frozen protocol and receipts.

## SWIFT-Review workbench and case studies — `howard2016swiftreview`

Crossref resolves DOI
[`10.1186/s13643-016-0263-z`](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1186/s13643-016-0263-z)
to Brian E. Howard and colleagues, *SWIFT-Review: a text-mining workbench for
systematic review*, *Systematic Reviews* 5 (2016). The independent
[PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27216467/) confirms the title,
13-author list, journal, year, DOI and article number 87. The
[open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4877757/) documents 20
case studies including 15 public data sets and names the BPA, Fluoride,
Neuropain, PFOA/PFOS and Transgenerational review decisions used by the later
panel.

**Relevance boundary.** This citation establishes the workbench paper and the
documented case-study identities. It does not attest the ORION-side downloaded
bytes, preprocessing, controller comparison, panel independence, or any V1--V5
terminal. Those remain content-bound by the experiment artifacts, and the
adverse and `CANNOT_CHECK` outcomes are not changed by citation closure.
