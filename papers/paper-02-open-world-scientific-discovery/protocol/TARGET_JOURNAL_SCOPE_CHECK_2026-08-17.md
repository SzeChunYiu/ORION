# P2 target-journal scope check — 2026-08-17

## Decision

**Primary target:** *Information Processing & Management* (IP&M).

**Fallback:** *Journal of the Association for Information Science and Technology* (JASIST), only if the manuscript is reframed more explicitly around information-science use/consequences rather than primarily retrieval-system governance.

**Submission decision today:** **NO-GO**.  This is a completed scope check, not a declaration that the paper is submission-ready.  The current bounded evidence supports a methods/system-design paper, but the planned externally supported ORION-vs-baseline discovery claim remains open.  Submission should wait for either (a) admissible external evidence that supports the frozen promotion rule, or (b) an explicit final reframe that removes that unresolved superiority claim.

## Why IP&M is the primary fit

Elsevier currently describes IP&M as publishing original research at the intersection of computing and information science, including research methods and critical applications/system-design work.  That scope matches the surviving Paper II contribution: earned route independence, cumulative question-conditioned read state, typed route/task stopping, fail-closed coverage, and recall-first promotion around scientific-literature discovery.

## Why JASIST is fallback rather than primary

JASIST currently welcomes methodological innovation and work from computing communities, but its author guidance asks submissions to make an explicit information-science contribution and says empirical studies of specific tools/techniques should extend beyond a narrowly defined technique and involve studies of use.  The current P2 evidence is strongest as a controlled retrieval-governance/methods paper and does not include a user/use study, so JASIST would require a larger conceptual/use-oriented reframe.
