# P2 closure-receipt reframe — 2026-08-21

Issue: **#650** ("Require route-level and task-level closure receipts").
Terminal touched: **P2-U-T2** — *"Simultaneous non-inferiority/superiority on
false closure."*
Artifact: `evidence/offline_results/CLOSURE_RECEIPT_LEDGER_V1.json`, generated by
`scripts/build_closure_receipt_ledger.py`.

This receipt does not add a measurement. It adds the **denominator** to a
measurement P2 already had, and that changes what P2 is entitled to say — in
both directions.

## The defect

`premature_task_closure_rate` is a mean over every task in a slice. Its
numerator is the count of false closures; its denominator is the count of tasks
*run*, not the count of closures *claimed*. `gold._stop_audits` correctly refuses
to call a non-claim premature —

```python
if decision.scope == StopScope.TASK.value and not decision.claimed_complete:
    # Prematurity is a property of the *claim*, not of stopping.
    premature = False
```

— so a system that never claims completeness scores exactly what a system that
claims 260 times and is never wrong scores: `0.0`.

On the 24-task external Wide acquisition slice, both arms of
`P2_V2_ACQUISITION_DEV3R_RESULT_2026-08-18.json` record
`tasks_closed_as_complete: 0` and `tasks_with_open_obligations: 0`. Every task
ended in a state the V1 schema cannot name. Read through the old metric, P2-U-T2
would have been satisfiable there by exact parity between two arms that never
closed anything.

Failure class: `VACUOUS_GUARD_ZERO_DENOMINATOR`
(`research/failures/2026-08-vacuous-guard-zero-denominator/`).

## What this strengthens

The controlled claim gets **wider**, not narrower:

> On the frozen complete-gold world, ORION declared task-global completeness on
> 260 of 390 tasks and was premature on **none** of them, while every
> non-exploratory baseline closed all 390 and was premature on all 390. ORION's
> abstention is selective, not blanket: it withheld closure on 130 tasks and
> was never wrong on the 260 it took.

"Rate 0.0 over 390 tasks" was compatible with never closing at all, and a
sceptical reader was right to discount it. The denominator removes that reading.

It also separates three systems the old metric merged. `orion_full`,
`no_question_conditioned_read_ledger` and `no_content_identity_dedup` all publish
`0.0`, but they close 260, 260 and 192 times respectively — the read-ledger
ablation costs nothing in closure discipline, the dedup ablation costs 68
closures. That is a mechanism result the rate could not express.

And it rescues a **negative** the old metric hid. `no_unavailable_route_open_state`
publishes `premature_task_closure_rate: 0.0` and fails 12 tasks with
`failure_class: premature_closure`. Both are true: its 12 false closures are all
*censored-material* closures, which never set `StopAudit.premature` because the
audit's live-reach calculation excludes dead routes. `FalseClosureKind` now
carries the evaluator's two grounds separately —
`REACHABLE_GOLD_OUTSTANDING` and `CENSORED_MATERIAL_OUTSTANDING` — so the
ablation that removes the open-obligation mechanic is scored by the failure it
actually causes.

## What this narrows

**P2-U-T2 is not dischargeable from the external Wide campaign.** With zero
closures in both arms, `assess_guard` returns `CANNOT_CHECK` per arm and
`assess_non_inferiority` returns `CANNOT_CHECK / NEITHER_ARM_EXERCISED`. By
`Outcome.blocks`, that blocks promotion exactly as a `FAIL` would. The
non-inferiority half of P2-U-T2 therefore rests on the controlled campaign
alone, at `BOUNDED_PROTECTED` grade, and the external half is an open obligation
rather than a satisfied one.

This does not change `P2_NARROWED`. It states precisely *why* one of its
components was never checkable, in a form the harness can re-evaluate the moment
an admissible external campaign exists.

## Mechanism added

| module | obligation |
| --- | --- |
| `orion.programme.guard_exercise` | a guard verdict carries its denominator; zero opportunities is `CANNOT_CHECK`; `GuardAssessment` refuses at construction to pair `PASS` with a vacuity reason |
| `orion.study.p2.closure_receipts` | total route/task closure taxonomies; both false-closure grounds carried separately; `require_closure_receipts` refuses outcome access until every cell has a receipt |

`require_closure_receipts` is #650's ask stated as a **precondition** rather than
a report field: a cell nobody looked at is not a zero, and admitting it into an
aggregate is how a denominator shrinks by omission.

## Reopen rule

Recompute this ledger whenever a new P2 campaign produces outcomes. A campaign
whose arms record zero closures has not tested the closure guard, and its
false-closure result may not be reported as parity, non-inferiority, or a pass —
only as `CANNOT_CHECK` with the opportunity count that produced it.
