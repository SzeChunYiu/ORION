\begin{abstract}
Open-world scientific discovery has two separable control problems: acquiring useful evidence and deciding when the evidence collected so far is authorized to certify task completion. Paper II makes this distinction explicit. After strong verification-aware coverage, decision/utility stopping, finite route certificates, deduplication, and question-conditioned processing state are granted to the comparison product, the post-saturation P2-X successor attains \textbf{400/400 exact scientific closure decisions} on 400 protected heterogeneous acquisition contracts versus 250/400 for the donor-complete available-route product, a paired advantage of \textbf{0.375} with 95\% CI $[0.3275,0.4225]$. P2-X records zero false task closures, preserves 1.000 clean task-stop accuracy, and has a positive advantage in all five acquisition families. An information-equivalent typed product implementing the same unresolved-route authority relation also attains 400/400, establishing portability of the closure semantics across implementations. A complementary frozen \OfflineTaskCount{}-task complete-gold controlled index shows full ORION mean recall of \textbf{0.979487} versus 0.666667 for the strongest protocol-frozen confirmatory comparator, with no premature task closures; the frozen statistical plan retains its predeclared underpowered designation for inferential superiority, so this companion result is used as strong mechanism evidence rather than promoted beyond its authority. A credential-free MetaSyn probe further provides external stage evidence with retrieval recall 0.7485 and inclusion recall 0.5651. Together, the studies support a strong systems conclusion: \emph{route-local acquisition success and task-global scientific closure are different authority relations}, and explicitly preserving unresolved material routes prevents false closure even after mature acquisition and stopping mechanisms are already present.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} scientific literature discovery; information retrieval; deep research agents; systematic review; recall; stopping rules; open-world search.

\section{Problem and scientific contribution}
Scientific discovery agents increasingly combine retrieval, query reformulation, reranking, graph expansion, selective reading, verification-aware coverage, and learned stopping. Paper II absorbs these capabilities as donor substrate and asks the higher-level systems question they do not settle by themselves: \emph{what authority does a local acquisition signal have to certify scientific completeness?} The scientific object is therefore the closure boundary around increasingly capable acquisition machinery: when is a route genuinely independent, what has already been processed for the current scientific question, when may a route stop, and when must task-level coverage remain open?

The paper makes four coupled contributions. First, it formalizes \emph{earned route independence}: tool or provider names carry no evidential weight without distinct backend, query-derivation and capture identities. Second, it separates route-local stopping from task-global closure and treats unavailable/censored material routes as typed obligations rather than negative evidence. Third, it separates content identity from question-conditioned read state, preventing duplicate discovery inflation without suppressing legitimate rereading. Fourth, it makes the acquisition layer donor-composable: stronger lexical, dense, metadata-augmented, reasoning-aware, field-aware, graph-expansion, verification or utility mechanisms can be incorporated without inheriting authority to erase unresolved obligations. P2-X tests this interface after those donor capabilities are already granted.

Let route $r$ be characterized by a backend identity $b_r$, query-derivation process $q_r$, and capture/content identity policy $c_r$. A distinct route label is insufficient for independence. Evidence for route independence must be derived from the actual generation and capture process rather than asserted as a boolean.

\section{Question-conditioned cumulative read state}
A paper being previously encountered is weaker than the statement that it has been processed for the current research question, extraction schema and content version. ORION therefore retains content identity separately from question-conditioned read state. A changed question or materially changed content can legitimately reopen a source for processing without pretending that it is newly discovered evidence.

\section{Route stopping versus task stopping}
Paper II separates route outcomes such as continue, reformulate, switch, budget-stop, unavailable and locally flat from task-level scientific closure. A route may stop because its marginal yield is low while another route remains untried, unavailable or censored. Coverage diagnostics therefore remain advisory; they cannot promote global completeness.

This fail-closed rule is especially important for capture--recapture-style diagnostics. Adaptive search routes are not automatically independent capture occasions, and zero overlap cannot justify a confident bounded estimate of unseen literature.

\section{Nearest work}
AutoResearchBench shows that both specific-target and open-ended scientific literature discovery remain difficult \cite{autoresearchbench2026}. SAGE demonstrates that strong lexical retrieval can outperform LLM retrievers inside deep-research workflows \cite{sage2026}, while AgentIR shows that an agent's reasoning trace can itself become a retrieval signal \cite{agentir2026}. SIEVE further demonstrates that fielded Boolean constraints, structure-rich inspection and selective section fetching can improve deep-research retrieval efficiency \cite{sieve2026}. Paper II therefore does not claim lexical, reasoning-aware, field-aware or generic adaptive retrieval as novel; these are candidate acquisition modules whose outputs remain subject to the same closure authority boundary.

MetaSyn separates retrieval from eligibility/screening and shows why retrieval ceilings do not imply inclusion recall \cite{metasyn2026}. AgentSLR demonstrates that end-to-end systematic-review automation is itself an active baseline area \cite{agentslr2026}. Bibliography expansion can also transform literature-search recall, while human reference lists are not a complete single-axis ground truth \cite{rethinkinglitsearch2026}. These results motivate stage attribution, donor composition and denominator caution rather than a claim that ORION invented literature-search automation.

The stopping boundary is also crowded prior art. DeepControl regulates retrieval continuation using marginal information utility \cite{deepcontrol2026}; decision-theoretic screening rules optimize stopping against explicit cost/payoff models \cite{decisionstop2026}; HALT frames stopping as claim-evidence coverage and can abstain when coverage is not verifiable \cite{halt2026}; MiCP applies conformal prediction to adaptive multi-turn reasoning to maintain target coverage while permitting early stopping \cite{micp2026}; and a 2026-08-13 Search-R1 study trains structured sufficiency/gap STOP judgments while explicitly not claiming safe stopping \cite{multiragstop2026}. Accordingly, Paper II does not claim verification-aware, conformal-coverage, utility-based or learned STOP/CONTINUE policies as novel. Its contribution is an authority rule: relevance, utility, coverage and sufficiency may guide acquisition or route-local stopping, but unresolved unavailable/censored route obligations cannot be silently erased or promoted into global scientific closure.

Question-conditioned memory is likewise broader than this paper: MemChain constructs question-conditioned evidence plans and grounded memory traces \cite{memchain2026}. The P2 contribution is the dual bookkeeping needed for literature discovery---content identity to prevent duplicate discovery inflation, kept distinct from question-conditioned processing state so a changed extraction question can legitimately trigger rereading.

The post-saturation claim-expansion search tightens the architecture-level attribution while strengthening the remaining systems claim. Don't Stop Early already combines coverage-driven objectives with evidence-aware completion \cite{donotstopearly2026}; Confidence-Based Stopping targets decision sufficiency rather than recall alone \cite{confidencebasedstop2026}; KnowPlan uses atomic source obligations and an explicit finite closure certificate \cite{knowplan2026}; iCORE binds generic work/obligation transitions to evidence \cite{icore2026}; and Science of Intent explicitly names open-world closure gaps and misclosure \cite{scienceintent2026}. Paper II therefore claims none of evidence-based completion, decision-sufficiency stopping, finite obligation ledgers, generic obligation graphs or ``closure gap'' terminology. After those donor mechanisms are granted, P2-X isolates the decisive authority condition: when a \emph{material} acquisition route is unavailable, censored or provider-invalid, excluding it from the global denominator converts missing authority into false closure. The exact successor tests that condition and the separate question-processing obligation directly (Section~\ref{sec:p2x}).

\section{Transfer scope and integrity}
The controlled-index companion provides a large descriptive mechanism separation---mean recall 0.979487 versus 0.666667---but its frozen statistical plan retains the predeclared underpowered label for inferential superiority because the achieved half-width exceeds the registered $0.03$ margin. That designation is preserved exactly; the result is used to support the route/read/stopping mechanism and not to manufacture a stronger statistical conclusion. All \OfflineRunRecordCount{} system/task/repeat executions reproduce exactly, and the \OfflineRepeatCount{} deterministic repeat seeds demonstrate stable execution while remaining nested within task.

The P2-X result has a different and stronger authority. Its 400-case exact battery spans five acquisition settings, seed-permuted route order, hostile unresolved-route archetypes, and non-material unavailable decoys. Route state, materiality, and question-processing state are supplied explicitly, so the demonstrated 1.000 ESCD result is an exact-contract result rather than a claim about arbitrary noisy web inference. The information-equivalent B3 tie establishes that the closure semantics are portable; it does not reduce the 150--0 separation from the realistic donor-complete B1 interface.

External-provider campaigns remain useful transfer tests rather than evidence for or against the exact-contract result. The archived OpenAIRE/Crossref Wide campaign failed its prospectively frozen provider-validity gate and therefore remains \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}; its zero paired observation is not interpreted as a null effect. Aggregate-recall-neutral ablations are likewise retained for what they instrument: the question coordinate changes legitimate reread behavior, and the unavailable-route state changes closure semantics even when recall is numerically unchanged. These integrity rules prevent unrelated transport or metric failures from diluting or inflating the demonstrated closure-authority claim.

\section{Conclusion}
Paper II establishes a fail-closed authority layer for open-world discovery. After donor-complete evidence coverage, decision/utility stopping, finite route certificates, deduplication, route state, and question-conditioned processing are granted, P2-X reaches \textbf{400/400 exact scientific closure decisions} versus 250/400 for the strong available-route donor product. The paired advantage is \textbf{0.375} with 95\% CI $[0.3275,0.4225]$, with zero false task closures, perfect clean closure, and a positive effect in all five acquisition families. All 150 B1 errors occur exactly when a material route is unavailable, censored, or provider-invalid and is incorrectly removed from the closure denominator.

The information-equivalent typed product also reaches 400/400. This is a portability result: the scientific closure rule is sufficient whether implemented as ORION or as a correctly enriched donor product. The contribution is therefore the explicit authority distinction that prevents route-local success from laundering itself into task-global completeness after mature acquisition and stopping machinery has already done its work.

The controlled-index companion reinforces the architecture with mean recall 0.979487 versus 0.666667 for the strongest frozen confirmatory comparator and no premature task closures, while its predeclared inferential ceiling remains intact. The MetaSyn probe adds external stage evidence for retrieval and screening. Taken together, the evidence supports the strongest current conclusion: \textbf{material unresolved routes are scientific closure obligations, and representing them explicitly can convert a donor-complete acquisition system from 0.625 to 1.000 exact closure accuracy on the registered heterogeneous contracts without sacrificing any clean closures}. Live multi-provider and retrieval-engine transfer are the next empirical stress tests of an already demonstrated authority relation.
