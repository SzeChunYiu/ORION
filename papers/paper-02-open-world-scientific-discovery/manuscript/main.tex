\begin{abstract}
Scientific-literature discovery has two distinct control problems: acquiring useful evidence and deciding what that evidence is allowed to certify. Paper II separates an increasingly capable, donor-composable acquisition layer from a fail-closed scientific-closure authority layer. ORION counts routes as independent only when independence is earned through backend, query-derivation and capture identity; separates content identity from question-conditioned processing state; prevents route-local stopping from certifying task-global completeness; and retains unavailable or censored routes as explicit open obligations. In a frozen \OfflineTaskCount{}-task complete-gold controlled index, full ORION reaches mean recall 0.979487 versus 0.666667 for the strongest protocol-frozen confirmatory comparator and makes no premature task closures; targeted negative ablations expose the intended failure modes. The frozen statistical plan awards this campaign its committed \texttt{TIER\_B\_committed} precision tier, but the achieved half-width still exceeds the frozen superiority margin, so the primary offline result carries the plan's mandatory underpowered label and these numbers support mechanism behavior rather than an inferential superiority claim. A separate credential-free MetaSyn probe, evaluated with the pinned official ID-only evaluator on all 86 released test reviews, reaches retrieval recall 0.7485 and inclusion recall 0.5651; it is a bounded external retrieval/screening probe rather than evidence of full multi-provider ORION superiority. A matched external ORION-vs-baseline Wide/Deep result is not archived in this revision, so the paper-level real-world superiority claim remains \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}. The architectural contribution is a composable authority boundary beneath replaceable acquisition mechanisms: local utility, confidence, coverage, sufficiency or provider failure does not by itself erase unresolved scientific-search obligations.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} scientific literature discovery; information retrieval; deep research agents; systematic review; recall; stopping rules; open-world search.

\section{Problem and claim boundary}
Scientific discovery agents increasingly combine retrieval, query reformulation, reranking, graph expansion, selective reading and learned stopping. The central claim of Paper II is that progress in those components does not remove a separate systems question: \emph{what authority does a local acquisition signal have to certify scientific completeness?} Paper II does not claim scientific RAG, agentic literature search, automated systematic review, capture--recapture estimation, lexical retrieval, reasoning-aware retrieval or learned stopping as novel. It studies the control boundary around those mechanisms: when is a route genuinely different, what has already been processed for the current scientific question, when may a route stop, and when must task-level coverage remain open?

The paper makes four scoped contributions. First, it formalizes \emph{earned route independence}: tool or provider names carry no evidential weight without distinct backend, query-derivation and capture identities. Second, it separates route-local stopping from task-global closure and treats unavailable/censored routes as typed obligations rather than negative evidence. Third, it separates content identity from question-conditioned read state, preventing duplicate discovery inflation without suppressing legitimate rereading. Fourth, it makes the acquisition layer donor-composable: stronger lexical, dense, metadata-augmented, reasoning-aware, field-aware, graph-expansion, verification or utility mechanisms can be incorporated without inheriting authority to erase unresolved obligations.

Let route $r$ be characterized by a backend identity $b_r$, query-derivation process $q_r$, and capture/content identity policy $c_r$. A distinct route label is insufficient for independence. Evidence for route independence must be derived from the actual generation and capture process rather than asserted as a boolean.

\section{Question-conditioned cumulative read state}
A paper being previously encountered is weaker than the statement that it has been processed for the current research question, extraction schema and content version. ORION therefore retains content identity separately from question-conditioned read state. A changed question or materially changed content can legitimately reopen a source for processing without pretending that it is newly discovered evidence.

\section{Route stopping versus task stopping}
Paper II separates route outcomes such as continue, reformulate, switch, budget-stop, unavailable and locally flat from task-level scientific closure. A route may stop because its marginal yield is low while another route remains untried, unavailable or censored. Coverage diagnostics therefore remain advisory; they cannot promote global completeness.

This fail-closed rule is especially important for capture--recapture-style diagnostics. Adaptive search routes are not automatically independent capture occasions, and zero overlap cannot justify a confident bounded estimate of unseen literature.

\section{Nearest work}
AutoResearchBench shows that both specific-target and open-ended scientific literature discovery remain difficult \cite{autoresearchbench2026}. SAGE demonstrates that strong lexical retrieval can outperform LLM retrievers inside deep-research workflows \cite{sage2026}, while AgentIR shows that an agent's reasoning trace can itself become a retrieval signal \cite{agentir2026}. SIEVE further demonstrates that fielded Boolean constraints, structure-rich inspection and selective section fetching can improve deep-research retrieval efficiency \cite{sieve2026}. Paper II therefore does not claim lexical, reasoning-aware, field-aware or generic adaptive retrieval as novel; these are candidate acquisition modules whose outputs remain subject to the same closure authority boundary.

MetaSyn separates retrieval from eligibility/screening and shows why retrieval ceilings do not imply inclusion recall \cite{metasyn2026}. AgentSLR demonstrates that end-to-end systematic-review automation is itself an active baseline area \cite{agentslr2026}. Bibliography expansion can also transform literature-search recall, while human reference lists are not a complete single-axis ground truth \cite{rethinkinglitsearch2026}. These results motivate stage attribution, donor composition and denominator caution rather than a claim that ORION invented literature-search automation.

The stopping boundary is also crowded prior art. DeepControl regulates retrieval continuation using marginal information utility \cite{deepcontrol2026}; decision-theoretic screening rules optimize stopping against explicit cost/payoff models \cite{decisionstop2026}; HALT frames stopping as claim-evidence coverage and can abstain when coverage is not verifiable \cite{halt2026}; MiCP applies conformal prediction to adaptive multi-turn reasoning to maintain target coverage while permitting early stopping \cite{micp2026}; and a 2026-08-13 Search-R1 study trains structured sufficiency/gap STOP judgments while explicitly not claiming safe stopping \cite{multiragstop2026}. Accordingly, Paper II does not claim verification-aware, conformal-coverage, utility-based or learned STOP/CONTINUE policies as novel. Its candidate residual contribution is an authority rule: relevance, utility, coverage and sufficiency may guide acquisition or route-local stopping, but unresolved unavailable/censored route obligations cannot be silently erased or promoted into global scientific closure.

Question-conditioned memory is likewise broader than this paper: MemChain constructs question-conditioned evidence plans and grounded memory traces \cite{memchain2026}. The residual here is only the dual bookkeeping needed for literature discovery---content identity to prevent duplicate discovery inflation, kept distinct from question-conditioned processing state so a changed extraction question can legitimately trigger rereading.

Because directly relevant stopping work appeared as recently as 2026-08-13, the nearest-work state is frozen only through 2026-08-17 and is not labelled saturated. The source-bound donor ledger and pre-submission refresh rule treat later material work as a reopen trigger rather than silently preserving stale novelty.

\section{Limitations and integrity}
The completed controlled experiment is deliberately synthetic. Its complete denominator is what makes missed relevant material and premature closure observable, but the authored routes, public probe vocabulary and easy screening text make it a mechanism test rather than a realistic estimate of retrieval difficulty. The observed +0.31282 recall difference therefore cannot be transferred to the open web or to AutoResearchBench by analogy.

The suite carries \OfflineTaskCount{} tasks, which reaches precision tier \OfflineAchievedTier{} at an achieved half-width of \OfflineAchievedHalfWidth{} for a standalone rate. The frozen statistics plan fixed those tiers before outcomes were inspected and assigns this campaign authority \OfflineAnalysisAuthority{}; because the achieved half-width still exceeds the frozen superiority margin of $0.03$, the primary offline result carries the plan's underpowered label whatever the task count becomes. All \OfflineRunRecordCount{} system/task/repeat executions reproduce exactly, and the \OfflineRepeatCount{} deterministic repeat seeds demonstrate stable execution; repeats nest within task and never increase the statistical unit.

Open-world completeness remains generally unprovable from finite search. External providers are mutable, rate limited and differently scoped, and public benchmark questions/gold identifiers create search-time contamination risk. The corrected MetaSyn run is external but narrow: it uses pinned BM25 retrieval plus a deterministic public-protocol screening rule and the official ID-only evaluator, not the complete ORION route system. Its exact evaluator-side ledger contains 1,677 reference links, of which 598 are missed at retrieval and another 343 are lost after retrieval, leaving 941 final false negatives. These numbers characterize that probe and are not promoted to a general ORION claim.

Several external authorities remain partially unavailable rather than negative. AutoResearchBench Deep official scoring has now executed through a local OpenAI-compatible adapter for the bounded keyless probe, with the judge control passing in full, but the matched multi-provider Deep comparison remains unexecuted. SAGE still lacks its published retrieval corpus and official evaluator. The frozen three-route live-provider protocol requires a funded OpenAlex key for two of its three route families; a same-repository preflight found no \texttt{OPENALEX\_API\_KEY}, so the protocol cannot honestly enter \texttt{EXECUTION\_FROZEN} here. Provider or credential unavailability is therefore retained as \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}, never converted into evidence of absence or a flattering zero-cost result.

The mechanism ablations themselves impose an interpretive limit. Removing the question coordinate from the read ledger changes legitimate reread behavior but does not change aggregate recall in this suite; removing the unavailable-route open state changes censoring/closure semantics while leaving aggregate recall numerically unchanged. These null-on-recall mechanism findings are retained rather than converted into a stronger claim.

Rows without an archived supporting result remain \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}; an access audit, implementation test, or credential preflight cannot substitute for a system outcome.

\section{Conclusion}
Paper II advances a systems thesis broader than a contest between individual retrievers: acquisition quality and scientific-closure authority are different layers. The frozen controlled-index companion supports a narrow engineering result: under a complete denominator and matched host-owned budgets, the route/read/stopping governance mechanisms behave in the intended fail-closed directions, and the strongest confirmatory controlled comparator still leaves substantial reachable gold. The separate MetaSyn probe supplies real external retrieval/screening evidence but does not exercise or validate the complete multi-route ORION system. The controlled evidence remains descriptive and synthetic, and the paper's matched external discovery/stopping claim remains open until denominator-valid Wide/Deep or other admissible external results are archived. If those authorities remain unavailable or do not support the frozen promotion rule, the claim must remain narrowed rather than promoted. This positions the architecture as an authority contract beneath replaceable acquisition modules: lexical, metadata-augmented, reasoning-aware, field-aware, citation-expansion, verification, conformal, sufficiency and utility mechanisms may be composed without granting local signals permission to erase unresolved obligations.
