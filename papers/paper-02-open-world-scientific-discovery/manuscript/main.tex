\begin{abstract}
Scientific-literature discovery has two coupled but non-equivalent control problems: acquiring useful evidence and deciding what that evidence is allowed to certify. We separate a donor-composable acquisition layer from a fail-closed scientific-closure authority layer. ORION counts routes as independent only when independence is earned through backend, query-derivation and capture identity; separates content identity from question-conditioned processing state; prevents route-local stopping from certifying task-global completeness; and retains unavailable, censored, or provider-invalid material routes as explicit open obligations. The strongest test isolates this authority question after granting a donor product strong evidence coverage, decision/utility stopping, finite route certificates, deduplication, and question-processing state. On 400 prospectively frozen heterogeneous exact acquisition contracts, P2-X attains 400/400 exact scientific-closure decisions versus 250/400 for the donor-complete available-route product, a paired advantage of 0.375 (95\% CI [0.3275,0.4225]), with zero false task closures and perfect clean closure. All 150 comparator errors arise when material unresolved routes are excluded from the global closure denominator. An ideal typed product implementing the same unresolved-route semantics also attains 400/400, showing that the authority contract is portable rather than dependent on centralized ORION expressivity. A frozen \OfflineTaskCount{}-task complete-gold controlled companion independently shows mean recall 0.979487 versus 0.666667 for the strongest protocol-frozen confirmatory comparator and no premature task closures; its registered precision plan labels the recall difference underpowered for inferential superiority, so it is used as mechanism evidence rather than over-promoted. A credential-free MetaSyn probe further separates retrieval and inclusion loss on all 86 released test reviews. Finally, a matched OpenAIRE/Crossref campaign is withheld from scientific comparison because it fails its predeclared transport-validity gate, demonstrating the evaluation rule that infrastructure failure cannot be converted into evidence of equivalence or absence. Together these results establish the systems claim: acquisition quality and scientific-closure authority are distinct, and unresolved material search obligations must remain authority-bearing until they are discharged, excluded by valid evidence, or explicitly left unresolved.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} scientific literature discovery; information retrieval; deep research agents; systematic review; recall; stopping rules; open-world search.

\section{Problem and claim boundary}
Scientific discovery agents increasingly combine retrieval, query reformulation, reranking, graph expansion, selective reading and learned stopping. The central claim of Paper II is that progress in those components does not remove a separate systems question: \emph{what authority does a local acquisition signal have to certify scientific completeness?} Paper II does not claim scientific RAG, agentic literature search, automated systematic review, capture--recapture estimation, lexical retrieval, reasoning-aware retrieval or learned stopping as novel. It studies the control boundary around those mechanisms: when is a route genuinely different, what has already been processed for the current scientific question, when may a route stop, and when must task-level coverage remain open?

The paper makes four coupled contributions. First, it formalizes \emph{earned route independence}: tool or provider names carry no evidential weight without distinct backend, query-derivation and capture identities. Second, it separates route-local stopping from task-global closure and treats unavailable/censored material routes as typed obligations rather than negative evidence. Third, it separates content identity from question-conditioned read state, preventing duplicate discovery inflation without suppressing legitimate rereading. Fourth, it makes the acquisition layer donor-composable: stronger lexical, dense, metadata-augmented, reasoning-aware, field-aware, graph-expansion, verification or utility mechanisms can be incorporated without inheriting authority to erase unresolved obligations. P2-X tests the resulting interface after those donor capabilities are already granted.

Let route $r$ be characterized by a backend identity $b_r$, query-derivation process $q_r$, and capture/content identity policy $c_r$. A distinct route label is insufficient for independence. Evidence for route independence must be derived from the actual generation and capture process rather than asserted as a boolean.

\section{Question-conditioned cumulative read state}
A paper being previously encountered is weaker than the statement that it has been processed for the current research question, extraction schema and content version. ORION therefore retains content identity separately from question-conditioned read state. A changed question or materially changed content can legitimately reopen a source for processing without pretending that it is newly discovered evidence.

\section{Route stopping versus task stopping}
Paper II separates route outcomes such as continue, reformulate, switch, budget-stop, unavailable and locally flat from task-level scientific closure. A route may stop because its marginal yield is low while another route remains untried, unavailable or censored. Coverage diagnostics therefore remain advisory; they cannot promote global completeness.

This fail-closed rule is especially important for capture--recapture-style diagnostics. Adaptive search routes are not automatically independent capture occasions, and zero overlap cannot justify a confident bounded estimate of unseen literature.

\section{Nearest work}
AutoResearchBench shows that both specific-target and open-ended scientific literature discovery remain difficult \cite{autoresearchbench2026}. SAGE demonstrates that strong lexical retrieval can outperform LLM retrievers inside deep-research workflows \cite{sage2026}, while AgentIR shows that an agent's reasoning trace can itself become a retrieval signal \cite{agentir2026}. SIEVE further demonstrates that fielded Boolean constraints, structure-rich inspection and selective section fetching can improve deep-research retrieval efficiency \cite{sieve2026}. Paper II therefore does not claim lexical, reasoning-aware, field-aware or generic adaptive retrieval as novel; these are candidate acquisition modules whose outputs remain subject to the same closure authority boundary.

MetaSyn separates retrieval from eligibility/screening and shows why retrieval ceilings do not imply inclusion recall \cite{metasyn2026}. AgentSLR demonstrates that end-to-end systematic-review automation is itself an active baseline area \cite{agentslr2026}. Bibliography expansion can also transform literature-search recall, while human reference lists are not a complete single-axis ground truth \cite{rethinkinglitsearch2026}. These results motivate stage attribution, donor composition and denominator caution rather than a claim that ORION invented literature-search automation.

The stopping boundary is also crowded prior art. DeepControl regulates retrieval continuation using marginal information utility \cite{deepcontrol2026}; decision-theoretic screening rules optimize stopping against explicit cost/payoff models \cite{decisionstop2026}; HALT frames stopping as claim-evidence coverage and can abstain when coverage is not verifiable \cite{halt2026}; MiCP applies conformal prediction to adaptive multi-turn reasoning to maintain target coverage while permitting early stopping \cite{micp2026}; and a 2026-08-13 Search-R1 study trains structured sufficiency/gap STOP judgments while explicitly not claiming safe stopping \cite{multiragstop2026}. Accordingly, Paper II does not claim verification-aware, conformal-coverage, utility-based or learned STOP/CONTINUE policies as novel. Its contribution is an authority rule: relevance, utility, coverage and sufficiency may guide acquisition or route-local stopping, but unresolved unavailable/censored route obligations cannot be silently erased or promoted into global scientific closure.

Question-conditioned memory is likewise broader than this paper: MemChain constructs question-conditioned evidence plans and grounded memory traces \cite{memchain2026}. The P2 contribution is the dual bookkeeping needed for literature discovery---content identity to prevent duplicate discovery inflation, kept distinct from question-conditioned processing state so a changed extraction question can legitimately trigger rereading.

The post-saturation claim-expansion search tightens the architecture-level attribution while strengthening the remaining systems claim. Don't Stop Early already combines coverage-driven objectives with evidence-aware completion \cite{donotstopearly2026}; Confidence-Based Stopping targets decision sufficiency rather than recall alone \cite{confidencebasedstop2026}; KnowPlan uses atomic source obligations and an explicit finite closure certificate \cite{knowplan2026}; iCORE binds generic work/obligation transitions to evidence \cite{icore2026}; and Science of Intent explicitly names open-world closure gaps and misclosure \cite{scienceintent2026}. Paper II therefore claims none of evidence-based completion, decision-sufficiency stopping, finite obligation ledgers, generic obligation graphs or ``closure gap'' terminology. After those donor mechanisms are granted, P2-X isolates the decisive authority condition: when a \emph{material} acquisition route is unavailable, censored or provider-invalid, excluding it from the global denominator converts missing authority into false closure. The exact successor tests that condition and the separate question-processing obligation directly (Section~\ref{sec:p2x}).

\section{Boundaries, validity controls, and external transfer}
The completed controlled experiment is deliberately synthetic. Its complete denominator is what makes missed relevant material and premature closure observable, but the authored routes, public probe vocabulary and easy screening text make it a mechanism test rather than a realistic estimate of retrieval difficulty. The observed +0.31282 recall difference therefore remains a controlled descriptive separation rather than a transferable estimate for the open web or AutoResearchBench.

The suite carries \OfflineTaskCount{} tasks, which reaches precision tier \OfflineAchievedTier{} at an achieved half-width of \OfflineAchievedHalfWidth{} for a standalone rate. The frozen statistics plan fixed those tiers before outcomes were inspected and assigns this campaign authority \OfflineAnalysisAuthority{}; because the achieved half-width still exceeds the frozen superiority margin of $0.03$, the primary offline result carries the plan's underpowered label whatever the task count becomes. All \OfflineRunRecordCount{} system/task/repeat executions reproduce exactly, and the \OfflineRepeatCount{} deterministic repeat seeds demonstrate stable execution; repeats nest within task and never increase the statistical unit.

P2-X broadens the \emph{contract families} tested, not the realism of retrieval. Its 400-case exact battery spans five acquisition settings and includes seed-permuted route order plus non-material unavailable decoys, but route state, route materiality and question-processing state are explicitly supplied rather than inferred from noisy web behavior. The 1.000 P2-X ESCD ceiling therefore supports the fail-closed authority rule on the registered exact contracts. B3 is especially informative: an ideal product given identical unresolved-route semantics is extensionally equivalent, showing that the decisive object is the authority contract itself rather than a proprietary retrieval engine or centralized architecture.

Open-world completeness remains generally unprovable from finite search. External providers are mutable, rate limited and differently scoped, and public benchmark questions/gold identifiers create search-time contamination risk. The corrected MetaSyn run is external but narrow: it uses pinned BM25 retrieval plus a deterministic public-protocol screening rule and the official ID-only evaluator, not the complete ORION route system. Its exact evaluator-side ledger contains 1,677 reference links, of which 598 are missed at retrieval and another 343 are lost after retrieval, leaving 941 final false negatives. This stage-separated ledger is useful precisely because it localizes where evidence is lost rather than compressing all failure into one retrieval score.

External campaign validity is itself governed fail-closed. AutoResearchBench Deep official scoring executes through a local OpenAI-compatible adapter for the bounded keyless probe, with the judge control passing in full, while the matched multi-provider comparison remains a separate unexecuted question. SAGE still lacks its published retrieval corpus and official evaluator. Most importantly, the archived OpenAIRE/Crossref Wide campaign fails its prospectively frozen provider-validity gate after transport errors. The protocol therefore withholds scientific authority from its observed zero paired effect instead of treating infrastructure failure as equivalence. This is the intended validity behavior: provider or credential unavailability remains typed unresolved evidence rather than being converted into evidence of absence, zero cost, or system parity.

The mechanism ablations also show why recall cannot serve as the sole authority metric. Removing the question coordinate from the read ledger changes legitimate reread behavior without changing aggregate recall, while removing the unavailable-route open state changes censoring/closure semantics with the same aggregate recall. These orthogonal controls show that two systems can match on recall while differing on scientifically important state and closure decisions; the authority layer therefore requires direct evaluation rather than inference from retrieval yield alone.

\section{Conclusion}
Paper II establishes acquisition quality and scientific-closure authority as separate system layers. The strongest evidence comes from P2-X, which grants the comparator strong coverage, stopping, certificate, deduplication, and question-processing mechanisms before isolating unresolved-route authority. Across 400 prospectively frozen exact heterogeneous contracts, P2-X reaches 400/400 exact closure decisions versus 250/400 for the donor-complete available-route product, a paired advantage of 0.375 with 95\% CI [0.3275,0.4225]. It preserves zero false task closures and perfect clean closure. Every one of the comparator's 150 errors occurs on a material unavailable, censored, or provider-invalid route that is incorrectly removed from the global denominator.

An ideal typed product implementing the same unresolved-route semantics also reaches 400/400. This result strengthens the architectural conclusion: the scientific-closure contract is portable across implementations. The contribution is not a proprietary stopping heuristic or a claim of inherent centralization advantage. It is the explicit authority relation that keeps unresolved material search obligations live until they are scientifically discharged or validly excluded.

The controlled-index companion independently supports the same architecture from the acquisition side. Under a complete denominator and matched host-owned budgets, full ORION reaches mean recall 0.979487 versus 0.666667 for the strongest frozen confirmatory comparator and makes no premature task closures. The frozen power plan prevents these descriptive numbers from being promoted into an inferential retrieval-superiority claim, while the MetaSyn probe provides a real external stage-separated view of retrieval and screening loss.

The failed OpenAIRE/Crossref transport gate is therefore not a scientific result against the method. It is a successful validity refusal: the evaluation protocol detects that the external comparison has lost its required transport authority and prevents an observed zero effect from becoming a false equivalence conclusion. Taken together, the exact-contract result, controlled companion, stage-separated external probe, and fail-closed evaluation behavior support one coherent systems statement: better acquisition is valuable, but scientific completeness requires a separate authority contract that cannot be erased by local stopping, provider failure, or apparently reassuring coverage signals.
