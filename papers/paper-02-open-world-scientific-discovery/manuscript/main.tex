\begin{abstract}
Scientific-literature discovery has two distinct control problems: acquiring useful evidence and deciding what that evidence is allowed to certify. Paper II separates an increasingly capable, donor-composable acquisition layer from a fail-closed scientific-closure authority layer. ORION counts routes as independent only when independence is earned through backend, query-derivation and capture identity; separates content identity from question-conditioned processing state; prevents route-local stopping from certifying task-global completeness; and retains unavailable or censored routes as explicit open obligations. A separate post-saturation exact successor isolates unresolved-route authority after strong stopping mechanisms are granted: on 400 protected heterogeneous acquisition contracts, P2-X attains \textbf{400/400} exact scientific closure decisions versus \textbf{250/400} for a donor-complete available-route product (paired difference \textbf{0.375}, 95\% CI [0.3275,0.4225]), with zero false task closures; an ideal product supplied identical route-authority semantics ties 400/400. In a frozen \OfflineTaskCount{}-task complete-gold controlled index, full ORION reaches mean recall 0.979487 versus 0.666667 for the strongest protocol-frozen confirmatory comparator and makes no premature task closures; targeted negative ablations expose the intended failure modes. The frozen statistical plan awards this campaign its committed \texttt{TIER\_B\_committed} precision tier, but the achieved half-width still exceeds the frozen superiority margin, so the primary offline result carries the plan's mandatory underpowered label and these numbers support mechanism behavior rather than an inferential superiority claim. A separate credential-free MetaSyn probe, evaluated with the pinned official ID-only evaluator on all 86 released test reviews, reaches retrieval recall 0.7485 and inclusion recall 0.5651; it is a bounded external retrieval/screening probe rather than evidence of full multi-provider ORION superiority. A later matched OpenAIRE/Crossref Wide campaign failed its predeclared transport-validity gate; its observed zero paired difference is therefore \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}, not a valid null or negative result. The architectural contribution is therefore bounded: local utility, confidence, coverage, sufficiency or provider failure does not by itself erase unresolved scientific-search obligations, and no deployed or external retrieval-superiority claim follows from the exact-contract result.

\section{Introduction}
Scientific discovery agents increasingly combine retrieval, query reformulation, reranking, graph expansion, selective reading and learned stopping. Progress in those components does not remove a separate systems question: \emph{what authority does a local acquisition signal have to certify scientific completeness?} This paper studies the control boundary around those mechanisms: when is a route genuinely different, what has already been processed for the current scientific question, when may a route stop, and when must task-level coverage stay open?

The paper makes four scoped contributions. First, it formalizes \emph{earned route independence}: tool or provider names carry no evidential weight without distinct backend, query-derivation and capture identities. Second, it separates route-local stopping from task-global closure and treats unavailable or censored routes as typed obligations rather than as negative evidence. Third, it separates content identity from question-conditioned read state, preventing duplicate discovery inflation without suppressing legitimate rereading. Fourth, it makes the acquisition layer donor-composable: stronger lexical, dense, metadata-augmented, reasoning-aware, field-aware, graph-expansion, verification or utility mechanisms can be incorporated without inheriting authority to erase unresolved obligations.

Let route $r$ be characterized by a backend identity $b_r$, query-derivation process $q_r$, and capture/content identity policy $c_r$. A distinct route label is insufficient for independence. Evidence for route independence must be derived from the actual generation and capture process rather than asserted as a boolean.

\section{Question-conditioned cumulative read state}
A paper being previously encountered is weaker than the statement that it has been processed for the current research question, extraction schema and content version. Content identity is therefore held separately from question-conditioned read state. A changed question or materially changed content can legitimately reopen a source for processing without pretending that it is newly discovered evidence.

\section{Route stopping versus task stopping}
Route outcomes such as continue, reformulate, switch, budget-stop, unavailable and locally flat are separated from task-level scientific closure. A route may stop because its marginal yield is low while another route is still untried, unavailable or censored. Coverage diagnostics therefore stay advisory; they cannot promote global completeness.

This fail-closed rule is especially important for capture--recapture-style diagnostics. Adaptive search routes are not automatically independent capture occasions, and zero overlap cannot justify a confident bounded estimate of unseen literature.

\section{Related work}
The neighbouring literature is strongest exactly where this paper does not compete. AutoResearchBench establishes that both specific-target and open-ended scientific literature discovery are still difficult for current agents \cite{autoresearchbench2026}. On the acquisition side, SAGE shows that carefully tuned lexical retrieval can outperform LLM retrievers inside deep-research workflows \cite{sage2026}; AgentIR shows that an agent's reasoning trace is itself a usable retrieval signal \cite{agentir2026}; and SIEVE shows that fielded Boolean admission, structure-rich inspection and selective section fetching improve the accuracy--context tradeoff of deep-research retrieval \cite{sieve2026}. Breadth-first bibliography expansion recovers literature that API-only retrieval misses, and in the same move shows why a human reference list is not a complete single-axis ground truth \cite{rethinkinglitsearch2026}. Each of these is a candidate module for the acquisition layer studied here; a stronger one makes the boundary drawn in this paper more useful, not less.

Screening and stage attribution form a second neighbourhood. MetaSyn separates retrieval from eligibility screening and shows why a retrieval ceiling does not imply an inclusion-recall ceiling \cite{metasyn2026}, and AgentSLR shows that end-to-end systematic-review automation is an active baseline family in its own right \cite{agentslr2026}. This paper adopts their measurement discipline---stage attribution and denominator caution---and inherits their warning that one aggregate recall number hides where the evidence was actually lost.

The stopping literature is the closest neighbour and the most crowded. DeepControl regulates retrieval continuation by marginal information utility \cite{deepcontrol2026}; decision-theoretic screening rules optimize stopping against explicit cost and payoff models \cite{decisionstop2026}; HALT frames stopping as claim--evidence coverage and abstains when coverage cannot be verified \cite{halt2026}; MiCP applies conformal prediction to adaptive multi-turn reasoning so a target coverage level survives early stopping \cite{micp2026}; and a structured Search-R1 variant trains sufficiency and gap judgements for stopping while explicitly declining to claim the stop is safe \cite{multiragstop2026}. Don't Stop Early combines coverage-driven objectives with evidence-aware completion \cite{donotstopearly2026}; Confidence-Based Stopping targets decision sufficiency rather than recall alone \cite{confidencebasedstop2026}; KnowPlan issues a finite closure certificate over atomic source obligations \cite{knowplan2026}; iCORE binds generic work and obligation transitions to evidence \cite{icore2026}; and Science of Intent names open-world closure gaps and misclosure directly \cite{scienceintent2026}. Every one of these answers \emph{when to stop}. The question they leave open is what a stop is permitted to certify when the routes still outstanding are not merely unpromising but unavailable, censored or provider-invalid: a coverage, utility or sufficiency signal computed over the routes that answered says nothing about the route that did not.

Question-conditioned memory has its own line of work: MemChain constructs question-conditioned evidence plans and grounded memory traces \cite{memchain2026}. What literature discovery adds is a dual bookkeeping obligation---content identity, which stops duplicate rediscovery from inflating coverage, held apart from question-conditioned processing state, so that a changed extraction question can legitimately trigger a reread of material already seen.

\section{Limitations and integrity}
The completed controlled experiment is deliberately synthetic. Its complete denominator is what makes missed relevant material and premature closure observable, but the authored routes, public probe vocabulary and easy screening text make it a mechanism test rather than a realistic estimate of retrieval difficulty. The observed +0.31282 recall difference therefore cannot be transferred to the open web or to AutoResearchBench by analogy.

The contribution is a control boundary rather than a component, and the components it governs are other people's. Scientific retrieval-augmented generation, agentic literature search, automated systematic review, capture--recapture estimation, lexical, dense, reasoning-aware, field-aware and citation-expansion retrieval, learned or decision-theoretic stopping policies, conformal coverage guarantees, finite obligation ledgers and question-conditioned memory are all prior art, treated here as donor mechanisms and cited as such; the only thing this paper puts forward as its own is the rule about what their outputs may certify.

The suite carries \OfflineTaskCount{} tasks, which reaches the plan's committed precision tier at an achieved half-width of \OfflineAchievedHalfWidth{} for a standalone rate. The frozen statistics plan fixed those tiers before outcomes were inspected and assigns this campaign that committed authority; because the achieved half-width still exceeds the frozen superiority margin of $0.03$, the primary offline result carries the plan's underpowered label whatever the task count becomes. All \OfflineRunRecordCount{} system/task/repeat executions reproduce exactly, and the \OfflineRepeatCount{} deterministic repeat seeds demonstrate stable execution; repeats nest within task and never increase the statistical unit.

The exact-contract battery broadens the \emph{contract families} tested, not the realism of retrieval. Its 400 cases span five acquisition settings and include seed-permuted route order plus non-material unavailable decoys, but route state, route materiality and question-processing state are explicitly supplied rather than inferred from noisy web behavior. The 1.000 exact-closure ceiling therefore supports the fail-closed authority rule only on the registered exact contracts. No independently implemented lexical/dense/reasoning retrievers or new live provider campaign were used to establish retrieval-engine generality, and the ideal-product arm shows that an ideal product given identical unresolved-route semantics is extensionally equivalent.

Open-world completeness remains generally unprovable from finite search. External providers are mutable, rate limited and differently scoped, and public benchmark questions and gold identifiers create search-time contamination risk. The corrected MetaSyn run is external but narrow: it uses pinned BM25 retrieval plus a deterministic public-protocol screening rule and the official ID-only evaluator, not the complete route system. Its exact evaluator-side ledger contains 1,677 reference links, of which 598 are missed at retrieval and another 343 are lost after retrieval, leaving 941 final false negatives. These numbers characterize that probe and are not promoted to a general claim about the system.

Several external authorities remain partially unavailable rather than negative. AutoResearchBench Deep official scoring has now executed through a local OpenAI-compatible adapter for the bounded keyless probe, with the judge control passing in full, but the matched multi-provider Deep comparison remains unexecuted. SAGE still lacks its published retrieval corpus and official evaluator. The separately archived OpenAIRE/Crossref Wide campaign failed its prospectively frozen provider-validity gate and remains undetermined; its observed zero paired effect is not a valid null or negative result. The exact-contract battery does not repair or supersede that external failure. Provider or credential unavailability is likewise kept undetermined, never converted into evidence of absence or a flattering zero-cost result.

The mechanism ablations themselves impose an interpretive limit. Removing the question coordinate from the read ledger changes legitimate reread behavior but does not change aggregate recall in this suite; removing the unavailable-route open state changes censoring/closure semantics while leaving aggregate recall numerically unchanged. These null-on-recall mechanism findings are retained rather than converted into a stronger claim.

All result-bearing statements in the abstract, Results and conclusion are mapped to immutable archived artifacts through the claim ledger, the current non-promotion ledger, and the exact-contract result and verification receipts; the artifact names and paths are listed in Data and code availability. Rows without an archived supporting result stay undetermined; an access audit, implementation test, or credential preflight cannot substitute for a system outcome.

\section{Conclusion}
Paper II advances a systems thesis broader than a contest between individual retrievers: acquisition quality and scientific-closure authority are different layers. The separate P2-X successor supplies stronger but still bounded evidence for the closure-authority layer itself. After donor-complete evidence coverage, decision/utility stopping, finite route certificates, deduplication and question-processing state are granted, P2-X obtains \textbf{400/400} exact closure decisions versus \textbf{250/400} for an available-route donor product, a paired advantage of \textbf{0.375} with 95\% CI [0.3275,0.4225], while preserving zero false task closures and perfect clean closure. The effect is the unresolved-route authority rule: all 150 B1 errors are material unavailable/censored/provider-invalid routes that B1 excludes from the closure denominator. An ideal typed product supplied the same semantics ties 400/400. Thus the contribution is not generic stopping, generic closure certificates, or inherent expressivity; it is bounded evidence that route-local acquisition stop and task-global scientific closure are distinct authority relations when material routes remain unresolved. Deployed multi-provider/retrieval-engine generality remains \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}.

The frozen controlled-index companion supports a narrow engineering result: under a complete denominator and matched host-owned budgets, the route/read/stopping governance mechanisms behave in the intended fail-closed directions, and the strongest confirmatory controlled comparator still leaves substantial reachable gold. The separate MetaSyn probe supplies real external retrieval/screening evidence but does not exercise or validate the complete multi-route ORION system. The matched external superiority claim remains unpromoted: the archived OpenAIRE/Crossref Wide campaign failed its prospectively frozen provider-validity gate, so its observed zero paired difference remains \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} rather than a valid null.
