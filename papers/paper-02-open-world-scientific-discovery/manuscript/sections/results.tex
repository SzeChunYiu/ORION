\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Authority of the result}
The only completed result-bearing campaign in this revision is the frozen
offline complete-gold companion.  It contains 20 tasks, 14 systems (full ORION,
six confirmatory baselines, one explicitly exploratory comparator, and six
ablations), and three predeclared repeat seeds, for 840 normalized run records.
The systems are deterministic; the repeats therefore test harness stability and
do not increase the statistical unit beyond 20 tasks.  The frozen statistical
plan requires at least 97 tasks for its lowest inferential tier.  Consequently
this campaign is \texttt{DESCRIPTIVE\_ONLY}: no confidence interval, $p$-value,
or superiority decision from the offline companion is promoted to a paper-level
claim.

The publication summary is
\texttt{evidence/offline\_results/RESULTS\_SUMMARY\_V1.json}.  It binds the full
840-record set by SHA-256
\texttt{611808dc80846d5057c84c12af7ff8ec3fa88ef15a6ede91807e590f4edb6f1f}
and the corresponding rich-artifact hash list by
\texttt{2da59dc21e6473e4a81eb93910501a67bed008dbc3e29c26116041b68dfbb325}.
The exact run manifest was committed before the first outcome snapshot.  The
mechanism projection used for Figures~\ref{fig:p2-3}--\ref{fig:p2-5} is archived
separately as \texttt{evidence/offline\_results/OFFLINE\_MECHANISMS\_V1.json};
it records the same two source digests and is regenerated from host-owned traces
rather than copied from manuscript numbers.

\subsection{Controlled-index comparison}
Full ORION attains mean complete-gold recall 0.994444 and precision 1.0.  It
passes 19 of 20 tasks and returns \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} on the remaining task
because a route holding unretrieved relevant material becomes unavailable; it
never certifies that censored task complete.  Its premature-task-closure rate is
0.  The strongest of the six protocol-frozen confirmatory baselines is the
protocol-driven systematic-review comparator, with mean recall 0.666667.  That
comparator claims task completion on all 20 tasks while reachable gold remains,
so its premature-closure rate is 1.0.  The descriptive recall difference is
therefore +0.327777, with a descriptive premature-closure difference of -1.0.
These numbers are evidence about the authored controlled world, not an estimate
of real-web superiority.

\subsection{Mechanism ablations}
The safety ablations fail in the directions the frozen design intended them to
probe.  Removing the route-independence check reduces mean recall to 0.444444
and produces premature closure on every task.  Allowing a route stop to certify
task closure yields recall 0.333333 with the same 100\% premature-closure rate.
Letting a coverage diagnostic acquire stopping authority yields recall 0.555556
and again closes every task prematurely.  These are negative controls: their
purpose is to demonstrate that the forbidden transitions are observable, not to
serve as competitive systems.

Removing content-identity deduplication is less catastrophic but measurably
wasteful in the controlled world: mean duplicate-processing rate rises from 0
to 0.101429, five of 20 tasks exhaust the read budget, pass rate falls from 0.95
to 0.70, and mean recall falls to 0.966667.  Removing the question coordinate
from the read ledger leaves aggregate recall unchanged at 0.994444 but changes
the extraction-question-shift mechanism exactly where expected: the mean count
of legitimate rereads on that family falls from 4.0 to 1.0.  This is a mechanism
result, not evidence of a recall gain from the ledger.  Finally, removing the
unavailable-route open state leaves mean recall numerically unchanged but turns
the one censored \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} case into an asserted completion and a
premature-closure failure.  The distinction is therefore one of epistemic
safety rather than headline recall.

Figure~\ref{fig:p2-5} exposes why ORION does not equate numerical disjointness
with route independence.  In particular, lexical and reformulation have zero
mean content overlap here yet are still rejected as independent because they
share a backend.  Conversely, lexical and semantic have nonzero mean overlap
(0.079167) but remain structurally independent because neither backend nor query
derivation is shared.  The independence decision is therefore made from capture
construction, while overlap remains a measured diagnostic.

\subsection{Failure analysis and external status}
Under the evaluator's fixed terminal-failure precedence, premature closure is
the dominant failure class in the controlled companion; the exploratory
adaptive comparator also exposes three transport-failure classifications, while
the no-dedup ablation exposes five budget-exhaustion failures.  Full ORION has no
terminal failure classification: 19 tasks pass and one is censored.  The full
per-system taxonomy is archived beside the publication summary as Table P2-3.

No external Wide, Deep, MetaSyn or SAGE performance result is reported in this
revision.  The access audit establishes that AutoResearchBench Wide scoring is
credential-free and that MetaSyn retrieval/screening scoring does not itself
need an LLM credential; it does not turn those audits into system outcomes.
AutoResearchBench Deep still needs an OpenAI-compatible judge for its official
metric, and SAGE lacks both the published retrieval corpus and an official
evaluator.  Therefore the externally supported discovery-benefit claim remains
\texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}.  The controlled-index result supports only the narrower
statement that the frozen governance mechanisms behave as designed under a
complete denominator and matched host-owned budgets.
