\section{Successor interface: structure-conditioned and source-grounded acquisition}
\label{sec:p2-structure-conditioned-interface}

The acquisition--closure separation also applies when a search route is generated from a structural need rather than from topical vocabulary.  A successor ORION discovery route may derive its query from a method signature, a failure signature, or a representation analogy.  This is an acquisition choice only.  The label ``structural'' does not make a route independent: backend identity, query-derivation identity, and capture identity must still earn independence under the same accounting used elsewhere in Paper II.

Returned material is retained as a candidate donor, not as a transfer certificate.  Before a structurally interesting candidate is even passed downstream, its declared assumptions, protected invariants, effects, and reconstruction obligations can expose an acquisition-local obstruction or an unresolved mapping.  Semantic distance is not evidence of novelty, and surface similarity is not evidence of valid transfer.  Paper III owns correspondence and obstruction analysis; the later authority layer owns scientific promotion.  Paper II therefore gives a structure-conditioned route no authority to certify transfer, claim novelty, or close the task.

The successor evidence layer also records historical discovery and failure episodes with source-bound assertions and chronology.  An episode distinguishes what a source documents from a later structural interpretation and from what cannot be inferred from the source.  Pre-cutoff visibility, later reinterpretation, missing provenance, and source-level uncertainty remain explicit.  These records can support mechanism extraction, candidate generation, morphology studies, or benchmark design, but curation does not turn historical examples into protected confirmatory gold.

Route-local stopping remains local under this extension.  Exhaustion, censorship, provider failure, budget stopping, or a temporarily empty structural route does not establish that no useful method exists.  Such a route remains an open or revisitable acquisition obligation whenever a new representation, source family, or upstream structural object could materially change the search.  The successor interface therefore extends the ways Paper II can acquire candidates without weakening its central rule: acquisition evidence does not acquire scientific-closure authority merely because it was generated from a richer representation.
