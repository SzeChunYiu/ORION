\section{Successor interface: structure-conditioned and source-grounded acquisition}
\label{sec:p2-structure-conditioned-interface}

The acquisition--closure separation also applies when a search route is generated from a structural need rather than topical vocabulary. A successor discovery route may derive its query from a method signature, failure signature, or representation analogy. This is an acquisition choice only: backend identity, query-derivation identity, and capture identity must still earn independence, while semantic distance is not evidence of novelty and surface similarity is not evidence of valid transfer.

The successor evidence layer records historical discovery and failure episodes with source-bound assertions and chronology. An episode distinguishes what a source documents from a later structural interpretation and from what cannot be inferred. Pre-cutoff visibility, later reinterpretation, missing provenance, and source uncertainty remain explicit. These records may support mechanism extraction, candidate generation, morphology studies, or benchmark design, but curation never turns historical examples into protected confirmatory gold.

The completed atlas work makes that limitation concrete. The discovery morphology study retained 51 source-grounded episodes across formal mathematics, empirical science, engineering/design, and everyday practical invention, including fixed-regime no-Jump controls; it yielded a bounded descriptive morphology rather than a universal creativity basis. The complementary failure atlas stabilized its pilot schema on 30 deeply sourced cases across five domains, but deliberately did not claim corpus readiness because independent domain-expert agreement was not obtained. Paper II therefore treats these corpora as structured acquisition/design evidence and provenance-bearing negative history, not as a shortcut to scientific validation.

Route-local stopping remains local under this extension. Exhaustion, censorship, provider failure, budget stopping, or a temporarily empty structural route does not establish that no useful method exists. A new representation, source family, or upstream structural object can reopen acquisition obligations without granting task-global closure.
