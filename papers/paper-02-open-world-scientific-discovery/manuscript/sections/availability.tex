\section{Data and code availability}
\label{sec:availability}

All code implementing the systems, the controlled index, the evaluation harness,
the analysis pipeline and every figure generator is in the ORION repository, at
the exact subject revision bound in the run manifest. The frozen offline
complete-gold world is distributed with the paper: it is generated by us, is
regenerable from its recorded seed, and reproduces its committed content hash on
a clean checkout, so a reader can reconstruct the denominator rather than trust
it.

External benchmark artifacts are not redistributed. Their licences, access
states and pinned revisions are recorded per artifact in
\texttt{protocol/EXTERNAL\_ACCESS\_AUDIT\_V1.json}, with the fetch attempts that
established each state retained under \texttt{evidence/access/}, and are
summarised in Table~P2-1. Where an artifact could not be obtained, the record
carries the verbatim attempt log rather than an assertion of absence. The
AutoResearchBench Wide evaluator is vendored verbatim under its Apache-2.0
licence with attribution, so the official metric travels with the reproduction
package even though the benchmark data does not.

Live-provider evidence is archived rather than re-derived: every request, raw
response, timestamp, transport failure and query derivation from the frozen
campaign is retained, because live results are mutable and a rerun months later
answers a different question. Provider identities, endpoint versions and the
rate policy in force are bound in the campaign manifest.

\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:reproducibility}

Three levels are distinguished, and we claim only what each supports.

\textbf{Fully reproducible.} The offline controlled-index results regenerate
from the seed and the subject revision with no network access and no
credentials, in a clean environment with the standard library alone. This
includes every figure and table derived from them: result-bearing artifacts are
generated from archived records by committed scripts, never transcribed by hand,
and the tests read the same specification the generators do so a figure cannot
drift from the state machine or the summary it depicts.

\textbf{Reproducible given the benchmark.} The external Wide results regenerate
for anyone who obtains the benchmark bundle under its own licence, because the
scoring function is deterministic and vendored. One upstream metric family is
sampled without a seed and is therefore not reproducible even upstream; it is
labelled noisy and excluded from primary claims.

\textbf{Archived, not reproducible.} Live-provider outcomes depend on services
that change, meter and occasionally disappear --- one backend used during design
became paid mid-programme, and another returned rate-limit refusals to
unauthenticated callers. Those results are evidence about a moment, and the
archive, not a rerun, is what a reader should audit. We report the cost and
wall-clock of the frozen campaign so its scale is auditable, and we do not
present a live number as though a reader could obtain it again by running the
same command.
