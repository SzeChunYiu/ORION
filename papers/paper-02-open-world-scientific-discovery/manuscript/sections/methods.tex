\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

This section is written before the final runs and is fixed at execution freeze.
Every quantity named here is bound in a machine-readable artifact rather than in
prose: the design freeze in \texttt{protocol/PROTOCOL\_V1.json}, the measurement
and statistical rules in \texttt{protocol/MEASUREMENT\_PLAN\_V1.md} and
\texttt{protocol/STATISTICAL\_PLAN\_V1.json}, the external-artifact states in
\texttt{protocol/EXTERNAL\_ACCESS\_AUDIT\_V1.json}, and the frozen offline world
in \texttt{protocol/OFFLINE\_GOLD\_WORLD\_V1.md}. Where this prose and those
artifacts could ever disagree, the artifacts govern.

\subsection{Prospective discipline}
The protocol is registered as \texttt{P2.open-world-discovery.v1}. It moves from
\texttt{DESIGN\_FROZEN} to \texttt{EXECUTION\_FROZEN} only when every execution
identity is bound --- subject revision, dataset and split hashes, provider and
model revisions, baseline configuration hashes, evaluator hash and evaluation
epoch --- and that transition is committed before any final outcome is
inspected. Implementation of the systems under test, of the analysis pipeline
and of every figure generator was completed while the protocol was still
outcome-blind, so no constant in this paper was chosen by looking at a result.
A change to any hypothesis, task family, baseline, ablation, metric, exclusion
rule, statistical rule, margin or evaluator after outcome access creates a new
protocol version and leaves this one immutable.

\subsection{Evaluation environments}
Three environments are used because no single one supplies both a complete
denominator and open-world realism.

\paragraph{Offline complete-gold controlled index.}
Recall is only a measurable quantity where the relevant set can be enumerated.
On the open web it cannot: ``we found forty'' is indistinguishable from ``forty
of forty-one'' and from ``forty of four hundred''. The controlled index is
therefore synthetic and self-generated. Each topic declares required concepts
and a document is relevant if and only if it carries all of them, so the gold
set is a materialised cache of a rule applied to the corpus rather than a
hand-authored list; the test suite recomputes it over every document in both
directions instead of spot-checking. Discoverability is deliberately
heterogeneous: some relevant works are reachable only lexically, some only
through a paraphrase whose keys share no token with the lexical keys, some only
by citation chaining from a document the system already holds, some only after
reformulation, and some sit behind a provider that dies mid-run. No single route
reaches the whole gold set at any budget. Two routes share one backend with
different query derivations --- the nominally-distinct-but-dependent pair that
H2 needs --- while others hit distinct backends and can earn independence.
Identity is separated three ways: a work, a republication that shares content
identity at a different locator, and a revision that shares identity with a
changed digest, so that counting locators inflates coverage while rereading a
revision is legitimate. Case families exercise multi-route coverage, route
exhaustion, an unavailable route, an extraction-question shift and duplicate
identity under a tightened read budget. Budgets are set below the cost of
exhaustive probing, so allocation and stopping are what vary. The suite is
frozen by content hash and regenerable from its seed; its task count, hashes and
the achieved precision tier are reported in Table~P2-1.

\paragraph{External benchmark tasks.}
AutoResearchBench supplies specific-target (Deep) and open-set (Wide) discovery
tasks \cite{autoresearchbench2026}. Its Wide evaluator scores set
intersection-over-union over normalised arXiv identifiers and is fully
deterministic, requiring no credentials; our systems are scored by that official
function, vendored verbatim under its Apache-2.0 licence so the comparison is to
the published metric rather than to a reimplementation. One upstream component,
the sampled \texttt{max\_iou\_at\_k} family, draws without a seed and is
therefore not reproducible from a pinned code hash; it is reported only as a
declared-noisy secondary, and the exact quantities (mean IoU, recall, precision)
carry the primary. Deep scoring in its official form uses a language-model judge
for title matching; where no judge is available we report the deterministic
identifier-match variant and label it as a declared deviation rather than as the
official metric. SAGE supplies twelve hundred expert-curated retrieval queries with ground truth:
six hundred short-form tasks that name one target paper, and six hundred
open-ended tasks with a two-tier graded gold set \cite{sage2026}. We run its
queries but cannot reproduce its setting, and the distinction matters in three
ways that are carried explicitly rather than smoothed over. Its two-hundred
thousand paper retrieval corpus is not published, so the tasks are run
open-world rather than against the authors' index. No evaluator ships with the
benchmark and the open-ended tier weights are not stated, so short-form exact
match is computed on a declared title normalisation and the open-ended weighted
recall uses weights we declare; neither is presented as the official metric. Its
gold set is public plaintext, so search-time contamination is measured rather
than assumed away. MetaSyn's separation of retrieval recall from eligibility
recall is adopted as a measurement requirement whether or not its judged report
metrics can be executed \cite{metasyn2026}.

\paragraph{Frozen retrieval snapshots.}
Running every system live over hundreds of benchmark queries is impossible at
polite request rates, and it would make the comparison irreproducible even if it
were affordable. External benchmark tasks are therefore executed in two passes.
In the first, a candidate pool is fetched once per query from the live
backends, frozen by content hash, and every system is then run offline against
that same pool under matched budgets. The pool is built **gold-blind**, from
query-derived probes only: a pool assembled with knowledge of the answer would
inflate every recall figure and invalidate the comparison. Because it is
gold-blind, some gold items are absent from the pool; that fraction is measured
and reported as pool coverage, and it is the shared ceiling for every system
rather than a per-system disadvantage. In the second pass a smaller
pre-declared subsample is run live, end to end, as evidence about open-world
behaviour.

\paragraph{Live-provider campaign.}
Open-world behaviour is measured against real scholarly backends. Every request
and raw response is retained with its timestamp, and provider identities and
query derivations are frozen in a campaign manifest. Transport failure, empty
result, rate-limit deferral and backend exhaustion are distinct recorded
outcomes; none is converted into evidence that nothing further exists. Metrics
that require a complete denominator are structurally refused in this
environment: the analysis code raises rather than reporting a recall figure
whose denominator does not exist.

\subsection{Systems under test}
Full ORION is compared against six baselines --- lexical BM25 retrieval, a
distributional retriever, their hybrid, one-pass retrieval-augmented retrieval,
an agentic single-route searcher, and a protocol-driven systematic-review
imitation --- and against six ablations of itself, each removing exactly one
mechanism: the route-independence check, the question-conditioned read ledger,
the separation of route stop from task closure, the unavailable-route open
state, content-identity deduplication, and a negative safety ablation that lets
the coverage diagnostic influence stopping. Ablations share all other code with
full ORION through one parameterised implementation, so an ablation cannot
differ in two ways at once. The distributional retriever is lexical, not neural;
we name it as such because the object under test is route governance, not
embedding quality. Every system consumes budget only through a host-owned
session that meters and records each call, so a system cannot win by spending
more, and budget exhaustion closes the session permanently rather than
degrading it.

\subsection{Custody}
A system receives only a public view of the task and a session over a label-free
index. Nothing it holds carries the relevance rule or the gold set; a hostile
fixture in the test suite searches for both and finds neither, and every
mutation it attempts is refused. Route calls, reads and stop decisions are
recorded host-side as they occur, so a system cannot under-report a route or
edit its own trace, and a claim about material that was never retrieved is
recorded as invalid rather than credited.

\subsection{Measurements}
Metric definitions, their statistical units and --- critically --- their
denominator-validity conditions are fixed per metric in the measurement plan.
Paper-level recall and false-negative counts are reported only where the gold
denominator is complete, signalled per task by an explicit completeness flag;
where it is not, those metrics are emitted as invalid rather than as numbers.
Route-stop false positives and negatives, and premature task closure, are
defined against an oracle the world computes and the candidate cannot see: the
still-reachable relevant count on a route at the moment it stopped, and the
count still discoverable within remaining budget at the moment closure was
declared. Duplicate processing is separated from legitimate reread by the four
read coordinates of Section~\ref{sec:formalism}. Route overlap, per-route unique
contribution and marginal gain are computed over content digests after alias
closure, never over retrieval identifiers.

\subsection{Statistics}
The statistical unit is the task; repeated stochastic runs nest within
task and system and never count as additional tasks. Standalone binary rates
carry Wilson intervals; paired differences use a paired percentile bootstrap
with ten thousand resamples and a recorded seed, and every comparison reports a
point estimate, an interval and an absolute effect size. The primary hypothesis
is tested once in its frozen direction against a superiority margin of $0.03$
absolute on the family primary, with a safety guard requiring premature task
closure not to worsen by more than $0.02$, so that a recall gain cannot be
purchased with a larger closure regression than the gain itself.
Non-inferiority, where the value proposition is coverage safety at bounded cost,
uses a tighter margin of $0.01$ and is inadmissible outside matched budgets or
above a consumed-cost ratio of $1.25$. Secondary families use Holm correction;
exploratory metrics remain exploratory and cannot be promoted after the fact.
Sample size is planned with a conservative proportion half-width at
$p = 0.5$, which also bounds the variance of the $[0,1]$-valued IoU and recall
measures; a family whose achieved half-width exceeds the superiority margin is
reported as underpowered with that label attached to its primary result.

\subsection{Failures, exclusions and contamination}
Candidate-caused failures, malformed outputs, timeouts and abstentions are
retained as outcomes. Only infrastructure outages may be excluded, under a rule
declared in advance that applies identically to every system, and every
exclusion remains visible in the archive. Because the external benchmark's
questions and gold identifiers are public, live routes are audited for
search-time exposure to the benchmark's own artifacts, and any contaminated case
is reported separately rather than allowed to support the headline claim.

\subsection{Decision rule}
The rule is fixed before outcomes exist. Promotion requires the frozen
superiority margin to be cleared on a family primary under matched budgets with
the safety guard intact. Where superiority is absent but coverage safety at
bounded cost is demonstrated, the non-inferiority rule applies. Where neither
holds, the declared outcome is to shrink or reframe the paper's claim, not to
present a null as a success; an underpowered family is reported as such rather
than resolved by a favourable reading.
