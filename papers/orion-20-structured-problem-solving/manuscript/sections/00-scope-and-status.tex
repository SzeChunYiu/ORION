\section*{Scope and status}

\paragraph{What this paper establishes.}
A formal treatment of obstruction-certified method-language expansion
(OCME) and the exact input contract required to measure it: the closure
object $Cl(L)$ and the sense in which a target outside it is unreachable by
any procedure whose output range is that closure; the definition of a
certified expansion and its macro-rejection lemma; a definition of
primitive minimality relative to a registered complexity order; and a
content-bound specification of the donor, evaluator, task and verifier
inputs a comparative study must supply. Two exact finite method-language
studies, reported separately in the
\breakablecode{top\codebreak tier} receipts, instantiate the
obstruction/outside-closure/transfer gate and show it is non-vacuous.

\paragraph{What this paper does not establish.}
No verified problem-solving superiority, no search-efficiency gain, no
obstruction-diagnosis validity, no false-expansion rate, no donor-frontier
strict extension, no cross-domain transfer, and no autonomous method
invention. The donor-complete empirical programme specified in
the sections below is a separate successor
study. Its frozen execution
(\breakablecode{P10\codebreak DES\codebreak 01}) returned
\textsc{P10\codebreak FULL\codebreak FROZEN\codebreak DONOR\codebreak EVALUATOR\codebreak INPUTS\codebreak ABSENT}: of $12{,}960$
planned run cells across $480$ protected cases, four domains, nine arms and
three seeds, \textbf{zero were executed} and all $480$ case rows retained
\textsc{CannotCheck}. All six hypotheses H1--H6 remain
\textsc{Prospective\codebreak Not\codebreak Executed}. That is neither a negative result nor
authorisation for a positive one.

\paragraph{Preserved adverse findings.}
The prior native-Lean attempt is retained as
\textsc{CannotCheck\codebreak Native\codebreak State\codebreak Coverage}: $457$ files were traced and
$11{,}842$ candidate transitions generated, but \emph{zero} satisfied the
prospectively frozen eligibility contract. This is a coverage failure, not

be removed by relaxing the contract after seeing the outcome. Historical

is permitted, are stated in the section on immutable negative and donor-closed history and are not
relabelled.

\paragraph{Limits of the integrity record.}
The two finite studies are exact and independently replayed, but their
held-out transfer and false-expansion endpoints are entailed by their own
construction rather than measured, and their candidate grammars and
composition templates were supplied prospectively by the experiment. An
adversarial audit of the formal objects, including gaps this paper has not
closed, is recorded in

Hashes and deterministic replays establish that a stated computation was
performed and can be performed again; they establish nothing about whether
its conclusion is correct.
