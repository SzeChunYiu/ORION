# Primitive sufficiency and minimality — ORION20.PRIMITIVE_SUFFICIENCY_MINIMALITY.v1

Top-tier promotion target from issue #1649. Frozen before outcome access.
`scientific_authority_delta: NONE`.

## The instruction, and the trap in it

#1649 asks for: exact closure membership decidability in the stated finite setting; a
precise strength/minimality order; minimal primitive bases with witnesses of indispensable
primitives; comparison only under identical information. It also says a tie found by
exhaustive enumeration is acceptable, that the positive contribution must be structural
minimality and certification rather than manufactured search superiority, and — decisively —

> If this theorem [is] not materially stronger [than the] narrowed formal paper, stop
> promotion ... do not waste effort enlarging formula size/operators over an unchanged
> vocabulary.

The trap is that this setting is small and classical. Establishing the facts is easy;
establishing that they are **not already owned by a donor** is the whole question. This
packet therefore treats donor subtraction as a **gate that can fail the promotion**, not as
a citation paragraph written afterwards.

## The frozen setting, exactly as committed

`run_generated_ocme_v1.py` fixes:

- binary Boolean primitives as truth-table codes `0..15`;
- the affine binary codes, `{c ⊕ ax ⊕ by}`, of which there are exactly 8;
- the template `T_p(x_i,x_j,x_k) = p(x_i,x_j) ⊕ p(x_i,x_k) ⊕ p(x_j,x_k)`;
- the protected target `majority3`;
- selection admitting a primitive `p` iff `p` is **outside** the affine set and `T_p` equals
  the target exactly, minimised over the registered complexity order `popcount(code)`.

## Definition M1 (membership order)

For primitive sets `S, S'` write `S ≼ S'` when `Cl(S) ⊆ Cl(S')`. The **registered
complexity order** on single primitives is `popcount` of the truth table, as committed in
the runner.

## Theorem M2 (exact decidability)

Membership of a binary primitive in the affine class is decidable exactly, in constant
time per primitive: `p` is affine iff its algebraic normal form has degree at most 1,
equivalently iff `p(x,y) = c ⊕ ax ⊕ by` for some `c,a,b ∈ {0,1}`. The class has exactly 8
of the 16 binary codes.

*Proof.* The map `(c,a,b) ↦ code` is injective into the 16 codes and its image has size 8;
ANF degree is computed by Möbius inversion over a 4-point domain. ∎

## Definition M3 (sufficiency and minimal bases)

A primitive set `S` is **sufficient** when the protected target lies in the closure
generated by the frozen basis together with `S`, as witnessed by the committed template.
`S` is a **minimal basis** when it is sufficient and no proper subset is. A primitive is
**indispensable** when it belongs to every minimal basis.

## What is computed

Exhaustively over all 16 codes and all subsets: the sufficient sets, the minimal bases, the
indispensable primitives, and the minimum under the registered complexity order — together
with whether that minimum is **unique** or a tie.

Uniqueness matters more than the value. A unique minimum is a certification statement: the
expansion the system chose is *the* minimal one, not *a* minimal one.

## The donor, named before the result

Boolean clone theory is the donor and it is **Post's lattice** (E. L. Post, 1941), together
with the standard facts that the affine functions form a clone, that ANF degree decides
affineness, and that closure properties of Boolean function classes are fully classified.

The subtraction question, fixed here before any computation:

> After granting Post's lattice and standard ANF decidability, does the minimal-basis and
> indispensability result state anything a reader holding that donor does not already have?

Two honest possibilities, and the protocol enumerates both as terminals:

- **Entailed.** If the content reduces to "majority3 is not affine, so a non-affine
  primitive is required, and here is which of 16 codes happens to fit a fixed template",
  then it is a finite instance of classical clone theory. The promotion **fails** and the
  paper returns to the bounded formal lane. #1649 mandates that outcome explicitly.
- **Not entailed.** If minimality and indispensability under the *registered complexity
  order*, tied to the OCME expansion contract, yield a certification statement that clone
  theory does not supply — that an expansion is provably the minimal admissible one — the
  promotion survives its first gate.

The second is a genuinely higher bar than it sounds, because clone theory already answers
sufficiency. Only the **certification of minimality under a registered order** is a
candidate delta.

## What would refute the promotion

The donor-subtraction gate failing, the minimum not being unique, or no indispensable
primitive existing. Any of those returns ORION-20 to the bounded lane without a second
rescue attempt, as #1649 requires.
