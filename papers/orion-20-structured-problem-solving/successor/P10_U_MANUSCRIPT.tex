\begin{abstract}
Structured proof state, premise retrieval, proof repair, theorem proving, program synthesis, evolutionary code search, tactic learning, and tool-using language models can all improve difficult reasoning. ORION Paper X therefore targets a claim strictly above those donor mechanisms: across formal mathematics, algorithms/programs, scientific problem solving, and other verifier-backed domains, can a structured epistemic workspace improve verified solving under matched information and total compute, and, only when a prospectively frozen method basis is independently shown to be inadequate under the declared access/resource model, can ORION expand the method language with a minimal semantics-preserving edit that increases protected held-out reach without false escalation? Existing negative P9/P10 and ORION-Q results remain immutable, including regimes where exhaustive or donor-complete search already closes the registered space. P10-U gives first right of refusal to native proof-state systems, proof repair, failure-triggered learned interventions, tactic/macro/library mining, exact search, program synthesis, AlphaEvolve/CodeEvolve-style evolutionary method search, and P9 representation repairs. Method-language expansion is inaccessible until an Obstruction-Certified Method-Language Expansion (OCME) gate rules out representation, search, implementation, environment, library, and donor-search explanations under the same frozen model. A timeout is never an obstruction certificate, and a new tactic name or macro that expands to old closure is never method-space expansion. Primary outcomes include verified solve rate, verifier calls, branching/search depth, time-to-first-correct, invalid-action rate, repair success, obstruction-diagnosis accuracy, false expansion, outside-closure verification, held-out transfer, semantic preservation, and total resource cost. The intended terminal is replicated verified problem-solving superiority plus at least one independently verified outside-closure method-language expansion that transfers and remains unavailable to stronger search, synthesis, library learning, and evolutionary baselines at matched resources.
\end{abstract}

\section{Largest scientific question}
P10-U asks two nested questions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Does a structured epistemic workspace improve \emph{verified} problem solving under matched semantic information and total compute across heterogeneous domains?
    \item When the frozen registered method language is genuinely inadequate, can ORION establish that inadequacy independently and then expand the language with a minimal semantics-preserving operator/grammar/interface edit that improves held-out verified reach?
\end{enumerate}
The second question is not entered merely because the first system failed. Existing search, representation, proof-repair, synthesis, and evolutionary methods receive first right of refusal.

\section{Nearest-work pressure moves the claim upward}
Current systems already own much of the obvious method-search space. \section{Stage C: OCME obstruction certificate}
Method-language expansion is inaccessible until all of the following are frozen and independently checked:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item exact pre-freeze method basis and closure/composition operation;
    \item exact computational, information, tool, and access model;
    \item target task/family and verifier;
    \item search/compute budget;
    \item representation/interface repair routes from P9;
    \item implementation/environment repair routes;
    \item premise/library retrieval and macro-mining routes;
    \item exact/heuristic theorem-proving baselines;
    \item program-synthesis baselines;
    \item evolutionary method/program-search baselines;
    \item a bounded obstruction witness showing that the target remains outside registered reach under the declared model.
\end{enumerate}
A timeout, low probability, failed proof attempt, or missing tactic prediction is not an obstruction certificate.

Valid obstruction evidence may be an exact finite non-reachability proof, a machine-checked lower bound, exhaustive bounded non-reachability, or an independently verified resource-bounded obstruction under a predeclared search class. If the registered access model is incomplete, the correct terminal is \textsc{CannotCheck}, not invention.

\section{Stage D: method-language jump}
Only an OCME-certified obstruction may open a method-language jump. Candidate edits may include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item a genuinely new semantic operator;
    \item a new composition law unavailable in the old closure;
    \item a new grammar/primitive that changes reachable verified solutions;
    \item a representation-regime edit only when it changes method semantics/reach and carries an explicit correspondence;
    \item a new verification/selection primitive that changes the reachable verified solution set.
\end{enumerate}
A tactic alias or macro that expands into an existing old-language composition is classified as \textsc{KnownComposition}, not expansion.

\section{Outside-closure and semantics verification}
A claimed method-space expansion must satisfy all of:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item an independent checker verifies that the candidate edit lies outside the frozen old closure;
    \item the semantic target and verifier remain fixed, or an explicit valid correspondence is established;
    \item no stronger oracle/tool/access channel is silently introduced;
    \item the edit closes or materially moves the certified obstruction;
    \item held-out tasks/families were frozen before edit generation and show positive transfer;
    \item known-method controls show a low false-expansion rate;
    \item strong search, synthesis, library-learning, and evolutionary baselines do not recover the same reach at matched cost;
    \item an independent implementation/replay reproduces the reach expansion.
\end{enumerate}
The generated method cannot certify its own novelty, correctness, or adoption authority.

\section{Primary hypotheses}
\paragraph{H1, verified problem-solving superiority.}
Across heterogeneous verifier-backed tasks, ORION achieves higher verified solve/success utility than the strongest runnable donor-complete comparator under matched information and total compute.

\paragraph{H2, search-efficiency superiority.}
ORION reduces verifier calls, search depth, branching, or time-to-first-correct at matched verified quality.

\paragraph{H3, obstruction-diagnosis validity.}
ORION distinguishes true method-language inadequacy from representation, search, implementation, environment, library, and verifier failures with lower false escalation than donor-complete diagnosis systems.

\paragraph{H4, outside-closure method-language expansion.}
At least one protected certified obstruction is closed by a minimal edit independently verified outside the old closure, with positive held-out transfer and no hidden access-model widening.

\paragraph{H5, low false expansion.}
On known-method controls, ORION rarely declares a language jump necessary when the registered basis suffices.

\paragraph{H6, cross-domain transfer.}
The problem-solving and OCME mechanism transfers prospectively to multiple domains without target-specific refitting.

\section{Adversarial controls}
Mandatory attacks include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item search timeout mislabeled as method-language inadequacy;
    \item generated edit merely renames/compresses known composition;
    \item compiler/verifier feedback leaks the target action;
    \item stronger oracle/tool/access model is silently introduced;
    \item edit wins only on originating instances;
    \item method invention fires when an existing method suffices;
    \item native state improves prediction but not verified solve/search;
    \item larger search budget closes the claimed ORION advantage;
    \item representation change alone closes the obstruction;
    \item proof-repair or learned-intervention baseline closes the obstruction;
    \item donor-complete program synthesis or evolutionary search finds the same edit at matched cost.
\end{enumerate}
