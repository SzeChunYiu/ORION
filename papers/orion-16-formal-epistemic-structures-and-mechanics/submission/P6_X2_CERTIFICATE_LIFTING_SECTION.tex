\section{Post-saturation successor: certificate lifting over donor-complete execution and workflow certificates}

Modern governed-agent and scientific-workflow systems already provide strong certificate machinery: runtime execution attestation, certified traces, portable action/approval receipts, replay evidence, workflow provenance/reproducibility signatures, and attested executor boundaries. We treat these as donor certificates rather than as P6 contributions. P6 asks how such certificates can be \emph{reused} when the scientific state changes.

Let $d$ be a donor certificate with native validity predicate $D(d)$, and let
\[
s=(c,m,e,i,t)
\]
record, in the bounded successor instance, exact claim/content binding, measurement semantics, evidence semantics, inferential obligation and scientific epoch. Define
\[
\operatorname{Lift}(d,s)=D(d)\wedge c\wedge m\wedge e\wedge i\wedge t.
\]
The five coordinates are a registered theorem instance rather than a claim of a universal scientific ontology. If a donor system already exposes an equivalent coordinate and bridge rule, that information belongs to the donor side of the comparison.

\paragraph{Donor conservativity.}
Projection from the lifted semantics to the donor object preserves $D(d)$. A valid proof-of-execution, action, workflow or attestation certificate is not made invalid merely to create a scientific distinction.

\paragraph{Lifting separation.}
For each registered donor-certificate family and each non-inert scientific lift coordinate, two extensions of the same valid donor certificate can differ only on that coordinate and have different scientific-lifting judgments. Thus a material scientific change may invalidate the old scientific standing while leaving execution, replay, provenance or attestation valid.

\paragraph{Certificate-product non-laundering.}
A product of native-valid donor certificates cannot infer an absent scientific lift coordinate without an explicit bridge rule. Accumulating execution and workflow evidence is useful, but it is not by itself a proof that a claim-specific measurement or inference obligation remains discharged.

\paragraph{Exact selective revalidation.}
If a transition changes a nonempty set $S$ of load-bearing scientific lift coordinates while the donor certificate remains native-valid, revalidating all coordinates in $S$ restores the lift when all unchanged coordinates remain valid. Revalidating a proper subset of $S$ does not. This turns the earlier erasure result into a positive preservation rule: retain unaffected lower-level certificates and revalidate the affected scientific bridge rather than rebuilding every certificate.

\paragraph{Ideal-product equivalence.}
An information-equivalent donor product carrying the same scientific fields and lift predicate agrees extensionally with P6. The contribution is therefore the reusable scientific lifting interface and selective-revalidation semantics, not centralized expressive power.

\paragraph{Exhaustive bounded support.}
The registered model spans five donor-certificate families and five scientific lift coordinates. The checker evaluates 320 states with zero donor-conservativity violations and zero ideal-product mismatches. It contains 25 minimal one-coordinate separation witnesses, 31 donor-product non-laundering countermodels, 155 exact full-revalidation successes, and 1,055 failures of proper-subset revalidation. An independent implementation reproduces the canonical row digest \texttt{e1e3c48bcefea3750d952c6b0ff37ac660a2e21f9823fdfdeb50bb62e819ff93}. Those counts carry a multiplicity and are reported with it: \texttt{liftable} takes no donor argument, so the donor loop repeats every count under it --- 320 is 64 counted five times, 25 is 5 counted five times, and 155 and 1,055 are 31 and 211 counted five times. Only the 31 product countermodels are 31 distinct facts.

The successor therefore widens P6 constructively: mature certificate systems are absorbed and preserved, while scientific standing is carried by an explicit lift that can fail and be selectively repaired after change. The result remains formal and bounded; it is not deployed-agent superiority and it does not claim universal minimality of the five registered coordinates.
