## Iterated revision — the nearest parent, and absent

This is the consequential omission. `A6_DONOR_MATRIX_V5` records it plainly: the
corpus contained `AGM` in six documents but **zero** occurrences of
`iterated revision`, `entrenchment`, `ranking function`, `Spohn`, `Darwiche` or
`Pearl`. The bibliography inherits that shape.

- **Gärdenfors & Makinson**, *Revisions of Knowledge Systems Using Epistemic
  Entrenchment* (1988). Entrenchment as the structure deciding what survives a
  revision.
- **Spohn**, *Ordinal Conditional Functions* (1988). A graded, iterable strength.
- **Darwiche & Pearl**, *On the Logic of Iterated Belief Revision*, Artificial
  Intelligence, DOI 10.1016/S0004-3702(96)00038-0 (1997). The move from
  knowledge bases to **epistemic states**, where the state carries everything
  needed for coherent reasoning *including the revision strategy itself*,
  precisely so revision can be iterated coherently.
- **Lehmann** (1995), who identified the inconsistency that motivated the move;
  **Booth & Chandler** on the DP postulates and the Independence principle.

"The object being revised must carry its own revision machinery, not just its
beliefs" is Darwiche & Pearl, following Lehmann. That is not adjacent to *Formal
Epistemic Structures and Mechanics*; **it is the same move.** §3's
residual-obligation preservation theorem and its recursion and fixed-point claims
must be stated as satisfying, violating, or refining the DP postulates. One
mitigating fact worth keeping in view: those postulates are contested, so a
considered disagreement with them is a recognised position rather than a defect.

## Temporal logic and process calculi

Separation logic and typed effects were already dispositioned in
`A6_DONOR_SUBTRACTION_V1` / `_COMPLETION_V1`; the temporal and process halves of
the same §2 item were not.

- **Pnueli**, *The Temporal Logic of Programs*, FOCS (1977). Properties over
  time — safety, liveness, until — rather than at a point.
- **Milner**, *A Calculus of Communicating Systems* (1980), and the π-calculus.
  Compositional descriptions of interacting processes with an equivalence theory.

Any ORION-16 claim of the form "this property is preserved along every
trajectory" is a temporal-logic claim and should be written as one.

## Cognitive architectures and language-agent formalisms

- **Laird, Newell & Rosenbloom**, *SOAR: An Architecture for General
  Intelligence*, Artificial Intelligence (1987); **Anderson**, ACT-R;
  **Rao & Georgeff**, *BDI Agents: From Theory to Practice* (1995). Decomposition
  into typed memory and a decision cycle, with belief/desire/intention as typed
  mental state.
- **Sumers, Yao, Narasimhan & Griffiths**, *Cognitive Architectures for Language
  Agents* (CoALA), arXiv:2309.02427 (2023), TMLR 2024. The same decomposition
  applied to LLM-based agents.

One asymmetry is worth stating in the manuscript rather than leaving implicit:
**CoALA is explicitly a taxonomy, not an implementation or a formal semantics.**
The language-agent literature therefore does not own a *formal* mechanics, which
is where ORION-16's residual must be located — and it is a narrow opening that
should be claimed narrowly.
