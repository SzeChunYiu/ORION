# Five-paper editor/reviewer synthesis

Date: 2026-08-24  
Method: recursive application of `academic-paper-skills` editor/reviewer decision engine, explanatory-sufficiency contract, manuscript-content selection, literature audit and figure contracts. Reviewer lenses below are independent analytical lenses in one execution context, **not mutually blind human peer review**.

## Decision model

For each paper the sequence is:

`integrity/evidence boundary -> editorial triage -> validity review -> positioning review -> reproducibility/boundary review -> editor synthesis -> revision closure`.

A paper is not called `TOP_TIER_READY` merely because all mathematical statements in its current scope are correct. Target-specific significance/advance and unresolved donor or evidence gates remain separate.

- `A-E1` editorial significance: **TARGET-DEPENDENT**. Strong for `Quantum`; PRX Quantum requires external judgment that the exact compiler/proof-language connection constitutes an exceptional and broadly consequential insight.

**Editor synthesis:** `STRONG_SPECIALIST_SUBMISSION_CANDIDATE`; not internally labelled PRX-ready.

## Editor brief

**Question:** has the step-five exact tail begun, or is `D_4` the final defect?  
**Answer proved so far:** all later constants lie in a width-one corridor, and `D_4=30` would force the exact lower line forever; saturation gives a strong local defect grammar.  
**Decisive missing evidence:** exact `D_4`, `31 in C_0(C_5^3)`, an upper-line extremal, or a reusable global support-obstruction theorem.  
**Boundary:** support-at-least-23 is currently a bounded computational frontier whose own receipt says theorem authority is false.

## Reviewer lenses

- `N-P1` positioning: **PASS WITH DONOR SUBTRACTION**. Freeze–Schmid owns the recurrence/lower framework; `C_0` localization and Property C are donors.

- `N-E1` editorial significance: **FATAL-CURRENT-TOP-TIER-TARGET**. JCTA/Combinatorica-level framing is not earned while exact `D_4` or a comparable conceptual obstruction remains open.

**Resolution test:** one of (i) prove `31 in C_0(C_5^3)`, (ii) construct a valid `D_4=31` extremal, or (iii) prove a reusable symbolic contradiction for the saturation-defect grammar that substantially advances the open problem. One additional bounded support row does not close the concern.

# Cross-paper publication decision

The five manuscripts are scientifically separable and should remain five identities. A and B may cite each other for the normal-form/certificate distinction; C supplies a second independent information-sufficiency family for D; none of those dependencies transfers theorem ownership.

Under the wave's pre-registered release rule, **integration to `main` is blocked** because all five papers have not independently passed the top-tier-ready gate. The correct repository action is therefore a reviewable **draft PR**, not a merge. This is a successful gate, not a workflow failure: the publication process is preventing an unresolved non-quantum theorem from being hidden behind four stronger manuscripts.
