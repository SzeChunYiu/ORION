\subsection{What produces the remaining joint-criterion separation}
\label{sec:advantage-attribution}

The costed-ordering study also permits a stricter post-hoc decomposition of an
already frozen criterion.  Its primary comparison is the conjunction
\emph{protected root-task success and no forbidden high-level mutation}.  The
previous discriminating-power audit found that all six registered comparator
contrasts favoured the licensed policy on that conjunction, but that result did
not identify which conjunct supplied the separation.

Decomposing the same committed comparison rows without re-running any arm gives
an adverse answer.  Against the exact dynamic-programming oracle the licensed
policy wins zero and loses 183 discordant worlds on raw task success alone.  Two
other unsafe comparators also outperform it on raw success, and a fourth is not
discriminated.  The only two comparators it beats on raw success are the two
whose forbidden-mutation rate is already zero.  Conversely, every comparator
that the licensed policy beats only under the joint criterion has a nonzero
forbidden-mutation rate.  For the two safety-matched arms, the raw-success and
joint-criterion discordant counts are identical because the safety conjunct is
vacuous.

The strongest safety-matched diagnostic is a random policy constrained by the
same no-forbidden-mutation rule.  The licensed policy still beats that arm, but
by a much smaller margin (139 versus 75 discordant worlds) than the margins
against unsafe arms.  Thus the large six-of-six joint-criterion separation is
primarily a consequence of enforcing the safety boundary; it is not evidence
that the licensed ordering is generally better at solving the underlying task.
This is consistent with the separately registered economy failure and with the
faithful-comparator falsification above.

This decomposition is deliberately not treated as a new confirmatory test.  It
uses only committed outcome views, moves no threshold, changes no gate, and can
only narrow the interpretation.  It therefore leaves the registered terminal
and all adverse history unchanged.  The supported bounded claim is that the
licensing construction enforces the declared scientific-mutation safety
constraint while retaining full success on its generated family; comparative
ordering competence, naturalistic superiority, and mechanism necessity remain
unsupported.
