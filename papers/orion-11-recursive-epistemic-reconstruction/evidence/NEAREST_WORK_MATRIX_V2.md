## Verification gaps — stated rather than papered over

1. **SCION checkpoint-failure semantics — CLOSED 2026-08-17.** Full paper text extracted and read (pdftotext, 1289 lines). SCION has two distinct failure-handling layers: (a) *precompiled fallback conditions* in the Research Execution Plan, defining what to do when a checkpoint fails at the planning layer; and (b) a *runtime rollback mechanism* in which the coordinator can trigger rollback, branch termination, re-routing, or resource reallocation when critic checkpoints detect agent drift, hallucinated content, unproductive branches, or physical-constraint violations. The runtime rollback is triggered by *procedural anomalies* (deviations from plan, consistency violations, unproductive execution), not by an evidence-graph inconsistency or a formulation-coordinate change. SCION does not have dependency-directed reopening of already-completed dependent stages conditional on the *type* of responsibility for the failure. EviGraph independently establishes dependency-directed reopening from evidence-graph inconsistency, so P1.D3 is struck regardless of SCION's design. **This gap is resolved:** the gap changes no verdict, and the precise finding is that SCION's rollback is runtime-procedural (anomaly-detection gated) rather than epistemic (responsibility-typed formulation-change gated), which is orthogonal to ORION's P1.D2 claim. The gap is now closed.

## Residual after absorption

After absorbing all 36 mechanisms above, the smallest surviving Paper-I claim is:

> **In a research process whose object knowledge `K`, relevance/search-universe model `W`, and method state `M` are separately represented, ORION types the responsibility for an observed residual so that responsibility determines authority: only a formulation or search-universe responsibility licenses a coordinate-specific rewrite of `W` or `M`, while evidence and execution responsibilities are routed to acquisition and repair and are denied the rewrite licence; and it is that licensed rewrite — not an inconsistency inside the evidence graph — that scopes the dependency-directed reopening of prior closure. The contribution is the licensing relation binding typed responsibility to the reframe target and to the reopen scope, evaluated on hidden formulation-shift tasks against matched baselines with evidence-only and execution-only negative controls.**

Everything else has a parent. The state representation is Iris's; the reopening machinery is EviGraph's, EA-Graph's, Doyle's and change-impact analysis's; the recursive audit is AREX's; the missing-cell obligation is the requirements verification matrix's; the coarse execution-vs-hypothesis diagnosis is ARTS's; the metareasoning budget is De Sabbata et al.'s and Cognitive Friction's; the gap statement itself is Arbor's and Bisht et al.'s.

What no verified work does is make the *type of the diagnosed responsibility* the thing that grants or withholds permission to rewrite the formulation, and then use the rewritten coordinate to bound what gets reopened. EviGraph reopens from an evidence-graph inconsistency while holding Problem and Gap immutable; Iris revises claims about the task without a separate search-universe model; ARTS diagnoses at two levels and navigates a fixed hypothesis space; the failure-attribution literature localizes an agent and a step. The residual is a licensing relation, and it is compositional by construction.

This residual is **narrower than the composition asserted** in `manuscript/sections/06-related-work-boundary.tex` — the sentence beginning *"The surviving candidate comparison is narrower and compositional"* — and in `JOURNAL_READINESS.md` §1, item *"Rewrite the novelty sentence so it claims only the smallest surviving composition"*. That sentence lists four conjuncts as if each contributed; two of them are now fully parented and contribute only as substrate. The novelty sentence should be rewritten to the paragraph above before submission.

## Per-delta verdicts for P1.D1–P1.D4

Deltas as defined in `research/paper-programme-v1/PAPER_01_RECURSIVE_RECONSTRUCTION.md`, section *"Surviving candidate deltas"* (lines 70–73 at time of writing).

### P1.D1.KWM_RECONSTRUCTION — does **not** survive as an independent delta

Knowledge, relevance/search-universe model and method as co-evolving state rather than hidden agent context.

**Verdict: composition-only.** Iris implements the `K` half completely and better than "explicit state" language suggests: revisable claims carrying scope, status, and supporting/refuting evidence pointers, with Add/Update/Invalidate operators [body-checked]. SCION's REP plus layered epistemic memory covers explicit method state, and Kosmos covers a persistent structured world model. The only component without a verified parent is `W` — a *separate* model of the relevant search universe, distinct from knowledge about the task, that is itself revisable. Iris does not formalize one; Bisht et al. (arXiv:2605.08956) *recommend* building one as future work, which both confirms the gap is real and means ORION cannot claim to have identified it. A single unparented organ inside a three-part state is not an independent delta. Report `W` as the delta-bearing organ of the composition, not as a standalone contribution.

### P1.D2.TYPED_FORMULATION_REVISION — survives as an independent delta, in narrowed form

Discoveries/failures can target question, representation, decomposition, interface, measurement, search policy or method separately.

**Verdict: survives, narrowly.** The narrowing is severe and must be stated in the paper. ARTS already diagnoses implementation-fault versus bad-hypothesis, so the binary split is struck. Iris already attributes a failure to which specific claim is wrong. The Who&When line of work attributes to a responsible agent and decisive step; MAST (arXiv:2503.13657) classifies failures into 14 modes across system design issues, inter-agent misalignment and task verification; *Auditable Agents* lists responsibility attribution as a named auditability dimension. What remains unparented across all of these is that the *type* of the responsibility functions as an **authority gate**: a formulation/search-universe diagnosis selects which coordinate may be rewritten, and an evidence-only or execution-only diagnosis withholds the rewrite licence entirely. Every neighbour above produces a *descriptive label*; none attaches a *permission* to it. ORION has local mechanical evidence that this distinction bites — the Paper-I hidden-shift falsifier exposed an over-broad reframe gate in which a singular responsibility diagnosis was wrongly treated as sufficient licence, and the repair is recorded in `CLAIM_LEDGER.md`. This is the one delta that should be defended as independent, and it must be claimed as *responsibility-typed licensing of formulation rewrite*, never as "failure attribution".

The P1.D2 verdict is safe against the failure-attribution cluster (TRAIL, AEGIS, AgenTracer, AgentErrorBench, ErrorProbe, AgentRx, REFLECT, span-level/DRIFT — all retrieved and checked). None produces a permission to modify the formulation or search universe based on the diagnosed error type.

### P1.D3.SCOPED_REOPENING — **struck**; does not survive as an independent delta

A material reframe invalidates only dependent closure and forces renewed search under the changed formulation.

**Verdict: struck.** This is the clearest removal in the pass, and it is a win. EviGraph (arXiv:2608.04738) performs exactly this inside a 2026 autonomous research agent: it identifies a weak node, forms a repair group containing that node and its downstream subordinates, removes the subordinates, repairs the node, regenerates the affected nodes in dependency order, and version-checks the result [body-checked]. EA-Graph (arXiv:2608.04278) adds content-hash anchoring, staleness detection independent of evidence strength, and claim withdrawal under upstream drift [body-checked]. Beneath both, Doyle's 1979 JTMS invalidates every dependent conclusion when a justification is retracted, and change impact analysis, regression test selection and incremental build systems have done transitive-closure invalidation in engineering practice for decades. ORION must delete any claim that scoped reopening is a novel mechanism, cite EviGraph and EA-Graph as the nearest work, and adopt EA-Graph's *unprovable* outcome as prior art for its own fail-closed semantics. The residual is only the **trigger and scope**: EviGraph reopens from an inconsistency internal to the evidence graph while holding Problem and Gap nodes immutable, whereas ORION reopens because a formulation/search-universe coordinate changed. That residual is inseparable from P1.D2 and belongs to the composition.

The research workflow is itself recursively decomposed into inspectable mechanic cells whose missing dimensions generate work orders mechanically.
