Success on the frozen replay shows that **a method-basis change is not required for these known worlds**: existing search-universe expansion plus functional parent-domain matching can express the repair. It does not prove that the original RAKL run faithfully executed its routes, nor does it establish open-world parent-discipline recall.

The learned rule is to treat finite discipline lists as seeds only. Important mechanics should generate name-independent functional signatures and repeatedly challenge the search universe for mature fields studying the same operations/failures/invariants.
