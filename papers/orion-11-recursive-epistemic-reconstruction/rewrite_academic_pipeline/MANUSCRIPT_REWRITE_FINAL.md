# Typed Responsibility Licensing for Scientific Revision: Exact Decision Boundaries without Comparative Superiority

**Authority:** bounded exact-contract result; comparative superiority withdrawn

## Abstract

A successful diagnosis or repair does not determine which scientific layer the evidence authorizes changing. We formalize **typed responsibility licensing** as a target-specific relation between an observed failure, the responsibility it bears on, the transition currently proposed and the scientific authority required for that transition. The relation is represented through epistemic transition envelopes, exact interface factorization and legal-action deficiency. In prospectively frozen primary and disjoint runs of 2,882 mechanical worlds each, a licensed-transition policy solves all 480 hidden-shift cases while making no unnecessary high-level change on 2,402 controls.

The comparative interpretation is deliberately adverse. The original parent mechanisms solved only 49.375% of hidden shifts, but a later faithful-comparator study gives each parent the same ordered public repair search. All repaired parents reach 100% hidden-shift success, and the strongest also makes zero forbidden high-level mutations. The former mechanism-necessity margin is therefore retracted. A separately frozen costed-ordering successor tests the remaining economy interpretation and also rejects it: at equal success and safety, the licensed policy costs 1.82 times the faithful Active-VOI comparator, against a preregistered gate requiring a ratio below 0.80. No comparative cost residual survives.

What remains is an information and decision-boundary result. On 400 authored exact contracts, the target-aware licensing relation makes 400 correct promotion/transition decisions; a donor-complete interface that carries provenance, verification, version, evaluator-custody, epoch and generic authorization information but omits the responsibility-to-authority coupling makes 275 correct decisions. An information-equivalent product given the same target-defining coordinates ties at 400/400. The result is therefore portable and non-architectural: scientific revision is recoverable when the representation preserves the distinctions on which the target transition depends. A historical 48-case precursor remains negative. In the naturalistic programme, 117,649 source-to-target maps yield 116,929 rejections, 720 unresolved maps and zero certifications; the external-authority lane has received 0/7 signed outputs and closed 0/4 authority acts. The contribution is a bounded licensing calculus and exact mechanism boundary, not evidence of naturalistic or comparative agent superiority.

## 1. Introduction

Scientific work changes at several levels. A local parameter can be corrected. A measurement can be repeated. A representation can be replaced. A research question can be reformulated. A negative result can be reopened after material new evidence. These transitions are not interchangeable. Evidence that identifies a defective component does not automatically authorize changing a broader scientific responsibility.

This distinction is easy to lose in automated repair. A system observes failure, searches an intervention menu and selects a repair. If the repair restores task success, the workflow may treat the intervention as scientifically licensed. Yet the same observable failure can arise under states requiring different legal changes. A high-level reformulation may be justified in one case and prohibited in another because a protected sibling, dependency or lower-level obligation remains active.

We ask:

> What information must a repair interface preserve in order to determine which scientific transition is authorized?

The paper originally appeared to answer a stronger comparative question. A governed recursive-repair policy substantially outperformed three parents. Faithful comparator work later showed that the success margin came from ordered search that the parents had been denied, not from demonstrated necessity of the responsibility mechanism. A costed successor then refuted the remaining economy reading. Those failures are not ancillary. They determine the paper's present claim.

The surviving object is a target-specific licensing relation. It separates:

- whether a repair succeeds;
- whether the intervention is scientifically permitted;
- whether the visible representation contains the coordinates needed to decide that permission;
- whether a particular implementation has comparative advantage.

The first three can be studied exactly without claiming the fourth.

## 2. Scientific transitions and responsibility

Let a scientific state contain objects, obligations and dependencies. A proposed transition `a` may modify one or more levels. A responsibility assignment identifies which obligations the observed failure actually bears on. A transition is licensed only when the available evidence establishes that the action changes no protected responsibility and satisfies the authority conditions for every responsibility it does change.

We represent this with three objects.

1. **Transition envelope.** The set of state changes compatible with the currently established evidence.
2. **Responsibility-to-authority coupling.** A mapping from the responsibility affected by a transition to the scientific evidence and authority required to perform it.
3. **Legal-action deficiency.** The set of distinctions needed by the target licensing decision that are absent from the observable interface.

The calculus is non-compensatory. Strong provenance, successful execution or a plausible repair cannot compensate for a missing authority condition. An unresolved responsibility produces an unresolved transition rather than a guessed approval.

## 3. Exact interface factorization

A representation partitions underlying scientific states into observable fibres. If two states in one fibre require different transition decisions, no downstream rule restricted to that representation can be exact on both. This is the same information-loss principle that appears in target-sufficient verification: optimization cannot recover a distinction erased by the interface.

For the registered finite contract, the licensing decision factors through the representation if and only if the correct terminal is constant on every positive-mass fibre and can be expressed by the interface's output alphabet. Mixed fibres impose an irreducible decision floor.

This gives a decisive control. A competing product enriched with the same target-defining responsibility coordinates and the same licensing relation must tie extensionally. Such a tie is expected and desirable: it shows that the scientific object is portable rather than tied to a proprietary architecture.

## 4. Mechanical-world evaluation

The primary controlled programme contains 2,882 credential-free mechanical worlds per run. Four hundred eighty are protected hidden-shift cases; 2,402 are controls. Every policy has the same four-unit intervention envelope.

The target criterion is conjunctive:

- restore the protected root task on hidden-shift worlds;
- avoid every forbidden high-level mutation.

A policy that restores success by changing a protected scientific layer does not pass. A policy that refuses all transitions does not pass the hidden-shift cases. The evaluation therefore separates effectiveness from intervention legality.

The licensed policy uses responsibility, dependency and protected-sibling coordinates to restrict the admissible transition envelope. In the primary and disjoint runs it solves every hidden-shift case and performs no unnecessary high-level change on the controls. Two independently generated world sets reproduce the bounded mechanism behavior.

## 5. Faithful comparator retracts mechanism necessity

The original parents each choose one top-confidence repair. They solve 49.375% of hidden-shift cases, while the licensed policy solves all of them. The difference initially appeared to support a responsibility-mechanism necessity claim.

The faithful-comparator study changes one operation: each parent searches the same public repair menu in order. No new evidence or budget is added.

All repaired parents reach 100% hidden-shift success. One repaired parent also reaches zero forbidden high-level mutation and therefore matches the licensed policy on both primary gates. The paired success comparison contains no disagreements.

The conclusion is mandatory:

> Ordered search explains the former success margin, and the strongest faithful parent removes the cross-policy safety margin as well.

The original comparative necessity claim is retracted. Within-system ablations can still show that removing a component changes the licensed implementation, but they do not establish that the component is uniquely necessary among all information- and capability-matched mechanisms.

## 6. Costed successor refutes comparative economy

After primary-gate equivalence, one possible residual remained: perhaps responsibility licensing achieves the same safe success at lower intervention cost.

A separately frozen successor compares the licensed ordering with a faithful Active-VOI comparator under the same success and safety requirements. The preregistered positive gate requires the licensed-policy cost to be less than 0.80 times the comparator cost.

The observed ratio is 1.82. The licensed policy is more expensive, not cheaper. The economy hypothesis is therefore negative. It is not rescued by returning to the older comparison against non-searching parents, and it is not reframed as a different positive cost claim.

This second falsifier removes the final comparative headline. The paper is not a leaderboard result about recursive repair.

## 7. Exact 400-contract licensing study

The strongest remaining evidence directly tests the decision relation. The registered study spans five scientific artifact domains, eight case archetypes and ten protected variants, producing 400 exact contracts.

The target-aware licensing rule is correct on 400/400. A strong generic product carrying provenance, local verification, artifact and version identity, evaluator custody, epoch information and generic authorization is correct on 275/400. It fails when those generic coordinates do not encode which scientific responsibility the proposed transition affects.

An ideal product supplied with the same target-defining responsibility coordinates and licensing rule is correct on 400/400 and ties exactly.

This three-arm pattern is the paper's central evidence:

- generic assurance is valuable but insufficient for the target decision;
- target-sufficient responsibility information closes the finite contracts;
- architecture disappears when information and relation are matched.

The result supports a decision contract, not a claim that one implementation owns scientific authority.

## 8. Negative precursor and naturalistic boundary

A historical 48-case precursor remains negative and contributes no upgraded authority. Its failure motivated the present factorization but is not rewritten after later exact work.

The naturalistic programme is also deliberately non-positive. Across 117,649 source-to-target maps, 116,929 are rejected, 720 remain unresolved and zero are certified. This is not evidence that useful transitions never exist. It shows that the required source-native authority, pairing and responsibility evidence was not available for certification.

A content-addressed external-authority protocol has been prepared, but 0/7 signed outputs have been returned and 0/4 authority acts have closed. Missing external evidence remains `CANNOT_CHECK`; same-programme agents cannot manufacture independent authority by agreeing with one another.

## 9. What is established

The current evidence establishes:

1. a formal responsibility-to-authority licensing relation for scientific transitions;
2. exact finite conditions under which an observable interface can recover that relation;
3. successful operation on two 2,882-world controlled runs;
4. exact 400-contract separation between generic assurance and target-sufficient licensing;
5. equivalence of an information-matched alternative product;
6. falsification of both comparative mechanism necessity and comparative cost economy.

It does not establish naturalistic transfer, broad agent safety, higher research productivity, architecture superiority or external scientific authority.

## 10. Relation to prior work

Belief revision, truth maintenance, dependency-directed repair, planning, authorization, provenance, value-of-information search and scientific workflow systems own the donor mechanisms. The paper does not claim ordered search, dependency tracking or generic authorization as new.

The residual is the exact coupling between a scientific responsibility and the authority required to change it, together with a preserved falsification sequence showing what that coupling does **not** buy comparatively once strong donors receive equal search and cost opportunities.

## 11. Limitations

The controlled worlds and exact contracts are authored finite environments. Their gold transition decisions depend on the registered responsibility ontology and authority rules. The paper does not show that those coordinates are universally minimal or that they exhaust natural scientific practice. Same-programme independent implementations improve defect detection but do not replace external adjudication.

The naturalistic programme has no positive certified cases. That absence blocks empirical promotion but does not weaken the exact finite theorem within its stated boundary.

## 13. Conclusion

Scientific repair is not licensed by success alone. The evidence must identify which responsibility the failure bears on and which transition that responsibility authorizes. In exact finite contracts, a target-aware responsibility interface closes decisions that a strong generic assurance product cannot, while an information-equivalent alternative ties exactly.

The comparative story is intentionally negative. Giving parents equal ordered search removes the original success and safety margin, and a costed successor shows the licensed policy is 1.82 times as expensive as the faithful Active-VOI comparator under the registered test. The durable result is therefore a portable licensing boundary for scientific revision, not superiority of a recursive-repair architecture.
