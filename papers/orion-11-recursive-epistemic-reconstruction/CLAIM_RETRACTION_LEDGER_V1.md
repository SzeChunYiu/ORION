## 1. What is retracted, and what is not

### Retracted — the comparative reading

The claim that the governed policy's protected hidden-shift advantage over the three
registered strong parents is evidence of **mechanism necessity relative to those
parents**. R4 shows the frozen `+0.50625` primary margin (`+0.5167` replication) is
**fully recovered by ordered search alone**: giving each parent one change — a single
top-confidence pick replaced by an ordered search over the same public repair and
diagnostic menu — moves all three repaired parents from `0.49375` to `1.00000` on
`protected_root_task_success` over the 480 hidden-shift worlds.

`activevoi_search_admitted_parent` then matches ORION on **both** registered
components (hidden-shift success `1.00000`, forbidden high-level mutation rate
`0.00000`), with McNemar `b = 0`, `c = 0`, `p = 1.0`, and bootstrap 95% CI
`[0.0, 0.0]` on the paired difference. `matches_orion_within_margin: true`.

The margin therefore measured **comparator non-iteration**, not the necessity of the
ORION composition relative to a faithful parent.
