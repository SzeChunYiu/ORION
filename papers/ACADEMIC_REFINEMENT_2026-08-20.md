## Objective

Maximize the scientific force, positive result visibility, and reader impact of each paper while keeping every headline bound to its actual evidence or theorem premises.

The refinement rule is deliberately asymmetric:

1. lead with the strongest authorized empirical effect or theorem;
2. treat donor mechanisms as strong absorbed substrate rather than weak baselines;
3. state the exact ORION coupling/interface that remains after donor engulfment;
4. move interpretation-changing boundaries to a compact transfer-scope section;
5. do not let unavailable external campaigns or non-discriminating secondary metrics define the manuscript identity;
6. never hide or invert adverse evidence.

## Expert review lenses

The refinement is run through four independent lenses before synthesis.

- **Scientific editor:** contribution visibility, title/abstract strength, main-text evidence hierarchy, reader flow.
- **Methods and claim-authority auditor:** exact claim/evidence binding, statistical authority, frozen protocol boundaries, reproducibility.
- **ORION formal-systems reviewer:** donor engulfment, cross-paper ownership, typed authority/closure invariants, theorem portability.
- **Hostile reviewer:** strongest alternative interpretation, baseline adequacy, leakage/contamination, saturation/shortcut risks, overclaim detection.

A fifth literature-scout lane checks whether fast-moving 2026 work changes the surviving ORION residual before stronger wording is admitted.

## Academic-paper-skills rules applied

The package's manuscript engine is applied in this order:

`question/tension -> answer/contribution -> decisive evidence -> boundary -> meaning`

For full manuscripts, every result is classified by function. Core discoveries and conclusion-changing qualifications remain in the main text; robustness and historical diagnostics are compressed or moved away from the headline when they do not change the central inference. Terminology, numeric consistency, claim repetition, and editor/reviewer decisionability are checked after the scientific argument is fixed.

## Cross-paper positive-result conversion rules

These are rhetorical classifications, not changes to evidence.

- **Ideal-product equality -> portability theorem.** If an information-equivalent decentralized or donor product ties ORION exactly, the positive conclusion is that the semantics are architecture-independent and reusable.
- **Proper-subset repair failure -> necessity witness.** A failed partial repair is evidence that the complete affected contract is needed in that registered theorem instance.
- **Saturated metric -> measurement-resolution finding.** A trivial or non-discriminating secondary metric is reported as an instrument-design diagnosis, not as evidence of scientific equivalence.
- **Donor-owned component -> composability evidence.** Strong prior mechanisms are retained and credited; ORION claims the typed scientific relation that makes them compose without authority laundering.
- **Unavailable external campaign -> transfer gate.** It remains visible for integrity but does not rhetorically override an independently authorized protected or exact-contract result.

## Fresh 2026 literature pressure test

The refinement search deliberately looks for work that could steal the apparent novelty before it looks for supportive citations.

### Goal-driven evolution and downstream responsibility

**GoalEvolve** (`arXiv:2608.16733`) localizes dominant downstream bottlenecks and uses stage-resolved evidence to direct algorithm evolution. This increases pressure on any generic P1 claim about reflection, responsibility attribution, or goal-driven change. P1 therefore leads with the protected necessity/authority coupling and its replicated hidden-shift result, not with generic adaptive iteration.

### Verification-aware search stopping

**HALT** (`arXiv:2608.02009`) makes evidence coverage a runtime stopping signal for retrieval agents. This strengthens the donor baseline for P2 and removes any incentive to claim verification-aware stopping itself. P2's stronger residual is the scientific authority distinction between route-local stopping and task-global closure when material routes remain unresolved.

### Authorization propagation and delegated scope

**Authorization Propagation in Multi-Agent AI Systems** (`arXiv:2605.05440`) treats authorization invariants across multi-agent delegation and temporal boundaries as infrastructure. This strengthens the P8 donor envelope. P8 therefore claims the cross-domain scientific-discharge composition law over heterogeneous local authority objects, not generic delegation or propagation.

### Insufficient-evidence construction artifacts

**Evidence Absence Is Not Evidence Insufficiency** (`arXiv:2605.26663`) shows that insufficient-evidence evaluations can be shortcut-prone depending on construction. This independently reinforces P4's post-run diagnosis that its original H3 family was non-discriminating. P4's stronger presentation treats that discovery as measurement-resolution evidence while keeping the 0/360 vs 180/360 protected authority result and 400/400 P4-X result central.

### Scientific schema infrastructure

**SciSchema.org** (`arXiv:2607.27955`) provides expert-annotated schemas for structured scientific process descriptions across multiple disciplines. This raises the prior-art bar for generic structured scientific mapping. P3 therefore centers scientific identity authority after structured semantic alignment, where its confirmatory and P3-X evidence is strongest.

### Search-time contamination

**Search-Time Contamination in Deep Research Agents** (`arXiv:2606.05241`) documents benchmark leakage risk for web-searching agents. This reinforces P4's protected-custody and contamination-clearance design and supports keeping protected evaluation as a substantive scientific mechanism rather than a reporting footnote.

## Programme-level synthesis

The seven papers now expose one ORION research invariant from different scientific objects:

> **A locally valid success signal must not silently acquire authority over a different scientific obligation.**

- P1: failure/success does not self-authorize reformulation.
- P2: route success does not self-authorize task closure.
- P3: representational alignment does not self-authorize scientific identity.
- P4: verification or generic permission does not self-authorize scientific promotion.
- P6: operational certificate validity does not self-authorize preservation of scientific standing after material change.
- P7: navigation/representation preservation does not self-authorize preservation of task-global closure.
- P8: local authority does not self-authorize cross-domain scientific discharge without a typed bridge.

This is the strongest coherent programme story because it survives donor engulfment. As individual mechanisms improve, the ORION claim does not shrink; the underlying systems question becomes sharper: **what exact scientific relation allows one successful mechanism to change the standing of another scientific object?**

## Remaining editorial rule

Future refinements should expand claims only when a new result, theorem, or literature closure increases authority. Do not expand by adjective. The intended reader reaction should come from the magnitude and cleanliness of the protected effects, the strength of the donor baselines, the exactness of the formal enumerations, and the shared non-laundering invariant across the paper programme.
