## Objective

Maximize the scientific force, positive result visibility, and reader impact of each paper while keeping every headline bound to its actual evidence or theorem premises.

The refinement rule is deliberately asymmetric:

1. lead with the strongest authorized empirical effect or theorem;
2. treat donor mechanisms as strong absorbed substrate rather than weak baselines;
3. state the exact ORION coupling/interface that remains after donor engulfment;
4. promote adverse outcomes to the strongest different scientific claim they genuinely establish;
5. move interpretation-changing boundaries to a compact transfer-scope section;
6. never hide, invert, or cosmetically relabel adverse evidence.

The detailed adverse-result analysis is frozen in `research/publication/NEGATIVE_RESULT_PROMOTION_2026-08-20.md`.

## Expert review lenses

The refinement is run through four independent lenses before synthesis.

- **Scientific editor:** contribution visibility, title/abstract strength, main-text evidence hierarchy, reader flow.
- **Methods and claim-authority auditor:** exact claim/evidence binding, statistical authority, frozen protocol boundaries, reproducibility.
- **ORION formal-systems reviewer:** donor engulfment, cross-paper ownership, typed authority/closure invariants, theorem portability, necessity/sufficiency, and monotonicity.
- **Hostile reviewer:** strongest alternative interpretation, baseline adequacy, leakage/contamination, saturation/shortcut risks, overclaim detection.

A fifth literature-scout lane checks whether fast-moving 2026 work changes the surviving ORION residual before stronger wording is admitted.

## Academic-paper-skills rules applied

The manuscript engine is applied in this order:

`question/tension -> answer/contribution -> decisive evidence -> exact law/boundary -> meaning`

For full manuscripts, every result is classified by scientific function. Core discoveries and conclusion-changing qualifications remain in the main text; robustness and historical diagnostics are compressed unless the diagnostic itself establishes a stronger theorem, falsifier, or benchmark-validity result.

## Negative-result promotion rules

These are claim-type corrections, not changes to evidence.

- **Failed historical superiority -> falsification-to-repair chronology.** Keep the failed hypothesis failed; promote the exact unsafe rule it falsified and the prospectively frozen corrected rule that succeeds afterward.
- **Stage-local negative -> failure localization.** A loss between retrieval and inclusion is positive evidence that processing stages carry distinct obligations.
- **Invalid external campaign -> transport-authority firewall.** Valid candidate execution plus invalid provider transport establishes that a well-formed run is not automatically a scientifically valid comparison.
- **Ideal-product equality -> representation-independence theorem.** Exact equality to an information-equivalent donor/decentralized product establishes that the semantics are portable rather than architecture-specific.
- **Full repair succeeds + every strict subset fails -> necessary-and-sufficient repair law in the registered model.** Restorations prove sufficiency; strict-subset countermodels prove necessity.
- **Saturated metric + perfect shortcut -> benchmark non-identifiability.** If the intended competence and a trivial construction cue are observationally indistinguishable, the benchmark cannot authorize the intended comparative claim.
- **Matched widening chains fail -> monotone non-widening authority law.** Local validity composes only within its scientific type unless a protected bridge establishes the broader target relation.
- **Donor ownership -> compositional strength.** The stronger the donor substrate, the more precise the residual ORION contribution becomes.

## Fresh 2026 literature pressure test

The refinement search deliberately looks for work that could steal the apparent novelty before it looks for supportive citations.

### Goal-driven evolution and downstream responsibility

**GoalEvolve** (`arXiv:2608.16733`) localizes dominant downstream bottlenecks and uses stage-resolved evidence to direct algorithm evolution. This increases pressure on any generic P1 claim about reflection, responsibility attribution, or goal-driven change. P1 therefore leads with the protected necessity/authority coupling and its falsification-to-prospective-repair chronology, not generic adaptive iteration.

### Verification-aware search stopping

**HALT** (`arXiv:2608.02009`) and **Don't Stop Early** (ACL 2026 Industry) make evidence coverage and explicit sufficiency conditions strong donor mechanisms. P2 therefore claims the higher closure-authority relation over material routes and processing obligations, not generic evidence-aware stopping.

### Authorization propagation and proof-carrying action

**Authorization Propagation in Multi-Agent AI Systems** (`arXiv:2605.05440`) makes workflow authorization propagation a strong donor for P8. **Proof of Execution** (`arXiv:2607.05397`) and **Proof-Carrying Agent Actions** (`arXiv:2606.04104`) make governed execution/action certificates strong donors for P6. The ORION residual is therefore the scientific lift/discharge relation above those mechanisms.

### Benchmark construction, saturation, and abstention

**Evidence Absence Is Not Evidence Insufficiency** (`arXiv:2605.26663`) shows that insufficient-evidence constructions can teach shortcut recognition rather than the intended competence. **When AI Benchmarks Plateau** (`arXiv:2602.16763`) shows saturation is widespread and destroys resolving power. **AgentAbstain** (`arXiv:2607.10059`) separates should-act from should-abstain competence. These results strengthen P4's exact H3 interpretation: the registered battery is non-identifying for abstention competence because its label is perfectly recoverable from evidence-list emptiness.

### Scientific-agent benchmark integrity

The 2026 verified refresh of **ScienceAgentBench** explicitly repairs false negatives in scientific-agent evaluation. This supports the programme-level principle that evaluator/benchmark validity is part of scientific claim authority rather than clerical cleanup.

### Scientific schema infrastructure

**SciSchema.org** (`arXiv:2607.27955`) raises the prior-art bar for generic structured scientific mapping. P3 therefore centers the scientific identity authorization relation after structured alignment rather than claiming structured semantics themselves.

### Search-time contamination

**Search-Time Contamination in Deep Research Agents** (`arXiv:2606.05241`) reinforces P4's protected-custody and contamination-clearance design and supports treating evaluator integrity as a scientific mechanism rather than a reporting footnote.

## Programme-level synthesis

The negative-result audit sharpens the shared ORION law:

> **A locally valid success signal must not silently acquire authority over a different scientific obligation.**

- P1: failure does not self-authorize reformulation.
- P2: route success does not self-authorize task closure; a locally valid external run does not self-authorize a transported comparison.
- P3: representational alignment does not self-authorize scientific identity.
- P4: verification or generic permission does not self-authorize scientific promotion; a benchmark score does not self-authorize the competence claim it is intended to measure.
- P6: operational certificate validity does not self-authorize preservation of scientific standing after material change; complete affected-bridge repair characterizes restoration in the registered model.
- P7: navigation/representation preservation does not self-authorize task-global closure; complete affected-contract repair and explicit intermediate binding characterize restoration/composition in the registered model.
- P8: local authority does not self-authorize cross-domain widening; unbridged composition is monotone/non-widening in the registered 13×13 product.

The programme-level claim is therefore larger than seven isolated wins: **ORION studies the discharge relation that determines when one scientifically relevant success is entitled to change the status of another scientific object.** The adverse results are valuable exactly where they reveal missing discharge relations, invalid instruments, or necessary bridge conditions.

## Remaining editorial rule

Future refinements should expand claims only when a new result, theorem, countermodel family, or literature closure increases authority. Do not expand by adjective. The intended reader reaction should come from the magnitude and cleanliness of the protected effects, exact necessary/sufficient laws, complete finite composition results, benchmark non-identifiability proofs, strong donor baselines, and the shared non-laundering invariant across the paper programme.
