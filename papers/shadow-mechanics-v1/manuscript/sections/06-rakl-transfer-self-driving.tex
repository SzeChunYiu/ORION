\section{RAKL transfer and Shadow self-driving development}
The strengthened mechanics audit exposes fourteen provisional structural dimensions for each of 59 registered mechanic cells, yielding 826 explicit questions before transfer.  The V1 transfer programme treats the mature RAKL repository as prior design knowledge rather than as imported authority.  All 24 registered RAKL method surfaces are reconstructed as scoped ORION-facing profiles.  Forty-nine leaf/cross-cutting mechanics receive direct or adjacent surface profiles, while the ten root/top-level mechanics receive explicit relational-composition contracts over their children.

The transfer does not directly clear provisional markers.  Instead it generates one evidence-bound \texttt{AnswerRecord} for every current mechanic/dimension pair.  The fourteen answered dimensions are verification, failure semantics, observability, handoff, state, transition model, mathematics, dependencies, metrics, uncertainty, invariants, parent discipline, search coverage, and saturation.  RAKL-derived records cite the frozen method-contract/test provenance; top-level composition records cite explicit ORION child/dependency derivations.  ORION's answer engine validates evidence presence, typed payloads, conflicts and supersession, and then removes only the corresponding provisional marker.  The application audit requires closure to be attributable to applied records.

The reconciled implementation passes the structural criterion
\[
826\ \text{open questions}\;\longrightarrow\;0\ \text{open questions}
\]
through 826 evidence-bound answer applications with zero answer or audit residuals.  This is specification closure only:
\[
\text{specification closure} \neq \text{empirical validity} \neq \text{promotion authority}.
\]
Fresh ORION conformance, ablation, construct validity, live retrieval performance and protected assurance remain explicit empirical coordinates.

The resulting Shadow development controller composes the following loop:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{local/RAKL absorption}
\rightarrow \text{evidence-bound structural answers}
\rightarrow \text{empirical work ranking}
\rightarrow \text{DevelopmentFibre}\\
&\rightarrow \text{ORION research}
\rightarrow \text{evidence gate}
\rightarrow \text{coding proposal}\\
&\rightarrow \text{content-addressed patch}
\rightarrow \text{isolated sandbox}
\rightarrow \text{protected fresh assurance}\\
&\rightarrow \text{append-only evolution record}
\rightarrow \text{host-promotion recommendation}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The coding proposal provider may be an LLM, Codex-like coding agent, program synthesizer, or other worker.  It is not the controller.  Patch bytes are stored as content-addressed artifacts, candidate execution occurs behind a host-owned sandbox boundary, protected evaluator/governance paths are hard rejection coordinates, and the controller exposes no merge primitive.

Several RAKL V3 control mechanics are reconstructed explicitly: multi-axis saturation vectors, structural novelty classification, experience-conditioned scheduling, and append-only evolution history.  An invention-readiness gate further prevents repeated failure from being interpreted as evidence for a new representation/operator/ontology until relevant search axes are bounded-flat, ordinary causes are excluded, residuals survive distinct variations, and discriminator evidence supports exactly one missing-structure class.

The state reached by this section is therefore \emph{Shadow self-driving architecture readiness}: with research, coding, sandbox, and protected-evaluation providers attached, ORION can select and execute its next development investigation, request and test a candidate repair, and recommend an assured challenger to the external host.  It has not yet demonstrated that this loop improves real scientific research on frozen wide/deep tasks, nor that its development policy beats a simpler baseline.  Governed Self-ORION remains an empirical gate rather than an architectural declaration.
