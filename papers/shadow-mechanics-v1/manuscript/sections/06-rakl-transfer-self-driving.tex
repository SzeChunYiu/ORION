\section{RAKL transfer and Shadow self-driving development}
The first Shadow mechanics audit left 472 step-specific structural questions open: eight provisional dimensions for each of 59 registered mechanic cells.  The V1 transfer programme treats the mature RAKL repository as prior design knowledge rather than as imported authority.  All 24 registered RAKL method surfaces are reconstructed as scoped ORION-facing profiles.  Forty-nine leaf/cross-cutting mechanics receive direct or adjacent surface profiles, while the ten root/top-level mechanics receive explicit relational-composition contracts over their children.  The transfer supplies step-specific handoff, state, observability, transition, mathematical, failure, verification, and dependency semantics while simultaneously retaining fresh ORION conformance and ablation coordinates.

The implementation therefore distinguishes
\[
\text{specification closure} \neq \text{empirical validity} \neq \text{promotion authority}.
\]
The current transfer test requires the registered structural question count to move from $472$ to $0$ without deleting empirical-open coordinates.  RAKL test paths and contracts are provenance and design evidence; they do not certify the reconstructed ORION implementation on unseen live tasks.

The resulting Shadow development controller composes the following loop:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{local/RAKL absorption}
\rightarrow \text{empirical work ranking}
\rightarrow \text{DevelopmentFibre}\\
&\rightarrow \text{ORION research}
\rightarrow \text{evidence gate}
\rightarrow \text{coding proposal}\\
&\rightarrow \text{content-addressed patch}
\rightarrow \text{isolated sandbox}
\rightarrow \text{protected fresh assurance}\\
&\rightarrow \text{append-only evolution record}
\rightarrow \text{host-promotion recommendation}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The coding proposal provider may be an LLM, Codex-like coding agent, program synthesizer, or other worker.  It is not the controller.  Patch bytes are stored as content-addressed artifacts, candidate execution occurs behind a host-owned sandbox boundary, protected evaluator/governance paths are hard rejection coordinates, and the controller exposes no merge primitive.

Several RAKL V3 control mechanics are reconstructed explicitly: multi-axis saturation vectors, structural novelty classification, experience-conditioned scheduling, and append-only evolution history.  An invention-readiness gate further prevents repeated failure from being interpreted as evidence for a new representation/operator/ontology until relevant search axes are bounded-flat, ordinary causes are excluded, residuals survive distinct variations, and discriminator evidence supports exactly one missing-structure class.

The state reached by this section is therefore \emph{Shadow self-driving development readiness}: with research, coding, sandbox, and protected-evaluation providers attached, ORION can select and execute its next development investigation, request and test a candidate repair, and recommend an assured challenger to the external host.  It has not yet demonstrated that this loop improves real scientific research on frozen wide/deep tasks, nor that its development policy beats a simpler baseline.  Governed Self-ORION remains an empirical gate rather than an architectural declaration.
