\section{Mechanical self-research loop}
Let $Q(\mathcal C_i)$ denote the typed questions implied by missing mechanic coordinates.  ORION orders the current questions by non-compensatory development gates and schedules them breadth-first across mechanics, rather than exhausting one large subsystem before inspecting its peers.

A selected question becomes a provider-neutral search task mechanically.  Parent-domain questions route to mature-discipline discovery; coverage questions route to adversarial omission search; saturation questions route to literature-bridge/flatness challenge.  The LLM is therefore not required to remember which questions a research method ought to ask.

For every mechanic, candidate parent disciplines are search seeds only.  Route obligations include current terminology, lexical variants, function-only search, parent disciplines, historical precursors, mathematical equivalents, implementation analogues, methodological inspiration, cross-domain search, literature bridges, citation neighborhoods, adversarial omission, cross-language search where applicable, and freshness.  These obligations define a bounded search basis; they do not imply completed retrieval.

Bounded saturation additionally requires repeated flatness of knowledge, formulation, route coverage, parent-domain growth, contradiction/obstruction state, failure experience and the method/operator frontier.  A resource bound with open obligations yields \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}; universal completeness is not representable as a positive result.
