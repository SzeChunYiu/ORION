\section{Mechanic cell}
A mechanic cell is a typed partial contract
\[
\mathcal C_i=(I_i,O_i,H_i,X_i,Y_i,A_i,F_i,\Lambda_i,J_i,M_i,U_i,R_i,D_i,P_i,V_i,E_i,S_i),
\]
where $I/O$ are inputs/outputs, $H$ handoff schema, $X$ state, $Y$ observables, $A$ admissible actions, $F$ the transition relation, $\Lambda$ invariants, $J$ objectives, $M$ metrics, $U$ uncertainty, $R$ resources, $D$ diagnosis/failure semantics, $P$ storage/provenance/dependency contracts, $V$ verification/authority constraints, $E$ engineering constraints and $S$ the search/saturation specification.

The cell is intentionally incomplete at first.  A deterministic question generator maps absent coordinates to research questions.  The current Shadow decomposition contains the root solve, nine recursive epistemic operators, cross-cutting mechanics and provisional child mechanics recovered from both ORION residuals and RAKL provenance.  Historical RAKL method surfaces are treated as decomposition evidence rather than a final ontology.

Specification completion is non-compensatory: an impressive objective or optimizer cannot compensate for missing verification, failure semantics, observability, state or authority boundaries.
