\section{Mechanics of Mechanics and Failure Experience}

ORION treats each atomic problem-solving operation as a research object.  This is distinct from merely implementing an operator and later measuring end-to-end performance: the operator itself must expose enough state, observability, interfaces, mathematics, failure semantics, and verification structure to support recursive diagnosis.

For mechanic $i$ we use the provisional cell
\[
\mathcal C_i=(I_i,O_i,H_i,X_i,Y_i,A_i,F_i,\Lambda_i,J_i,U_i,R_i,\Phi_i,P_i,V_i,E_i,S_i),
\]
where $I_i$ and $O_i$ are typed inputs and outputs; $H_i$ is the downstream handoff contract; $X_i$ is internal state; $Y_i$ contains directly observable variables; $A_i$ contains admissible actions; $F_i$ is a transition relation; $\Lambda_i$ contains invariants and hard constraints; $J_i$ is a non-compensatory objective/metric vector; $U_i$ represents uncertainty; $R_i$ represents resource coordinates; $\Phi_i$ represents failure signatures, falsifiers, and diagnosis rules; $P_i$ represents persistence and provenance; $V_i$ binds verification and authority; $E_i$ records engineering contracts; and $S_i$ records search-coverage, saturation, and reopen semantics.

A mechanic need not admit a deterministic dynamics model.  The generic form
\[
F_i:X_i\times I_i\times A_i\rightarrow \mathcal P(X_i\times O_i)
\]
allows set-valued transitions, while observations may be represented by
\[
Y_i=h_i(X_i,\eta_i)+\epsilon_i.
\]
The distinction between latent state and observed signals prevents a convenient runtime metric from silently becoming the scientific state itself.

\subsection{Mechanical questions}

The current Shadow implementation contains a fixed question-family registry.  If a mechanic cell omits a dimension, the audit produces an explicit question rather than assuming the dimension is irrelevant.  Examples include which numerical signals are observable, what formal relation maps inputs/actions/state to outputs, what metric vector and uncertainty must be handed downstream, which failure signatures separate competing diagnoses, what must be stored, which parent discipline treats the operation as canonical, and how apparently flat search could still be falsely saturated.  Explicit waivers are permitted but must carry a reason.  Universal runtime envelopes are marked provisional: they provide scaffolding but do not remove the corresponding step-specific question.

The registry is intentionally independent of a language model.  A language model may help answer an open question, but the architecture need not rely on model memory to remember that the question should have been asked.  The V0 research planner deterministically maps prioritized open questions to typed search-route families.  This fixed policy is an auditable baseline rather than a claim of optimal metareasoning.

\subsection{Experience and failure learning}

Every traced mechanic execution emits a typed receipt containing invoked actions, outputs, handoff values, full metric observations, residuals, failure signatures, content-bound evidence/provenance, cost, and latency.  A lossless receipt projection becomes an immutable task episode carrying root/atomic run, parent-run, evaluation-epoch and split identities.  Provider/operator exceptions fail closed into such receipts, while external process death and persistence failure remain durability coordinates,
\[
e_t=(p_t,v_t,m_t,x_t^{-},a_t,y_t,o_t,\phi_t,r_t,E_t,x_t^{+},c_t),
\]
where $v_t$ records the task variation and $\phi_t$ the observed failure signature.

Failures are therefore eligible for the same retrieval and abstraction process as other knowledge, including typed \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} residuals.  If independent runs under distinct variations fail under the same mechanic with shared structural coordinates, ORION may construct a candidate failure pattern and a candidate guard.  Construction, receipt minting, and assessment independently recheck the recurrence manifest, and candidate records cannot self-assert promoted authority.  This recurrence does not establish causality or reusable method authority.  The current governance requires a replay run bound to a supporting task/problem/variation and an actually invoked host-installed guard before local verification.  Conditional reuse additionally requires distinct fresh run, task, split, evaluation, and variation identities.  A host-owned protected assessor verifies an Ed25519 receipt against pinned public keys; the receipt binds the exact candidate and complete support/replay/transfer episode contents.  Process, evaluator, and canonical evidence-artifact lineages are compared mechanically rather than accepted as independence booleans.  Unrelated successes, unprotected assessment, forged receipts, and same-identifier evidence substitution cannot promote the pattern.  A failed transfer contradicts or narrows the pattern rather than being averaged away.

\subsection{Evidence-bound RAKL transfer}

The strengthened answer-loop keeps fourteen generic dimensions provisional for every one of the 59 registered mechanics.  Before transfer, the graph therefore exposes 826 structural questions.  Rather than interpreting these as 826 missing inventions, ORION re-audits the mature RAKL method system as prior design knowledge.  All 24 registered RAKL method surfaces are reconstructed as scoped transfer profiles.  Forty-nine leaf/cross-cutting mechanics receive direct or adjacent profiles and ten root/top-level mechanics receive explicit child-composition profiles.

Crucially, the transfer cannot directly remove provisional flags.  For every mechanic/dimension pair it constructs one evidence-bound \texttt{AnswerRecord}.  The fourteen answer families cover verification, failure, observability, handoff, state, transition model, mathematics, dependencies, metrics, uncertainty, invariants, parent discipline, search coverage, and saturation.  RAKL answers cite the frozen source contract and relevant tests; composition answers cite explicit ORION child/dependency derivations.  The answer engine verifies evidence presence, typed content, conflicts/supersession and structured metric/handoff payloads before updating the cell.  An audit then checks that the number of questions closed is attributable to applied answers.

On the reconciled RAKL/answer-loop implementation, the registered structural frontier moves from
\[
826\rightarrow 0
\]
through exactly 826 answer applications with zero application/audit residuals.  This is a structural result only:
\[
\text{structural specification closure}\not\Rightarrow\text{empirical validity}\not\Rightarrow\text{promotion authority}.
\]
Every transferred mechanic retains fresh conformance, ablation, live-performance, and protected-assurance coordinates.

\subsection{Shadow self-driving development}

The resulting controller can be composed as
\[
\text{absorb local/RAKL knowledge}
\to\text{rank empirical work}
\to\text{research}
\to\text{diagnose}
\to\text{propose change}
\to\text{sandbox}
\to\text{protected assurance}
\to\text{host recommendation}.
\]
A coding LLM or Codex-like agent is a proposal provider inside this loop, not the controller.  The proposed diff is stored as a content-addressed artifact; a host-owned isolated sandbox verifies those exact bytes before execution.  Protected evaluator/governance paths are hard-rejection coordinates.  The change controller exposes no merge primitive, and its strongest successful verdict is \texttt{RECOMMEND\_HOST\_PROMOTION}.

Several mature RAKL V3 controls are reconstructed mechanically rather than copied wholesale: multi-axis saturation vectors, structural novelty classification, experience-conditioned scheduling, append-only evolution history, and an invention-readiness gate.  The invention gate blocks the inference ``repeated failure $\Rightarrow$ invent a new operator'' until relevant search axes are bounded-flat, ordinary failure causes are excluded, the residual persists across distinct variations, and discriminator evidence identifies exactly one missing-structure class.

This establishes a Shadow self-driving \emph{architecture} when research, coding, sandbox and protected-evaluation providers are supplied.  It does not establish that the policy improves real scientific research or ORION development.  Governed Self-ORION still requires frozen live wide/deep tasks, matched baselines, protected evaluator chronology, development improvement, fresh assurance improvement, negative-history preservation and scoped external promotion.

This layer therefore remains a substrate plus known-answer/hostile contract.  The completeness of the mechanic inventory, the correctness of transferred contracts on fresh tasks, the benefits of the development controller, and live self-improvement remain empirical-open coordinates.
