Date: 2026-08-25

Branch: `research/five-paper-math-r3-20260825`

Purpose: prevent new R5 mathematics from being reported with novelty broader than the proofs and current primary literature support.

## 1. Correction standard

For each paper, distinguish:

1. generic donor mathematics;
2. a model-specific theorem or construction;
3. finite exact evidence;
4. an unresolved transfer or production claim; and
5. a possible application that still lacks domain validation.

A theorem may remain useful after its generic mechanism is assigned to prior work. The correction is to ownership, not to truth.
