\section{Related work and boundary}

The paper sits between quantum resource estimation, analytical cost modeling, certified optimization, proof-carrying computation, and surrogate/static prediction. Closed-form estimates, restricted-family exact solvers, and compiler resource models all have extensive prior art. The contribution claimed here is not the generic idea of predicting cost without running a full optimizer.

The bounded residual is the explicit certificate layering demonstrated by the TARE refutation/repair chain: a theorem-backed exact restricted-family cost evaluator, a separately falsifiable structural explanation, prospectively staged predictions, hostile exact-referee tests, and row-level verification authority. A fresh literature pass must test whether an existing resource-estimation or certified-compiler framework already packages these components equivalently.

All underlying TARE, Pauli/symplectic, dynamic-programming, and donor-family machinery remains donor-owned or is credited to the companion technical results. QG2's claim is about forecasting/certification structure and its empirical demonstration.
