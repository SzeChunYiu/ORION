\begin{abstract}
Delegation logics, capability systems, attenuating credentials, provenance and formal agent authorization already prevent many forms of technical authority escalation. ORION Paper VIII therefore asks a different and stronger question: across typed scientific workflows, when can authority to construct state, verify an intermediate result, or use evidence compose into authority to promote a downstream scientific conclusion, and how must that authority be revoked after load-bearing evidence, representation, objective or evaluator change? Existing exhaustive evidence over 39,936 states and 169 ordered donor pairs remains a finite instance. P8-U seeks machine-checked scientific no-amplification, bridge, attenuation, fixed-point and exact-revocation laws, plus naturalistic evidence that they catch invalid scientific discharge missed by donor-complete authorization/provenance systems without excessive blocking. Every counterexample drives \textbf{Authority Bridge and Revocation Closure (ABRC)} toward a sharper authority primitive or impossibility boundary. The intended terminal is \textbf{GENERAL\_SCIENTIFIC\_AUTHORITY\_COMPOSITION\_AND\_REVOCATION\_THEOREM}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Donor boundary}
Authorization logics already formalize delegation and speaks-for relationships \cite{authlogic}; macaroons provide decentralized credentials whose caveats monotonically attenuate delegated authority \cite{macaroons2014}. Modern evidence/provenance-bound agent authorization raises this baseline further. P8 therefore does not claim first non-amplifying delegation, capability attenuation or authorization certificates.

The P8 residual is scientific responsibility composition: local technical permission or verification is insufficient when the downstream responsibility is broader than any evidence-bearing scientific scope actually discharged.

\section{Authority semantics}
An authority certificate binds
\[
(subject,target,responsibility,scope,support,context,evaluator,owner,time,revocation).
\]
Responsibilities include state construction, state-scope certification, downstream use, verification, scientific promotion and repair/revision. A producer cannot widen its own certificate by declaration.

Let $\preceq$ be a prospectively frozen scientific-scope relation. A bridge $b:s\Rightarrow t$ is evidence-bearing only when independently valid evidence establishes the semantic relation required to move from scope $s$ to $t$ for the requested responsibility.

\section{Target theorem family}
\begin{theorem}[Scientific no amplification]
For a well-founded typed workflow with complete declared load-bearing authority support, composition of locally valid certificates cannot authorize a final responsibility outside the closure of their scopes under explicitly valid bridges.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Bridge soundness]
Widening from scope $s$ to $t\not\preceq s$ requires an independently valid bridge whose own support and authority discharge the missing relation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Attenuation]
Compatible composition may preserve or narrow authority without new evidence, but cannot broaden it beyond bridge closure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Exact revocation]
Under complete load-bearing support/bridge information, a change revokes exactly the dependent authority obligations whose validity may change, while unaffected native scientific standing is preserved.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[State-interface non-amplification]
Composing responsibility-scoped state interfaces cannot produce authority for a responsibility unsupported by the composed declared scopes and bridges.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Hidden-support revocation limit]
If load-bearing support is unobservable, exact revocation cannot be guaranteed in general; sound enforcement must over-revoke or acquire additional support information.
\end{proposition}

Cycles are admitted only under a well-founded or independently justified fixed-point condition. Circular authority in which a verifier/owner depends on the very conclusion it authorizes remains blocked/\textsc{CannotCheck}.

\section{Authority Bridge and Revocation Closure}
ABRC is the ChatGPT-led negative-to-positive formal programme. Counterexamples are classified as missing scope relation, missing bridge, aggregation/non-monotonicity, cycle, temporal expiry, representation transport, state-responsibility mismatch, support-incomplete revocation, evaluator/owner circularity or genuine non-composability.

ABRC freezes each countermodel, derives the minimal evidence-bearing bridge or revocation condition that would make the target law sound, machine-checks the revised law, and tests it on a fresh workflow family. If no exact law is possible, ABRC proves the sharp information requirement or safe over-revocation boundary rather than hiding the failure.
