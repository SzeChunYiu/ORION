## Strongest allowed headline
> P8 establishes a complete finite composition law for cross-domain scientific authority over thirteen heterogeneous donor families. Native authority is conserved; all 169 registered ordered donor pairs compose when the scientific target is compatible, narrowed, or explicitly protectedly bridged; matched widening variants fail without that bridge, yielding a monotone non-widening law; blockers fail closed; complete independent support is exactly revocable; and an equally typed decentralized implementation is extensionally identical.

## Positive interpretation of the 169 widening failures
The matched widening cases are not negative composition results. They are countermodels establishing the non-widening direction of the registered authority law: native-valid local steps cannot silently mint broader scientific authority. Paired with 169 successful compatible compositions, they show both what composes and exactly what additional relation is required when the target widens.

## Positive interpretation of decentralized equivalence
The zero-mismatch decentralized-product result is a portability theorem. It shows that the scientific-discharge semantics are the contribution and can be deployed in a shared ORION calculus or a correctly integrated decentralized donor architecture without changing any registered authority judgment.

## Scope ceiling
The bounded 13-donor envelope does not itself establish deployed-agent safety, universal minimality of the five type coordinates, or global literature saturation. Those remain separate extension questions rather than limitations on the proved cross-domain composition, monotonicity, blocker, revocation, and portability laws.
