## Current official criteria captured for preflight

### Quantum

Official author guidance checked 2026-08-21:
- submission is via an arXiv reference and the preprint must be posted to or cross-listed with `quant-ph`;
- no fixed typesetting, format or length constraints;
- main results and assumptions should be stated clearly in the first couple of pages;
- selection emphasizes technical correctness, significance, clarity, and verifiability/reproducibility;
- correct but incremental work is below threshold;
- broad/interdisciplinary scope is explicitly not required;
- data/code publication under open licences is strongly encouraged where possible.

Primary source: `https://quantum-journal.org/instructions/authors/`.

### PRX Quantum

Official APS guidance checked 2026-08-21:
- highly selective, open-access QIST journal seeking outstanding and lasting impact;
- research articles have no length limit;
- manuscripts should be written for broad QIST research communities;
- APS/REVTeX source is preferred although PDF is sufficient for review;
- Data Availability Statement is required for published articles;
- supplemental material cannot carry information essential to understanding or convincing the paper;
- accessibility includes readable figures and non-color-only encodings;
- a nontechnical popular summary is required before publication.

Primary sources: `https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/scope`, `https://journals.aps.org/prxquantum/authors`.

### Quantum Science and Technology

Official IOP guidance checked 2026-08-21:
- highly selective across theoretical and experimental quantum science and technology;
- expects work to be essential reading for a subfield and of interest to the broader QST community, with lasting impact;
- supports single- or double-anonymous review at author choice;
- applies IOP research-data policy; relevant data may be supplementary or deposited/cited externally.

Primary source: `https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/journals/quantum-science-technology/about-quantum-science-technology/`.

### TMLR

Official TMLR guidance checked 2026-08-21:
- mandatory TMLR LaTeX style/template;
- double-blind review through OpenReview;
- primary acceptance question: are claims supported by accurate, convincing and clear evidence?;
- second question: would at least some TMLR readers be interested in the findings?;
- novelty/significance/SOTA are not necessary acceptance criteria;
- scope includes empirical/theoretical insight into intelligent systems, formalization of new tasks/performance assessment, analytical frameworks, reproducibility, and new approaches to understanding intelligent systems;
- no overlap with already archival peer-reviewed work; preprints are allowed.

Primary sources: `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html`, `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html`, `https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html`.

### Artificial Intelligence (AIJ)

Current public scope checked 2026-08-21:
- broad AI topics include automated reasoning/inference, knowledge representation, machine learning, multi-agent systems, planning/action, and reasoning under uncertainty;
- both achieved results and new ways of looking at AI problems are in scope, but must demonstrate value/effectiveness;
- application papers should emphasize principled AI contribution rather than conventional application only;
- challenge/competition summary papers are possible when they yield insights beyond the competition itself.

Primary public scope: `https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/artificial-intelligence/0004-3702`.
