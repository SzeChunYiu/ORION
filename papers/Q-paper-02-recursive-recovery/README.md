## One job

Q2 is the methodology paper. It asks whether a negative-result research programme can be made executable and auditable by freezing outcome rules prospectively, giving donor methods first right of refusal, retaining negative terminals, typing failure/authority state, and binding every result to replayable receipts.

It does **not** own Q1's TARE mathematics, Q3's dual-instrument benchmark claim, or Q4's typed-state mechanism claim. Those results appear only as bounded case studies showing how the methodology behaves when a candidate is absorbed, refuted, supported, or left unresolved.

`MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT_V1.md` remains the historical umbrella draft. The canonical rewrite separates Methods from programme outcomes and removes broad novelty language that cannot be authorized by the repository alone.

## Evidence spine

- prospectively frozen protocols with explicit honest terminals;
- donor first right of refusal and donor-absorption outcomes;
- proof-carrying exact optimization and serialized counterexamples where exactness matters;
- typed failure/authority records that keep orchestration status separate from scientific status;
- deterministic or digest-bound replay across the R6 and N-lane case studies;
- preserved negative history, including candidate hypotheses that failed or were donor-equivalent.

## Scope

This is a one-programme methodology study, not a statistical estimate of how often recursive recovery succeeds. No claim is made that receipt completeness implies evidence quality, that the method guarantees novelty, or that a negative should always be reopened.
