# Q2 — reproduction contract

Q2 is reproduced by reconstructing the method's **behavior across terminal types**, not by pooling its case studies into one performance score.

## Required verification classes

1. **Prospective freeze:** for each headline lane, verify that the protocol/gates precede the result-bearing receipt and that the allowed terminal vocabulary includes both positive and negative outcomes.
2. **Donor first refusal:** verify at least one donor absorption/closure where the candidate receives no novelty credit despite being implementable.
3. **Refutation preservation:** replay at least one exact counterexample from the R6 chain and confirm that later family enlargement does not erase the earlier negative.
4. **Typed authority:** confirm result/campaign records do not self-grant scientific, novelty, promotion, merge, or global-stop authority.
5. **Replay:** regenerate the canonical receipt outputs listed in `RECEIPT_INDEX.md` or independently recompute their decisive fields.
6. **Protected custody:** confirm the protected stretched-N2 subject remains unread in the lanes that claim it remained sealed.

## Headline case-study families

- R6 donor/closure/refutation chain: `development/orion-q-max-r0/` and `research/extensions/orion-q/`;

Q1, Q3, and Q4 own the scientific interpretation of their respective result sets. Q2 checks that the methodology recorded those outcomes honestly.

## Failure semantics

`CANNOT_CHECK` is not converted into `NOT_SUPPORTED`. A donor tie is not a candidate positive. A receipt replay does not prove that the underlying scientific hypothesis is true; it proves that the reported computation/provenance can be reconstructed under the stated contract.
