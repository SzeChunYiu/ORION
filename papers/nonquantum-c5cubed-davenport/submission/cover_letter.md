Dear Editors of The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics,

Please consider “A Width-One Corridor for Generalized Davenport Constants of
\(C_5^3\)” by Sze Chun Yiu.

The paper proves that (5k+10\le D_k(C_5^3)\le5k+11) for all (k\ge4), together
with a conditional exact tail if the lower value occurs at (k=4). It then
derives a saturation-defect lemma, an exact multiplicity grammar, a theorem-
grade support-at-least-eleven result, a rank-forcing phase, and an atom-overlap
identity for the remaining length-31 obstruction. The unresolved alternative is
stated throughout. A larger search through support 22 is labeled bounded
computational evidence and is not used as theorem authority.

We believe this structural compression of a rank-three generalized-Davenport
problem is within E-JC’s scope and is self-contained for specialist review.
