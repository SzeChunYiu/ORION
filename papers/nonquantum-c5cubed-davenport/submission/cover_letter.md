Dear Editors of The Electronic Journal of Combinatorics,

Please consider “Conditional Width-One Bounds for Generalized Davenport
Constants of \(C_5^3\)” by Sze Chun Yiu.

Conditional on four declared exact inputs, the paper derives
\(5k+10\le D_k(C_5^3)\le5k+11\) for all \(k\ge4\), together with a conditional
exact tail if the lower value occurs at \(k=4\). It then derives a
saturation-defect lemma, an exact multiplicity grammar, a rank-forcing phase,
and an atom-overlap identity for the remaining length-31 obstruction. Exact
searches through support ten and a larger search through support 22 are labeled
bounded computational evidence pending external replay and are not used as
theorem authority.

We believe this explicitly conditional structural analysis of a rank-three
generalized-Davenport problem is within E-JC’s scope.
