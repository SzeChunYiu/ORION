## 1. Authority boundary

This lane is a **publication-synthesis and verification lane**. It may reorganize, narrow, explain, cite, audit, render, and package claims already supported by committed ORION evidence. It must not turn publication work into scientific authority.

Rules:

1. **Evidence before prose.** Manuscript text cannot create a result, theorem, novelty fact, or experimental authority.
2. **Argument before sections.** Each paper must have an explicit central question, answer, evidence chain, counterevidence/negative history, boundary, and durable conclusion before prose is rewritten.
3. **Section contracts before paragraphs.** Every section gets purpose, inputs, allowed claims, forbidden claims, required evidence, and validation checks.
4. **No silent claim upgrade.** `MACHINE-EVIDENCED`, finite-domain, diagnostic, forecast-only, bounded, `OPEN`, `CANNOT_CHECK`, null and negative states retain those meanings.
5. **Negative results remain first class.** A refuted hypothesis may become the center of a paper if it reveals the scientifically important boundary; it may never be rewritten as a positive result.
6. **Publication cut is immutable unless explicitly advanced.** Results from unmerged successor branches (for example open QG theorem/tightness/phase branches) do not enter the paper merely because they exist. A later advance of the cut requires a fresh synchronization pass.
7. **Downstream derivatives grant zero scientific authority.** Slides, reconstructed slide graphics, patent drafts, cover letters, reviewer responses and visual polish cannot feed evidence back into the scientific claim ledger.
8. **External novelty requires external evidence.** Repository donor-subtraction is necessary but not sufficient for a submission-date novelty statement; primary literature must be searched and read again near submission.

## 2. Dynamic expert panel

The review pool is selected by failure mode rather than reused mechanically. Up to five independent roles may be active for a major closure decision:

- **Quantum compilation / quantum algorithms theorist** — grammar definitions, donor ownership, theorem scope, physical-resource interpretation.
- **Exact algorithms / formal-methods reviewer** — DP/referee correctness, machine-checked theorem boundaries, witness validity, replay and independence.
- **Scientific-method / reproducibility reviewer** — prospective freeze, custody, negative-result retention, independent replay, claim-to-receipt traceability.
- **Statistics / empirical-design reviewer** — experimental unit, panel independence, uncertainty, post-hoc versus preregistered analyses, timing claims and finite-domain generalization.
- **Novelty / journal editor** — nearest-work saturation, contribution class, overlap among Q/QG papers, target fit, manuscript architecture and decision risk.

Each review must distinguish evidence-backed findings, plausible but unverified interpretation, missing information, and recommendations that would change scope.

## 5. Foundation files required per paper

Each paper will receive or internally reconstruct the following logical objects, whether stored as separate files or embedded in its publication ledger:

1. `scope` — target paper identity, audience, claim ceiling, publication cut, exclusions.
2. `research_canon` — immutable scientific facts/results, definitions, negative history and explicit unknowns.
3. `evidence_table` — `claim | source/receipt | evidence class | strength | section | risk | status`.
4. `argument_map` — tension/question -> answer -> evidence dependencies -> counterarguments -> limitations -> final move.
5. `section_contracts` — purpose, required inputs, allowed claims, forbidden claims, validation.
6. `style/terminology ledger` — canonical grammar/family names, symbols, authority vocabulary and forbidden promotional phrases.

## 6. Closure loop for every paper

Run recursively in this order:

1. **Evidence synchronization.** Reconcile manuscript/claim ledger with committed receipts and the publication cut.
2. **Nearest-work search.** Search current field sources and contradictory/limiting literature; obtain and read load-bearing papers.
3. **Donor Paper Cards.** Build deep cards for the strongest donors and nearest analogues.
4. **Foundation rebuild.** Canon -> evidence table -> argument map -> section contracts.
5. **Claim surgery.** Remove, narrow, split or promote only where authority permits; distinguish theorem, exact counterexample, finite-domain evidence, forecast and open question.
6. **Manuscript reconstruction.** Rewrite title/abstract/intro/results/discussion/conclusion from the argument map, preserving author voice and negative evidence.
7. **Statistics/data/figure pass.** Audit experimental units and uncertainty, create data/code availability plan, build/repair figures from the evidence contract.
8. **Citation/reference pass.** Atomic claim-to-source support, then independent metadata verification.
9. **Three-blind-review pass.** Validity; novelty/positioning; reproducibility/generalization. Freeze reports before synthesis.
10. **Minimum-sufficient repair.** Prefer reanalysis, clarification, claim narrowing/removal or target transfer over unnecessary new experiments.
11. **Polish + consistency sweep.** Terminology, numbers, units, tense, cross-references, figure/table values and layout.
12. **Submission validation.** Every factual claim cited, every headline claim in evidence ledger, every display regenerable/bound, exact journal mechanics resolved, PDF visually inspected.
13. **Stop or reopen.** Stop on peer-review-ready bounded claim; reopen science only for a decision-relevant missing discriminator, never because smoother prose would be easier than reporting an evidence gap.

## 7. Stopping rules

A manuscript revision loop stops when any applies:

- maximum three publication-revision rounds for the same stable evidence cut;
- two consecutive review-score improvements below 0.5;
- a key claim requires missing evidence rather than writing repair;
- specialist conflict cannot be resolved honestly from available evidence;
- the bounded target claim has reached the paper's peer-review-ready definition.

When stopped, record current status, remaining problem, reason for stopping, and exactly one recommended next scientific/publication action.

## 9. Definition of done

A Q/QG paper is `PEER_REVIEW_READY` only when:

- scientific scope and publication cut are explicit;
- manuscript and claim ledger agree with every load-bearing receipt;
- nearest-work search has no unresolved route capable of collapsing the headline residual;
- negative/null/refuted outcomes remain visible and correctly typed;
- every headline claim has a decision proof and evidence pointer;
- statistics/reporting is honest about units and finite-domain limits;
- figures/tables are evidence-bound and visually audited;
- bibliography metadata is verified;
- data/code/reproduction statements point to real artifacts only;
- three mutually blind mock reviews have no unresolved central blocking concern, or the claim has been narrowed until that is true;
- language/layout polish does not change scientific strength;
- exact target-journal package and final rendered PDF pass their checks.

External peer review, acceptance, author declarations and real submission IDs remain later human/editorial events.
