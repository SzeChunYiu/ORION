Dear Editors,

Please consider “Conditional Davenport Corridors and Saturated Obstructions in
\(C_5^3\)” by Sze Chun Yiu.

Using cited recurrence, lower-bound, Property C, and short-subsequence
results, and conditional only on H1: \(D_3(C_5^3)=25\) and H2:
\(s_{\le6}(C_5^3)=24\), the paper derives
\(5k+10\le D_k(C_5^3)\le5k+11\) for all \(k\ge4\), together with a conditional
exact tail if the lower value occurs at \(k=4\). It then derives a
saturation-defect lemma, an exact multiplicity grammar, a rank-forcing phase,
and an atom-overlap identity for the remaining length-31 obstruction. Exact
searches through support ten are labeled bounded computational evidence pending
external replay and are not used in any analytic proof.

The manuscript is offered as an explicitly conditional research note; it does
not claim to determine \(D_4(C_5^3)\).
