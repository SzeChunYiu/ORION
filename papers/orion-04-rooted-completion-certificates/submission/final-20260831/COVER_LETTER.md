Please consider our manuscript, "A one-unit generalized-Davenport corridor and a support-at-least-14 theorem in C_5^3," for a regular research article.

The paper studies the remaining low-index uncertainty for generalized Davenport constants in `C_5^3`. It proves a one-unit corridor, gives a saturation/multiplicity reduction for a hypothetical length-31 obstruction, and combines a committed parent exclusion through support 10 with a complete exact replay at supports 11-13. The resulting computer-assisted theorem is deliberately bounded: any remaining obstruction has support at least 14.

The manuscript does **not** determine `D_4(C_5^3)`, does not promote the exploratory search through support 22, and does not claim external independent replication. Those limits are stated in the abstract, theorem discussion, claim ledger, and submission package. The related-work companion likewise avoids priority language and treats the retrieved literature as novelty subtraction rather than certification.

The computational component is exact and finite at the stated supports, with two in-repository exact state representations, a non-generating checker, deterministic bindings, and hostile semantic mutations. The paper presents those controls only as support for the bounded theorem, not as a substitute for the unresolved global proof.
