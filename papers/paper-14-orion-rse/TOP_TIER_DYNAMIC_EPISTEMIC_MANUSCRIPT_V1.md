P14 treats governance as an intervention on acquisition, diagnosis, experiment selection, reopening, promotion, and stopping decisions over a dynamic epistemic state. It absorbs AI-for-science benchmarks, sequential testing, online error control, metascience, safety cases, and research evaluation.
