## Evidence corrections

### P14A gate attainability

`P14_GATE_ATTAINABILITY_ADJUDICATION_V1.md` records that both of P14A's failing gates read one quantity — the prevalence of the single fact state on which `MULTI_REVIEW` and the full contract disagree — whose supremum over P14A's own declared sampling support is `0.042326`, below both the `0.05` and the `0.08` bar. No admissible draw could have satisfied either, and the seven-gate conjunction had one reachable value. The P14A protocol, seed, thresholds, receipt and terminal are retained verbatim and nothing is relabelled positive; the correction is to the evidential reading, which becomes `CANNOT_CHECK`.

The same two thresholds, registered verbatim and read on the P14C benchmark where the corresponding quantity is `0.142857`, are both met, and the `0.08` margin bar is genuinely refutable there — it fails for the donor, interaction and negative-history ablations.

### P14B

`P14B_PROTOCOL_CONFORMANCE_CORRECTION_V1.md` records two independent authority blockers. First, the original full arm reused the same adjudication function that generated gold, making it circular. Second, hostile PR review found that the runner did not realize the protocol's promised random nuisance reminting across all strata. P14B is retained as diagnostic development history only; its numerical separation is not used to authorize the paper claim.

A third reading, added 2026-08-22 and recorded under `p14b` in `P14_GATE_ATTAINABILITY_ADJUDICATION_V1.json`: P14B's terminal is *reachable in both directions* — the four component ablations its own protocol registers, placed in the graded slot, make the conjunction print both its words, and no threshold sits outside its statistic's reach. That half is a genuine property P14A's terminal lacked. But only four of its eight gates could have gone either way. `full_discovery_recall_one` is satisfied by all nine arms in every admissible run (exactly three of the 256 fact assignments are promotable by gold and no registered policy declines any of them) and `matched_budget` reads a module literal assigned identically to all nine arms; both are hypothesis gates. The remaining two unconditional gates are preconditions, for which holding everywhere is the intended behaviour. Disposition: `TERMINAL_REACHABLE__GATE_COUNT_INFLATED`. Nothing frozen is edited and the non-authoritative downgrade stands.

### P14C replay

The P14C V1 runner evaluated the registered scientific/conformance gates but did not put the protocol's two-run byte-identity requirement into the runner terminal. The V1 terminal is therefore non-authoritative alone. `verify_p14c_protocol_adjudication_v2.py` runs the exact unchanged V1 runner plus frozen adjudication table twice in fresh subprocess directories, verifies every original gate, and requires byte identity before emitting the authoritative terminal. Both complete result payloads hash to `74032348de7e6508b6c1827aabcf1bf9d354d30b9c6f81c8259fdb3535f01a63`.

No case, gold label, policy, comparator, threshold, metric, or result was changed by this correction.
