# P14 top-tier promotion V1 — Scientific Governance as a Research-Decision Machine

## Maximum claim to earn

> **Scientific governance can improve research decisions, not merely documentation.** Under matched model, information, tool and budget access, a fail-closed recursive governance process that subtracts donor ownership, freezes discriminators, preserves negative/null/subsumed evidence, separates evaluator from authority, and reopens under regime change reduces false novelty and unsupported claim widening without materially suppressing independently judged useful discovery.

Reflection, debate, multi-agent review, preregistration/checklists, literature-RAG, provenance, autonomous research agents and generic truth maintenance are donors.

## Benchmark object

Build blinded research packets requiring systems to:

1. formulate or refine a claim;
2. identify nearest work/donors;
3. choose a protected discriminator;
4. select/interpret an experiment or evidence packet;
5. adjudicate positive/null/negative/subsumed/non-identifiable outcomes;
6. decide split/subsume/reopen/stop;
7. produce a final allowed claim + evidence ledger.

Packets must not reveal ORION disposition vocabulary as gold labels to candidate systems.

## Comparator stack

Run at matched model/tool/web/token/time budgets:

- strong raw research agent;
- reflection/self-critique;
- multi-agent debate/reviewer workflow;
- preregistration/checklist-only workflow;
- literature-RAG + experiment/reasoning workflow;
- strongest practical composed donor workflow;
- ORION-RSE;
- component ablations: no donor subtraction, no negative history, no protected freeze, no authority separation, no recursion stops, no reopen semantics.

## Protected packet families

At minimum include:

- apparent positive subsumed by stronger donor;
- interaction-only effect falsely decomposed into atoms;
- broad novelty already owned despite positive toy result;
- meaningful null with live parent question;
- leaky/corrupted benchmark;
- valid science with unrelated infrastructure failure;
- evidence/regime change invalidating an old certificate/negative;
- genuinely non-identifiable question requiring `CANNOT_CHECK`;
- valid novel residual that should be promoted;
- case where over-conservative governance would lose a useful discovery.

Use both exact hidden-contract cases and realistic multi-domain packets.

## Independent authority design

The final allowed claim and novelty/support judgments must be established independently from candidate workflows through:

- deterministic contracts/verifiers for controlled cases;
- blinded domain experts for technical realistic cases where feasible;
- blinded independent model panels only as supplementary authority;
- adjudication protocol that records disagreement and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

Candidate ORION terminology must not influence scoring.

## Longitudinal value test

Run at least two research rounds on linked cases. Round 2 changes evidence, donor field or regime. Test whether preserved negative/subsumed history improves later decisions versus memoryless or summary-only baselines.

This is necessary to establish that negative-history retention has scientific utility rather than archival value only.

## Primary outcomes

Report separately and jointly:

- false novelty-claim rate;
- unsupported claim-widening rate;
- protocol/endpoint drift;
- donor-subsumption detection;
- negative/null preservation;
- correct `CANNOT_CHECK`;
- reproducibility/evidence completeness;
- independent reviewer agreement on final allowed claim;
- useful-discovery recall/precision;
- time/token/tool/reviewer cost;
- longitudinal correction/reopen quality.

No single scalar may hide a collapse in useful discovery. A Pareto analysis between scientific defensibility, productivity and cost is mandatory.

## Strongest hostile attacks

- benchmark cases are copied from ORION rules and leak the answer;
- evaluator rewards ORION vocabulary/style;
- ORION wins by abstaining from claims;
- donor search receives more access than comparators;
- multi-agent comparator receives weaker models or less budget;
- candidate workflow and final judge share hidden state;
- negative history improves score but not later research decisions;
- recursive decomposition adds bureaucracy without scientific benefit;
- results hold only on synthetic hidden-contract cases;
- external reviewers cannot reproduce ORION dispositions.
