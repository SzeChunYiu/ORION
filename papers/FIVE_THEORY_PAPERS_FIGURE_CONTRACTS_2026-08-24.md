# Five theory papers — claim-driven figure contracts

These are planning-stage evidence contracts under `academic-paper-skills/nature-figure`. They specify scientific roles, not journal dimensions or production formats. Exact sizing/export rules wait for an exact journal, article type and submission stage.

## Paper A

1. **Constraint-rank mechanism:** one frame, partner, shared Tags, per-coordinate signatures, and zero-signature deletion. Role: orient the reader to the proof mechanism.
2. **Two-axis theorem map:** horizontal `b` controls Restore sensitivity `b-1`; vertical realized rank controls support ceiling. Overlay the proof-validity inequality `mu >= (b-1)t_R`. Role: show why block count and constraint count affect different theorem coordinates.
3. **Sharp specialization:** R6M support-one obstruction versus support-two theorem. Role: visually separate general upper certificate from the one specialization with intrinsic sharpness.

## Paper B

1. **Three support quantities:** intrinsic `kappa`, normalization ceiling, proof-system `beta`. Role: prevent category confusion before numerical examples.
2. **Tight versus loose controls:** R6M `2=2` beside R6I `1<5`, with proof moves shown schematically. Role: decisive conceptual comparison.
3. **Product amplification:** `t` versus `5t` and gap `4t`, explicitly labelled as registered direct-enumeration support budgets rather than a complexity-class lower bound.

## Paper C

1. **Decision certificate:** all set partitions in the background, four-index pair/two-pair clauses in the foreground, with the exact `m=4` falsifier as boundary inset.
2. **Same pair information, different optimum:** one C2 gadget pair showing identical weights/pair matrix but triple-versus-pair optimizer structure and value gap.
3. **Boolean-lattice kernel:** cube/lattice view of proper upper marginals and the alternating parity kernel. Role: make the Möbius minimality theorem inspectable.
4. **Information hierarchy:** decision, value and optimizer as separate outputs versus information order supplied. Role: synthesis, not a new dataset.

## Paper D

1. **Authority before/after falsifier:** seeds, rules, direct refutation and least-fixed-point survivors. Role: central formal object.
2. **Cycle controls:** unsupported cycle stays unauthorized; seeded cycle closes. Role: explain why least fixed point removes the acyclicity assumption safely.
3. **Two falsifier cases:** TARE `10<11` and Paper C pair/value separation, with retained versus retracted claim types. Role: show cross-family applicability without implying the same scientific claim.

## Non-quantum

1. **One-unit Davenport corridor:** lower and upper lines `5k+10`, `5k+11`, exact `D_2,D_3`, and the `D_4 in {30,31}` bifurcation. Role: the paper's central problem in one figure.
2. **Saturation-defect grammar:** multiplicity 1/2/4 support points and their forced local certificates; multiplicity 3 crossed out. Role: turn the symbolic lemma into a reusable object.
3. **Evidence-typed support frontier:** proven support<=10 exclusion, bounded-computational support 11–22 frontier, and open support>=23 region rendered with visibly different authority styles. Role: prevent readers from mistaking computation for theorem.

## Shared QA

- No figure may encode a stronger claim than its claim ledger.
- Negative/falsifying examples stay visible in the main figure sequence.
- Machine-evidence panels must identify finite versus all-size authority in legends.
- Repository paths, test names and PR history belong in reproducibility documentation, not figure artwork.
- Final designs require analogue-paper calibration and visual inspection at physical size after the exact target is chosen.
