## Proposed title

**Typed Scientific Epistemic State Under Partial Knowledge: Six Matched-Information Mechanism Studies**

Alternative broader title:

**When Scientific State Needs Types: Controlled Studies of Decision-Making Under Partial Knowledge**

## One-sentence result

Across six prospectively frozen exact-synthetic studies in which visible information is matched, explicit bindings for type, scope, uncertainty, transport history, decision relevance and remint status change research decisions relative to untyped or decision-agnostic controls, while strong donor and no-value regimes bound where the mechanisms add nothing.

## Significance statement

Agent-memory systems increasingly expose provenance, stale-state handling and value-of-information mechanisms, so the important question is no longer whether such primitives exist. ORION-04 isolates whether *scientific decision state* changes outcomes when the facts themselves are held fixed. Six controlled worlds separately test type-conditioned priors, scope-bound reopening, Pareto-targeted verification, full-chain transport obligations, decision-coupled acquisition and representation-remint status. Exact donor and no-value controls prevent the paper from turning those effects into a universal typed-memory or policy claim.

## Editor pitch

We study a narrow but increasingly important component of scientific agents: how partial knowledge is represented at decision time. In six prospectively frozen exact-synthetic studies, all non-oracle arms receive the same serialized facts and differ only in whether scientific applicability/provenance/uncertainty/transport/decision-role information is represented and consumed explicitly. The typed/scoped mechanisms improve the registered decision objective in the regimes designed to require them, while exact first-right-of-refusal controls show zero added value where the mechanism should not help. Two additional donor/negative studies further restrict the interpretation. We make no priority claim for typed memory, stale-memory handling or value-of-information, all of which have active neighboring literatures; the contribution is the matched-information scientific-decision mechanism suite and its reusable frozen benchmark worlds.
