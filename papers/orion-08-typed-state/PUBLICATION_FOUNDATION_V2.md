## 00 — Scope

### Scientific object
A mechanism-isolation study asking whether **the type and scope attached to partial knowledge changes what a research process may rationally reuse, verify, reopen, transport, probe or act upon** under matched information/resource conditions.

### Fresh donor pressure
- STALE and follow-up stale-memory work own broad implicit staleness detection, belief retirement and downstream adaptation in agent memory.
- ContextNest owns substantial versioned/provenanced context-governance machinery beneath retrieval.
- VOI, active learning, Pareto/interval decision making, provenance, temporal invalidation and retrieval are mature donor primitives.

ORION-04 therefore does **not** claim any one of those mechanisms in isolation. Its residual is the cross-family result that **typed/scoped state is the load-bearing information boundary under deliberately matched hostile alternatives**, across several distinct downstream responsibilities.

### Claim ceiling
Exact-synthetic mechanism evidence only. No deployment, real quantum pipeline, real-adversary security, generic LLM-capability, cryptographic, or universal active-learning claim.

## 03 — Argument map

### Tension
Partial knowledge is often represented as a set of facts plus confidence/relevance. But research decisions depend on more than whether a datum is present or likely true: **what kind of claim is it, what scope does it support, what dependency/epoch does it belong to, and what downstream responsibility is trying to consume it?**

### Central question
Across distinct research-control decisions, does adding typed/scope-bearing state create value that matched-information untyped or locally sufficient alternatives cannot recover?

### Central answer
Across six frozen exact-synthetic stress worlds, the registered typed/scoped mechanisms pass their hostile controls and outperform matched alternatives exactly where the missing type/scope distinction is decision-relevant; when a donor already has the relevant information/policy, the advantage disappears and is retained as a negative. The result is a **mechanism-isolation synthesis**, not a deployment theorem.

### Evidence sequence
1. Define the typed/scoped-state thesis and what counts as matched information.
2. Hidden-structure/VOI world: type affects what to probe.
3. Staleness/reopening world: scope affects what remains usable.
4. Interval/Pareto world: decision geometry affects what to verify.
5. Transport/laundering world: chain scope/provenance affects whether evidence survives composition.
6. Active-experiment world: decision relevance beats entropy under decoy uncertainty.
7. Remint world: typed invalidation balances reuse and re-derivation.
8. First-right-of-refusal negatives: ideal VOI and model-selection donors erase broader policy/predictor claims.
9. Synthesis across responsibility classes; explicit synthetic-world boundary.

### Strongest alternative interpretation
**"Six bespoke worlds simply encode the answer into the type system."** The paper must expose each world's information parity, hostile trap, first-right-of-refusal control and non-degeneracy gate. The claim is mechanism sufficiency *within those constructed worlds*, not spontaneous discovery of types in natural data.

### Durable conclusion
Typed/scoped state can matter even when raw information is unchanged because downstream decisions ask different questions of that information; however, the result is bounded to constructed mechanism-isolation worlds and disappears where an ideal donor already carries the same decision-relevant structure.
