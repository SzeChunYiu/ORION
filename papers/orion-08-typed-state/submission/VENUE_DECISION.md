# Venue decision — ORION-08

**Target: TMLR.**

The routing allowed TMLR or the strongest methodology venue. TMLR is the
strongest venue this paper can defend, and the reason is in its own terminal:
every result is bounded to exact-synthetic mechanism isolation. The worlds are
constructed so that typing is the only thing that varies; there is no real
domain instantiation. A venue that selects for demonstrated impact would be
judging the paper on a property it deliberately does not have.

What the paper does have is the property TMLR selects for. The comparisons are
matched-information by construction, the strongest available comparison gets
first right of refusal, each world contains a hostile regime designed to expose
the shortcut the typing is meant to prevent, and the studies where the
candidate does *not* win are reported at equal prominence.

**Article type.** Regular submission, 6 pages.

**Fallback.** None required. A real-domain instantiation would extend venue
reach, but it is a new experiment, and the bounded mechanism-isolation claim
does not need it.
