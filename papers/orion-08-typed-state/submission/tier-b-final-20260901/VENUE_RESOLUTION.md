# Venue and article-type resolution

**Resolved on:** 2026-09-01
**Journal:** Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)
**Article type:** research paper
**Review model:** double blind through OpenReview
**arXiv route:** attributed `cs.AI`, with `cs.LG` cross-list if accepted

The current TMLR author guide requires anonymous review material and complete private OpenReview profiles, conflicts and submission metadata. The journal screens scope, anonymity and completeness before review; reviewers and an action editor then evaluate the paper and any revision. The final journal PDF and source archive are anonymous, while the arXiv PDF and source archive are attributed. Portal profiles, conflicts, editor selection, declarations and the actual filing remain author-controlled.

The paper fits TMLR as a controlled machine-learning and decision-systems research paper: it presents matched-information mechanism studies, a finite decision criterion used at donor scope, and bounded real-data transfer with adverse results retained. It does not seek a deployed-agent or broad AI-for-science performance route.

Official source checked live: https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html

This routing decision is not an acceptance prediction and grants no authority beyond the V4 claim ledger.
