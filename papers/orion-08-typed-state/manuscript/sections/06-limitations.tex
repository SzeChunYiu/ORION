\section{Limitations}

The six primary mechanism studies are exact synthetic. Their enumerable truth and cleanly typed state provide internal control but omit the extraction and misclassification errors faced by real systems. The three real-data studies construct bindings from existing metadata rather than observing a deployed research agent, so they do not close that external-validity gap.

The arms labelled fixed-heuristic proxies are fixed heuristics by construction and measure no deployed model. No conclusion about current or future language models follows. The certificate-transport family does not study cryptographic attackers; its hashes are construction identifiers and its completeness statement assumes end-to-end receipts cannot be forged consistently within the world definition.

The verification family reports scalarized regret, not full Pareto-front quality. The reopening family excludes intra-episode receipt accrual, and two of its comparisons are undetermined rather than positive: scoped versus never reopening has a paired interval containing zero in both regimes (Table~\ref{tab:paired-uncertainty}), so the study is not entitled to a scoped-versus-never claim in either direction. The programme has no lower-bound or impossibility theorem showing that typing is necessary for every mechanism family.

Reported intervals are per-comparison on the mean estimand, and the family-wise correction supported by the preserved analysis has been executed rather than left open: exact two-sided sign tests over the published win/loss counts, Holm--Bonferroni-adjusted across the twelve-comparison family (\texttt{analysis/familywise-multiplicity-v1/}), leave all nine interval-positive comparisons surviving and the prespecified tie regime as the only non-survivor. Corrected bootstrap intervals on the mean remain non-recomputable, because the preserved publication analysis stores summary statistics rather than paired difference vectors, and the sign test addresses a different and more conservative estimand --- direction reliability rather than the mean --- so it upgrades no row: the two contrasts whose mean intervals contain zero stay undetermined. Targeted verification and decision-coupled acquisition against the fixed-heuristic proxy are the surviving contrasts closest to zero relative to their width.

The real-data evidence is also limited. OpenML was corrected after outcome inspection and is descriptive. The Defects4J binding was amended after fibre-size inspection, although before catch outcomes, and the project count is twelve. WorkflowHub contributes one corpus and retains a cannot-assess conclusion for the missing no-value contrast. Neither rows nor bugs are independent cross-domain replications. Multiple positive synthetic families likewise do not constitute independent populations, and no prevalence claim follows. All worlds, gold labels, audits and adjudications in this programme are same-programme and single-author; no result reported here has been checked by an independent implementation or an out-of-programme reviewer.

Finally, the earlier shorthand for refinement strictness was too broad and has been withdrawn. The corrected theorem concerns simultaneous optimality across refined subfibres on a fixed distribution. It does not say that a refinement transfers out of sample, remains useful under drift, or supplies a minimal or complete epistemic-state schema.
