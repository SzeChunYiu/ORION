## Corrected and withdrawn wording

The earlier Theorem 2 sentence, "strictly exactly when a refinement splits an action-impure fibre," was underspecified and is withdrawn. Merely splitting an impure coarse fibre is not sufficient. The correct condition is simultaneous optimality across the refined subfibres, as stated in O8V4-8. The historical V1 result files and terminals are retained, but their language is not publication authority.
