## Frozen boundary

The scope is **exact-synthetic and unchanged**. No deployment claim and no novelty
claim is made. The formal literature already settles that the epistemic types are
distinct in principle; what this paper adds is whether that distinction is
observable in a running mechanism under matched information. It does not show that
any real system implements the distinction, nor that the observed separations
transfer outside the frozen synthetic families.

Content binding is recorded as `BOUND_PARTIAL / PASS`, unchanged by this freeze.

A real-system study under matched information is successor work. It must not
retroactively promote the exact-synthetic result, and a tie observed here must not
be reported later as a failure of the mechanism under test.
