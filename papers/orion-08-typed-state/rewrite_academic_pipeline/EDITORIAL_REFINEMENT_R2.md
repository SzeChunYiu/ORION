## 1. Editorial diagnosis

The final master has the correct scientific ceiling and should not be replaced by a broader architecture paper. Its strongest contribution is a **matched-information evaluation method** that separates factual payload from epistemic bindings and then gives stronger donors the same structured state. The six studies are evidence blocks, not six independent votes for a universal ontology.

The remaining desk risks are:

1. the abstract is numerically dense before the reader sees the common experimental logic;
2. a reader may misread the post-completion taxonomy as a preregistered six-axis theory;
3. the large information-gain comparison can overshadow the stronger deterministic donor that absorbs most policy novelty;
4. exact constructed-chain counts can be misread as cryptographic-security rates;
5. synthetic mechanism isolation can be mistaken for real scientific-agent transfer.

## 3. Refined abstract for adoption

Scientific decision systems can receive the same factual propositions yet behave differently because those facts are bound to different information about applicability, transformation history, uncertainty, or decision role. We test whether such **epistemic bindings** change decisions when factual payload and primary non-oracle resources are otherwise matched. Six separately frozen exact-synthetic studies examine type-conditioned acquisition, applicability scope, decision-relevant verification, transformation lineage, decision-coupled probing, and evidence reminting. The studies were not preregistered as one omnibus theory and are not pooled.

The experiments identify both binding value and policy absorption. Type-conditioned value-of-information improves mean utility by 1.111 over the same planner with a uniform prior. Scope-aware reopening prevents severe over-reopening, while its comparisons with a conservative never-reopen policy remain compatible with zero. Targeting verification to unresolved decision ambiguity reduces scalarized regret by 0.142 relative to matched-budget random verification. Full-chain checking detects all 200 constructed invalid chains, including 68 deep splices, with no false positives on 200 honest chains. Decision-coupled acquisition improves utility by 2.146 over pure information gain, but a stronger deterministic proxy absorbs most of that margin. Typed reminting improves utility by 2.264 in the mixed-transport regime and ties re-derivation exactly when transport has no value.

The bounded conclusion is representational rather than architectural: relations that determine whether information is applicable, transportable, or decision-relevant can change scientific decisions even when the factual payload is fixed. Strong donors and no-value controls correspondingly narrow policy novelty. All headline studies are synthetic; real-agent transfer, minimality of the six axes, and a universal scientific-state schema remain unestablished.

## 4. Introduction refinement

The first page should follow this dependency chain:

1. same factual payload does not imply same usable scientific state;
2. ordinary comparisons often change payload and representation together;
3. matched-information evaluation isolates the relation;
4. stronger donors test whether the residual belongs to state or policy;
5. six separately frozen studies instantiate the method;
6. synthesis is descriptive, not an omnibus preregistration.

Keep the research question as a displayed or indented sentence. Immediately after it, state the two nonclaims:

- the paper does not propose a minimal scientific-state ontology;
- the paper does not establish real-agent performance.

This early boundary reduces the risk that reviewers spend the report correcting a claim the paper does not make.

Where semantic type is itself the manipulated information, say that the test measures its decision value under an otherwise identical planner rather than pretending the inputs are byte-identical.

Repair: keep the strongest donor in each result block and make the acquisition absorption a headline control.

### “Exact counts imply external security or reliability.”

Repair: state that constructed-chain counts establish behavior on the complete registered corpus, not an open-population detection rate.

### “Synthetic evidence does not matter.”

Repair: frame the purpose as mechanism isolation and benchmark design. The decisive broader extension is a real-domain matched-information study, not rhetorical generalization.
