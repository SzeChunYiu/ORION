Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, "Typed Epistemic State under Partial Knowledge: Six Matched-Information Mechanism Studies," as an original research article.

The paper asks a narrow question. Research systems carry partial knowledge that is not a world fact: feasibility beliefs, failed interfaces, uncertain costs, transported certificates, unresolved questions and representation edits. We ask whether explicitly typing and scoping that epistemic state changes decisions when competing mechanisms receive the same visible information. Matching information across arms is what makes the comparison about typing rather than about access.

Six prospectively frozen exact-synthetic studies isolate one state operation at a time. Type-conditioned value-of-information planning recovers 71% of oracle utility and exceeds an identical uniform-prior planner. Verification targeted at interval-dominance ambiguity yields 0.1096 mean regret against 0.2518 for random verification at the same budget. Full-chain transport checking detects all constructed laundering cases at recall 1.000 and false-positive rate 0.000, including 68 deep splices that defeat last-hop inspection.

One result is reported as not separated. Scope-bound receipt reopening avoids wasteful global reopening by a wide paired margin, but its advantage over never reopening at all is not distinguishable from zero at this sample size, and we do not claim it. The studies are exact-synthetic by construction, which buys isolation of a single state operation at the cost of external validity; the manuscript states that boundary rather than arguing around it.

The work should interest TMLR readers concerned with epistemic state in agents, value-of-information planning and evidence provenance. Formal work establishing that ignorance is not a single notion, and that disbelieving ignorance is not definable in terms of knowledge, is cited as the ground this paper builds on; what that literature does not settle is whether the distinction is observable in a running system, which is what these studies test.

The submission uses the official double-blind TMLR style. The manuscript discloses language-model assistance and states the authors' responsibility for all scientific content. No human participants, animals, clinical data or personal data are involved.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. Human filing metadata, declarations and the final archival deposit will be verified in the submission portal by the authors.

Thank you for your consideration.
