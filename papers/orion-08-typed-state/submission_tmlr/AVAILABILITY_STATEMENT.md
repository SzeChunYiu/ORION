# Data and code availability

The six studies are exact-synthetic. Each instance is constructed from a frozen specification rather than sampled from an external corpus, so the study scripts and their specifications fully determine every reported number. One script corresponds to each study: unknown-value-of-information planning, stale-receipt reopening, interval-Pareto verification targeting, laundering detection, active experiment selection, and transport reminting.

Reproduction therefore requires no external dataset and no restricted resource. The reported utility, regret, recall and false-positive figures are recomputable from the committed scripts alone, and the reproduction guide gives the exact invocation for each study.

The scripts and the paired-analysis record are available under the MIT licence, and the result records under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. A permanent archival identifier will be deposited after the double-blind constraint is lifted and the authors authorize the deposit.

The repository also contains real-data transfer experiments on external corpora. **Those experiments are outside the scope of this manuscript**, which reports the six exact-synthetic studies and does not make a real-agent deployment claim. They are named here only so that a reader encountering them in the released tree does not mistake them for evidence behind the results reported above.

No personal, clinical or third-party restricted data are used.
