# ORION top-tier evidence contract V2

**Branch-base authority:** parent of the first package commit  
**Scientific authority created here:** `NONE`

A top-tier target is a scientific design constraint, not a label. A stronger claim may enter a manuscript only after the following gates are jointly satisfied.

## 1. Claim, novelty, and significance

1. State one central claim with explicit quantifiers, scope, and falsifier.
2. Subtract the strongest primary-source donor result claim by claim.
3. Separate generic shared mathematics from paper-specific mechanism, construction, or consequence.
4. Demonstrate a material consequence: a new theorem boundary, constructive algorithm, prospectively predicted transfer, or real safety/cost gain.
5. A null, tie, counterexample, contamination event, retraction, `BROKEN`, or `CANNOT_CHECK` remains load-bearing and cannot be rewritten as support.

## 2. Formal work

- The theorem statement, production implementation, and checker use independent formulations.
- Hidden completeness, separability, positivity, independence, and measurability assumptions are explicit.
- Exact enumeration establishes the frozen finite universe only unless a proof extends it.
- A single valid counterexample defeats a universal claim.
- Random adaptive spending uses either deterministic summable debits or a predictable conditional error bound. Unconditional probabilities are not compared with random realized debits and then summed pathwise.
- Generic partition-risk mathematics is cited as shared donor logic; it is not counted as seven paper-specific novelties.

## 3. Empirical design

Before outcome access, bind:

- question and claim identity;
- corpus/task/system/version manifest;
- inclusion/exclusion and unavailable-unit rules;
- unit of inference and dependency structure;
- information-, action/terminal-, and budget-matched baselines;
- primary/co-primary endpoints, margins, multiplicity family, and stop rule;
- resource accounting, adverse terminals, and `CANNOT_CHECK` format;
- expected positive, negative, and no-value strata.

The dataset, project, organization, model family, attack family, task family, authority, or real system—not its folds, examples, calls, rows, repeats, or fault cells—is ordinarily the transfer unit. A complete within-system census can prove a fixed-universe statement; it cannot manufacture external replication.

## 4. Control adequacy

- Information, action/terminal interface, and budget are matched.
- A claimed mechanistic separation requires a **positive-demonstration control**: the disfavoured arm must exhibit the failure attributed to it on the same instrument. Otherwise terminate `CANNOT_CHECK_EXPLANATION`.
- A stopping/governance comparator must be able to continue, abstain, or emit `CANNOT_CHECK` whenever the treatment can.
- Circular self-validation is blocked by a record or implementation not produced by the same computation.
- Corner policies, constant baselines, and oracle-information bounds are labelled by function; they are not presented as competitive methods.
- A treatment benefit that exists only because the comparator lacks a legal action is an interface result, not an algorithmic superiority result.

## 5. Statistics and reporting

- Use paired analyses for paired outcomes and cluster/hierarchical summaries for nested observations.
- Register primary outcomes and multiplicity handling before outcomes.
- Report estimates and uncertainty at the actual transfer unit; do not hide a small number of independent units behind thousands of rows.
- Fixed-universe exact claims and population/transfer claims appear in separate sentences and tables.
- A principled refusal to compute is serialized, for example `"pooled_significance_test": "NOT_COMPUTED_BY_PROTOCOL"`.
- Every executed check emits its verdict. A missing verdict is not a pass.
- Never move margins, denominators, task membership, comparator definitions, or outcome definitions after inspection.
- Small all-success studies report exact intervals: 3/3, 5/5, or 9/9 do not establish near-certain transfer.

## 6. Reproducibility and authority

- Pin source versions, native verifier commands, environments, content hashes, and render epochs.
- Serialize per-unit outcomes sufficient to reproduce every headline contrast.
- Open an apparently empty artifact before classification: a justified absence is a finding; an unexplained absence is a gap.
- Independent reimplementation improves reproducibility but does not create an independent investigator, institution, trust domain, or scientific authority.
- Identity, content integrity, chronology, custody, liveness, and scientific validity are separate predicates.
- A hash re-pin requires an explained legitimate content change and cannot repair an unresolved semantic or identity conflict.
- Missing authority or missing bytes terminate `CANNOT_CHECK`, never inferred success.

## 7. Editorial and venue gate

Promotion requires technical soundness, evidence aligned to the exact claim, reproducibility, primary-source donor subtraction, and importance beyond the frozen toy surface. The manuscript must state the strongest bounded result even when the promotion discriminator fails. A failed promotion attempt does not invalidate the bounded paper. Returned, deferred, spent, and external-authority lanes remain closed unless explicitly re-authorized.

## 8. Mandatory manuscript table

Every paper should carry a compact claim-evidence table with columns:

`claim | scope/quantifiers | authority | supporting artifact | unit of inference | uncertainty/exactness | falsifier | status`

The status vocabulary includes at least `SUPPORTED`, `REFUTED`, `RETRACTED`, `BROKEN`, `CONTAMINATED`, `CANNOT_CHECK`, `NOT_COMPUTED_BY_PROTOCOL`, and `OPEN_PROSPECTIVE`.

## 9. Final acceptance test

A proposed stronger sentence is blocked if any reviewer can answer “no” to one of these questions:

1. Is the sentence exactly what the theorem or prospective endpoint tests?
2. Is the comparator allowed the same information, actions, terminals, and budget?
3. Is uncertainty stated at the true transfer unit?
4. Are all adverse and unavailable outcomes still visible?
5. Can an independent party reproduce the supporting bytes and verdict?
6. Is the contribution materially beyond the strongest prior result?
7. Does the evidence come from the authority required by the claim?
