## Gate ownership

- **Editorial/package owner:** abstracts, conclusions, limitations, references, figures, renders, manifests, claim-to-PDF audits and cover letters.
- **Formal owner:** theorem statements, assumptions, proof/checker independence, counterexamples and scope lifting.
- **Empirical owner:** protocol freeze, task/corpus selection, comparator interfaces, outcome definitions, uncertainty and multiplicity.
- **Reproducibility owner:** immutable source identities, replay, native verification, checksums, content/render binding and fail-closed terminals.
- **External authority owner:** independent institutions/humans/native objective gold. This cannot be delegated to another same-programme AI session.

## Adoption rule

A manuscript may import a safe action from this matrix only after checking the paper’s current claim ledger/authority files. A stronger claim is adopted only after its scientific gate emits a registered positive terminal. Adverse/null/`CANNOT_CHECK` evidence remains visible in the submitted paper.
