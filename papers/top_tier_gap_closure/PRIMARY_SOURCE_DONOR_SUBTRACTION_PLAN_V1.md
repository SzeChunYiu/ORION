# Primary-source donor subtraction plan V1

**Status:** editorial research registry, not a completed literature review  
**Authority delta:** `NONE`

Top-tier novelty cannot be established by self-comparison or by citing only adjacent ORION papers. Before a manuscript is promoted, its central claim must be subtracted against foundational and recent primary work in the donor families below.

## Required search and adjudication protocol

For each paper:

1. Freeze the exact ORION claim, quantifiers, assumptions, endpoint, and consequence before searching.
2. Search at least two bibliographic indexes plus backward/forward citation chains from the strongest donor papers.
3. Include at minimum two foundational primary sources, three recent primary sources, one strongest competing method/theorem, and one adverse or limitation result where available.
4. Record exact theorem/experiment overlap, not topical similarity. Compare assumptions, information access, action/terminal interface, inference unit, evidence authority, and consequence.
5. Assign each claim component one status: `KNOWN`, `KNOWN_UNDER_DIFFERENT_ASSUMPTIONS`, `INCREMENTAL`, `POTENTIALLY_NEW`, `DUPLICATED_WITHIN_ORION`, or `CANNOT_CHECK_SOURCE`.
6. A generic theorem used by several ORION papers is cited once as donor mathematics. Novelty must come from the paper-specific mechanism, construction, sharp boundary, or prospectively predicted transfer.
7. A venue decision is deferred until all `POTENTIALLY_NEW` components survive primary-source review by someone other than the authoring agent.

## Donor-subtraction output required per manuscript

Each paper should contain or link a table:

`ORION claim component | strongest primary donor | donor assumptions/evidence | exact overlap | residual novelty | material consequence | independent reviewer verdict`

A `POTENTIALLY_NEW` label is provisional. It becomes a novelty claim only after the cited donor text has been opened, the exact theorem/experiment compared, and an independent reviewer signs the residual. Absence of a discovered donor is not proof of novelty.

## Venue-readiness implication

- **Theory-led paper:** requires a sharp theorem/counterexample beyond donor mathematics, explicit assumptions, proof review, and independent checker.
- **Systems-led paper:** requires native real systems, strong interface-matched baselines, objective endpoints, fault/ablation mechanisms, and a reproducible artifact.
- **Empirical methods paper:** requires prospective heterogeneous transfer, true independent units, uncertainty/multiplicity at that level, and an effect large enough to matter.
- **Governance/authority paper:** additionally requires genuinely independent institutions or experts; internal process replication cannot substitute.

Until the donor table is complete, venue language should use “candidate contribution” rather than “first,” “novel,” “unique,” or “state of the art.”
