Date: 2026-08-25
Assessment boundary: author-side recursive pre-submission review, not a journal decision or external peer review.

## Target criteria used

- `Quantum`: correctness, significance and clarity; current policy places incremental improvements below threshold and requires a very significant technical or conceptual contribution or a strong combination.
- `PRX Quantum`: exceptionality through exceptional advance, connection, capability or insight.
- Top combinatorics targets: original substantial work on finite/discrete structures; exact acceptance significance is editorial and cannot be certified internally.
- Formal-methods targets: theory, implementation and application of automated reasoning; final target depends on whether the paper ships a reusable evaluator/schema.

## Frozen Reviewer V — validity

**Decision:** PASS. The alphabet-Davenport theorem is correct under three named assumptions. The binary-rank equality is proof-language exact only with an allowed basis. General multi-Tag sharpness is not claimed.

### B

**Decision:** PASS. The lower witness belongs to the abstract language and transfers to a production compiler only by realization. R6M and R6I realization are bound separately. The `Theta(n^(4t))` statement is explicitly confined to direct support enumeration.

### C

### D

**Decision:** PASS. Powerset intersection with rule caps is monotone and distributive. The proof-tree theorem follows license by license. No claim extends to arbitrary negation, probability or inconsistency.

### Non-quantum

## Frozen Reviewer P — positioning and significance

### A

The alphabet-sensitive invariant materially broadens the theorem and clarifies the relation between compiler and proof language. Strong `Quantum` candidate if the TARE connection and practical search consequence are made immediate. PRX Quantum remains a stretch because exceptionality is an editorial judgment.

### B

The exact tight-versus-loose controls and product search-degree separation are a stronger conceptual paper than a sequence of improved bounds. `Quantum` is defensible; a broader proof-system lower bound would strengthen the case further but is no longer required for a specialist top-selectivity submission.

### C

This is the strongest paper. The primary-source matrix now absorbs Markov-basis and hierarchical-model parents explicitly. Exact additive and multiplicative impossibility turns a counterexample into a decision-theoretic theorem. Recommend top-tier theory submission after external novelty/proof review.

### D

Internalizing types fixes V1's largest conceptual weakness. The third non-quantum case demonstrates that the calculus is not just a forecast bookkeeping scheme. Strong JAR/TOCL candidate. Broad AI venue positioning would still need a reusable implementation and external-domain evaluation.

### Non-quantum

The phase theorem and atom budget are useful and explain the computational frontier. They do not substitute for exact `D_4`, `C_0(31)`, or a global obstruction theorem. Top-tier combinatorics gate remains blocking.
