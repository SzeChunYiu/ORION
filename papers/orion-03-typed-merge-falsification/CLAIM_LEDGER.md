## Headline decision proof

**Question:** after an exact falsifier, which scientific claims must lose authority and which independent claims should survive?  
**Answer:** recompute the least fixed point from non-refuted seeds; the difference from the pre-falsifier fixed point is the exact well-founded retraction.  
**Strongest alternative:** this is ordinary Datalog provenance/truth maintenance.  
**Resolution:** generic fixed-point/provenance mathematics is fully subtracted; the paper claims a typed scientific-authority specialization with exact two-family falsifier semantics and explicit nonpromotion of post-outcome repair.  
**Boundary:** positive conjunctive rule systems only.
