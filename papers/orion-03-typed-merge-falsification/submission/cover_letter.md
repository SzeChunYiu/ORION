Dear Editors,

Please consider “Typed Evidence Licenses for Finite Positive Rule Graphs” by
Sze Chun Yiu.

This research note defines a finite positive-rule semantics in which evidence licenses
propagate only through capped conjunctive derivations. It proves fixed-point and
proof-tree equivalence, nonpromotion, refutation monotonicity, and canonical
semantic retraction. A compact deterministic evaluator, public JSON
Schema, nine regression/exhaustive tests, and three bounded case encodings make
the formal semantics executable. The schema validates structural shape; the
evaluator separately enforces uniqueness and referential integrity.

The scope is intentionally limited to positive finite rule graphs. The three
case encodings are illustrative executions, not independent empirical
validation. We claim no
generic novelty for least fixed points, proof trees, Datalog provenance, belief
revision, or broad scientific judgment. The contribution is the typed,
nonpromoting specialization and its auditable implementation. A full journal
submission would require a stronger separation from existing provenance
semantics and a substantial independent application.
