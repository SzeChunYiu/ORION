# ORION-03 scientific display decision

The paper is a theory/executable-semantics hybrid. Its contribution-defining displays are the formal equations and one exact policy-outcome table.

| Display | Reader question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule transfer and fixed-point equations | What object is evaluated and how can a license cross a rule? | Main text | Contribution-defining formal spine. |
| Proof-tree/refutation relations | Why do cycles, caps and added refutations behave fail closed? | Main text | Required to understand the theorems and boundaries. |
| X.509 policy table | What differs empirically across the five fixed policies? | Main text | Four exact columns communicate the comparator trade-off more clearly than a plot. |
| Workflow schematic | How does the software repository run? | Omit from manuscript | Artifact-navigation content, not scientific evidence. |
| Bar chart of the five policy rows | Which policy has the smallest count? | Omit | Would obscure exact denominators and could visually promote the definitionally perfect origin-witness row as measured superiority. |
| Plot of three synthetic fixtures | Do the finite cases execute? | Artifact only | The fixtures test semantics; a plot would be decorative. |

The negative Cedar transfer, five OpenSSL anchor disagreements, and analytic identity are carried in prose because their meaning is categorical and compact. No adverse result was removed to save space.

**Decision:** `PASS__ONE_TABLE_AND_FORMAL_DISPLAYS_ARE_SUFFICIENT`.
