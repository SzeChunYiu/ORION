# Cover-letter draft — Journal of Automated Reasoning

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript *Typed Scientific Authority with Fail-Closed Nonpromotion* for the Journal of Automated Reasoning. The paper studies a finite positive conjunctive authority calculus in which evidence licenses propagate only through permitted rule caps and directly refuted claims fail closed. The journal-facing contribution is deliberately narrower than generic fixed-point or provenance theory: it is the scientific evidence-license/nonpromotion specialization, an executable evaluator, and a third-party X.509 obstruction/cost instantiation.

The manuscript explicitly treats generic least-fixed-point, Datalog, truth-maintenance, provenance, and retraction mathematics as prior work. It also distinguishes measured X.509 obstruction/cost quantities from analytic identities of the typed-witness policy and makes no security or broad human-science deployment claim.

The accompanying source and artifact archive reproduce the executable cases. No adverse result has been removed or promoted.

Sincerely,
Sze Chun Yiu
