## Paper A

Submission thesis: exact finite-support normal forms can be certified compositionally through realized quotient atoms, and exactness can be engineered by support regularization.

The main results are all-size theorems with explicit hypotheses; no production equivalence is needed.

## Paper B

Submission thesis: one frozen production-instantiated proof language certifies support five while the same compiler model has intrinsic support one; a missing whole-system rule explains the gap and the product penalty.

The exact scope is named, the lower witness and normalization are independently recorded, and direct products are audited by interaction scopes.

## Paper C

Submission thesis: a low-order representation can be exact for a decision while having unbounded value ambiguity, and the least repair from a candidate feature library is exactly a conflict-cover problem.

Decision, value, optimizer, minimax, and repair theorems form one closed hierarchy with scalable exact witnesses.

## Paper D

Submission thesis: typed evidence caps turn separate Horn projections into one upset-valued fixed point, exactly compressible by antichains and by min-max thresholds in ordered policies.

Generic provenance claims have been removed; the remaining typed calculus has symbolic proofs and a domain-faithful exact retraction record.

## Non-quantum paper

Submission thesis: the one-bit Davenport corridor forces a highly compressed near-squarefree obstruction, with exact support replay, atom-deficit restrictions, quotient patterns, and saturation-driven alternative factorizations.

The paper claims a structural obstruction theorem, not the unresolved exact value. Every unresolved branch is explicit future work rather than a missing premise of the advertised result.
