# Human filing checklist

The scientific and anonymous-package pipeline cannot verify the following human-controlled fields. The corresponding author must confirm them in OpenReview immediately before filing.

- [ ] Final author list and order.
- [ ] Affiliations and institutional naming.
- [ ] Corresponding-author designation.
- [ ] ORCID records.
- [ ] Funding sources and grant numbers.
- [ ] Conflicts of interest.
- [ ] Any institution-specific ethics or data declarations.
- [ ] Accuracy of the first-page language-model assistance disclosure.
- [ ] OpenReview profiles and current email addresses for every author.
- [ ] Confirmation that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] Upload the exact byte-bound anonymous PDF, clean source archive and anonymous review supplement.
- [ ] Authorize and mint the permanent archival deposit when anonymity permits.
- [ ] Record the submission identifier after filing.

No item above may be inferred or fabricated by the closeout pipeline.
