# ORION-07 headline decision proofs

## H1. A prospective dual-instrument contract was executed on three valid unresolved questions

- **Target relevance:** TMLR scope includes evaluation methodology and new task formalizations.
- **Decisive evidence:** V0 replay plus the complete records for ORION-03-R1/QG-19 and ORION-03-R2/QG-20; chronology gate passed for the two replacement cases.
- **Strongest alternative:** later outcomes or shared research context contaminated the freezes.
- **Resolution:** the result surfaces were absent at the replacement freeze, instrument outputs preceded analyzer introduction, and two candidates with visible outcome-oriented surfaces were retired rather than scored.
- **Boundary:** three questions in one programme; no population sampling.

## H2. Agreement did not validate the diagnosis in case C

- **Target relevance:** agent evaluation often uses self-consistency, agreement or judge consensus as a confidence signal.
- **Decisive evidence:** both instruments froze `S1_P0_BOUNDARY_OBJECTIVE_SCOPED` and selected the complete reweighted census. The predeclared zero-mismatch branch instead maps diagnosis alignment to `S2_P0_STRUCTURALLY_INVARIANT_UNDER_SELECT_RESCALE`, while retaining credit for the census move.
- **Observed outcome:** 729 + 38,760 = 39,489 cases; zero predicate/label mismatches; one positive two-object case and no one-object positives.
- **Strongest alternative:** the deferred map was revised after outcome.
- **Resolution:** the question, vocabulary and scoring branches were frozen before the scientific analyzer/result.
- **Boundary:** explicit single-case counterexample; no estimate of how often agreement is wrong.

## H3. Diagnostic and experiment-selection alignment are distinct coordinates

- **Decisive evidence:** in case C, both diagnostic-alignment fields are false while both experiment-selection fields are true.
- **Strongest alternative:** a correct experiment retroactively validates its motivating diagnosis.
- **Resolution:** scoring is coordinate-specific; the census discriminated between frozen diagnoses and falsified the selected diagnosis on the frozen domain.
- **Boundary:** one observed separation; no claim that experiment selection is generally more robust.

## H4. Fail-closed retention prevents favorable retrospective case selection

- **Decisive evidence:** two candidates remained recorded as contaminated after outcome-oriented surfaces became visible before instrument freeze. Two known malformed-success/recoverability defects remain accepted limitations and were not repaired between valid cases.
- **Strongest alternative:** excluding candidates or repairing an instrument improves the apparent valid series.
- **Resolution:** contaminated candidates are reported outside the valid count; an outcome-bearing malformed case would become invalid/CANNOT_CHECK with original bytes retained.
- **Boundary:** protocol-integrity design, not a reliability or security guarantee.

## Minimum-sufficient evidence chain

The paper needs one chronology schematic, one complete three-row results table and prose for the two contaminated candidates and two instrument failure modes. No agreement percentage, confidence interval, calibration plot or aggregate performance figure is scientifically permitted.
