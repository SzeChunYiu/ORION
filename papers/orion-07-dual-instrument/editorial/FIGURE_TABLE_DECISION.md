# ORION-07 figure and table decision

## Main Figure 1: prospective measurement lifecycle

Reader question: how can decisions be compared before an answer exists without letting agreement become truth?

The figure must show:

1. unresolved question and admitted evidence frozen;
2. two separate instrument decisions;
3. agreement/disagreement recorded only as a relation;
4. later science generated outside the instruments;
5. diagnosis and experiment choice scored separately;
6. contamination or instrument invalidity retained as fail-closed exits.

The figure is a conceptual diagram and contains no quantitative result. It must not use internal programme labels or repository identifiers.

## Main Table 1: complete valid case series

Reader question: what happened in every valid question?

Rows are cases A, B and C. Columns are frozen question, inter-instrument diagnosis relation, inter-instrument experiment relation, deferred diagnostic alignment for each instrument, deferred experiment alignment for each instrument and bounded later observation. No total, mean, percentage, interval, colour-coded score or ranking is permitted.

## Table 2: protocol-retained non-valid events

Reader question: what adverse or contaminated events are excluded from the valid count and why?

Rows are two retired candidates plus two known instrument failure modes. The table reports disposition and scoring consequence. The two failure modes did not trigger in the valid cases, but remain visible limitations.

## No additional figures

The 53-row panel and 39,489-case census are each represented by exact counts and bounded outcomes. Plotting a degenerate zero-gap or zero-mismatch distribution would add visual weight without additional inference. Aggregate agreement, reliability and calibration plots are forbidden because the design does not support them.
