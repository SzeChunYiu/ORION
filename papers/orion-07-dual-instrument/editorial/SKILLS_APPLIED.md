# Recursive academic-paper skill application

The Wave-1 closeout applied the vendored academic-paper protocol recursively.

`skills-applied: nature-writing, nature-proposal-writer, nature-polishing, nature-figure, nature-citation, nature-academic-search, nature-literature-pipeline, nature-data, nature-reviewer, nature-reader, nature-response`

Application map:

- `nature-proposal-writer`: research canon, evidence table, argument map and claim boundaries before prose.
- `nature-writing`: TMLR-resolved argument spine and reader-question sequence.
- `nature-polishing`: proposition-level rewrite with stable terminology and local claim stance.
- `nature-figure`: one claim-driven chronology schematic; no decorative or unsupported plots.
- `nature-citation`, `nature-academic-search`, `nature-literature-pipeline`: live metadata refresh, donor subtraction and bibliography binding.
- `nature-data`: anonymous review-data inventory, access route, licence and availability statement.
- `nature-reviewer`: editorial triage, three frozen reviewer lenses and post-review synthesis.
- `nature-reader`: full-PDF text extraction and page-by-page reading/inspection rather than abstract-only review.
- `nature-response`: concern-to-edit mapping, minimum-sufficient revisions and targeted rereview.

The protocol did not treat local checks, hashes or receipts as scientific novelty or journal authority.
