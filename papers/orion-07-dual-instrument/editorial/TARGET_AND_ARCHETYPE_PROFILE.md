# ORION-07 target and archetype profile

**Audit date:** 2026-08-28  
**Primary target:** Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)  
**Article type:** Original research article / evaluation methodology  
**Fallback:** Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR)  
**Authority:** private editorial planning only; not acceptance or submission authority

## Target decision

TMLR is the strongest defensible current target because the paper contributes an evaluable methodology for measuring research-agent decisions before a scientific outcome exists. The paper does not need to claim state-of-the-art performance or broad significance. It does need to show a clear claims/evidence match and interest to at least part of the machine-learning evaluation community.

Current official TMLR guidance checked on 2026-08-28:

- <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/>
- <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/acceptance-criteria.html>
- <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/editorial-policies.html>
- <https://jmlr.org/tmlr/author-guide.html>

The applicable requirements are double-blind review, the official TMLR style, an anonymous main PDF and supplementary package, evidence sufficient for each claim, and human responsibility for any language-model assistance. Supplementary PDF/ZIP material may be supplied up to the current 100 MB limit. Author, affiliation, funding, conflict and OpenReview profile fields remain human filing inputs.

## Contribution archetype

The paper is a small prospective methodology case series, not a reliability study, agent leaderboard or quantum-compilation result paper.

Argument spine:

1. **Question:** Can heterogeneous research-decision instruments be frozen on an unresolved scientific question and scored later without treating their agreement as truth?
2. **Answer:** A fail-closed prospective contract can keep agreement, diagnostic alignment, experiment selection, contamination and instrument invalidity separate.
3. **Evidence:** Three valid prospectively frozen questions; two retired contaminated candidates; one explicit agreement-with-misdiagnosis counterexample; independent scientific replays and bindings.
4. **Boundary:** one programme, three non-random cases, shared upstream evidence and ontology, no reliability/calibration/population/independence inference.
5. **Meaning:** research-agent evaluation can preserve informative decisions and failures before a gold answer exists instead of retrospectively rewriting the task or using consensus as validation.

## Fallback logic

JAIR is the fallback if TMLR editors judge the scientific-decision case series too domain-specific for their readership. A transfer must preserve the exact scientific scope and adverse outcomes. It may change framing and formatting but must not add reliability, calibration, population-rate, statistical-independence or generalization language.

## Filing boundary

The pipeline may close the scientific manuscript, anonymous source/PDF, reviewer archive, cover letter, availability statement and exact byte manifest. It cannot supply or verify affiliation wording, ORCID, funding, conflicts, OpenReview author profiles, final author order, or a permanent repository DOI. Those fields remain `AUTHOR_INPUT_NEEDED` or `HUMAN_FILING_INPUT`.
