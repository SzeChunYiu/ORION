## Why it matters to TMLR readers

Machine-learning agents increasingly act before an evaluator possesses a contemporaneous answer. A measurement protocol that makes later evidence score an immutable earlier decision, while preserving agreement-with-error and contamination, supplies a useful evaluation primitive for research-agent studies. The three-case series does not establish reliability, but it exposes a concrete failure of agreement-as-validation and demonstrates how future larger studies can avoid that inferential error.
