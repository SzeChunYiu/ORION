## 00 — Scope

### Scientific object
A benchmark for **inter-instrument epistemic agreement on unresolved frontier research questions**, where:

- Lane A is a host/LLM-driven, receipt-bound research process;
- Lane B is a typed non-LLM controller over a frozen manifest;
- both diagnosis/next-move outputs are frozen before later research outcomes are used for deferred scoring;
- agreement does not itself confer correctness or scientific authority.

### External donor pressure
Current scientific-agent benchmarks such as ScienceAgentBench and AstaBench primarily score task success, programs, expert/published answers, costs or known outcomes. Automated multi-model review studies score produced papers. These are adjacent but do not by themselves instantiate the same unresolved-question / cross-instrument / deferred-outcome object.

### Publication claim ceiling
Until at least 2–3 additional prospectively frozen instances are completed, ORION-03 is a **benchmark definition with a first measurement**, not a reliability/evaluation study.

### Cross-paper ownership
- ORION-03 owns dual-instrument agreement measurement and the experiment comparing differently governed research instruments.
- ORION-25 may own generic ORION research-harness systems engineering.
- ORION-01/QG own the quantum content of any frontier question used as a benchmark instance.

## 01 — Research canon

1. The ORION research harness uses deterministic content-derived request identities, receipt binding and replay.
2. Missing/failed capabilities are orchestration conditions and are not admitted as scientific evidence.
3. Campaign decisions can be made natively from typed observations without an LLM in the decision path once the manifest exists.
4. Manifest construction itself is not LLM-independent and must remain an explicit limitation.
5. Protected-reference guards are custody checks on declared surfaces, not general information-flow security.
6. Authority-grant fields remain false by construction in the registered campaign schemas.
7. Benchmark V0 was frozen before either lane outcome and admitted AGREE/PARTIAL/DISAGREE outcomes.
8. V0 produced agreement on the responsible layer and next move; the typed revision gate withheld an unresolved revision; the later R6P/R6Q outcomes aligned with the selected move.
9. One instance cannot support an agreement rate, forecasting skill or reliability claim.
10. D2/D3 remain real instrument defects: malformed-successful LLM content can crash a recursive solve and pin a deterministic request identity such that the malformed successful receipt cannot be replaced by the failed-receipt recovery path.
11. D2/D3 halt/fail the instrument rather than entering scientific state; they still must be repaired or accepted as explicit limitations before submission.
12. Additional ORION-03 instances must be genuinely prospective: lane outputs frozen before the result used for deferred scoring is known to either lane.

## 03 — Argument map

### Tension
Most scientific-agent benchmarks require known answers, published targets or expert labels. On a genuine research frontier, those may not exist when a decision must be made. Yet research systems can still be compared at the level of *what they diagnose and what next move they license*.

### Central question
Can differently governed research instruments be placed on the same unresolved question and produce receipt-bound, independently generated epistemic diagnoses/moves that can later be compared to each other and to deferred scientific outcomes?

### Central answer at current evidence cut
Yes as an instrument/benchmark construction; V0 is a first measurement. ORION-03 cannot yet claim reliability because the series has one instance.

### Evidence dependency
1. Define the two instruments and their non-equivalent control paths.
2. Define agreement coordinates and ensure all outcomes are reachable.
3. Freeze an unresolved question and both lane outputs.
4. Compare lane diagnoses/moves without using later outcome.
5. Score deferred coordinate only after external research outcome exists.
6. Repeat on heterogeneous frontier questions before making any rate/generalization statement.
7. Audit instrument defects and cost boundary.

### Strongest alternative interpretations
- **Same answer because Lane B manifest encodes Lane A's conclusion.** Control by independent manifest construction requirements and observation-discriminable hypotheses; still acknowledge human/LLM authorship of the manifest.
- **Agreement is consensus, not correctness.** Correct; ORION-03 separates agreement from deferred alignment and never uses agreement as authority.
- **One example is anecdote.** Correct; submission requires additional instances.

## 04 — Additional-instance protocol contract

The next three instances should come from materially different unresolved questions and be frozen before their outcomes.

### Candidate classes
- unresolved formal/theorem question where later proof/refutation is independently checkable;
- representation/boundary-identifiability question where later exhaustive/hostile search provides deferred evidence;
- objective/phase or compiler-family question where later exact calculation/referee decides the registered coordinate.

### Required per-instance files
1. question freeze with exact source/base and what is currently unknown;
2. Lane A receipt set;
3. Lane B manifest digest + decision receipt;
4. pre-outcome agreement score;
5. sealed deferred-scoring rule;
6. later outcome receipt generated independently of the two lane outputs;
7. final alignment/disagreement disposition;
8. experiment log including anomalies and any human intervention.

### Non-compensatory gates
- lane outputs must predate deferred outcome;
- hidden protected outcome material cannot enter either lane;
- an invalid instrument run becomes `CANNOT_CHECK`, not disagreement;
- no post-outcome change to agreement coordinate;
- disagreements are preserved verbatim;
- all three additional instances must be reported, not only agreeing ones.

## 05 — Section contracts

### Abstract
Until series completion: benchmark definition + first measurement only. After series completion, report exact number of valid instances and their agreement/alignment outcomes without extrapolating beyond them.

### Introduction
Distinguish known-answer scientific-agent benchmarking from unresolved-frontier instrument agreement. Credit AstaBench, ScienceAgentBench and other agent-evaluation work for rigorous task/ground-truth benchmarks.

### Instrument Methods
Report deterministic receipt/replay guarantees and exact boundaries. Explicitly state that digests are integrity bindings, not cryptographic signatures; local path controls are not an OS sandbox; protected-reference scanning is not general taint tracking.

### Benchmark Methods
Define agreement coordinates, outcome space and deferred-scoring contract. Agreement and correctness must be separate variables.

### Results
Report every instance, including disagreement/CANNOT_CHECK. Cost numbers must include/exclude manifest-construction cost explicitly and never be used for efficiency superiority without matched accounting.

### Defects / Limitations
D2/D3 must either be prospectively repaired and replayed or retained as named limitations with why they do not enter scientific evidence.

### Discussion
Do not infer that agreement validates a research diagnosis. Ask instead whether cross-instrument agreement/disagreement is a useful *measurement object* when ground truth is deferred.

## Expert reconciliation

### Benchmark-methods reviewer
The benchmark class is coherent only if disagreement is as recordable as agreement and invalid instrument runs are separate from scientific disagreement.

### Scientific-agent evaluation reviewer
Current benchmarks supply strong adjacent prior art. ORION-03 must avoid claiming general rigorous agent benchmarking; unresolved-frontier dual-instrument agreement is the residual.

### Formal/reproducibility reviewer
Every deferred score needs a pre-outcome digest/freeze and an independently checkable later receipt. No outcome-aware reminting of coordinates.

### Systems reviewer
D2/D3 must be dispositioned before submission. Claims about local path containment/replay should remain narrower than security/tamper-proofing.

### Statistics reviewer
With 1–4 total instances, report a case series, not a population reliability estimate. No meaningless confidence interval around heterogeneous frontier questions.
