# ORION-03 hostile novelty refresh — 2026-08-22

This is a publication-positioning artifact, not a scientific-authority receipt.

## Review lenses

The refresh used four hostile lenses:

1. scientific-agent benchmarking;
2. research-integrity/provenance systems;
3. multi-agent / LLM-as-judge agreement evaluation;
4. delayed-ground-truth metrology and prospective evaluation.

The search specifically attempted to collapse the candidate ORION-03 residual rather than collect broadly related citations.

## Direct donor pressure

### ValueBlindBench — arXiv:2604.25224

**ValueBlindBench: Agreement-Gated Stress Testing of LLM-Judged Investment Rationales Before Returns Are Observable** directly owns important generic territory that an earlier ORION-03 framing could have overclaimed:

- delayed ground truth as an evaluation problem;
- preregistered agreement-gated evaluation before outcomes are observable;
- explicit publish/qualify/invalidate gates;
- the principle that judge agreement does not itself establish true downstream skill.

**Novelty subtraction:** ORION-03 must not claim that agreement before delayed outcomes, agreement gating, or later outcome calibration is new in general.

### REFLECT — arXiv:2605.19196

**Time to REFLECT: Can We Trust LLM Judges for Evidence-based Research Agents?** meta-evaluates LLM judges on evidence-based/deep-research agents using controlled process/outcome failure interventions and reports substantial judge unreliability.

**Novelty subtraction:** ORION-03 must not imply that the need to validate automated research evaluators/judges, or the existence of process-level failure taxonomies, is novel.

### Agreement Metrics for LLM-as-Judge Evaluation — arXiv:2606.00093

This work shows that agreement reporting depends materially on scale, abstention/tie handling and aggregation, and recommends explicit reporting of coverage/confusion structure rather than stacking redundant scalar coefficients.

**Consequence for ORION-03:** with only three valid prospective question units after closure, ORION-03 must not manufacture a kappa/reliability statistic. Report the full per-instance diagnosis/move relation and deferred outcome disposition instead.

### HeurekaBench — ICLR 2026

HeurekaBench provides open-ended research questions grounded in scientific studies/workflows and quantitatively evaluates AI co-scientists.

**Novelty subtraction:** ORION-03 does not own open-ended scientific-agent benchmarking, realistic research questions, or quantitative co-scientist evaluation broadly.

### Existing ORION donor set retained

ScienceAgentBench, AstaBench, SciAgentArena and ScientistOne remain primary donors for authentic scientific tasks, controlled scientific-agent benchmarking and claim/evidence integrity. The AI Scientist/related systems remain donors for end-to-end autonomous research workflows.

## Surviving candidate residual

After subtraction, the narrow ORION-03 object is:

> A prospectively frozen **heterogeneous dual-instrument measurement** on live scientific frontier questions in one evolving research programme: one instrument is a tool-capable LLM host diagnosis; the other is a typed non-LLM campaign controller over the same receipt-transcribed question state. Their diagnosis/move relation is committed before the scientific successor outcome exists, disagreement/abstention remain valid data, immutable receipts preserve the chronology, and later independently generated scientific results score the already-frozen move without retroactively changing either instrument.

This is intentionally **not**:

- a generic delayed-ground-truth benchmark;
- a generic LLM-judge benchmark;
- a claim that inter-instrument agreement validates a scientific diagnosis;
- a benchmark of task success against pre-existing ground truth;
- a reliability/generalization result from three question units;
- a claim that the typed controller is human/LLM-free end to end (manifest construction is authored upstream).

## Submission-safe novelty sentence

> We study a small prospective case series in which a tool-capable LLM host and a typed non-LLM campaign controller are independently frozen on the same unresolved scientific question before the successor scientific result exists, then scored later against the independently generated result while retaining disagreements, abstentions and contaminated instances in the audit history.

Do not prepend “first” without another submission-date search and direct full-text comparison.

## Reporting consequence

The final manuscript should foreground the **measurement protocol and chronology**, not an agreement rate. Valid units are displayed individually. Contaminated candidate instances are retained as protocol failures, not silently removed. Deferred scientific outcomes remain external to the instruments and do not confer scientific authority on ORION-03.
