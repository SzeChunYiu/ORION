# ORION-07 genuinely prospective deferred-outcome series — frozen question

Identity: `ORION07.PROSPECTIVE_DEFERRED_OUTCOMES.v1`.

Can two frozen scientific instruments be meaningfully calibrated when their predictions are committed before a sufficiently large set of public objective outcomes exists, including agreement-but-jointly-wrong events?

Historical time-slicing cannot satisfy this identity. Only items unresolved at the freeze and later resolved by a pre-existing objective public mechanism count.
