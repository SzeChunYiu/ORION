\section{Limitations}

The decisive limitation is sample size: three frontier questions. No reliability, agreement-rate, predictive-validity, or calibration claim follows, and the one instance in which both instruments were jointly wrong is a warning about the construct rather than an estimate of how often that happens. The prospectively recorded publication plan treated this as a blocker and required additional prospectively frozen instances; two have since been completed, and the series audit keeps the two contaminated slots that they replaced visible.

The lanes are not fully independent. They share the receipt corpus, originate in the same research programme, and Lane B's manifest is human/LLM-authored even though the native decision is not. Manifest construction can encode framing choices. Lane A's LLM synthesis may also share conceptual priors with the people who designed the typed hypotheses.

Deferred scoring is endogenous to the same programme and is not external ground truth. The current benchmark does not establish that agreement predicts scientific success, only that the chosen V0 direction was later aligned with the programme's own frozen frontier outcomes.

Finally, reproducible receipts do not make a decision scientifically correct. They make the evidence/decision path attributable. Systems-level guarantees about the harnesses, and methodological claims about receipt-first negative recovery, are separate subjects with their own evidence, and neither is settled by the benchmark interpretation offered here.
