\section{Results}

\subsection{V0 agreement}
Lane A identifies the responsible layer as representation-regime characterization and selects regime-predicate characterization as primary, with the support-two closure test as a complementary probe. Its answer is bound to 13 host capability receipts and the verified evidence items admitted by the frozen protocol.

Lane B identifies the corresponding typed responsibility state, defeats the current-search, donor-family, and method-language alternatives, and selects the regime-characterization compute action. Its native decision is bound to the frozen manifest and transition receipts. The revision gate reports the representation split as unresolved because the required predicate obligation has not yet been satisfied.

Under the protocol's compatibility rules the contemporaneous verdict is `AGREE`: the diagnosis and primary move match at the scored level, and the evidence sets overlap by construction while remaining receipt-bound.

\subsection{Deferred coordinate}
After V0, the independently launched frontier lanes produce an exact regime-membership predicate on their stated domains and restore family closure at support two, the two directions prioritized by Lane A and structurally licensed by Lane B. Coordinate 4 is therefore recorded `ALIGNED`. This retrospective score is descriptive; it does not turn the subsequent result into a gold label for all frontier decisions.

\subsection{Historical defects are repaired}
The V1 manuscript documented two real harness defects: malformed successful reasoner content could escape as an unstructured crash, and the deterministic request identity could then be pinned by an immutable semantically invalid receipt. Current regression tests now require the harness to report a structured host-capability failure and provide an explicit, reason-bearing invalid-content archival path that preserves the original bytes before allowing a corrected receipt. These repairs update the implementation status but do not add benchmark instances.

\subsection{Two further prospectively frozen instances}
Two additional frontier questions were frozen, run, and scored after V0, under
the same contract. Both replaced earlier candidate slots that were withdrawn for
outcome contamination; the withdrawal is retained in the series audit rather than
being absorbed silently.

In the first, on an outside-cone sharpness question, both instruments identified
the same responsible layer and selected the same targeted exact panel as the
primary move. The contemporaneous relation is `AGREE' on responsibility, next
move, and revision abstention, and both lanes also match the later deferred
outcome. Its scientific terminal records zero gap on the frozen panel while
leaving sharpness open.

The second instance is the more informative one, because the instruments agree
with each other and are jointly wrong. On a boundary-invariance question, both
lanes diagnose the responsible layer as objective-scoped and both select the
complete reweighted census as the primary move. The contemporaneous relation is
again `AGREE' on all three coordinates. The later deferred outcome, however,
identifies a different responsible layer: the boundary predicate is structurally
invariant under the rescaling in question. Both lanes are therefore scored as
not aligned on responsibility, while remaining aligned on the move actually taken.

This is the outcome the benchmark is built to make visible. Cross-instrument
agreement is not evidence of correctness: two architecturally distinct decision
procedures, constrained to the same admitted evidence, converged on a diagnosis
that the subsequent frontier result did not support. Any use of this benchmark
that reads agreement as a proxy for validity is unsupported by its own series.

\subsection{What the series does not license}
The scored series is three questions. That is enough to close the prospectively
frozen evidence gate on standalone submission and it is not enough to estimate
anything. We report no agreement percentage, confidence interval, calibration
measure, or predictive-validity statistic, and the instance records explicitly
withhold aggregate reliability authority. Each instance was independently
replayed to a byte-identical result digest.
