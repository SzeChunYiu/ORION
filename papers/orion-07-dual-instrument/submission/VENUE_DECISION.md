# Venue decision — ORION-07

**Target: TMLR (Transactions on Machine Learning Research).**

TMLR was the assigned route and it is the correct one. TMLR's acceptance
criterion is whether claims are supported by convincing evidence, not whether
the result is significant or beats a baseline. This paper's central result is a
negative one from a three-question series: two instruments agreed and were
jointly wrong. That is a claim TMLR can evaluate on its merits. A venue
selecting for impact would be a poor fit, and reshaping the paper to survive
one would mean overstating a series of three.

**Article type.** Regular submission. Current compiled length is 6 pages, which
is short for TMLR but proportionate to a benchmark definition plus three scored
units. TMLR has no page limit and no minimum.

**No fallback needed.** If TMLR declines, the honest next step is more scored
instances, not a different venue.
