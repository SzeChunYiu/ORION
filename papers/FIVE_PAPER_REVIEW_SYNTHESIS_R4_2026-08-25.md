# Five-Paper Independent Review Synthesis R4

Date: 2026-08-25

## Review architecture

The final cycle followed the updated `academic-paper-pipeline` and kept three
independent lanes separate before editor synthesis:

1. a formal-validity lane reconstructed every definition, theorem, quantifier,
   proof dependency, and boundary case;
2. a reproducibility/surface lane rebuilt public packages, ran clean-room
   executables, scanned archives for private labels and paths, and verified PDF
   surfaces; and
3. an editorial/target lane assessed title-to-claim fidelity, overlap,
   contribution depth, analogue calibration, and realistic venue fit.

This is a simulated review and engineering audit, not external peer review.

explicitly bounded evidence label. No manuscript turns a finite computation
into all-parameter authority. The five titles match the final claim domains,
and the struck universal-calculus claim remains absent.

preprints. It does **not** pass a top-tier-journal threshold. C is currently the
strongest standalone journal candidate; A+B have a clearer journal future as a
