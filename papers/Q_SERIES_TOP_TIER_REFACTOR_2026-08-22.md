## 2. Q1 — flagship science paper

### Canonical draft

`papers/Q-paper-01-tare-expressivity/MANUSCRIPT_SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md`

### Final scientific thesis

For the frozen three-block shared-one-bit-Tag TARE-M2 grammar and frozen support-count objective,

\[
\boxed{\kappa_{R6M}=2.}
\]

where `kappa` is the smallest uniform frame-support cap guaranteed to contain an exact optimum for every admitted instance and every qubit count.

### Why this is now stronger than the original paper

The original draft was written before R6S and therefore treated all-`n` support closure as open. The publication refactor establishes:

1. **analytic support-two theorem** — no 18,432-case table is logically required;
2. **sharpness** — R6O's complete support-one family loses `5<6` on an exact `n=2` instance;
3. **proof/obstruction identity** — the only weight-two parity patterns where the exchange fails correspond to the actual frame-for-Tag coupling mechanism;
4. **normal-form count** — every optimum lies in a raw frame family of `O(n^12)` candidates for the fixed six-slot grammar, a representational-complexity corollary;
5. **finite-domain regime evidence kept separate** — R6Q/R6R remain exact finite/prospective evidence, not an all-`n` taxonomy;
6. **known QG counterexamples disclosed** — later support-two subregimes do not weaken `kappa=2` but prohibit overselling the original two-trade classifier.

### Top-tier target logic

A PRX Quantum attempt is justified only if external experts agree the exact theorem is both novel and consequential. PRX Quantum's acceptance standard is exceptionality: an exceptional advance, connection, capability, or insight with broad/lasting QIST impact. The paper should therefore be sold as an **exact structural law/normal form**, not as an ORION experiment log.

Fallbacks should preserve the same science rather than weaken it: npj Quantum Information / Quantum / another strong quantum-information venue depending on external referee feedback and fit.

## 3. Q2 — recovery methodology paper

### Canonical draft

`papers/Q-paper-02-recursive-recovery/MANUSCRIPT_V2.md`

### Residual thesis after the 2026 literature update

Provenance itself is not the novelty. The autonomous-science literature now explicitly treats complete provenance as foundational. Q2 studies the **recovery layer above provenance**:

- typed negative/absorbed/mixed/saturated/cannot-check terminals;
- donor first right of refusal;
- prospectively frozen successors;
- lower-bound/saturation stopping;
- preserved failed hypotheses;
- productive backtracking from failure into a new bounded question.

The completed R6 arc is unusually strong as a case study:

negative -> exact donor saturation -> explanatory lemma -> refutation -> repair -> second refutation -> finite closure -> prospective prediction -> all-`n` theorem.

### Top-tier research upgrade

A broad methodology journal should not receive only one self-designed programme. Pre-register the same recovery contract in at least two additional materially different domains and compare against:

- naive retry/iterate-until-positive;
- strongest-donor-only stopping;
- ordinary agentic research loop;
- ablated recovery without typed terminals/scope where meaningful.

Measure false-novelty avoidance, productive recoveries, evidence/time cost, stop calibration and final scientific terminal quality.

Until that study is executed, Q2 remains a strong complete case study rather than a universal productivity claim.

## 4. Q3 — dual-instrument benchmark

### Canonical draft

`papers/Q-paper-03-dual-instrument/MANUSCRIPT_V2.md`

### Critical current correction

The V1 manuscript lists malformed-success receipt defects D2/D3 as open. Current main repairs them:

- invalid reasoner task content maps to structured `HOST_CAPABILITY_FAILED`;
- successful-but-invalid content has explicit reason-bound archival preserving original bytes;
- the deterministic identity can be re-serviced with a corrected receipt;
- regression tests cover the path.

V1 remains historical evidence that the defect was discovered live.

### Scientific upgrade

Agreement among models/agents is known to be an unreliable correctness proxy when errors are correlated. Q3 should therefore measure **deferred calibration**, not agreement rate.

The prospective protocol in `TOP_TIER_UPGRADE_PROTOCOL_2026-08-22.md` requires >=20 live unresolved frontier decisions across >=3 programmes, with AGREE/PARTIAL/DISAGREE/CANNOT_CHECK all admissible and later ALIGNED/MISALIGNED/UNRESOLVED scoring.

A valuable result may be positive or negative. Evidence that agreement fails after controlling shared evidence bias is itself scientifically useful.

Its residual question is **matched-information scientific decision state**:

## 7. Research-quality rule going forward

Before a future flagship begins, freeze:

- `IMPACT_THESIS.md` — field-level statement that would change if successful;
- `NOVELTY_MAP.md` — strongest known neighboring results and donor subtraction;
- `KILL_GATES.md` — evidence that would demote/refute the flagship hypothesis;
- `ADVERSARIAL_PLAN.md` — independent counterexample strategy;
- `GENERALIZATION_PLAN.md` — second/third family or real-domain transfer requirement;
- `PUBLICATION_LADDER.md` — what result level justifies top-tier attempt versus specialist fallback.

The research programme should optimize for theorem/mechanism/generalization/validation, not number of positive experiments.
