\section{Limitations and authority boundary}

The formal properties in this paper are consequences of explicit contracts. They
do not show that an implementation is free from bypasses, that a trust root is
secure, or that the declared environment captures every causal dependency. The
adversarial experiments increase confidence only within their registered attack
models and finite sampling frames.

The trust-domain result is deliberately narrower than an organizational-security
claim. ``Independent domain'' is instantiated by independent cryptographic key
generation. The measured panel uses four real receipts, three semantic roles, one
compromised domain, and $d\in\{1,2,3\}$ at fixed $k=3$. Separate institutions,
teams, machines or administrative owners may provide stronger operational
separation, but they were not all tested. Multiple-domain compromise and larger
role systems remain open.

Likewise, the chain-length result covers $k\in\{1,2,3\}$ and the registered attack
families. The flat detection rate shows that adding links did not improve this
endpoint; it does not say that semantic role decomposition is useless in every
application. A longer chain may carry different evidence while leaving the
cryptographic compromise threshold unchanged.

The false-rejection endpoint covers six semantics-preserving serializations of one
committed result. It does not estimate rejection under clock skew, partial memory
failure, scheduler interruption or resource pressure. The host/process study
instead exposes a sharper boundary: an artifact checker can correctly accept a
stale valid artifact after the current producer was killed. Liveness therefore
requires occurrence binding by the orchestrator. No signature-chain property in
this paper substitutes for that run-level evidence.

The reported overhead is composition-side only. It excludes verification, I/O,
queueing, scheduler behavior and the surrounding workflow, and it is measured on
four cases. The $13.8\times$ relative multiple is dominated by a roughly
$0.105$ ms no-attestation baseline; the absolute added median is about $1.34$ ms.
Neither number is an end-to-end throughput claim.

The independence ordering is conservative but not universally total. Different
applications may value model, executor, evidence and organizational independence
differently. Two independently implemented deterministic checkers over the same
evidence can be a strong software-bug diagnostic while still sharing a scientific
premise. The framework therefore requires component predicates and trust domains
to be reported rather than replacing them with one ordinal label.

Cryptographic attribution introduces further operational dependencies: issuer
governance, credential security, revocation, transparency infrastructure and
privacy. Some research settings cannot reveal executor or evidence identities
publicly. Privacy-preserving attestations are compatible with the framework only
if they retain enough verifiability to prevent self-declared identity from
satisfying an independence claim.

Complete environment capture is also difficult. A container digest does not bind
an external service's mutable behavior, a model alias may not identify an immutable
checkpoint, and hardware or nondeterministic libraries can change outputs. Receipt
schemas must allow an explicit undetermined state rather than encode unavailable
metadata as empty but acceptable fields.

Most importantly, provenance is not epistemic warrant. FAIR principles concern the
findability, accessibility, interoperability and reuse of digital objects
\citep{wilkinson2016fair}; provenance standards describe how objects were produced
\citep{moreau2013prov}. Neither determines whether a premise is true, a measurement
is valid, a comparator is fair or a conclusion follows. Scientific authority
remains with the study design, evidence and adjudication, not the execution layer.

The bounded execution-integrity programme is now sufficiently complete for this
paper's claim: artifact corruption, benign re-encoding, chain length, trust-domain
count, process termination and composition overhead have all received frozen
measurements. The next scientific step, if pursued, is external independent custody
or a broader multi-campaign reliability study. It is successor work rather than a
prerequisite for the bounded framework paper. A submission package still requires a
fresh deterministic render from the current source, exact content binding, archive
and licence records, and human filing metadata; none of those administrative steps
changes scientific authority.
