# ORION-25 bounded closeout question

## Scientific question

Within the frozen finite protocols already executed for ORION-25, what can the
execution-integrity stack establish about committed-artifact corruption, benign
re-encoding, chain length, cryptographic trust-domain count, process liveness,
and composition cost?

## One-sentence argument

The bounded evidence shows that the registered checker rejected every applied
committed-artifact corruption without rejecting the registered benign
re-encodings, that resistance to one-domain compromise changed with
cryptographic trust-domain count rather than chain length, and that artifact
validity remained distinct from current-run liveness and scientific warrant.

## Boundary

This closeout does not establish production-scale reliability, organizational
or institutional independence, secure key custody, resistance to multiple-domain
compromise, superiority over production Sigstore/TUF/in-toto deployments, or
scientific truth from a valid signature. A fresh PDF render, page-level visual
audit, archive binding, and filing metadata remain administrative gates.
