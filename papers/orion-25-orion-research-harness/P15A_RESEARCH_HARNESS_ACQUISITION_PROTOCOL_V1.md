# P15A research-harness acquisition protocol v1

This prospectively frozen acquisition contract advances P15 beyond an unscoped
methods directory without fabricating a scientific result.

## Proposed question

Can a research execution harness keep host/capability failures out of scientific
evidence while preserving complete attribution and recovery receipts under a
matched hostile fault-injection corpus?

No H1 is active yet.  The question becomes executable only after the following
inputs are acquired and content-bound:

1. a programme paper issue assigning the P15 question;
2. a donor matrix covering research-execution, workflow-provenance and durable
   experiment systems, with explicit ownership subtraction;
3. at least one matched external harness comparator or an explicitly
   noncomparative estimand;
4. a protected fault-injection corpus with host, capability, process, filesystem,
   malformed-content, concurrency and recovery cases;
5. a common receipt schema and resource envelope;
6. an evaluator separated from the harness implementation;
7. attainable positive and negative terminals frozen before execution.

Repository tests and the ORION-Q dual harness are planning inputs.  They are not
the protected P15 corpus, not external comparators and not scientific results.
Until all seven acquisitions pass, the terminal is
`P15A_ACQUISITION_BLOCKED_NO_SCIENTIFIC_RESULT`.
