## Preprints and later journals

Springer Nature states that posting a preprint is not prior publication and
does not jeopardize journal consideration. Elsevier permits authors to share a
preprint anywhere at any time, including arXiv, subject to later
version/licensing rules. A journal-specific policy must still be checked again
at the date of each submission.

## Author-controlled arXiv boundary

arXiv accepts TeX/LaTeX ZIP uploads and requires the processed PDF to be
previewed. It also expects registered authors to self-submit, may require
category endorsement, and requires an irrevocable distribution license and
agreement to the submittal terms.

The following cannot be inferred or silently selected:

1. affiliation and ORCID;
2. primary/cross-list category where arXiv eligibility is uncertain;
3. distribution license;
4. endorsement response if arXiv requests one;
5. certification of authorship, rights, AI disclosure, and metadata;
6. approval of the arXiv-generated PDF preview and final `Submit Article`
   action.
