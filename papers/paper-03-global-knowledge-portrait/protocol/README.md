# ORION-P3 prospective atlas protocol

**Protocol:** `P3.cross-domain-atlas.v1`  
**Status:** `DESIGN_FROZEN`  
**Outcome access:** false

Paper III cannot be completed by synthetic worlds alone. This protocol freezes the real cross-domain evaluation design and, separately, the gold-annotation process required to decide referent/construct/measurement/context mappings, contradictions, GLUE/obstruction and source recoverability.

The annotation handbook and adjudication policy are frozen **before** final labels and before model outputs are inspected. Domain-expert escalation is allowed under the frozen policy; inconvenient disagreements are not deleted.

The MUSE implementation/data source is reference-pinned to `cohentsofia/MUSE@f7a40317db46145d0c90b221311d8324db5da1b9`. Final source corpora, sampled cases, annotation artifact and evaluator hashes remain unbound until `EXECUTION_FROZEN`.
