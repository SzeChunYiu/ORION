\section{Limitations, ethics, and reproducibility}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{Claim boundary}

The two executed studies establish the mapping/identity-authority layer, not raw-text extraction. The public-reference route uses two separately frozen 32-case mapping sets assembled from pinned public human/expert resources, with a case-disjoint confirmatory holdout. P3-X separately evaluates 400 prospectively frozen exact heterogeneous mapping contracts. Both therefore test decisions over already-structured scientific projections: the confirmatory study shows zero false merges against flat canonicalization without an added false-split penalty, and P3-X shows 400/400 exact decisions against 250/400 for the strong semantic product while preserving zero false \glue{} decisions and full clean-merge coverage.

The broader expert/raw-text atlas has not been executed. Consequently, this paper does not use newly commissioned inter-annotator agreement, raw-paper coordinate extraction, live ontology-engine behavior, generated-portrait recoverability, or downstream answer quality as support for the headline. Those are prospective transfer tests rather than qualifications on the demonstrated structured-mapping result. Scientific identity can also remain theory- and domain-dependent, which is why the contract retains \unresolved{}, \obstruction{}, and \pluralview{} rather than forcing every mapping into a universal canonical identity.

The registered coordinate system is sufficient for the executed studies, not asserted to be the only possible scientific ontology. An information-equivalent typed product supplied the same scientific coordinates and authorization rules ties P3-X exactly. This establishes architecture portability of the tested semantics: the positive claim concerns the claim-relative scientific-identity contract, not a requirement that it be implemented in one centralized ORION component.

\subsection{Ethics}

The scoped public-reference study reuses published research resources and collects no new human-participant data; P3-X uses exact authored contracts rather than new human annotations. ORION's \obstruction{} and \pluralview{} terminals are provenance-bound integration states, not rankings of scientific communities or perspectives. Source lineage and non-preserved coordinates remain inspectable so a downstream user can audit why a merge was admitted, blocked, pluralized, or left unresolved.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

The public-reference initial and confirmatory sets, source revisions, execution identities, evaluator, bootstrap seed, practical margins, and decision rule are content-bound before confirmatory outcomes. P3-X is separately frozen under \texttt{research/claim\_expansion/p3}, with protected identities, outcome-access chronology, canonical-row digest, result artifact, and independent verifier. The original broader protocol remains preserved as an extension path; any raw-text, expert-gold, live-engine, recoverability, or downstream-utility promotion requires a new prospective artifact rather than reinterpretation of the two current studies.
