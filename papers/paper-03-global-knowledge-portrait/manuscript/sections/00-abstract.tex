% Abstract (standalone fragment included by main.tex).
Cross-domain scientific synthesis is vulnerable to false integration:
identical names may denote different referents, nominally similar
constructs may rest on incompatible operationalizations, and apparently
contradictory claims may differ only in context, modality or attribution.
This paper treats integration as a decision that has to be licensed rather
than as a similarity score to be thresholded. Each source is represented as
a source-local scientific projection bound to its provenance, and a proposed
cross-source mapping records referent identity, construct identity,
measurement and operationalization, context and representation equivalence as
separate coordinates. Compatible projections may be glued into one global
statement only under explicit preservation conditions; where a load-bearing
coordinate is incompatible or unresolved the mapping remains a typed
obstruction or a plural view rather than being forced into one canonical
summary. Local exact worlds falsify the mapping semantics. In a disjoint,
prospectively execution-frozen 32-case confirmatory holdout assembled from
pinned external human and expert resources, coordinate-governed mapping made
zero false merges against 0.1875 for flat predicate canonicalization; the
paired difference was $-0.1875$ (95\% bootstrap CI $[-0.34375,-0.0625]$).
Against an exact-coordinate conservative control, the false-split difference
was $0.000$ with interval $[0.000,0.000]$, satisfying the rule declared before
those outputs were seen. A separate post-saturation battery then granted its
comparator a strong semantic product carrying structured construct,
measurement, context, provenance and missingness information, and tested only
whether representational alignment is sufficient authority for scientific
identity. On 400 prospectively frozen exact heterogeneous mapping contracts,
the claim-relative compatibility layer decided 400/400 exactly against 250/400
for that strong product, a paired difference of $+0.375$ (95\%
domain-stratified bootstrap CI $[0.3275,0.4225]$), with no false merges and
complete clean-merge coverage. An ideal information-equivalent typed product
given the same coordinates and rules tied at 400/400, which rules out an
inherent-expressivity reading. These results concern already-structured
scientific projections and exact successor contracts only. Raw-text
extraction, live ontology-engine generality, newly commissioned expert
agreement, recoverability in generated portraits and downstream utility all
remain undetermined.
