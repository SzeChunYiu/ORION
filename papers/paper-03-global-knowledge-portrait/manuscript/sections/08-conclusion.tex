\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

ORION Paper III asks whether a global knowledge portrait --- an integration of scientific claims across disciplines and measurement systems --- can be built without treating surface or representational similarity as scientific identity. The framework makes three structural commitments.

First, \emph{projection before mapping}. Each source retains a local projection whose scientific meaning is represented by typed coordinates and bound to exact provenance before any cross-source decision is made. This keeps the question of what a source means separate from the question of which sources may be combined.

Second, \emph{GLUE-or-obstruction}. Integration is decided over typed mapping hypotheses: projections are merged only when the relevant compatibility conditions are satisfied; otherwise the system reports a typed obstruction, contextual difference, plural portrait or unresolved state rather than silently flattening the distinction.

Third, \emph{recoverability}. The intended global portrait exposes its construction --- source projections, mapping path, preservation conditions, and non-preserved coordinates --- so that a downstream consumer can audit a global statement back to its origins.

The formal projection/mapping semantics are verified by local exact worlds. The prospectively frozen public-reference route provides a bounded external check of the mapping layer without requiring a newly commissioned annotation team. On the disjoint 32-case confirmatory holdout assembled from pinned public human/expert resources, ORION's typed comparison produced no false merges, whereas flat predicate canonicalization false-merged 0.1875 of cases; the paired difference was $-0.1875$ with a 95\% bootstrap interval of $[-0.34375,-0.0625]$. Against the exact-coordinate conservative control, the false-split difference was $0.000$ with interval $[0.000,0.000]$, so the predeclared confirmatory rule passed.

The separate post-saturation P3-X successor tests a wider systems interpretation after granting strong semantic integration and current interoperability/provenance mechanisms to the comparator. On 400 prospectively frozen exact already-structured contracts, P3-X made 400/400 exact scientific-integration decisions versus 250/400 for a strong semantic product and 50/400 for canonical matching; the paired P3-X-minus-strong-product effect was $+0.375$ with a domain-stratified 95\% bootstrap interval of $[0.3275,0.4225]$. It made zero false \glue{} decisions and preserved 1.0 clean-merge coverage. An ideal product receiving the exact same scientific coordinates and rules tied P3-X 400/400, which is an explicit negative boundary against inherent-expressivity or centralization claims.

Together, the two studies support a bounded principle stronger than the original flat-canonicalization result: \emph{representational compatibility is not itself scientific identity authority}. In the tested contracts, an explicit claim-relative compatibility layer can preserve obstruction, plurality and unresolved state after structured semantic alignment rather than letting a technically valid mapping self-authorize a scientific merge.

The later protected zero-error regime-transition study provides a complementary ownership boundary. ORION-JUMP and the strongest verified representation-regime revision parent tie exactly on both disjoint frozen splits, including correspondence/preservation and protected-consequence outcomes with zero false jumps. The result therefore does not establish a distinct ORION representation-invention mechanism. It instead reinforces the role Paper III can legitimately own: representation changes must carry explicit correspondence, preservation, obstruction and reopening semantics, while the transformation mechanism itself remains parent-owned when the strongest registered parent is extensionally sufficient.

That statement remains deliberately narrower than end-to-end scientific integration. I-ADOPT-style variable semantics, ontology/schema/entity alignment, provenance, provenance-aware pluralism and generic consistency checking remain donor territory. Raw-text extraction, live ontology-engine generality, expert agreement on new domain corpora, recoverability of generated global portraits and downstream scientific utility remain \conststatus{} until their own prospectively frozen evidence exists.
