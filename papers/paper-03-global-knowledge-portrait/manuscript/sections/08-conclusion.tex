\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Paper III establishes a strong principle for global scientific synthesis: \emph{representational compatibility is useful evidence for integration, but it is not scientific identity authority}. ORION therefore performs projection before mapping. Each source retains a local, provenance-bound scientific projection, and cross-source integration is decided over explicit coordinates for referent, construct, measurement, context, representation, and unresolved obstruction. The resulting global portrait remains recoverable because every accepted \glue{} decision and every preserved plural or obstructed view can be traced back to the source-local scientific meaning that justified it.

The confirmatory public-reference study shows that this discipline prevents false synthesis without paying a splitting penalty on the tested cases. On the prospectively frozen disjoint 32-case holdout, ORION produces \textbf{zero false merges} while flat predicate canonicalization false-merges 0.1875 of cases; the paired difference is $-0.1875$ with 95\% bootstrap CI $[-0.34375,-0.0625]$. Against the exact-coordinate conservative control, the false-split difference is exactly $0.000$ with interval $[0.000,0.000]$. The result therefore improves integration precision without replacing false merges with indiscriminate separation.

The post-saturation P3-X successor raises the comparison standard by granting the comparator a rich semantic integration substrate before testing the remaining scientific-identity authority question. Across \textbf{400 prospectively frozen exact heterogeneous mapping contracts}, P3-X makes \textbf{400/400 exact scientific-integration decisions} versus 250/400 for the strong semantic product and 50/400 for canonical matching. The paired P3-X-minus-strong-product advantage is $+0.375$ with domain-stratified 95\% bootstrap CI $[0.3275,0.4225]$, while P3-X records zero false \glue{} decisions and 1.0 clean-merge coverage.

An information-equivalent typed donor product implementing the same scientific coordinates and claim-relative authorization rules also reaches 400/400. This is a portability theorem in systems form: the decisive scientific-integration semantics survive architecture changes. P3 therefore contributes a reusable integration contract that can be implemented centrally or as a correctly typed product without changing the registered scientific decision.

Together, the studies support the strongest demonstrated statement of Paper III. For already-structured scientific projections and the registered exact heterogeneous contracts, \textbf{claim-relative identity authority converts semantic alignment into recoverable scientific integration while preserving meaningful obstruction and plurality}. It eliminates the false merges observed under both flat canonicalization and the strong alignment-only comparator while retaining perfect clean-merge coverage. Raw-text and live-system transfer remain natural next empirical extensions with current authority \conststatus{}, not reductions of the demonstrated contract-level result.
