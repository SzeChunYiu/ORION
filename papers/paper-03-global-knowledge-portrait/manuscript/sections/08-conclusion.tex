\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Paper III establishes a strong principle for global scientific synthesis: representational compatibility is useful evidence for integration, but it is not by itself scientific identity authority. ORION therefore performs projection before mapping. Each source retains a local, provenance-bound scientific projection, and cross-source integration is decided over explicit coordinates for referent, construct, measurement, context, representation, and unresolved obstruction. The resulting global portrait remains recoverable because every accepted \glue{} decision and every preserved plural or obstructed view can be traced back to the source-local scientific meaning that justified it.

The confirmatory public-reference study shows that this discipline prevents false synthesis without paying a splitting penalty on the tested cases. On the prospectively frozen disjoint 32-case holdout, ORION produces zero false merges while flat predicate canonicalization false-merges 0.1875 of cases; the paired difference is $-0.1875$ with 95\% bootstrap CI $[-0.34375,-0.0625]$. Against the exact-coordinate conservative control, the false-split difference is exactly $0.000$ with interval $[0.000,0.000]$. Thus the confirmatory gain is not merely a move from aggressive merging to indiscriminate separation.

The post-saturation P3-X successor strengthens the conclusion by granting the comparator a rich semantic integration substrate before testing the remaining authority question. Across 400 prospectively frozen exact heterogeneous mapping contracts, P3-X makes 400/400 exact scientific-integration decisions versus 250/400 for the strong semantic product and 50/400 for canonical matching. The paired P3-X-minus-strong-product advantage is $+0.375$ with domain-stratified 95\% bootstrap CI $[0.3275,0.4225]$, while P3-X records zero false \glue{} decisions and 1.0 clean-merge coverage.

An information-equivalent typed donor product implementing the same scientific coordinates and claim-relative authorization rules also reaches 400/400. This is a positive systems result: the decisive semantics are portable across architecture boundaries. P3's contribution is therefore a reusable scientific-integration contract, not a claim that one centralized implementation is inherently more expressive than any correctly typed product.

Together, the studies support the strongest demonstrated statement of Paper III. For already-structured scientific projections and the registered exact heterogeneous contracts, claim-relative identity authority converts semantic alignment into recoverable integration while preserving scientifically meaningful obstruction and plurality, eliminating the false merges observed under flat canonicalization and the strong alignment-only comparator. Raw-text extraction, live ontology-engine generality, newly commissioned expert agreement, generated-portrait recoverability, and downstream scientific utility are separate extension axes; they do not reduce the established contract-level result.
