## Typed scientific ignorance V1

`IgnoranceProjection.v1` preserves source-local statements of missing/incomplete knowledge and their implied knowledge goals. It intentionally does **not** assert that a gap is globally real/current/exhaustive. Eight action-relevant kinds route to evidence search, subquestion opening, discriminator design, replication/experiment, barrier repair or future-work retention/freshness checking. Every plan requires independent confirmation and creates no scientific authority.

A frozen routing discriminator verifies that this typing selects the correct distinct next actions for incomplete evidence, controversy, anomalies, research barriers and future-work proposals, and strictly outperforms a generic `gap -> search evidence` controller on the exact cases. Real text-to-ignorance extraction remains empirical-open.
