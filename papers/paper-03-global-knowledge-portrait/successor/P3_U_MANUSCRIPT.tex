\begin{abstract}
Scientific knowledge integration fails when lexical, schema, graph, or embedding similarity is mistaken for scientific identity. ORION Paper III targets the strongest upward claim: across heterogeneous scientific domains, source types, representations, temporal regimes, and downstream tasks, can ORION build a more scientifically faithful global knowledge portrait than a donor-complete integration system that already has modern scientific knowledge-graph extraction, ontology/schema alignment, entity matching, provenance, RAG synthesis, and compiled-state mechanisms? The bounded predecessor result that an information-equivalent typed product can match ORION is preserved as evidence that representation centralization alone is not sufficient. P3-U generalizes over that tie by forcing the comparator to receive the same strong representational mechanisms and asking whether ORION can better acquire, compose, transport, and revise the scientific identity relation itself from raw evidence. Protected cases include construct drift, measurement mismatch, context dependence, provenance/stance differences, temporal change, contradictions, one-to-many mappings, cross-representation transport, and states that are adequate for prediction but insufficient for integration authority. Primary outcomes are false merge, false split, plurality preservation, obstruction detection, unresolved calibration, provenance recoverability, downstream scientific synthesis/QA utility, cross-domain transfer, and resource cost. The intended terminal is replicated general scientific identity/integration superiority over the strongest donor-complete system, ideally including at least one newly discovered identity coordinate or integration mechanic that expands protected reach beyond the frozen donor basis. Historical ties and negative cases remain immutable and drive upward mechanism search rather than default claim contraction.
\end{abstract}

\section{Largest scientific question}
The target is deliberately broad:
\begin{quote}
Can ORION outperform the strongest donor-complete scientific integration systems at deciding what scientific objects are the same, different, plural, incompatible, or unresolved, and can that advantage improve downstream science across domains and representations?
\end{quote}

Scientific literature can already be transformed into provenance-aware knowledge graphs with schema-constrained LLM pipelines \cite{scigraphllm2026}, end-to-end scientific ontology/KG generation is active prior work \cite{oarga2026}, and scholarly identity matching is studied directly \cite{ontodup2026}. P3-U therefore treats these mechanisms as available to the comparator. Nearest-work pressure increases the baseline and moves the paper upward: ORION must generalize over extraction, alignment, matching, provenance, and representation mechanisms rather than survive by claiming a smaller local residual.

\section{Upward-generalization principle}
Any donor that solves a P3-U subclass is absorbed into the strongest comparator. The ORION claim is earned only through broader behavior such as:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item superior identity decisions after coordinate-equalized extraction;
    \item superior raw-to-identity end-to-end performance across domains and source types;
    \item correct routing between glue, distinction, plurality, obstruction, and unresolved regimes;
    \item robustness to representation and schema changes with valid transport/reopening;
    \item higher downstream scientific synthesis/QA utility;
    \item discovery of a new identity coordinate or integration mechanic that expands protected reach and transfers beyond the originating failure family.
\end{enumerate}
The intended terminal is \textbf{GENERAL_SCIENTIFIC_IDENTITY_INTEGRATION_SUPERIORITY}. Narrower results remain fallback scientific terminals only if this broad claim is not earned.

\section{Immutable predecessor boundary}
The predecessor's information-equivalent typed product tie is retained as a portability result. It rules out the idea that ORION wins merely because its representation is centralized, graph-shaped, or typed. P3-U turns that tie into a stronger regime-selection question: when strong representations are equalized, can ORION still outperform by learning and applying a more general scientific identity relation, and can it recognize when the current state is insufficient for that responsibility?

\section{Identity relation and evidence coordinates}
P3-U evaluates
\[
I(x,y\mid c,e)\in\{\textsc{Glue},\textsc{Distinct},\textsc{Plural},\textsc{Obstruction},\textsc{Unresolved}\},
\]
where $c$ is scientific context and $e$ is provenance-bound evidence.

Candidate decisions may use construct definition, operationalization, measurement procedure, scale/unit, population/context, intervention, causal role, temporal validity, provenance, source stance, uncertainty, and correspondence maps. No fixed list is assumed universal. The programme tests whether ORION can identify which coordinates are load-bearing in a new regime and, when existing coordinates are insufficient, propose a new coordinate/mechanic prospectively.

\section{Compiled-view and representation stress test}
The same case is presented through four views:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item raw source evidence;
    \item canonical universal extracted state;
    \item task-compiled state;
    \item responsibility-carrying state that declares which identity obligation it is sufficient to answer.
\end{enumerate}
If two views are certified semantics-preserving and sufficient for the identity responsibility, the promoted decision must be invariant. If a compact state is adequate for QA or prediction but omits a coordinate needed for identity, the correct terminal is state insufficiency / \textsc{Unresolved}, and the raw source must be reopenable. Representation or schema changes require explicit certificate transport or invalidation.

\section{Source recoverability}
Every promoted \textsc{Glue}, \textsc{Distinct}, \textsc{Plural}, or \textsc{Obstruction} decision must cite the source spans/records that carry its load-bearing coordinates. A generated ontology label, embedding score, or confidence value cannot self-authorize identity.

\section{Baselines}
All systems receive matched model, evidence access, and resource budgets. Required comparators include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item lexical/embedding entity matching;
    \item schema/ontology alignment;
    \item provenance-aware scientific KG extraction in the style of modern constrained pipelines \cite{scigraphllm2026};
    \item LLM raw-text pair judge;
    \item RAG synthesis with entity canonicalization;
    \item scientific ontology/KG generation;
    \item compiled-state/responsibility-aware integration;
    \item donor-complete product containing all preceding mechanisms and allowed to route among them;
    \item strongest current scientific integration system available at freeze time;
    \item an oracle identity-coordinate ceiling for analysis only.
\end{enumerate}
Coordinate-equalized arms isolate the identity rule; raw-text arms measure full-system value.

\section{Primary hypotheses}
\paragraph{H1, generalized identity superiority.}
Across the heterogeneous benchmark, ORION reduces false scientific merges versus the strongest runnable donor-complete comparator by a prospectively frozen margin.

\paragraph{H2, non-compensatory plurality guard.}
The H1 gain is invalid if false splits or loss of legitimate plurality exceeds frozen non-inferiority guards.

\paragraph{H3, downstream scientific superiority.}
The ORION portrait yields higher protected scientific synthesis/QA utility under matched downstream models and evidence.

\paragraph{H4, cross-representation and cross-domain transfer.}
The H1--H3 advantage survives held-out domains, schemas, source forms, and state representations under independent annotation and implementation.

\paragraph{H5, mechanism expansion.}
At least one negative/tied family yields a new identity coordinate or integration mechanic that expands protected reach beyond the pre-freeze donor-complete basis and transfers to fresh scientific families.

\section{Statistics and annotation authority}
The scientific item/pair, not repeated model samples, is the default independent unit. Before protected outcomes, freeze sampling, family weights, adjudication procedure, agreement statistic, superiority/non-inferiority margins, multiplicity handling, confidence-interval method, and treatment of unresolved cases.

Report false-merge and false-split effects separately with uncertainty. Do not collapse them into one F1 score that can hide asymmetric scientific harm. Report expert agreement and disagreement strata; adjudication does not erase genuine ambiguity.

\section{Adversarial controls}
Mandatory attacks include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item identical terminology with scientifically different constructs;
    \item different terminology for the same construct;
    \item schema-compatible objects with incompatible measurement semantics;
    \item same typed graph but different provenance/stance;
    \item globally information-equivalent states where one interface hides the decisive identity coordinate;
    \item compact state adequate for QA but inadequate for integration;
    \item source-round-trip failure after a promoted merge;
    \item LLM judge agreement caused by shared prompt/template leakage;
    \item donor-complete dynamic routing that selects among extraction, ontology, matching, provenance, and compiled-state mechanisms.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Negative-result recursion as upward search}
Each false merge, false split, plurality loss, obstruction miss, or transfer failure is attributed to one primary candidate cause:
\[
\{\text{extraction},\text{identity rule},\text{state/interface sufficiency},\text{provenance},\text{temporal validity},\text{annotation ambiguity},\text{representation transport},\text{benchmark leakage}\}.
\]
The first discriminator localizes the cause. The programme then searches materially different successor mechanisms and parent disciplines for a more general identity rule, coordinate system, or transport mechanism. For example, an extraction error is re-tested with gold coordinates before changing the identity rule; recurrent identity failures across multiple coordinate-complete families may justify adding or learning a new scientific coordinate; representation failures may justify a transport or responsibility-sufficiency mechanism rather than a new ontology label. Successors must transfer to fresh families and improve downstream science, not merely repair the originating pair.

ORION issue \#651 is the authority-bearing P3-U recovery programme. New child issues are opened only for a distinct discovered coordinate/mechanic or transport family with a frozen discriminator. Gold relabeling after viewing system errors, post-hoc exclusion of hard pairs, and baseline weakening are prohibited.

\section{Claim ladder and current status}
The intended progression is: protocol frozen; protected identity superiority; downstream scientific superiority; cross-domain/cross-representation replication; \textbf{GENERAL_SCIENTIFIC_IDENTITY_INTEGRATION_SUPERIORITY} with H5 mechanism expansion. A narrower terminal is an honest fallback only if the broad claim is not earned. Negative, tied, and \textsc{CannotCheck} families remain visible.
