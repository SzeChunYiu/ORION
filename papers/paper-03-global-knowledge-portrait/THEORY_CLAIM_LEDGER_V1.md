## Authority rules

1. Theorems may be stated generally only with their observation/world/loss
   assumptions visible.
2. The 36-case partial-observation corpus witnesses `P3.EPE.T1`; it does not
   estimate a natural prevalence of partial identification.
3. The 768-cluster protocol may test floor-adjusted harm, but local preflight
   and planned power are not scientific outcomes.
4. A narrow envelope is not a success unless independent gold is contained at
   the declared coverage rate. Sharpness without validity is failure.
5. No self-label, synthetic source, or language-model proposal becomes
   independent gold by appearing in a theorem or development packet.
6. The information-equivalent product tie is retained. The theory targets a
   sufficient authority interface, not inherent expressivity or centralised
   implementation.
