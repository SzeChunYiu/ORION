## 3. Refined abstract for adoption

Scientific workflows increasingly accumulate provenance, verification, version, authorization, and reproducibility signals before deciding whether a claim may be promoted. More favorable signals do not necessarily make that decision recoverable. If the representation given to a decision rule merges scientific states that require different target terminals, no downstream classifier, search procedure, or aggregation rule can reconstruct the erased distinction.

We formalize promotion as a target-specific finite decision problem. Exact recovery from a representation is possible if and only if the correct terminal is constant on every positive-mass representation fibre and is expressible by the interface's output alphabet. Mixed fibres impose the exact fibrewise Bayes-error floor. The criterion makes information adequacy logically prior to model strength and gives a required equivalence control: any competing implementation supplied with the same target-defining coordinates and terminal semantics should tie.

Two protected campaigns test the consequence. In the first, the target-sufficient rule promotes all 60 clean cases and makes no false promotions among 360 hostile or insufficient-evidence opportunities; the strongest frozen generic mechanism proxy makes 180 false promotions on the same hostile set. A prespecified non-discriminating hypothesis remains negative. In a second study spanning five artifact domains, eight case archetypes, and ten protected variants, the target-sufficient rule is correct on all 400 cases. A strong generic product carrying provenance, local verification, version, evaluator custody, epoch, and generic authorization information is correct on 250 of 400; the paired difference is 0.375 with a domain-stratified bootstrap 95% interval of 0.3275–0.4225. An information-equivalent product ties exactly at 400/400.

The bounded conclusion is that scientific promotion is a target-bound relation rather than a scalar accumulation of generic assurance. The contribution is the sufficiency boundary and a non-compensatory evaluation contract, not universal authority, a universally minimal coordinate system, deployed-agent generalization, or architectural superiority.

> Exact promotion from representation `r` is possible iff, on every positive-mass fibre induced by `r`, the target terminal is constant and belongs to the available output alphabet.

Immediately distinguish:

- interface inexpressivity from scientific inferiority of an underlying artifact;
