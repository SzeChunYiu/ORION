# TMLR editor note — 2026-08-28

**Manuscript:** *When Verification Axes Can Support Scientific Claims: Identifiability, Attainability, and Non-Compensatory Promotion*  
**Corresponding author:** Sze Chun Yiu  
**Email:** sze-chun.yiu@fysik.su.se

Dear TMLR Editors,

Please consider this manuscript for review at *Transactions on Machine Learning Research*. It addresses a measurement question for learning-based research agents and other intelligent systems: when can a verification score support a competence claim, rather than merely reflect an unattainable terminal, a construction shortcut, or a non-discriminating panel?

The paper contributes a formal framework based on fibrewise Bayes risk, target factorization, terminal-preserving adapters, data processing, total variation, nuisance identifiability, and panel resolution. Three finite evidence layers instantiate different parts of that framework without being pooled. In the protected V2 battery, the governed non-compensatory relation produced 0/360 false promotions versus 180/360 for the strongest frozen mechanism proxy, while both promoted 60/60 clean positives. The registered V2 H3 contrast was saturated and remains reported as a negative measurement result. A distinct V3 battery reports 30/30 versus 0/30 and 15/30 only as terminal/interface attainability. In 400 P4-X exact contracts, the target-bound relation scored 400/400 versus 250/400 for a donor-complete generic product and 50/400 for a compensatory product; an information-equivalent typed product tied at 400/400, which rules out a centralization or unique-expressivity interpretation.

The manuscript does not claim novelty for provenance, attribution, verification, abstention, evaluator integrity, or generic authorization in isolation. It also does not claim naturalistic, deployed-system, or external-software superiority. Comparator labels refer to protocol-matched mechanism reimplementations. The anonymous supplement contains safe aggregate result objects, canonical-source checksums, and a standalone verifier for the released counts and boundaries; protected per-case gold and raw traces remain withheld under the declared custody design.

A first-page footnote discloses the use of a large language model for manuscript editing and review-package preparation. The authors remain responsible for all scientific ideas, claims, results, citations, and submission content.

Thank you for your consideration.
