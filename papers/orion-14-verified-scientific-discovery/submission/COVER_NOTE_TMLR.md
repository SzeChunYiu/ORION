# Draft note to the TMLR Action Editor

**Manuscript:** *Non-Escalating Scientific Authority under Content-Bound Evidence and Protected Evaluation*

This submission studies whether scientific-authority promotion should be a non-compensatory transition over evidence identity, source ownership, semantic support, checker independence/discrimination, evidence influence, evaluator/holdout integrity, and contamination status.

We explicitly do **not** claim provenance, attribution, iterative verification, contamination detection, or evaluator protection as standalone novelties. The empirical contribution is a prospectively frozen, independently hosted 420-case protected campaign on a repaired subject. Full ORION produced 0/360 false promotions versus 180/360 for the strongest frozen comparator mechanism while both promoted 60/60 clean positives. The predeclared abstention-superiority hypothesis was null and is reported as such. Eight registered ablations and an independent reproduction are included.

Comparator arms are protocol-matched mechanism reimplementations under a common packet/budget; the manuscript does not present them as executions of the cited authors' original systems. Protected labels remain in custody, while safe aggregates, a post-run public slice, code, hashes, and the reproduction receipt are archived.
