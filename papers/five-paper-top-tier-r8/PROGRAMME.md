## R8 work completed in this branch packet

### AB: realized production separation outside Q1

A complete XOR-aggregation production grammar is added as a non-Pauli calibration. States are nonzero-total multisets of nonzero vectors in `F_2^d`. The weak proof language deletes proper zero-XOR submultisets. The production language additionally fuses two distinct fragments `u,v` into `u xor v`.

The basis word is a real production state, is terminal under the weak language, and has length `d`. Every nonzero-total production state reduces to one fragment under the strong language. Thus the exact weak and strong terminal budgets are `d` and `1`, with certificate waste `d-1`. The finite controls exhaust all nonzero-total multisets through length `d+1` for `d=2,3,4` and reproduce the theorem.

This closes the *existence* of a realized production separation outside Q1. It does not by itself establish broad compiler significance or novelty; the nearest-work and production-relevance audit remains open.

### C: exact multi-domain FiberGuard benchmark

This closes the internal three-domain exact benchmark and matched refinement blocker. It does not establish real-world collision prevalence or predictive performance on distributional data.

### D: typed merge-safety checker and hostile census

The R8 artifact implements coordinatewise Horn closure, merged closure, and the exact criterion:

> a merged coordinate derives exactly the union of the original coordinate closures if and only if that union is closed under every merged rule.

Three explicit unsafe classes are replayed: seed/rule splice, conjunctive-premise splice, and recursive bridge splice. Dominance and disconnected-support controls remain safe.

An exhaustive census over all ordered pairs of 4,096 license signatures on three claims and a nine-rule pool checks 16,777,216 coordinate pairs. It finds 878,592 unsafe merges and zero disagreements with the safe-merge criterion.

This closes the internal checker/attack-suite blocker. The independent real-policy/domain-expert gate remains open.

## Evidence vocabulary

- `VERIFIED`: an all-parameter proof appears in the manuscript.
- `FINITE_EXACT`: a complete finite state space is specified and exhaustively evaluated.
- `INDEPENDENT_REPLAY_PENDING`: a primary result exists but clean-room reproduction is incomplete.
- `CONDITIONAL`: all premises are explicit and at least one is not proved in the paper.
- `EXTERNAL_GATE`: a production, domain-expert, empirical, or journal decision is absent.
- `NOT_INFERRED`: the tempting stronger claim does not follow.
- `CANNOT_CHECK_RESOURCE_BOUND`: a declared method exhausted its resources without a verdict.

## Top-tier promotion rule

A manuscript is promoted only when all of the following hold:

1. its scientific identity is independent of Q1 and the other papers;
2. every theorem is integrated into one canonical manuscript rather than left in addenda;
3. every computational theorem has a complete state-space reduction, hostile controls, and independent replay;
4. every headline novelty statement survives a current primary-source audit;
5. every systems/application headline has direct evidence in the named domain;
6. the target venue's contribution bar matches the actual theorem and evidence.

Internal scores, green workflows, or model agreement cannot grant publication authority.
