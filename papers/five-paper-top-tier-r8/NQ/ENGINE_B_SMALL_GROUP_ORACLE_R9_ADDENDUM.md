# NQ R9 Addendum: Independent Small-Group Oracle for Generalized Davenport Engines

## 1. Role in the proof programme

The registered `D_3(C_5^3)=25` computation uses a specialized complement/extension architecture. A second implementation should not merely translate that code. Before an independent Engine B is trusted on LUNARC, its disjoint-zero-sum packing core needs a complete exact test suite generated by a different decomposition.

This addendum supplies that suite. It enumerates every sequence multiset at the two decisive lengths `D_k(G)-1` and `D_k(G)` for several small finite abelian groups. Each sequence is expanded into labeled positions, all nonempty zero-sum position subsets are computed directly, and the maximum number of pairwise disjoint zero-sum blocks is solved twice by distinct bitmask recurrences.

The oracle is exact on its declared small groups. It does not independently replay `C_5^3` and grants no authority to the main computer-assisted theorem.

## 2. Definition

For a finite abelian group `G`, let `D_k(G)` be the least length `ell` such that every sequence of length `ell` over `G` contains `k` pairwise disjoint nonempty zero-sum subsequences.

For one labeled sequence `S` of length `n`, define the zero-sum hypergraph:

- vertices are the `n` sequence positions;
- a nonempty vertex subset is a hyperedge exactly when its group sum is zero.

The maximum number of disjoint zero-sum subsequences is the hypergraph matching number.

## 3. Two exact packing formulations

### Solver A — first-position branching

For a set of available positions, choose its least position. Either leave that position unused or place it in one zero-sum hyperedge contained in the available set. Recurse after deleting the chosen hyperedge. Memoization by the available-position mask gives the exact packing number.

### Solver B — full submask dynamic programming

For every available-position mask, compare leaving one position unused with removing each zero-sum submask. This recurrence is evaluated independently of Solver A's hyperedge-indexed branch structure.

Both formulations permit unused positions, require nonempty blocks, and enforce disjointness by mask deletion. The verifier requires equality on every enumerated sequence.

## 4. Registered exact panel

The panel checks the following classical constants:

- `D_k(C_2)=2k` for `k=1,2,3`;
- `D_k(C_3)=3k` for `k=1,2,3`;
- `D_k(C_2^2)=2k+1` for `k=1,2,3`;
- `D_k(C_3^2)=3k+2` for `k=1,2`.

For each pair `(G,k)`, the verifier exhausts every multiset sequence of lengths `D_k(G)-1` and `D_k(G)`. It requires:

1. a lower-length sequence whose packing number is at most `k-1`;
2. every upper-length sequence to have packing number at least `k`;
3. the minimum packing numbers at the two lengths to be exactly `k-1` and `k`; and
4. Solver A and Solver B to agree on every sequence.

The panel contains 21,604 complete sequence-multiset checks. It includes zero elements, repeated elements, and all labeled-position zero-sum subsets.

## 5. Why this is structurally independent

This oracle does not use:

- rank-three complement classification;
- six-term extension generation;
- normalized complement representatives;
- source-lift constraints;
- the registered 98,622/230,983 enumeration; or
- the existing exact three-bin packing implementation.

Its state space is the complete sequence multiset itself, and its packing problem is solved on position masks. It is therefore useful for differential testing of sequence encoding, zero-sum detection, repeated-element handling, disjointness, and off-by-one terminals.

It is not computationally suitable for a naive full `C_5^3` replay. Engine B must introduce a separate exact scaling architecture, such as SAT/CP-SAT, meet-in-the-middle, orbit decomposition, or another independently justified method.

## 6. Required use by Codex and LUNARC

Before Engine B is submitted on the main `C_5^3` task, it should consume the oracle panel through a neutral interchange format and reproduce every terminal without importing the oracle's solver code.

The required differential terminals are:

- `AGREE_ALL_SMALL_GROUPS`;
- `DISAGREE_SEQUENCE_ENCODING`;
- `DISAGREE_ZERO_SUM_SUBSETS`;
- `DISAGREE_PACKING_NUMBER`;
- `DISAGREE_DK_BOUNDARY`;
- `RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED`; or
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

Any disagreement is a scientific blocker. The test case and both traces must be preserved before repair.

## 7. Authority boundary

A PASS establishes an exact small-group differential oracle and a hostile regression suite. It does not establish:

- independence of a future `C_5^3` replay;
- completeness of an unimplemented scaling reduction;
- correctness of the registered complement/extension architecture;
- `D_3(C_5^3)=25`;
- `D_4(C_5^3)`; or
- journal acceptance.

Those claims require the separately registered LUNARC replay, source manifests, negative controls, scheduler receipts, and independent proof audit.
