## Claim subtraction

FiberGuard does **not** claim as new:

- algorithm selection from instance features;
- PAR10 or ASlib evaluation;
- random-forest runtime prediction or oracle-action classification;
- feature acquisition with cost;
- adaptive decision trees;
- minimax regret or LP duality;
- set-cover complexity;
- abstention, selective prediction or conformal calibration;
- database-style hashing, receipts or replay.

The residual candidate contribution is the following combined object:

1. the exact complete fibre induced by the action system's declared representation;
2. deterministic/randomized action-regret and epsilon-safe action certificates on that fibre;
3. action-count witness compression and minimal action-conflict hypergraphs;
4. exact static repair as coverage of those representation-owned conflicts;
5. statewise common-oracle loss profiles for acquisition costs that vary within an observation cell;
6. compact mixed-tree upper and adversarial-prior/Bayes-tree lower receipts;
7. an evidence programme that retains complete-corpus positives, prospective robust failures, adverse domains and learned-comparator losses without promotion across evidence classes.

## Current nearest-work judgment

The exact bundle above has not been identified in the registered search, but that is not external novelty authority. The empirical method is deliberately complementary to established selectors: R16 shows common runtime regression can outperform the exact robust cell action. The publishable positioning is therefore **representation certification around learned action selection**, not a new universally superior selector.

## Required independent review

Before a top-tier submission, an independent algorithm-selection/ML reviewer must audit:

- whether the configured and censor-aware comparator set is adequate;
- whether all ASlib aggregation, missingness, feature-cost and CV choices are faithful;
- whether the residual theorem bundle overlaps information-based complexity, robust decision theory or adaptive diagnosis more closely than this matrix records;
- whether the empirical evidence supports the final abstract without promotion from finite panels to population claims.
