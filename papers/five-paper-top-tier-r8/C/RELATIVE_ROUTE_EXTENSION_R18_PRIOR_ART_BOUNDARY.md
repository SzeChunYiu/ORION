# FiberGuard R18 prior-art subtraction boundary

The following mechanisms are treated as donor-owned and receive zero novelty credit:

- selective classification and reject-option prediction;
- learning to defer to an expert or fallback system;
- contextual routing and mixture-of-experts gating;
- finite minimax and linear-programming duality;
- split conformal and conformal-risk calibration;
- active feature acquisition and value-of-information control;
- Lipschitz and metric extension from labeled anchors;
- covariate, domain, and concept-shift bounds;
- algorithm selection from instance features;
- nearest-neighbor and learned loss prediction;
- generic adaptivity gaps and decision-tree dynamic programming.

The residual candidate contribution is narrower:

1. exact complete representation fibres are the authority-bearing closed-world object;
2. every action, fallback, defer leaf, and acquisition charge is reduced to total excess over one common statewise solver oracle;
3. state-dependent acquisition is represented by complete loss profiles, not a scalar refund;
4. deterministic and randomized static/adaptive policies admit exact finite upper and lower receipts;
5. exact fibre authority is separated from structural metric authority and statistical marginal authority;
6. fallback alignment is a paired action obligation, not a consequence of learned-action calibration;
7. pre- and post-acquisition routing are different decision problems unless the route is measurable from free information;
8. cross-scenario certificate transfer requires a content-bound bridge and an explicit relative-loss drift margin;
9. positive transductive results and prospective inductive refutations are both retained in the same theorem/evidence story.

This document is a subtraction rule, not a novelty certificate. Current specialist source review and external adjudication remain `CANNOT_CHECK`.
