## Submission rule

A high-selectivity theory/algorithms submission can proceed on the exact theorem package after hostile replay and current source audit. A broad ML impact claim additionally requires C-R9-13 and a production-derived prevalence/value study. Failure of that gate narrows the paper; it does not weaken the exact fibre theorems.
