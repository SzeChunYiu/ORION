# Five-paper R9 primary-source audit seed

**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Status:** search plan and known-donor matrix, not a novelty certificate

## Audit rule

For every claimed contribution, the final record must contain:

1. exact primary source and version/date;
2. theorem, proposition, algorithm, or mechanism inspected;
3. hypotheses and representation compared;
4. relation `IDENTICAL`, `STRICTLY_STRONGER_DONOR`, `STRICTLY_WEAKER_DONOR`, `ORTHOGONAL`, `APPLICATION_ONLY`, or `NOT_DETERMINED`;
5. what is adopted, adapted, composed, deferred, or rejected;
6. source and note digests; and
7. unresolved databases/authors/topics.

`NOT_FOUND` under bounded search is never encoded as novelty authority.

## 5. Search execution and terminals

Each paper receives independent search strands:

1. exact theorem/name search;
2. citation graph backward/forward search;
3. author/topic search;
4. journal/database search;
5. arXiv/preprint current search through 2026-08-26; and
6. direct reading of the closest primary sources.

Allowed terminals per atomic claim:

- `DONOR_IDENTICAL__NOVELTY_WITHDRAWN`
- `DONOR_STRONGER__CLAIM_SUBSUMED`
- `DONOR_WEAKER__RESIDUAL_SPECIFIED`
- `ORTHOGONAL__DISTINCTION_RECORDED`
- `NOT_LOCATED_IN_DECLARED_SEARCH__NO_NOVELTY_CERTIFICATE`
- `CANNOT_CHECK_SOURCE_ACCESS`
- `CANNOT_CHECK_DATABASE_COVERAGE`

The final novelty statement is the intersection of residuals that survive all source lanes, not the union of ideas appearing in the manuscript.
