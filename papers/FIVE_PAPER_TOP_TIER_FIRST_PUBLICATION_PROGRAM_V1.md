## Publication policy

Every paper is developed to the strongest scientific standard justified by its claim class. The first submission target is the strongest realistically defensible journal. If the top-tier significance/generalization gate is not met, the paper falls back to a strong specialist journal by narrowing positioning and claims only.

A fallback may change title, framing, venue-specific length, exposition, and which result is emphasized. It may not:

- weaken a theorem premise or proof obligation;
- drop an adverse, null, disagreement, timeout, or `CANNOT_CHECK` result;
- replace an independent replay with a same-programme replay;
- widen an empirical result beyond its frozen population, machine, budget, representation, or domain;
- turn a bounded computation into an all-size theorem;
- convert representation/search savings into physical hardware advantage without a faithful resource map;
- repair a prospective experiment after outcome exposure and reuse the same registration as prospective evidence;
- promote a defective or quarantined execution;
- bypass content binding, source/result/environment custody, or hostile controls.

## Expert veto board

Every submission decision is reviewed from four independent roles.

1. **Theorem / formal-methods reviewer** — proof reconstruction, quantifiers, assumptions, counterexamples, model boundaries.
2. **Empirical / reproducibility reviewer** — prospective registration, leakage, hostile/null controls, denominators, replay independence, source/result/environment custody.
3. **Domain / prior-art reviewer** — strongest current donor, exact semantic alignment, novelty subtraction, production/domain significance.
4. **Venue / editor reviewer** — whether the surviving evidence supports the claimed significance at the proposed venue; may narrow claims but cannot weaken science gates.

A top-tier submission requires all four roles to have no unresolved major objection. A specialist fallback still requires roles 1–3 to clear; role 4 may conclude only `TOP_TIER_SIGNIFICANCE_GATE_NOT_MET`.

## Common publication terminals

For each paper, record exactly one route before submission:

- `TOP_TIER_GATE_PASS__SUBMIT_STRONGEST_DEFENSIBLE_VENUE`
- `TOP_TIER_SIGNIFICANCE_GATE_NOT_MET__SCIENTIFIC_CORE_CLEAR__SUBMIT_SPECIALIST_FALLBACK`
- `SCIENTIFIC_CORE_NOT_CLEAR__DO_NOT_SUBMIT`

A rejection decision is not itself scientific evidence. Reviewer criticism changes the scientific package only when it identifies a reproducible error, missing comparator, invalid authority transfer, or other evidence-backed defect.
