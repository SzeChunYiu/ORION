# Paper QG3 claim ledger — Intrinsic Support Numbers for Quantum Compilation Families

One row per quantitative claim made in `MANUSCRIPT_V1.md`. Status vocabulary:
`THEOREM-ALL-N` (machine-checked theorem holding for every n of the stated frozen
grammar and objective, accepted by both the independent generic harness and the
native harness), `THEOREM-GRADE-ON-DOMAIN` (exhaustively verified over the
complete stated finite domain, or an exactly verified explicit counterexample),
`MACHINE-EVIDENCED` (verified on stated finite frozen panels only; not a theorem
for all n), `NEGATIVE-RECORD` (a frozen search that returned no witness and
carries no positive authority), `BOUNDED-RECORD` (a dated development/programme
statement carrying no scientific or novelty authority), `OPEN` (stated, not
established). Receipt paths are relative to the repository root; protocols under
`development/orion-qg-regime-geometry/` were frozen before their outcomes.

**Authority note.** The QG-9 ladder, QG-16, QG-6 and QG-8 receipts carry their
authority as a *terminal string* plus explicit authority-negation fields
(`novelty_authority: false`, `physical_quantum_advantage_claim: false`, and on
the QG-9 rungs `new_theorem_authority: false`, `tightness_claim: false`,
`support{N-1}_claim: false`). Every parent authority string they bind terminates
in `NOT_R6`
