# ORION papers

The paper tree is a first-class projection of the executable framework, not a separate historical archive.

## Rule

A paper may describe only mechanics present in the canonical ORION registry or explicitly label a mechanic as proposed/research-only. Framework-changing commits must update the paper snapshot when they alter a published mechanic, state coordinate, authority rule, or saturation rule.

Every flagship paper must also maintain a **nearest-work case**: mechanisms from adjacent systems/parent disciplines are absorbed before a novelty delta is stated. A paper whose nearest-work routes remain materially open cannot claim novelty.

## Canonical five-paper programme

1. `paper-01-recursive-epistemic-reconstruction/` — core framework: K/W/M state, mechanic cells, residual-driven reframe/reopen and recursive epistemic reconstruction.
2. `paper-02-open-world-scientific-discovery/` — multi-route literature discovery, earned route independence, cumulative read memory, route/task stopping and recall-first evaluation.
3. `paper-03-global-knowledge-portrait/` — typed absorption, scientific-language projections, identity/context/measurement mapping, GLUE/obstructions and recoverable global portrait reconstruction.
4. `paper-04-verified-scientific-discovery/` — content-bound evidence, attribution/support, independent checks, authority non-escalation and protected evaluation.
5. `paper-05-self-orion/` — failure-as-knowledge, attribution before repair, challenger evolution, fresh assurance and governed self-development.

`research/paper-programme-v1/` contains the current nearest-work atlas, absorption decisions, scoped candidate deltas and benchmark/falsifier plan for all five papers.

## Historical planning paths

The original four-paper bootstrap paths are retained as redirect/provenance nodes where applicable. They are not separate paper claims.

RAKL papers remain immutable provenance and are selectively remapped in `legacy-rakl-map.md`.
