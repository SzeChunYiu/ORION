Dear Quantum Editorial Board,

Please consider “Zero-Sum Deletion Normal Forms for Multi-Tag Quantum
Compilation” by Sze Chun Yiu.

The manuscript proves an alphabet-sensitive finite-support normal form for a
deletion-closed quantum-compiler grammar and instantiates it in an arbitrary-
block MultiTag-TARE model. Its sharp statement is deliberately narrow: the
one-Tag, three-block specialization has intrinsic support two, while the general MultiTag result
is a sufficient normal form inside the stated objective cone. The manuscript
does not claim the upstream block-encoding primitive, necessity outside the
stated specialization, or a physical-resource advantage.

We believe the work fits Quantum because it gives an exact compiler theorem,
states its semantic assumptions explicitly, and separates a proof certificate
from intrinsic compiler support.
