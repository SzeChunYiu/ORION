Dear Quantum Editorial Board,

Please consider “Zero-Sum Deletion Normal Forms for Multi-Tag Quantum
Compilation” by Sze Chun Yiu.

The manuscript proves an alphabet-sensitive finite-support normal form for a
deletion-closed quantum-compiler grammar and instantiates it in an arbitrary-
block MultiTag-TARE model. Its statement is deliberately narrow: the general
MultiTag result is a sufficient normal form inside the stated objective cone,
while the finite one-Tag, three-block record is reported only as a bounded
calibration. The manuscript does not claim the upstream block-encoding
primitive, necessity outside the stated specialization, or a physical-resource
advantage.

The manuscript states its semantic assumptions explicitly and separates a
proof certificate from intrinsic compiler support. Its fit for Quantum remains
subject to establishing a direct, source-supported bridge from the abstract
compiler grammar to the published quantum primitive.

This letter is a preparation draft, not a submission-ready representation. The
corresponding author must still provide an affiliation and certify authorship,
conflicts, funding, originality, classification, and permission to submit.

Sincerely,
