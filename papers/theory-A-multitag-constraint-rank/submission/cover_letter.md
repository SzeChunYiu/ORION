Dear Editors,

Please consider “Zero-Sum Deletion Normal Forms for a Multi-Tag Pauli Grammar”
by Sze Chun Yiu.

The manuscript proves an alphabet-sensitive finite-support normal form for a
deletion-closed finite grammar and instantiates it in an arbitrary-block
multi-Tag Pauli model. Its statement is deliberately narrow: the result is a
sufficient normal form inside the stated objective cone. TARE motivates the
model vocabulary, but the manuscript does not claim equivalence with the full
TARE construction, necessity outside the cone, or a physical-resource
advantage.

The manuscript states its semantic assumptions explicitly and separates a
proof certificate from intrinsic compiler support. It is submitted as a
mathematical optimization paper rather than as a production quantum-compiler
result.

This letter is a preparation draft, not a submission-ready representation. The
corresponding author must still provide an affiliation and certify authorship,
conflicts, funding, originality, classification, and permission to submit.

Sincerely,
