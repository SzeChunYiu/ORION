# R12 expert panel and manuscript adjudication

## Panel

1. **Formal-methods and theorem auditor** — checks quantifiers, scope, sharpness, proof dependence, theorem-to-mechanism gaps, and whether finite controls can support an all-size statement.
2. **Domain-science and experimental-design auditor** — checks whether the discriminator answers a real scientific question, whether baselines share a valid unit/oracle, whether selection was prospective, and whether transfer/generalization claims are licensed.
3. **Reproducibility and receipt-custody auditor** — checks branch/parent/content binding, immutable terminals, independent source custody, deterministic replay, first disagreement, timeout, resource exhaustion, and null/adverse preservation.
4. **Skeptical top-tier reviewer** — asks whether the surviving contribution is coherent, important, novel, independently scrutinized, and supported by decisive evidence rather than a collection of green harnesses.
5. **Manuscript and claim-ledger editor** — admits only receipt-supported claims, separates local conformance from scientific and external authority, and prevents outdated identities or stronger historical wording from laundering the current evidence ceiling.

## Adjudication rule

A claim enters a manuscript only when the theorem/experiment, immutable receipt, scope statement, and authority ceiling agree. A local checker PASS is evidence about the declared checker contract only. It cannot by itself establish novelty, top-tier sufficiency, external review, production value, hardware value, or unseen-instance generalization.

Null, adverse, contradiction, first-disagreement, timeout, resource-exhaustion, and `CANNOT_CHECK` terminals are evidence and remain visible. Identity consolidation is permitted only when the surviving scientific contribution and receipt graph justify it; no destructive consolidation was justified in this tranche.
