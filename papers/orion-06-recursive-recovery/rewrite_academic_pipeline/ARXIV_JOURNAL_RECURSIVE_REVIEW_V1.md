# ORION-06 recursive academic-paper-skills review V1

**Scientific-authority delta:** `NONE`  
**Release posture:** bounded executable negative-recovery methods paper; broad effectiveness optional.

## Editorial spine

The paper should present executable negative recovery: how failed claims, invalid receipts and defeated mechanisms are preserved and used to control the next research action. It must not imply broad task-performance gains that were not measured.

## Atomic claim boundary

- Separate recovery correctness, provenance and auditability from downstream problem-solving effectiveness.
- Treat theorem proving, repair, retrieval, synthesis and evolutionary search as donor families unless native evidence establishes a paper-owned residual.
- Bind every headline recovery example to its negative history and replay receipt.
- State that a three-domain benchmark is strengthening, not required for the bounded methods claim.

## Recursive reviewer verdicts

- **Theory:** the method object is coherent if recovery states and transitions are defined exactly.
- **Evidence:** case counts are demonstrations, not an estimate of recovery prevalence.
- **Hostile review:** test whether the system simply relabels failure without changing a licensed next action.
- **Reproducibility:** regenerate the receipt index against the final claim set and replay headline receipts on the submission commit.
- **Editor:** use one running failure/recovery example from introduction through methods and results.

## Computation rule

A multi-domain native-verifier study is optional for a stronger effectiveness headline. It must give donor systems first refusal and retain null/harmful recoveries.
