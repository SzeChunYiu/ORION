`SCIENTIFIC_CAUSALITY_AUTHORITY=NOT_GRANTED_BY_VALIDATOR`. The validator confirms

converted into a pass. An undetermined outcome here is recorded and reported
separately from a negative one, and the distinction is load-bearing: no reader may
take the absence of a cross-domain result as evidence that the method fails outside
the studied families.
