Two Coupling Trades, and Support-Two Sufficiency*

instances witness-by-witness (R6P); (iv) the explanation of chemistry behavior — on real
DUCC Hamiltonian six-term batches (H4, equilibrium N2) the weight-one donor family is
*exactly* optimal, now explained rather than observed; grounded by the R4B split-TARE
majorization theorem (0/8,700 hostile checks) and the R4D implementation-aware compiler
positive on a blob-locked public H2O subject.

**Novelty claim (donor-subtracted):** the TARE primitive, shared-Tag circuit identity,
Restore factoring, and unitary-partitioning/Pauli-frame optimization are donor-owned
(Schillo–Sturm–Quay TARE, arXiv:2601.05740, and the donor stack per the frozen hostile
novelty search of 2026-08-20). What is new is the **exact, witness-carrying expressivity
map of the joint optimization family**: a machine-verified dominance inequality, minimal
counterexamples for both coupling trades, and a proven-sufficient support bound — none of
which exists in the donor literature, which treats these grammars as heuristic search
spaces rather than exactly characterized ones.

**Honest scope:** finite verified domains (exhaustive n=1, full structured n=2, seeded
random n=2–3, two chemistry subjects); the frozen support-count objective; six-term
batches. The all-n composition theorem is stated as open.

**Submission gates:** R6Q predicate receipt (pending) — if positive, add the decidable

*definition* with a first measurement, not an evaluation study.

**Submission gates:** score coordinate 4 once R6P/R6Q land (R6P already aligns); run at
