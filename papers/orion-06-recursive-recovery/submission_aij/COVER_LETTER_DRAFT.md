# Cover Letter — Artificial Intelligence

Dear Editors of *Artificial Intelligence*,

Please consider the manuscript **“Recursive Recovery of Negative Quantum Results: An Auditable Successor Protocol for Research Programmes”** for publication in *Artificial Intelligence*.

The paper studies a narrow problem in AI-assisted research systems: once an evidence-bound scientific hypothesis receives a negative, refuted, donor-absorbed, mixed, or `CANNOT_CHECK` disposition, what makes a proposed next research question an auditable successor rather than a retrospective relabeling of the failed claim? We present an executable successor-state representation and a longitudinal case study from the ORION-Q quantum-compilation programme.

The manuscript deliberately makes no productivity or autonomous-research-quality claim. Its bounded contribution is methodological and auditable: a frozen 51-receipt publication universe is partitioned into 23 graph nodes and 28 explicit exclusions; 13 successor edges carry stated predecessor-to-successor reasons; seven negative or absorbed nodes deliberately have no invented successor; and a fail-closed validator binds the denominator and graph to immutable repository artifacts. The case study includes exact refutations, bounded positives, a later all-n theorem, donor absorption, a prospective confirmation followed by broader refutation, and an open proof state without flattening them into a single success score.

We position the work relative to current scientific-agent evaluation and verification rather than claiming those areas as new. ScienceAgentBench, AstaBench, and SciAgentArena receive credit for rigorous scientific-agent benchmarking, while ScientistOne receives credit for broad Chain-of-Evidence and research-integrity auditing. The residual studied here is the authorized transition **after** an evidence-bound scientific disposition has been reached.

We believe the paper may be relevant to *Artificial Intelligence* readers interested in autonomous and semi-autonomous research systems, reasoning under persistent state, scientific agents, and auditable decision processes. The manuscript is reproducible at its declared publication cut; no protected subject is opened by the publication workflow; and the publication branch does not rewrite the underlying scientific receipts or protocols.

The intended publication sequence is arXiv first, followed by submission to one journal at a time. The author will make the journal's originality and non-concurrent-submission attestations in the submission system at the time of filing.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
