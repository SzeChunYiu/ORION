# ORION-06 controlled cross-domain recovery benchmark — frozen question

Identity: `ORION06.CROSS_DOMAIN_RECOVERY.v1`.

Given research tasks with a pre-existing failed/negative attempt and objective verifier, does preserving the negative state and performing diagnosis-targeted recovery improve verified successor yield or reduce false revival/claim drift relative to naive retry and donor/domain repair at matched information and compute budgets?

This is a controlled benchmark of recovery protocols, not a population claim about scientists.
