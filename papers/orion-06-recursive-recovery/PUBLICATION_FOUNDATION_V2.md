## 03 — Argument map

### Tension
Autonomous and computational research systems can generate many plausible hypotheses and apparently positive results. Verification/provenance methods increasingly make individual claims traceable, but a research programme still needs a disciplined answer to a harder question: **what should happen after a prospectively frozen claim fails?**

### Central question
Can negative results be represented as active, replayable research state that constrains the next experiment, gives prior work first right of refusal, and prevents later successes from rewriting the history that produced them?

### Central answer
The ORION-Q programme demonstrates one executable negative-recovery discipline. Its record contains failed closure hypotheses, donor absorptions, exact counterexamples, bounded positives and all-`n` theorems, linked by successor freezes. The contribution is not that evidence can be traced; it is the explicit **transition discipline from a negative disposition to the next licensed scientific question**.

### Evidence chain
1. Define authority/receipt vocabulary and why replay is weaker than scientific truth.
2. Define negative-recovery transition and allowed terminals.
3. Show a simple donor-absorption case: candidate idea disappears, negative retained.
4. Show exact-counterexample recovery: a closure claim fails and identifies the missing coupling mechanism.
5. Show escalation from finite evidence to theorem without rewriting the finite predecessor.
6. Show prospective confirmation that remains bounded.
7. Show later refutation of a broader closed-form interpretation despite the earlier confirmation.
8. Audit lineage: every successor was frozen after the predecessor disposition and before its own outcome.
9. Compare to evidence-chain, preregistration, reproducibility and autonomous-science donors.

### Strongest alternative interpretation
**"This is just preregistration plus provenance."** ORION-02 must demonstrate the extra semantics: terminal typing, negative retention, responsibility assignment, donor-first-refusal and successor-freeze relation. If the manuscript cannot show those as distinct operational obligations, the novelty collapses to synthesis.

### Durable conclusion
Negative results become reusable scientific state only when the programme preserves what failed, why it failed, what prior work already owns, and which successor question was licensed *before* the successor outcome.

## 05 — Style and terminology

Canonical terms:
- `negative result` — a registered hypothesis/gate not supported; not synonymous with execution failure.
- `CANNOT_CHECK` — unavailable authority/evidence under the declared model, not zero or failure.
- `donor absorption` — prior work subsumes the candidate mechanism; scientific information can increase while novelty credit decreases.
- `successor freeze` — next claim/protocol fixed after predecessor disposition and before successor outcome.
- `receipt` — attribution/replay object; never a truth certificate by itself.
- `recovery` — generating a better-specified next scientific question from a retained disposition, not converting a negative into a positive label.

Forbidden phrases:
- "first evidence-chain research agent";
- "proves automated science is trustworthy";
- "negative results guarantee discovery";
- "fully autonomous scientific method";
- "statistically improves research" absent a real comparative study.
