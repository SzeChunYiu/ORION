## Positive interpretation of the proper-subset failures
The 1,055 strict-subset failures are not merely adverse ablations. Together with the 155 complete-revalidation restorations they establish the two directions of a registered repair theorem: complete affected-coordinate revalidation is sufficient for restoration, and every strict subset is insufficient. The negative rows are therefore necessity countermodels for the exact constructive rule.

## Positive interpretation of the ideal-product result
The zero-mismatch ideal-product result is a portability theorem, not a loss of contribution. It establishes that P6 identifies a reusable scientific semantics that can be implemented either as an ORION layer or as a correctly enriched donor product without changing the scientific judgment.

Those are separate empirical or literature claims rather than qualifications on the proved certificate-lifting and necessary-and-sufficient repair result.
