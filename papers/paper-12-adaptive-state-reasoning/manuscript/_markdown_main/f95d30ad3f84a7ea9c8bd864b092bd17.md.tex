{}Strategic test-time-compute allocation treats inference budget as a learnable or bandit decision across examples. Constrained policy approaches optimize accuracy under average compute. Adaptive in-context demonstration and generation methods jointly alter conditioning and generation effort. Recent “when to think” work likewise emphasizes selective reasoning to reduce unnecessary inference.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
