\markdownRendererHeadingTwo{Statistical analysis}\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}The protected unit of generalization is the held-out family. Policies see paired items inside each family, while headline uncertainty is computed by a deterministic \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{20,000-resample family-block bootstrap} over the 16 family-level joint gains. The registered lower bound is positive.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}The analysis does not pool items as if 8,192 individual trials were independent domains. Hyperparameters are frozen before protected evaluation. The worst-family gain is reported to prevent a favorable mean from hiding a family-level failure.
