\markdownRendererHeadingTwo{Adaptive test-time compute is prior-owned}\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}Recent systems allocate inference compute dynamically based on predicted difficulty, value or resource constraints. Bandit formulations, constrained policy optimization, adaptive demonstration/generation strategies and “when to think” policies already own the primitive that different examples deserve different reasoning budgets. P12 therefore does not claim adaptive inference allocation itself.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
