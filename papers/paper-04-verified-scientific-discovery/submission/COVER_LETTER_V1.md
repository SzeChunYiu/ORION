# Cover letter — Nature Machine Intelligence

Dear Editors,

Please consider our Article, **“Non-Escalating Scientific Authority under Content-Bound Evidence and Protected Evaluation,”** for publication in *Nature Machine Intelligence*.

AI research systems increasingly retrieve, synthesize and verify scientific material, but factual support or a well-formed citation does not by itself justify promoting a claim to scientific authority. The manuscript studies a narrower systems question: whether scientific-authority promotion should be a non-compensatory transition requiring exact evidence content/provenance, admissible checker lineage, protected evaluator/holdout identity and contamination/access checks, with `CANNOT_CHECK/BLOCK` when a prerequisite is unresolved rather than a confidence score that can compensate for failure elsewhere.

The publication-authorizing experiment was prospectively frozen before outcome access. An independent host generated a secret-seeded 420-case campaign across 13 clean and hostile families. Full ORION, nine matched comparator mechanisms and eight resource-matched ablations were each evaluated over five repeats. Protected labels were inaccessible to scored candidate/comparator jobs; protected gold was downloaded only after those jobs completed, and actual file/network syscall telemetry was retained.

On the frozen campaign, full ORION produced **0/360 false scientific-authority promotions**, compared with **180/360** for the strongest frozen comparator, while both promoted **60/60 clean positives**. The preregistered ORION-minus-comparator false-promotion effect was **−0.500** with a paired-bootstrap 95% CI of **[−0.553, −0.447]**, and clean-coverage difference was **0.000** with 95% CI **[0.000, 0.000]**. An independently implemented reproduction recovered the same headline counts and effect, and scored-process telemetry recorded zero protected-identifier accesses and zero external IP connections. We also report the negative result: the secondary H3 comparison for superior `CANNOT_CHECK` selection was a tie and is **not supported**.

We believe the work will interest readers studying reliable and responsible machine intelligence, AI evaluation, autonomous research systems and scientific-agent governance. The contribution is not provenance tracking, attribution, fact checking or contamination detection in isolation; the manuscript explicitly credits prior work on those components. The empirical claim is bounded to the frozen attack taxonomy, subject revision, resource policy and custody model, and the limitations section discusses finite attack coverage, mechanical gold and unseen evaluator attacks.

The repository contains the frozen protocol/bindings, accepted safe aggregate evidence, all comparator/ablation definitions, deterministic figure/table regeneration scripts, a public non-secret replay, and an independent protected reproduction receipt. Protected raw labels/traces remain in custody rather than being misrepresented as public data; releasable artifacts are separately archived for peer review.

This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. All claims in the manuscript are mapped to exact evidence in the protected claim ledger, and the final submission package is generated only from the accepted immutable campaign artifacts.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

The ORION authors
