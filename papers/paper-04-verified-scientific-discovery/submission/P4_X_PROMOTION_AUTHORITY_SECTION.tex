\section{Post-saturation successor: donor-complete scientific-promotion authority}
\label{sec:p4x-successor}

The protected V2 result asks whether a non-compensatory content/provenance/checker/evaluator pipeline reduces false scientific-authority promotion relative to frozen mechanism proxies. We subsequently ran a separate prospectively frozen successor that raises the comparison standard: ordinary provenance, claim verification, artifact/version binding, evaluator custody, epoch handling and generic authorization are granted to the strong donor product, and the disputed object is only the final \emph{scientific-promotion relation}. The V2 and successor campaigns are separate experiments and are not pooled.

\subsection{Donor-engulfment boundary}

P4 does not claim source-aware factuality, provenance, claim/evidence verification, citation fidelity, evaluator auditing, contamination defense, abstention or generic authorization as atomic novelties. Those mechanisms are retained as donor-owned local components \citep{provenanceguard2026,provenai2026,provsen2026,benchguard2026,certifiedspeculative2026}. The successor tests whether successful local verification and generic permission may self-authorize promotion of a target scientific claim.

The P4-X relation is non-compensatory. A target claim may be promoted only when the registered hard scientific-promotion obligations are discharged. In the exact successor battery, the discriminating obligations concern target claim scope, independence of supporting evidence, and unresolved scientific-promotion authority after the ordinary provenance/verification/authorization checks already available to the donor product.

\subsection{Exact successor design}

The frozen successor contains 400 protected cases spanning five heterogeneous scientific artifact domains, eight hostile/control archetypes and ten protected variants per domain--archetype cell. All systems receive the same candidate-visible information.

The principal arms are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{P4-X}: the non-compensatory scientific-promotion relation;
  \item \textbf{B1}: a strong donor-complete generic-authorization product with the ordinary provenance, verification, artifact/version, custody and epoch information, but without the full target scientific-promotion relation;
  \item \textbf{B2}: a compensatory all-signal product that permits successful coordinates to offset failed or unresolved ones;
  \item \textbf{B3}: an ideal typed donor product given every P4-X scientific coordinate and the same promotion predicate; B3 is expected to tie extensionally.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Protected result}

P4-X selected the correct scientific-promotion terminal on 400/400 protected cases. B1 was correct on 250/400, B2 on 50/400, and B3 on 400/400 with zero decision mismatches to P4-X. The paired P4-X minus B1 effect was $+0.375$ with a domain-stratified bootstrap 95\% CI $[0.3275,0.4225]$; paired McNemar discordance was 150--0. P4-X made zero false promotions, retained clean promotion at 1.0, and its advantage over B1 had positive direction in every exact domain. An independent implementation that does not import the P4-X execution scorer reproduces the canonical protected-row digest and all arm counts.

The result supports a bounded distinction between local mechanism success and target scientific-promotion authority. In these exact contracts, ordinary provenance, verification, custody and generic permission can all be available while claim scope, evidence dependence or an unresolved scientific obligation still blocks promotion. Conversely, when the donor product receives the same scientific coordinates and the same promotion predicate, it ties exactly; P4 therefore claims no inherent expressivity or centralization advantage.

\subsection{Relation to the historical V2 hypotheses}

The successor does not retroactively alter the V2 secondary findings. V2 H2 remains a non-inferiority guard in a battery where all primary systems achieved perfect clean coverage. V2 H3 remains not supported/non-discriminating because the historical insufficient-evidence construction was structurally saturated. The P4-X clean controls show that the successor result does not arise from blanket refusal; they do not establish general abstention superiority or difficult-benign recall.

\subsection{Claim ceiling}

The strongest supported successor statement is therefore architectural but bounded: after strong donor-owned provenance, verification, custody and generic authorization mechanisms are granted, \emph{scientific claim promotion remains a distinct non-compensatory authority relation} in the tested heterogeneous exact contracts. Deployed verifier/provider generality, universal necessity of the exact registered coordinates, general abstention superiority and universal scientific-authority superiority remain outside the evidence.
