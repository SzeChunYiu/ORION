\subsection{Successor discovery, failure, and representation-transition objects}
\label{sec:p4-successor-discovery-failure}

The broader ORION research programme produces source-grounded discovery/failure episodes, scoped failure-knowledge records, bounded atom-study reports, thought-experiment proposals, concept/representation candidates, and correspondence or regime-transition witnesses. Paper IV treats every such object as a proposal or evidence-bearing input, never as scientific authority in its own right. A richer generator, structural analogy, or successful internal reconstruction does not bypass the content, ownership, support, checker, influence, evaluator, access, and contamination gates above.

Historical evidence is especially easy to over-promote. A documented episode may motivate a mechanism hypothesis or prospective discriminator, while a later structural interpretation may organize episodes into a useful family. Neither operation turns the historical material into protected confirmatory gold. The failure atlas therefore remains schema-stable rather than corpus-ready until independent expert review is actually obtained.

Scoped negative knowledge is likewise non-promotional. A prior exclusion may constrain a later proposal only while its applicability conditions remain satisfied; changing a load-bearing coordinate can make the old exclusion inapplicable or reopen it, while unresolved applicability cannot become a universal negative. The prospective failure-epistemology study further found that a strong composition of truth-maintenance/nogood context, responsibility/refutation gating, semantic transport, provenance, and defeater-directed inquiry matched the ORION scoped-failure arm exactly. The useful ORION contribution is a typed, auditable boundary; generic failure learning is not promoted as a new scientific inference primitive.

A claimed representation transition reaches Paper IV only after source and target objects, correspondence obligations, preserved commitments, and protected consequences are explicit. The zero-error Jump study satisfied those structural obligations but still tied a strong verified regime-revision parent. Thus representation change and correspondence remain candidate capabilities, while scientific promotion stays an external, non-compensatory transition.
