\subsection{Successor discovery, failure, and representation-transition objects}
\label{sec:p4-successor-discovery-failure}

The broader ORION research programme produces objects that can be useful upstream of scientific promotion: source-grounded discovery and failure episodes, scoped failure-knowledge records, bounded atom-study reports, thought-experiment proposals, concept/representation candidates, and correspondence or regime-transition witnesses.  Paper IV treats every such object as a proposal or evidence-bearing input, never as scientific authority in its own right.  A richer generator, a cleaner structural analogy, or a successful internal reconstruction does not bypass the content, ownership, support, checker, influence, evaluator, access, and contamination gates above.

Historical evidence is especially easy to over-promote.  A documented episode may motivate a mechanism hypothesis or a prospective discriminator, while a later structural interpretation may organize several episodes into a useful family.  Neither operation turns the historical material into protected confirmatory gold.  What the source states, what the later analysis infers, and what remains unsupported must stay distinct, and chronology or pre-cutoff visibility must remain available to the evaluator whenever historical examples influence benchmark design.

Scoped negative knowledge is likewise non-promotional.  A previously supported exclusion may constrain a later proposal only in the context for which its applicability conditions remain satisfied.  If a load-bearing coordinate changes, the exclusion can become inapplicable or explicitly reopened; if the applicability context is unresolved, Paper IV cannot treat the old failure as a universal negative.  Conversely, reopening a failure does not count as positive evidence for the new proposal.

A claimed representation transition therefore reaches Paper IV only after its source and target objects, correspondence obligations, preserved commitments, and protected consequence have been made explicit.  The authority question is then ordinary and non-compensatory: are the exact evidence bytes bound, does the assigned evidence own and support the claim, are the relevant checks discriminating and admissibly independent, and is the protected evaluation uncontaminated?  The transition witness can make the claim checkable; it cannot certify its own validity, novelty, or adoption.  Result-bearing statements about any such successor remain outside Paper IV until those protected gates are satisfied for the exact frozen subject.
