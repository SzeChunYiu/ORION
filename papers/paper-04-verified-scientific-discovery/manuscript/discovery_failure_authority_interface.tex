\subsection{Successor discovery, failure, and representation-transition objects}
\label{sec:p4-successor-discovery-failure}

The broader ORION research programme produces objects that can be useful upstream of scientific promotion: source-grounded discovery and failure episodes, scoped failure-knowledge records, bounded atom-study reports, thought-experiment proposals, concept/representation candidates, structural-assimilation receipts, and correspondence or regime-transition witnesses. Paper IV treats every such object as a proposal or evidence-bearing input, never as scientific authority in its own right. A richer generator, a cleaner structural analogy, a source-grounded receipt, or a successful internal reconstruction does not bypass the content, ownership, support, checker, influence, evaluator, access and contamination gates above.

The merged programme supplies concrete negative controls for that rule. The 51-episode Discovery Morphology Atlas is a stable descriptive research basis, but its historical examples remain mechanism-extraction/design evidence rather than protected gold. The 30-episode Failure Atlas reaches only \texttt{FAILURE\_ATLAS\_SCHEMA\_STABLE}; it does not self-issue the stronger corpus-ready authority. The structural-assimilation programme itself closes \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} because the MDA full structural source remains ungrounded, demonstrating that a useful governance process may still be unable to certify one donor. These are intended examples of non-escalation, not administrative incompleteness to be averaged away.

The protected zero-error Jump result is similarly non-promotional. ORION-JUMP ties the verified representation-regime revision parent exactly on both disjoint frozen splits, including correspondence, protected consequences and zero false jumps, so the registered terminal is \texttt{REPRESENTATION\_INVENTION\_NO\_INCREMENTAL\_VALUE}. That tie cannot authorize a new ORION representation-invention claim merely because the candidate passed its own internal pipeline. The scientifically relevant outcome is strongest-parent subsumption.

Scoped negative knowledge is also non-promotional. A previously supported exclusion constrains a later proposal only while its applicability conditions remain satisfied. If a load-bearing coordinate changes, the exclusion can become inapplicable or explicitly reopened; if the applicability context is unresolved, Paper IV cannot treat the old failure as a universal negative. Conversely, reopening a failure is not positive evidence for the new proposal.

A claimed representation transition therefore reaches Paper IV only after its source and target objects, correspondence obligations, preserved commitments and protected consequence have been made explicit. The authority question is then ordinary and non-compensatory: are the exact evidence bytes bound, does the assigned evidence own and support the claim, are the relevant checks discriminating and admissibly independent, and is protected evaluation uncontaminated? A transition witness can make a claim checkable; it cannot certify its own validity, novelty or adoption.
