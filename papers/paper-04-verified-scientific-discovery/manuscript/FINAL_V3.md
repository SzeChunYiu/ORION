## Replacement abstract for V3

Scientific verification can establish plausibility, citation support, provenance, checker success, or action permission without automatically authorizing a scientific claim for promotion. Paper IV studies promotion as a distinct, non-compensatory scientific-authority transition.

The prospectively frozen protected V2 campaign requires exact evidence-content binding, source ownership, semantic support, checker independence and hostile discrimination, evidence influence, evaluator/holdout integrity, and contamination clearance. ORION produces **0 false promotions among 360 hostile opportunities**, compared with **180/360** for the strongest frozen mechanism proxy, while both systems promote **60/60 clean positives**. The paired false-promotion difference is $-0.50$ with 95% CI $[-0.553,-0.447]$. Every registered ORION ablation preserves clean coverage while increasing false promotion, showing that the effect is carried by the hard authority composition rather than blanket refusal.

P4-X then asks the harder post-saturation question after a donor-complete comparison product is granted provenance, claim verification, artifact/version binding, evaluator custody, epoch handling, and generic authorization. Across **400 prospectively frozen exact scientific-promotion contracts** spanning five heterogeneous artifact domains and eight hostile/control archetypes, P4-X selects the correct terminal on **400/400** cases, versus **250/400** for the strong donor-complete generic-authorization product and **50/400** for a compensatory all-signal product. The paired P4-X minus strong-product effect is **+0.375** with domain-stratified bootstrap 95% CI **[0.3275,0.4225]**; P4-X makes zero false promotions, preserves clean promotion at 1.0, and has a positive effect in every exact domain. An information-equivalent typed donor product implementing the same scientific-promotion relation also attains 400/400.

Together, the studies establish a strong systems result: **scientific claim promotion is a distinct non-compensatory authority relation above donor-owned provenance, verification, custody, and generic permission**. Missing claim scope, evidence independence, or unresolved scientific-authority obligations cannot be repaired by confidence or success elsewhere. The ideal-product tie establishes portability of the promotion semantics across implementations.

## 9. Post-saturation successor: donor-complete scientific-promotion authority

### 9.1 Scientific object

P4-X separates three relations that can coincide on easy cases but diverge on hostile ones:

1. **evidence/verification success** — a source, checker, or evidence object passes its local test;
2. **generic action/decision authorization** — an ordinary authorization product permits the relevant operation under its policy;
3. **scientific claim-promotion authority** — the exact target claim may acquire promoted standing at the registered scope/epoch only if every load-bearing scientific obligation is discharged.

The successor assumes the first two relations are useful and gives their machinery to the donor-complete product. The discriminator is whether successful local components may self-authorize the third relation.

### 9.2 Exact successor battery

The successor is a prospectively frozen exact-contract study with five heterogeneous scientific artifact domains, eight archetypes per domain, and ten protected variants per cell, for 400 protected cases total. The registered families include clean promotion, artifact/version mismatch, scope overreach, epoch/freshness mismatch, provenance/custody defects, evidence-dependence/correlation defects, and unresolved scientific-authority obligations.

All arms receive the same candidate-visible information. The principal arms are:

- **P4-X** — non-compensatory scientific-promotion relation;
- **B1 strong donor-complete generic-authorization product** — receives mature provenance/verification/custody/authorization information but lacks the full target-bound scientific-promotion coupling on scope, evidence independence, and unresolved scientific obligations;
- **B2 compensatory product** — receives the same signals but allows successful coordinates to compensate for failed or unresolved ones;
- **B3 ideal typed product** — receives every P4-X scientific coordinate and the same promotion predicate, testing portability of the semantics.

### 9.3 Protected result

On the frozen 400-case battery:

- P4-X: **400/400** correct scientific-promotion terminals;
- B1 strong donor-complete product: **250/400**;
- B2 compensatory product: **50/400**;
- B3 ideal information-equivalent typed product: **400/400**, with zero decision mismatches to P4-X;
- P4-X minus B1: **+0.375**, domain-stratified bootstrap 95% CI **[0.3275, 0.4225]**;
- paired McNemar discordance: **150--0**;
- P4-X false promotions: **0**;
- clean P4-X promotion rate: **1.0**;
- P4-X minus B1 direction: positive in **every** exact domain.

An independent implementation that does not import the P4-X execution scorer reproduces the protected canonical-row digest and all four arm counts.

### 9.4 What P4-X adds above protected V2

Protected V2 shows that non-compensatory content/provenance/checker/evaluator authority can eliminate false promotion relative to a panel of strong frozen mechanism proxies while preserving every clean positive. P4-X raises the comparison standard by placing ordinary provenance, verification, custody, artifact binding, epoch state, and generic authorization inside the strongest comparator.

The successor therefore supports the wider conclusion that **successful local verification and generic authorization are not sufficient conditions for scientific promotion in the registered heterogeneous contracts**. Promotion remains a separate target-bound authority transition whose hard coordinates cannot be compensated by confidence or aggregate success.

### 9.5 Portability theorem in systems form

B3 obtains 400/400 and is decision-identical to P4-X. This is an implementation-portability result. It shows that the scientific-promotion abstraction is sufficient whether implemented as an ORION layer or as a correctly enriched decentralized product. The contribution is therefore the target-bound promotion relation and its hard composition law, not a dependence on centralized organization.

Protected V2 shows that hard content/provenance/checker/evaluator gates can eliminate false scientific promotion across the frozen hostile battery while retaining all clean positives. The strongest supported conclusion is direct: **local evidence quality, verification success, provenance correctness, and generic permission do not by themselves authorize a scientific claim to acquire promoted standing; promotion requires target-bound discharge of the hard scientific-authority obligations.** ORION's contribution is the explicit, non-compensatory composition law that makes that transition auditable and enforceable.
