\section{Data and code availability}
\label{sec:availability}

Every artifact named anywhere in this paper is listed here with its path, so that no repository location has to be carried in the narrative. Source, protocols, and evidence are version-controlled in the \idt{SzeChunYiu/ORION} repository under \idt{papers/paper-04-verified-scientific-discovery/}; persistent DOI assignment remains pending publication.

\subsection{Preregistration}
The study was preregistered and frozen before any outcome was accessed, under the identifier \idt{P4.protected-authority.v1}. A reader checking whether a reported analysis was planned or chosen after the fact should compare against that freeze; it is the reason every number reported here can be traced to a decision made in advance of seeing it.

The machine-readable freeze is \idt{protocol/PROTOCOL_V1.json}, the case requirements are \idt{protocol/ATTACK_CASE_SCHEMA_V1.json}, and the statistical rules, metric definitions, and threat model are \idt{protocol/STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS_PLAN_V1.md}, \idt{protocol/METRICS_REGISTRY_V1.json}, and \idt{protocol/THREAT_MODEL_V1.md}. The custody policy and the execution-freeze checklist for the post-repair campaign are \idt{protocol/CUSTODY_POLICY_V1.md} and \idt{protocol/EXECUTION_FREEZE_CHECKLIST_V2.md}. The prospective execution binding, including the exact subject revision and the frozen harness identity, is \idt{protocol/PROTECTED_RUN_BINDINGS_V2.json}. The comparator and ablation disclosure is \idt{host/BASELINE_CONFIGS_V2.json}, and the frozen case generator and verdict-leak check are \idt{protocol/generate_attack_manifest.py} and \idt{protocol/check_verdict_leak_v1.py}.

\subsection{Reproduction}
The host-side scoring and independent reproduction paths are \idt{host/evaluate_campaign_v2.py} and \idt{host/independent_reproduce_v2.py}; the latter recomputes the headline counts from the safe bundle without sharing code with the scorer. The binding manifest that ties subject, split, harness, and evaluator identities together is \idt{reproducibility/BINDING_MANIFEST_V2.md}. Reproduction reconstructs the aggregates and the published figures and tables; it does not reconstruct the protected per-case gold, which is what the custody model deliberately withholds.
