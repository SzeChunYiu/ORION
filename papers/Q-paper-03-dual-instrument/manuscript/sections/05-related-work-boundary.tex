\section{Related work and boundary}

Relevant neighboring traditions include agent benchmarks with known task endpoints, self-consistency and ensemble methods, multi-agent debate, LLM-as-judge evaluation, workflow/provenance systems, and non-LLM decision controllers. Q3's candidate residual is the specific measurement contract: same frozen frontier question, architecturally distinct decision paths, receipt-bound evidence, and deferred scoring when contemporaneous ground truth is unavailable.

That residual remains conditional on a fresh submission-date search. The manuscript therefore avoids claiming that no prior benchmark measures any form of cross-instrument agreement. A close parent should narrow the benchmark claim rather than be excluded by terminology.

The broader host/capability receipt architecture is not claimed as Q3's standalone systems novelty. That subject now has an explicit P15 directory and requires its own donor matrix/protocol before becoming a paper.
