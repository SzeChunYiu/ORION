## ORION-21 — State as Computation

**State:** `PEER_REVIEW_PACKAGE_READY__CONTROLLED_THEORY_SYSTEMS_BOUNDARY_CHARACTERIZED__EXTERNAL_REAL_SYSTEM_OPEN` · **Priority:** `P2_EXTERNAL_SYSTEM_PLACEMENT` · **Track:** learning systems / resource-rational computation / systems ML

**Strongest defensible claim.** For the registered query families, state is a responsibility- and access-conditioned computational placement decision: compilation can move structural search out of bounded downstream access, but its value depends on width, decoder class, update cost, and the number and tolerance of future responsibilities.

**Boundaries.**
- Compiled state is not a universal compression win: the r=3 capacity-augmented attack wins and the registered ten-responsibility quality gate is not met.
- P11F is non-authoritative because its execution deviated from the frozen contract; P11G is arm-scoped and cannot license a general hostile-nonlinear claim.
- The current real-data evidence does not establish placement value in a deployed system under end-to-end latency, memory, update, and future-query uncertainty.
- The family-scale miss is not merely an n=10 artefact at the preregistered near-uniform bar, but it cannot be read as absence of all moderate capability.

**Immediate repairs.**
- Rewrite the abstract and results around the conditional placement law and place the r=3 and ten-responsibility negatives in the main evidence arc, not only limitations.
- Report all costs as a resource vector before any scalar utility is introduced; provide sensitivity curves for any scalar exchange rate used for a venue-facing summary.
- Predeclare which existing receipts are confirmatory, historical, adverse, or protocol-invalid so no pooled table silently upgrades them.
- Wire the exact gate operating characteristic and Clopper-Pearson intervals into the main result so the claim reads below-the-registered-bar, not capability absent.
- For any successor, choose whether the objective is certification near one or estimation in the moderate 0.6-0.8 region and power the design for that objective before execution.

**Remaining science gap.**
- An externally sourced, end-to-end placement study in which compilation, state materialization, cache invalidation, recovery, and inference are all charged under one vector resource contract.
- A prospective optionality analysis under changing query families, including leave-one-responsibility-out transfer and break-even-horizon uncertainty.
- Matched strong decoders and online-search/retrieval baselines so state value cannot be attributed to a deliberately weak readout.

**Decisive successor.** When does precomputed or learned state lower total decision cost without sacrificing task quality once compiler work, state memory, invalidation, recovery, inference, and future responsibility uncertainty are all charged?

**Primary estimand/theorem.** The primary estimand is the paired difference in task loss at a fixed resource envelope, accompanied by the break-even query horizon and a Pareto-frontier comparison over the full registered resource vector. A co-primary robustness estimand is leave-one-responsibility-out regret under responsibility change.

**Minimum design.** Freeze at least two public real-system workloads with naturally repeated heterogeneous queries and mutable source state. Predeclare task/query episodes, update events, resource instrumentation, responsibility families, quality floor, non-inferiority margin, and scalarization sensitivity grid. Evaluate compiled state, universal/raw state, online search, and cache/materialization strategies under identical hardware and budgets. Keep the r=3 and ten-responsibility failures as prespecified stress strata rather than filtering them out.

**Critical controls/checks.**
- narrow r=3 capacity regime in which the hostile decoder should erase the claimed advantage
- large responsibility family in which the predeclared quality floor is expected not to hold
- source-update episodes that invalidate cached state
- paired or hierarchical uncertainty by workload, update epoch, and responsibility
- quality non-inferiority interval and risk of violating the floor
- complete vector resource accounting with Pareto and scalarization sensitivity

**Terminals:** `ORION21_EXTERNAL_PLACEMENT_REGION_SUPPORTED_UNDER_REGISTERED_RESOURCE_CONTRACT` / `ORION21_EXTERNAL_PLACEMENT_VALUE_NOT_ESTABLISHED__BOUNDARY_AND_NEGATIVES_RETAINED`

**Externality:** new_external_data, independent_implementation, independent_evaluator, external_compute_or_system. The decisive missing evidence is an end-to-end external-system placement comparison, not another synthetic width sweep.

**Forbidden moves.**
- Do not average the r=3 hostile failure or the ten-responsibility negative into a broad positive headline.
- Do not compare a compiled state to a weak decoder while giving the compiled arm privileged task information or preprocessing.
- Do not collapse the resource vector to one scalar without publishing the exchange rates and sensitivity analysis.
- Do not use the >=8/10 miss to claim capability is absent throughout the moderate 0.6-0.8 region.

## ORION-23 — Responsibility-Carrying State

**State:** `BOUNDED_REAL_AND_VERIFIER_BACKED_RESPONSIBILITY_RELATIVE_REUSE_SUPPORTED__EXTERNAL_WORKFLOW_BREADTH_OPEN` · **Priority:** `P3_OPTIONAL_EXTERNAL_WORKFLOW_IF_CLAIM_WIDENS` · **Track:** responsible AI systems / certificate reuse / adaptive scientific state

**Strongest defensible claim.** Scientific state reuse is responsibility-relative rather than provenance-relative on the registered real-data and verifier-backed domains: a current, well-provenanced record can still be unsupported for a changed responsibility, while a responsibility-aware transport rule can preserve valid state and reopen only what the drift invalidates.

**Boundaries.**
- P13A's self-scored zero-harm endpoint is entailed by the same support bit used by the policy and permanently cannot authorize safety superiority.
- Current donor-complete and transport results are bounded to authored CNF/digits mechanisms and do not establish arbitrary semantic drift or deployed-workflow safety.
- More benchmark count is not itself useful; additional work is justified only when the final claim widens to external responsibilities or real workflows.

**Immediate repairs.**
- Make P13A's outcome-entailment failure the first design correction in Methods, then lead Results with digits, verifier-backed responsibility change, donor-complete D2, and certificate transport.
- State that no additional basic benchmark is needed for the current bounded headline; mark each proposed external workflow as claim-widening research.
- Add a responsibility-source field to every result table so internally authored, public, and externally governed responsibilities are never conflated.

**Remaining science gap.**
- Externally determined responsibilities and drift events in a real workflow if the paper seeks a deployed or broad scientific-agent claim.
- A real-data replication of the strongest provenance-tiered donor comparison at matched information and cost.
- Adversarial semantic drift beyond clause-diff transport, with evaluator/evidence-source changes and explicit limits on what can be certified.

**Decisive successor.** When responsibilities and evidence semantics change in real workflows, can responsibility-carrying state avoid unsupported reuse while reducing unnecessary raw recovery relative to strong provenance-tiered memory?

**Primary estimand/theorem.** Primary: unsupported-reuse rate under responsibility change, with task/verifier correctness as a hard safety floor. Co-primary: raw recovery/read cost relative to always-raw and donor-complete provenance-tiered baselines. Secondary: false revocation or needless reissue under benign drift.

**Minimum design.** Only if widening beyond the current bounded claim, freeze at least two organization-disjoint workflows where responsibilities, gold outcomes, and drift events are set outside the ORION implementation. Match information, provenance, and read budgets across RCS, strongest provenance-tiered donor, unconditional reuse, and always-raw. Include delayed evaluator/evidence-source changes and adversarial responsibility remappings. Predeclare a zero-unsupported-reuse or stringent non-inferiority safety gate and an efficiency effect large enough to matter operationally.

**Critical controls/checks.**
- retain the P13A entailed endpoint as a self-scoring negative control
- current provenance but changed unsupported responsibility
- benign redundant drift where reissue is unnecessary
- clustered uncertainty by workflow and organization
- hard correctness/unsupported-reuse gate with exact adverse accounting
- paired raw-read or recovery-cost interval

**Terminals:** `ORION23_EXTERNAL_RESPONSIBILITY_RELATIVE_REUSE_SUPPORTED` / `ORION23_EXTERNAL_WORKFLOW_VALUE_NOT_ESTABLISHED__CURRENT_BOUNDED_CLAIM_RETAINED`
