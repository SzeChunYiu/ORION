- Freeze the question, corpus/task identities, comparator implementations, metrics, resource budget, thresholds, adverse terminal and stop rule before any protected outcome is opened.
- Use the strongest donor-complete baseline at matched information, action support, evaluator access, compute, memory, preprocessing and human/external services.
- Distinguish external data, independent implementation, independent evaluator and institutional/authority independence. None implies another.
- A proof checker establishes validity of a formal statement under its dependencies; it does not by itself establish that the statement expresses the intended scientific claim.
- Report adverse, null, retracted, protocol-invalid and CANNOT_CHECK outcomes in the main evidence arc when they constrain the headline.
- Use intervals, hierarchical or paired analyses, equivalence/non-inferiority margins, sensitivity analyses and gate-attainability checks appropriate to the inference unit.
- For probabilistic claims, use proper scoring rules, a testable/actionable calibration analysis and risk-coverage or selective-prediction behavior; do not use ECE alone as decisive authority.

- Do not equate review packaging, deterministic replay, signatures, provenance, multi-agent agreement or an internally issued terminal with external scientific authority.

- For every claimed arm separation, require the disfavoured arm to exhibit the attributed failure at least once on the same instrument; otherwise the terminal is CANNOT_CHECK, not PASS.
- Emit per-item or per-case values alongside aggregates whenever the inferential plan may require pairing, clustering, discordance or heterogeneity analysis; aggregate-only serialization must be labelled an irrecoverable emission gap.
- An analysis or manuscript change on an open pull request is pending evidence, not current-main authority. Record its PR state and independently bind it before using it in an abstract, title, conclusion or active claim ledger.
- When a protocol declines a statistic, record the declination as a named result value such as NOT_COMPUTED_BY_PROTOCOL with its reason; an absent field is not an auditable decision.
