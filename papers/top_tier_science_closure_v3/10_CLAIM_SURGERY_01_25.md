# Manuscript-ready claim surgery for ORION-01 through ORION-25

**Status:** editorial repair language, not new scientific evidence.  
**Authority delta:** `NONE`.  
**Use:** replace over-broad abstract, results, discussion, conclusion, cover-letter and graphical-abstract text while retaining paper-specific notation. Do not copy a block into a manuscript until all numbers and artifact identifiers are rebound to the paper's current authority files.

A top-tier paper does not need a universally positive conclusion. It does need one identifiable question, one auditable answer, a visible failure boundary, and a decisive next test. The blocks below are deliberately written to survive skeptical statistical, formal-methods, systems and reproducibility review.

## ORION-01 — Certificate Realization

**Headline replacement.** We characterize certificate and registry realizability for the declared finite language and prove the bounded results under the stated ceiling. These results establish a scoped formal boundary; they do not establish source-complete production realization.

**Mandatory limitation.** The registered production gate was rejected, and neither a production move/guard registry nor semantic preservation for an implemented system is presently proved.

**Conclusion replacement.** The contribution is therefore a bounded realization theory and an auditable account of where production transfer remains open, rather than evidence of deployed completeness.

**Next-test sentence.** A successor study must freeze a source-complete registry, independent implementation and semantic-preservation oracle before observing production outcomes, with realization and impossibility as symmetric terminals.

**Conclusion replacement.** The theorem-level contribution survives, but portable selective value is not established by the present campaign.

**Next-test sentence.** The decisive experiment is a frozen heterogeneous task set with predicted value/no-value strata, per-task emission and matched geometric, lexical, learned and oracle refinements under identical information and abstention support.
