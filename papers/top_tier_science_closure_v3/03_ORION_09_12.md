**State:** `PROGRAMME_SYNTHESIS__UNIVERSAL_LOW_ORDER_MOTIF_REFUTED` · **Priority:** `P1_NEGATIVE_THEORY_SYNTHESIS` · **Track:** quantum compilation / structural geometry

**Boundaries.**
- No universal low-order boundary or generic small trade basis exists.
- The size-transfer capacity invariant adds no predictive value and cannot be rescued by another feature search under the same identity.
- TARE closed-form explanations were refuted even while support-two exactness survived.

**Immediate repairs.**
- Replace any universal motif language with a fibre-purity/identifiability theorem and counterexample taxonomy.
- Quantify how many feature vocabularies are observationally equivalent and which interventions separate them.
- Move search histories to supplement; lead with the theorem/countertheorem pair.

**Remaining science gap.**
- A mathematically sharp characterization of the equivalence classes/fibres that explains both successful families and the StabPrep obstruction.
- A synthesis theorem about when regime geometry is identifiable from a feature vocabulary, rather than another low-order predictor.
- A manuscript rewrite that makes the negative transfer result central.

**Decisive successor.** Under what necessary and sufficient conditions can a finite feature vocabulary identify compilation regimes, and when are mixed fibres an irreducible obstruction?

**Primary estimand/theorem.** A general fibre-purity theorem for exact regime separation plus a lower bound on unavoidable error for mixed fibres; optional transfer corollaries must predict both successful and failed families without post-hoc vocabulary changes.

**Minimum design.** Formalize the feature map, target regime and equivalence relation. Prove exact separability iff each fibre is pure, then derive quantitative error floors for mixed fibres and characterize minimal separating refinements. Validate on all frozen families, including StabPrep as a required adverse instance. A new family may be used only as a predeclared holdout.

**Critical controls/checks.**
- vacuous capacity bound
- mixed fibre with abundant unused capacity
- feature refinement that memorizes outcomes
- human theorem proof
- mechanized finite corroboration
- independent fibre construction

**Terminals:** `ORION09_FIBRE_IDENTIFIABILITY_THEORY_EXPLAINS_SUCCESS_AND_FAILURE_FAMILIES` / `ORION09_UNIVERSAL_TRANSFER_REMAINS_REFUTED__SPECIALIST_SYNTHESIS_RETAINED`

**Externality:** independent_implementation, independent_evaluator. The strongest remaining path is a theory synthesis, not another promotion benchmark.

**Forbidden moves.**
- Do not revive or imply uniqueness of a low-order boundary.
- Do not treat a true but never-firing invariant as useful.
- Do not run a second rescue feature search after the registered promotion stop.

**Decisive successor.** Can theorem-backed static certificates reduce exact compilation cost while preserving calibrated refusal outside their domain, and when can their explanations be faithful?

**Terminals:** `ORION10_STATIC_CERTIFICATE_HAS_OPERATIONAL_VALUE_WITH_CALIBRATED_DOMAIN_REFUSAL` / `ORION10_OPERATIONAL_TRANSFER_NOT_ESTABLISHED__EXACT_COST_THEOREM_RETAINED`
