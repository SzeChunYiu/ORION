## ORION-09 — Symbolic circuit discovery
- `BAND`: A
- `CURRENT_TOP_TIER_READY`: false
- `BASELINE_PROMOTION_ALLOWED`: true
- `IDENTITY`: active_top_tier_closure
- `HARD_RETRACTION`: false
- `CURRENT`: Strong holdout and hostile controls exist; a second human-curated truth domain is the decisive remaining gap.
- `GAPS`: The proposed symbolic separator may reflect one annotation convention or circuit family. || Human gold uncertainty and inter-rater agreement are not yet a first-class endpoint. || Transfer across unseen size, architecture, and semantic families must be separated from interpolation.
- `NEXT_EVIDENCE`: A1: commission blinded dual annotation for a second truth domain. || A2: freeze family/size-disjoint transfer before tuning. || A3: equalize comparator representation access and budgets.
- `PRIMARY_ENDPOINTS`: Exact or near-exact symbolic recovery on family-disjoint external circuits. || Transfer of the claimed separator across unseen size and semantic families. || Improvement over all matched-information comparators with uncertainty over circuit families.
- `EXTERNAL_STATUS`: required_not_yet_credited
- `EXTERNAL_REPLICATION`: Two human-curated truth domains from independent curators, one using unseen circuit families and an annotation protocol not written by the model authors. || Curators retain the final circuit identities and gold labels; adjudication is blinded to method output and disagreements remain in the released data.
- `CALIBRATION_UNCERTAINTY`: Use family-cluster bootstrap or exact permutation tests over families. || Calibrate confidence/abstention against adjudicated and disputed labels using proper scores and risk–coverage curves.
- `CONTROLS`: Random codebook and label permutation. || Lookup baseline that can memorize development circuits but cannot transfer.
- `SUCCESS`: All primary endpoints pass on the second curated domain and both curators agree on the claim interpretation. || The separator survives function-preserving perturbations and defeats matched-information baselines.
- `KILL`: Any dependence on symbol names, one annotation convention, or memorized families blocks the broad claim. || If a capacity-matched baseline ties under transfer, novelty must rest on a proved property or efficiency—not accuracy rhetoric.
- `MANUSCRIPT_UNLOCK`: Only after independent human-curated domain transfer, family-level inference, annotation-uncertainty calibration, and matched comparators pass.
- `FALLBACK`: Publish a high-quality benchmark/resource with a bounded symbolic result and explicit failure circuits.

## ORION-10 — Controller-complete saturation for gcd/multiplication theorem families
- `BAND`: A
- `CURRENT_TOP_TIER_READY`: false
- `BASELINE_PROMOTION_ALLOWED`: true
- `IDENTITY`: active_top_tier_closure
- `HARD_RETRACTION`: false
- `CURRENT`: Emergence, mutation, stability, and negative checks pass in a finite scope; independent theorem-family and formal-scope closure remain.
- `GAPS`: A finite census cannot support a universal theorem without a proof or an explicit finite statement. || The controller-completeness definition and saturation boundary require independent formal review for circularity. || Transfer to an independently chosen theorem family is missing.
- `NEXT_EVIDENCE`: A1: split universal and finite claims in the ledger. || A2: formalize controller completeness and non-circularity tests. || A3: commission an external theorem-family challenge.
- `PRIMARY_ENDPOINTS`: Machine-checked theorem for the exact stated scope, or an explicitly finite exhaustive result with no universal language. || Exact agreement with the independent oracle on the complete preregistered finite domain. || Frozen-controller success on an externally selected theorem family without rule changes.
- `EXTERNAL_STATUS`: required_not_yet_credited
- `EXTERNAL_REPLICATION`: An independent formal-methods group selects at least one theorem family, re-encodes the controller, and reproduces finite and formal claims without the original proof scripts. || No shared proof lemmas or generated certificates for the clean-room lane; only the public theorem statement, interface, and protocol are shared.
- `CALIBRATION_UNCERTAINTY`: For randomized comparators report seed-level intervals and exact failure counts. || Calibrate scope by a coverage map: proved, exhaustively verified, empirically sampled, undecided, or out of scope.
- `CONTROLS`: Seeded false lemmas and controller mutations. || Theorem families where saturation is known to be insufficient.
- `SUCCESS`: The machine-checked scope, exhaustive oracle agreement, and external-family transfer all pass. || Independent formal reviewers accept non-circular definitions and reproduce certificates.
- `KILL`: Proof/checker disagreement, circularity, or a universal claim supported only by finite cases blocks promotion. || Failure on the externally chosen family narrows the paper to the proved family.
- `MANUSCRIPT_UNLOCK`: Only after exact scope separation, independent formalization, externally selected theorem-family transfer, and full cap accounting pass.
- `FALLBACK`: Publish the finite verified corpus and controller mutation benchmark with a theorem scoped only to what is actually proved.
