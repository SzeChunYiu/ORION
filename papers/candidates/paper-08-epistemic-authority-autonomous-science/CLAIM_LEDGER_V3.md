## Allowed headline
> ORION-18 composes modern action authorization, delegation provenance, heterogeneous authorization receipts, cross-domain authority relations, typed verifier certificates, scientific release gates, claim-evidence chains, research harnesses and evidence-ledger adjudication into a common scientific-discharge calculus. Local donor authority remains intact, while authority to establish a scientific conclusion propagates only through typed non-widening chains or protected coercions, fail-closed blocker semantics and surviving complete support families.
