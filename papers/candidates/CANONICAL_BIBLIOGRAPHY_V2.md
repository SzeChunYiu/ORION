## Terminology note

“Authority laundering” is already used in 2026 agent/delegation/AI governance contexts and in adversarial-ML work. ORION-18 claims no ownership of the term. Its formal contribution is the scientific discharge/coercion interface and associated theorems.

## Submission rule

Before any paper is submitted, search for peer-reviewed versions of cited preprints and replace the preprint citation when an archival version exists. Run one final literature delta because 2026 agent-governance work is changing rapidly.
