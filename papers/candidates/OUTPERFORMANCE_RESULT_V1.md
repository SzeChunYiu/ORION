## Why this is the right ORION result

The purpose of engulfing is not to remove credit from the donor. It is to make the donor's strong mechanism one coordinate of a larger scientific system and discover which other coordinates are necessary to answer the larger question.

The isolated donor remains correct in its native scope. The strict result appears only because the cross-structure task asks a decision that depends on information outside that native projection.

The ideal donor product is the fairness check: once all strong structures and correct interfaces are supplied, ORION must earn any further advantage through a real extra law or measurable engineering property.
