# Verified venue requirements — 2026-08-18

**Verification date:** 2026-08-18  
**Primary sources:** current official journal author guides.

## Artificial Intelligence (AIJ) — ORION-16 and ORION-17

Official guide: `https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence/publish/guide-for-authors`

### Fit and completeness

AIJ accepts regular papers presenting mature, complete research across automated reasoning, knowledge representation, heuristic search, planning/action, multi-agent systems and reasoning under uncertainty. Mathematical papers are welcome but must include accessible high-level motivation, explanatory commentary and implications for AI systems.

**Submission consequence:** ORION-16/ORION-17 retain formal proofs/checkers but their submission sources must foreground the AI problem, donor relations, falsifiers and system implications.

### Required/checked package items

- editable manuscript source; LaTeX is supported;
- keywords;
- all references cited in text and all citations present in the reference list;
- competing-interests statement;
- spell/grammar and cross-reference check;
- transparent declaration of generative-AI/AI-assisted manuscript use when applicable, placed before references;
- corresponding-author e-mail and full postal address in submission metadata;
- no fixed manuscript-length limit, while unnecessarily long manuscripts can impede review.

### Highlights

Elsevier guidance specifies 3–5 concise highlights, each no more than 85 characters. ORION-16 and ORION-17 therefore include separate highlight files.

### Review model

The current AIJ guide does not instruct authors to anonymize the regular manuscript. The package therefore keeps a separate title-page file but does not assume double-anonymous review.

## Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems (JAAMAS) — ORION-18

Official guide: `https://link.springer.com/journal/10458/submission-guidelines`

### Mandatory information sheet

Every submission must include a 1–2 page information sheet. For a regular paper it must precisely answer:

1. the main claim and why it matters to autonomous-agent/MAS research;
2. the evidence and principal results/implications;
3. the closest work by other authors and the exact relation;
4. any prior publication of the work and the added value of the journal submission.

An incomplete/uninformative sheet may be returned without review. ORION-18 therefore includes `JAAMAS_INFORMATION_SHEET.md`.

### Manuscript package

- original research with transparent relation to any prior archival material;
- editable source files at submission and revision; LaTeX is accepted for mathematical content;
- title page with author, affiliation and active corresponding-author e-mail; ORCID if available;
- abstract 150–250 words;
- 4–6 keywords;
- numbered in-text references in square brackets and a consecutively numbered reference list;
- relevant `Statements and Declarations`, including competing interests;
- substantive LLM use documented in Methods or another suitable manuscript section;
- no LLM/AI system listed as an author.

## Shared submission integrity rules

For all three papers:

- no simultaneous submission to another journal;
- no fabricated empirical result;
- no uncredited donor mechanism;
- no `first`, `only`, `unprecedented`, `subsumes`, or superiority wording unless a specific claim ledger authorizes it;
- the exact submitted source must correspond to a green reproducibility/CI commit;
- author-only personal declarations not available from public/repository evidence remain confirmation-at-submit metadata under #377, not guessed text.
