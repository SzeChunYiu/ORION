## Purpose

This file turns the ORION-21–ORION-24 issue family into one executable publication programme while preserving scientific authority boundaries between papers. It follows the repository rule that the top-level `papers/` numbered directories remain the five canonical ORION flagships; ORION-21–ORION-24 therefore live as publication candidates under `papers/candidates/` until their own external promotion gates are earned.

The programme hypothesis is deliberately stronger than any current paper terminal:

> State is a task- and responsibility-relative computational interface. A system may spend resources constructing state, reasoning over state, retaining or recovering state, and certifying state for particular responsibilities; a separate fail-closed research-governance layer controls which resulting scientific claims may be promoted.

This is **not yet an earned portfolio claim**. Each paper must survive its own donor subtraction, protected protocol, hostile controls, negative-history retention, reproduction route and publication gate.

## Research cell and division of responsibility

The programme uses four independent expert lenses. These are roles, not scientific authorities; none can self-certify the claim it helps design.

1. **Theory and donor-subtraction lead** — formalizes rank/sufficiency/resource objects; searches for classical or recent work that already owns the primitive; narrows claims when donors subsume them.
2. **Experimental design and statistics lead** — freezes estimands, protected splits, matched-budget controls, uncertainty procedures and negative-result dispositions before outcomes.
3. **Systems and reproducibility lead** — owns resource receipts, deterministic replay, source identity, state transformations, real-system execution and exact verifier/tool accounting.
4. **Hostile reviewer and governance lead** — attacks leakage, answer laundering, arbitrary scalarization, self-certification, hidden extra compute, stale certificates, benchmark memorization and overclaim; preserves failed terminals.

For every headline finding, all four lenses must agree on: (a) what was actually measured or proved, (b) the nearest donor, (c) the strongest alternative explanation, (d) the exact resource/evidence boundary, and (e) the allowed claim. Disagreement narrows or reopens the claim; it never gets resolved by prose.

## Shared state objects

The following objects must remain distinct in code, receipts and manuscripts:

- `RAW` — original evidence/state;
- `UNIVERSAL` — materialized state intended to support a broad query family;
- `TASK_COMPILED` — state constructed for the current task/query;
- `CACHE_SET` — retained task compilations;
- `RESPONSIBILITY_CARRYING_STATE` — state plus externally grounded responsibility/reopen contract.

Every transformation records source identity, compiler identity, information loss, recoverability, cacheability and resource cost. Re-encoding is not automatically compression; compression is not automatically lossless; recoverability is not immediate accessibility.

## Shared resource-accounting rule

#664 owns a vector-valued resource receipt. No paper may manufacture a winner by redefining cost after outcomes or hiding compiler work. The minimum common fields are:

`compile_ops, compile_time, state_bytes_or_tokens, model_identity, active_capacity, generated_tokens_or_steps, search_nodes, verifier_calls, tool_calls, cache_bytes, recovery_cost, wall_time`.

Training cost for learned compilers/allocators is reported separately and amortized only under a prospectively stated reuse horizon. Paper-specific analyses may project this vector but may not replace it with a post-hoc scalar. See `P11_P14_RESOURCE_ACCOUNTING_SCHEMA_V1.md`.

**Earned controlled positives.** The frozen parity-query theorem establishes that a fixed query-agnostic representation supporting exact linear readout of all size-`s` parity queries requires at least `binom(d,s)` dimensions, while a query-conditioned direct compiler can expose one active coordinate. The more general rank theorem states `m >= rank(span(F))` for exact fixed linear support and gives the orthonormal average approximation lower bound `1 - m/N`.

The frozen ORION-21 confirmatory run passed all gates with deterministic replay SHA-256 `8e790cf8bb8012bea8e575549730a58b21a0e1e96e51a2928d165c6fa89f3567`. Universal/compiled dimension ratios were 91×, 1820×, 816× and 1140×; compiled state reached the 0.90 threshold at `n=32` in every frozen cell, while universal state required 128, 1024, 1024, or did not reach threshold by 1024.

The P11B no-answer-laundering control also passed with deterministic replay SHA-256 `6f0260d84c5aba236c247960feef837428f2aa7806782c0698bb49073243abf6`. It exposed only 5–7 active latent components, never the final answer or its negation, yet reduced the observed 0.95 sample threshold by 32× or more in high-dimensional cells.

The controlled optionality result established exact compile/cache/materialize workload crossings. Under the frozen uniform workload, first grid crossover horizons were 0.50N, 1.00N and 2.00N for batch-efficiency parameters 0.25, 0.50 and 0.75. Compiled-only future single-query coverage after raw loss is exactly `r/N`.

**Unearned gates.** Learned/non-oracle compiler, stronger nonlinear decoder attack, common accessibility-work accounting, and real-system replication remain open. Therefore ORION-21’s current manuscript claim is controlled/theorem-backed only.

## Negative-result elimination programme

“Eliminate” means eliminate the *cause of uncertainty or failure through new discriminating evidence*, not erase the historical result.

### N1 — ORION-21 stronger-decoder erasure risk

**Threat:** a sufficiently strong matched decoder over universal state may remove the apparent benefit.

**Programme:** freeze decoder-capacity sweep before outcomes; equalize total compiler+decoder resources; report the crossover where universal state catches up. If the effect disappears, narrow the claim to bounded-decoder accessibility/sample burden. If a learned compiler still creates a strict Pareto gain at matched total resources on unseen queries, promote the stronger result.

### N2 — ORION-21 compiler-cost laundering

**Threat:** compilation appears beneficial only because preprocessing/training is free.

**Programme:** #664 schema is mandatory for every arm; separate one-shot inference cost, reusable training cost and amortization horizon. No scalar winner is reported without a prospectively supplied cost vector.

### N3 — ORION-22 no joint advantage

**Threat:** state construction and reasoning may not provide complementary information, or allocation signals may be too weak.

**Programme:** use a crossed controlled generator with independent access difficulty and reasoning-depth difficulty so compile-first, think-more, both and neither are all truly optimal somewhere. Freeze pre-outcome signals and family holdouts. If the joint learner cannot approach oracle value, diagnose signal insufficiency versus policy-class insufficiency with prespecified ablations; do not inspect protected outcomes to redesign the labels.

### N4 — ORION-23 failed finite-sample sentinel

**Threat:** the old sentinel was statistically brittle rather than scientifically meaningful.

**Programme:** retain P14A unchanged; define a new estimand—higher-rung verified performance difference conditional on prospectively matched lower-rung performance—with confidence intervals/noninferiority rather than a maximum-deviation threshold. Freeze a fresh generator and seeds before execution. The successor must succeed on its own terms and may coexist with the historical negative.

### N5 — ORION-23 always-abstain / always-reopen degeneracy

**Threat:** unsafe reuse can be reduced trivially by never using compact state.

**Programme:** primary safety endpoint is unsafe reuse subject to frozen task-success noninferiority; report unnecessary reopen, raw bytes/tokens loaded, latency and verifier calls. Responsibility-carrying state must beat confidence-only/unqualified compression and lie strictly inside the always-raw/always-reopen safety–cost frontier.

### N6 — ORION-24 bureaucratic safety

**Threat:** ORION-RSE reduces false claims only by suppressing useful discovery or consuming more compute.

**Programme:** co-primary structure: false scientific promotion/overbroad-claim rate plus a prospectively frozen noninferiority margin on independently accepted useful residual/discovery rate. Match model/tool/token/search budgets. Run ablations for donor subtraction, protected freeze, negative history, authority separation, recursion stops and reopen semantics. If safety improves only through blanket abstention, the superiority claim fails.

### N7 — ORION-24 leakage / vocabulary reward

**Threat:** benchmark cases mirror ORION’s own development history or judge rewards ORION terminology.

**Programme:** generate fresh hidden-gold cases with reminted identities and multiple lexical/structural templates; keep evaluator-only fields hidden; use independent blinded reviewers for realistic cases; score scientific dispositions and bounded claims, not ORION words.
