# Upward-claim promotion rule V1

**Owner:** #977

This rule is normative for the P6–P15 promotion wave.

## Rule

When a newly located nearest work materially owns a mechanism currently used in an ORION headline:

1. record the donor and ownership explicitly;
2. absorb the mechanism into the strongest comparator/product;
3. prohibit novelty language for the donor-owned component;
4. do **not** automatically shrink the paper to the leftover wording;
5. reopen the scientific question at the next abstraction level;
6. freeze a discriminator that tests a relation, decision problem, transport law, obstruction, allocation law, responsibility boundary, governance benefit or integrity guarantee **above** the donor component;
7. execute the protected test before promoting the broader claim;
8. if the donor product closes the higher question too, retain equivalence/subsumption/negative authority and recurse upward only if a new falsifiable question exists.

## Forbidden moves

- hiding or omitting a stronger donor to preserve novelty;
- swapping vocabulary while claiming the same donor-owned mechanism;
- using a weaker comparator after a stronger donor is known;
- calling a planning hypothesis an earned claim;
- deleting negative or donor-subsumed results;
- claiming breadth that appears only in motivation/Discussion but not in protected evidence;
- moving sibling-paper results into a manuscript without an explicit ownership/interface boundary.

## Promotion invariant

**Claim breadth may move upward only when evidence breadth moves upward with it.**

The programme prefers a broad falsifiable negative/equivalence result to a narrow positive manufactured by comparator selection or wording.
