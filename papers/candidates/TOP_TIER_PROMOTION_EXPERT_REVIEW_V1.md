# P6–P15 top-tier promotion expert review V1

**Programme:** #977

Every promotion wave must be reviewed through four independent lenses. A single reviewer may fill multiple roles only when the judgment artifacts remain separately reasoned and conflicts are recorded.

## Role A — formal methods / semantics

Background: formal verification, programming languages, epistemic logic, theorem proving or equivalent.

Responsibilities:

- attack theorem assumptions and hidden equivalences;
- distinguish definitions from earned theorems;
- test composition/transport/revocation boundaries;
- reject universal wording unsupported by quantified assumptions;
- identify donor-equivalence results that should replace novelty claims.

## Role B — empirical ML / systems

Background: experimental ML, agents, systems measurement, benchmarking or equivalent.

Responsibilities:

- enforce matched information/access/total resources;
- require strongest runnable donor-product comparators;
- audit experimental unit, uncertainty, multiplicity and missing-run handling;
- attack data leakage, post-hoc tuning and weak baselines;
- require real-system/non-toy validation at the abstraction level of the headline.

## Role C — nearest-work / editor

Background: broad field expertise and publication/editorial judgment.

Responsibilities:

- search changed vocabularies and adjacent parent fields;
- classify donors as `ADOPT / ADAPT / COMPOSE / DEFER / REJECT`;
- force upward generalization when a lower component becomes donor-owned;
- detect sibling-paper overlap and salami slicing;
- check that the surviving object is interesting to readers beyond ORION itself.

## Role D — hostile reproducibility / authority

Background: reproducible science, security/adversarial testing, independent validation or equivalent.

Responsibilities:

- separate generator, evaluator and authority;
- attack content binding, replay, race, stale-state and self-certification paths;
- preserve negative/null/subsumed/protocol-defect history;
- require clean-environment reproduction and exact result/manuscript binding;
- keep `CANNOT_CHECK` available when evidence is incomplete.

## Required joint disposition

For every headline claim, the four lenses must jointly answer:

1. **What strongest donor product already exists?**
2. **What higher scientific question remains after absorbing it?**
3. **What exact protected observation could falsify the higher claim?**
4. **Who/what independently decides that observation?**
5. **What negative outcome would change the framework or claim rather than be explained away?**
6. **Does the evidence support the same breadth as the manuscript language?**

A paper cannot receive `TOP_TIER_SUBMISSION_READY` while any role records a material unresolved blocker.
