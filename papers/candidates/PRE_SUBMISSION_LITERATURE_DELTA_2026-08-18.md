## Method and stop rule

The rounds are considered no-material-change only if later results add donors or terminology but do not force a new formal object, theorem premise, benchmark terminal or paper disposition.

## Round 1 — close-current-work pressure

### ORION-16 additions

- **BoardroomAI: Dependency-Aware Human-Steerable Multi-Agent Deliberation through Evolving Decision Graphs**, arXiv:2608.13046 (2026). Typed decision graphs, intervention compilation, dependency-aware propagation, preservation of unaffected artifacts, specialist reactivation and decision-validity evaluation. **Disposition:** ADOPT as current dependency-repair/typed-decision donor; selective propagation/preservation is not ORION-16 novelty.
- **Validation Evidence in LLM Repair Agents: How Much of What Passes Actually Tests the Bug?**, arXiv:2607.28871 (2026). Strong pressure that a positive validation event need not contain bug-discriminating evidence. **Disposition:** ADOPT as evidence-role pressure; it does not supply ORION-16's scientific-admissibility contract.
- **Action Evidence Boundary for Consequential Agent Effects** and **Authorization Evidence Chains** (IETF Internet-Drafts, 2026). Exact-action binding, evidence-role/freshness distinctions and local authorization decisions. **Disposition:** ADOPT as effect/authorization donors; ORION-16 cannot claim exact artifact/evidence binding in general.

**ORION-16 material effect:** no theorem change. The surviving claim is still the scientific-certification layer and typed-erasure separation, not dependency repair/evidence binding.

### ORION-17 additions

- Fiona Y. Wang and Markus J. Buehler, **Self-Revising Discovery Systems for Science: A Categorical Framework for Agentic Artificial Intelligence**, arXiv:2606.01444 (2026). Scientific discovery is modeled as revision of a typed representational regime; old artifacts are transported across verified regime transitions. **Disposition:** ADOPT as a very close representation/provenance-transport donor. ORION-17 must not claim representational-regime transport itself.
- David N. Olivieri and Roque J. Hernández, **Sheaf-Theoretic Transport and Obstruction for Detecting Scientific Theory Shift in AI Agents**, arXiv:2605.14033 (2026). Formalizes transport/gluing obstruction and representation-extension decisions. **Disposition:** ADOPT; ORION-17 does not own transport/obstruction generally.
- Maximiliano Armesto and Christophe Kolb, **Toward a Science of Intent: Closure Gaps and Delegation Envelopes for Open-World AI Agents**, arXiv:2604.25000 (2026). Introduces explicit closure-gap/delegation-envelope language and separates misclosure from undersearch. **Disposition:** ADOPT as close closure/stopping pressure; ORION-17 does not own the generic notion of an open-world closure gap.

**ORION-17 material effect:** claim narrowing only. The surviving object is the stronger relation between representation/objective change and **scientific closure/obligation transport**, especially evidence-validity-without-closure plus censored/open stopping semantics.

### ORION-18 additions

- Rui Tang et al., **Context Is Not Authority: Structured Runtime Governance for Financial Market Agents**, arXiv:2608.09025 (2026). Typed adapter-bound candidates, missing/stale obligations as coverage debt, exact-artifact receipts and reauthorization after state change. **Disposition:** ADOPT as a close authority-handoff donor. ORION-18 may not claim `evidence/context is not authority`, exact-artifact binding, stale-obligation tracking or pre-commit rechecking as broad novelty.
- **Action Evidence Boundary for Consequential Agent Effects** and **Authorization Evidence Chains** (IETF Internet-Drafts, 2026). **Disposition:** ADOPT; heterogeneous evidence roles, action binding and fail-closed authorization are donor territory.
- **SteerBench-Work: A Benchmark for Agent Steering at Action Boundaries**, arXiv:2608.12654 (2026). Bidirectional action-boundary evaluation exposes substantial over-refusal as well as unsafe allow. **Disposition:** ADOPT as evaluation pressure. Any future ORION-18 empirical evaluation must measure unnecessary refusal/clean authorized coverage, not only blocked attacks.
- **Mandato** and current cross-domain agent authorization/delegation specifications remain governance donors. **Disposition:** ADOPT for generic mandate/delegation semantics, not scientific-discharge novelty.

**ORION-18 material effect:** claim narrowing only. The surviving object is target **scientific-obligation discharge** across epistemic domains with full type composition, support-family revocation and the product-equivalence negative theorem.
