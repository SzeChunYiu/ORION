## Evidence levels

### `SUPPORTED_DESIGN_IMPLICATION`

May be stated as a consequence of current bounded P9 evidence.

### `PLAUSIBLE_APPLICATION_DONOR_SUPPORTED`

May be discussed because primary-source systems in the domain use structurally analogous objects/mechanisms. This is **not** evidence that P9 improves the application.

### `PROSPECTIVE_PILOT_UNRUN`

May be described only as a future/application experiment. No performance verbs such as `improves`, `outperforms`, `reduces`, or `solves` may refer to P9 without a new verified receipt.

## Negative-to-positive rule

The manuscript may say that a negative finding **suggests**, **motivates**, or **defines** a positive successor hypothesis. It may not say the successor is positive until its prospective experiment is verified.

The immutable sequence is:

`NEGATIVE -> RESPONSIBILITY -> MINIMUM REPAIR -> NEW PROTOCOL -> RESULT -> VERIFICATION`.
