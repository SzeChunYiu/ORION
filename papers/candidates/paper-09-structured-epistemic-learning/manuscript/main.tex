\begin{abstract}
Learning-system failures can arise because a representation omits information, because a learner fails to exploit information that is present, or because the remaining operation is computational rather than statistical. We study these cases in a prospectively frozen hierarchy of exact synthetic and procedural method-structure tasks. Model-visible views are restricted so that observational collisions produce exact empirical deterministic ceilings on the frozen sample. Generic classical learners receive first right of refusal; when an information-sufficient view still leaves a residual, a separately frozen explicit-inference control tests whether computation closes it before neural complexity is escalated. In M1, the semantic view supports mechanic selection at \MOneSemanticMechanicAccuracy, while affine gluing remains at \MOneSemanticGluingAccuracy despite an exact task ceiling of \MOneSemanticGluingCeiling; exact affine composition closes that residual. On a whole held-out domain of transactional workflows, typed relational method comparisons reach \DOneTypedAccuracy protected accuracy, versus \DOneUntypedAccuracy for untyped pair structure, \DOneSerializedAccuracy for a same-information typed serialization, and \DOneTranscriptAccuracy for reminted transcript features. We do not introduce a new graph, sheaf, neuro-symbolic, or latent architecture. The contribution is a bounded diagnostic methodology and evidence object for separating information, learning, computation, and transfer before architecture escalation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

A poor benchmark score does not uniquely diagnose a poor model. Two task instances may be indistinguishable under the representation given to the learner, in which case additional capacity cannot recover information that was never exposed. Conversely, a representation may contain enough information while a selected model family still fails because the target requires composition, search, or another operation that the predictor does not execute. Treating both situations as invitations to add parameters or test-time computation confounds representation quality with learning and inference.

This paper asks a deliberately bounded question: \emph{what can be learned, transferred, or inferred once representation identifiability is controlled explicitly?} We construct exact hostile pairs, restrict what a learner can observe, and derive the best deterministic accuracy possible on the frozen sample under each view. We then separate four quantities:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{information sufficiency}: whether a model-visible view can distinguish the protected targets;
    \item \textbf{learning sufficiency}: whether a generic learner reaches the performance permitted by an information-sufficient view;
    \item \textbf{computational sufficiency}: whether a separately frozen explicit procedure closes a residual once the required coordinates are visible;
    \item \textbf{transfer}: whether a representation learned or scored on some procedural domains remains useful on a whole held-out domain with reminted surface identities and protected corruptions.
\end{enumerate}

The broader ingredients are not new. Relational and graph inductive biases are established in graph Transformers and heterogeneous attention \citep{ying2021graphormer,hu2020hgt,rampasek2022gps}; local vector spaces and relation maps are established in sheaf-based representation learning \citep{gebhart2021knowledge,bodnar2022sheaf}; neural algorithmic reasoning and Transformer--algorithmic-reasoner hybrids already test structured execution and out-of-distribution generalization \citep{velickovic2022clrs,bounsi2024transnar}. Reusable abstractions and libraries are central to DreamCoder and LILO \citep{ellis2020dreamcoder,grand2023lilo}; reasoning outside token space is studied by continuous-latent methods such as Coconut \citep{hao2024coconut}; rule/entity factorization is established by Neural Production Systems \citep{goyal2021nps}; and mechanism-centric scientific representations are an active research direction \citep{posner2026mwm}.

The diagnostic principle is also not ours in the abstract. Recent work explicitly separates representation or perception failures from downstream reasoning failures \citep{liem2026temporal}, and deterministic benchmarks with symbolic--neural oracle controls can isolate algorithmic choke points \citep{anonymous2026chokepoints}. Missing-information benchmarks likewise ask systems to recognize when a task is underspecified and information must be acquired rather than guessed \citep{li2025questbench}. We therefore make no claim to be the first to separate representation from reasoning failure, and the collision-derived ceiling below is not a new theorem. Our residual contribution is the combined controlled study: exact view collisions, simple-learning first right of refusal, explicit computational closure, and protected cross-domain method-coordinate transfer under weaker and same-information controls.

The study supports three bounded conclusions. First, specified typed coordinates are genuinely load-bearing on exact hostile pairs: weaker views are non-identifying by construction rather than merely difficult. Second, the remaining M1 affine-gluing gap is computational: the semantic view contains enough information, but a frozen generic classical grid stays near chance while an exact payload-only composition closes the operation. Third, typed relational method comparisons transfer on a protected whole-domain holdout substantially beyond reminted transcript, untyped structure, and a same-information typed serialization. No neural architecture is required by the final bounded evidence.

\section{Related work and claim subtraction}

\paragraph{Structured representations.}
Graphormer, Heterogeneous Graph Transformer, and GraphGPS establish multiple ways to encode graph topology and relation type inside attention and message-passing systems \citep{ying2021graphormer,hu2020hgt,rampasek2022gps}. Knowledge Sheaves and Neural Sheaf Diffusion establish local vector spaces, restriction maps, and consistency-oriented computation \citep{gebhart2021knowledge,bodnar2022sheaf}. These works own the general idea that relational organization can be a useful inductive bias; P9 does not introduce it.

\paragraph{Algorithmic and hybrid computation.}
CLRS provides a broad benchmark for neural algorithmic reasoning, while TransNAR combines a language Transformer with a graph neural algorithmic reasoner \citep{velickovic2022clrs,bounsi2024transnar}. Compositional Choke Points goes further in the direction relevant here by using a deterministic benchmark and symbolic--neural oracle analysis to isolate failures of iterative computation \citep{anonymous2026chokepoints}. Our explicit-inference arm should therefore be read as a bounded diagnostic control, not a new symbolic fallback architecture.

\paragraph{Reusable mechanics, latent reasoning, and mechanism models.}
DreamCoder and LILO learn reusable program abstractions and libraries \citep{ellis2020dreamcoder,grand2023lilo}; Neural Production Systems factor rule templates from entity bindings \citep{goyal2021nps}; Coconut studies continuous latent reasoning \citep{hao2024coconut}; and mechanistic world-model work argues for reusable explanatory mechanisms in scientific representations \citep{posner2026mwm}. These are donor families, not novelty targets for the present paper. Our final study does not promote a learned module library, dedicated binding mechanism, recurrent latent reasoner, or causal-discovery architecture.

\paragraph{Representation diagnosis, missing information, and serialization.}
Temporal neuro-symbolic QA has been used to distinguish representation inconsistency from reasoning failure \citep{liem2026temporal}, while QuestBench tests whether a model can ask for missing information rather than answer an underspecified query \citep{li2025questbench}. Most directly for our D1 control, serialization friction shows that preserving underlying structured content while changing its serialization can materially change performance \citep{lo2026serialization}. We therefore do not claim the general principle that native relational structure can outperform a flat serialization. D1 instead asks whether that distinction remains load-bearing on our exact method-coordinate transfer protocol, with the semantic fields matched across the two typed arms.

\section{Problem formulation}

\subsection{Structured worlds and restricted views}

A benchmark world contains typed atoms, typed relations, optional local affine representation maps, candidate mechanics, admitted failure history, and an evaluator-only target. The learner never receives the protected target or protected generation metadata. Five progressively richer views are exposed:
\[
V_{\mathrm{surface}} \subset V_{\mathrm{topology}} \subset V_{\mathrm{typed}}
\subset V_{\mathrm{current}} \subset V_{\mathrm{semantic}}.
\]
Surface contains reminted surface structure; topology retains connectivity without semantic relation labels; typed adds semantic atom/relation/mechanic coordinates; current additionally exposes local transport values; semantic also admits scoped negative history.

For a finite protected sample $\mathcal{D}$ and view $V$, let $f_V(x)$ be the exact model-visible fingerprint. Partition $\mathcal{D}$ into groups $g$ of equal fingerprints. The empirical deterministic ceiling is
\[
C_V = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{D}|}\sum_g \max_y |\{x\in g : y(x)=y\}|.
\]
This is elementary accounting over the frozen equivalence classes. It is not a new theorem about Bayes error, sufficient statistics, state aliasing, or asymptotic learnability. Its role is operational: a model above this ceiling indicates leakage or an evaluator defect; a model at the ceiling of a non-identifying view cannot be improved deterministically without changing the information supplied or the task.

\subsection{Candidate-relative mechanic selection}

Mechanic identities are reminted across worlds and splits. The learned task therefore scores a current candidate $m_i$ relative to context $E$,
\[
s(E,m_i) \in \mathbb{R},
\]
rather than classifying against a global mechanic vocabulary. Candidate presentation order is derived from declared model-visible information and an independent seed. This rule was frozen after a pre-outcome hostile review found that ordering candidates using a richer hidden fingerprint could leak semantics through presentation position.

\subsection{Local affine gluing}

The D0 transport family uses exact one-dimensional affine maps between local charts. A complete directed cycle composes to
\[
x \mapsto ax+b.
\]
The protected target is \textsc{glue} when the composition is identity under the frozen exact/tolerance rule, \textsc{obstruction} when a complete unique cycle is non-identity, and \textsc{unknown} when visible information does not determine a unique complete composition. The family is intentionally small. Its purpose is to create a transparent global-composition discriminator, not to claim general sheaf-theoretic reasoning.

\section{Experimental design}

\subsection{D0 hostile information pairs}

Three paired families isolate different load-bearing coordinates while holding weaker views fixed.

\paragraph{Relation semantics.}
Two worlds share weaker structure but differ in whether an evidence relation supports or defeats a claim, changing which candidate effect is appropriate.

\paragraph{Local transport.}
Two worlds share typed endpoints and topology but differ in affine map values, yielding \textsc{glue} versus \textsc{obstruction}. Source-insufficient cases remain \textsc{unknown}.

\paragraph{Admitted failure history.}
Two worlds share the current typed state and candidate set but differ in scoped admitted negative history. Only the semantic view contains the decisive historical coordinate.

Hand-authored fixtures were generalized into deterministic train/dev/test corpora with opaque content-derived identities, split-disjoint namespaces, evaluator/model metadata separation, and exact manifests. These data are exact traces, not pseudo-gold extracted from natural scientific papers.

\subsection{M0 evaluator hardening}

Before learned execution, M0 froze a shared task interface, null predictors, an exact semantic oracle, and leakage sentinels. Pre-outcome reviews corrected two defects that are retained in the audit history: hidden semantic state initially influenced candidate order, and an early task adapter removed incidence information promised by the surface view. The result-bearing protocols use the corrected interfaces. The oracle is tested against falsified evaluator gold and is a harness ceiling, not a competitive model.

\subsection{M1: simple-model first right of refusal}

M1 uses generic classical models before any neural escalation: logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, and nearest neighbours. Mechanic selection uses field features and context--candidate interactions without a hard-coded global candidate vocabulary. Gluing uses generic field features and, when map values are visible, a canonical sequence of local affine coefficients; it does not compute the composed residual directly. Hyperparameters are selected using train $\rightarrow$ dev only, and each selected configuration receives one protected test evaluation. Candidate-order and row-order invariance, label-shuffle checks, and per-task information ceilings are mandatory.

Two implementation amendments occurred before protected M1 outcome access and did not alter the scientific protocol: a runtime compatibility repair made `DictVectorizer` output dense arrays after scikit-learn 1.9 rejected CSR index metadata, and a later smoke-test-only repair increased the tiny smoke fixture so the already-frozen $k=15$ nearest-neighbour configuration was defined. The official 64/24/48 train/dev/test corpus sizes, feature semantics, model grid, dev selection, protected test, and terminal rules were unchanged.

\subsection{Explicit inference first right of refusal}

When a residual is algorithmically transparent, a separately frozen payload-only procedure receives first right of refusal before richer learned architectures are considered. The A5 transport procedure reconstructs the visible local cycle and composes unique affine maps exactly. A2 maps visible typed relation semantics to declared candidate effects. A4 filters candidates using only admitted scoped failure history, with an explicit fallback when no such history is visible. These controls do not establish that symbolic inference is generally superior; they test whether neural complexity is necessary for these bounded atoms.

\subsection{D1: whole-domain procedural transfer}

D1 moves from generated state worlds to exact authored method structures. Training domains are numerical methods and graph algorithms; the entire protected test domain is transactional workflows. Surface action identities are reminted and domain-disjoint. Training/dev contain aligned examples, single-coordinate obstructions, and source-insufficient \textsc{unresolved} examples. The protected test additionally includes multi-coordinate corruptions whose constituent coordinates appeared separately in training.

Four arms receive progressively different organization of the data:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item reminted transcript/action bag;
    \item untyped pair/topology features;
    \item explicit typed relational comparison features;
    \item a same-information typed canonical serialization without the explicit relational comparison.
\end{enumerate}
The last contrast is central. It does not test whether the serialized arm lacks the semantic fields; it tests whether the relational organization of those matched fields is load-bearing on this protocol. Because serialization friction is established prior work \citep{lo2026serialization}, the result is interpreted only at this application-specific scope.

The D1 protocol was amended before results to compare dependencies by method-local role topology rather than literal action names and to make dependency corruptions reverse an actually present edge rather than silently no-op.

\subsection{Verification and authority boundaries}

Each result-bearing study had a pre-artifact independent expectation frozen before official outcome access. Official results were compared to those expectations and preserved with execution and verification receipts. A2/A4 and D1 have zero discrepancies. M1 has one retained non-material discrepancy: the independent implementation expected a random-forest-class selected model, while the official selected nearest-neighbour model tied the random forest exactly on dev accuracy and won under the pre-frozen lower-complexity tie-break. Material discrepancy count is zero.

All outputs are diagnostic. No model score can grant scientific truth, novelty, execution permission, or adoption authority.

\subsection{M1 separates information from computation}

Across the frozen view ladder, M1 never exceeds its empirical deterministic ceiling. Surface and topology both attain their ceilings (\MOneSurfaceAccuracy/\MOneSurfaceCeiling{} and \MOneTopologyAccuracy/\MOneTopologyCeiling{}, respectively), while typed reaches \MOneTypedAccuracy{} at a ceiling of \MOneTypedCeiling{}. Richer views expose more information but also reveal a residual: current obtains \MOneCurrentAccuracy{} against an overall ceiling of \MOneCurrentCeiling{}, and semantic obtains \MOneSemanticAccuracy{} against \MOneSemanticCeiling{}.

The task decomposition identifies the source of that gap. Semantic mechanic ranking reaches \MOneSemanticMechanicAccuracy{}, so the visible typed relation/history coordinates are sufficient and exploited on the mechanic task. In contrast, semantic affine gluing remains at \MOneSemanticGluingAccuracy{} despite an exact task ceiling of \MOneSemanticGluingCeiling{}; current gluing is likewise \MOneCurrentGluingAccuracy{}. This is the pattern expected from a computation residual rather than an information deficit on the frozen benchmark. The general idea of separating representation from downstream reasoning failure has close parents \citep{liem2026temporal,anonymous2026chokepoints}; our claim is limited to this prospectively controlled instance.

\subsection{Explicit computation closes the D0 residual}

A5 confirms that the residual need not motivate a richer neural architecture. With only typed endpoints and no local map values, the payload-only affine procedure remains unresolved (full-task accuracy \AFiveTypedAccuracy{}). Once the current view exposes the local affine maps, exact cycle composition reaches \AFiveCurrentAccuracy{}. Thus the operation missed by M1 is exactly computable from visible coordinates in this D0 family.

A2 and A4 show a complementary pattern. Topology alone gives \ATwoTopologyAccuracy{} on the relation hostile pair, while typed relation semantics yield \ATwoTypedAccuracy{} using the explicit relation selector. On the history family, current-state information gives \AFourCurrentAccuracy{}, while the semantic view with admitted scoped failure history yields \AFourSemanticAccuracy{}. These results establish that the coordinates are load-bearing on their exact hostile pairs, not that neural mechanic or memory modules are required.

\subsection{Typed relational structure transfers across a whole held-out domain}

D1 provides the positive transfer result. On transactional workflows held out as an entire procedural domain, the typed relational arm reaches \DOneTypedAccuracy{}, versus \DOneUntypedAccuracy{} for untyped pair structure, \DOneSerializedAccuracy{} for the same-information typed serialization, and \DOneTranscriptAccuracy{} for reminted transcript features. The typed relational gain is \DOneTypedMinusTranscript{} over transcript and \DOneTypedMinusSerialized{} over the same-information serialized control. It also retains \DOneTypedDoubleAccuracy{} accuracy on protected multi-coordinate corruptions and \DOneTypedUnresolvedAccuracy{} on explicit \textsc{unresolved} cases.

The typed-versus-serialized result must be interpreted carefully. It does not establish the novel principle that serialization can obscure native structure; that principle is directly studied elsewhere \citep{lo2026serialization}. It shows that, on this exact method-coordinate protocol, explicit relational comparison remains load-bearing after semantic-field information is matched and surface identities are reminted.

\section{Discussion}

\subsection{A diagnostic ladder before architecture escalation}

The results motivate a practical order of operations for structured learning experiments. First ask whether the representation identifies the target on a frozen diagnostic set. If it does not, capacity comparisons are uninterpretable as tests of reasoning. If it does, give a simple learner first right of refusal. If a residual remains, test whether an exact or otherwise appropriate explicit computation closes it. Only a residual that survives those steps licenses a more complex learned architecture within the scope of the study.

This doctrine is deliberately non-universal. Deterministic or symbolic controls are not appropriate for every open-world task, and a finite collision analysis is not an asymptotic learning theorem. The broader ideas of oracle decomposition, missing-information recognition, and representation diagnosis are already present in prior work \citep{anonymous2026chokepoints,liem2026temporal,li2025questbench}. P9 contributes a concrete, reproducible instantiation in which all three diagnostics materially change the interpretation of the same programme of experiments.

\subsection{Why no neural P9 model is promoted}

The original design space included graph, sheaf, neural-algorithmic, recurrent-latent, binding, causal, and advanced-training branches. The final evidence does not justify promoting them. Relation semantics and failure history are closed by explicit selectors; affine transport is closed by exact composition; and the positive D1 transfer result is already achieved with a classical logistic model over the typed relational representation. A new neural reasoner would therefore answer a different, prospectively reopened question rather than complete the present one.

This is not evidence that richer models are generally useless. It is a scope-specific stop decision: no frozen residual remains in the final P9 claim that requires a neural architecture.

\subsection{Design implications and prospective applications}

The bounded results support four design implications. First, expose a semantic coordinate only when it is externally grounded, because topology can be non-identifying. Second, preserve scoped negative history where the current state alone aliases distinct decisions, but do not globally poison a mechanic after one failure. Third, use exact computation for known invariants when the necessary operands are visible rather than relearning deterministic algebra. Fourth, retain explicit unresolved states when the source does not identify a target.

Software incident diagnosis, proof planning, process/workflow diagnosis, and robotics are plausible structure-native application domains, but no application-success number is claimed here. A separate prospective pilot is required before these design implications become application-performance claims.

\section{Limitations}

The evidence is intentionally narrow. D0 consists of exact synthetic hostile worlds. D1 consists of exact authored/procedural method structures in three computational domains, not independently annotated natural-science papers. The study therefore provides no estimate of annotation reliability or ecological validity for broad scientific-method extraction.

The empirical ceilings are finite-sample statements over exact model-visible equivalence classes. They do not establish Bayes-optimal error, asymptotic sample complexity, or universal representation sufficiency. The M1 model family is deliberately classical and bounded; the absence of a justified neural escalation in this study is not a claim that neural methods cannot help on a future discriminator.

D1 is one whole-domain transfer study. Although its surface identities are reminted and the protected test contains multi-coordinate corruptions and unresolved cases, it does not establish universal cross-domain compositional generalization. The same-information serialization contrast is likewise application-specific and should be read against the broader serialization-friction literature \citep{lo2026serialization}.

We do not test causal discovery, dedicated variable binding, recurrent latent reasoning, LLM integration, theorem-proving improvement, method invention, or scientific authority. Those topics remain outside the final claim rather than being silently scored as negative.

\section{Conclusion}

P9 began as a question about how much learned structure an autonomous research system might need. The bounded evidence yields a smaller and more diagnostic conclusion. Some structural coordinates are necessary information: removing relation semantics, local transport values, or admitted failure history can make paired targets non-identifying. Once those coordinates are visible, simple learning is sufficient for several tasks, while the one clear classical-learning residual is a global affine composition that exact inference already closes. On an exact whole held-out domain, typed relational method comparison nevertheless produces a strong protected transfer result beyond weaker and same-information controls.

The resulting contribution is not a new graph, a new theorem, or a new general-purpose reasoner. It is an evidence-bound methodology for deciding what a failed structured learner is actually missing before escalating model complexity, together with a controlled cross-domain result showing that the organization of typed method coordinates can matter on a protected procedural task.
