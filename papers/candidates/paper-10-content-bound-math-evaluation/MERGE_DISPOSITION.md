## Decision

ORION-20 passes the corrected numerical discriminator and retains a valuable hostile
parser invalidation, exact corpus, native receipts and mutation controls. It
does not pass the independent-paper residual test after knowledge assimilation.

- Versioned benchmark identity is established practice.
- Native checking and statement faithfulness are separate mature evaluation
  gates.
- Sequential proof mining predates ORION-20, and TacMiner supplies a stronger
  dependency-graph object and downstream utility test.
- ORION's remaining identity/correctness/authority separation is owned by
  ORION-14/ORION-18.

A standalone manuscript would either rebrand solved infrastructure or promote
a coarse source projection beyond its evidence. The technical note preserves
the result, correction and implementation without doing that.

   shared framework.

3. The technical note and claim ledger can be reviewed or excerpted by ORION-14/ORION-18.
4. Missing Phase 2B data and graph/native-trace baselines remain explicit
   triggered follow-ups, never disguised as passed gates.

a TacMiner-class baseline on the same frozen corpus, downstream proof utility,
multi-revision robustness, and a contribution not already owned by ORION-14/ORION-18.
