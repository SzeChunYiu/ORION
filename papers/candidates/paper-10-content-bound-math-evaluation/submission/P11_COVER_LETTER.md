# P11 cover letter draft

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Bytes, Builds, and Meaning: Content-Bound Evaluation for Evolving Lean Repositories”** for peer review in an automated-reasoning venue.

The paper is an evaluation-methods and empirical failure-analysis study in Lean 4. Its central contribution is not a new theorem prover or tactic miner. Instead, it demonstrates a measurement failure that can survive inside an otherwise machine-checkable formal corpus: a prospectively frozen source analysis initially appeared numerically positive, but hostile audit showed that 1,289 of 4,861 extracted trajectories crossed intervening top-level commands. The manuscript preserves that result as invalid, reports a separately frozen corrected analysis, and then connects the failure to content-bound native-verification receipts and controlled source/revision/statement/attempt substitutions.

The paper’s main message is that formal native acceptance is a powerful but local evidence coordinate. Artifact identity, extraction validity, native acceptance, statement faithfulness, and scientific authority should remain distinct in evolving formal-repository evaluation. This position is supported by committed machine-readable artifacts, exact environment identities, deterministic replays, an invalid negative control, full null distributions, and a claim ledger that prevents the corrected source recurrence result from being promoted into unsupported tactic-utility or theorem-faithfulness claims.

The manuscript explicitly absorbs and credits the closest prior work on Lean repository tracing, evolving/versioned benchmarks, tactic dependence mining, repository-scale evaluation, semantic faithfulness, benchmark defects, and version-aware refactoring. The resulting contribution is intentionally bounded as an empirical measurement case study and reproducibility discipline.

All result-bearing artifacts and reproduction instructions are available in the accompanying ORION repository package. The earlier invalidated protocol generation remains visible rather than overwritten.
