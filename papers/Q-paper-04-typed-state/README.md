## One job

Q4 isolates whether typing and scoping partial-knowledge state changes decisions when competing arms receive the same visible information. Six prospectively frozen exact-synthetic study families cover unknown feasibility, stale failure receipts, interval costs, transported certificates, active experiments, and receipt remint/transport. Each family includes a matched-information comparator and hostile control; two additional negative/donor-absorbed studies show that the methodology can refuse a mechanism claim.

The rewrite makes the experimental contract a Methods section and reports the six families as Results rather than repeating a protocol preamble before every study. It also separates mechanism-isolation evidence from deployment/generalization language.

## Durable result

Within the six frozen worlds, the typed/scoped mechanism strictly improves on the matched-information ablation when the typing is decision-relevant, and exactly ties the strongest comparator in the prespecified no-value regime. Hostile controls verify that the corresponding untyped shortcut is actually exposed by the constructed world.

## Boundary

All primary worlds are exact synthetic constructions. LLM-named arms are deterministic proxies, not evaluations of real models. There is no cryptographic-security, impossibility, real-interface, or deployment claim. N1-C's allocation policy is donor-closed; N2-F5B's original-world claim is donor-absorbed and survives only under the specified misspecification condition.
