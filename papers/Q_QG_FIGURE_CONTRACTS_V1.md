# ORION-Q / ORION-QG main-figure contracts V1

Date: 2026-08-21
Method: `nature-figure` scientific-contract pass.

Every proposed display below has a scientific job, a data/evidence source, an authority boundary and a forbidden visual shortcut. No figure may create a stronger claim than the manuscript/claim ledger. Exact target production dimensions are deferred to venue-specific packaging; scientific content and accessibility are fixed here first.

## Global figure rules

1. **Evidence role before aesthetics.** Every panel must answer a reader question needed by a headline claim.
2. **No color-only meaning.** Use labels, shapes, line styles or symbols as redundant encodings.
3. **No pooled score across incomparable experiments.** ORION-04 worlds and Q/QG authority classes retain separate scales.
4. **Exact counterexamples are visible.** Do not hide one refuting row in an aggregate success rate.
5. **Negative/donor outcomes stay main-text visible when load-bearing.**
6. **Theorem/exhaustive/evidenced/forecast-only states use visibly different annotation or panel labels.**
7. **Timings are descriptive unless a frozen inferential design exists.**
8. **Every quantitative figure has a machine-readable source table generated from committed receipts.**
9. **No screenshot of terminal output as a scientific figure.** Receipts are data sources, not visual design.
10. **No figure copied from an analogue paper.** Analogue papers may guide evidence roles only.
