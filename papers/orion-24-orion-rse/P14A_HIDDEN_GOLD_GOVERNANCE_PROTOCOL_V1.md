# P14A Hidden-Gold Research Governance Decision Benchmark V1

**Paper:** ORION-24 — ORION-RSE  
**Issue:** #669; programme #670  
**Protocol:** `ORION.P14A.ResearchGovernanceDecisionBench.v1`  
**Frozen:** 2026-08-21 before protected execution.

## Purpose

Test whether the *governance decision contract* adds controlled scientific-decision value beyond strong positive-evidence, reflection/checklist, donor-aware and multi-review rule baselines when every arm sees the same research packet and has the same maximum decision budget.

This benchmark does **not** simulate an autonomous scientist and cannot by itself authorize a claim of superiority over real research agents. It is a hidden-gold contract discriminator.

## Case facts

Each synthetic research packet contains independently generated facts about:

- positive versus null observation;
- evidence integrity;
- whether the endpoint/protocol was frozen before observation;
- identifiability;
- nearest-donor ownership;
- whether apparent value exists only in an interaction;
- whether an earlier negative/subsumed history exists;
- whether the new evidence is genuinely independent/materially regime-changing versus a same-evidence rereading.

All candidate policies receive the same facts. Protected gold is generated by a separate adjudication function and is not exposed as a terminal label.

## Gold dispositions

- `CANNOT_CHECK`: evidence integrity, protocol freeze or identifiability is insufficient.
- `SUBSUMED`: positive evidence exists but nearest donor fully owns the proposed residual.
- `INTERACTION_ONLY`: evidence supports only a joint interaction, not a standalone atom.
- `RETAIN_NEGATIVE`: a putative positive is only a same-evidence rereading of a live negative/subsumed history without a material new discriminator.
- `SUPPORTED_RESIDUAL`: valid, identifiable, prospectively frozen positive evidence with a non-donor-owned standalone residual and no unreopened negative-history conflict.
- `NEGATIVE`: no positive residual is supported.

Only `SUPPORTED_RESIDUAL` authorizes positive scientific promotion.

## Compared policies

All policies receive identical records and are charged the same fixed seven-check decision budget.

1. `RAW_POSITIVE`: promotes any positive observation.
2. `REFLECTION_CHECKLIST`: additionally checks evidence integrity, freeze and identifiability.
3. `DONOR_AWARE_REVIEW`: additionally subtracts donor-owned positives.
4. `MULTI_REVIEW`: additionally detects interaction-only cases.
5. `ORION_RSE_FULL`: additionally preserves live negative/subsumed history and allows reopening only after independent/material new evidence.
6. ORION-RSE component ablations: no donor subtraction, no freeze, no interaction handling, no negative history.

The strongest non-ORION baseline is selected prospectively as the best of arms 1–4 on disposition accuracy, not after outcome by a custom metric.

## Protected split

- Seed `2026082114`.
- 20 held-out families.
- 400 cases per family.
- Family Bernoulli rates are independently varied within prospectively fixed ranges; each family is mixed with a common base distribution to prevent degenerate single-failure families.
- No ORION development episode is copied.

## Endpoints

Primary safety: **false scientific promotion rate** — `PROMOTE` when gold is not `SUPPORTED_RESIDUAL`.

Co-primary productivity: **useful discovery recall** — promotion rate among gold `SUPPORTED_RESIDUAL` cases.

Secondary:
- full disposition accuracy;
- donor-subsumption accuracy;
- interaction-only accuracy;
- `CANNOT_CHECK` accuracy;
- retain-negative/reopen accuracy;
- family-block rates;
- component-ablation deltas.

## Positive terminal

`P14A_CONTROLLED_GOVERNANCE_SUPERIORITY_SUPPORTED` requires all:

1. ORION-RSE false promotion = `0`;
2. ORION-RSE useful discovery recall = `1`;
3. strongest non-ORION baseline false promotion >= `0.05`;
4. ORION-RSE disposition accuracy exceeds the strongest non-ORION baseline by >= `0.08`;
5. each registered ablation creates at least one additional false promotion or disposition error;
6. ORION-RSE correctly distinguishes all retained-negative versus materially reopened cases;
7. every arm uses the same fixed decision-budget receipt;
8. two fresh executions are byte-identical.

## Claim authority

A positive result authorizes:

> In a preregistered hidden-gold controlled benchmark of scientific admissibility decisions, the full ORION-RSE governance contract achieves lower false-promotion error than positive-only, reflection/checklist, donor-aware and interaction-aware rule baselines without reducing valid residual promotion.

It does not authorize real-agent, cross-domain, longitudinal human-review, or autonomous-scientist superiority. Those remain separate ORION-24 gates.
