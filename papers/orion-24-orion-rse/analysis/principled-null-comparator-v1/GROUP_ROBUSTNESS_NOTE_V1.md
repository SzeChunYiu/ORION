# ORION-24 group-deletion robustness — fixed internal panel

**Subject:** frozen 67-packet `external_v1` gold + SYSTEMB decisions  
**Terminal:** `INTERNAL_ADVANTAGE_NOT_DRIVEN_BY_ANY_SINGLE_FAMILY_OR_DOMAIN`  
**Scientific authority delta:** `NONE`

The principled-null result can still be misleading if one family or one domain supplies
nearly all of its separation. This analysis deletes each group in turn and **recomputes the
per-domain majority null on the retained rows** rather than keeping an obsolete null fixed.

## Result

Full panel: SYSTEMB 56/67 versus recomputed stratified-majority 16/67, with 40 SYSTEMB-only
correct packets and 0 null-only correct packets.

All eight leave-one-family-out analyses still favour SYSTEMB. The weakest deletion leaves
**28 SYSTEMB-only correct versus 0 null-only correct**. All three leave-one-domain-out
analyses also favour SYSTEMB; the weakest leaves **24 versus 0**.

The family-local margin against the full-panel stratified null is positive in **8/8
families**. The smallest is `INTERACTION_ONLY` at +1; the largest is
`APPARENT_POSITIVE_SUBSUMED` at +12. Thus no family has a negative local contribution to
the fixed-panel separation.

## What this changes

It removes two internal explanations of the headline result:

1. the separation is not generated by one dominant family; and
2. the separation is not generated by one domain whose label skew makes the null unusually
   weak.

This is useful robustness because the comparator is regenerated after every deletion.

## What it does not change

All 67 gold dispositions remain internally authored/adjudicated. Group deletion therefore
does **not** create external-investigator authority, a population sampling model, or a
standalone top-tier empirical claim. The strongest remaining gate is still independent
external/blinded adjudication on a genuinely external construct set.

The analysis also does not convert families into independent statistical sampling units.
The exact paired p-values printed by the executable are descriptive sensitivity checks on
this fixed panel; they are not used here as population inference.
