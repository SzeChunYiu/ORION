\markdownRendererHeadingOne{ORION-RSE contract, lifecycle and evaluation}\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}A research atom carries a bounded claim/question, parent identity, nearest donors, protected discriminator, protocol identity, evidence/resource receipts, evaluator identity, authority owner, disposition and stop/reopen conditions. Atomization is an investigation unit, not automatic publication fragmentation.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}The contract distinguishes \markdownRendererCodeSpan{SUPPORTED\markdownRendererUnderscore{}RESIDUAL}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{SUBSUMED}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{INTERACTION\markdownRendererUnderscore{}ONLY}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{REDUNDANT\markdownRendererUnderscore{}EQUIVALENT}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{OVERREACH\markdownRendererUnderscore{}HARMFUL}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{NON\markdownRendererUnderscore{}IDENTIFIABLE}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{CANNOT\markdownRendererUnderscore{}CHECK}, negative/null outcomes and \markdownRendererCodeSpan{RETAIN\markdownRendererUnderscore{}NEGATIVE} when new material does not justify reopening. Positive observation is therefore not identical to positive scientific promotion.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}Before novelty promotion, nearest prior mechanisms are recorded and the residual is classified as adopted, adapted, composed, deferred or subsumed. The discriminator and terminal rules are frozen before protected outcomes; a material post-outcome change requires a new protocol identity. Negative results can motivate successors but cannot be rewritten.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}The lifecycle is:\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}\markdownRendererCodeSpan{question -> atoms -> donor subtraction -> protected discriminator -> protocol freeze -> execution/receipts -> disposition -> recurse/stop -> later reopen if materially warranted}.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}\markdownRendererCodeSpan{CANNOT\markdownRendererUnderscore{}CHECK} is a valid scientific disposition when evidence or authority is insufficient.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator

\markdownRendererHeadingTwo{Evaluation contract}\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}All policies receive identical case facts and identical fixed decision-check budgets. Registered policies are \markdownRendererCodeSpan{RAW\markdownRendererUnderscore{}POSITIVE}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{REFLECTION\markdownRendererUnderscore{}CHECKLIST}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{DONOR\markdownRendererUnderscore{}AWARE\markdownRendererUnderscore{}REVIEW}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{MULTI\markdownRendererUnderscore{}REVIEW}, \markdownRendererCodeSpan{ORION\markdownRendererUnderscore{}RSE\markdownRendererUnderscore{}FULL} and component ablations. Primary safety endpoint is false scientific promotion; productivity endpoint is useful-discovery recall among gold \markdownRendererCodeSpan{SUPPORTED\markdownRendererUnderscore{}RESIDUAL} cases; full disposition accuracy and component-specific correctness are secondary. This prevents an always-abstain system from winning.
