# Specification-Separated Conformance for Scientific Claim-Promotion Policies

## Abstract

Research agents can generate candidate claims faster than they can determine what those claims scientifically warrant. We evaluate a fail-closed claim-promotion contract that distinguishes bounded support, donor subsumption, interaction-only evidence, retained negative history, non-identifiability, and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

The evaluation sequence contains two preserved benchmark failures before the result-bearing study. In the first benchmark, the registered comparator thresholds are unattainable over the protocol's own sampling support: both depend on one rare discriminator whose maximum prevalence is 0.042326, below required bars of 0.05 and 0.08. The terminal is therefore `CANNOT_CHECK`, not evidence for or against the governance contract. A balanced seven-disposition successor separates the intended semantics but is implementation-circular because the focal policy calls the same adjudication function that generates gold.

The central study separates specification from implementation. Twenty-eight explicit gold cases comprise four precedence variants for each of seven scientific strata. Case identity, stratum, rationale, and gold label are hidden; policies receive only factual booleans. The full contract conforms on 28 of 28 cases with zero false promotion and full retention of registered valid discoveries. The strongest partial policy, `MULTI_REVIEW`, conforms on 24 of 28 and false-promotes in the four cases requiring distinctions it omits. These are exact counts over specification variants, not estimates from 28 independent draws.

The supported contribution is a falsifiable governance specification and an implementation-independent conformance test. No externally held research incidents were available, so the paper does not establish improvement in open-ended science, causal reduction of false discoveries, or general research-agent safety.

## 1. Introduction

A positive result is not automatically a promotable scientific claim. Apparent novelty may belong to a donor. Evidence may support an interaction but not an isolated component. A live negative result may remain binding. A protocol may have changed after outcome. The evaluator may lack authority to decide.

Research agents amplify both productivity and these failure modes. A useful governance layer must therefore operate on scientific disposition, not merely on confidence or reviewer count.

We ask a narrow question:

> Can a complete promotion contract conform to a separately frozen scientific specification more exactly than partial review policies under matched factual input?

This is a conformance question. It does not assume that the specification is the final or universally correct model of scientific judgment.

## 2. Promotion contract

Each research atom carries:

- a bounded claim and parent identity;
- nearest donors and residual contribution;
- protected discriminator and protocol identity;
- evidence and verification status;
- evaluator and authority identity;
- scientific disposition;
- stop and material-change reopening conditions.

The disposition vocabulary includes supported residual, donor-subsumed, interaction-only, redundant equivalent, overreach, non-identifiable, negative, retained negative history, and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

The contract is non-compensatory. A favorable coordinate cannot offset a missing hard obligation. Evidence validity does not create novelty; reviewer multiplicity does not erase a live negative; and a same-evidence reinterpretation does not count as material reopening.

## 3. Donor subtraction and chronology

Before promotion, the nearest donor mechanisms are recorded. The residual is classified as adopted, adapted, composed, deferred, or subsumed.

The protected question, comparator, and terminal rule are frozen before outcome. A material post-outcome change creates a successor protocol rather than rewriting the predecessor's disposition.

This chronology is essential in recursive research. Without it, an agent can change the target and the explanation together until every failure appears to have been progress toward the final positive result.

## 4. Negative history and reopening

A negative or null result remains active state. Reopening requires material new evidence, a changed regime, or a new authority-bearing object. Rephrasing the same evidence is insufficient.

This rule is the main difference between the complete contract and the strongest partial comparator in the central specification. Both policies can process validity, donors, and interaction evidence. Only the full contract distinguishes legitimate reopening from resurrection of a live negative without new support.

## 5. Authority separation

A benchmark gold artifact can own the label for a specification test. It does not own real scientific truth. Similarly, an automated verifier can check a rule without acquiring authority to promote a publication claim.

The study therefore separates:

- specification authority for the finite benchmark;
- policy implementation;
- scientific authority for external research.

The central result concerns only the first two.

## 6. Benchmark failure one: unreachable thresholds

The first mixed benchmark produces zero false promotions for the full contract and nonzero false promotion for a partial comparator. Its preregistered terminal nevertheless fails.

A support analysis shows that both required comparison gates are functions of the same discriminator state. Across the protocol's declared sampling support, that state has maximum prevalence 0.042326. The required false-promotion and accuracy-separation bars are 0.05 and 0.08.

No sampled world could have satisfied the conjunction. The instrument was incapable of producing a positive result before the seed was drawn.

The execution is preserved, but its scientific disposition is `CANNOT_CHECK`. It cannot be converted into a favorable or adverse result about the mechanism.

## 7. Benchmark failure two: circular conformance

A balanced successor gives equal weight to seven dispositions. The full policy reaches exact accuracy one, zero false promotion, and valid-discovery recall one. The strongest partial policy reaches accuracy 0.857143 and false promotion 0.142857.

The focal implementation, however, invokes the same adjudication routine used to generate protected gold. The result shows that the intended semantics differ from the ablations, but it does not test an independent implementation of those semantics.

The circular benchmark remains diagnostic history and motivates the third study.

## 8. Specification-separated study

The result-bearing benchmark contains seven scientific strata:

1. clean bounded support;
2. material reopening;
3. retained negative history;
4. donor subsumption;
5. interaction-only evidence;
6. `CANNOT_CHECK`;
7. negative evidence.

Each stratum has four precedence variants, for 28 explicit cases. The gold case table is frozen separately from every policy implementation.

Before evaluation, policies are denied case identity, stratum, rationale, and gold disposition. They receive only factual booleans describing the scientific state. Precedence conflicts require the full ordering of the contract rather than one marginal rule.

The rows are specification variants. Their role is coverage of semantic branches, not sampling from a population.

## 9. Exact conformance result

The full contract returns the registered disposition on all 28 cases:
\[
28/28.
\]
It has zero false promotion and retains every registered valid discovery.

`MULTI_REVIEW` returns the registered disposition on:
\[
24/28.
\]
Its four failures all occur on distinctions supplied by the full contract and omitted from the partial one. The comparator retains valid discoveries but false-promotes in the affected precedence cases.

Every registered component ablation fails at least one stratum. Two executions produce identical canonical decisions.

No binomial interval or population \(p\)-value is attached to these rows. The result is exact conformance to a finite specification, not estimated accuracy on research incidents.

## 10. Why blanket abstention cannot win

A safety-only benchmark can reward a policy that never promotes. The specification therefore includes valid supported residuals and scores useful-discovery retention.

The full contract succeeds on both sides: it blocks every registered false promotion and retains every registered valid discovery. This does not prove optimal scientific productivity. It shows that the finite contract is not satisfied by universal rejection.

## 11. Donor boundary

Preregistration supplies chronology discipline. Provenance and truth-maintenance systems supply dependency history. Multi-agent review and reflection supply iterative critique. Research-agent systems supply recursive generation and tool use.

The residual contribution is an integrated promotion lifecycle evaluated as a decision object: donor subtraction, negative-history retention, material reopening, non-identifiability, authority separation, and `CANNOT_CHECK` are explicit scored outcomes rather than informal principles.

## 12. External boundary

A successor requires independently held research incidents, blinded adjudication, frozen thresholds, and separate custody. Those artifacts were unavailable, and zero external cases executed.

The missing panel is not a negative result. It is the reason the claim stops at specification conformance. Retrospective incidents from the same programme cannot substitute for prospectively unseen external authority.

## 13. Limitations

The gold specification originates inside the programme, even though its artifact and implementation are separated. Another scientific institution could choose different precedence or disposition rules. Twenty-eight semantic variants do not estimate performance on an open population.

The work does not establish external reviewer agreement, causal reduction in false discovery, improved research productivity, or general agent safety.

## 14. Conclusion

Scientific-governance policies should be tested as falsifiable decision contracts. One benchmark here is non-adjudicative because its thresholds are unreachable; a second is circular because policy and gold share an implementation; a third separates the frozen specification from the policy. On that 28-case specification, the full contract conforms everywhere, while partial review fails exactly where it lacks negative-history and precedence rules. The result is deliberately narrow: exact specification conformance, not autonomous scientific authority.
