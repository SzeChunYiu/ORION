## Stage 2 — obstruction certification

Valid certificates include exact finite non-reachability, machine-checked lower bounds, exhaustive bounded non-reachability, or independently verified resource-bounded obstruction under a predeclared search class. Timeout/low probability/one failed proof is not an obstruction.

The current generated studies certify exact old-language non-reachability and therefore rule out stronger search/synthesis/evolution **within those registered finite semantics**. Native systems remain a separate claim surface because their method languages/access interfaces are larger and messier.
