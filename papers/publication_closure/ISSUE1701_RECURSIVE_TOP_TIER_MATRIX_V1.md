## Recursion rule

For each row, after its next lane reaches a fixed terminal:

1. update the atomic claim ledger;
2. run implementation-independent hostile checking and mutation controls;
3. perform current donor/literature subtraction;
4. ask whether a materially different, non-rescue scientific question remains;
5. if yes, freeze a new identity and recurse; if no, the paper is at its strongest scientifically defensible ceiling;
6. if the only missing evidence is future events, independent human/institutional judgement, or genuinely separate administrative custody, record that external boundary rather than simulating it.
