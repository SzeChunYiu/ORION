## 3. Recursive workflow

Each manuscript passed through the following loop:

1. freeze the currently admissible evidence;
2. build a terminology and claim ledger;
3. identify the paper archetype and dominant scientific spine;
4. atomize headline definitions, numbers, proofs, and boundary statements;
5. subtract donor-owned ideas and remove priority inflation;
6. draft or restructure the complete manuscript;
7. run hostile counterexample and alternative-explanation review;
8. correct inference-unit and authority errors;
9. run editor synthesis and decision-risk review;
10. produce a second, shorter refinement where material allowed;
11. run consistency, artifact-leakage, punctuation, and standalone-surface checks.

\[
zsf(\mathbb F_2^d;A)=\operatorname{rank}(\operatorname{span}A),
\]
rather than only an ambient inequality with a special equality case. The refined paper now separates effective signature rank, ambient rank, and intrinsic compiler support.

### ORION-09

The stale “no clean predicate” account was reconciled with the later result. The first vocabulary retains its 43-of-1,146 irreducible floor. An expanded 127-feature vocabulary has an exact minimum four-feature separator on \(n\le3\), but the separator makes 32 errors among 120 unseen \(n=4\) cases and is not a transferred law. Normal-form complexity, boundary complexity, and transfer are treated as separate axes.

### ORION-11

The mechanism-necessity story remains retracted. Ordered search restores all three parent policies to full protected success, and one faithful parent matches both success and mutation-safety gates. The lower-cost residual is retained without mechanism attribution because the dedicated comparison has checker disagreement on 34 of 36 cells.

### ORION-18

The authority-state audit distinguishes semantic configurations from replicated donor labels. The 39,936 evaluations are 3,072 state configurations replayed under 13 labels; they are not described as 39,936 independent scientific mechanisms. The manuscript also separates cryptographic or formal rule conformance from externally governed scientific authority.

### ORION-19

The five-family intervention comparison is framed as mechanism evidence. On the false-compute-escalation endpoint, four family-level discordances favor the diagnostic and none favor generic compute escalation; the exact value is \(p=0.125\), and the generic comparator escalates compute by construction. The strongest retained transfer comparator is the non-degenerate untyped arm: 1.0 versus 0.90625, with 12 cases favoring typed and none reversing. Case-level uncertainty is not promoted because mutation-family cluster identifiers are absent. Transcript and same-information serialization margins remain withdrawn.

### ORION-23

The 3,840-point safety study is withheld because its harm endpoint changes in zero cases despite 2,304 action changes. The paper instead centers exact responsibility-relative sufficiency, the authenticated finite panel, and the 750-world three-valued transport law.

### ORION-24

The 28 cases are specification variants, not independent population draws. The manuscript reports exact conformance only: 28 of 28 for the full contract and 24 of 28 for `MULTI_REVIEW`. The unreachable-threshold and circular-gold predecessor studies remain visible as benchmark-design failures.
