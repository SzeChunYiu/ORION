## A third near-miss, same shape as the first two

Two ORION-15 claims first returned **zero** on the patterns `180 cases` and
`executable.rule`. Re-probing the directory — 186 files present — found `180` in 8,
`V4` in 16, `executable` in 8, `noncontrolling` in 1 and `preservation refusal` in
1. The claims are intact; the patterns were too specific.

That is now three times in this series that a single failed pattern would have
produced a fabricated absence, in three different papers. The rule that has caught
every one of them: **a pattern returning nothing is evidence about the pattern
until the directory is shown non-empty and a different literal has been tried.**
