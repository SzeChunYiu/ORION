## ORION-05 — sharp support-two TARE normal form

### Current bounded claim

For the declared three-block shared-Tag TARE-M2 grammar under the frozen support-count objective, the intrinsic uniform support number is exactly two: every admitted instance has an optimum with frame support at most two, and an exact two-qubit counterexample proves support one is not uniformly sufficient.

### Closest-work subtraction

**ADOPT — TARE donor.** Schillo, Sturm and Quay's TARE work owns the TARE primitive, Tag/Restore construction and anticommuting-unitary compiler setting. ORION-05 must claim only the sharp optimum-support theorem for the declared coupled grammar/objective.

**BACKGROUND / NOT SUBSUMING — MQT Predictor.** Quetschlich, Burgholzer and Wille, *MQT Predictor: Automatic Device Selection with Device-Specific Circuit Compilation for Quantum Computing*, ACM Transactions on Quantum Computing 6(1), 2025, DOI `10.1145/3673241`. It learns device choice and compiler-pass combinations across public quantum circuits. It does not prove a sharp all-size normal form for the TARE-M2 auxiliary representation.

**BACKGROUND / NOT SUBSUMING — QuTuner.** Zhong et al., *QuTuner: Feature- and Learning-Guided Optimization Pass Tuning for Quantum Compilers*, arXiv:`2607.04586` (2026). It learns pass-sequence tuning from static and optimization-aware features on Qiskit/PyTKET. This owns a strong adaptive compiler-tuning route, not the exact TARE support-two theorem.

### Closure consequence

No new experiment is required for the bounded theorem paper. The publication object must keep the support-count objective explicit, retain the support-one counterexample and negative/non-generalization boundaries, and avoid compiler-speedup, fault-tolerant-resource or quantum-advantage language.
