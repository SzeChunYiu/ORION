## Expert board

- **formal methods and theory:** Formal verification, exact algorithms, combinatorics, and theorem/certificate auditing. Rejects any theorem or impossibility claim lacking a checkable proof object, exhaustive boundary, or explicit CANNOT_CHECK terminal.
- **experimental design and statistics:** Prospective study design, power, negative controls, sequential testing, and error-rate control. Rejects post-outcome tuning, underpowered transfer claims, missing fair baselines, or unregistered endpoint changes.
- **domain and novelty:** Quantum compilation, software/research systems, knowledge representation, and primary-source novelty subtraction. Rejects donor relabelling, domain-forced equivalence, or claims whose novelty disappears after nearest-work subtraction.
- **reproducibility and forensics:** Content-addressed artifacts, clean-room replay, provenance, CI, and hostile mutation testing. Rejects unbound artifacts, stale packages, missing adverse records, or same-implementation replay presented as independent authority.
- **top tier editor:** Cross-disciplinary editorial triage focused on importance, broad relevance, surprising conclusions, and readable claim scope. Rejects technically correct but narrow work from a Nature-class claim unless the scientific consequence and external reach are demonstrated.
