## 1. Objective

Every ORION paper is to be prepared for an arXiv preprint at the strongest scope already earned, then routed to the highest journal whose scope and evidentiary expectations it can honestly satisfy. A broader title, abstract, or conclusion is allowed only after the registered computation, comparator, transfer, hostile test, independent replay, rights, custody, or external-adjudication gate that licenses it.

This programme deliberately separates four states:

1. **Manuscript quality** — argument, exposition, figures, references and reproducibility are publication-grade.
2. **Bounded scientific authority** — every claim is entailed by the evidence actually bound to the paper.
3. **Maximum-claim promotion** — the broad successor headline has survived its strongest registered falsifiers.
4. **Submission packaging** — the exact PDF, supplement, source archive, metadata and availability statements are current and byte-bound.

Polishing can improve state 1 and packaging can improve state 4. Neither can manufacture states 2 or 3.

## 2. Recursive expert review team

Each paper is reviewed recursively through five independent roles.

- **Theory and novelty lead:** reconstructs the theorem/claim dependency graph, identifies donor-owned machinery, checks counterexamples and removes novelty that disappears after donor subtraction.
- **Evidence and statistics lead:** audits experimental units, controls, denominators, uncertainty, nulls, adverse outcomes and any gap between deterministic conformance and population inference.
- **Reproducibility and custody lead:** binds code, data, environment, seeds, receipts, independent checks, negative history and exact final artifacts; same-researcher replays are not described as external replication.
- **Hostile reviewer:** searches for leakage, degenerate comparators, hidden information advantages, post-outcome adaptation, scope laundering, missing alternatives and claims that remain true only under one representation.
- **Journal editor:** tests whether the title, abstract, first figure, contribution paragraph, limitations and venue choice form one decisionable paper rather than a programme report.

A pass is repeated whenever one reviewer changes a claim, evidence boundary, comparator, contribution, title, or venue route. The loop stops only when all five roles agree that every atomic claim has a warrant and every remaining gap is named rather than hidden.

## 3. Required recursive passes

### Pass A — claim architecture

- Reduce the manuscript to one primary question and one principal answer.
- Create an atomic claim ledger covering title, abstract, results, discussion and conclusion.
- Mark each claim `PROVED`, `EXACT_BOUNDED`, `EMPIRICAL_BOUNDED`, `DESCRIPTIVE`, `CANNOT_CHECK`, `OPEN`, `DONOR_OWNED`, `WITHDRAWN`, or `FORBIDDEN`.
- Remove any implication that a generated denominator is a population sample or that a same-lane replay is external authority.

### Pass B — donor and literature subtraction

- State the strongest donor-complete baseline before describing ORION's residual contribution.
- Search nearest work immediately before arXiv deposit and again immediately before journal submission.
- Prefer primary papers, standards, official repositories and original datasets.
- If an information-equivalent donor product ties, report the tie and redefine the contribution around the residual interface, theorem or diagnostic.

### Pass C — evidence and hostile alternatives

- Audit information parity, resource parity, configuration parity and evaluator parity.
- Retain negative, null, unstable and ungradable outcomes in the main scientific narrative when they delimit the claim.
- Run only experiments that can change a named claim disposition. More seeds, generated cases, or internal replays are not automatically promotion evidence.

### Pass D — manuscript reconstruction

- Title: one bounded scientific contribution, no programme slogan.
- Abstract: problem, gap, method, exact evidence, principal result and principal limitation.
- Introduction: donor frontier first, then the unresolved obstruction and contribution.
- Results: evidence in dependency order, with failed gates adjacent to the claims they limit.
- Discussion: distinguish what is established, what is suggested and what remains open.
- Methods and availability: enough detail for a competent independent group to reproduce the reported object.

### Pass E — arXiv and journal package

- Build an arXiv-safe source package with verified references, figures, licenses and no confidential or deanonymizing material.
- Freeze the preprint claim ledger and exact PDF digest.
- Recheck the selected journal's current scope, article type, word/figure limits, data/code rules and preprint policy on the submission date.
- Produce a journal-specific title, abstract, cover letter, highlights where required, reporting checklist, competing-interests statement and exact submission manifest.

## 5. Computation waiting policy

Long-running computation remains worthwhile when all of the following hold:

1. the protocol and claim-changing terminal are frozen before outcome access;
2. the result can change a named claim status or venue route;
3. donor, information, resource and evaluator parity are specified;
4. null, adverse and `CANNOT_CHECK` terminals are publishable outcomes;
5. the run has durable custody, nonduplication and failure receipts;
6. manuscript promotion waits for an independently checked result packet.

Computation must not delay arXiv deposit of a scientifically complete bounded paper unless the pending result is logically required by its current title or abstract. A successor may continue after the bounded preprint is deposited, but its result should become a clearly versioned revision or a separate paper rather than being retrofitted without provenance.

## 6. arXiv-first release contract

Each paper receives a release candidate containing:

- canonical manuscript source and built PDF;
- abstract and category choice checked by a domain expert;
- complete, verified bibliography with no placeholder citations;
- figure and data licenses;
- claim ledger and evidence manifest frozen to the preprint version;
- code/data/receipt availability statement using public identifiers or a precise release plan;
- author names, affiliations, ORCID identifiers, contributions, funding, acknowledgements and competing interests supplied by the authors;
- visual PDF audit and exact SHA-256 digest;
- a version note distinguishing bounded results from running successors.

The arXiv version should be a real citable scientific paper, not an unfinished teaser for the journal submission. Journal-specific anonymization or formatting is generated from the same content-bound source.
