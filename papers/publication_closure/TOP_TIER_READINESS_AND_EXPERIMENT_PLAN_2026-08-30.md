# ORION top-tier readiness and experiment plan — 2026-08-30

## Decision

The 22 evidence-ready manuscript candidates are **not all top-tier-complete**. They are better described as evidence-bounded papers at different points on two separate axes:

1. **bounded-paper readiness** — whether the strongest currently admissible result can be written and submitted without collecting new science; and
2. **maximum-headline authority** — whether the broad/top-tier successor claim has survived the paper-specific external, hostile, comparator, transfer, and reproducibility gates.

Those axes must not be collapsed. A paper can be scientifically honest and publishable at bounded scope while still needing a decisive experiment for a broader top-tier headline. Conversely, an adverse successor can strengthen the bounded paper by fixing its ceiling rather than making the paper unpublishable.

This record follows the evidence-bounded, claim-ledger-first workflow in `academic-paper-skills`: theorem, exact finite computation, empirical inference, external authority, and manuscript polish are treated as different objects. Manuscript prose does not upgrade scientific authority.

## Four-lens review panel

The portfolio was challenged under four independent review roles.

### 1. Theory and novelty reviewer

Role: test whether the central claim is stronger than donor results, whether negative/counterexample results are retained, and whether a theorem is being confused with an observed regularity.

Primary question: **What is the strongest claim that survives donor subtraction and known counterexamples?**

### 2. Evidence and reproducibility reviewer

Role: inspect receipt binding, checker independence, replay status, rights/custody boundaries, frozen identities, and whether an internal replay is being mislabeled as external reproduction.

Primary question: **Could a hostile reader reconstruct exactly which bytes and which authority justify every quantitative statement?**

### 3. Experiments and inference reviewer

Role: identify the scientific unit, detect pseudoreplication, distinguish conformance from population inference, require non-degenerate comparators, and ask whether another experiment can actually change the paper's claim.

Primary question: **Would the proposed next experiment close a named claim gap, or only add denominator?**

### 4. Top-tier editor / adversarial reviewer

Role: decide whether the paper has a crisp contribution, a decisive comparison against the strongest relevant donor product, a meaningful negative regime, and a result whose scope matches the title and abstract.

Primary question: **Why should a top venue care after the strongest prior mechanism is supplied with the same information and resources?**

## Portfolio conclusion

### Maximum-headline top-tier status

No paper should be called `TOP_TIER_SUCCESSOR_EARNED` merely because it has a polished manuscript. The repository's stricter disposition work recorded zero such promotions on `main`, and later branch work improves several papers without eliminating every paper-specific promotion gate.

The useful operational split is therefore:

- **bounded submission can proceed without new scientific experiments** when the manuscript is narrowed to the earned result and only production/literature/replay work remains;
- **targeted experiment required** when a named falsifier, comparator, transfer or external-authority gate can still change the headline;
- **new successor identity required** when the old broad claim has already failed or cannot be rescued by another analysis of the same outcome;
- **external authorization/custody required** when internal computation is explicitly not allowed to manufacture the missing authority.

## What “more experiments” should mean

The programme should not run more experiments indiscriminately. New work is justified only when it attacks one of five atomic gaps:

1. **Comparator gap** — the strongest donor product has not received the same information/resources.
2. **Transfer gap** — the headline crosses domains/regimes but evidence is still single-family or synthetic.
3. **Hostile-invariance gap** — a representation/format/resource change could explain the result.
4. **External-authority gap** — the missing fact is independent adjudication, legal rights, protected custody, or one-shot authorization rather than compute.
5. **Inference gap** — the scientific unit/cluster structure does not support the population statement suggested by the headline.

Adding seeds, generated rows or internal replays does not close an external-authority gap. Likewise, a larger benchmark does not rescue a claim already falsified under its registered identity.

## Submission rule

A manuscript may be marked `READY_TO_SUBMIT` only when all of the following are true for its chosen bounded headline:

- every abstract/title claim maps to a proved, exact-enumeration, descriptive, empirical, or external-authority ledger row;
- all adverse/null/CANNOT_CHECK results that change interpretation are visible;
- nearest-work and donor subtraction are refreshed immediately before submission;
- no internal or same-researcher replay is described as external reproduction;
- the scientific unit and uncertainty statement match the design;
- figures/tables and journal package are render-current and visually audited;
- reproducibility/availability statements match actual rights and custody;
- the final submission bytes are bound to the claim/evidence manifest.

The objective is therefore **not “25 papers that sound top-tier.”** It is 25 papers whose strongest honest claims are maximally sharpened, plus targeted successor experiments only where those experiments can genuinely earn a stronger result.
