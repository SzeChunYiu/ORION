## 1. Expert group and delegated review roles

Every manuscript decision was examined through five independent roles before synthesis.

1. **Claim-and-evidence auditor** — split title, abstract, headings, results, limitations, availability, and conclusion into atomic claims; checked each claim against the controlling ledger, proof, result, protocol chronology, or external-source boundary.
2. **Formal/statistical reviewer** — checked quantifiers, finite-versus-all-size authority, units of analysis, exact-count semantics, uncertainty language, hidden dependence, null/adverse outcomes, and whether an ideal information-equivalent comparator must tie.
3. **Nearest-work and donor-subtraction editor** — gave established formalisms, stronger baselines, explicit-inference controls, and donor products first right of refusal; removed novelty language where a donor absorbed the apparent contribution.
4. **Journal/desk-risk editor** — tested question, answer, decisive evidence, target fit, readership, routing clarity, and the strongest plausible desk-rejection argument without estimating acceptance probability.
5. **Reproducibility and surface reviewer** — separated scientific prose from repository operations; checked frozen chronology, replay identity, availability boundaries, anonymization risk, figures/tables, and whether current PDFs/packages actually bind the manuscript bytes.

The synthesis step did not count reviewer votes. A single valid blocker controlled the disposition until it was repaired or explicitly preserved.

## 2. Recursive workflow

The portfolio loop was:

`evidence freeze -> atomic claim inventory -> donor/nearest-work subtraction -> formal/statistical red team -> shortest sufficient evidence chain -> natural scholarly prose -> independent editor/reviewer simulation -> claim narrowing/correction -> consistency and surface QA -> repeat`

The loop stopped only when one of two conditions held:

- no remaining claim-safe revision materially improved decision readiness; or
- a real evidence, authority, prospective-outcome, package, or human-metadata blocker remained.

No paper was upgraded because prose became smoother. Negative, null, absorbed, non-identifying, and `CANNOT_CHECK` terminals remain scientific results where they determine the supported paper.

## 6. Cross-paper atomic-gap review

The recursive pass found and enforced these programme-wide invariants:

- **Finite does not become universal.** Exact finite universes are named and held apart from all-size proofs or open-population estimates.
- **Agreement does not become authority.** Multi-instrument agreement, internal independent checkers, and same-programme clean-room implementations are not external validation.
- **A favourable secondary metric does not rescue a failed gate.** ORION-12's nDCG gain, ORION-09's finite separator, and other partial positives retain their registered adverse terminals.
- **Strong donors get first refusal.** Ordered search absorbs ORION-11's success margin; alternative minimal bases refute ORION-20 indispensability; ideal typed products tie ORION-14, 16, and 18; exact closure remains the ORION-17 safety donor.
- **Unknown is not negative.** ORION-23 abstains when premise truth is not identified; unavailable authority in ORION-04 and 18 is not rewritten as refutation.
- **Prospectivity is preserved.** ORION-07 remains frozen; ORION-17 predictions precede outcomes; retrospective corpora are labelled retrospective.
- **Security boundaries are typed.** ORION-25 establishes cryptographic key independence, not organizational independence.
- **Repository artifacts are translated into science.** File names, branch history, CI labels, and commands remain in audits and artifact documentation rather than the paper narrative.
