## 1. Independent editorial panel

Four lenses were applied recursively to every paper.

1. **Claim/evidence auditor** — formal methods and empirical methodology. Checks every headline against the strongest current ledger/disposition; rejects theorem inflation, post-outcome promotion, denominator inflation, and silent replacement of adverse gates.
2. **Nearest-work/venue editor** — journal triage and literature subtraction. Forces donor-owned primitives out of the novelty claim and asks whether the bounded residual fits the selected journal rather than a more prestigious but mismatched venue.
3. **Methods/reproducibility reviewer** — preregistration, statistics, exact verification, provenance, and resource accounting. Separates prospective, retrospective, synthetic, finite exact, theorem, and same-programme replay authority.
4. **Manuscript/desk-risk editor** — title, abstract, first-two-page contribution statement, limitations, figure/story architecture, anonymity, and target-format risk. A concern is considered closed only when the manuscript changes or the limitation is made explicit.

No simulated reviewer verdict creates scientific authority or predicts acceptance.

## 2. Live target-policy checks

Target policies were refreshed on 2026-08-30 rather than inherited from an older package.

- **TMLR** — double-blind; anonymous complete PDF; mandatory TMLR LaTeX style/template; anonymous supplementary materials. The journal emphasizes technically sound learning/intelligent-systems research and supports preprints provided the submission itself preserves double blindness.
- **Quantum** — the submitted preprint must be on or cross-listed with `quant-ph`; no mandatory manuscript format/length; the main results and assumptions should be stated clearly in the first couple of pages; the journal is highly selective and expects a very significant technical or conceptual contribution beyond the state of the art.
- **Artificial Intelligence (AIJ)** — broad AI scope including automated reasoning, knowledge representation, planning, learning, and reasoning under uncertainty; results or new views must demonstrate value/effectiveness. AIJ currently accepts both regular papers and Research Notes.
- **Information Processing & Management (IP&M)** — accepts original research, methods papers, and critical system-design work at the intersection of computing and information science.
- **Semantic Web Journal (SWJ)** — full papers are reviewed for originality, significance, writing, and reproducibility; critical evaluation resources should be provided through a stable accessible archive for review.
- **Journal of Automated Reasoning (JAR)** — current publication activity continues to center formalized and automated reasoning, theorem proving, proof structures, verification, and related logic/solver work. ORION-03's formal evidence-licensing calculus remains a specialist-fit submission rather than a security paper.

Venue fit does not imply acceptance probability.

## 6. Production gates still open after manuscript completion

These are not reasons to invent new scientific experiments, but they must be closed before portal submission.

1. **Integrate final editorial master into the canonical target source.** The new final files are complete editorial masters; existing release PDFs were built from older bytes and cannot be reused as evidence that these versions compile or render correctly.
2. **Bibliography and citation reconciliation.** Existing canonical bibliographies should be retained and re-keyed against the final prose; every novelty/donor sentence must have primary-source support.
3. **Figure/table reconciliation.** Retain only figures/tables that support claims still present; remove panels tied only to retracted/broader successor claims. Verify captions against the final endpoints.
4. **Target-format build and visual audit.** Rebuild PDFs from the final text and inspect equations, tables, references, page breaks, anonymous metadata, hyperlinks, and supplemental-file references.
5. **Claim-to-PDF and byte binding.** Regenerate manifests/checksums only after the final target PDF and supplement are frozen. Old green package digests do not cover the new editorial masters.
6. **Venue-specific surface:** TMLR requires anonymization and its style; Quantum requires an arXiv `quant-ph` posting/cross-list; SWJ requires stable evaluation resources; Elsevier/JAR packages require current author declarations and final article metadata.
7. **Submission-date literature refresh.** Recheck closest donors and venue scope immediately before filing; a new material donor can lower the claim even if the current manuscript text is otherwise final.
8. **Human-only metadata and attestations.** Author order, affiliations, CRediT, funding, conflicts, permissions, concurrent-submission attestations, and any required AI-use disclosure must be supplied truthfully at filing time rather than fabricated by the editorial workflow.
