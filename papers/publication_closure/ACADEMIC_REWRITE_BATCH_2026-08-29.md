## Review panel

Four independent lenses are used before synthesis.

1. **Theory / novelty editor** — background in formal methods, AI reasoning, and theory-heavy computer science. Role: identify the residual contribution after strongest-donor subtraction; reject priority language not supported by the literature boundary; test whether the target journal is plausible for the bounded contribution.
2. **Evidence / reproducibility reviewer** — background in empirical software/AI evaluation, preregistration, provenance, and reproducible research. Role: separate prospective from retrospective evidence, finite validation from proof, internal replication from external validation, and package evidence from scientific evidence.
3. **Claims / statistics reviewer** — background in statistical inference and benchmark methodology. Role: audit denominators, independent units, uncertainty statements, non-inferiority/equivalence language, exact-count studies, donor absorption, null/adverse results, and repeated-loop pseudo-replication.
4. **Journal / manuscript editor** — background in journal triage and technical scientific writing. Role: optimize title/abstract/introduction/evidence order, remove repository-to-manuscript leakage, make assumptions and claim boundaries early, and keep reproducibility details in their proper publication surfaces.

No reviewer vote is treated as acceptance evidence. A concern remains open until the underlying prose or evidence boundary is repaired.

## Pipeline rules applied

- Atomic claims are checked at the smallest falsifiable scope; qualifiers, domains, comparators, quantifiers, and negative results travel with the claim.
- Finite exact checks are never promoted into all-size theorems; theorems are not presented as empirical reliability estimates.
- Synthetic studies remain synthetic; protected/internal campaigns remain bounded; same-programme reconstruction is not called external replication.
- Stronger donors and information-equivalent controls are kept in the main argument when they absorb a broader novelty or superiority claim.
- Failed preregistered gates, counterexamples, non-identifying panels, unavailable routes, and unresolved successor studies remain visible.
- Repository paths, branch/PR history, helper names, and development chronology are translated into scientific abstractions or moved to artifact documentation.
- The V2 rewrite is additive and does not replace the current release master without a separate bibliography/figure/package consistency audit.
