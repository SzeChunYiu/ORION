## 1. Purpose

This programme converts every ORION V1 project that currently has a defensible bounded claim/evidence spine into a reader-facing editorial manuscript master. It also applies a second acceptance-readiness pass to current upload-green papers.

The work is additive. It does not replace frozen evidence, protocols, canonical LaTeX sources, PDFs, journal packages, readiness ledgers or release manifests. A Markdown final master remains an editorial candidate until its exact claims, bibliography, figures, anonymous/named surfaces, target format, archive and PDF are rebuilt and audited against authoritative paper-local records.

## 2. Recursive expert group

Five independent review roles were used for inclusion and manuscript revision.

1. **Claim and formal-authority reviewer** — atomized headline propositions, separated theorem from finite check, found hidden quantifiers, and removed statements lacking a current proof or result.
2. **Empirical and statistical reviewer** — checked comparator capability, independent units, chronology, uncertainty, null/adverse outcomes and preregistered versus secondary analyses.
3. **Nearest-work and venue editor** — gave mature donors first right of refusal, separated adopted primitives from the residual, and tested the residual against target-journal triage.
4. **Reproducibility and artifact auditor** — separated scientific closure from stale PDFs, superseded sources, missing archives, replay defects and unbound external handoffs.
5. **Manuscript and desk-risk editor** — rebuilt title, abstract, narrative order, evidence sequence, limitations and contribution language while removing repository-to-manuscript leakage.

The roles were applied recursively:

`evidence freeze -> atomic claims -> donor subtraction -> manuscript -> hostile review -> claim repair/narrowing -> cohesion/content-selection pass -> acceptance-readiness stress test -> final editorial master`.

An information-equivalent or capability-matched tie never licenses a stronger claim. It transfers the residual from architecture superiority to information, representation, decision boundary or method where warranted.

## 6. Cross-paper scientific corrections

The recursive review imposed the following non-negotiable corrections.

1. **Finite enumeration is not an all-size theorem.** Machine checks corroborate unless a proof or mechanically verified theorem supplies unbounded authority.
2. **Equal budget is not equal capability.** A comparator denied actions the treatment can emit cannot support causal superiority; ORION-22's historical P12A reading remains withheld.
3. **Agreement and replay are typed evidence.** They support execution/reproducibility, not scientific truth or claim authority; ORION-25 makes the separation explicit.
4. **Unreachable gates are non-identifying.** ORION-24 P14A remains historically negative but scientifically `CANNOT_CHECK` because the positive branch was outside the frozen support.
5. **Information-equivalent ties are expected.** ORION-14, ORION-16 and related papers claim portable target relations, not privileged architecture.
6. **Strong donor absorption narrows novelty.** ORION-11's faithful ordered-search and costed Active-VOI comparators remove both comparative headlines; ORION-20's exact alternative basis removes primitive indispensability.
7. **Adverse evidence stays in the main scientific story.** ORION-12's recall/cost gate, ORION-19's wine/Qwen negatives, ORION-21's sparse/pooled attacks, ORION-22's shift failures and ORION-24's benchmark defects are not hidden or relabelled.
8. **Synthetic, finite-contract and same-programme evidence remains bounded.** It supports theorem, mechanism, conformance or benchmark semantics, not automatic transfer to deployed scientific agents.
9. **A zero-execution protocol has no empirical outcome.** ORION-20/21/24 external successors remain acquisition failures, not negative performance results.
10. **A local exact law is not a transferable law.** ORION-09's four-feature separator is exact on the frozen `n<=3` domain and fails on unseen `n=4` states.

## 7. Adoption and release contract

A final editorial master becomes a release master only after all dependent gates are rerun on the exact adopted bytes:

1. paper-local claim-authority review;
2. verified bibliography reconciliation and submission-date literature refresh;
3. figure/table regeneration from bound results and repeated-number/unit audit;
4. exact venue × article type × stage × effective-date resolution;
5. separate anonymous and named/arXiv surfaces where required;
6. exact target-format compilation and page-by-page visual/metadata inspection;
7. claim-to-proof, claim-to-PDF, anonymity/identity, availability, licence, archive, link and byte-manifest audits;
8. immutable binding of source, PDF, code/data, reproduction receipt and archive identifier;
9. human author, affiliation, conflict, funding, ethics and filing metadata;
10. dashboard update only from an immutable current release receipt.

No editorial master predicts acceptance. A primary venue cannot widen a claim. Rejection on breadth should trigger the recorded specialist fallback or article-type change, not scientific inflation.
