## 1. Purpose

This programme converts every ORION V1 project that currently has a defensible, bounded claim/evidence spine into a reader-facing editorial manuscript master. It also applies a second acceptance-readiness pass to the papers whose existing upload-facing packages are already green or close to green.

The programme is deliberately additive. It does not replace frozen evidence, protocols, current LaTeX sources, PDFs, journal packages, readiness ledgers or release manifests. A new Markdown master is an editorial candidate until its claims, bibliography, figures, anonymous/named surfaces, target format and final PDF are rebuilt and audited against the authoritative paper-local records.

## 2. Recursive expert group

Five independent review roles were used for every inclusion and every manuscript.

1. **Claim and formal-authority reviewer** — atomized headline claims, checked theorem versus finite-check authority, found hidden quantifiers and removed propositions that no current proof or result entails.
2. **Empirical and statistical reviewer** — checked comparison capability, independent units, protected/test chronology, uncertainty, null/adverse outcomes and post-hoc versus preregistered analyses.
3. **Nearest-work and venue editor** — gave mature donor fields first right of refusal, separated adopted primitives from the residual, and tested whether the residual can survive an exact target's editorial triage.
4. **Reproducibility and artifact auditor** — distinguished scientific closure from stale PDFs, missing archives, superseded sources, unbound external handoffs, replay defects and incomplete release surfaces.
5. **Manuscript and desk-risk editor** — rebuilt title, abstract, narrative order, claim-evidence-boundary progression, limitations and contribution language while removing repository-to-manuscript leakage.

The roles were applied recursively:

`evidence freeze -> atomic claims -> donor subtraction -> first manuscript -> hostile review -> claim narrowing/repair -> sentence and content-selection pass -> acceptance-readiness stress test -> final editorial master`.

A reviewer tie never licenses a stronger claim. When an information-equivalent or capability-matched comparator ties, the paper retains the representation, information, decision-boundary or methodology result and gives up architecture superiority.

## 4. Cross-paper scientific corrections

The recursive review imposed the following corrections across the portfolio.

1. **Finite enumeration is not an all-size theorem.** Machine checks are corroboration unless a proof or mechanically verified theorem supplies unbounded authority.
2. **Equal budget is not equal capability.** A comparator denied actions the treatment can emit does not support causal superiority; ORION-22's P12A reading remains withheld.
3. **Agreement and replay are typed evidence.** They establish reproducibility/execution facts, not scientific truth or claim authority; ORION-25 makes this separation explicit.
4. **Unreachable gates are non-identifying.** P14A's terminal remains historically negative, but its comparison is `CANNOT_CHECK` because the frozen statistic could not reach either registered bar.
5. **Information-equivalent ties are expected.** ORION-14/16 and related papers claim a target-sufficient relation, not privileged architecture.
6. **Strong donor absorption narrows novelty.** ORION-11's faithful ordered-search comparator and ORION-20's matched-information audit remove earlier superiority readings.
7. **Adverse evidence stays visible.** ORION-12's recall/cost gate, ORION-19's wine null and Qwen scaling negative, ORION-21's sparse/pooled attacks, ORION-22's shift failures and ORION-24's P14A/P14B defects are not buried in appendices or rewritten.
8. **Synthetic and exact-contract evidence remains bounded.** It supports mechanism, conformance, theorem or benchmark semantics, not automatic transfer to deployed scientific agents.

## 5. Adoption and release contract

A final editorial master becomes a release master only after all dependent gates are rerun on the exact adopted bytes:

1. merge scientific content only after paper-local claim-authority review;
2. reconcile every citation with the verified paper bibliography and run a submission-date literature refresh;
3. regenerate figures/tables from bound results, and verify every repeated number and unit;
4. select the exact venue, article type, stage and effective-date instructions;
5. rebuild anonymous and named/arXiv surfaces separately where required;
6. compile the exact target-format PDF and inspect every page, metadata field, link, glyph, table and final-page spill;
7. run claim-to-PDF, anonymity/identity, availability, licence, archive and byte-manifest audits;
8. bind the final source, PDF, code/data release, reproduction receipt and archive identifier;
9. add human author, affiliation, conflict, funding, ethics and filing metadata;
10. update the portfolio dashboard only from the resulting immutable release receipt.

No editorial master predicts acceptance. No primary venue is allowed to widen a claim beyond its evidence. A rejection on breadth should trigger the recorded specialist fallback or article-type adjustment, not a scientific overclaim.
