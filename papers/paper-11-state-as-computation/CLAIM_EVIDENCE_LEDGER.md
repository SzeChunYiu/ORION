## Strongest residual claim

> Query-conditioned state compilation is a computational placement decision: in controlled query families it externalizes structural search from the downstream learner, giving exact combinatorial accessible-rank savings and large finite-sample gains under weak access, while a preregistered sparse universal decoder buys part of that advantage back. The surviving sparse-decoder residual is 2×–4× in sample threshold, and future-query optionality is lost unless raw, cached or universal state is retained.

This is the claim to defend. A reviewer attack that stronger access should reduce the gap is predicted by the paper and empirically observed rather than treated as a contradiction.
