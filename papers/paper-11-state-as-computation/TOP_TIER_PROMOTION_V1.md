## Theory expansion

### T11.1 — General query-family rank/access law

Generalize the exact parity/rank substrate to arbitrary finite linearly independent query families and an approximate low-rank/noisy extension. Explicitly separate:

- semantic information rank;
- representation/access rank;
- decoder/access-mechanism complexity;
- compiler complexity.

No unrestricted nonlinear/time lower bound may be implied.

### T11.2 — No-answer-content-leakage condition

Formalize a sufficient condition of no answer-content leakage — compiled state being informative/access-enhancing without containing or trivially encoding (laundering) the final protected answer — as an information-hiding property of compilation. This is distinct from P8's authority laundering, which is a decision-layer confusion owned by P8 (donor, `papers/SYNC_CONTRACT.md`). Include hostile constructions showing why query identity or latent-feature selection can otherwise leak answer content.

### T11.3 — Optionality law

For a future-query process with horizon/diversity/drift parameters, derive exact or bounded conditions governing when to:

- compile per query;
- compile union-rank state;
- cache compiled state;
- retain raw state;
- materialize a universal state;
- retain reversible raw+summary state.

Current-task efficiency and future optionality must remain separate coordinates.
