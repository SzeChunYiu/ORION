\section{Development recursion and Self-ORION}

ORION development follows the same research discipline as target-domain problem solving. A high-impact mechanic is first atomized, then researched across relevant same-domain and parent-domain knowledge, challenged for false saturation, and implemented only after the development packet records a bounded saturation assessment, omission hypotheses, and reopen triggers.

The intended development trajectory has three regimes. During bootstrap, an LLM-led engineering process constructs the minimum executable kernel. During Shadow Self-ORION, ORION diagnoses and proposes repairs to its own failures while external development remains primary. During Governed Self-ORION, the framework may become the primary problem-solving process for ordinary method development, but promotion still requires protected evaluation and fresh assurance.

A failed run is therefore potentially method evidence. A failure must be preserved, attributed, generalized where justified, and converted into a discriminator or regression guard before it can count as learned method knowledge. Repeated failure without attribution is not automatically evidence for a new operator.
