# Author confirmation checklist

This file is for the author and submission operator; it is not a reviewer-facing attachment.

- [ ] Confirm the complete institutional affiliation.
- [ ] Confirm whether an ORCID identifier will be supplied.
- [ ] Supply the funding statement.
- [ ] Supply the competing-interest statement.
- [ ] Supply acknowledgements, or confirm that none are required.
- [ ] Approve the sole-author contribution statement.
- [ ] Approve the generative-artificial-intelligence disclosure.
- [ ] Supply the public preprint identifier and licence.
- [ ] Supply the permanent review-materials archive URL and reuse licence.
- [ ] Inspect the exact final PDFs and approve the scientific claims and wording.
- [ ] Confirm the primary venue choice and authorize filing.
