## 1. Overall assessment

Its model-specific mathematical claims are explicitly bounded, and the adverse state-preparation results are retained rather than obscured.

## 2. Central claim and evidence readout

The central claim is that an exact quantum-compilation regime map should distinguish logically different objects that a performance footprint can conflate.

The supporting evidence comprises:

- an all-size support-two normalization theorem for the shared-tag Pauli model;
- a tight all-size support-one theorem for the rank-two dependent-triple model, contrasted with a proof-derived ceiling of five;
- an objective-conditioned validity region that is explicitly silent outside its sufficient inequalities;
- an exact pair-gain boundary for the six-term model;
- complete weighted Clifford state-preparation graphs through three qubits and a frozen four-qubit panel showing both representation-level limitations and failed prospective transfer.

These results support the bounded proposition that the coordinates can behave differently in the four declared models. They do not by themselves establish a universal framework, a transferable compiler law or a broadly useful prediction method. The manuscript appropriately disclaims those interpretations.

## 3. Major strengths

1. The manuscript subtracts substantial prior territory rather than inflating novelty. Algorithm selection, instance-space analysis, compiler-option prediction, exact optimization, benchmarking, Clifford synthesis and Pauli compilation are all acknowledged as established.

2. The distinction between intrinsic support and a proof-derived ceiling is scientifically valuable. The rank-two example gives a particularly clear demonstration that a conserved quantity from one proof strategy need not characterize the optimum.

3. The objective-conditioned certificate is interpreted correctly. Silence outside the proved region is not converted into a converse theorem.

4. The negative feature-transfer results are prominent and appropriately calibrated. The near-injective representation, negligible prospective coverage, shuffle-null result and earlier failed forecast are not presented as predictive success.

5. The model-specific evidence classes are kept separate. All-size theorems, complete finite censuses and a frozen prospective panel are not conflated.

6. The manuscript preserves its important non-claims concerning physical runtime, hardware advantage, production transfer, universal compiler structure and compact prediction.

## 4. Major Concerns

### R2-M1 [prior_work_and_positioning, originality]

**Severity** Major

**Blocking** Yes

**Target criterion** Originality and meaningful advance beyond the state of the art

**Claim pointer** The manuscript states that its surviving contribution is a typed combination of constructive witnesses, intrinsic versus proof-derived support, objective-conditioned proof validity, representation impossibility and prospective falsification.

**Evidence pointer** Introduction; Related work and contribution boundary; `resources/LITERATURE_ENTAILMENT_AND_NOVELTY_AUDIT_2026-08-28.md`, especially Nearest-work entailment and Hostile alternative

**Concern** The literature comparison subtracts broad task families effectively, but it does not yet compare the proposed contribution with prior work at the level needed to establish originality. The cited literature is assigned to categories such as instance-space analysis, exact optimization, verified transformations and Pauli compilation. The manuscript does not show, claim by claim, whether the closest existing frameworks already distinguish proof scope, objective-conditioned guarantees, constructive counterexamples or representation sufficiency in comparable forms. The absence of an identical six-part bundle from a bounded author search is not itself evidence that the bundle is an original or non-obvious advance. The author-side audit correctly disclaims being a novelty certificate, and it should not be treated as one.

**Alternative interpretation** The genuinely original contributions may instead be the model-specific normalization and exact-boundary theorems. If so, the regime-map construct is primarily an organizing device, while the manuscript currently positions that device as the surviving contribution.

**Why it matters** *Quantum* requires a meaningful advance. The manuscript must distinguish a new scientific contribution from a careful synthesis of established analytical practices. The current positioning leaves that distinction unresolved even if all model-specific results are correct.

**Resolution test** Identify the closest prior work for each primary claimed advance and compare the mathematical object, assumptions, guarantee and consequence directly. The revised manuscript should select one primary contribution and explain precisely what prior work does not entail. If the defensible novelty is confined to the individual theorems, center those results and present the regime map as reporting context. If the residual is only the combination or reporting discipline, contract the claim and consider a target or article type suited to such a contribution. No additional non-discriminating experiment is required.

### R2-M2 [significance_or_priority, field_advancement]

**Severity** Major

**Blocking** Yes

**Target criterion** Meaningful advance and field significance

**Claim pointer** The manuscript claims that the four models show why the distinctions matter and that these distinctions constrain how exact compiler results should be reported and compared.

**Evidence pointer** Introduction, final two paragraphs; Table 1; Discussion; Conclusion; Limitations

**Concern** The four models establish useful existence examples, but the manuscript does not yet show what field-level conclusion follows from combining them into one regime map. Different models supply different coordinates, and the models are expressly described as heterogeneous examples rather than a representative sample. The resulting record does not currently yield a cross-model theorem, a reusable comparison criterion, a new compiler capability or a transferable analytical prediction. Consequently, the evidence supports a disciplined collection of bounded case studies more clearly than it supports a meaningful general advance in exact quantum compilation.

**Alternative interpretation** The work may be a scientifically useful reporting framework illustrated by four technically substantial examples. That interpretation is coherent, but it may represent a target-fit problem rather than a deficiency repairable by adding more examples or benchmarks.

**Why it matters** Scientific validity and journal-specific significance are separate. Exact and carefully limited results can be sound without yet demonstrating the level of advancement required by the stated target.

**Resolution test** Using the existing results, state the concrete change in understanding or practice produced by the combined record that standard instance-space analysis and separate theorem reporting do not provide. Formalizing the common record and demonstrating one consequential cross-model inference could close this issue if such an inference is already supported. Otherwise, reframe the paper around its strongest exact theorem or exact-boundary result and demote the regime map to organizational context. If neither route produces a meaningful advance for *Quantum*, target transfer is preferable to additional non-discriminating experiments or inflated significance language.

**Originality**

**Fit**

The subject matter fits quantum compilation. The principal vulnerability is not topical scope or an identified validity failure. It is whether the manuscript, in its current combined-framework form, meets *Quantum*’s requirement for a meaningful advance.

The manuscript is scientifically coherent and responsibly bounded, but the present framing does not yet establish its originality and significance for *Quantum*. Major revision would require a discriminating nearest-work comparison and a clearer choice between two contributions. One is a genuinely consequential regime-map framework. The other is a paper centered on the strongest model-specific exact theorem or boundary result.
