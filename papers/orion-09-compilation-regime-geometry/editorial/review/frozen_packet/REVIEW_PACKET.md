# Immutable reviewer packet

Freeze date: 2026-08-28

## Assessment boundary

Review the supplied research article for *Quantum* as original research.  Current official author guidance separates correctness, significance and clarity and expects a meaningful advance beyond the state of the art.  The main results and assumptions should be visible early.  Limitations and reproducibility must be honest.

All numerical and theorem checks supplied here remain within one project and custody chain.  They are not external replication or independent proof review.  The paper makes no physical-runtime, hardware-advantage, production-transfer or universal-compiler claim.

The required arXiv record, permanent archive and licence, affiliation, ORCID disposition, funding, conflicts, acknowledgements, final contribution statement, generative-AI disclosure approval and final author approval are human-controlled filing inputs.  Do not treat their absence as evidence against the mathematical results, but report any effect on reproducibility or filing compliance.

## Common report contract

Use only this frozen packet.  Do not read any editorial triage, other reviewer report, shared concern ledger, editor synthesis or revision plan.  Do not invent reviewer identity, hidden editor knowledge, experiments, citations or manuscript locations.

Use these sections:

1. Overall assessment
2. Central claim and evidence readout
3. Major strengths
4. Major Concerns
5. Minor Comments
6. Blocking technical failings
7. Assessment against *Quantum* criteria
8. Recommendation posture

Every major concern must have a reviewer-local stable ID and include severity, `Blocking Yes` or `Blocking No`, target criterion, claim pointer, evidence pointer, concern, alternative interpretation when relevant, why it matters and a resolution test.  Every minor comment must have a reviewer-local stable ID, evidence pointer and required correction.  Minor comments are never blocking.  Do not manufacture a concern quota.

Allowed recommendation postures are bounded mock-review descriptions, not claims about the real journal's decision.  Avoid numerical acceptance probabilities.
