# Editorial triage simulation before external-review simulation

Date: 2026-08-28

This is a pre-submission simulation based only on the frozen manuscript, Supplementary Information and verified public criteria for *Quantum*.  It is not a prediction of the journal's decision and was completed before any reviewer report was generated.

## Review setup

- **Exact target:** *Quantum*.
- **Article type:** original research article.
- **Publication model:** selective field-advancement journal requiring correctness, significance and clarity.
- **Verified criteria:** correctness; a meaningful advance beyond the state of the art; clear early presentation of main results and assumptions; honest limitations and reproducibility; required filing metadata and declarations.
- **Unresolved target criteria:** the real editor's assessment of whether the typed synthesis is significant enough for the journal; current private editorial priorities are unknowable.
- **Assessment boundary:** all scientific checking is within one project and custody chain.  There is no external proof review, external replay, physical-runtime claim, hardware-advantage claim or production-transfer claim.

## Triage posture

`send_to_review_but_positioning_risk`

## Scope and article-type fit

The article is within quantum compilation and exact circuit synthesis.  The research-article format is appropriate for the two all-size normalizations, the exact six-term boundary and the complete finite-domain state-preparation study.

## Question and contribution clarity

The central question is recoverable from the first page.  The manuscript asks which parts of a compiler regime map are intrinsic, proof-conditioned, representation-dependent or prospectively transferable.  The abstract and early cross-model table disclose both exact results and adverse transfer results.

## Decisive evidence class

The central case combines analytic all-size compositions, exhaustive finite local obligations, complete finite-domain shortest-path calculations, a frozen prospective panel and same-project deterministic verification.  These evidence classes are correctly separated.  The lack of external proof review remains visible.

## Target-specific advancement readout

The strongest field-advancement case is not a new compiler that dominates alternatives.  It is the exact separation of constructive mechanisms, intrinsic support, objective-conditioned certificate validity, representation determination and prospective transfer across four compiler models.  This is coherent and evaluable, but a *Quantum* editor may regard the cross-model synthesis as insufficiently unified or insufficiently consequential.  Stronger adjectives would not close that risk.

## Review-readiness and evaluability

The scientific object is ready for adversarial external assessment.  Assumptions, negative results, same-custody limits and nearest-work subtraction are visible.  The main vulnerability is significance and unification, not a hidden technical claim.  Human-controlled affiliation, declarations, archive, arXiv and approval items block filing but do not prevent the scientific mock review.

## Best next action before filing

Run isolated validity, positioning and reproducibility reviews.  Repair only concerns that change correctness, evaluability or the defensible *Quantum* contribution.  If the residual contribution remains scientifically sound but below *Quantum*'s significance threshold, transfer to *ACM Transactions on Quantum Computing* rather than inflating the claim or adding non-discriminating experiments.
