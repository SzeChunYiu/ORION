## 1. Editor synthesis (post-review; simulated)

The manuscript has a coherent and responsibly bounded scientific purpose. It distinguishes intrinsic compiler structure, proof-conditioned bounds, objective-dependent certificates, finite feature determination and prospective transfer. The preservation of adverse results and the explicit limits on external authority are important strengths.

External review nevertheless changes the dominant assessment. The pre-review triage identified positioning and *Quantum*-specific field advancement as the principal vulnerability and judged the scientific object ready for adversarial review. Reviewers 1 and 3 independently found that the central mathematical claims and prospective analyses are not yet reconstructable from the supplied article, supplement and frozen artifacts. These are technical evaluability blockers, not requests for external replication or generic additional experiments.

Reviewer 2 separately identified two target-specific publication-criteria blockers. The manuscript does not yet demonstrate either the originality of the typed combination relative to the closest work or the field-level consequence required for a meaningful advance at *Quantum*. These concerns do not imply that the bounded mathematical results are invalid. A technically valid revision could still remain mismatched to the target.

The minimum sufficient route is therefore sequential.

1. Establish reader-visible technical validity and auditability.
2. Correct internal inconsistencies and recalibrate the null claim.
3. Determine the defensible primary contribution through direct nearest-work comparison.
4. Reassess *Quantum*-specific significance without stronger adjectives or non-discriminating experiments.
5. Transfer or reposition if the technically sound residual contribution does not meet the target criterion.

## 2. What external review changed relative to triage

The triage posture was `send_to_review_but_positioning_risk`. That assessment correctly anticipated the significance and unification risk later formalized by Reviewer 2.

External review added two more serious findings.

- **The editor underestimated technical evaluability risk.** Reviewers 1 and 3 independently found that the concrete model grammars, theorem hypotheses, six-term condition, finite proof obligations and prospective protocol are insufficiently exposed.
- **The editor overestimated scientific review readiness.** The manuscript is conceptually readable, but several central claims cannot be reconstructed or independently scrutinized from the frozen submission object.
- **The positioning risk remains.** Reviewer 2 did not convert it into a validity criticism. The question is whether the typed synthesis or the strongest model-specific theorem constitutes a meaningful advance for *Quantum*.
- **The repair path does not primarily require new experimental evidence.** Formal specification, correction, protocol disclosure, artifact binding, claim narrowing and possible target transfer are the discriminating routes.
- **Filing incompleteness remains separate.** Missing author metadata, declarations, arXiv registration, archive identifier and licence affect filing readiness. They are not evidence against the mathematical results.

## 3. Consensus strengths

The following strengths were independently identified by at least two reviewers.

1. **Evidence classes are kept distinct.** All-size theorems, finite exhaustive censuses and one frozen panel are not conflated.
2. **Adverse results remain visible.** The failed forecast, negligible transfer coverage, 32 transfer errors, null result and representation limitations are not hidden or converted into success.
3. **Generalization boundaries are explicit.** The manuscript does not claim physical runtime, hardware advantage, production transfer, a universal compiler law or a compact transferable predictor.
4. **Intrinsic support is separated from proof-derived ceilings.** This is a potentially valuable conceptual distinction.
5. **Sufficient and necessary conditions are not generally conflated.** The objective region is presented as a sufficient certificate and is nominally silent outside its proved region.
6. **Same-custody checks are described honestly.** Hashes, fresh same-project replay and a structurally separate verifier are not promoted to external replication or independent proof review.
7. **The negative transfer result is scientifically useful.** Its value depends on supplying the exact frozen rule and protocol, not on replacing it with a favorable experiment.
