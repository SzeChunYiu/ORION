# Venue decision — ORION-09

**Target: _Quantum_.** Not PRX Quantum.

PRX Quantum was allowed as a stretch conditional on the significance gate. The
paper closes that door itself, and correctly. Its third family refutes the very
cross-family motif that would have carried a broad-impact case: no predicate in
the frozen natural feature vocabulary separates donor-exactness on StabPrep, and
mixed feature cells impose an irreducible error floor of 43 over 1,146
instances regardless of predicate budget. The transferable object is therefore a
mapping discipline, not a universal low-order boundary law, and the manuscript
says exactly that. It also carries no physical quantum-advantage claim.

A PRX Quantum submission would require asserting the general law the paper just
refuted.

_Quantum_ is the right venue. It publishes exact, machine-checked, finite-domain
results under stated grammars and objectives, and it does not require the
generality claim this paper declines to make. The negative transfer is a
contribution here rather than a liability.

**Article type.** Regular research article, 6 pages.

**Fallback.** Quantum Science and Technology, if _Quantum_ declines on scope.

## Concurrent-submission note for the filer

Two sibling manuscripts from the same research programme are also routed to
_Quantum_ in this wave: an exact support-normal-form result for shared-Tag TARE
compilation, and a layered certificate/forecasting paper. The three are
genuinely distinct contributions -- an exact theorem, a cross-family mapping
programme with a refutation, and a certificate-hierarchy argument -- and each
stands alone. They nevertheless share a substrate and some evidence artifacts.

Filing all three simultaneously without disclosure invites a reasonable
editorial concern about least-publishable-unit slicing. The filer should either
stagger them or state the relationship in the cover letters. This is an
editorial judgement for a human, not something to resolve silently.
