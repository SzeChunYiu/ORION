# Cover letter — ORION-09

To the Editors, _Quantum_

**Manuscript:** Compilation Regime Geometry: A Receipted Programme for Mapping
Exact Optimization Families
**Article type:** Regular research article

Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript above for consideration in _Quantum_.

Compiler optimization is usually evaluated by the best cost found on a benchmark
set. That endpoint hides the things a compiler writer actually needs to know:
where a simple donor family is already exact, which structural trades buy
anything, how large an exact search family has to be, and whether regime
membership can be predicted from input structure at all. This paper proposes
mapping that geometry directly, and reports what happened across three
optimization families.

Two families behave well. In a rank-2 grammar, an initial support-five theorem
is progressively tightened until the exact intrinsic support number is one, which
illustrates that a first valid bound is not a tight one and that tightening
requires new transformations rather than more confidence. In a second family, a
predicate derived from pairs is upgraded to an all-instance theorem even though
optimal witnesses can require globally large block structure, which separates
certificate arity from witness arity.

The third family is why we are submitting the paper. It refutes the motif the
first two suggest. On StabPrep, no predicate in the frozen natural feature
vocabulary exactly separates donor-exactness, and mixed feature cells impose an
irreducible error floor of 43 over the 1,146-instance exhaustive domain
regardless of predicate budget. Zero error is unachievable at any budget, not
merely unachieved at ours.

We think that negative is the paper's most useful contribution, and it changes
what we are willing to claim. The transferable object is the mapping discipline
itself, not a universal low-order boundary law. Without the third family the
first two would read as evidence for exactly the general claim that the third
family shows to be false, and we would rather publish the refutation with the
transfers than the transfers alone.

All results are machine-checked and exact over finite domains under stated
grammars and objectives. We make no physical quantum-advantage claim.

We believe _Quantum_ is the right venue because it publishes bounded, exactly
verified results and does not require a generality claim that this paper
specifically declines to make.

We note for the editors that two related manuscripts from the same research
programme are being routed to _Quantum_ in the same period. They are distinct
contributions and each stands alone, and we are glad to stagger them or to
clarify the relationship at the editors' preference.

The manuscript is original and is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your consideration.
