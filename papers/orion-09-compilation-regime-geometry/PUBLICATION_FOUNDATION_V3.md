## 03 Argument map V3

### Central tension

Compiler performance regions are already an established object in algorithm selection/ISA, but exact compiler families admit a richer question: **what logically distinct structures determine the region, and which of those structures are invariant, theorem-backed, objective-indexed or representation-dependent?**

### Central thesis

A compilation regime geometry should not be one phase diagram or one learned classifier. It is a typed scientific record containing:

1. exact donor/incumbent region authority;
2. minimal structural trade witnesses;
3. intrinsic and proof-system-dependent search/support bounds;
4. objective-indexed validity regions;
5. boundary representations and their identifiability limits;
6. prospective confirmations/refutations.

The current cases show why all six are needed:

- R6M: intrinsic support ceiling 2 while named trade taxonomy remains refinable;
- R6I: intrinsic support number 1, but a weaker syndrome-rank proof gives 5 until whole-system Tag relocation exposes the tighter structure;
- QG16: the same R6I support-1 certificate has a nontrivial objective cone;
- SixLCU: simple exact feature boundary;
- StabPrep: frozen natural features are insufficient even with unlimited classifier complexity over that vocabulary.

### Strongest alternative explanation

“ORION-09 is merely Instance Space Analysis with quantum examples.”

**Response:** treat ISA as the primary parent, then show the additional exact objects that ISA performance footprints do not by themselves provide: witness-level compiler transformations, theorem versus finite-panel authority, intrinsic versus proof-system support, exact objective cones and mixed-cell representation refutations.

### Counterargument we keep

Three families do not establish a universal taxonomy of compiler regimes. The paper claims a falsifiable template and a set of demonstrated phenomena, not a universal compiler law.
