## Frozen boundary

**The universal motif this programme was testing is refuted, and the refutation is a
result rather than a gap.** StabPrep transfers the five-component mapping programme
and then breaks the universal low-order-boundary motif: no predicate in the frozen
natural feature vocabulary separates donor-exactness exactly (`QG1V2-C6`, transfer
plus exact finite refutation).

QG-15b establishes that this failure is information-theoretic rather than a search
shortfall: within that vocabulary, mixed feature cells impose an irreducible error
floor of **43 in 1,146**, whereas SixLCU is exact with a single literal
(`QG1V2-C7`). A complete finite feature-vocabulary result of that shape is stronger
than an unexplained negative, and it is why the paper can state the limit precisely
instead of hedging it.

So the paper does not claim that every compiler family has a small trade basis, a
low-order predicate, or a support bound inferable from one generic formula.
**StabPrep is the explicit counterexample to that universalization**, named as such.

A cross-lane observation is retained without being promoted: production syndrome
rank can recover a valid finite-support theorem candidate but need not equal
intrinsic support — R6I shows rank 5 against intrinsic support 1 (`QG1V2-C8`).

Four promotions are forbidden by the ledger and are not licensed here: describing
`boundary-is-low-order` as a universal principle; presenting the first finite
support theorem as intrinsically tight; transferring TARE's objective cone or
support theorem to other grammars; and reading exact compilation cost as hardware
quantum advantage.
