  authority contains `NOT_R6`, and the charter and closure verdict grant no
  scientific or novelty authority (QG1-C1, QG1-C31).
- The charter's "no donor discipline owns this as a method" is a bounded
  programme statement, not a hostile-search-backed novelty claim, and must not
  be cited as one.
- THEOREM rows are theorems only on their stated grammar/objective/domain:
  QG1-C3 (R6M grammar, unit-cost objective — objective-scoped per QG1-C14) and
  QG1-C4 (R6I grammar; tightness open per QG1-C10) must not be merged into a
  single "support bounds are universal" claim.
- MACHINE-EVIDENCED equalities and predicates (QG1-C9, C18–C20, C23–C27) must
  not be described as theorems for all n or all inputs; the QG-5 refutation
  (QG1-C28) is the standing proof of why not.

  claim that a real chemistry batch sits in a trade regime may be made.
- The QG-2 O1 chemistry D++ referee is UNRESOLVED (QG1-C15); no O1 chemistry
  sufficiency claim may be made.
- QG-5 library-table rows without committed DP receipts verify nothing
  (QG1-C30) and must not be cited as confirmations.
- Donor-owned machinery (TARE and its enlargements; SixLCU incumbents,
  identical-term collection, hybrid-grouping bookkeeping) must not be described
  as novel.
- The protected stretched-N2 subject is unread in every cited receipt
  (`reserved_stretched_n2_accessed: false` in all five lane receipts) and no
  claim may imply otherwise.
