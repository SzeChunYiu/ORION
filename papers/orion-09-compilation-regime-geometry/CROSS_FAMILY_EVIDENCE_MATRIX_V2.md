## 1. Authority vocabulary

- `PROVEN_ALL_N` — theorem over every `n`/instance in the exact registered family/objective.
- `THEOREM_GRADE_DOMAIN` — exact theorem/exhaustive finite-domain statement on the stated domain.
- `EXACT_COUNTEREXAMPLE` — explicit feasible witness/referee refutes a named claim.
- `MACHINE_EVIDENCED` — exact/frozen finite panels, not universal theorem.
- `PROSPECTIVE_CONFIRMED` — prediction frozen before truth and later matched on named domain.
- `PROSPECTIVE_REFUTED` — prediction frozen before truth and later contradicted.
- `REPRESENTATION_NONIDENTIFIABLE` — same frozen feature vector occurs with opposite exact labels; no classifier over that vocabulary can be exact on that domain.
- `OPEN` — no publication-cut authority closes the statement.

## 4. ISA / algorithm-selection parent comparison

## 5. Non-comparability rule for raw costs

TARE, SixLCU and StabPrep use different frozen structural objectives. Raw cost magnitudes, trade counts, state-space sizes and donor-exact fractions are **not normalized cross-family performance scores**.

Cross-family figures compare:

- which structural questions are answerable;
- what authority class closes them;
- whether the boundary is representable;
- whether prospective predictions confirm/refute.

They must not plot one shared y-axis implying “family A is better than family B.”
