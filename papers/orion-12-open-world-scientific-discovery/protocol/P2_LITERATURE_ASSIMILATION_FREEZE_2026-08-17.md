## Residual after absorption

The narrowed Paper 2 does **not** claim novelty for:

- scientific/deep-research retrieval;
- lexical, dense, hybrid, reasoning-aware or field-aware retrieval;
- query decomposition or metadata augmentation;
- citation/bibliography expansion;
- retrieval/screening stage attribution;
- adaptive retrieval continuation by utility;
- verification/evidence-sufficiency stopping;
- generic question-conditioned memory;
- automated systematic review.

The only candidate residuals retained are:

1. **typed route/task authority under unknown coverage:** a route may be exhausted, budget-stopped, unavailable or censored without thereby licensing global scientific closure; relevance, utility, coverage and sufficiency signals remain evidence/control signals unless a protocol separately grants closure authority;
2. **earned route identity:** route diversity is supported by backend/query/capture provenance rather than a different tool name;
3. **dual content/read-state bookkeeping:** content identity prevents duplicate discovery inflation while question-conditioned processing state permits a legitimate reread for a changed scientific extraction question.

Even these are bounded claims. The current external evidence does not demonstrate general ORION-vs-baseline superiority.
