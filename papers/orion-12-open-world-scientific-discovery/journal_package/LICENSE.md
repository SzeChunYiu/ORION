Repository code is Apache-2.0 under the root `LICENSE`; paper text is CC BY 4.0
under `LICENSE-PAPERS-CC-BY-4.0.txt`. Third-party data and provider terms remain
separate and are not broadened by those licences.

AutoResearchBench data is not bundled. SAGE corpus/evaluator are unavailable and
must not be substituted as official. MetaSyn archives metrics and false-negative
ledgers, not upstream full texts. OpenAIRE/Crossref mirrors retain public captures
and evaluator artifacts without placing evaluator gold in candidate custody. The
offline complete-gold denominator is repository-owned synthetic data.
