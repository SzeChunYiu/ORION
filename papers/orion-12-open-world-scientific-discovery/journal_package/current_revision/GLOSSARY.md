# Glossary

- **Acquisition policy:** rule selecting the next query, route, fetch, reread or route-stop action.
- **Closure authority:** rule deciding what the current evidence and obligation state permits the system to certify.
- **Content identity:** digest-based identity of the retrieved rendition, separate from its locator.
- **Material route:** route whose unresolved evidence is declared decision-relevant by the frozen task contract.
- **Open obligation:** material evidence requirement not discharged or explicitly resolved.
- **Premature task closure:** task-close decision while relevant material remains reachable within the task contract.
- **Question-conditioned read state:** record of the work, content, extraction schema and scientific frame under which material was processed.
- **Route capture:** route kind, backend, query derivation and captured content set.
- **Route stop:** local decision to stop or suspend one route; it does not certify task completeness.
- **Undetermined:** outcome used when material evidence cannot be assessed; it is neither zero nor evidence of absence.
