# Human-only filing inputs

No automation may infer the following:

1. final author order and spelling beyond the supplied author name;
2. institutional affiliation, department, full postal address and country;
3. corresponding-author postal address and phone;
4. ORCID disposition;
5. funding and grant identifiers;
6. conflicts/declaration of interest;
7. acknowledgements;
8. author-approved CRediT roles;
9. final human approval of the AI-assistance declaration;
10. Editorial Manager classifications, suggested reviewers and submission ID;
11. public archive URL/DOI if the journal or author elects to deposit one.

Known fields used here: Sze Chun Yiu; sze-chun.yiu@fysik.su.se. Any byte change to the manuscript or anonymous archive requires a new manifest and affected-claim audit.
