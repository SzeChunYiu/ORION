Please consider the manuscript above for publication in *Information Processing &
Management* as a full-length research article.

The manuscript addresses a control problem in scientific-literature discovery
rather than proposing another general retrieval architecture. Discovery systems
search through heterogeneous routes, and a locally exhausted, unavailable, or
provider-invalid route does not establish that the scientific task is complete.
We separate evidence acquisition from closure authority: route stopping stays
local, structural route distinctness is screened by provenance rather than
labels, content identity is recorded separately from question-conditioned
processing, and unresolved material routes remain explicit open obligations
instead of being converted into evidence of absence. An exact-contract battery
isolates the resulting aggregation rule: the governed controller matches 400 of
400 protected closure labels, an authored comparator that drops unresolved
routes from the closure denominator matches 250 of 400, and an
information-equivalent comparator also matches 400, bounding the claim to the
closure contract rather than to a retrieval advantage.

The central empirical finding is negative, and the paper is built to report it
as the measured phenomenon. In the registered TREC-COVID comparison, the
strongest comparator (BM25) reaches mean recall@100 of 0.110334 against the
candidate's 0.092642, a paired difference of $-0.01769$ with a 95\% percentile
bootstrap interval of $[-0.02729, -0.00906]$, while reads increase by 175.7\%.
The candidate's higher nDCG@10 ($+0.1488$) was defined out of the
noncompensatory gate before outcomes and is retained only as secondary
evidence. Two prospectively frozen BEIR successor estimators leave all five
ArguAna stopping decisions unchanged, and public screening panels favor the
frozen active comparator. The controlled complete-gold index is reported at its
earned authority: its achieved half-width of 0.0496 does not meet the frozen
0.03 superiority margin, the honest statistical unit is the 78-topic cluster
rather than the 390 task variants, and the manuscript carries the plan's
underpowered label in its limitations section.

We believe this is evidence the field needs more of: a system-design paper
whose adverse gates are declared before outcomes, executed, and retained in the
published record. The contribution is the control layer, the exact-contract
isolation method, and a disciplined negative about external retrieval
superiority — not a leaderboard claim.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not
been published previously. Generative-AI tools supported literature searching,

were checked against archived evidence, and the authors remain responsible for
the content. The submitted package uses the anonymized venue wrapper with
