# Glossary

- **BEIR:** Benchmarking IR, a heterogeneous information-retrieval benchmark.
- **BM25:** A probabilistic lexical ranking method used as the strongest
  observed comparator in the descriptive TREC-COVID comparison.
- **Exact-contract battery:** A fixed authored set of cases with declared closure
  labels used to test agreement with the closure contract.
- **nDCG@10:** Normalized discounted cumulative gain at rank 10.
- **Open obligation:** A material route that remains unresolved and therefore
  cannot be removed from the task-closure denominator.
- **TREC-COVID:** The Text REtrieval Conference COVID-19 test collection.
