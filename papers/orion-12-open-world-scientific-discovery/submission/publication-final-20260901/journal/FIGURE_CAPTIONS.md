# Figure captions

**Figure 1.** Acquisition authority as an interval-censored control problem.
Unavailable observations remain possible contributions, so any claim of task
closure requires a finite bound that is at most the declared tolerance.

**Figure 2.** The discovery pipeline separates retrieval, screening, route
stopping, and task closure. Solid arrows carry state updates. The dashed coverage
diagnostic informs search but does not certify closure, and transport failure
leaves an open obligation rather than evidence of absence.

**Figure 3.** Cumulative complete-gold recall against host-recorded query count.
Deterministic repeats are checked for identical traces and collapsed within task.
Curves are descriptive means over 390 task variants.

**Figure 4.** Terminal premature-closure classifications for selected safety
controls. Bars are task counts in the fixed controlled suite; lower-precedence
mechanisms can be masked by the evaluator's terminal-failure ordering.
