# Three-reviewer hostile audit and closure map

Three independent reviews attacked the same bounded filing claim from validity,
novelty, and release perspectives. Their reports were frozen before synthesis.

## Reviewer 1 - validity and inference

The initial blocker was disagreement between the headline evidence and stale
Methods/Results text, together with effective-unit, route-independence, and BEIR
interpretation risks. The filing manuscript now reports the exact-contract,
TREC-COVID, and BEIR studies directly; treats the controlled variants as
dependent and descriptive; calls provenance separation structural distinctness;
and limits BEIR conclusions to decision-inert transformations under the tested
rule. Independent recalculation confirmed the three TREC-COVID headline values.

## Reviewer 2 - novelty and ownership

The initial blocker was an inconsistent contribution boundary. The manuscript
now identifies B1 as an authored comparator assembled from prior mechanisms,
retains the B3 400/400 tie, disclaims ownership of generic retrieval, stopping,
memory, and obligation mechanisms, and positions the residual against directly
adjacent bounded scholarly-search work. Exact-contract performance is described
as declared-contract conformance rather than universal correctness.

## Reviewer 3 - release and evaluability

The initial blocker was route conflation: attributed arXiv files had been reused
for a double-anonymized journal submission. The final package has separate
attributed arXiv and anonymous CAS journal PDFs and sources, a 211-word abstract,
separate title page and declarations, clean source-archive rebuilds, identity
scans, typography checks, and complete-page visual inspection.

## Targeted validity re-review

A targeted re-review found one remaining provenance defect: the TREC-COVID
decision had been called registered even though the repository contains no
separate pre-outcome record binding the exact comparator-selection, margin,
cost, and bootstrap rule. The filing manuscript now labels that comparison
post-hoc and descriptive. The adverse recall and cost results, favorable nDCG
result, all 50 topics, and the no-superiority conclusion are unchanged.

## Post-review synthesis

No optional new experiment is needed for the bounded filing claim. The paper is
submission-ready after author-controlled portal confirmations. Broader claims
would require successor science. Contextual review signals remain disclosed:
some exact-contract and fixed-world quantities require unusually fine decimal
precision; many closest 2026 works are still preprints; and the committed P2-2
PDF cannot be renderer-compared locally because the Matplotlib versions differ,
although its TeX and source-data hash match.
