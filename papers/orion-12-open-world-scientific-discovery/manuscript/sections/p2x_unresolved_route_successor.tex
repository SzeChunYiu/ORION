\subsection{Exact-contract closure test}
\label{sec:p2x}

The governed controller matched all 400 declared closure labels in the
exact-contract battery. B1, the authored comparator composed of prior mechanisms,
matched 250 labels, while B2 matched 100. The 150 B1 disagreements were exactly
the unavailable, censored, and provider-invalid material-route cases. B1 passed
every other archetype. The governed controller produced no false task closure
and did not confuse the held-out unavailable non-material decoys with material
obligations.

The governed-minus-B1 difference in exact scientific closure decision was
$+0.375$. Its domain-stratified bootstrap 95\% interval was
$[0.3275,0.4225]$, and the exact McNemar discordance counts were $b=150$ and
$c=0$. These statistics describe the fixed authored battery; they are not
population uncertainty or evidence of real-world retrieval performance.

B3, the information-equivalent comparator, also matched 400 of 400 labels and
was decision-identical to the governed controller. This tie is load-bearing. It
shows that the unresolved-route rule is portable to an enriched product and
rules out an inherent-expressivity or centralized-ownership claim. The supported
residual is narrower: when a declared material route is unavailable, censored,
or provider-invalid, excluding it from the task-closure denominator violates
the declared contract.
