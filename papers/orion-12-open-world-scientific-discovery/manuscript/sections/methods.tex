\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

This section reports the designs that generated the controlled-index,
exact-contract, TREC-COVID, and BEIR evidence. Post-outcome manuscript edits do
not alter their systems, tasks, endpoints, margins, budgets, exclusions, or
decision rules. The versioned design, execution, and result records govern when
their status differs from historical prose. Section~\ref{sec:availability}
describes the archived evidence and its access conditions.

\subsection{Three-valued outcomes}
Every decision this paper measures is three-valued rather than binary.  A task
may be determined complete, determined incomplete, or left \emph{undetermined};
a route may be determined exhausted, determined to have more to give, or left
undetermined.  ``We could not determine this'' is recorded, reported and counted
separately from ``we determined this is false'', and the two are never merged.
The distinction is the point: a provider outage, a missing credential or a
censored route produces an undetermined outcome, and an undetermined outcome is
not evidence of absence.  Throughout the paper an outcome described as
\emph{undetermined} is this third value, carried in the evidence record and in
every downstream count, never silently resolved to a negative.

\subsection{Prospective discipline}
The prospective protocol \texttt{P2.open-world-discovery.v1} is frozen and
versioned before execution.  A result-bearing
execution is admissible only after subject code, data, evaluator, system
identities, budgets and repeat seeds are bound.  For the completed offline
companion, the run manifest records that no outcome was accessed before the
freeze, together with the exact subject revision, content hashes,
\OfflineSystemCount{} system identifiers, three repeat seeds and host-owned
resource limits.  The bound subject revision passed the full test suite before
the first aggregate outcome snapshot was recorded.  Publication summaries and
manuscript prose may be generated after outcomes, but they may only project the
frozen records; they cannot change the scientific decision rules.

\subsection{Evaluation environments}
Three environments are distinguished because no single one supplies both a
complete denominator and open-world realism.  The first supplies the only full
system comparison with a complete denominator; later bounded external probes
have narrower acquisition or screening authority and are reported separately.

\paragraph{Offline complete-gold controlled index.}
Recall is only measurable where the relevant set can be enumerated.  The
controlled index is therefore fully synthetic and self-generated.  Each topic
has a materialized gold set under the frozen generator, and tests recompute the
world and tasks from the recorded seed and compare all committed hashes rather
than trusting a hand-authored denominator.  Discoverability is heterogeneous:
relevant items are partitioned across lexical, semantic, citation,
reformulation and restricted/provider routes; two route labels deliberately
share a backend; duplicate locators share content identity; revisions share
identity but change digest; extraction-question-shift cases require legitimate
rereads; and unavailable-route cases distinguish transport censoring from
absence.  Budgets are below exhaustive probing, so route allocation and stopping
remain observable variables.

The completed suite has \OfflineTaskCount{} tasks over five case families, drawn from \OfflineTopicCount{} topics over \OfflineDocumentCount{} documents, frozen at suite fingerprint \texttt{\OfflineSuiteFingerprintShort}.  Every candidate uses
the same host-owned session.  The session alone can access the world, meters
queries and reads, records route stops and transport states, and evaluates claims
after the candidate returns.  The candidate receives only a public task view and
label-free retrieved records; protected gold and the relevance rule are never
passed through the system interface.

The five task variants generated from each topic share the same synthetic world
and question. We therefore treat the topic, not the variant, as the independent
cluster. The \OfflineTaskCount{} variant-level outcomes are descriptive mechanism
diagnostics; deterministic repeats test execution stability and do not increase
the number of independent units.

\paragraph{Exact-contract battery.}
The exact-contract battery isolates the task-closure rule from retrieval
quality. It spans five acquisition families: scientific literature, web and API
evidence, dataset registries, code and artifact repositories, and experimental
or measurement routes. Each family instantiates eight hostile archetypes,
including an available route that remains open; an unavailable, censored, or
provider-invalid material route; duplicate coverage; acquired but not
question-processed content; and low-utility routes that remain material. Route
order is seed-permuted, and held-out variants add unavailable non-material
decoys so that a blanket refusal to close cannot pass.

The primary comparator, B1, is an authored composite of prior mechanisms rather
than a published product. It receives route identity and materiality, evidence
coverage, decision sufficiency, utility stopping, finite route certificates,
deduplication, route state, and question-conditioned processing state. It makes
the same route-local decisions as the governed controller. The sole systematic
difference is task-level aggregation: B1 excludes unavailable, censored, or
provider-invalid material routes from the closure denominator once all
available material routes have stopped. B2 allows a global sufficiency signal
to authorize closure. B3 receives the same unresolved-route semantics as the
governed controller and tests whether the rule is portable to an
information-equivalent product. The protected unit is the authored case. Exact
scientific closure decision is agreement with the declared case label.

\paragraph{Descriptive TREC-COVID comparison.}
The external comparison uses the 50 official Round~5 TREC-COVID topics and
judgments \cite{voorhees2020treccovid}. The governed multi-route arm and three
comparators share the frozen corpus, topics, maximum of 300 distinct reads, six
route calls, and 400 tool calls. Citation and restricted-provider routes are
unavailable in this collection and remain recorded rather than being treated as
empty successful routes. No separate pre-outcome record was found that binds the
comparator-selection rule, the $-0.02$ non-inferiority margin, the 25\% read
reduction criterion, or the bootstrap procedure. We therefore treat the
completed four-arm comparison as post hoc and descriptive. The result artifact
selects the strongest observed comparator, BM25, and applies those recall, cost,
and safety reference criteria. Paired topic differences are summarized with a
10,000-resample percentile bootstrap interval. nDCG@10 is reported alongside
them but does not establish an overall superiority claim.

\paragraph{BEIR mechanism diagnostics.}
Three prospectively separated studies use SciFact, NFCorpus, and ArguAna with
fixed corpus hashes, held-out query halves, and depths 10, 20, 50, 100, and 200.
Five retrieval functions over each local corpus create route heterogeneity.
The original route-aware rule compares an estimated unread-route contribution
with an absolute threshold. The first successor changes only the threshold scale
through development-split relevance-density normalization. The second restores
the original threshold and changes only the estimator by scaling its global
marginal with each held-out query's unseen-route fraction. Its falsifier was
predeclared: if all five ArguAna decisions matched the original rule, the
query-conditional estimator would be recorded as decision-inert and no fourth
estimator would be introduced under the same study identity.

\paragraph{Other external benchmark tasks.}
AutoResearchBench provides Wide and Deep scientific-discovery tasks
\cite{autoresearchbench2026}. Its completed probes remain bounded acquisition or
transport diagnostics, not matched evidence for the full controller.

SAGE is retained as neighbouring work and as a lexical-baseline warning
\cite{sage2026}, but is not executed as if its published setting were available:
the paper-linked repository is data-only at the audited revision and provides
neither an official runner nor a licence. Any substitute corpus, runner or
weighting rule would be a declared deviation rather than the SAGE system.
MetaSyn's separation of retrieval from screening is adopted as a measurement
constraint \cite{metasyn2026}; its credential-free BM25 plus public-protocol
screening probe subsequently completed on all 86 released test reviews and is
reported as a bounded retrieval/screening result, not as the complete proposed
controller.
AgentSLR remains a protocol-driven systematic-review neighbor/baseline family
rather than an official evaluator dependency \cite{agentslr2026}.

\paragraph{Live-provider campaigns.}
The OpenAIRE and Crossref campaigns freeze gold-blind candidate pools and retain
request, query-derivation, timestamp, raw-response, and typed transport records.
Their provider-validity gates failed, so their numerical ties do not support a
null, equivalence, or superiority conclusion. A separate fresh 48-task campaign
was not accessed and contributes no outcome.

\subsection{Systems under test}
The implementation evaluated here is the ORION discovery layer; it is the
artifact under test, and the rest of the paper calls it the \emph{governed
system} so that the claims read as claims about the mechanism rather than about
a product.  The frozen offline companion compares the governed system with six
confirmatory comparators: a BM25 keyword retriever, a dense retriever, a
sparse--dense hybrid, a one-pass retrieval-augmented pipeline, a single-route
agentic searcher, and a protocol-driven systematic-review comparator.  A seventh
comparator, an adaptive multi-route exploratory searcher, was kept explicitly
exploratory and was not retrofitted into the frozen confirmatory baseline set.
The registry that binds each of these to its frozen system identifier is
archived with the campaign.

These controlled-index systems are mechanism comparators, not claims to
replicate a production BM25 engine, a particular embedding model or an external
agent implementation.  They consume the public synthetic route interface under
identical budgets so that the variable under test is route/read/stopping
governance.  Their relevance screen sees only the public question and retrieved
bibliographic text.  The authored synthetic text makes screening deliberately
easy; the experiment is not presented as evidence about classifier quality.

Six frozen ablations remove exactly one governance mechanism or authorize one
forbidden transition: route-independence checking, the question coordinate in
the read ledger, route/task stop separation, unavailable-route open state,
coverage-diagnostic non-authority, and content-identity deduplication.  All use
the same session and budgets as the governed system.

\subsection{Identity, reads and stopping}
A read is keyed by work identity, content digest, extraction-schema version,
and scientific question. A new question or schema can therefore reopen
unchanged content for a legitimate reread, while mirrors with the same work and
digest do not become new evidence. The corresponding ablations remove the
question coordinate or replace content identity with document locator to expose
suppression or duplicate work.

Route stop and task closure are separate actions.  A route may stop after its
public probe space is exhausted or after a typed transport outcome, but task
closure is invalid if relevant material remains reachable within budget.  An
unavailable route stays censored and open rather than being converted to an
empty result.  Coverage diagnostics may inform a route policy but have no
authority to certify global completeness.

\subsection{Measurements}
The offline evaluator reports denominator-valid complete-gold recall, precision,
missed-gold count, route use, route/content overlap diagnostics, marginal
relevant gain after the first route, premature task closure, censoring,
duplicate processing and legitimate rereads, plus host-owned resource counters.
Claims are normalized by content identity rather than locator so mirrors cannot
inflate recall.  Rich per-run artifacts retain route calls, reads, stop events,
authority flags and raw-artifact hashes; the publication summary binds the full
\OfflineRunRecordCount{}-record and rich-artifact sets by SHA-256.

The external measurement plan additionally defines official Wide/Deep scores,
screening metrics and ranking-only metrics where their denominators and task
semantics support them.  Those definitions do not imply that an external result
exists.

\subsection{Statistics and authority}
The controlled-index outcomes are reported descriptively because the five
within-topic variants are dependent and the study does not establish a
population-sampling model. Deterministic repeats nest within topic, task, and
system and test only stable execution. The 400 exact-contract cases are also a
fixed authored battery. Their stratified bootstrap interval and McNemar count
describe that battery and are not population uncertainty. TREC-COVID uses the
topic as the paired unit under its archived external gate. The BEIR studies use
held-out queries for their fixed-corpus mechanism decisions. These authorities
are not pooled or averaged.

\subsection{Failures, exclusions and contamination}
Candidate failures, malformed outputs, budget exhaustion, abstention and typed
transport failures remain outcomes.  Provider unavailability cannot become
evidence of absence.  The frozen evaluator uses an explicit failure taxonomy and
terminal precedence; the publication table therefore warns that a lower-priority
mechanism can be masked by a higher-priority terminal classification such as
premature closure.  Public benchmark questions and gold identifiers also create
a contamination risk for future live search.  A credential-free search-time
self-exposure diagnostic over the frozen 400-task AutoResearchBench Wide
candidate run is archived with the external results and reports a task rate of
0.0.  That diagnostic does not claim absence of indirect, training-time, or
live-provider contamination.

\subsection{Decision rule}
The paper-level rule is fail-closed. The offline companion can support a
mechanism claim only at descriptive authority. External discovery superiority
requires a denominator-valid external result that passes its archived
matched-resource and safety rule. The TREC-COVID result fails that gate, the
BEIR successors remain negative mechanism diagnostics, and provider-invalid or
unaccessed campaigns remain undetermined. None is promoted by analogy from the
controlled world.
