\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Authority of the evidence}
The evidence families answer different questions and are not pooled. The
complete-gold index supplies descriptive mechanism diagnostics. The
exact-contract battery tests agreement with authored closure labels. The
TREC-COVID comparison supplies a descriptive external stress test and is
adverse on recall and cost. The BEIR successors localize a stopping-rule failure
without rescuing it.
Provider-invalid or unaccessed campaigns remain undetermined.

\subsection{Descriptive TREC-COVID comparison is adverse}
Across 50 paired TREC-COVID topics, the governed controller reaches mean
recall@100 of 0.092642, compared with 0.110334 for the strongest comparator,
BM25. The paired difference is $-0.01769$, with a 95\% percentile-bootstrap
interval of $[-0.02729,-0.00906]$. Its lower bound extends below the post-hoc
$-0.02$ non-inferiority reference margin. Mean reads rise from 85.52 to 235.8,
an increase of 175.7\%, rather than a 25\% reduction. Recall and cost therefore
both move against the proposed superiority interpretation.

The governed controller has higher nDCG@10, 0.566746 compared with 0.417992,
for a paired difference of $+0.1488$. This favorable head-ranking result and
the adverse recall and cost results describe a metric-dependent trade-off; the
former does not establish overall superiority. Citation and restricted-provider
routes are unavailable in the collection, so the controller also declines to
declare task completeness on all 50 topics. The paper therefore makes no
external retrieval-superiority claim.

\subsection{BEIR successors retain the mechanism failure}
The original BEIR route-aware stopping rule fails all three registered
conditions. Its worst recall deficit from fusion is 0.101, its worst
false-complete-rate excess over generic active stopping is 0.084, and it is
cheaper than fusion at matched recall in only 5 of 15 corpus-depth cells.
Favorable nDCG@10 values remain secondary and do not alter that terminal.

Density normalization leaves every ArguAna and SciFact decision identical to
the original rule and changes NFCorpus without passing the full gate. All 15
cells still fail at least one condition. The subsequent query-conditional
estimator again leaves all five ArguAna decisions and all five SciFact decisions
unchanged. NFCorpus changes slightly but remains far from fusion at the deepest
setting. The predeclared ArguAna falsifier therefore fires. Under the tested
rule, both registered transformations are decision-inert on ArguAna. This is
consistent with a rank-overlap marginal that supplies no usable decision signal
there, but it does not establish that every alternative statistic would be
uninformative. The no-rescue rule forbids a fourth estimator under the same
study identity.

\subsection{Controlled-index comparison}
The governed system attains mean complete-gold recall 0.979487 and precision
1.0. It passes 260 of \OfflineTaskCount{} tasks, reaches honest budget exhaustion
on 59, and leaves 71 undetermined because an unavailable route still holds
unretrieved relevant material. It never certifies a censored task complete, so
its premature-task-closure rate is zero. The strongest frozen comparator has
mean recall 0.666667 and claims completion on all \OfflineTaskCount{} tasks while
reachable gold remains. The descriptive recall difference is therefore
$+0.31282$, with a premature-closure difference of $-1.0$. These quantities
describe the authored controlled world rather than real-web performance.

\subsection{Mechanism ablations}
The safety ablations fail in the intended directions. Removing the
structural-distinctness check reduces mean recall to 0.444444 and produces
premature closure on every task. Allowing route stop to certify task closure
yields recall 0.333333 with the same 100\% premature-closure rate. Giving a
coverage diagnostic closure authority yields recall 0.555556 and again closes
every task prematurely. These negative controls establish that the forbidden
transitions are observable.

Removing content-identity deduplication raises mean duplicate processing from
zero to 0.31453. It exhausts the read budget on 193 tasks, lowers pass rate from
0.666667 to 0.369231, and lowers mean recall to 0.939031. Removing the question
coordinate leaves aggregate recall unchanged at 0.979487 but reduces legitimate
rereads on extraction-question-shift cases from 3.666667 to 1.0. Removing the
unavailable-route open state also leaves aggregate recall unchanged, but turns
71 censored undetermined cases into false completion claims. These null-on-recall
effects are retained because they isolate semantic safety rather than ranking
gain.

The gold-defined route-stop replay records 13 false-positive route stops among
1,950 events. None becomes a task-level false closure because the material
obligation remains open. Among 1,878 task-routes that reach oracle exhaustion,
the evaluator observes zero route-stop false negatives. This null is reported
without being generalized beyond the controlled world.

\subsection{Bounded external probes and retained adverse results}
The MetaSyn probe completes all 86 released test reviews under the official
identifier-only evaluator. Macro retrieval recall is 0.7485 and final inclusion
recall is 0.5651. Among 1,677 reference links, retrieval misses 598; screening
then removes 343 of the 1,079 retrieved links, leaving 941 final false negatives.
This localizes loss across acquisition and screening but does not exercise the
complete controller.

A bounded AutoResearchBench Deep probe produces no official title matches on
600 tasks after a 9-of-9 judge control passes. A judge-free analysis likewise
finds zero exact and substring recoveries among 10,850 candidate titles, with
only eight half-token-overlap recoveries. The result is an acquisition-stage
failure diagnostic, not a matched comparison. The SAGE setting remains
unavailable as an executable arm because its audited release lacks the required
runner, corpus, and licence combination.

The OpenAIRE and Crossref matched campaigns do not yield a valid null. One
campaign records provider success of 0.666667 and another 0.715833, both below
the frozen 0.90 validity gate. Their zero or near-zero paired differences are
therefore transport-invalid and remain undetermined; they do not establish
equivalence. A separate fresh 48-task campaign was not accessed and contributes
no result.

Public screening diagnostics are also retained at their actual scope. In a
five-review panel, the candidate trails the frozen active comparator on recall
at 10\% screened and work saved at 95\% recall in all five reviews. A later
14-review public-development panel fails six locked gates, and a cross-fitted
residual envelope harms both held-out reviews on which it activates. Subsequent
source and identity audits stop before performance when their bindings fail or
remain incomplete. These outcomes concern screening within already acquired
pools; they neither support nor refute open-world acquisition superiority.

No external discovery-superiority claim is supported. The controlled-index and
exact-contract results support a bounded authority mechanism; TREC-COVID and
BEIR preserve adverse evidence; provider-invalid and unaccessed campaigns remain
undetermined. Favorable secondary metrics never compensate for a failed primary
gate.
