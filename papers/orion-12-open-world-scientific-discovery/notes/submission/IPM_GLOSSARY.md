# IP&M glossary — Paper 2

- **Candidate:** a retrieved item before eligibility/screening decisions are complete.
- **Capture identity:** the content/source identity used to decide whether retrieval events correspond to the same underlying item.
- **Censored route:** a route whose unresolved evidence obligations cannot be evaluated because access, transport, credential, corpus or evaluator authority is unavailable.
- **Complete-gold world:** a controlled retrieval world in which the full set of relevant items is known to the evaluator.
- **Content identity:** the identity used to prevent duplicate discovery from being counted as new evidence.
- **Earned route independence:** route independence supported by backend, query-derivation and capture provenance rather than by different route/tool labels alone.
- **Open obligation:** an unresolved route/task requirement that prevents a fail-closed protocol from asserting global scientific completeness.
- **Question-conditioned read state:** processing state recording that a content identity has been examined for a specific scientific question/extraction purpose; it is distinct from content identity itself.
- **Route stop:** a local decision to stop, switch, reformulate, budget-stop or suspend one retrieval route.
- **Task closure:** the global decision that the scientific-discovery task may terminate with its current evidence state.
- **`CANNOT_CHECK`:** a typed terminal used when required authority/evidence is unavailable; it is not a scientific zero and is not evidence of absence.
- **Fail-closed coverage:** a control rule under which missing/unavailable authority or unresolved route obligations cannot silently become a positive completeness claim.
