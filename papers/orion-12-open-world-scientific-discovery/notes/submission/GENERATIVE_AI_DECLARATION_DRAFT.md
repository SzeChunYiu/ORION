# Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in manuscript preparation — draft

**Author approval required before submission.** The current IP&M Guide for Authors requires disclosure of generative-AI use in manuscript preparation and places the declaration before the references.

Proposed disclosure:

> During the preparation of this work, the authors used OpenAI ChatGPT to assist with literature-search support, source checking, code and reproducibility review, manuscript editing, and submission-package preparation. The tool was not treated as an author and did not replace human scientific judgment. Before submission, the authors reviewed and edited the resulting material, verified cited sources and result-bearing claims against the archived evidence, and take full responsibility for the content of the article.

Do not promote this draft into the submitted manuscript until the human authors have actually completed the stated review/verification and approve the wording.
