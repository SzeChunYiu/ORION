## 3. Contradictory and limiting literature retained

- General integer-program sparsity limits any claim that Paper B invents sparse optimal support.
- Recent global-BSF Hamiltonian-compilation work limits any claim that Paper A uniquely recognizes global symplectic structure.
- Markov-basis and Möbius literature means Paper C's trade minimality must be phrased as a compiler consequence, not new algebraic statistics.
- Truth maintenance, Datalog provenance, deletion provenance, and rollback literature mean Paper D's fixed-point/retraction machinery is a scientific-authority specialization, not a new general provenance theory.
- Freeze–Schmid already owns the generalized-Davenport recurrence used by the non-quantum tail theorem.

## 4. Current venue decision profiles

### PRX Quantum — stretch for A/B, conditional for C
Current official acceptance criteria require exceptionality via an exceptional advance, connection, capability, or insight. A correct narrow theorem is not enough. The present A/B drafts therefore treat PRX Quantum as a stretch only if external experts judge the proof-language/intrinsic-support connection to have broad and lasting QIST consequences.

### Quantum — primary high-selectivity target for A/B and possible C
Current official criteria require a very significant technical or conceptual contribution that significantly advances its subfield, plus correctness, clarity, reproducibility, and honest limitations. Broad interdisciplinary readership is explicitly not required. This makes Quantum the more defensible primary target for exact narrow-but-deep quantum-compilation theory.

### JCTA / Combinatorica — non-quantum stretch
JCTA's current guide states that accepted work should solve or make a significant step on an important open problem, develop a new proof technique, or otherwise substantially advance combinatorics. The current non-quantum manuscript does not yet clear that bar while exact `D_4(C_5^3)` remains open; polishing cannot repair this gate.

### Formal-reasoning venues for D
Paper D is evaluated as a formal reasoning/provenance paper, not as a quantum-journal paper. Journal of Automated Reasoning / ACM TOCL-style field venues are the natural primary family. A broader AI venue would require a stronger independently useful general system/evaluation than the current two compiler case studies.
