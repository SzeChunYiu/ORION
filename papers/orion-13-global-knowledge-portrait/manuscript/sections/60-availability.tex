\section{Data availability}
\label{sec:availability}

The repository snapshot contains the frozen public-reference datasets,
successor evidence and content bindings used by this study. Every
artifact named anywhere in this paper is listed here with its path, so that no
repository location has to be carried in the narrative. Paths below are
relative to the paper directory unless they begin with \idt{src/} or
\idt{research/}.

\subsection{Preregistration}

The end-to-end study was preregistered and frozen before any outcome was
accessed, under the identifier \texttt{P3.cross-domain-atlas.v1}. Its
machine-readable freeze is \idt{protocol/PROTOCOL_V1.json}, with the

\idt{protocol/ANNOTATION_SCHEMA_V1.json},
\idt{protocol/ANNOTATION_HANDBOOK_V1.md} and
\idt{protocol/ADJUDICATION_POLICY_V1.md}. That expert-gold design has not been
executed; it is preserved so that a reader can see what was planned, and it
supplies no evidence for anything reported here.

The executed mapping study has its own preregistration chain. The design
freezes are \idt{protocol/PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL_V1.json} and
\idt{protocol/PUBLIC_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL_V1_1.json}, and the execution freeze
for the confirmatory holdout is
\idt{protocol/PUBLIC_REFERENCE_CONFIRMATORY_EXECUTION_V1.json}, registered
under the identifier \texttt{P3.public-reference-mapping.v1.1-confirmatory}. A
reader checking whether a reported analysis was planned or chosen after the
fact should compare against that freeze; it is the reason every number in
Section~\ref{sec:results} can be traced to a decision made in advance of
seeing it. The successor battery is frozen separately under
\idt{research/claim_expansion/p3/}, whose protocol, protected freeze,
outcome-access receipt and result are \idt{P3_X_PROTOCOL_V1.md},
\idt{P3_X_PROTECTED_FREEZE_V1.json}, \idt{P3_X_OUTCOME_ACCESS_RECEIPT_V1.md}

\section{Code availability}

Central custom code for the mapping calculus, public-reference builders,
evaluators and publication projection is present in this repository snapshot
at the paths listed above, with executable instructions in \idt{REPRODUCE.md}.
The current package does not yet bind a public archive URL, archive DOI,
immutable submission commit or verified repository-level licence. A submission
must therefore give editors and reviewers access to the exact snapshot and bind
those four release coordinates before external review; this manuscript does
not replace them with an unqualified availability-on-request statement.

\subsection{Reproduction}

Two make targets regenerate the reported analysis and the publication
artifacts from committed inputs: \texttt{make paper03-public-reference}
produces the headline analysis, and \texttt{make
paper03-public-reference-publication} produces the publication figures and
tables. Both are deterministic, and continuous integration re-runs the second
on a clean checkout and requires the regenerated bytes to be identical to the
committed ones. The short reproduction route is \idt{REPRODUCE.md}.

Reproduction establishes only what the frozen artifacts establish. The
unexecuted raw-text expert atlas, external retrieval and schema baselines,
generated-portrait recoverability and downstream answer quality have no
artifacts here, and their outcomes remain undetermined rather than absent by
oversight.
