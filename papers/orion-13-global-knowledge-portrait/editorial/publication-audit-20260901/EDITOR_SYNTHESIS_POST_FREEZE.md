## Major repairable concerns

R2-MC-03 asks for a short closest-prior-work comparison that identifies the exact incremental contribution. This is non-blocking and should be satisfied by claim calibration, not by new benchmark work or a widened novelty claim.

## Claim-recalibration opportunities

R1-04 notes that the current wording can be read as assigning separate causal credit to retained polarity and the non-merge terminal even though the reported interventions are bundled. The safest statement is that the complete polarity-sensitive non-merge condition prevented the six observed errors. This retains the manuscript's existing disclaimer that the panel does not establish the independent value of every coordinate.

The reports request stable comparator naming and explicit definitions of false merge, false split, abstention, scoring denominators and the treatment of abstentions. These are compact reporting repairs. They must not be used to hide or reclassify the null results.
