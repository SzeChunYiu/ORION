# ORION-13 anti-confounded ontology successor — frozen question

Identity: `ORION13.ONTOLOGY_ANTI_CONFOUNDING.v1`.

When polarity/surface match is deliberately held constant, do the richer semantic coordinates become necessary or useful for correct mapping decisions on external ontologies with machine-readable identity and relation gold?

The current corpus's unique `{polarity}` reduct is retained as a diagnosis of confounding, not promoted as a universal law. This successor is designed to break that confound prospectively.
