# ORION-13 bounded publication authority

**Status:** `RECLASSIFIED_AS_BRIEF_REPORT`
**Decision date:** 2026-09-01

## Current publication object

ORION-13 is a Brief Report about one bounded mapping observation and one
deterministic conformance battery. It is not a full scientific-integration or
knowledge-graph systems paper.

On a prospectively frozen, case-identifier-disjoint 32-case holdout of
already-structured scientific projections, coordinate-governed mapping made
zero false merges while the registered flat-canonicalization rule made six
(`0/32` versus `6/32`; paired difference `-0.1875`; fixed-panel 95% bootstrap
diagnostic `[-0.34375,-0.0625]`). All six discordances were polarity contrasts
within one authored family. The false-split difference from the registered
conservative rule was `0 [0,0]`.

A separate deterministic battery contains 400 generated records but only eight
unique candidate-visible decision states. The complete relation scores
`400/400`, an authored structured-semantic comparator with a narrower terminal
interface scores `250/400`, canonical matching scores `50/400`, and an
information-equivalent typed implementation ties at `400/400`. These counts
establish conformance to the registered interfaces; they are not 400
independent scientific observations or evidence of general superiority.

## Why the earlier full-paper disposition is superseded

The current paper-existence and scientific-mass gate identified three
load-bearing limits:

1. the holdout has three authored families, and only one supplies observed
   between-rule discrimination;
2. the 400 generated records reduce to eight unique decision states; and
3. the 250/400 comparator result partly reflects a narrower output relation,
   whereas the information-equivalent implementation ties exactly.

The supported result remains useful, but these limits make a Brief Report the
appropriate standalone form. Packaging, replay and exact hashes do not raise
the scientific mass.

## Preserved boundaries and negative results

- the initial 32 development cases are reported separately and are not pooled
  into confirmation;
- the four zero-effect coordinate-ablation cells remain null results and do not
  establish dispensability;
- two source-locator/content-hash records recur across panels despite case-ID
  separation;
- gold, candidate rule and evaluation remain within one authorship and research
  programme;
- the flat rule is an intentionally weak registered foil, not a complete
  deployed integration system;
- raw-text extraction, naturalistic transport, external adjudication,
  downstream utility and deployed-system superiority remain untested;
- the information-equivalent tie forbids an inherent-expressivity claim.

No optional broader experiment is required for this Brief Report. A future
full paper would require a separately authorized, independently governed and
source-disjoint naturalistic study with strong same-universe comparators and a
downstream or raw-text estimand.
