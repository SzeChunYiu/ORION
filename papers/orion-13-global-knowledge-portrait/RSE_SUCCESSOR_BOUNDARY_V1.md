# P3 RSE successor boundary V1

The RSE exact studies reinforce P3's preservation/obstruction discipline: stable object identity is not sufficient for stable scientific meaning or standing after a representation/schema transition. A successor may preserve a claim only when its justification-bearing correspondence remains valid; otherwise the affected scientific object must be transported, reopened, invalidated or left unresolved.

This is not a new P3 semantic-transport theorem. Ontology/schema evolution, dynamic assurance, proof/justification systems and generic validity conditions are donor-owned; the registered RSE four-family state-schema effect is explicitly donor-subsumed.
