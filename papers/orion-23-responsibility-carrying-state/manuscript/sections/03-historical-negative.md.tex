\section{Permanent negative history}\label{permanent-negative-history}

The historical experiment constructed independent binary latent
variables \texttt{(x,m,r)} and responsibilities \texttt{PREDICT=x},
\texttt{DECIDE=x}, \texttt{INTERVENE=x*m}, \texttt{VERIFY=x*m}, and
\texttt{REPAIR=r}. Representations were \texttt{Z1=(x)},
\texttt{Z2=(x,m)} and \texttt{Z3=(x,m,r)}.

Exact enumeration produced the intended responsibility ladder: all
representations were perfect for prediction/decision; \texttt{Z1} was
exactly 0.5 on intervene/verify while \texttt{Z2/Z3} were 1.0;
\texttt{Z2} was 0.5 on repair while \texttt{Z3} was 1.0. Exact upward
debts were therefore +0.50.

However, the frozen protocol also required the maximum deviation among
100 finite-sample sanity replicates of \texttt{n=1024} to be ≤0.05. The
observed maximum was \texttt{0.0556640625} at replicate 92. The
controlled sufficiency-debt gate is therefore permanently not met.

\subsection{Root cause}\label{root-cause}

The harness grouped a finite sample by compact state, selected the
majority target value from that same sample and credited that majority
on the same observations, then maximized deviation over 100 replicates.
The statistic combines within-sample majority optimism with an
extreme-value operation. The correct response is not to change
\texttt{0.05} after observing \texttt{0.0556640625}. The old terminal
stays negative. The successor changes the estimand: exact support is
evaluated by exhaustive equivalence classes, while efficacy is evaluated
on a fresh safety--cost benchmark.
