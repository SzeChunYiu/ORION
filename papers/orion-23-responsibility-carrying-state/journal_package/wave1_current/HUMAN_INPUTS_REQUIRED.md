# Human filing inputs

Before submission, the author must complete the venue’s private metadata fields:

- final author order, affiliation, and persistent researcher identifiers;
- funding and grant information;
- competing-interest and ethics declarations;
- confirmation of the venue’s current artificial-intelligence and confidentiality policies;
- the persistent public artifact record and licence selections;
- final OpenReview upload and submission identifier.

These are filing actions. They do not change the anonymous scientific claims or review materials.
