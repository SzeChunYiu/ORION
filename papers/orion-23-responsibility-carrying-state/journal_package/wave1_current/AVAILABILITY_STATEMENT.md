# Availability statement

The anonymous review archive contains the numerical evidence required to reconstruct every table and quantitative statement in the manuscript, together with a standard-library verifier independent of the original model-fitting and Boolean-search programs. The anonymous source archive contains the complete manuscript source and bibliography.

The full study programs, frozen finite panels, and execution records will be deposited in a persistent public research artifact on publication. They are withheld during anonymous review because their development history contains author-identifying metadata. The paper does not depend on a private dataset: the learned-data study uses the handwritten-digits dataset bundled with scikit-learn, and the Boolean-formula panels are finite constructed objects described completely in the review archive.

The software and manuscript source will be released under permissive open licences stated on the eventual archive record. No confidential participant data or access-controlled human data are used.
