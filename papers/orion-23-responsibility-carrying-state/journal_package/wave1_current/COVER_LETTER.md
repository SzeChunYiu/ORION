# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider **“Responsibility-Relative Reuse of Learned and Verified State”** as a research paper for Transactions on Machine Learning Research.

The paper asks a narrow systems-and-learning question: when stored state remains current and well provenanced, what additional condition licenses its reuse for a changed downstream task? We formalize support relative to a named responsibility and evaluate a fail-closed reuse policy on one learned dataset and three finite verifier-backed panels. The manuscript explicitly separates results that follow from routing by construction from measured failures of confidence-only, provenance-only, unconditional-transport, and signature-only comparators.

The central learned-data result uses 1,797 handwritten digits under parity and exact-identity responsibilities. Responsibility-aware routing matches the same component predictors used by an always-raw ceiling while reading 33 rather than 64 state values per episode. Exact Boolean-formula studies then test certificate revocation, provenance-tiered reuse, and bounded drift transport. An earlier zero-harm endpoint is retained as an adverse measurement result because it was self-scored and supplied no independent harm opportunity.

The submission claims neither deployment safety nor broad population generalization. All finite and single-dataset boundaries are stated in the abstract, results, and limitations. Anonymous review data and an independent verifier reconstruct every reported quantity, while the complete source archive builds the submitted manuscript.

The manuscript is original, is not under review elsewhere, and has no confidential participant data. Author identity, declarations, and the persistent public artifact record will be completed through the venue’s filing workflow.
