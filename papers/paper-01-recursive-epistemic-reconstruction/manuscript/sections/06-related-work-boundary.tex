\section{Nearest-work and claim boundary}

The nearest-work programme deliberately removes broad automation claims from Paper I. AI Scientist-v2 contributes progressive agentic/tree exploration and experiment-management mechanisms; AI Co-Scientist contributes specialized generation, reflection, ranking, evolution, proximity, and resource-allocation roles; Kosmos contributes long-horizon coordination through a structured world model; Agent Laboratory contributes staged research artifacts, human checkpoints, and cost accounting; systems engineering contributes recursive decomposition, interfaces, traceability, and verification planning; rational metareasoning contributes the principle that additional computation should be justified by expected downstream value.

The 2026 literature narrows the claim further. AREX explicitly alternates research with constraint-wise audit and targeted follow-up \cite{arex2026}, so recursive follow-up is not a standalone novelty. SCION compiles scientific intent into staged objectives, dependencies, verification checkpoints, artifacts and fallback conditions \cite{scion2026}, so structured/dependency-aware scientific planning is not novel. Iris maintains an evolving information state containing revisable claims and uses epistemic actions to acquire decision-critical information \cite{iris2026}, so Paper I cannot rely on ``explicit evolving knowledge state'' as a broad novelty statement. SciAgentArena provides realistic stepwise scientific-agent evaluation and demonstrates that open-ended autonomy remains difficult \cite{sciagentarena2026}. A complementary 2026 assessment shows that scientific-agent outcome success can coexist with weak evidence use and limited refutation-driven belief revision \cite{scientificreasoning2026}; this motivates process-level epistemic evaluation but does not provide evidence that Paper I generalizes beyond its frozen mechanical world family.

ORION may absorb or adapt those mechanisms. The principle of undiscovered public knowledge \cite{swanson1986} motivates the search-universe model $W_t$ as a first-class state: relevant connections may exist across domain boundaries that no single query or prompt would retrieve. The capture-mark-recapture technique \cite{kastner2009} and open-world evaluation for diverse perspectives \cite{chenchoi2025} inform the coverage and stopping rules for $W_t$ exploration. A systematic mechanism-matrix pass (Table~\ref{tab:P1-T1}) evaluates each nearest work against the four candidate deltas P1.D1--P1.D4 defined in the foundations section.

\subsection{Per-delta verdicts}

\paragraph{P1.D1 (K/W/M separation) --- composition-only.}
Iris implements the $K$ half completely: revisable claims carrying scope, status, and supporting/refuting evidence pointers, with Add/Update/Invalidate operators \cite{iris2026}. SCION's Research Execution Plan plus layered epistemic memory covers explicit method state, and Kosmos covers a persistent structured world model \cite{scion2026,kosmos2025}. The only component without a verified parent is $W$---a separate model of the relevant search universe, distinct from knowledge about the task, that is itself revisable. Bisht et al.\ independently recommend building one as future work \cite{bisht2026}, confirming the gap is real but meaning ORION cannot claim to have identified it. A single unparented organ inside a three-part state is not an independent delta.

\paragraph{P1.D2 (typed formulation revision) --- survives, narrowly.}
ARTS already diagnoses implementation-fault versus bad-hypothesis \cite{arts2026}, so the binary execution-versus-hypothesis split is struck. Iris already attributes a failure to which specific claim in the knowledge state is wrong \cite{iris2026}. MAST classifies multi-agent failures into 14 modes across three categories (system design, inter-agent misalignment, task verification); none of the 14 mode names targets a formulation, search-universe, representation, decomposition, or measurement-model coordinate \cite{mast2025}. Who\&When and Who\&When Pro annotate trajectories with the failure-responsible agent and decisive step \cite{whowhen2025,whowhenpro2026}. Auditable Agents names responsibility attribution as a surveyed dimension \cite{auditable2026}. HarnessFix goes further: it aligns failed trajectories with responsible harness artifacts and maps the resulting flaw records to scoped repair operators under regression-aware validation \cite{harnessfix2026}. Generic failure attribution and even diagnosis-conditioned scoped repair are therefore prior art. What remains unparented across these neighbours is narrower: the \emph{epistemic type} of the responsibility participates in an \textbf{authority gate} for a high-level formulation or search-universe mutation. A formulation/search-universe diagnosis may make that mutation admissible only after lower-level alternatives are excluded, whereas evidence-only or execution-only responsibility withholds the high-level rewrite licence. Paper I has local mechanical evidence that this distinction bites---the hidden-shift falsifier exposed an over-broad reframe gate in which a singular responsibility diagnosis was wrongly treated as sufficient licence, and the repair is recorded in the claim ledger.

\paragraph{P1.D3 (scoped reopening) --- struck.}
EviGraph performs dependency-directed reopening inside a 2026 autonomous research agent: it identifies a weak node, forms a repair group containing that node and its downstream subordinates, removes the subordinates, repairs the node, and regenerates affected nodes in dependency order \cite{evigraph2026}. EA-Graph adds content-hash anchoring, staleness detection independent of evidence strength, and claim withdrawal under upstream drift \cite{eagraph2026}. Dependency-guided rollback likewise builds a typed provenance graph, preserves state with independent trusted support, deactivates unsupported downstream memory state, and selectively replays affected computation \cite{dependencyrollback2026}. Beneath these systems, Doyle's justification-based truth maintenance invalidates every dependent conclusion when a justification is retracted \cite{doyle1979}; change impact analysis, regression test selection, and incremental build systems have done transitive-closure invalidation in engineering practice for decades. ORION therefore claims no independent novelty for scoped reopening, selective rollback, or preservation of unaffected state. The residual is only the \emph{trigger and scope}: EviGraph reopens from an evidence-graph inconsistency while holding Problem and Gap nodes immutable, whereas ORION reopens because an admitted formulation or search-universe coordinate changed. That residual is inseparable from P1.D2 and belongs to the composition.

\paragraph{P1.D4 (mechanic self-audit) --- composition-only.}
AREX already alternates research with recursive constraint-wise audit and emits targeted follow-up research from what the audit finds \cite{arex2026}, which is the ``audit generates further research'' loop in full. The NASA Systems Engineering Handbook treats an empty cell in a requirements verification matrix as a defect that obliges new work, which is the ``missing dimension mechanically generates an obligation'' rule in full \cite{nasa2016}. Auditable Agents establishes agent self-audit as a named, surveyed property \cite{auditable2026}. What is left---that the audited object is ORION's own research mechanics and that the emitted obligations are typed research questions rather than test procedures---is a domain transfer of two established disciplines, not a mechanism. It is presented as engineering substrate enabling the P1.D2 claim to be inspected and reproduced.

\subsection{Residual claim}

After the initial matrix, the mechanism itself was exposed to further nearest-work rounds before any confirmatory world was executed. REFLECT and Causal Agent Replay absorb intervention-supported attribution and causal replay; R2Act and DARC absorb diagnosis-conditioned recovery admission; HERALD and CausalFlow absorb counterfactual outcome-flip auditing and inclusion-minimal repair; dependency-guided rollback absorbs selective replay with preservation of independently supported state \cite{dependencyrollback2026}; and CausalRepair contributes dual static/dynamic causal-context slicing. Certificate/broker work separately absorbs proposal/admission/execution separation and scoped enforcement. These \emph{pre-outcome} donors were not citation-only exclusions: their structures determined the executable substrate, strongest-parent policies, and direct ablations. Earlier rounds G and H found no material change to the post-engulfment design and were recorded as a bounded fixed point, not universal bibliographic completeness.

HarnessFix was surfaced later, during the 2026-08-19 #489 saturation pass. It independently demonstrates diagnosis-to-scoped-repair at the harness layer \cite{harnessfix2026}, so it further narrows the literature wording, but it is not retroactively claimed as an input to the frozen experiment. The same saturation pass found strict reference-metadata defects. These post-result findings change citation/ownership wording, not the already frozen experimental terminal.

The smallest surviving and empirically tested Paper~I claim is therefore:

\begin{quote}
In the frozen mechanical world family, after generic attribution, active diagnosis, diagnosis-conditioned scoped repair, admissible recovery, counterfactual minimal repair, dependency rollback, and certificate enforcement are granted to the parents or otherwise removed from ORION novelty, a scientific high-level K/W/M mutation is accepted only when lower-level alternatives have been excluded, the candidate causes a same-task recovery, a protected sibling or invariant is preserved, and the observed dependency impact matches the proposed reopen scope. This science-specific protected necessity composition improves recovery over every registered assimilated strong parent while withholding high-level mutation on evidence/execution controls.
\end{quote}

Everything else has a parent. The state representation is Iris's; generic diagnosis-to-scoped-repair is HarnessFix territory; the reopening machinery is EviGraph's, EA-Graph's, Doyle's, dependency-guided rollback's and change-impact analysis's; recursive audit is AREX's; the missing-cell obligation is the requirements verification matrix's; attribution and replay are REFLECT/CAR territory; admission is R2Act/DARC territory; and minimal counterfactual repair is HERALD/CausalFlow territory. ``Reformulation'' itself has decades of history in automated planning \cite{reformulation2023}; metareasoning budgets are formalised elsewhere \cite{desabbata2024,cognitivefriction2026}; and the gap is independently identified by Arbor \cite{arbor2026} and Bisht et al.\ \cite{bisht2026}. The bounded residual is a science-specific composition of protected necessity conditions, not ownership of any ingredient.

The SCION checkpoint-failure semantics question raised in an earlier pass has been closed by reading the full paper text: SCION distinguishes precompiled fallback conditions in the Research Execution Plan from a runtime rollback mechanism in which critic checkpoints trigger rollback, branch termination, re-routing, or resource reallocation when an agent drifts, hallucinates, or enters an unproductive branch \cite{scion2026}. The runtime rollback is therefore gated on procedural anomalies rather than on the type of responsibility for a failure, and SCION does not reopen already-completed dependent stages conditional on a responsibility type; that residual trigger remains ORION-specific and is unaffected.

Round A of the new #489 saturation programme additionally verified dependency-guided rollback and HarnessFix as close donors and found bibliographic metadata errors in several recent entries. Those findings change citation/ownership wording but do not alter the powered primary/replication result. A second fresh primary-source round is required before literature saturation can be claimed.
