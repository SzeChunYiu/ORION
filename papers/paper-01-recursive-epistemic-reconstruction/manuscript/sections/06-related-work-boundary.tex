\section{Nearest-work and claim boundary}

The nearest-work programme deliberately removes broad automation claims from Paper I. AI Scientist-v2 contributes progressive agentic/tree exploration and experiment-management mechanisms; AI Co-Scientist contributes specialized generation, reflection, ranking, evolution, proximity, and resource-allocation roles; Kosmos contributes long-horizon coordination through a structured world model; Agent Laboratory contributes staged research artifacts, human checkpoints, and cost accounting; systems engineering contributes recursive decomposition, interfaces, traceability, and verification planning; rational metareasoning contributes the principle that additional computation should be justified by expected downstream value.

ORION may absorb or adapt those mechanisms. Paper I therefore does \emph{not} claim the first autonomous scientist, first iterative scientific agent, first agent tree search, first structured world model, or first recursive engineering process.

The surviving candidate comparison is narrower: explicit co-evolution of $K/W/M$, responsibility-targeted formulation revision, dependency-directed reopening, and recursive mechanic-cell self-audit. Even that difference is presently a candidate rather than an externally established novelty result.

Before submission, the nearest-work atlas and bibliography require a fresh literature-closure pass. The current repository intentionally distinguishes an internal comparison record from a publication-authorizing literature review. This manuscript therefore avoids converting an incomplete search state into a novelty statement.
