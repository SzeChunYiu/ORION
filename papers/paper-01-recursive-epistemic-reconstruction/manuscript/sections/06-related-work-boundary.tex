\section{Nearest-work and claim boundary}

The nearest-work programme deliberately removes broad automation claims from Paper I. AI Scientist-v2 contributes progressive agentic/tree exploration and experiment-management mechanisms; AI Co-Scientist contributes specialized generation, reflection, ranking, evolution, proximity, and resource-allocation roles; Kosmos contributes long-horizon coordination through a structured world model; Agent Laboratory contributes staged research artifacts, human checkpoints, and cost accounting; systems engineering contributes recursive decomposition, interfaces, traceability, and verification planning; rational metareasoning contributes the principle that additional computation should be justified by expected downstream value.

The 2026 literature narrows the claim further. AREX explicitly alternates research with constraint-wise audit and targeted follow-up \cite{arex2026}, so recursive follow-up is not a standalone novelty. SCION compiles scientific intent into staged objectives, dependencies, verification checkpoints, artifacts and fallback conditions \cite{scion2026}, so structured/dependency-aware scientific planning is not novel. Iris maintains an evolving information state containing revisable claims and uses epistemic actions to acquire decision-critical information \cite{iris2026}, so Paper I cannot rely on ``explicit evolving knowledge state'' as a broad novelty statement. SciAgentArena provides realistic stepwise scientific-agent evaluation and demonstrates that open-ended autonomy remains difficult \cite{sciagentarena2026}.

ORION may absorb or adapt those mechanisms. The principle of undiscovered public knowledge \cite{swanson1986} motivates the search-universe model $W_t$ as a first-class state: relevant connections may exist across domain boundaries that no single query or prompt would retrieve. The capture-mark-recapture technique \cite{kastner2009} and open-world evaluation for diverse perspectives \cite{chenchoi2025} inform the coverage and stopping rules for $W_t$ exploration. A systematic mechanism-matrix pass (Table~\ref{tab:P1-T1}) evaluates each nearest work against the four candidate deltas P1.D1--P1.D4 defined in the foundations section.

\subsection{Per-delta verdicts}

\paragraph{P1.D1 (K/W/M separation) --- composition-only.}
Iris implements the $K$ half completely: revisable claims carrying scope, status, and supporting/refuting evidence pointers, with Add/Update/Invalidate operators \cite{iris2026}. SCION's Research Execution Plan plus layered epistemic memory covers explicit method state, and Kosmos covers a persistent structured world model \cite{scion2026,kosmos2025}. The only component without a verified parent is $W$---a separate model of the relevant search universe, distinct from knowledge about the task, that is itself revisable. Bisht et al.\ independently recommend building one as future work \cite{bisht2026}, confirming the gap is real but meaning ORION cannot claim to have identified it. A single unparented organ inside a three-part state is not an independent delta.

\paragraph{P1.D2 (typed formulation revision) --- survives, narrowly.}
ARTS already diagnoses implementation-fault versus bad-hypothesis \cite{arts2026}. so the binary execution-versus-hypothesis split is struck. Iris already attributes a failure to which specific claim in the knowledge state is wrong \cite{iris2026}. MAST classifies multi-agent failures into 14 modes across three categories (system design, inter-agent misalignment, task verification); none of the 14 mode names targets a formulation, search-universe, representation, decomposition, or measurement-model coordinate \cite{mast2025}. Who\&When and Who\&When Pro annotate trajectories with the failure-responsible agent and decisive step \cite{whowhen2025,whowhenpro2026}. Auditable Agents names responsibility attribution as a surveyed dimension \cite{auditable2026}. What remains unparented across all of these is that the \emph{type} of the responsibility functions as an \textbf{authority gate}: a formulation or search-universe diagnosis selects which coordinate may be rewritten, and an evidence-only or execution-only diagnosis withholds the rewrite licence entirely. Every neighbour above produces a \emph{descriptive label}; none attaches a \emph{permission} to it. ORION has local mechanical evidence that this distinction bites---the hidden-shift falsifier exposed an over-broad reframe gate in which a singular responsibility diagnosis was wrongly treated as sufficient licence, and the repair is recorded in the claim ledger.

\paragraph{P1.D3 (scoped reopening) --- struck.}
EviGraph performs dependency-directed reopening inside a 2026 autonomous research agent: it identifies a weak node, forms a repair group containing that node and its downstream subordinates, removes the subordinates, repairs the node, and regenerates affected nodes in dependency order \cite{evigraph2026}. EA-Graph adds content-hash anchoring, staleness detection independent of evidence strength, and claim withdrawal under upstream drift \cite{eagraph2026}. Beneath both, Doyle's justification-based truth maintenance invalidates every dependent conclusion when a justification is retracted \cite{doyle1979}; change impact analysis, regression test selection, and incremental build systems have done transitive-closure invalidation in engineering practice for decades. ORION therefore claims no independent novelty for scoped reopening. The residual is only the \emph{trigger and scope}: EviGraph reopens from an evidence-graph inconsistency while holding Problem and Gap nodes immutable, whereas ORION reopens because a formulation or search-universe coordinate changed. That residual is inseparable from P1.D2 and belongs to the composition.

\paragraph{P1.D4 (mechanic self-audit) --- composition-only.}
AREX already alternates research with recursive constraint-wise audit and emits targeted follow-up research from what the audit finds \cite{arex2026}, which is the ``audit generates further research'' loop in full. The NASA Systems Engineering Handbook treats an empty cell in a requirements verification matrix as a defect that obliges new work, which is the ``missing dimension mechanically generates an obligation'' rule in full \cite{nasa2016}. Auditable Agents establishes agent self-audit as a named, surveyed property \cite{auditable2026}. What is left---that the audited object is ORION's own research mechanics and that the emitted obligations are typed research questions rather than test procedures---is a domain transfer of two established disciplines, not a mechanism. It is presented as engineering substrate enabling the P1.D2 claim to be inspected and reproduced.

\subsection{Residual claim}

After absorbing all verified nearest work, the smallest surviving Paper~I claim is:

\begin{quote}
In a research process whose object knowledge $K$, relevance/search-universe model $W$, and method state $M$ are separately represented, ORION types the responsibility for an observed residual so that responsibility determines authority: only a formulation or search-universe responsibility licenses a coordinate-specific rewrite of $W$ or $M$, while evidence and execution responsibilities are routed to acquisition and repair and are denied the rewrite licence; and it is that licensed rewrite---not an inconsistency inside the evidence graph---that scopes the dependency-directed reopening of prior closure. The contribution is the licensing relation binding typed responsibility to the reframe target and to the reopen scope, evaluated on hidden formulation-shift tasks against matched baselines with evidence-only and execution-only negative controls.
\end{quote}

Everything else has a parent. The state representation is Iris's; the reopening machinery is EviGraph's, EA-Graph's, Doyle's and change-impact analysis's; the recursive audit is AREX's; the missing-cell obligation is the requirements verification matrix's; the coarse execution-versus-hypothesis diagnosis is ARTS's; ``reformulation'' itself is a named operation with decades of history in automated planning, where it re-represents a problem in the same language while preserving the solution set \cite{reformulation2023}---the absorbed distinction is that planning reformulation is solution-preserving and efficiency-motivated, whereas ORION's is triggered by evidence that the formulation is wrong and therefore changes what counts as a correct answer; the metareasoning budget is formalised elsewhere \cite{desabbata2024,cognitivefriction2026}; the gap statement itself is independently identified by Arbor \cite{arbor2026} and Bisht et al.\ \cite{bisht2026}. What no verified work does is make the \emph{type of the diagnosed responsibility} the thing that grants or withholds permission to rewrite the formulation, and then use the rewritten coordinate to bound what gets reopened. The residual is a licensing relation, and it is compositional by construction.
