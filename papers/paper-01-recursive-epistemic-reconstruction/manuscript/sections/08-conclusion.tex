\section{Conclusion}

Paper I reframes recursive research as an epistemic-state problem rather than a synonym for repeated model calls. ORION separates the knowledge state, relevance/search-universe model, and governed method state; diagnoses typed residuals so that responsibility determines authority to rewrite a formulation coordinate; and reopens only closure that depends on a changed coordinate, using the rewritten coordinate---not an evidence-graph inconsistency---to bound the scope. The recursive mechanic-cell self-audit and the K/W/M separation are engineering substrate absorbed from prior work, not independent deltas; the surviving contribution is the licensing relation binding typed responsibility to the reframe target and to the reopen scope.

The current contribution is therefore an inspectable mechanism plus a falsifiable hypothesis. The local falsifier has already served its intended role by exposing an invalid reframe path and forcing a repair. The stronger scientific question---whether this architecture improves fresh hidden-formulation tasks relative to matched static and agentic baselines while avoiding unnecessary reframes---remains open. Until that prospective evidence exists, Paper I should be read as a completed scoped working manuscript with an explicit external \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} boundary, not as a publication-ready superiority claim.
