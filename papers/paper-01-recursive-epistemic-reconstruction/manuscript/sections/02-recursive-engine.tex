\section{Recursive reconstruction engine}

The minimum engine is
\[
\mathrm{FRAME}\to\mathrm{SEARCH}\to\mathrm{ABSORB}\to\mathrm{RECONSTRUCT}
\to\mathrm{DETECT}\to\mathrm{DIAGNOSE}\to\mathrm{REFRAME}
\to\mathrm{REOPEN}\to\mathrm{RECURSE}.
\]

FRAME binds the question, scope and success conditions. SEARCH produces candidate source material and has no scientific-authority write capability. ABSORB maps source material into typed contributions and may create a verified claim only through a distinct certificate-producing verifier. RECONSTRUCT builds a global portrait from retained claims. DETECT emits typed residuals. DIAGNOSE separates candidate responsibility levels before high-impact revision. REFRAME changes only the supported scope of $K_t$, $W_t$ or, under separate Self-ORION governance, $M_t$. REOPEN stales dependent closure. RECURSE repeats the process from the changed state.

A proposer, LLM, query generator or search engine is therefore computationally important but epistemically non-authoritative. The software contract forbids non-verification operators from directly increasing claim authority.
