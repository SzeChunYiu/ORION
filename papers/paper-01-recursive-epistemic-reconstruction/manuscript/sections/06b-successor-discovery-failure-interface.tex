\section{Successor interface: bounded meta-discovery and scoped negative knowledge}
\label{sec:p1-successor-discovery-failure}

Paper I's separation of object knowledge $K_t$, search-universe/formulation state $W_t$, and governed method state $M_t$ also supplies a boundary for later ORION meta-discovery studies. A successor interface does not treat a surprising trace, recurring failure, or semantically distant analogy as evidence that a new scientific mechanic exists. Instead, a bounded epistemic-atom study freezes the candidate relation, resource envelope, positive and no-atom controls, parent-decomposition hypotheses, protected fields, evaluator identity, and interaction bookkeeping before outcome-bearing evaluation. Its question is comparative: after registered parents and their permitted interactions are composed, is an additional relation still required for the protected contract?

The same successor interface makes negative experience contextual. Reusable failure knowledge states which coordinates must remain the same for an exclusion to apply and which changes reopen the question. A failure record with no classified context cannot become a global prohibition by omission; a mismatch on a load-bearing required-same coordinate makes the old exclusion inapplicable, while a declared semantic or representation change may reopen it. Epistemic-tension candidates, thought experiments, and concept/representation candidates remain proposal records rather than success terminals, and a concept candidate's declared correspondence or preservation obligations do not establish validity or adoption.

The completed bounded successor studies narrow, rather than broaden, Paper I's mechanism claims. The recursive atom-study calculus remained useful as an evaluation discipline, but the proposed monolithic tension, imagination, and concept labels decomposed into multiple candidate atoms; the failure-side pilots likewise found multiple signal families, while scoped negative-knowledge extraction and staleness reduced to truth-maintenance/responsibility and P7 transport respectively. In the protected zero-error regime-transition benchmark, a verified regime-revision parent matched the full ORION composition exactly across primary and disjoint replication, so the programme closed as regime invention without incremental ORION value rather than as a universal Jump mechanism. These successor results therefore support stricter decomposition and parent subtraction; they do not convert Paper I into evidence for open-ended scientific creativity.

Paper III owns correspondence and obstruction analysis, Paper IV owns scientific-evidence authority, and Paper V owns later method-adoption governance. Paper I contributes the bounded exclusion, preservation, reopening, and responsibility interface only.
