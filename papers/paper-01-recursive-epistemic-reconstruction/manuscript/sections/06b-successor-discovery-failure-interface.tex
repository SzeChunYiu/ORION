\section{Successor interface: bounded meta-discovery and scoped negative knowledge}
\label{sec:p1-successor-discovery-failure}

Paper I's separation of object knowledge $K_t$, search-universe/formulation state $W_t$, and governed method state $M_t$ also supplies a boundary for a later ORION meta-discovery programme.  The successor interface does not treat a surprising trace, a recurring failure, or a semantically distant analogy as evidence that a new scientific mechanic exists.  Instead it exposes these observations as typed research proposals that must survive bounded comparison against already available parent mechanisms before any new structural claim is considered.

A bounded epistemic-atom study freezes the candidate relation, resource envelope, positive and no-atom controls, parent-decomposition hypotheses, protected fields, evaluator identity, and interaction bookkeeping before outcome-bearing evaluation.  Its question is deliberately comparative: after the registered parents and their permitted interactions are composed, is an additional relation still required for the protected contract?  A positive study can justify further investigation of that bounded residual; it cannot by itself authorize a method change, a novelty claim, or an open-ended creativity claim.

The same successor interface makes negative experience explicitly contextual.  Reusable failure knowledge records which coordinates must remain the same for an exclusion to apply, which coordinates are irrelevant to that exclusion, and which declared changes reopen the question.  A failure record with no classified context cannot silently become a global prohibition.  A mismatch on a load-bearing required-same coordinate makes the old exclusion inapplicable; a declared semantic or representation change can reopen it.  This preserves Paper I's dependency-reopening principle when negative knowledge is carried across recursive reformulations.

Epistemic-tension candidates, thought experiments, and concept/representation candidates are therefore proposal records rather than success terminals.  A concept candidate may name a correspondence map, preservation obligations, and contracts it is intended to unlock, but those declarations do not establish that the mapping is sound, that the candidate is scientifically valid, or that it should be adopted.  Paper III owns correspondence and obstruction analysis, Paper IV owns scientific-evidence authority, and Paper V owns later method-adoption governance.  Paper I contributes the bounded exclusion, preservation, and reopening interface only.

This interface adds no claim that ORION has discovered a distinct universal ``Jump'' operator or a general mechanism of scientific imagination.  Any result-bearing statement about a representation transition remains downstream of a prospectively frozen discriminator and the separate authority layers described in the later papers.
