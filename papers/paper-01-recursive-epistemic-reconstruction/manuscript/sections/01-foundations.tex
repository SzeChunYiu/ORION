\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The scientific transition problem}

A research process can improve its local performance while changing the wrong
scientific object. More search does not repair a missing representation. A
better optimizer does not decide whether the measurement model or the question
is wrong. A successful intervention does not by itself authorize rewriting the
task, and more evidence does not justify a formulation change when the defect is
merely execution. The general problem is to determine the admissible frontier
among changes to questions, representations, measurements, model classes,
experimental designs, search policies, and methods when different changes
require different evidence and authority.

Section~\ref{sec:escalation-theory} formulates this problem independently of a
particular agent architecture. For the empirical instance in this paper, the
evolving research state is represented as
\[
X_t=(K_t,W_t,M_t),
\]
where $K_t$ is the provenance-preserving state of knowledge about the target object, $W_t$ is the current model of which domains, representations, mechanisms, and search routes may be relevant, and $M_t$ is the governed research method. The three coordinates are separated because they require different evidence and different authority to change. This engineering separation is in the spirit of Parnas's information-hiding principle: change-prone design decisions should be isolated behind explicit interfaces rather than entangled implicitly \cite{parnas1972}. The revision policy for $K_t$ likewise borrows the minimal-change distinction between expansion, contraction, and revision from the AGM belief-revision tradition \cite{agm1985}; no claim is made here that the operational update rules satisfy the full AGM postulates, which would have to be established separately.

A new source may update $K_t$ without changing $W_t$. A previously unrecognized
parent discipline or representation may require a change to $W_t$ while
leaving the method intact. A persistent, well-attributed failure can become
evidence for a challenger to $M_t$, but method revision is governed separately
in the evaluated instance. The theory is not restricted to these three
coordinates: any partial order of scientific transitions can supply its
revision space.

\subsection{Contributions}

The paper makes four contributions at different authority levels.

First, Section~\ref{sec:escalation-theory} defines epistemic transition
envelopes over well-founded partial orders. The object applies to transitions
among scientific questions, representations, measurements, model classes,
experiments, search policies, and methods; $K/W/M$ is only the evaluated
realization.

Second, it proves an exact donor-substitution theorem: any product of prior
mechanisms reproduces a required transition decision if and only if its exposed
interface separates all contexts requiring different decisions. A companion
Bayes result gives the irreducible error of mixed interface fibres. The earlier
licence-coupling impossibility is recovered as one corollary rather than the
whole theory.

Third, it proves a conservative-envelope criterion for absorbing a stronger
donor theory without erasing old results, and a universal representation result
for antichain-valued transition policies. These results replace the claim that
the architecture owns its components with a theory of when arbitrary donor
components are sufficient, insufficient, or conservatively extensible.

Fourth, Sections~\ref{sec:necessity} and~\ref{sec:revision} evaluate one
five-gate minimal-escalation instance in powered mechanical worlds and exact
heterogeneous contracts. The information-equivalent product is required to
tie; the donor-complete product without the licence coupling is the tested
lossy interface. The earlier 66-case study remains negative design history:
it exposed an unsafe diagnosis-to-reframe shortcut but was underpowered and
contained template shortcuts, so it is not promoted into evidence for the
active claim.

$K/W/M$ state, failure attribution, counterfactual repair, scoped reopening,
recursive audit, and generic mutation authorization are architectural substrate
with identified parents.  Section~\ref{sec:relatedwork} assigns those
ingredients, while Section~\ref{sec:limitations} keeps the empirical authority
at the registered mechanical and exact-contract families.
