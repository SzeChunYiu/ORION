\section{Limitations and open empirical boundary}

The present repository establishes an implemented reference architecture, local deterministic falsifiers, a powered credential-free mechanical comparison with disjoint replication, and a separate exact heterogeneous revision-responsibility contract study. It does not establish reliable open-web retrieval, general scientific-language interpretation, open-world parent-discipline recall, model-general superiority, deployed revision-layer diagnosis, or autonomous method improvement.

Recent autonomous-research benchmarks report substantial remaining difficulty in wide literature discovery and end-to-end scientific rediscovery \cite{autoresearchbench2026,researchclawbench2026}. We treat those results as motivation for hostile external evaluation, not as evidence that ORION solves the problem.

Several boundaries are especially important.

\paragraph{Exact contract families are not external science.}
The v2.2.4 study uses fresh pre-bound seeds, protected matrices, three strong assimilated parents, and independent rescoring, but its worlds are generated mechanical response environments. P1-X broadens the \emph{types of revision contract} tested across five exact families, not the perceptual or linguistic realism of the agent. Its comparator-fair V2 and interface-order substitutions show that the tested escalation contract can matter once donor mechanisms are granted, but they do not show that a language-model research agent will correctly infer the same responsibility classes from ambiguous natural scientific evidence. Independently learned diagnosis/recovery engines, noisy observations, open-web evidence, and deployed scientific workflows remain untested.

\paragraph{Ideal-product equivalence is a substantive boundary.}
P1-X does not have an inherent expressivity advantage over a donor product supplied the same responsibility classes, partial order, counterfactual results, preservation/reopen predicates, and authority semantics: B3 ties exactly. Any practical advantage therefore depends on the value of making this scientific escalation interface explicit in realistic systems, not on a claim that centralization or ORION branding creates additional semantic power.

\paragraph{Negative execution history matters.}
P1-X V1 is retained because an independently discovered post-access B1/B2 implementation defect weakened its clean-control comparison. The defect was not patched after seeing outcomes; the publication-authorizing successor comparison is the separately frozen, disjoint V2. This protects the chronology but also emphasizes that the wider claim rests on exact contract engineering that still needs external validation.

\paragraph{Reconstruction quality is only partially measured.}
The current local suite checks key transition behaviors, but the deeper hypotheses about late-depth trace fidelity, reviewer localization time, and semantic mismatch require a dedicated prospective protocol.

\paragraph{Search and semantic adequacy are delegated, not solved here.}
Paper II owns route coverage/recall and Paper III owns scientific semantic projection and global reconstruction. A Paper-I success cannot be used as evidence that those capabilities work on real literature.

\paragraph{Verification remains separate.}
Search, proposal, diagnosis, and reframe mechanisms do not mint scientific authority. Paper IV owns the stronger evidence-binding and protected-verification claim.

\paragraph{Method change remains gated.}
Paper I can expose a possible method failure, but it does not authorize $M_t$ to rewrite itself. Paper V owns failure attribution, challenger construction, replay/fresh transfer, protected assurance, and host promotion.

\paragraph{Literature closure is bounded rather than universal.}
Rounds A--H and the later claim-expansion hostile searches reached bounded fixed points for their exact residuals and directly shaped the strongest parents. Because the 2026 repair-agent literature is moving rapidly, a final pre-submission delta search and replacement of preprints by archival versions remain required. No broad novelty statement follows from the bounded fixed point.

\subsection*{Observed limitations}

\paragraph{The historical V1 arm remains underpowered.}
With N=\TestCaseCount{} cases, the original study achieves \AchievedTier{} (below TIER D) and is \Underpowered{} for its original broad primary hypothesis. Those records, including the low root-success rates in Tables P1-2 and P1-3, are retained as negative history and are not pooled with the powered successor. The v2.2.4 result addresses a narrower prospectively frozen mechanical necessity claim on 2,882 worlds per run; it does not retroactively validate V1's broader formulation.

\paragraph{Perfect success is generator-bounded.}
Full ORION attains protected success 1.0000 in both v2.2.4 runs, and P1-X attains ESRD 1.000 on its exact V2 contract battery. These ceilings are credible only at the level of their deterministic registered generators and explicit action/response vocabularies. Strong parents, hostile ablations, the comparator-fair P1-X B1, and the ideal-product tie make the tested distinctions non-vacuous, but the ceiling results may not survive perceptual ambiguity, noisy interventions, learned proposal generation, or imperfect responsibility inference.

\paragraph{Template-level leaks exist in the local suite.}
The solvability audit identified perfect separation on fixed phrases (``The team's framing is'' distinguishes all 44 hidden-shift cases from all 22 controls) and filename-based family separation (proposal/trial stems identify DECOMPOSITION cases). These leaks were documented but not repaired in this iteration.

\paragraph{Mechanical arm generality is untested.}
Half the correct attributions (32 of 66) rest on rules that fire on exactly one case. Only four general relations cover three or more cases. A fresh set of naturalistic cases is required to distinguish general mechanisms from case-specific recognizers.

\paragraph{Blind-responder shortcuts affect half the suite.}
Thirty-three of 66 cases can be solved by a classifier that reads only resource path stems and two fixed template phrases. The remaining 33 PARENT/REPRESENTATION/MEASUREMENT cases require genuine arithmetic reasoning.
