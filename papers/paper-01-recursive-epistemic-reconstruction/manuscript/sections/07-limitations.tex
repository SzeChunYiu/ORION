\section{Limitations and open empirical boundary}

The present repository establishes an implemented reference architecture, local deterministic falsifiers, and auditable evidence/claim ledgers. It does not establish reliable open-web retrieval, general scientific-language interpretation, open-world parent-discipline recall, empirical superiority, or autonomous method improvement.

Recent autonomous-research benchmarks report substantial remaining difficulty in wide literature discovery and end-to-end scientific rediscovery \cite{autoresearchbench2026,researchclawbench2026}. We treat those results as motivation for hostile external evaluation, not as evidence that ORION solves the problem.

Several boundaries are especially important.

\paragraph{Local worlds are not external science.}
The hidden-shift falsifier demonstrates registered semantics on controlled cases. Fresh tasks with independently prepared labels and matched baselines are still required.

\paragraph{Reconstruction quality is only partially measured.}
The current local suite checks key transition behaviors, but the deeper hypotheses about late-depth trace fidelity, reviewer localization time, and semantic mismatch require a dedicated prospective protocol.

\paragraph{Search and semantic adequacy are delegated, not solved here.}
Paper II owns route coverage/recall and Paper III owns scientific semantic projection and global reconstruction. A Paper-I success cannot be used as evidence that those capabilities work on real literature.

\paragraph{Verification remains separate.}
Search, proposal, diagnosis, and reframe mechanisms do not mint scientific authority. Paper IV owns the stronger evidence-binding and protected-verification claim.

\paragraph{Method change remains gated.}
Paper I can expose a possible method failure, but it does not authorize $M_t$ to rewrite itself. Paper V owns failure attribution, challenger construction, replay/fresh transfer, protected assurance, and host promotion.

\paragraph{Literature closure is incomplete.}
The current nearest-work comparison is a working internal atlas. External bibliographic and claim-level closure remains \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK}; no broad novelty statement is authorized until that gap is closed.
