\section{Limitations and transfer boundary}

The publication-authorizing evidence consists of two prospectively controlled objects: the powered credential-free v2.2.4 hidden-formulation study with disjoint replication and the separately frozen P1-X heterogeneous revision-responsibility study. Both are designed to isolate the scientific escalation contract. Across v2.2.4, ORION reaches protected hidden-shift success 1.000 in both runs while preserving zero unnecessary high-level reframes, protected-sibling regressions, and forbidden high-level mutations; P1-X reaches 400/400 exact revision decisions versus 275/400 for the donor-complete greedy product with zero false high-level reframes and protected-invariant violations. These are the evidence-bearing results of this paper.
The present repository establishes an implemented reference architecture, local deterministic falsifiers, a powered credential-free mechanical comparison with disjoint replication, a separate exact heterogeneous revision-responsibility contract study, and merged successor studies of recursive atom decomposition, scoped failure knowledge and zero-error representation transition. It does not establish reliable open-web retrieval, general scientific-language interpretation, open-world parent-discipline recall, model-general superiority, deployed revision-layer diagnosis, autonomous method improvement, or a distinct general ORION creativity/Jump mechanism.

\paragraph{Contract-level evidence versus deployed inference.}
The evaluated worlds and P1-X cases provide exact or mechanically adjudicable responsibility, intervention, preservation, and dependency consequences. They test whether the escalation contract makes the correct decision once the relevant evidence is available; they do not estimate how often a deployed language-model research agent will infer the same responsibility classes from noisy, ambiguous scientific prose, open-web retrieval, or incomplete measurements. That transfer question requires a prospectively bound naturalistic campaign. It does not alter the demonstrated contract-level separation.

\paragraph{Portability rather than centralized expressivity.}
An ideal donor product supplied the same scientific responsibility, minimality, preservation, reopening, and authority semantics ties P1-X at 400/400. The positive conclusion is therefore implementation-independent: the tested escalation semantics are sufficient whether realized inside ORION or an equivalently integrated donor product. ORION's measured advantage over the realistic donor-complete comparator comes from making the coupling explicit and enforceable, not from a claim that centralization itself creates additional semantic power.

\paragraph{Historical diagnostics are not headline authority.}
The repository retains the earlier underpowered V1 study, template/filename shortcut audits, case-specific recognizer diagnostics, and the comparator-defective P1-X V1 as negative history. None is pooled into the publication-authorizing effects. The disjoint powered successor and prospectively repaired P1-X V2 were created precisely so those historical diagnostics could not be converted into favorable evidence after outcome access. Full details remain in the evaluation, reproducibility, and archived evidence records rather than occupying the central claim surface.

Recent autonomous-research benchmarks continue to show that wide literature discovery and end-to-end scientific rediscovery are difficult \cite{autoresearchbench2026,researchclawbench2026}. Paper I therefore makes no retrieval or deployed-agent superiority claim from analogy. Its strongest supported statement is the one directly tested here: on the registered mechanical and exact heterogeneous families, protected necessity gating makes high-level scientific reformulation markedly more successful when the formulation is wrong while remaining selective when lower-level repair is sufficient.
Several boundaries are especially important.
\section{Limitations and claim boundary}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{What is and is not claimed}

The smallest surviving claim that the experiments above actually test is this.
In the frozen mechanical world family, after generic attribution, active
diagnosis, diagnosis-conditioned scoped repair, admissible recovery,
counterfactual minimal repair, dependency rollback and certificate enforcement
are granted to the parents, a scientific high-level $K/W/M$ mutation is accepted
only when lower-level alternatives have been excluded, the candidate causes a
same-task recovery, a protected sibling or invariant is preserved, and the
observed dependency impact matches the proposed reopen scope. That composition
improves recovery over every registered assimilated strong parent while
withholding high-level mutation on evidence and execution controls.

Everything else in the architecture has a parent, and Section~\ref{sec:relatedwork}
names them. Nothing here establishes priority for iterative scientific agency,
tree search, structured world models, explicit knowledge state, recursive
systems engineering, failure attribution, scoped repair, dependency-directed
reopening, evidence-gated belief revision, model-class expansion, or mutation
authorization. The claim is about a condition on composition, not about
ownership of any ingredient.

Recent autonomous-research benchmarks report substantial remaining difficulty in
wide literature discovery and end-to-end scientific rediscovery
\cite{autoresearchbench2026,researchclawbench2026}. Those results are treated
here as motivation for hostile external evaluation, not as evidence that this
problem is solved.

\subsection{Boundaries of the evidence}

\paragraph{Exact contract families are not external science.}
The mutation-necessity experiment uses fresh pre-bound seeds, protected
matrices, three strong assimilated parents, and independent rescoring, but its
worlds are generated mechanical response environments. The
revision-responsibility battery broadens the \emph{types of revision contract}
tested across five exact families, not the perceptual or linguistic realism of
the agent. Its comparator-fair execution and interface-order substitutions show
that the tested escalation contract can matter once donor mechanisms are
granted, but they do not show that a language-model research agent will
correctly infer the same responsibility classes from ambiguous natural
scientific evidence. Independently learned diagnosis and recovery engines, noisy
observations, open-web evidence, and deployed scientific workflows remain
untested.

\paragraph{The meta-discovery results are narrowing results.}
The bounded study programme freezes a method for asking whether a proposed
epistemic mechanic has incremental value; it does not establish a universal
basis of atomic mechanics. Its first three broad labels each decompose into
several candidates. The scoped failure-memory study finds a useful
representation of negative knowledge but no incremental mechanism beyond strong
standard parents. The protected zero-error representation-transition study is
stronger evidence and still negative for this architecture's ownership: the full
candidate ties the verified regime-revision parent on both disjoint frozen
splits. None of these results licenses an automatic $K/W/M$ mutation or a
general scientific-creativity claim.

\paragraph{Successor meta-discovery results are narrowing results.}
The recursive atom-study programme freezes a method for asking whether a proposed epistemic mechanic has incremental value; it does not establish a universal atom basis. Its first tension, thought-experiment and concept pilots each decompose into multiple candidate atoms. The scoped failure-memory study finds a useful representation of negative knowledge but no incremental generic ORION mechanism beyond strong standard parents. The protected zero-error Jump study is stronger evidence but still negative for ORION-specific ownership: the full candidate ties the verified representation-regime revision parent on both disjoint frozen splits. None of these results licenses an automatic $K/W/M$ mutation or a general scientific-creativity claim.

\paragraph{Ideal-product equivalence is a substantive boundary.}
The escalation contract has no inherent expressivity advantage over a donor
product supplied the same responsibility classes, partial order, counterfactual
results, preservation and reopen predicates, and authority semantics: that
product ties exactly. Any practical advantage therefore depends on the value of
making this scientific escalation interface explicit in realistic systems, not
on a claim that centralization creates additional semantic power.

\paragraph{Negative execution history matters.}
The first execution of the revision-responsibility battery is retained because
an independently discovered post-access defect in two comparators weakened its
clean-control comparison. The defect was not patched after outcomes had been
seen; the publication-authorizing comparison is the separately frozen, disjoint
second execution. This protects the chronology, but it also emphasizes that the
wider claim rests on exact contract engineering that still needs external
validation.

\paragraph{Reconstruction quality is only partially measured.}
The current local suite checks key transition behaviors, but the deeper hypotheses about late-depth trace fidelity, reviewer localization time, and semantic mismatch require a dedicated prospective protocol.

\paragraph{Search and semantic adequacy are delegated, not solved here.}
Paper II owns route coverage/recall and Paper III owns scientific semantic projection and global reconstruction. A Paper-I success cannot be used as evidence that those capabilities work on real literature.

\paragraph{Verification remains separate.}
Search, proposal, diagnosis, and reframe mechanisms do not mint scientific authority. Paper IV owns the stronger evidence-binding and protected-verification claim.

\paragraph{Method change remains gated.}
Paper I can expose a possible method failure, but it does not authorize $M_t$ to rewrite itself. Paper V owns failure attribution, challenger construction, replay/fresh transfer, protected assurance, host promotion, and the decision to record strongest-parent subsumption instead of preserving a new operator label.
The local suite checks key transition behaviours, but the deeper hypotheses
about late-depth trace fidelity, reviewer localization time, and semantic
mismatch require a dedicated prospective protocol.

\paragraph{Adjacent capabilities are delegated, not solved.}
Route coverage and recall for open-world search, scientific semantic projection
across sources, evidence-binding and protected verification, and the evolution
of the method state itself are all separate problems with their own evidence
requirements. A success here is not evidence about any of them. In particular,
the mechanisms studied here can expose a possible method failure but do not
authorize $M_t$ to rewrite itself.

\paragraph{Literature closure is bounded rather than universal.}
The nearest-work rounds reached bounded fixed points for their exact residuals
and directly shaped the strongest parents. Because the repair-agent literature
is moving rapidly, a final pre-submission delta search and replacement of
preprints by archival versions remain required. No broad novelty statement
follows from a bounded fixed point.

\subsection{Observed limitations}

\paragraph{The hidden-formulation study remains underpowered.}
With $N=\TestCaseCount{}$ cases its achieved half-width is
\AchievedHalfWidth{}, short of the lowest registered precision tier, and it is
underpowered for its original broad primary hypothesis. Those
records, including the low root-success rates in Tables~\ref{tab:P1-T2}
and~\ref{tab:P1-T3}, are retained as negative history and are not pooled with
the powered successor. The mutation-necessity experiment addresses a narrower
prospectively frozen claim on 2,882 worlds per run; it does not retroactively
validate the earlier broader formulation.

\paragraph{Perfect success is generator-bounded.}
The governed policy attains protected success 1.0000 in both mutation-necessity
runs, and the escalation contract attains 400 of 400, an exact rate of $1.000$, on
its exact contract battery. These ceilings are credible only at the level of their deterministic
registered generators and explicit action and response vocabularies. Strong
parents, hostile ablations, the comparator-fair donor product, and the
ideal-product tie make the tested distinctions non-vacuous, but the ceiling
results may not survive perceptual ambiguity, noisy interventions, learned
proposal generation, or imperfect responsibility inference.

\paragraph{Template-level leaks exist in the local suite.}
The solvability audit identified perfect separation on fixed phrases---``The
team's framing is'' distinguishes all 44 hidden-shift cases from all 22
controls---and filename-based family separation, in which proposal and trial
stems identify decomposition cases. These leaks were documented but not repaired
in this iteration.

\paragraph{Mechanical arm generality is untested.}
Half the correct attributions, 32 of 66, rest on rules that fire on exactly one
case. Only four general relations cover three or more cases. A fresh set of
naturalistic cases is required to distinguish general mechanisms from
case-specific recognizers.

\paragraph{Blind-responder shortcuts affect half the suite.}
Thirty-three of 66 cases can be solved by a classifier that reads only resource
path stems and two fixed template phrases. The remaining 33 parent-domain,
representation and measurement cases require genuine arithmetic reasoning.
