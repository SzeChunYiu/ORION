\section{Limitations}

The theorem is scoped to six targets, three blocks, one shared binary label, the stated factor rule, and fixed support multipliers. Changing the objective can make support three beneficial. Other term counts, shared-label ranks, block grammars, routing constraints, or factor rules require new proofs.

The direct complexity result is a word-RAM upper bound. It is not a lower bound, an exponent-optimality result, or an asymptotic statement about other implementations. The available finite verifier shares some algebraic primitives with the direct solver and is not an externally independent implementation of every witness identity.

The runtime panel is small and adverse. It provides no acceleration, memory, energy, logical-depth, fault-tolerant, or hardware claim. Earlier finite chemistry observations and heuristic structural-cost reductions are not used as theorem or production-value evidence here. Patents, private manuscripts, unindexed software, post-search publications, and independent venue-level novelty judgments were not assessable in the bounded literature audit.
