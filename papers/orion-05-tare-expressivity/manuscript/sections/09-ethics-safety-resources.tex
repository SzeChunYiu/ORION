\section{Ethics, author contributions, and resource statement}

This work uses no human participants, animals, clinical data, or personal data. Its principal integrity risks are overgeneralizing a grammar-specific theorem and suppressing negative performance evidence; the manuscript addresses both by stating the exact scope and retaining every timeout.

The author is responsible for the mathematical claims, source verification, code, and final manuscript. Large language models were used for literature-search assistance, proof-audit prompts, code and prose refinement, and manuscript formatting. Mathematical statements, citations, calculations, and interpretive decisions were checked against the cited sources and underlying evidence during preparation; the author retains final responsibility for the manuscript.

No energy or carbon estimate is claimed because the records do not support a reliable life-cycle comparison. The finite checks and runtime study used ordinary CPUs.
