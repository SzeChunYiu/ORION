\section{Conclusion}

For the declared shared-Tag Pauli compilation grammar, every optimum has a support-two frame representation, and an exact two-qubit witness shows that support one is insufficient. The sharp normal form yields a direct exact $O(n^9)$ word-RAM algorithm. Finite checks corroborate the proof and implementation, but a pre-specified runtime comparison finds no production advantage because the direct implementation times out on every full-subject cell. The durable result is therefore an exact, objective-scoped normal form with a preserved negative performance outcome, not a claim of general compiler or physical-resource superiority.
