\section{Related work and contribution boundary}

The studied grammar descends from Tag-and-Restore block encoding, which owns the primitive, freely chosen anticommuting frames, shared-label construction, and associated resource tradeoffs \cite{schillo2026blockencoding}. Anticommuting-unitary partitioning, Pauli-cluster compilation, and Pauli-level compiler representations are also established \cite{izmaylov2020unitary,vandenberg2020clusters,paykin2023pcoast,yang2025phoenix,yang2026symphony}.

Support reduction itself is not new as a general idea. Phase-gadget synthesis decomposes Pauli exponentials using one- and two-qubit components \cite{cowtan2020phasegadget}, and parity-network work exploits sharing across related terms \cite{amy2018cnotphase}. Two-qubit gates are universal for quantum computation \cite{divincenzo1995universal}, while stabilizer-state normal forms are classical tools of the Pauli formalism \cite{fattal2004entanglement}. These results remove any claim that support-two building blocks, parity sharing, or two-local universality originate here. They address different objects from the grammar-specific statement proved above: the present theorem transforms auxiliary frames while preserving one fixed shared-label objective and exact optimum. Hamiltonian weight reduction and perturbative gadgets likewise change the encoded Hamiltonian, often by adding degrees of freedom or approximation, rather than proving this cost-monotone frame exchange \cite{hastings2021weight,kempe2006local}.

Current bounded searches also inspected Pauli compilers, exact and approximate synthesis, low-ancilla block encodings, fault-tolerant resource models, and compiler benchmarks. No exact equivalent of the full grammar-specific theorem was located, but a bounded search cannot establish absence or priority. The residual contribution is therefore stated descriptively: a cost-monotone normal-form proof, its sharp obstruction, and the induced exact upper bound for this declared grammar. Novelty beyond that narrow formulation remains a matter for independent expert review.
