# Manuscript surface and explanatory-sufficiency audit

Date: 2026-08-28

## Explanatory dependencies

The final manuscript follows this dependency order:

1. exact grammar and objective;
2. local correction-cost lemma;
3. parity-class subset lemma;
4. feasibility and cost of the exchange;
5. well-founded global reduction;
6. explicit sharpness instance and exhaustive lower witness;
7. constructive pair count and exact algorithm;
8. finite corroboration and adverse runtime result;
9. prior-art subtraction, limitations and meaning.

Every new symbol is defined before load-bearing use. The target-to-branch map, nonidentity and anticommutation constraints, common-syndrome set, complete normalized objective, central/noncentral multipliers, local factor cost and support restriction are explicit. The counterexample now includes the actual target pairs, support-two witness, component cost and enumeration reductions; no private lookup is required to understand the `5<6` statement.

## Surface scan classes

The final PDF text and exact review-archive names/content are scanned separately for:

- internal paper, experiment and result codes;
- internal round/lane/decision strings;
- repository names, paths and filenames;
- issue, pull-request, branch, commit and workflow history;
- hashes and machine-only package terminals on reviewer-facing surfaces;
- undefined single-letter internal labels;
- author name, email and account identifiers.

The allowed mathematical symbols `A`, `A'`, `S`, `F`, `N_pair(n)` and `T(n)` are defined in the paper. No internal `R*`, `H*`, `B*`, Q-series or project identifier remains on the manuscript surface. The deterministic archive builder applies the corresponding exact entry-name and content scan before writing the review ZIP.

## Typography and local intelligibility

- Anonymous authorship is present in source; PDF metadata carries the paper title and identifies the author only as `Anonymous authors`.
- Hyperlinks are hidden; no colored boxes appear.
- All references resolve.
- The evidence table is locally intelligible and contains the adverse row.
- The abstract contains the main scope, assumptions, theorem, algorithmic bound, adverse result and non-claims.
- The conclusion does not widen the abstract.
- The AI-use disclosure is explicit and assigns responsibility to the author.
