# Headline decision proofs

Date: 2026-08-28

## HDP-1 — Cost-nonincreasing support-two transformation

- **Exact claim:** Every feasible configuration in the declared six-target, three-block grammar can be transformed without greater cost so that all six frame Paulis have support at most two.
- **Decisive evidence:** complete normalized objective; analytic correction-exchange lemma; analytic zero-sum-subset lemma; strictly decreasing total-frame-support measure.
- **Strongest plausible alternative:** a support-three coordinate set may be irreducibly coupled to frame anticommutation and the common shared-operator syndrome.
- **Discriminating test:** for every frame of support at least three, exhibit a nonempty proper subset of at most two coordinates with zero total contribution to both parity constraints and show the correction increase is bounded by the refunded frame charge.
- **Boundary:** exact declared grammar and objective only; no other factor rule, multiplier family, label rank or block count.
- **Target decision axis:** technical validity. The proof clears this axis after the objective and feasibility repairs.

## HDP-2 — Sharp support threshold

- **Exact claim:** Support one is not uniformly sufficient; the displayed two-qubit paired instance has support-two cost 5 and support-one minimum 6.
- **Decisive evidence:** explicit targets; explicit frame/shared-operator witness with component cost `2+2+1`; exhaustive enumeration of 12 ordered support-one pairs per block and all reduced constant choices.
- **Strongest plausible alternative:** an omitted target order, central choice, syndrome orientation or compatible shared operator attains cost 5 with support-one frames.
- **Discriminating test:** justify the global branch-swap quotient, analytic central-choice minimization and minimum-weight shared-operator reduction; rerun the exhaustive enumerator.
- **Boundary:** one paired two-qubit instance; not a general circuit or resource lower bound.
- **Target decision axis:** theorem sharpness. The executable check and blind validity review clear this axis at its bounded scope.

## HDP-3 — Constructive exact-solvability bound

- **Exact claim:** Enumerating three ordered anticommuting support-two frame pairs gives an exact `O(n^9)` word-RAM algorithm with `O(n^3)` working memory.
- **Decisive evidence:** exact ordered-pair count; active union of at most nine coordinates; 64-syndrome shared-operator optimization; linear target preprocessing.
- **Strongest plausible alternative:** candidate scoring hides an additional `n` factor, or a compatible minimum shared operator requires support outside the active union.
- **Discriminating test:** prove active-union confinement and list every candidate-scoring operation after preprocessing.
- **Boundary:** six explicit `n`-qubit targets, constant target count, word-RAM representation; bit accounting may add polylogarithmic factors.
- **Target decision axis:** formal algorithmic consequence. No same-problem asymptotic improvement or exponent optimality is claimed.

## HDP-4 — Adverse implementation result

- **Exact claim:** The pre-specified 120-attempt panel produced 108 completions and 12 timeouts; the support-two implementation timed out in six three-qubit and six full-subject cells, so no measured performance improvement was established.
- **Decisive evidence:** immutable raw rows; pre-measurement design; recorded environment; deterministic sanitized aggregation.
- **Strongest plausible alternative:** timeouts were dropped, rescheduled, or excluded from the decision, or completed solvers disagreed on exact cost.
- **Discriminating test:** require exactly 120 unique scheduled rows, retain every timeout, recompute counts and cellwise costs, and apply the frozen positive rule without retuning.
- **Boundary:** recorded implementation, subjects, machine and time limit; not a hardware, energy, memory-improvement or general compiler claim.
- **Target decision axis:** practical utility. The result is negative.

## HDP-5 — Venue fit

- **Exact decision:** The paper is scientifically coherent at its fixed-model boundary but does not meet the demonstrated significance burden for PRX Quantum or *Quantum*.
- **Decisive evidence:** hostile prior-art subtraction; fixed grammar and nonphysical objective; no same-problem asymptotic improvement; adverse runtime study; explicit non-claims.
- **Strongest plausible alternative:** the exact theorem and sharp obstruction alone constitute a very significant subfield advance.
- **Discriminating test:** independent target-aware review must identify either a broadly reusable theoretical consequence or a recognized compiler/resource benefit. The frozen reviews identify neither.
- **Boundary:** simulated fit assessment, not a journal decision or rejection.
- **Target decision axis:** significance and breadth. A specialist retarget remains plausible but requires an author-selected exact venue contract.
