## Figure decision

No figure is required for the current eight-page formal note. A proof-flow diagram would duplicate a short linear proof; a runtime chart would visually amplify a small adverse panel without adding inferential content; and a support-two cartoon could be mistaken for general two-local circuit synthesis. Omission is therefore evidence-aware, not a missing-asset placeholder.

## Table decision

The single evidence-layer table earns its place by preventing evidence layers from being conflated. It maps each evidence type to one supported inference and retains the negative performance result. No table value is calculated independently of bound evidence.

## Main-versus-support allocation

- Main: complete theorem proof, explicit sharpness instance/witness, pair count, complexity and adverse aggregate.
- Anonymous review archive: executable checker, direct solver, sharpness enumeration, all sanitized runtime rows and their deterministic aggregate.
- Omitted: earlier chemistry observations, heuristic savings, broader regime taxonomy and all internal workflow/custody history.
