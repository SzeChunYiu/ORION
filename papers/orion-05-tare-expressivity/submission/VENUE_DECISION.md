# Venue decision — ORION-05

**Target: _Quantum_.** Not PRX Quantum.

The routing instruction allowed PRX Quantum as a stretch target conditional on
the significance gate being clearly met. It is not met, for reasons internal to
the paper rather than to its execution quality:

- The result is bounded to one frozen six-term grammar under one unit
  support-count objective. The abstract states plainly that no full-circuit,
  hardware, or global block-encoding optimality is claimed.
- The headline theorem is a support normal form for that grammar. It is exact
  and machine-checked, but it does not change what a practitioner outside this
  compilation family can do.
- Two of the paper's four scientific movements are refutations of its own
  earlier closed forms. That is the honest shape of the work, and it is
  publishable, but it is not the broad-impact shape PRX Quantum selects for.

Submitting to PRX Quantum on a significance case the manuscript itself declines
to make would be a predictable desk rejection and would cost a review cycle.

_Quantum_ is the right fit. It publishes rigorous, bounded, exactly-verified
results in quantum information and quantum computation, it is comfortable with
negative and refutation-carrying content, and it does not require a
broad-impact claim. The paper's machine-checked all-`n` exchange theorem, its
complete local audit, and its explicitly retained refutation ladder are all
directly in scope.

**Fallback.** npj Quantum Information, if _Quantum_ declines on scope. The
paper should not be reshaped to chase either venue's framing; the claim ceiling
is set by the ledger, not by the target.

**Article type.** Regular research article. Current compiled length is 7 pages
at 11pt/1in margins, which is short for the venue but proportionate to a single
bounded theorem plus its refutation ladder.
