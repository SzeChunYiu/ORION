## Abstract

A self-improving research system can generate useful changes while remaining unable to authorize its own adoption. P5 separates diagnosis, proposal origin, causal credit, transfer, negative history, evaluator custody, and adoption authority in a dynamic evolution state. It absorbs automated repair, self-improving agents, anytime-valid selection, causal intervention evaluation, and constitutional safety. We prove a reflexive-custody impossibility: if the candidate controls proposal, evaluator, threshold, and adoption, genuine improvement and evaluator gaming may be observationally indistinguishable. We give sufficient protected-adoption conditions for the declared workflow class and a well-founded evolution law. SWE-bench Verified and fresh externally held issues will compare no-edit, direct self-edit, strong donor systems, and protected ORION under matched budgets.
