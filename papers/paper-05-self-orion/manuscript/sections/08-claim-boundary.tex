\section{Nearest-work and claim boundary}

Self-ORION does not claim the first self-improving agent, the first evolutionary program search system, the first agent archive, the first issue-centric self-improvement state, or the first failure-driven adaptation loop. The programme explicitly absorbs mechanisms from ADAS, Darwin G\"odel Machine, A Self-Improving Coding Agent, AlphaEvolve, AI Co-Scientist evolution, ADIAS-style persistent issue state and evaluation-integrity research.

The remaining P5 contribution is an authority architecture for self-improvement rather than ownership of self-modification itself. A failure-governed method-evolution fibre retains persistent negative history, performs causal discrimination and invention-readiness checks, executes challengers in isolation, separates replay from fresh transfer, places assurance outside candidate custody, and gives the internal controller no primitive that can convert its own recommendation into adoption. Method generation and method adoption are therefore different scientific-status transitions.

Prospective V3 work sharpens that separation before any broader self-revision experiment. Competing revision-responsibility hypotheses may remain ambiguous or unresolved, and required interface-adequacy checks may block escalation from an observed failure to a model, method or objective rewrite while a narrower observation/measurement/representation repair remains indicated. The implementation can nominate a claim-relative minimal candidate mechanic for later testing, but the responsibility report, interface report and revision gate are structurally non-authorizing. They do not replace the P5 invention-readiness gate and cannot grant adoption or promotion.

A second successor tranche makes three additional authority separations explicit. Internal computation is represented as a costed epistemic action, so a hard verification obligation can require an expensive check even when a cheaper LLM/planning action has greater scalar expected value. An uncertainty-containment envelope can restrict where a model or claim is admissible without rewriting the model or certifying it as true. Social corroboration is evidence-bound: distinct worker identities are not counted as independent when their upstream support overlaps, and unresolved hidden contributors prevent exclusive causal credit. T7 composes these reports into a read-only next-step recommendation while preserving hard-obligation precedence and denying revision, adoption, promotion, merge and global-stop authority.

These contracts yield the paper's strongest current positive invariant: \textbf{Self-ORION's registered internal reasoning path is non-self-promoting}. A diagnosis, candidate mechanic, computation choice, containment result, social-evidence report or composed recommendation can change what should be investigated next, but cannot by itself change the adopted scientific method. The final status transition remains protected and host-owned.

The diagnostic hidden-cause archive makes this invariant non-vacuous. glm-5.2 attributes 21/24 cases correctly and retains three real mistakes. P5 does not relabel those mistakes as successes. Instead, they instantiate the design condition the architecture is meant to tolerate: a fallible internal diagnosis still lacks adoption authority. This is an error-containment property of the registered control structure, not evidence that the diagnostic model is accurate enough for autonomous self-improvement.

The prospective V3 experiment remains a separate, protected-custody revision-level campaign. Its development-only cause-confusable panel, matched comparators, feedback interventions and separate scorer establish that the future study can be executed and audited. The confirmatory preflight remains \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} while final subject/split/evaluator/protected-suite identities and load-bearing comparator bindings are absent. That blocks a transferable-improvement superiority claim; it does not erase the already established non-self-promotion architecture.

The former Shadow Mechanics manuscript is therefore not a separate scientific contribution. Its mechanic representation belongs to Paper I, while its failure-to-method and self-driving development theory is absorbed here; the historical packet remains only as technical provenance under \texttt{research/technical-companions/mechanics-of-mechanics-v1/}.

\section{Conclusion}

Paper V separates three operations that self-improving systems often collapse: \emph{diagnose a problem}, \emph{propose a change}, and \emph{adopt the change}. Self-ORION allows the first two to be automated while keeping the third behind a protected status-transition boundary. Negative history remains immutable evidence; recurrence is not equated with cause; unresolved responsibility is allowed to remain unresolved; invention waits for ordinary explanations and transfer alternatives to be challenged; challengers run in isolation; replay and fresh transfer remain different evidentiary obligations; protected assurance is outside candidate write authority; and the final adoption decision remains in host custody.

The strongest supported result is therefore positive even though fresh-task self-improvement superiority is not yet measured: \textbf{within the registered architecture, fallible self-diagnosis and method proposal are structurally non-self-authorizing}. The controller exposes no self-merge route, and the T1--T7 successor mechanics culminate in a recommendation that deliberately lacks revision, adoption, promotion, merge and global-stop authority. Local hostile tests exercise the authority boundaries, and the retained 21/24 attribution archive demonstrates that the separation is relevant under actual diagnostic error rather than only under perfect self-knowledge.

This changes the role of the pending performance campaign. H1--H4 are no longer needed to justify the architectural contribution; they test the additional prospective question of whether the protected architecture also improves fresh development outcomes relative to strong self-improving baselines while reducing harmful repair, evaluator gaming and false broad revision. A positive future campaign would add performance authority to the already established governance result; a null campaign would leave the non-self-promotion invariant intact.

P5 therefore contributes the self-modification instance of the broader ORION status-transition programme: \textbf{a locally generated diagnosis or proposal may guide research without acquiring authority to promote itself into an adopted scientific method}. Claim-to-evidence bindings for this scoped result live in \texttt{evidence/CLAIM\_LEDGER\_V1.md}.
