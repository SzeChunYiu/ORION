\section{Ethics, safety, and reproducibility}

\subsection{Ethical considerations}
This paper studies governed method evolution for research software. The frozen local falsifier and the diagnostic attribution suite use constructed hidden-cause cases. They do not involve human-subject records or personally identifiable information. No live provider campaign was executed in this revision.

\subsection{Safety and authority}
Self-ORION has no self-merge primitive. The strongest internal terminal remains a host-only promotion recommendation. Isolated self-certification ablations, if ever executed, are forbidden on the production/main authority path. Negative, null, blocked and cannot-check outcomes are retained rather than dropped.

\subsection{Reproducibility and data/code availability}
Source, protocol, the diagnostic JSONL, and the table generator are version-controlled in \texttt{SzeChunYiu/ORION}. Persistent DOI assignment remains pending publication.

Archived-result regeneration, without executing a live system, is:

\begin{verbatim}
make paper05-results
\end{verbatim}

which runs \texttt{python -m orion.study.p5.tables}. The command recomputes 21/24 from \texttt{evidence/glm-5.2-attribution/results.jsonl}, writes Table P5-3 and the residual-error ledger, and writes \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} stubs for P5-2, P5-4, P5-5, P5-6, P5-7 and Tables P5-T2/P5-T3. A rewrite of the archive that hides the three errors is refused (exit 2). A missing archive is \texttt{CANNOT\_CHECK} (exit 3). Exact commands are in \texttt{papers/paper-05-self-orion/REPRODUCE.md}.
