\section{Successor interface: failure episodes, scoped failure knowledge, and atomized invention gates}
\label{sec:p5-successor-failure-discovery}

The successor failure programme separates two records that are easy to conflate. A \emph{failure episode} preserves what happened: source or execution identity, chronology, observed failure mode, propagated effect, candidate causes, recovery attempts, residuals, and evidence available at the time. \emph{Failure knowledge} is stronger: it states which conclusion may be reused, the evidence and responsibility attribution supporting it, the context coordinates that must remain the same, and the changes that reopen the question. The episode can exist without the reusable exclusion.

This makes the recurrence--cause boundary operational. Repeatedly observing a signature can justify a recurrence hypothesis, but a reusable method lesson still needs responsibility evidence and an applicability contract. Every load-bearing context coordinate has an explicit role; required-same mismatch makes the old exclusion inapplicable, semantic/regime change may reopen it, and unclassified context cannot make a failure globally applicable by omission. Reopening removes an old prohibition only for the changed regime---it is not positive evidence that the new method works.

The prospective failure-memory study found the strongest standard composition---truth-maintenance/nogood context, responsibility/refutation gating, semantic transport, provenance, and defeater-directed inquiry---matched the full ORION scoped-failure arm on all 32 invented cases. Failure history was useful, but no new scientific inference calculus was needed. The zero-error regime-invention programme reached the analogous disposition: representational transitions were required on registered positive worlds, but a verified regime-revision parent matched the full ORION system on primary and disjoint replication. Self-ORION must therefore retain parent subsumption and negative results rather than preserve new-operator labels for architectural convenience.

Historical discovery morphology and failure atlases can organize challenger families and expose discriminators, but remain design evidence. The adoption path stays the one defined in this paper: diagnose the issue, absorb known mechanisms, construct a challenger, execute it in isolation, require replay/fresh-transfer evidence where relevant, pass protected assurance, and leave final disposition to the external host. A bounded atom study can shrink the challenger space; neither an atom label nor a failure-memory improvement acquires self-promotion authority.
