Four things are closed exactly:

  and only if the independence label is constant on every internal-observable fibre;
- an exact **control-cut obstruction** — if independent and commonly controlled

  load-bearing governance observation outside the programme's control closure.

mechanisms. The question it asks instead is when a locally valid source-domain
judgment actually satisfies the different *scientific obligation* a heterogeneous

## Frozen boundary

concession.** The theorem explains why the final external-authority gate cannot be
discharged by recursively adding more programme-internal evidence. Running more of
our own system, with more agents or more replays, cannot close it — the multiplicity
boundary says so exactly.

Four things stay open, and none may be represented as achieved: actual
institution-disjoint adjudication; external expert agreement on the 20 real-domain
cases; conflict-of-interest and tie-break execution; and broad autonomous-science
population authority.

and are not superseded by the theorem — the theorem sits on top of them and explains
the shape of what is missing.
