## Donor ownership that must remain visible

ORION-18 treats as donor/pre-existing: deontic/action/input-output logics; authorization/trust-management languages and proof systems; delegation/revocation; UCON/ongoing authorization; typed effects and trace policies; FAVA evidence-backed authorization; multi-authority and authorization-propagation systems; stale/commit-time authorization control; agent abstention; provenance/source attribution/execution provenance; runtime shielding; ORION ORION-14 scientific-authority promotion and ORION-15 protected self-promotion boundary.

## Allowed headline

> Cross-domain scientific authority requires full evidence-to-obligation typing: valid permission or success in one domain does not discharge another domain's scientific obligation without an explicit protected coercion, and a shared calculus is behaviorally equivalent to an equally typed donor product when their semantics are identical.

## Disallowed headlines

- “The first theory of agent authority.”
- “ORION-18 invents ongoing/commit-time authorization.”
- “A centralized authority calculus is more expressive than independent gates.”
- “ORION-18 outperforms UCON, ETAS, FAVA, multi-authority governance, abstention or provenance systems.”

Separate-paper superiority requires a frozen discriminator versus the ideal donor-product baseline, potentially on interface consistency, proof/audit cost, revocation coverage or implementation-defect rate.
