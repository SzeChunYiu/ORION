\subsection{Three deliberately mixed outcome classes}

The claim below rests on three outcome classes that were not selected for
agreement, and two of them are adverse. They are stated first so that the
positive result is read against them rather than in place of them.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Real same-information accessibility intervention.} Breast-cancer
and digits are positive; \textbf{Wine is retained as a null cell}. It is not
dropped, reweighted or explained away, and no aggregate is reported that would
absorb it.
\item \textbf{Protected Qwen scaling hypothesis.} The registered
monotone-scaling hypothesis is an \textbf{authoritative negative}. It was
registered before the outcome and is \emph{not} repaired after the fact: the
hypothesis stands as it was written, and it failed.
\item \textbf{Cross-domain causal diagnostic.} The positive result, reported
below.
\end{enumerate}

A registered hypothesis that fails, and a cell that shows nothing, are the two
results most easily lost between a repository and a paper. Neither changes the
diagnostic result that follows; both change how far it may be carried.

\subsection{Main result: the diagnostic beats generic compute escalation}

Five task families were frozen across two qualitatively distinct domains. The
diagnostic selects among \textsc{information}, \textsc{accessibility} and
\textsc{computation} from development and probe intervention responses, under a
prospectively fixed lowest-cost-meeting-quality-target rule; the protected causal
gold is recomputed independently from held-out intervention outcomes.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\hline
policy & families diagnosed correctly & false compute escalations \\
\hline
registered diagnostic & \textbf{4/5} (0.8) & \textbf{0} \\
generic \textsc{uncertainty-escalate-compute} & 1/5 (0.2) & 4 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The second column is the one that carries the argument. The generic policy does
not merely score lower; it escalates compute in four of the five families,
including those where the binding constraint was information or accessibility and
where more compute cannot help. Diagnosing \emph{which} constraint binds is what
avoids paying for the wrong intervention, which is the paper's thesis stated as a
cost.

The result is bounded by design. The held-out digits accessibility cell remains
\textsc{cannot-check} because the registered threshold does not transport to it, and
that cell is reported rather than dropped. Five task families across two domains
is a small, frozen grid: it establishes that the two policies separate here, in
the predicted direction, not that the diagnostic transports generally.

\subsection{Decision-rule refinement: V2 gate failure and its V3 repair}

The registered V1 instrument above is not revised. Two further decision rules
were registered against the same frozen cells, arms, targets (D-A $0.965$, D-I
$0.95$, executable $1.0$), costs, ensemble size $R=24$ and draw seeds; only the
rule for deciding whether an arm meets its target changed.

V2 replaced the V1 point estimate with a hard threshold on the $R=24$ ensemble
mean. It raised diagnosis accuracy to $1.0$ with probe/protected agreement on all
five cells, but its terminal was
\textsc{p9\_causal\_diagnostic\_transport\_v2\_gate\_not\_met}: half-draw decision
stability failed on the digits-accessibility (D-A) cell, where protected half-1
mean $0.9662$ crosses the $0.965$ target while half-0 ($0.9625$) and the full mean
($0.9644$) do not. Stage-1 attribution localises this to exactly one stage --- the
decision margin is $0.001161$ against a single-split binomial standard deviation
of $0.009840$, a noise-to-margin ratio of $8.47$ --- so the rule was thresholding
inside its own noise band. The repair channel is bitwise lossless; splits,
targets and pipeline were not implicated.

V3 applies uncertainty-aware target satisfaction: an arm reaches its target iff
its ensemble $\mathrm{LCB}_{95} = \text{mean} - 1.96\,\mathrm{sd}/\sqrt{n}$ does,
uniformly across every cell, split and half, with halves deciding from their own
data only. \textbf{No target was relaxed}; in particular the D-A target of
$0.965$ is unchanged. The terminal is
\textsc{p9\_causal\_diagnostic\_transport\_v3\_supported} with no failing clauses:
diagnosis accuracy $1.0$ against $0.2$ for the generic policy, zero false compute
escalations against four, mean registered cost regret $0.0$, agreement on all five
cells, and half-draw stability on both digits cells. The run is deterministic ---
executed twice with byte-identical output.

Crucially, \textbf{the D-A protected cell is not converted to a pass}. Both its
probe and protected lower confidence bounds fall below $0.965$, so all four half
decisions return \textsc{cannot-check}. Under the frozen V1 gold rule D-A remains
\textsc{cannot-check}; what V3 changes is that the abstention is now
decision-stable and mechanistically grounded rather than a threshold artifact. The
V2 gate failure stands in the record as a failure. The Qwen scaling negative is
untouched: it is confirmed authoritative and, on recovery, shown unsolvable within
the frozen family, because the frontier claim needs at least two scales with
measurable contrast while only $0.5$B is non-degenerate and its delta at the
primary budget is negative ($-0.141$); $1.5$B and $3$B collapse to a constant
label in both arms.

\subsection{The invariant-orbit coverage gate: no representation successor}

A representation successor was gated on the invariant-orbit error floor being
computed first. It was, before anything was built. Mapping each instance to its
typed invariant profile partitions the protected split into seven orbit cells, one
mixed. The orbit-majority ceiling --- the most any isomorphism-invariant rule can
score --- is $112/128 = 0.875$, against the recorded frozen model score of
$96/128 = 0.750$, leaving an apparent recoverable gap of $16$ cases. Model
predictions are constant within every cell, independently confirming the deployed
path is invariant.

That gap is nevertheless not a representation deficiency. Training and protected
share \emph{no orbit cell}: development is $96/96$ in-orbit at accuracy $0.958$
while protected is $0/128$ in-orbit at $0.750$, so the drop is the in-orbit to
out-of-orbit transition rather than overfitting, and the ceiling is an upper bound
on invariant rules rather than a reachable target. A coverage/floor tradeoff makes
this structural: refining a partition cannot raise coverage and coarsening it
cannot lower the floor, so the one lever a representation successor pulls is ruled
out by the measurement. The gate verdict is
\textsc{gate\_evaluated\_\_representation\_successor\_not\_indicated}, and the
successor was not run. Every frozen disposition is preserved unchanged; no
\textsc{cannot-check} is converted and no accuracy is re-derived.

\subsection{M1 separates information from computation}

Across the frozen view ladder, M1 never exceeds its empirical deterministic ceiling. Surface and topology both attain their ceilings (\MOneSurfaceAccuracy/\MOneSurfaceCeiling{} and \MOneTopologyAccuracy/\MOneTopologyCeiling{}, respectively), while typed reaches \MOneTypedAccuracy{} at a ceiling of \MOneTypedCeiling{}. Richer views expose more information but also reveal a residual: current obtains \MOneCurrentAccuracy{} against an overall ceiling of \MOneCurrentCeiling{}, and semantic obtains \MOneSemanticAccuracy{} against \MOneSemanticCeiling{}.

The task decomposition identifies the source of that gap. Semantic mechanic ranking reaches \MOneSemanticMechanicAccuracy{}, so the visible typed relation/history coordinates are sufficient and exploited on the mechanic task. In contrast, semantic affine gluing remains at \MOneSemanticGluingAccuracy{} despite an exact task ceiling of \MOneSemanticGluingCeiling{}; current gluing is likewise \MOneCurrentGluingAccuracy{}. This is the pattern expected from a computation residual rather than an information deficit on the frozen benchmark. The general idea of separating representation from downstream reasoning failure has close parents \citep{liem2026temporal,anonymous2026chokepoints}; our claim is limited to this prospectively controlled instance.

\subsection{Explicit computation closes the D0 residual}

A5 confirms that the residual need not motivate a richer neural architecture. With only typed endpoints and no local map values, the payload-only affine procedure remains unresolved (full-task accuracy \AFiveTypedAccuracy{}). Once the current view exposes the local affine maps, exact cycle composition reaches \AFiveCurrentAccuracy{}. Thus the operation missed by M1 is exactly computable from visible coordinates in this D0 family.

A2 and A4 show a complementary pattern. Topology alone gives \ATwoTopologyAccuracy{} on the relation hostile pair, while typed relation semantics yield \ATwoTypedAccuracy{} using the explicit relation selector. On the history family, current-state information gives \AFourCurrentAccuracy{}, while the semantic view with admitted scoped failure history yields \AFourSemanticAccuracy{}. These results establish that the coordinates are load-bearing on their exact hostile pairs, not that neural mechanic or memory modules are required.

\subsection{Typed relational structure transfers across a whole held-out domain}
\label{sec:d1-hostile}

D1 supplies the external procedural-transfer test. The typed relational arm reaches \DOneTypedAccuracy{} protected accuracy on transactional workflows, compared with \DOneUntypedAccuracy{} for untyped pair structure, \DOneSerializedAccuracy{} for the same-information typed serialization, and \DOneTranscriptAccuracy{} for the reminted transcript bag. The \DOneTypedMinusSerialized{} difference against the same-information serialization is reported but withdrawn as a margin; Section~\ref{sec:d1-hostile} gives the hostile check that withdrew it. The \DOneTypedMinusTranscript{} figure against transcript is reported separately and is not a transfer margin: the transcript comparator answers \textsc{aligned} on all 128 protected cases, so the quantity is $1-\text{prior}(\textsc{aligned})$. Its content is that a surface-action view has an empty transferable vocabulary over a whole-domain holdout, which is a stronger and narrower statement than outperforming it. The typed relational arm also preserves \DOneTypedDoubleAccuracy{} accuracy on double corruptions and \DOneTypedUnresolvedAccuracy{} on source-insufficient \textsc{unresolved} cases.

The same-information contrast was originally the important one for interpretation, and a hostile check has since withdrawn it. The serialized arm does contain the same typed semantic fields --- all 512 of its token lists decode back to their typed payload exactly, which we have now verified rather than assumed --- so the \DOneTypedMinusSerialized{} difference cannot be described as simple information addition. But it also cannot be described as explicit relational comparison making those fields more useful, because the serialized arm's protected answers are a function of the symbol alphabet rather than of the semantics. Applying a bijection to the value alphabet --- 220 atoms to 220 distinct images, every gold label preserved --- moves that arm from 0.75 to 0.50 accuracy and from 0.5 to 0.0 informedness, changing 32 of 128 protected predictions, while renaming nothing but its feature keys. Its published figure is one point of that orbit. Carrying the information is not the same as answering from it, and this comparator does not answer from it; the difference therefore measures which symbols were drawn. The surviving relational margin is the typed-versus-untyped contrast, whose two arms are invariant under the same transform. Because the general fact that serialization can affect reasoning is established prior work \citep{lo2026serialization}, we do not claim a universal representation theorem from D1.

As a post-hoc robustness analysis of the already-frozen D1 endpoint, we compared the selected typed relational arm to each comparator on the identical 128 protected cases. Against transcript, the accuracy difference is \DOneTypedMinusTranscript{} with paired bootstrap 95\% interval [\DOneTranscriptPairedCILow, \DOneTranscriptPairedCIHigh]; all \DOneTranscriptDiscordantWins{} discordant cases favor typed relational and none favor transcript (exact McNemar $p=\DOneTranscriptMcNemarP$). Because the transcript arm is constant on these cases, that comparison is against a majority-class predictor and should be read as such rather than as a contrast between two fitted views. Against the same-information typed serialization, the difference is \DOneTypedMinusSerialized{} with paired interval [\DOneSerializedPairedCILow, \DOneSerializedPairedCIHigh] and discordance \DOneSerializedDiscordantWins{}--\DOneSerializedDiscordantLosses{} ($p=\DOneSerializedMcNemarP$). Against the untyped pair arm, the paired interval is [\DOneUntypedPairedCILow, \DOneUntypedPairedCIHigh] with discordance \DOneUntypedDiscordantWins{}--\DOneUntypedDiscordantLosses{} ($p=\DOneUntypedMcNemarP$). These intervals and exact tests were not preregistered primary endpoints; they quantify the frozen case-level predictions without changing the terminal.
