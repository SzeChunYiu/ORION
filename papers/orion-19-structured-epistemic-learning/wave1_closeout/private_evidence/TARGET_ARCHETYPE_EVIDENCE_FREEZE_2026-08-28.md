# Wave-1 target, archetype, and evidence freeze

- Exact target: Transactions on Machine Learning Research, Research Paper, initial submission.
- Policy date: observed active 2026-08-28.
- Dominant archetype: computational/ML empirical methods study.
- Secondary archetypes: diagnostic instrument; exact executable discriminators; negative-result integration.
- Intended reader: ML researchers working on evaluation, test-time computation, representation, selective decisions, and reproducibility.
- Acceptance objective used for simulation: accurate and convincing evidence for the claims, a clear argument, original archival contribution, in-scope work, and interest to at least some TMLR readers. Novelty, broad impact, and state-of-the-art performance are not imported as ordinary acceptance gates.

## Frozen primary evidence hierarchy

1. The original five-family prospective diagnostic remains the headline authority: four correct protected dispositions versus one for generic compute escalation; zero versus four false compute escalations.
2. The held-out digit accessibility family remains indeterminate under the original target. No retuning is authorized.
3. Corrected seven-coordinate accounting preserves the five decisions but does not define a scalar exchange rate.
4. The later redraw-ensemble follow-up may be reported only as a non-promoting stability analysis because its own half-sample gate failed.
5. The real-data accessibility study retains breast-cancer and digits positives and the Wine null.
6. The Qwen2.5 monotone scaling hypothesis remains negative.
7. The hostile symbol-reminting result withdraws the older same-information serialization contrast.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed: a bounded procedure can locate missing information, inaccessible representation, or insufficient computation better than the registered generic compute-escalation foil on five fixed families.

Not allowed: universal failure diagnosis; universal representation advantage; universal accessibility benefit; a general anti-scaling law; agent/LLM transfer; physical cost optimality; population prevalence; top-tier authority.

## Reader-surface rule

The PDF, cover letter, availability statement, source archive, and reviewer archive use scientific descriptions only. Private paper/study labels, repository transport, paths, internal filenames, checksums, machine terminals, and development-history language remain in this private audit layer.
