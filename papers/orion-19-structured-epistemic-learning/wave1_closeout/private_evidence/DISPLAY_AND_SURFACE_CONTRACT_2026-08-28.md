# Display and surface contract

## Main display

- Reader question: which registered intervention is selected on probe data, and what disposition is independently supported on protected data?
- Scientific unit: fixed task family.
- Object: five-family disposition table.
- Allowed inference: four of five exact family-level matches; zero versus four false compute escalations.
- Prohibited inference: population prevalence, statistical superiority over a sampled family population, universal diagnostic accuracy, or generic anti-compute superiority.
- Data object: the sanitized five-family table in the anonymous review materials.
- Caption denominator: all five families, including the indeterminate protected family.
- Accessibility: text table with explicit labels; no color dependency.

## Resource display decision

The full seven-coordinate matched ledger is kept in reviewer materials rather than a dense main-text table. Main text reports the two interpretation-changing directions and the zero-dominance finding. Coordinates are not scalarized.

## Secondary outcome display decision

Real-data positive/null cells, the Qwen negative, the hostile reminting result, and the failed follow-up gate are reported in prose because the reader task is disposition and boundary recognition, not a continuous-trend estimate. The reviewer evidence table retains exact values.

## Surface checks required

- Every standalone abstract/title/table/caption term is reader-defined.
- No unexplained private label or acronym.
- No internal repository, branch, workflow, issue, pull request, machine terminal, path, filename, or checksum in reader-facing prose or archive payloads.
- No author identity in anonymous artifacts.
- Every page visually inspected; table legibility and final-page spill checked separately.
