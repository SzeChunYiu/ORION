## Data and code availability

The anonymous review materials accompanying this submission contain the complete reader-facing primary-family outcome table, corrected matched resource vectors, all secondary positive, null, negative, adverse, and indeterminate outcomes discussed in the manuscript, and a standard-library verifier that recomputes the reported aggregate counts and checks preservation of the limiting results.

The accompanying source archive contains the complete anonymous manuscript source, bibliography, and journal style. The original protected records and full execution provenance will be included in the public research artifact after peer review. No restricted, personal, or third-party data are used; the only real datasets are the openly distributed scikit-learn breast-cancer, digits, and Wine datasets.
