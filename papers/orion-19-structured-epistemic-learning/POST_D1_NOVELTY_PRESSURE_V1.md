# ORION-19 post-D1 novelty pressure V1

This note is a claim-boundary input, not programme-wide novelty authority. It was written after the official D1 workflow result but before final paper promotion and does not alter any frozen experiment.

## Official D1 fact under review

On the frozen held-out `transactional_workflows` domain:

- reminted transcript bag: `0.25` accuracy;
- untyped pair/topology: `0.90625`;
- typed relational comparison: `1.00`;
- same-information typed canonical serialization: `0.50`.

The typed relational result also reaches `1.00` on protected double corruptions and explicit `UNRESOLVED` cases. Official workflow coordinates match the pre-artifact independent expectation with zero material discrepancies. Merge authority remains separate until PR #519 passes its exact-head gates.

## Claims that current literature already owns

ORION-19 must **not** claim the following as general novelty:

- relational inductive biases can improve relational/OOD generalization;
- explicitly relational architectures or bottlenecks can support compositional reasoning;
- structured representations do not automatically yield compositional generalization;
- representation choice can change generalization even when underlying task content is preserved;
- graph/relational structure can transfer across domains;
- serializing a natively structured object can create a performance penalty even when the same entries/information remain present.

Particularly direct pressure comes from current work on compositional-generalization conditions, relational concept/bottleneck models, and the 2026 `Serialization Friction` study of native structured layouts versus same-entry 1D serialization. These sources remove any broad `structure beats serialization` novelty phrase.

## Surviving bounded D1 residual

The candidate contribution is the **specific prospectively controlled method-coordinate discriminator**, not the general principle:

> A source-grounded procedural-method benchmark with reminted surface identities, exact typed method coordinates, explicit source-insufficient `UNRESOLVED`, a protected whole-domain holdout, unseen combinations of coordinate corruptions, and a same-information serialization control exposes a large representation-dependent transfer gap under one frozen classical-model protocol.

This remains a benchmark/methodology + bounded empirical contribution. It is not a universal representation theorem and not a new graph architecture.

## Strongest allowed wording before final M1/A2-A4 integration

Allowed:

> On the frozen D1 benchmark, the typed relational comparison arm transfers perfectly to the held-out procedural domain while the same-information serialized arm does not; this demonstrates that, for these method coordinates and this protocol, making the relevant relations explicit changes what the selected classical learner generalizes.

Not allowed:

- `typed relational structure is universally better than text/serialization`;
- `ORION discovered serialization friction`;
- `graphs solve cross-domain reasoning`;
- `the result proves a new neural substrate`;
- `the result generalizes to natural scientific papers`;
- `the result establishes compositional generalization generally`.

## Paper consequence

If M1/A2-A4 complete as independently expected, the paper's scientific contribution should be framed as a **controlled decomposition methodology plus two bounded findings**:

1. exact information coordinates determine what is identifiable, and explicit inference can close some computation residuals without neural escalation;
2. on one exact procedural transfer benchmark, explicit relational comparison materially outperforms a same-information serialization under protected whole-domain transfer.

The novelty claim is therefore the exact experimental object/protocol/combination after donor subtraction, not ownership of relational learning, serialization effects, or OOD generalization.
