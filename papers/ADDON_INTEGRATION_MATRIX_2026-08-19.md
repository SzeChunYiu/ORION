# Discovery/failure add-on publication integration matrix

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Scope:** recursive bounded atom studies, source-grounded discovery/failure episodes, scoped failure knowledge, Jump/failure atom decomposition, historical morphology/failure-atlas evidence, prospective failure memory, and bounded representation-transition studies.

This matrix is an integration checklist, not scientific authority. A repository add-on is not considered publication-integrated merely because its code, schema, development note, or programme ownership JSON exists. Relevant manuscript text must state what the add-on does, its authority ceiling, and the downstream paper that owns the next scientific transition.

## Candidate/successor papers

`papers/candidates/` contains prospective candidates, not additional flagship identities. Their publication integration is therefore handled differently:

- **P6 candidate — Formal Epistemic Structures and Mechanics:** existing higher-order/mechanics manuscript bridges must retain the negative possibility that useful components are parent-owned or do not require one universal calculus. A final candidate manuscript may discuss the atom studies only after its content binding is regenerated against the integrated dependency state.
- **P7 candidate — Epistemic Navigation in Open Worlds:** semantic transport, preservation, and reopening are the relevant interface. Surface translation must not transport a scoped exclusion when the load-bearing semantic coordinate changes.
- **P8 candidate — Theory of Epistemic Authority:** proposal capability remains distinct from permission to use a proposal as scientific authority. Discovery, failure, and representation-transition records therefore enter as typed inputs with no self-promotion authority.
- **P9 candidate — Executable Research Core:** the candidate programme currently has executable artifacts but no canonical manuscript. Any future manuscript must describe failure retention/routing and learned ranking as capability only, not adoption or scientific authority.
- **P10 candidate — Content-Bound Mathematical Evaluation / later invention lane references:** the current candidate programme does not provide a canonical manuscript for a general method-invention claim. No Jump/invention result may be attributed to P10 until an actual manuscript, claim ledger, prospective evidence, and novelty authority exist.

## Dependency-result writeback gate

The paper sections in this integration lane describe interfaces and nonclaims only. Numerical or terminal conclusions from still-open dependency PRs are written back only after all of the following hold for the exact result-bearing head:

1. the dependency PR is merged after its required repository/candidate CI;
2. its protected or prospectively frozen evidence is independently verified where required;
3. the strongest registered parent/comparator result is retained, including a tie or subsumption result;
4. the target paper's claim ledger is updated for every new result-bearing sentence;
5. the manuscript is compiled and its journal/candidate content binding is regenerated;
6. the rendered PDF is audited before any readiness status is restored.

For the current Jump/failure programme this means, in particular, that an interface section may explain bounded regime-transition testing now, while any final statement about incremental ORION value versus the verified regime-revision parent is withheld from paper Results/Conclusion until the #513/#514/.../#558/#560 dependency chain has earned its final integrated terminal.
