## Navigation, foraging and acquisition

- **Hart, Nilsson & Raphael**, *A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of
  Minimum Cost Paths*, IEEE Trans. Systems Science and Cybernetics (1968).
- **Xiong, Hoang & Wang**, *DeepPath*, EMNLP (2017); **Das et al.**, *Go for a
  Walk and Arrive at the Answer*, ICLR (2018). Learned multi-hop walks over an
  incomplete graph where the answer path is not given.
- **Bates**, *The Design of Browsing and Berrypicking Techniques*, Online Review
  (1989); **Pirolli & Card**, *Information Foraging*, Psychological Review
  (1999); **Marchionini**, *Exploratory Search: From Finding to Understanding*,
  CACM (2006). Patch-leaving under information scent, the evolving rather than
  fixed information need, and lookup versus open-ended investigation — these
  already predict the breadth/concentration tradeoff §6 measures.
- **Howard**, *Information Value Theory*, IEEE Trans. SSC (1966);
  **Kaelbling, Littman & Cassandra**, *Planning and Acting in Partially
  Observable Stochastic Domains*, Artificial Intelligence (1998). Deciding what
  to observe under uncertainty, with an optimality criterion ORION-17 does not
  improve on. `CANNOT_CHECK` is, in these terms, an unresolved belief.

## Representation, revision and goals

- **Sacerdoti**, *Planning in a Hierarchy of Abstraction Spaces*, Artificial
  Intelligence (1974); **Ravindran & Barto**, *Model Minimization in Markov
  Decision Processes*, AAAI (2002); **Li, Walsh & Littman**, *Towards a Unified
  Theory of State Abstraction for MDPs*, ISAIM (2006). The closest structural
  parents to the atlas/chart construction.
- **Tamar et al.**, *Value Iteration Networks*, NeurIPS (2016);
  **Ha & Schmidhuber**, *World Models* (2018); **Hafner et al.**, *Dream to
  Control* (2020). An adaptive atlas is not new because the chart is learned.
- **Alchourrón, Gärdenfors & Makinson**, *On the Logic of Theory Change*,
  Journal of Symbolic Logic (1985); **Ghallab, Nau & Traverso**, *Automated
  Planning and Acting* (2016). The reopening semantics must be checked against
  the AGM postulates: satisfying them makes it an instance, violating one owes
  an argument.
- **Klenk, Molineaux & Aha**, *Goal-Driven Autonomy for Responding to Unexpected
  Events* (2013); **Aha, Cox & Muñoz-Avila**, *Goal Reasoning: Research Survey*;
  **Kondrakunta & Cox**, *Autonomous Goal Selection and Operations* (2021).
  Goal reasoning already owns reframing, with its operations vocabulary.
- **Noy & Klein**, *Ontology Evolution: Not the Same as Schema Evolution*,
  Knowledge and Information Systems (2004); **Stojanovic**, *Methods and Tools
  for Ontology Evolution* (2004).
- **Lehman & Stanley**, *Abandoning Objectives: Evolution Through the Search for
  Novelty Alone*, Evolutionary Computation (2011); **Pugh, Soros & Stanley**,
  *Quality Diversity* (2016). "Useful structural breadth" needs a
  quality-diversity comparator.

## Deep-search stopping

- *To Search or Not to Search: Aligning the Decision Boundary of Deep Search
  Agents via Causal Intervention*, arXiv:2602.03304 (2026).
- *Diagnosing Search Behavior and Failure Modes in Long-Horizon Search Agents*,
  arXiv:2608.01913 (2026).
- *DeepSearchQA*, arXiv:2601.20975 (2026); *S1-DeepResearch*, arXiv:2606.15367
  (2026).

Premature stopping is an actively worked problem, not an ORION-17 finding. One
distinction in this literature is load-bearing and must be preserved in the
manuscript: **DeepSearchQA stops on exhaustiveness of a set; the others stop on
sufficiency for one answer.** Obligation coverage is the first target and
root-task success the second.

## Interface and contract theory — the composition calculus's actual parent

- **de Alfaro & Henzinger**, *Interface Automata*, ESEC/FSE (2001);
  *Interface-Based Design*, NATO Science Series 195 (2005).
- **Benveniste, Caillaud, Nickovic, Passerone, Raclet, Reinkemeier,
  Sangiovanni-Vincentelli, Damm, Henzinger & Larsen**, *Contracts for System
  Design*, Foundations and Trends in Electronic Design Automation (2018),
  ISBN 978-1-68083-402-4.

`COMPOSITION_LAW_PARENT_FINDING_V1.md` establishes that §18's calculus belongs
here rather than to planning abstraction: homomorphism composition composes on
strict object matching and supplies no compatibility relation, so it cannot
yield Theorem V4.1's `Match` side condition. Source/target interface contracts,
a compatibility relation gating composition, refinement and associativity are
this family's primitive apparatus.
