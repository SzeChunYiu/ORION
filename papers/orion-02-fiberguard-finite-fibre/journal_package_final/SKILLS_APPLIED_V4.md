## Skill contracts applied

- academic-paper-pipeline: evidence-first closure, acceptance-readiness separation, and no promotion from unresolved claims;
- academic-writing: theory-paper architecture, complete formal setup, section-function review, standalone manuscript QA;
- nature-writing compatibility layer: claim-ledger alignment, venue routing, submission-package completeness;
- nature-polishing: sentence logic, natural scholarly prose, removal of repository/version language, mathematical typesetting;
- nature-reviewer: hostile editor/referee simulation and bounded concern closure;
- nature-citation: citation-intent and bibliographic verification;
- nature-data: availability wording, archive contents, licences and recheck-versus-rerun boundary;
- nature-proposal-writer compatibility layer: claim-evidence map and stop-rule discipline;
- abstract-information-budget: single formal spine, no benchmark catalogue, no private identifiers;
- manuscript-section-craftsmanship: section-specific reader jobs, formal-spine activation, Results/adverse-evidence allocation, limitations and support surfaces.
- manuscript-excellence release gate: E1-E9 macro logic, section function, reader activation, Discussion, rhetoric, precision, archetype, venue allocation and abstract checks;
- venue-constrained manuscript budget: exact TMLR any-length policy, measured seven-page PDF, measured supplement size, section/function ledger and no invented total limit;
- manuscript-budget utilization and marginal value: functional-sufficiency review without padding; because TMLR has advisory rather than hard total length guidance, the current verifier correctly reports no hard-limit utilization fraction;
- narrative architecture and numerical precision: formal question chain, no surprise entities, real Discussion, exact finite counts and justified exact McNemar value;
- AI-session execution/routing: one release operation, minimum active context, delta-first correction, and a validated compact checkpoint;
- research-integrity verification: full-manuscript atomic-claim inventory, source identity/status records, claim warrants, citation-use mapping, and independent coverage gate;
- publication-release integrity: one scientific surface with separate anonymous TMLR and identified arXiv route renderings, explicit disposition of every plausible manuscript candidate, exact upload-set membership, source/PDF/archive hashes and byte counts, and decoded-payload local-path rejection.

## Positive-feedback contribution

This paper exercised the newly merged abstract, section-craftsmanship, research-integrity, and publication-release gates against a theory paper containing several preserved adverse studies. The useful pattern was to do exhaustive claim/provenance work behind the manuscript while spending reader-facing space only on the formal spine and the most decision-relevant adverse boundaries. The R24 defect also exposed a useful skill-level lesson: a `CANNOT_CHECK` classification must inspect every nested frozen record before concluding that paired inputs were not serialized. The final scanner dispositions below are concrete feedback for future semantic-gate refinement. No unvalidated change to the shared skill repository was made from this paper lane.
