# ORION-02 / FiberGuard R20 external review and reproduction protocol

Protocol date: 2026-08-27

Status:

`EXTERNAL_REVIEW_NOT_YET_EXECUTED`

This protocol is the hand-off boundary between internally verified evidence and authority that the research owner cannot grant.

## Frozen review subject

The external subject is the clean current-main R20 package generated only after the strict internal release receipt. The review bundle must contain:

- `MANUSCRIPT_R20.pdf` and source;
- `CLAIM_LEDGER_R20.json`;
- `EVIDENCE_MANIFEST_R20.json`;
- `PRIOR_ART_MATRIX_R20.md`;
- `JOURNAL_GATE_R20.json`;
- the exact R18 recovery result and terminal;
- the exact R19 theorem result and independent Rust result;
- the registered certified-neighborhood result and replay receipt;
- all source/environment/digest manifests;
- this protocol.

The reviewer must record the bundle tree digest before reading results.

## Independent roles

At least three role-separated reviewers are required. One person may hold more than one role only if every role is reported separately.

### Decision-theory reviewer

Check the deterministic and randomized fibre-regret theorems, witness compression, refinement monotonicity, state-dependent acquisition recursion, scalar-collapse boundary, no-free-extension theorem, fallback-alignment identities, marginal nonidentifiability, and lower-image criterion. The report must identify every implicit compactness, finiteness, oracle, timing, or randomization assumption.

### Algorithm-selection and empirical-method reviewer

Check corpus denominators, PAR10/timeout semantics, feature-cost accounting, nested fold custody, comparator information parity, development/validation/test separation, adverse terminals, and the interpretation of the complete-corpus result versus held-out selector performance.

### Reproducibility reviewer

From a fresh environment, reconstruct at least:

1. the R19 finite theorem-critical receipt without importing an ORION Python checker;
2. the R18 recovery terminal and 99-candidate/zero-route facts;
3. the certified-neighborhood adverse terminal on the pinned corpus;
4. the deterministic manuscript PDF or a documented semantically equivalent build.

The reproduction report must include commands, source revisions, environment, wall time, peak memory, output digests, and first disagreement.

## Novelty subtraction

The novelty reviewer must use current primary literature and answer the four questions in `PRIOR_ART_MATRIX_R20.md`. A result is acceptable only in one of these forms:

- `RESIDUAL_CLAIM_SURVIVES`, with exact nearest work and explicit differences;
- `RESIDUAL_CLAIM_NARROWED`, with replacement wording;
- `EQUIVALENT_PRIOR_OBJECT_FOUND`, with the affected manuscript claims withdrawn.

Search failure or phrase absence is not a novelty result.

## Required hostile checks

The external report must attempt to falsify all of the following:

- replace expected randomized loss by pathwise safety;
- refund learned-feature cost after post-acquisition rejection;
- apply a complete training fibre theorem to an unseen same-signature state;
- infer fallback safety from learned-arm calibration;
- infer a legal joint route from separate arm marginals;
- apply a fine-observation route certificate after deployed coarsening;
- use the withdrawn R18 positive terminal;
- relabel nearest-neighbor prediction as an exact structural certificate;
- delete adverse R14, R16, R18, or neighborhood results from the evidence chain;
- promote internal replay to external independence or journal authority.

Every hostile mutation must be either rejected by a theorem/receipt or recorded as an unresolved blocker.

## Result schema

Each reviewer signs a JSON result with:

- reviewer role and organization;
- conflict declaration;
- reviewed bundle digest;
- source and environment digests;
- claims checked;
- disagreements and severity;
- mutation outcomes;
- terminal;
- signature or independently verifiable identity.

Allowed terminals are:

- `EXTERNAL_REVIEW_PASS_AT_STATED_AUTHORITY`;
- `EXTERNAL_REVIEW_PASS_WITH_CLAIM_NARROWING`;
- `EXTERNAL_REVIEW_DISAGREEMENT`;
- `EXTERNAL_REPRODUCTION_FAIL`;
- `CANNOT_CHECK_RIGHTS_OR_SOURCE`.

No missing report is interpreted as a pass.

## Promotion rule

The manuscript may move from internal-release to submission review only when:

1. decision-theory and empirical-method reports pass or all requested narrowing is incorporated;
2. one structurally independent reproduction reaches the same load-bearing terminals;
3. novelty is independently adjudicated;
4. data/software rights are approved by the author or responsible institution;
5. author metadata, conflicts, funding, contributions, venue, and exact upload bytes are fixed.

Until then:

`external_independence=false`, `novelty=CANNOT_CHECK`, `journal_authority=false`, and `submission_authorized=false`.
