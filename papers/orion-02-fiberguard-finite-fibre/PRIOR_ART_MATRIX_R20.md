## Frozen novelty claim

The manuscript must not claim novelty for algorithm selection, feature acquisition, abstention, conformal calibration, nearest-neighbour prediction, minimax regret, set cover, Pareto pruning, or generic adaptivity gaps.

The only candidate residual is the following combination:

> A complete finite representation fibre is treated as a proof subject for downstream action regret under one statewise oracle. The resulting exact certificates, state-dependent acquisition profiles, and legal joint-route language are connected to a prospectively frozen failure atlas showing where complete-fibre authority is lost under induction, marginal calibration, fallback routing, and an invalid structural neighbourhood law.
