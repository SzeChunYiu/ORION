# ORION-02 revival-attempt policy

Operator policy received 2026-08-27: the material ORION-02 negative remains an
active research topic until a valid bounded positive or 100 genuine revival
attempts.

An attempt counts only when it is:

1. prospectively distinct and frozen before its own outcomes;
2. matched to a diagnosed mechanism rather than a vocabulary change;
3. compared with the strongest valid corrected parent on identical relevant
   identity, folds, costs, and outcomes;
4. executed to a scientific terminal (not a setup or infrastructure failure);
5. reproduced as required by its protocol; and
6. independently verified with all adverse/null evidence retained.

Setup, dependency, retrieval, scheduler, digest, or other infrastructure
failures do not consume the 100. The count may stop early only for a valid
bounded positive, an exact proof of within-scope impossibility, or a named
external authorization/resource block. Neither a local freeze receipt nor an
agent judgment supplies submission or top-tier authority.

`ORION02_REVIVAL_ATTEMPT_LEDGER_V1.jsonl` is append-only in event order. A
registration event does not count. Its completion event records whether the
attempt met every counting condition and binds the terminal and verification
receipts. Historical R22 stays visible but is not counted under this policy
because its scalar-F* evaluator defect invalidated the required matched
performance comparison.
