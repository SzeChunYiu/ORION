# ORION-16–ORION-25 top-tier promotion programme V1

**Owner issue:** #977  
**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `ACTIVE / BROAD_CLAIMS_NOT_YET_PROMOTED`

## 1. Purpose

This programme raises ORION-16–ORION-25 toward top-tier journal submission without protecting novelty by omission or by retreating to progressively smaller claims.

The governing scientific move is:

`nearest work -> absorb strongest mechanism -> build donor-complete comparator -> generalize upward -> freeze discriminator -> execute protected evidence -> independent authority -> promote`

A lower-level mechanism becoming donor-owned is not a reason to shrink the programme. It is a reason to move the research object to the next abstraction level while preserving correct credit.

## 2. Promotion constitution

### 2.1 Donor-first rule

For every material nearest work, record exactly one disposition:

- `ADOPT` — use the mechanism as a donor-owned component;
- `ADAPT` — preserve the donor mechanism but change its interface/assumptions for the higher problem;
- `COMPOSE` — integrate multiple donors into the strongest comparator/product;
- `DEFER` — material but not load-bearing for the current protected discriminator;
- `REJECT` — only with an explicit incompatibility or falsifier.

`IGNORE_TO_PRESERVE_NOVELTY` is invalid.

### 2.2 Upward-claim rule

When donor assimilation removes a lower-level claim, the paper must choose one of:

1. prove a stronger theorem about the relation among the donor mechanisms;
2. identify a higher-level decision problem that the donor mechanisms leave unresolved;
3. construct a protected experiment whose outcome distinguishes the composed donor product from the proposed higher-level mechanism;
4. retain a negative/impossibility result if the higher-level claim also closes.

A manuscript may state the maximum claim as a **target hypothesis**, but scientific authority remains `CANNOT_CHECK` until the corresponding protected gate closes.

### 2.3 No self-authority

The system that generates a claim, intervention, representation, method, certificate or research decision may not be the sole authority that certifies its novelty, safety, correctness or superiority.

Use, in descending preference:

1. deterministic external verifier / machine-checkable proof;
2. independently implemented evaluator;
3. blinded external human/domain adjudication;
4. blinded model adjudication with evaluator separation and agreement analysis.

### 2.4 Negative-history rule

No failed protocol, null, donor-subsumed positive, leakage finding, benchmark defect or hostile counterexample may be deleted from the causal evidence chain. Successor protocols must be independently frozen.

## 3. Common top-tier gate

A paper reaches `TOP_TIER_SUBMISSION_READY` only if all applicable gates below close.

### A. Scientific object

- [ ] one-sentence maximum claim is stable and non-overlapping with sibling papers;
- [ ] theorem/semantic assumptions are explicit;
- [ ] donor-owned components are identified without first-of-kind language;
- [ ] broad claim is supported at the same abstraction level at which it is written.

### B. Nearest-work saturation

- [ ] broad search across mature parent fields;
- [ ] current fast-moving 2025–2026 lane refreshed immediately before submission;
- [ ] strongest relevant donor mechanisms are implemented or fairly approximated in the donor-product baseline;
- [ ] two changed-vocabulary saturation rounds produce no material ownership change, or the claim is reopened.

### C. Protected evidence

- [ ] protocol/evaluator/task-family identities frozen before protected outcomes;
- [ ] protected data/tasks are disjoint from design/tuning episodes;
- [ ] matching covers semantic information, tools/access and total resources;
- [ ] primary endpoints, uncertainty method, multiplicity and missing-run policy frozen;
- [ ] catastrophic semantic/safety failure has a non-compensatory gate where appropriate.

### D. External validity

For empirical/systems claims:

- [ ] at least one non-toy real-system/domain validation;
- [ ] at least two qualitatively distinct domains for a cross-domain headline;
- [ ] strong donor-product and simple/cheap baselines;
- [ ] ablations identify the load-bearing mechanism;
- [ ] known-negative/non-beneficial regimes remain visible.

### E. Independent authority

- [ ] result replay or proof independently checked;
- [ ] evaluator/generator separation enforced;
- [ ] external/blinded adjudication where correctness/novelty is not mechanically decidable;
- [ ] disagreements and `CANNOT_CHECK` are retained rather than majority-laundered.

### F. Reproducibility

- [ ] one-command fail-closed reproduction path;
- [ ] exact content digests for load-bearing code/data/results/manuscript macros;
- [ ] clean-environment reproduction;
- [ ] byte-identical replay where determinism is claimed;
- [ ] environment/resource assumptions frozen and reported;
- [ ] paper cannot consume unverified result values through hand-copied prose.

### G. Referee attack surface

Each paper must carry explicit hostile cases for:

- hidden extra information/access;
- unmatched compute/preprocessing;
- outcome-conditioned protocol edits;
- weak/straw donor baseline;
- metric gaming/always-abstain behavior;
- self-certification;
- semantic-regime drift;
- sibling-paper claim leakage;
- accidental promotion from controlled/synthetic to universal/real-world language.

## 4. Shared external validation worlds

Shared infrastructure is encouraged, but protected evidence must be paper-disjoint.

### World L — open-weight language/procedural agents

Use for ORION-19, ORION-21, ORION-22 and selected ORION-23/ORION-24 tests. Required properties:

- exact same-information representation interventions where relevant;
- model/token/tool accounting;
- held-out task families and surface reminting;
- reproducible open-weight checkpoints or immutable remote-model identity if external APIs are unavoidable.

### World V — verifier-backed formal/problem-solving systems

Use for ORION-20–ORION-23 and ORION-25 execution-integrity tests. Required properties:

- correctness from an external verifier;
- verifier-call and search accounting;
- frozen access/tool/library model;
- no success from evaluator leakage.

### World R — scientific research decisions

Use for ORION-24 and ORION-25, and optionally ORION-16–ORION-18 transfer demonstrations. Required properties:

- hidden research packets with positive/null/negative/subsumed/non-identifiable cases;
- literature access matched across systems;
- independent final scientific authority;
- useful-discovery metric paired with overclaim/false-novelty penalties.

## 5. Common resource vector

Do not hide resource transfers behind a single scalar unless the downstream decision contract prospectively supplies weights.

Record at minimum:

`R = (preprocessing/compiler work, state/memory, model compute, inference/search, tool/verifier calls, latency, cache/reuse, recovery/reconstruction)`

ORION-20–ORION-23 should use the same accounting ontology so their results compose without sharing protected outcomes.

## 6. Portfolio-level upward synthesis

The intended generalization ladder is:

1. **ORION-16:** what state changes are scientifically admissible?
2. **ORION-17:** what evidence/closure survives a regime change?
3. **ORION-18:** what evidence actually authorizes a scientific commitment?
4. **ORION-19:** is failure caused by missing information, inaccessible representation or missing computation?
5. **ORION-20:** when is the current method language itself insufficient, and can it be expanded with certified new reach?
6. **ORION-21:** where should computation be placed between state construction and downstream access/reasoning?
7. **ORION-22:** where should the next unit of resource be allocated online?
8. **ORION-23:** for which future responsibilities is a state safe to reuse?
9. **ORION-24:** how should research claims be generated, tested, retained, subsumed, reopened and promoted?
10. **ORION-25:** what does the execution substrate prove about attribution/replay, and what scientific validity does it explicitly not prove?

These are separate publication objects but a coherent programme about auditable adaptive scientific intelligence.

## 7. Promotion terminals

Every paper-specific `TOP_TIER_PROMOTION_V1.md` defines three levels:

- `CONTROLLED_CORE_VALID` — existing scoped evidence remains valid;
- `EXTERNAL_PROMOTION_PENDING` — broad target is frozen but its external gate is open;
- `TOP_TIER_SUBMISSION_READY` — all paper-specific plus common gates close on one content-addressed package.

No text-only edit may move a paper between levels.
