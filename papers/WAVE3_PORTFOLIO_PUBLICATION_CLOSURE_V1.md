# Wave 3 portfolio publication closure

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Portfolio:** ORION-04, ORION-15 and ORION-24  
**Purpose:** replace programme-level ambition with the strongest manuscript that the committed evidence can presently support.

## Closure rule

A Wave 3 paper is not finished by preserving a protocol, opening a successor issue, compiling a historical manuscript or observing a favorable local score. It is finished only when:

1. one canonical scoped manuscript states every positive result at its earned authority level;
2. contradictory or stale reader-facing claims are superseded for the Wave 3 track;
3. a machine-readable disposition binds the evidence, terminal and withheld claims;
4. a separate review checks scientific validity, causal or external validity, reproducibility and publication framing;
5. automated guards fail on authority escalation or evidence drift; and
6. any unperformed external study is represented as a limitation or a new research programme, not as unfinished support for the current paper.

## Expert review group

The portfolio was reviewed as four independent roles before editorial synthesis.

### A. Theorem and computation auditor

Background: additive combinatorics, finite exhaustive proof, exact-state algorithms and proof-custody systems.  
Role: determine which ORION-04 statements follow from symbolic mathematics, which follow from bounded exact replay, and which remain conjectural or internally exploratory.

### B. Causal evaluation and sequential-inference lead

Background: protected evaluation, longitudinal intervention studies, optional stopping, retention and harmful-transfer measurement.  
Role: determine whether ORION-15 establishes protected self-improvement, and identify the strongest defensible contribution when it does not.

### C. Research-software and external-validity lead

Background: reproducible research software, blinded adjudication, independent agent evaluation, failure accounting and acquisition bias.  
Role: determine whether ORION-24 establishes external frontier-agent performance, and separate a reusable evaluation architecture from an unexecuted external endpoint.

### D. Journal editor and claim auditor

Background: methods and theory publishing, atomic-claim review, literature subtraction, data/code statements and submission-package integrity.  
Role: resolve disagreements by evidence rather than vote, choose one manuscript boundary per paper and prohibit venue or novelty language not supported by the record.

These terminals close the present papers. They do not prohibit future successor studies. A future exact-D4 proof, protected Self-ORION campaign or independently custodied frontier-agent evaluation must start under a new prospectively frozen identity and may amend the literature only additively.

## Shared publication boundary

Across all three papers:

- repository CI is reproducibility evidence, not external scientific independence;
- a checker written in the same project can be implementation-independent without being externally independent;
- protocol completeness is not outcome evidence;
- blocked, adverse and null records remain in the scientific history;
- no result is called general, autonomous, top-tier, exact or externally validated unless the corresponding disposition grants that authority;
- historical manuscripts remain archival inputs and are not the Wave 3 canonical reader-facing source where they conflict with the scoped manuscript.
