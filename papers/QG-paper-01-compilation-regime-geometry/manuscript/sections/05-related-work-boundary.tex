\section{Related work and boundary}

Regime geometry intersects several established areas: exact and heuristic quantum compilation, normal-form/synthesis theorems, parameterized search, phase diagrams in optimization, resource estimation, decision-region classification, and compiler-specific cost models. Dynamic programming, Pauli/symplectic representations, stabilizer synthesis, Carath\'eodory/sparsification ideas, and family-specific donor constructions receive no novelty credit merely for appearing in this programme.

The candidate residual is methodological and cross-family: making donor regions, exact trade refutations, sufficiency/tightness, structural recognizability, and prospective prediction into separately receipted components that can succeed or fail independently. The current manuscript does not claim that no earlier work maps any compiler family in this way. A fresh literature closure must compare the exact composition against normal-form and exact resource-estimation literature before submission.

The StabPrep result is particularly important for novelty discipline because it prevents the paper from selling an artifact of the first two families as a universal principle. Any field-level statement must include the negative transfer.
