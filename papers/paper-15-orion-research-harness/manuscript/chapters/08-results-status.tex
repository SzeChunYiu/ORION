\subsection{Attestation composition is not scientific admission}
\label{sec:attestation-composition}

The attestation-composition V2 protocol was frozen on an immutable digest and
executed (run \texttt{32664075763}, artifact \texttt{9499830847}, ZIP SHA-256
\texttt{fccf3b28f3f33af8b07a87eab6764742c7882de88018d65be16e2dba1dee3bff}), with
a second independent implementation GREEN on all 13 agreement-table entries. It
therefore meets the condition above, and it is reported here because its
principal finding is adverse.

\paragraph{The negative first: signatures do not observe key custody.} Under a
full key-set compromise, simulated by re-signing with genuine key material, the
signature layer detects \textbf{0/6}. Worse, those same six cases
\emph{false-promote} through \textsc{chain-plus-sei} (\textbf{6/6}). A chain
that verifies perfectly says nothing about whether the right party held the key.
Key custody is an inherited premise, now explicitly registered as one, not
something the cryptographic layer measures. Collapsing the chain directly into
the scientific-admission decision false-promotes a further \textbf{12} cases,
where the properly scoped cryptographic-only reading returns \cannotcheck{}
everywhere.

\paragraph{What did hold.} Over the frozen 22-case corpus, three-link Ed25519
chains verify and fail closed against every registered attack family: truncation
\textbf{66/66}, substitution \textbf{22/22}, splice-with-partial-re-signing
\textbf{22/22}, reorder \textbf{22/22}, cross-occurrence replay \textbf{22/22}
and stale or consumed re-presentation \textbf{22/22}. Chain-plus-SEI agreement
with the frozen gold is \textbf{22/22}, and scientific-field leakage into the
cryptographic/provenance layer is \textbf{0}.

\paragraph{The layer does not win by rejecting everything.} False rejection over
the full valid workload -- seven execution-valid fault cases plus four real
receipts -- is \textbf{0/11} at the chain layer and \textbf{0/5} at disposition
level, with \textbf{0/4} real false promotions. That matters because a detector
that refuses everything would score perfectly on the attack families above and be
useless.

\paragraph{Scope.} A frozen 22-case corpus with simulated per-role keys. No
hardware-backed custody, external timestamping or KMS, and no comparison against
production Sigstore or in-toto is claimed. The composition-and-boundary statement
is verified within that scope; the C15.5 superiority claim remains \cannotcheck{}.

When results exist, the primary table must contain one row per attack family and
report the frozen fixture count, false admissions, false rejections, coverage
alarms, incorrect labels, execution environment digest, receipt-set digest and
adjudication receipt. A separate reliability table may be reported only if the
multi-instance sampling gate passes. The abstract, title and conclusion must name
the highest verified independence level and must report all failed or
\cannotcheck{} gates.

Existing engineering checks, if cited in a future version, belong in a development
history table. They may motivate attacks or demonstrate that the protocol is
executable, but they cannot be promoted retrospectively into protected paper
evidence. Likewise, agreement between deterministic auditors on one source tree is
an L1 structural result; zero model calls remove model-generated semantic judgment
from that audit but do not create evidence or scientific independence.

The absence of a result is an explicit status and must not be hidden. It is the
present status of a framework paper whose nontrivial empirical claims are prospective.
This separation prevents the manuscript structure from manufacturing an apparent
positive terminal before the evidence exists.
