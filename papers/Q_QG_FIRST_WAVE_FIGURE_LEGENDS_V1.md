## ORION-01 — `Q1_counterexamples_and_support_ceiling`

**Figure X | Exact counterexamples break restricted shared-Tag families while the all-`n` support-two theorem bounds the full optimum.** Left, the split-anchor witness has exact structural cost 8 while the restricted common-anchor family costs 9. Right, the frame-for-Tag borrow witness has exact cost 5 while the enlarged weight-one-frame family costs 6. Each inequality is an exact verified counterexample to the stated restricted-family closure, not a sample estimate. Separately, R6S proves for every `n` in the frozen R6M grammar under the raw support-count objective that an exact optimum exists with frame support at most two (`C_DP=C_D++`). The proof is reduced to a finite local inequality (18,432 cases, zero violations) plus a combinatorial class argument (43,688 checked tuples, with the support-two obstruction explicitly retained). The theorem does **not** imply that a particular named split/borrow closed-form taxonomy is complete.

## ORION-04 — `Q4_primary_effects_small_multiples`

## ORION-09 — `QG1_R6I_support_bound_hierarchy`

**Figure X | A sound proof-derived support ceiling can be much looser than the compiler's intrinsic support requirement.** Under the frozen R6I grammar/objective, QG6's conserved-syndrome quotient has rank 5 and yields a safe support ceiling under its proof assumptions. A later proof system that permits whole-system Tag relocation establishes support-one sufficiency for all `n`, and support zero is infeasible; therefore the intrinsic support number is exactly `kappa_R6I=1`. The earlier rank-five certificate is not incorrect—it is a valid but loose upper bound produced by a more restrictive proof abstraction. Annotation also reports the separate StabPrep boundary result: on the registered 13-feature vocabulary/domain, 12 mixed feature cells carry both donor-exact and donor-inexact labels, yielding an irreducible 43/1,146 error floor for classifiers that use only that frozen feature vector.

## ORION-09 — `QG1_QG16_certificate_cone_slice`

**Figure X | Objective-indexed region where the R6I support-one proof certificate applies.** The plot shows a normalized two-dimensional slice for `t_r>0` under the registered order condition `t_c<=t_nc`. The all-`n` support-one certificate follows from the exact resource inequalities summarized in QG16; in this slice the boundary is the maximum of the Restore-dominated and Tag/Restore-dominated half-space requirements. The shaded region denotes objectives for which **this proof certificate applies**. Points outside the region are not labeled support-two-required or suboptimal: QG16 explicitly leaves global phase-boundary sharpness open, so outside the cone the correct statement is that the certificate is silent.

## ORION-10 — `QG2_forecast_refutation_and_repair`

**Figure X | One exact counterexample refutes universal closed-form exactness without invalidating the certified layers underneath it.** Across 9,546 registered exact-DP comparisons, the initial closed-form forecaster matches 9,545 and fails on one prospectively generated `n=3` row. The left panel reports these deterministic registered counts on a compressed scale; they are not IID accuracy estimates. The right panel makes the logically decisive row explicit: the old closed form returns 11, the exact unrestricted DP returns 10, and the theorem-backed support-two forecaster `F2=C_D++` returns 10 after the separately frozen repair analysis. The row refutes the universal compact equality/regime label. It does not refute the constructive upper bound (`10<=11`) or the independent all-`n` support-two theorem.

## Shared caption rules

- State grammar/objective/domain at first use in the paper or caption.
- `theorem`, `exhaustive finite domain`, `exact counterexample`, `seeded panel`, `prospective case`, `timing`, and `replay` are separate evidence classes.
- Never call a within-subject matching an independent Hamiltonian subject.
- Never report replay count as replication `n`.
- Never convert source-data values to percentages if the percentage hides a logically decisive exact counterexample.
- Final panel labels/source-data file names must match the archived figure build bundle.
