# ORION-10 theorem successor — primitive-fibre lower bound and realizability

Identity: `ORION10.PRIMITIVE_FIBRE_LOWER_BOUND.v1`.

The exact support-two cost certificate survives while the compact explanation vocabulary has known hybrid counterexamples. Instead of adding another post-hoc feature, freeze a vocabulary `Psi` first and characterize exactly what any `Psi`-only explanation can possibly recover.

For structure `x`, let `Psi(x)` be the frozen primitive observation and `C(x)` the exact static cost. A `Psi`-only exact explanation exists iff `C` is constant on every fibre of `Psi`. Mixed-cost fibres are therefore permanent information lower bounds, not evidence that the formula search was too small.
