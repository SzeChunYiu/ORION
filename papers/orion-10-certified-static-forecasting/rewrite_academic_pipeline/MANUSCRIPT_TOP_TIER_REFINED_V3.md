# Exact Static Cost Forecasting with Fallible Structural Explanations

## Abstract

Static resource forecasting is valuable when an exact optimum can be inferred from input structure without rerunning unrestricted search. The scientific difficulty is that three different claims are often collapsed into one accuracy statement: a theorem may certify the cost, a compact structural rule may explain the regime realizing that cost, and a finite benchmark may only check an implementation. We show that these layers can disagree and should carry separate authority.

For a shared-Tag TARE compilation grammar under a fixed unit support-count objective, an all-size normal-form theorem proves that every unrestricted optimum has an optimum in the complete support-at-most-two family. Minimizing over that family therefore yields the exact unrestricted cost for every admitted system size under the theorem assumptions. The implementation agrees with unrestricted dynamic-programming truth on all 9,547 registered comparison instances, but that finite zero-error panel is an implementation check rather than the source of exactness. A compact enlarged-borrow explanation repairs an earlier fresh-instance counterexample and fits its verification panels, yet a later hostile study finds 64 exact hybrid witnesses among 740 instances outside that explanation. The cost certificate survives because all witnesses remain inside the theorem-certified support-two family. A refined explanation later closes 10,481 verified instances, while an all-size explanation sector remains open.

A separate staged prospective sequence contains 102 committed predictions that agree with their frozen exact outcomes. Those rows establish forward use, not proof. The result is an authority hierarchy: theorem-backed cost exactness can survive refutation of a human-readable structural explanation, while finite and prospective evidence test different downstream claims. The result is restricted to the stated grammar and objective and makes no full-circuit, hardware, arbitrary-resource, or quantum-advantage claim.

## 1. Main results and assumptions

The main claims and their authority are stated first because the distinction is the paper's central contribution.

### Result A — exact cost certificate

For the declared shared-Tag TARE grammar under the unit support-count objective, every unrestricted optimum has an equally good representative in the complete support-at-most-two family. Consequently,

`min cost over complete support<=2 = unrestricted exact optimum`

for every admitted system size satisfying the theorem assumptions.

This is an all-size statement and is licensed by the normal-form proof.

### Result B — compact explanation is incomplete

A compact enlarged-borrow regime explanation is refuted by 64 exact hybrid optima among 740 hostile instances. Those optima remain support two. The explanation fails; the cost theorem does not.

### Result C — finite and prospective evidence are secondary authorities

The theorem-backed evaluator matches the unrestricted exact referee on 9,547 registered instances. In a staged prospective series, 102 committed predictions agree with later exact outcomes. The first checks implementation on finite instances; the second tests forward use. Neither is the proof of Result A.

The objective assumptions are load-bearing. Reweighting support, Tag or Restore terms can change which structural reductions are non-increasing and therefore requires a separate proof or counterexample analysis.

## 2. Why one forecast needs several evidence types

A compact formula can be interpretable but wrong outside its development regimes. A restricted exact search can be globally exact if a theorem proves the restriction complete. A large finite benchmark can verify an implementation while leaving untested sizes logically open. A prospective prediction can demonstrate chronology without becoming mathematical generality.

These distinctions determine how a counterexample should propagate. If a hostile witness escapes a named regime explanation but stays inside the theorem-complete family, it retracts the explanation and leaves the exact cost certificate untouched.

The paper therefore treats theorem, implementation, prediction and explanation as four linked but non-interchangeable evidence objects.

## 3. A compact formula fails on a fresh exact instance

The initial static forecaster selects the minimum over a small set of intuitive structural construction families. It fits the original development panel but fails on a frozen fresh instance: the unrestricted optimum is 10 while the compact forecaster predicts 11.

Because the cost is discrete and the unrestricted exact referee is available, the single witness is decisive against completeness of the closed form. Mean error or aggregate accuracy cannot rescue an exact certificate claim.

The witness exposes a broader support-two construction missing from the named family list and motivates a different predictor: optimize over the *complete* support-at-most-two family.

## 4. The complete support-two functional is exact

The stronger predictor is not made exact by the 9,547 successful comparisons. Its authority comes from the all-size normal-form theorem.

The theorem gives a sequence of feasible non-increasing exchanges that transforms any unrestricted optimum into an optimum whose relevant auxiliary support is at most two. Therefore optimizing the full support-two family is not a heuristic restriction under the theorem assumptions; it is an exact realization of the unrestricted optimum.

The finite comparison panel remains scientifically useful. It tests whether the software implementing the theorem-complete family, objective accounting and reference interface agrees with unrestricted dynamic-programming truth over a broad registered set. Zero mismatches across 9,547 instances is therefore implementation-validation evidence.

## 5. A repaired explanation fails without breaking the forecast

The fresh witness motivates an enlarged-borrow explanation intended to describe the missing support-two trade. The rule repairs that witness and fits its initial verification panels.

A later hostile campaign tests all-size explanatory completeness more aggressively. It finds 64 exact optimal hybrids among 740 instances that lie outside the enlarged-borrow description.

Every one of those hybrids remains inside the complete support-two family. Hence:

- the named explanation is refuted as a complete classification;
- the theorem-backed cost functional remains exact.

This is the manuscript's decisive adversarial result. It demonstrates why a static forecast should not borrow theorem authority for the human-readable story used to explain it.

## 6. A refined explanation remains finite

A refined taxonomy incorporates the hybrid mechanism and closes 10,481 verified instances. That is useful structural evidence, but it remains finite. One registered all-size explanation sector is not proved closed.

The paper therefore makes an asymmetric claim: the **cost problem is exact under the theorem assumptions; the compact explanation problem remains open**.

This separation is more informative than presenting one monolithic “zero-error forecaster.” It identifies exactly what future counterexamples can still change.

## 7. Prospective prediction tests use before the referee

The programme contains 102 staged cases in which structural predictions are committed before the unrestricted exact result is opened. All 102 agree with their frozen outcomes.

The chronology rules out one retrospective fitting interpretation on those cases. It does not establish a population reliability estimate or prove the theorem. A successful prospective prediction and a theorem answer different questions.

The evidence ladder is therefore:

`all-size proof -> exact cost authority`

`finite exact panel -> implementation validation`

`prospective series -> forward-use evidence`

`compact regime rule -> interpretation, independently falsifiable`.

## 8. Objective dependence and robustness boundary

The central theorem is stated for the unit support-count objective. Separate robustness work identifies a coefficient region in which the support-two conclusion remains valid and provides a support-three control outside that region.

The current paper does not turn that robustness study into a theorem for arbitrary physical resource models. Changing objective weights can change the economic balance of frame support, Tag support and Restore structure. An application with different cost semantics needs its own proof-validity check.

This boundary prevents a compiler normal form from being translated directly into fault-tolerant gate-count or hardware claims.

## 9. Relation to quantum compilation and resource estimation

Exact synthesis, dynamic programming, restricted structural families, resource estimation and compiler-specific regime rules are established. The contribution is not the generic idea of predicting a compiler cost from structure.

The residual is an **exact static certificate with explicit authority separation**. The cost functional is theorem-backed, yet the manuscript keeps finite implementation evidence, prospective use and refutable explanatory structure as distinct layers. The hostile hybrids make this separation scientifically consequential rather than merely organizational.

For Quantum, the conceptual advance is that exact resource forecasting can remain stable under explanatory falsification when the forecast's authority is carried by a stronger normal-form theorem.

## 10. Limitations

The theorem is specific to the declared TARE grammar and objective. No conclusion follows automatically for full-circuit gate count, logical depth, qubits, runtime, noise, device performance, or quantum advantage. The refined regime explanation has not been proved complete at arbitrary size.

The 9,547 and 10,481 instance counts are finite implementation/explanation checks, not proof denominators. The 102 prospective rows do not become a calibrated reliability estimate without a sampling model and independent prospective population.

## 11. Reproducibility and release

The publication archive should bind the theorem statement and proof, exact objective definition, support-two evaluator, unrestricted referee used for finite validation, fresh and hostile counterexamples, refined explanation records, and prospective commitments with their later exact bindings.

For Quantum, the arXiv submission must be posted or cross-listed to `quant-ph`. The submission should retain the first-page authority hierarchy rather than compressing theorem, benchmark and prediction into one accuracy claim.

## 12. Conclusion

An exact static forecast can survive the failure of its most intuitive explanation. In the studied TARE compiler family, an all-size support-two theorem certifies the exact cost, while exact hostile hybrids refute a compact regime description without escaping the theorem-complete family. Finite zero error and prospective prediction provide valuable implementation and forward-use evidence but do not create theorem authority. Keeping these layers separate produces a resource forecast whose scientific status remains clear even when the structural explanation changes.
