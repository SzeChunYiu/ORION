## 01 — Research canon

1. Original forecaster `F(t)=min(C_R6L,C_D+,f_B)` makes no unrestricted DP call in the forecast path.
2. Its upper-bound components are constructively sound because each named family is feasible: `C_DP <= F(t)`.
3. The R6S theorem independently establishes `C_DP=C_D++` all `n` under the frozen R6M/raw-support objective.
4. The original closed-form exactness identity was **not** a theorem; it was finite-domain evidence/conjecture.
5. On 9,546 DP-compared instances in QG5, 9,545 forecasts were exact and one prospectively frozen fresh `n=3` instance refuted the equality: `C_DP=10 < 11=F(t)`.
6. On that refuting instance, the proven components remained true: the forecast stayed an upper bound and support-two sufficiency stayed exact. The failed components were the closed-form equality and regime predicate.
7. The counterexample localizes an out-of-own-target-support phantom borrow omitted by the original B family.
8. QG5b enlarges the borrow family to B′ and uses the theorem-backed exact support-two forecaster `F2=C_D++`; it reproduces zero error on the stated finite panels while retaining the original counterexample in the receipt.
9. `F2=C_D++` is exact all `n` only because it enumerates the entire theorem-sufficient D++ family; the later **closed-form** identity `min(C_R6L,C_D+,f_B′)` remains finite-domain/conjectural and is subsequently refuted by QG7.
10. QG7/QG7b therefore belong in ORION-10 as a second lesson: repairing one closed-form missing family does not authorize universal completeness; the certified D++ forecaster remains exact while cheaper closed forms can continue to fail.
11. Library rows lacking a committed DP receipt remain `FORECAST_ONLY` and grant no verification authority.
12. Timing data are secondary/non-canonical under the inherited convention and must not be used to overtake the exactness/certificate story.
13. All `NOT_R6` authority boundaries remain in force.

## 03 — Argument map

### Tension
A useful static compiler forecast must be cheaper than exact optimization, but the very shortcuts that make it cheap can make it wrong. A binary 'accurate/inaccurate' evaluation hides an important distinction: **which parts of the forecast were guaranteed, which were extrapolated, and what survives a counterexample?**

### Central question
Can a static quantum-compilation forecast expose enough formal authority structure that a prospective refutation invalidates only the unsupported layer while preserving theorem-backed bounds—and can the localized failure drive a new frozen forecaster?

### Central answer
Yes for the registered TARE family. The original forecaster mixed constructive upper bounds, an all-`n` support theorem and a finite-domain closed-form identity. A prospectively generated counterexample refuted the identity while satisfying the proven components exactly. The failure identified a missing borrow configuration; the successor forecaster was frozen separately and repaired the registered panels. Later companion counterexamples repeat the lesson: **certificate scope, not raw empirical hit rate, determines what survives failure.**

### Evidence sequence
1. Define forecast output and authority layers.
2. Prove/establish constructive upper bound and all-`n` support theorem separately from exactness conjecture.
3. Report original benchmark, including 9,545/9,546 rather than hiding the one error.
4. Present the refuting instance in full: target structure, costs and which certificate components pass/fail.
5. Root-cause localization: missing out-of-support borrow home.
6. Prospectively frozen B′/F2 successor and zero-error registered panels.
7. Show why `F2` theorem-backed exactness differs from the cheaper closed-form B′ conjecture.
8. Use QG7 later refutation as boundary evidence: cheap closed forms can fail again without threatening D++ exactness.
9. Separate verified forecasts from forecast-only library rows.

### Strongest alternative interpretation
**"This is just a buggy heuristic corrected after seeing an error."** The paper must prove the correction protocol was separately frozen and emphasize that the original counterexample remains in the final evidence. More importantly, the theorem-backed components were established independently of the empirical hit rate, so the refutation has a formal localization rather than merely causing another fit.

### Durable conclusion
Static compilation forecasts should carry **typed authority**: feasible bound, theorem, finite empirical equality, conjectural classifier or unverified prediction. Exact counterexamples then become informative because they reveal which epistemic layer failed.

The paper's strongest property is that a counterexample cannot invalidate a theorem it satisfies. Make the certificate lattice explicit and machine-checkable.
