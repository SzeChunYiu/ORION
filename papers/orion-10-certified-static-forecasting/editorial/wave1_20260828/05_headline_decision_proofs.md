# Headline decision proofs

## H1: support-two exactness
Claim: every unrestricted unit-objective optimum has a support-at-most-two representative. Decisive evidence: zero-sum subset lemma plus complete 18,432-case local cost inequality and lexicographic descent. Strongest alternative: support at least three may strictly pay. Discriminator: a local inequality violation or exact unrestricted/support-two gap. Boundary: changed objective supplies such gaps, so the claim is not transferred. Target axis: correctness and technical significance.

## H2: initial formula refutation
Claim: the simple explanation is not globally exact. Decisive evidence: exact three-qubit row 10 < 11. Strongest alternative: evaluator or decoding error. Discriminator: independent cost/witness reconstruction. Boundary: feasible upper bound and support theorem remain valid. Target axis: falsifiability and honest scope.

## H3: final explanation closure
Claim: three descriptive families cover all unit-objective optima. Decisive evidence: complete 378-geometry, 2^24-state-per-geometry domination with zero residue and induction on commuting support-two blocks. Alternative: a residual/fifth family. Discriminator: any unclosed local state or invalid target permutation. Boundary: failed simpler routes retained; reweighted objective excluded. Target axis: conceptual contribution.

## H4: certificate separation
Claim: exactness, explanation and row verification have independent warrants. Evidence: explanation fails twice while exactness survives. Alternative: a single monolithic accuracy claim. Discriminator: whether failure in one layer logically falsifies another. Boundary: separation is demonstrated for this grammar; not asserted as a universal standard. Target axis: utility and presentation.
