- Exact grammar and cost definition.
- Unit-objective support-at-most-two theorem.
- Initial benchmark decomposition: 9,261 + 240 + 45 = 9,546 distinct inputs; 9,545 matches and one retained mismatch.

## Not authorized
- Runtime or speedup claims.
- Transfer to reweighted objectives, other grammars, fault-tolerant resources, or hardware.
- Physical quantum advantage.
- Absolute novelty or first-in-literature language.
- Permanent public archive, license, DOI, acceptance, or external peer-review authority.

Unsanitized receipts remain private evidence and are not part of the submission-facing archives.
