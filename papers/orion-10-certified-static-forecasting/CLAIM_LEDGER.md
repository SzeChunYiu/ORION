## Forbidden promotions

- The MACHINE-EVIDENCED components (C4, C5, and every benchmark equality) must
  not be described as theorems for all n; the exactness identity is CONJECTURE
  for all n per the receipt, and is refuted as a closed-form identity at n=3
  (C12) in its frozen B(t) parametrization.
- The refutation (C12–C14) is a pre-frozen outcome branch and must be reported
  as a result, with the instance verbatim; it must not be smoothed into an
  "accuracy of 99.99%" style summary that hides the boundary.
- The three UNVERIFIED_FORECAST library subjects (C18–C19) verify nothing and
  must never be cited as confirmations; only the one DP-receipt-bound subject
  carries a checked forecast.
- Positive split/borrow confirmations are synthetic-only (C16); no real
  chemistry batch has been observed in a trade regime, and none may be implied.
- The QG-5b repair (C22) is proven exact but NOT executed; no executed-repair
  claim may be made until its receipt exists.
- All statuses are objective-scoped (C23): no claim from this paper transfers
  to O1 or any other objective without re-proof.
- Speedup numbers (C11) are non-canonical timing by convention and must not be
  promoted to hardware or wall-clock claims beyond the recorded runs.
- No receipt cited here carries R6 authority; no row may be promoted to a
  compiled-resource novelty claim; donor-owned machinery (TARE, the R6L family,
  D+/D++ enlargements, the F3 factor rule, the DP referee) is not claimed as
  novel.
- The protected stretched-N2 subject is unread in every cited receipt and no
  claim may imply otherwise.
