# Author-controlled actions before filing

1. Confirm the final competing-interests declaration.
2. Confirm the final funding statement.
3. Add ORCID if desired or required by the submission system.
4. Choose and apply a public license to the source and review materials.
5. Create a persistent public archive and record its identifier.
6. Post or cross-list the preprint in quant-ph before submitting to Quantum.
7. Confirm rights for every redistributed item; the current package contains only author manuscript materials and neutral summaries.
8. Upload the exact audited files and record the submission identifier.

Do not upload private editorial ledgers or development receipts.
