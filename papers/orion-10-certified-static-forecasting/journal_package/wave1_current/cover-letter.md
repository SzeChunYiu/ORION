Dear Editors,

Please consider “Layered Certificates for Static Cost Forecasting in Pauli Compilation” for publication in Quantum.

The manuscript separates four claims that are often conflated in static compilation-cost analysis: feasibility, theorem-backed exactness, structural explanation, and finite row verification. For a fixed three-block shared-tag Pauli grammar, it proves that a support-at-most-two evaluator equals the unrestricted optimum for every qubit count under the unit structural objective. It also retains an exact counterexample to the original compact formula, a hostile refutation of its first repair, and an adverse reweighted objective for which the support theorem fails. A complete local-domain argument closes the final explanation classification without using finite benchmark accuracy as proof.

The work is intentionally scoped. It makes no claim about full-circuit, fault-tolerant, or hardware resources; no runtime superiority is asserted. The nearest-work discussion distinguishes the result from language-level resource analysis, verified circuit optimization, Pauli-aware compilation, and full-stack resource estimation. Sanitized source and review materials accompany the manuscript.

The author contribution and use of generative artificial intelligence are disclosed in the manuscript. The corresponding author is Sze Chun Yiu, Department of Physics, Stockholm University, sze-chun.yiu@fysik.su.se.

Sze Chun Yiu
