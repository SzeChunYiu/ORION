\section{Discussion}

The shared-tag sequence establishes a distinction that a final zero-error table would conceal.  The first forecast was interpretable and prospectively successful, yet wrong.  The second is exact because a support theorem constrains every admitted instance, not because 9,547 comparison events over 9,546 distinct inputs happened to agree.  Explanation then fails twice without changing that cost.  A certified forecast is therefore not one status: its value, implementation, explanation and row-level referee state have different failure conditions.

The commuting-support-two result also illustrates why failed proof routes must remain visible.  The successful per-block-permutation argument proves the exact envelope in Eq.~\eqref{eq:envelope}.  The single-pinner calculation proves a strong local statement but lacks a global chain, while the two-coordinate representation is refuted.  Treating either local success as the global theorem would overstate the evidence; suppressing them after another route succeeds would erase useful information about which proof abstractions fail.

This separation clarifies the relation to algorithm selection.  A performance footprint answers which method works on which features.  An exact regime record can additionally ask whether a restricted family is sufficient for every optimum, whether the bound is tight, whether a compact explanation is identifiable, and what happens outside a proof-validity region.  The state-preparation result shows why these coordinates cannot be filled by analogy.  Four features determine a complete small-size domain, yet the rule does not survive the frozen next-size test.

For a compilation or verification reader, the same record can be read as a study in warrant typing for static cost forecasts.  Three warrant types appear.  A \emph{theorem warrant} --- here the support-two normalization and the per-block-permutation envelope --- licenses an all-size statement for the frozen grammar and objective; its cost of obtaining is a proof plus complete-domain machine checks, and it says nothing about other grammars or objectives.  An \emph{agreement warrant} --- finite comparison against the unrestricted dynamic program --- licenses only an implementation check on the compared rows; it is the cheapest to obtain and regenerate, and it transfers to no untested size, which is exactly what the staged panel's 102/102 agreement followed by a single fresh-instance refutation demonstrates.  A \emph{structural-coverage warrant} --- a named explanation family covering all current minimizing witnesses --- licenses a search restriction rather than a value; obtaining it costs a hostile census, and here it was refuted twice without disturbing the cost certificate.  What the separation offers to this reader is a measurement of relative certification cost for static forecast cost: agreement is cheapest and weakest, structural coverage is intermediate and refutable, and theorem warrants are expensive but are the only ones that transfer across system sizes.  Whether a theorem warrant is cheaply available for a given compiler depends on whether its grammar admits a normal-form or domination argument; this paper exhibits one grammar where it was obtainable and delimits what it then covers.

The result is also orthogonal to improving the physical fidelity of resource estimation.  Compilation-driven and full-stack estimators aim to replace coarse fixed assumptions with configurable hardware/software models \cite{campbell2026resourceestimation,autoqureo2026}.  Description-aware objectives separate execution cost from the complexity of specifying a circuit \cite{sarkar2026decomp2}.  The present question is different: whatever quantity is estimated, what certifies the reported value and what independently certifies its explanation?  A more detailed cost model still benefits from keeping theorem, finite validation, structural interpretation and transfer distinct.

For compiler studies the practical reporting order follows.  State the model and objective before quoting exactness.  Identify the unrestricted reference and the restricted forecast family.  Preserve every exact counterexample in the denominator.  Report whether a normal form is merely sufficient or also tight.  Test explanation vocabularies against complete fibres rather than only selected gaps.  When a feature law is found, disclose its cell structure and freeze a transfer coordinate.  These steps do not guarantee publication or practical usefulness, but they prevent several common evidential promotions.
