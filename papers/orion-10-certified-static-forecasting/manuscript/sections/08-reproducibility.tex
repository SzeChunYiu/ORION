\section{Data, code, and reproducibility}

All numerical claims arise from finite algebraic enumeration or exact optimization over Pauli keys; no human-subject, clinical, or personal data are used. Sanitized supplementary review material accompanying this manuscript contains:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a machine-readable evidence summary with the benchmark decomposition, adverse rows, comparison-event accounting, hostile-search counts, complete-domain counts, and changed-objective boundary;
  \item a deterministic checker for the published arithmetic and inequality relations;
  \item a reviewer guide that distinguishes all-size proof obligations from finite validation.
\end{itemize}

The full scientific implementation and detailed execution receipts are available to editors and referees during review. They are kept separate from the reader-facing archive because the latter contains only neutral scientific names and no development-history metadata. Before public submission, the author will deposit a persistent, licensed snapshot and bind its identifier to the final manuscript version. Until that deposit exists, no permanent-public-archive claim is made.

A faithful reproduction should preserve chronology: evaluate the initial formula, retain its mismatch, check the support-two evaluator on the same inputs, test the enlarged explanation on the frozen hostile panel, and only then apply the final classification theorem. Collapsing the sequence into a final zero-error table would remove the falsification evidence that motivates the certificate design.
