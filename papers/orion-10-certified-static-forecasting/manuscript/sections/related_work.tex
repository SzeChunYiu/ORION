\section{Related work and contribution boundary}

Algorithm selection and instance-space analysis already establish the general programme of mapping feature-conditioned performance regions \cite{rice1976algorithm,smithmiles2009meta,smithmiles2023isa}.  Quantum work has used instance spaces for parameter initialization, selected between quantum and classical methods, and predicted compilation options or target devices \cite{katial2025qaoa,moussa2020selection,quetschlich2023options,quetschlich2025predictor}.  The regime-map language in this paper is an evidential organization of exact results, not a claim to have invented feature-conditioned compiler selection.

Exact, verified and algebraic optimization is likewise established.  Prior work supplies verified circuit rewrites and oracle compilation, exact pattern matching, graph-theoretic simplification and bounded optimal Clifford synthesis \cite{hietala2021verified,li2022oracles,iten2022patterns,duncan2020zx,bravyi2022clifford}.  Benchmark suites characterize compiler behavior across circuits and devices \cite{li2023qasmbench,quetschlich2023mqtbench}.  These works absorb any claim that formal checking, exact search, compiler benchmarking or Clifford synthesis is introduced here.

The Pauli model descends from TARE and established Pauli compilation and partitioning \cite{schillo2026tare,izmaylov2020unitary,peres2023pbc,vandenberg2020diagonalization,paykin2023pcoast}.  Recent Hamiltonian-simulation compilation further emphasizes global binary-symplectic simplification \cite{yang2026symphony}.  The structural objectives studied here do not replace hardware-aware resource estimation, connectivity analysis or production compilation.

The closest residual is the combination demonstrated by the refutation chain: an all-size exact restricted-family cost, a separately falsifiable explanation family, prospectively staged and hostile exact-referee tests, complete-fibre audits, and row-specific verification authority.  The cross-model controls show why these fields are not redundant.  Conversely, this paper does not provide a new production optimizer, a broad benchmark, external hardware validation, or a universal compiler theory.  Its empirical and theorem claims stop at the declared algebraic models and finite transfer panel.
