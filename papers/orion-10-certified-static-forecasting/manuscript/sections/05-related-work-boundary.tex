\section{Related work and residual contribution}

Static quantum cost analysis has been developed at several abstraction levels. Quantum expectation transformers provide compositional cost reasoning for probabilistic quantum programs \cite{avanzini2022qet}. Type systems have been used to infer flexible resource bounds and circuit width \cite{colledan2025flexible,colledan2025width}, while control-flow analyses expose the non-Clifford cost of error correction \cite{yuan2024tcomplexity}. Full-stack estimators instead connect logical programs, error-correction choices, and hardware assumptions \cite{vandam2023azure}. These approaches motivate certified and early-stage resource reasoning, but our cost is a compiler-specific structural objective rather than a general program or hardware resource vector.

A second line of work verifies circuit optimization. The verified optimizer VOQC proves semantics-preserving rewrites in a proof assistant \cite{hietala2021voqc}; Giallar automates verification conditions for compiler passes \cite{tao2022giallar}. Our theorem certifies the value of a restricted structural optimization, not semantic equivalence of an arbitrary circuit transformation. The two forms of assurance are complementary.

Pauli structure is central to simulation compilers and block-encoding methods. Paulihedral performs block-wise optimization of Pauli-string kernels \cite{li2022paulihedral}; PCOAST uses a Pauli intermediate representation \cite{paykin2023pcoast}; global binary-symplectic simplification has recently been proposed for Hamiltonian simulation \cite{yang2026symphony}; and stabilizer-formalism constructions support block encodings of Pauli linear combinations \cite{schillo2026blockencoding}. We adopt the neighboring algebraic language but do not claim these compiler or block-encoding mechanisms as contributions.

The residual contribution is the combination of (i) an all-size support-reduction certificate for the exact structural cost, (ii) a separately falsifiable explanation classification, (iii) chronologically staged positive and hostile tests, and (iv) row-level distinction between theorem authority and executable verification. A bounded literature search ending on 28 August 2026 did not locate an equivalent combination for this grammar. That search is not a novelty certificate, and we make no claim of being the first static resource analysis, verified compiler, or Pauli-aware optimizer.
