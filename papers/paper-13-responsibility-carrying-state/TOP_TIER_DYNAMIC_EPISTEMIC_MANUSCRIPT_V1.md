## Abstract

Scientific systems increasingly encode rich states as proliferating terminal labels. P13 replaces label-as-state with a heterogeneous dynamic epistemic state containing evidence, identified sets, coverage, obligations, provenance, verification, authority, support families, defeaters, custody, resources, method reach, and the knowledge web. It absorbs statistical sufficiency, Blackwell comparison, information bottlenecks, type systems, proof-carrying state, and lifelong memory. We prove responsibility-relative quotient minimality, family joins, legacy non-reconstruction, strict planning advantage over terminal-only control, and exact safe reuse/reopening. On a retrospective internal 144-state control, dynamic-state planning selected the correct next action in 144/144 cases, whereas terminal-only planning was correct in 97/144 and retained 47 error/regret rows. The panel contains 4,355 same-terminal/different-action collision pairs, all resolved by the structured state and none by terminal-only control. A separately bound common projection packet reproduced 5,760/5,760 frozen legacy oracle decisions. These results establish a bounded planning advantage and strict representational refinement on the declared internal class; they do not establish full five-arm dominance, external lifecycle benefit, safe-reuse/recovery advantage, or population authority.
