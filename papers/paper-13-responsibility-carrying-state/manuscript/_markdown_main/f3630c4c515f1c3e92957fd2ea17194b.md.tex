\markdownRendererHeadingOne{Certificate transport, related work and reproducibility}\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}Responsibility support is conditional on evidence identity, transform version, required context, witness identity and resource envelope. A semantic change can require preserve, reopen, revoke or \markdownRendererCodeSpan{CANNOT\markdownRendererUnderscore{}CHECK} behavior. The RCS object does not certify its own scientific authority; an evaluator can establish operational support without granting novelty, publication or safety-critical deployment authority.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}Recent proof-carrying agent-action work attaches model-agnostic certificates to actions and runtime governance. Provenance traces evidence and execution. Memory-staleness systems detect that stored state is no longer valid. These donors make it insufficient to claim simply that “state should carry a certificate.” P13's discriminator is the \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{responsibility key plus reopen semantics}: a state may be current, well-provenanced and high-confidence yet insufficient for another downstream responsibility.\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}The support matrix is deterministic and exact. The historical negative is reported exactly as frozen and is not reanalysed into a positive result. P13A uses fresh seed/families and a different primary estimand. The paper reports protected rates and deterministic replay rather than adding post-hoc inferential tests. A real-system extension should define task/domain as the unit of generalization and use paired matched comparisons with family/domain-block uncertainty.
