A compact state is not simply “sufficient” or “insufficient.” Sufficiency is relative to what the downstream system is responsible for doing. A representation adequate for prediction can omit distinctions required for intervention, verification, diagnosis or repair; confidence and provenance alone do not specify that boundary. We formalize \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{responsibility-relative sufficiency} and introduce a non-self-authorizing \markdownRendererCodeSpan{ResponsibilityCarryingState} (RCS) contract that binds compact state to the responsibilities it supports, its independent witness, explicit omissions, context assumptions, raw-state recoverability, resource envelope and reopen conditions. The paper preserves a historical negative: an earlier frozen sufficiency-debt experiment missed its finite-sample sentinel at \markdownRendererCodeSpan{0.0556640625 > 0.05}; the result is retained rather than retuned. An independently frozen successor uses exact responsibility equivalence classes and a held-out reuse benchmark. Across \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{12,288 protected episodes}, RCS produces \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{0 unsafe reuses}, \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{0 unnecessary reopens} and \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{0.9807 verified correctness}. Confidence-only makes \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{21.56\markdownRendererPercentSign{} structurally unsafe reuses}; provenance-only and unqualified compact state make \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{39.62\markdownRendererPercentSign{}}. RCS costs \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{2.875} units on average versus \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{5.732} for always reopening raw state and emits \markdownRendererCodeSpan{CANNOT\markdownRendererUnderscore{}CHECK} for every unsupported/nonrecoverable case. Two runs are byte-identical. The controlled claim is that \markdownRendererStrongEmphasis{an explicit responsibility/recovery contract can eliminate structurally unsafe state reuse without degenerating to always-reopen behavior, whereas scalar confidence and provenance do not encode the same sufficiency boundary}.\markdownRendererDocumentEnd
